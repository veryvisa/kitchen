Store and portion储存与分装

Refrigerate 3-5 days; freeze 6-9 months. Thaw a whole leg in the fridge overnight (about 24 hours for 2 kg).冷藏 3–5 天；冷冻 6–9 个月。整腿提前一晚放冷藏解冻（约 2 公斤要 24 小时）。

A whole leg can be cut in two roasting portions, each double wrapped before freezing.整腿可对半切成两个烤的量，各自包两层再冷冻。
