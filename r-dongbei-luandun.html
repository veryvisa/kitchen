Northeastern Mixed Vegetable Stew东北乱炖

Northeastern mixed vegetable stew braises pork ribs with green beans, potatoes, eggplant, and corn in one savory pot. The secret is simmering the ribs until nearly tender first, then adding the vegetables in stages so the beans cook all the way through without turning the potatoes and eggplant to mush.东北乱炖把排骨、豆角、土豆、茄子和玉米焖在一锅里，荤素借味、汤浓好下饭。做法不难，关键是先把排骨炖软，再按耐煮程度分批下菜，豆角务必炖透，土豆和茄子才不会提前化掉。

Ingredients食材

  • Pork back ribs猪肋排 600 g · 600 g pork spareribs, cut into 4 cm pieces猪肋排 600 克，剁成 4 厘米段
  • Green beans四季豆 250 g · 250 g Romano beans or green beans, trimmed and snapped in half油豆角或扁豆角 250 克，摘去老筋折半 Flat Romano beans hold up best to braising; if using thin regular green beans, add them with the potatoes instead推荐耐炖的油豆角或扁豆角；若用普通四季豆，可改在放土豆时一起下锅
  • Potatoes土豆 350 g · 2 medium potatoes, cut into large chunks2 个中等土豆，切滚刀块
  • Eggplant茄子 250 g · 1 long eggplant, cut into large rolling chunks1 根长茄子，切滚刀块
  • Corn on the cob玉米 300 g · 1 cob sweet corn, cut into 3 cm rounds1 根甜玉米，剁成 3 厘米厚段
  • Tomatoes西红柿 200 g · 1 large tomato, chopped1 个大番茄，切块

  • Dark soy sauce老抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 30 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Salt盐 6 g · about 1 tsp, added near the end约 1 茶匙，最后调味

Method做法

  1. Put the pork ribs in cold water with 2 slices of ginger and 1 tbsp cooking wine, and bring to a boil over high heat.排骨冷水下锅，加 2 片姜和 1 汤匙料酒，大火烧开。
  2. Skim off the foam, boil 2 more minutes, then lift out the ribs and rinse with warm water.撇净表面浮沫，继续煮 2 分钟后捞出排骨，用温水冲净沥干。
  3. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat and stir-fry the green onion, remaining ginger, garlic, star anise, and bay leaves for 1 minute until fragrant.炖锅倒油中火烧热，下葱段、剩余姜片、蒜瓣、八角和香叶炒 1 分钟，直到闻到香味。
  4. Add the ribs and stir-fry over medium-high heat for 3 minutes until dry on the surface and lightly browned at the edges.放入排骨中大火翻炒 3 分钟，直到表面水汽收干、边缘微焦。
  5. Pour in the remaining wine, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, and sugar, stirring 1 minute until the ribs are evenly coated.淋入剩余料酒、生抽、老抽和糖，翻炒 1 分钟让排骨均匀上色。
  6. Add the chopped tomato and cook 2 minutes until softened and juicy.加入番茄块翻炒 2 分钟，炒到番茄变软出汁。
  7. Pour in about 1.2 to 1.5 L boiling water so all the ingredients will be mostly submerged once the vegetables are added (top up with extra boiling water if using a wide pot), bring to a boil, cover, and simmer over low heat for 35 minutes.倒入约 1.2 到 1.5 升开水，使后续加入全部蔬菜加盖后能基本浸没食材（若炖锅口径较大可按需补加开水），大火烧开后转小火加盖炖 35 分钟。
  8. Add the corn rounds and Romano beans (if using thin regular green beans, hold them for the next step), cover, and simmer on low for 10 minutes.放入玉米段和油豆角（或扁豆角），加盖继续小火炖 10 分钟；若用普通四季豆，可等下步放土豆时再一起下锅。
  9. Stir in the potatoes and salt (along with thin green beans if using), cover, and simmer over low heat for 9 minutes, tasting and adjusting the seasoning.加入土豆块和盐（若用四季豆此时一并放入），拌匀后加盖小火炖 9 分钟，并尝味微调咸淡。
  10. Gently submerge the eggplant chunks in the broth, cover, and simmer 6 more minutes until the ribs are tender and pull easily from the bone, reach at least 71°C internally, and a chopstick slides easily into the potatoes.放入茄子块轻轻压入汤中，加盖再炖 6 分钟，直到排骨酥烂能轻松脱骨、中心温度达到 71°C 以上，且筷子能轻松扎透土豆。
  11. Uncover and boil over medium-high heat for 3 minutes until the broth thickens slightly and clings to the vegetables, then serve.开盖转中大火收汁 3 分钟，看到汤汁变浓亮、挂在菜上即可盛出。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Always cook green beans all the way through, as undercooked beans taste tough and can upset the stomach; start thick Romano beans ahead of the potatoes, or add thin regular green beans with the potatoes.豆角务必彻底炖熟，未熟透的豆角口感生硬且容易引起肠胃不适；厚荚油豆角或扁豆角先下锅多炖，薄荚四季豆可随土豆一起下锅。
  • Cut the eggplant into large rolling chunks and add it only after the potatoes have simmered partway so it soaks up the broth without breaking down into mush.茄子切滚刀块后等土豆炖到后段再下锅，轻轻翻动即可，既能吸足汤汁又不容易炖烂成泥。
  • Add salt around the time the potatoes go in and taste to adjust so the stew does not turn overly salty as it reduces, and hold back the potatoes until late in the cook so they do not disintegrate.盐在土豆下锅前后加入并尝味调整，避免过早加太多盐导致收汁后过咸；土豆也不宜过早下锅以免糊汤。
  • Always use boiling water rather than cold water, which tightens the meat fibers and slows down flavor extraction.加水必须用开水，排骨遇冷水会骤然收缩，后面的汤也不容易炖出香味。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The broth is watery汤汁太稀像水煮菜
    Uncover and boil over medium-high heat for 3-5 minutes, or mash one or two potato chunks to thicken the broth naturally.开盖中大火收汁 3 到 5 分钟，也可以压碎一两块土豆让汤汁自然变稠。
  • The potatoes and eggplant fell apart茄子和土豆全都炖碎了
    Next time add the potatoes only for the final 15 minutes and the eggplant for the last 5-8 minutes, keeping the pot at a gentle simmer rather than a hard boil.下次留到最后 15 分钟再放土豆、最后 5 到 8 分钟再放茄子，炖的期间保持微沸小火，不要用大火剧烈翻滚。
  • The green beans still taste raw or crunchy豆角发硬或还有豆腥味
    Simmer the beans until completely changed in color with no raw beany flavor or crunch; thin green beans take about 10-12 minutes while thick flat Romano beans often need 20 minutes or more, so taste one before turning off the heat.豆角必须炖至完全变色、无生豆腥味和脆生感；薄荚四季豆约 10 到 12 分钟，厚荚油豆角常需 20 分钟以上，出锅前夹一根试吃确认。
