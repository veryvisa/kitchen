Tomato Beef Brisket Soup西红柿牛腩汤

Brisket needs a slow simmer and tomatoes need to be cooked down until jammy, and together they make a rich, sweet-sour red soup. Blanching the brisket first clears the impurities, tomato paste adds depth, and carrots finish the soup with a rounder sweetness.牛腩要慢炖，番茄要炒出沙，两样东西慢慢合成一锅酸甜浓郁的红汤。先焯牛腩去血水，番茄炒到出汁、再加番茄酱提鲜，炖到肉软烂、汤变稠，加胡萝卜后味道更圆。

140 min total分钟 (20 + 120)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Beef brisket牛腩 600 g · about 600 g beef brisket, cut into 3 cm cubes牛腩约 600 克，切 3 厘米方块 chuck can substitute if brisket is unavailable牛腩不容易买到时可用牛肩肉
  • Tomatoes西红柿 600 g · 4 medium tomatoes, cut in large wedges4 个中等番茄，切大块
  • Onions洋葱 100 g · half a medium onion, chopped半个中等洋葱，切块
  • Carrots胡萝卜 150 g · 1 carrot, rolling-cut into chunks1 根，滚刀切块
  • Ginger姜 20 g · 6 slices6 片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 2 stalks, cut into sections2 根，切段
  • Tomato paste番茄膏 30 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 30 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Bay leaves月桂叶 1 g · 2 leaves2 片
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Salt盐 6 g · about 1 tsp, added at the end to taste约 1 茶匙，最后按口味加

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Put the brisket in a pot with cold water to cover, 3 ginger slices and 1 tbsp wine.牛腩放入冷水锅，加 3 片姜和 1 汤匙料酒，水刚没过肉。
  2. Bring to a boil, skim the foam and cook 3 minutes more.大火煮开，撇掉浮沫，再煮 3 分钟。
  3. Lift out the beef, rinse with hot water and drain.捞出牛腩，用热水冲净，沥干。
  4. In a clean heavy pot, heat the oil on medium and soften the onion and remaining ginger for about 3 minutes.锅里放 2 汤匙油，中火下洋葱和剩下的姜片，炒约 3 分钟到洋葱变软。
  5. Stir in the tomato paste and fry about 1 minute until it darkens.放入番茄酱，炒约 1 分钟，酱色变深。
  6. Add the tomatoes and cook about 5 minutes until they collapse and release juice.倒入番茄块，炒约 5 分钟，番茄变软出汁、边缘塌下去。
  7. Add the beef, remaining 1 tbsp wine, soy sauce, sugar and bay leaves and stir 2 minutes.放入牛腩、料酒 1 汤匙、生抽、糖和香叶，翻炒 2 分钟。
  8. Pour in enough hot water to cover the meat by about 3 cm and bring to a boil.倒入热水，没过肉约 3 厘米，大火煮开。
  9. Cover, turn to the lowest heat and simmer for 60 minutes.盖上盖，转最小火，炖 60 分钟。
  10. Add the carrot and simmer covered another 45 minutes, until a chopstick slips easily through the beef.放入胡萝卜，继续盖盖炖 45 分钟，牛腩用筷子能轻松插透。
  11. Uncover, season with salt and boil 5 minutes to the thickness you like; taste and add the scallion.揭盖，加盐调味，大火煮 5 分钟收汁到想要的浓度，尝过咸淡后加葱段。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cook the tomatoes down before adding water; jammy tomatoes melt their sweet-sour flavor into the soup, while boiled raw ones just taste sharp.番茄要先炒再炖，炒出沙的番茄才能把酸甜融进汤里，直接下水煮只有酸味。
  • Starting the brisket in cold water draws impurities out gradually, and adding hot water during the braise keeps the temperature from dropping so the meat does not seize.牛腩冷水下锅焯水，血水才慢慢出来；炖煮时只加热水，温度不掉，肉不紧。
  • Salt late; early salt firms the meat and, after the liquid reduces, makes the soup too salty.盐最后放：肉没炖软之前加盐，会让肉变紧、汤变咸。
  • Keep only small bubbles on the surface; a hard boil dries out the meat.牛腩要小火炖，汤面只能有小气泡；大滚会让肉变柴。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The soup is too sour汤太酸
    Add half a teaspoon of sugar and simmer 5 more minutes. Next time cook the tomatoes longer or add a second carrot.多放半茶匙糖，再煮 5 分钟；下次番茄炒久一点，或者多放一个胡萝卜。
  • The beef is still chewy after hours牛肉炖了很久还是嚼不动
    The heat was too high or the cubes too small. Keep to tiny bubbles for another 30 minutes and test with a chopstick.火太大或牛腩切得偏小；保持小气泡再炖 30 分钟，用筷子试到能插透。
  • A layer of fat floats on top汤面浮着一层油
    Skim it with a spoon, or chill the soup so the fat sets and lifts off easily.用勺子撇掉，或放凉后冷藏，油凝固后很容易去掉。
