Bibimbap石锅拌饭

Bibimbap brings rice, beef, and seasoned vegetables together in one bowl. A home cast-iron skillet can make a crisp rice crust; use rice that is not wet and leave it undisturbed once it is in the pan.石锅拌饭把米饭、牛肉和几样蔬菜分开调味，最后在滚烫的锅里合在一起。家用铸铁锅也能做出锅巴，关键是米饭要偏干，铺好后不要急着翻动。

50 min total分钟 (20 + 30)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Mung bean sprouts另购：绿豆芽

Ingredients食材

  • Short-grain rice短粒米(寿司米) 180 g · 1 cup short-grain rice, uncooked短粒米 1 杯，生米
  • Ground beef牛肉馅 180 g · ground beef牛肉末
  • Eggs鸡蛋 100 g · 2 large eggs2 个大鸡蛋
  • Carrots胡萝卜 100 g · 1 carrot, cut into thin matchsticks1 根，切细丝
  • Spinach菠菜 100 g · a small bunch一小把
  • Mung bean sprouts绿豆芽 120 g · mung bean sprouts绿豆芽
  • Zucchini西葫芦 120 g · 1 zucchini, cut into thin matchsticks1 根，切细丝
  • White mushrooms蘑菇 100 g · sliced切片
  • Gochujang韩式辣酱 30 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1 1/2 tbsp, divided between the beef and sauce1 汤匙半，分作牛肉和拌酱
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 23 g · about 1 1/2 tbsp, divided约 1 汤匙半，分次使用
  • Sugar白糖 12 g · 1 tbsp, divided1 汤匙，分次使用
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · about 4 cloves, minced约 4 瓣，切末
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp, divided2 汤匙，分次使用
  • Salt盐 3 g · about 1/2 tsp, divided约 1/2 茶匙，分次使用
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 6 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙

Method做法

  1. Rinse the rice until the water is mostly clear, then cook it with water according to the rice cooker markings.短粒米淘洗至水较清，按电饭锅刻度加水煮熟。
  2. Stir together the gochujang, 18 g soy sauce, 10 g sesame oil, and 8 g sugar for the serving sauce.将辣椒酱、生抽 18 克、芝麻油 10 克和糖 8 克搅匀成拌饭酱。
  3. Mix the beef with the remaining soy sauce, half the garlic, 4 g sugar, and 5 g sesame oil.牛肉末加入剩余生抽、蒜末一半、糖 4 克和芝麻油 5 克拌匀。
  4. Cut the carrot and zucchini into matchsticks and the mushroom into thin slices.胡萝卜、西葫芦和香菇分别切成细丝或薄片。
  5. Bring water to a boil, blanch the spinach for 30 seconds, drain it well and squeeze out excess water, then season with a little salt, sesame oil and half the remaining garlic.锅中烧开水，菠菜焯 30 秒，捞出沥干并挤去多余水分，拌入少许盐、芝麻油和剩余蒜末的一半。
  6. Boil the sprouts in the same pot for 2 minutes, drain them well and season with a little salt, sesame oil and the rest of the garlic.豆芽放入同锅沸水煮 2 分钟，捞出沥干并拌入少许盐、芝麻油和剩下的蒜末。
  7. Heat a little oil in a skillet over medium heat and cook the carrot for 2 minutes until tender-crisp.炒锅中火加少许油，胡萝卜炒 2 分钟至变软但仍有脆感。
  8. Cook the zucchini for 2 minutes until bright in color, then transfer it to a plate.西葫芦下锅炒 2 分钟至颜色鲜亮，盛出。
  9. Cook the mushroom for 3 minutes until softened and its released liquid has evaporated, then transfer it to a plate.香菇下锅炒 3 分钟至变软并收干水分，盛出。
  10. Add the beef to the skillet and cook over medium heat for about 6 minutes, breaking it up until fully cooked and at 71°C.锅中加入牛肉末，中火炒散约 6 分钟，直至全熟并达到 71°C。
  11. Fry the eggs in a little oil until the whites are set and the yolks reach your preferred doneness; for pregnant women, young children, older adults or anyone with a weaker immune system, use pasteurized eggs or cook the yolk fully.另用少许油煎鸡蛋，至蛋白凝固、蛋黄达到喜欢的熟度；孕妇、幼儿、老人或免疫力较弱者食用时，建议用巴氏杀菌蛋或把蛋黄煎至全熟。
  12. Preheat a cast-iron or heavy-bottomed skillet over medium heat for 3 minutes and lightly oil it.铸铁锅或厚底锅中火预热 3 分钟，薄薄抹油。
  13. Spread the rice in an even layer in the hot pan and cook over medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes until the edges begin to crisp.将米饭铺入热锅，压成均匀薄层，中火煎 4 至 5 分钟至边缘微微发脆。
  14. Arrange the spinach, sprouts, carrot, zucchini, mushroom, and beef over the rice, then keep over medium-low heat for 1 to 2 minutes until the bottom sizzles again and the toppings are heated through.把菠菜、豆芽、胡萝卜、西葫芦、香菇和牛肉分别摆在米饭上，保持中小火续煎 1 至 2 分钟，至锅底重新发出轻微滋滋声、配料热透。
  15. Add the fried eggs and sesame seeds, spoon on the serving sauce, remove the pan from the heat, and mix from the bottom before eating.放上煎蛋和芝麻，淋上拌饭酱，离火后从锅底翻拌均匀再食用。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cook the vegetables separately to keep their colors and textures distinct and avoid excess moisture in the pan.蔬菜分开炒或焯，颜色和口感更清楚，也不容易把锅底炒出太多水。
  • Spread the rice evenly and leave it alone while crisping so it can form a proper crust.煎锅巴时米饭铺薄且均匀，之后别挪动，才容易形成完整脆底。
  • Start with a little serving sauce and add more after tasting to control the heat and salt.拌饭酱可先少量加入，尝过后再添，辣度和咸度更容易控制。
  • Cook the ground beef to 71°C; an even brown color and no raw-looking spots help confirm it is ready.牛肉末炒到 71°C，颜色均匀变褐且没有粉红生肉，口感和安全都更稳妥。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The rice has no crisp crust米饭底部没有锅巴
    Preheat the pan well, use a thin film of oil, and leave the rice over medium heat for at least 4 minutes without stirring.先把锅充分预热并擦薄油，米饭铺好后保持中火至少 4 分钟，不要频繁翻动。
  • The bibimbap turned wet and soft拌饭变得湿软
    Drain the vegetables well and avoid wet rice; if liquid has collected, crisp the rice before mixing.蔬菜沥干后再摆入锅，米饭也不要煮得太湿；已出水时先把锅巴煎好再拌。
  • The beef formed large clumps or is undercooked牛肉结成大块或中心未熟
    Break up the beef as soon as it hits the pan and continue cooking until the center reaches 71°C; do not rely on surface color alone.牛肉下锅后立刻用锅铲拆散，继续炒至中心达到 71°C；不要只凭表面颜色判断。
