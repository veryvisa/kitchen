Shaoxing Drunken Chicken醉鸡

Shaoxing drunken chicken is a classic Jiangnan chilled dish with crisp skin, silky meat, and a warm aged-wine fragrance. Made a day ahead, it works effortlessly for weekend dinners or entertaining. Success depends on gentle poaching rather than hard boiling, an ice bath to firm the skin, and adding the wine only after the broth has chilled completely. For lunchboxes, keep it cold the whole way with an insulated bag and ice packs rather than leaving it at room temperature.醉鸡是江浙经典的冷菜，鸡肉经过冰镇后皮爽弹牙、肉质滑嫩，透着陈年花雕的醇香。提前一天做好放冰箱冷藏，周末加菜很省事；关键在于小火浸熟而不是大火滚煮，以及等鸡汤彻底冰凉后再混入花雕酒，才能保住浓郁酒香。带便当的话要用保温冷藏饭盒配冰袋全程冷藏，别在室温久放。

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 700 g · 4 boneless skin-on chicken thighs (about 700 g)带皮去骨鸡腿肉 4 块（约 700 克） bone-in thighs also work; add about 4 minutes to the poaching time带骨鸡腿也可，浸煮时间需增加约 4 分钟
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 240 g · 1 cup (240 mL) aged Shaoxing Huadiao wine1 杯（240 mL，正宗绍兴花雕酒） unsalted Shaoxing wine gives the cleanest aroma选用未加盐的花雕酒或陈年绍兴酒风味最好
  • Ginger姜 25 g · 5 slices, smashed5 片拍松
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 whole stalks, knotted3 根整葱打结
  • Salt盐 12 g · 2 tsp total (1/2 tsp for poaching, 1 1/2 tsp for the marinade)2 茶匙（煮鸡用半茶匙，调醉汁用 1 茶匙半）
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Dried goji berries枸杞 10 g · about 1 tbsp, rinsed约 1 汤匙，洗净

Method做法

  1. Trim excess fat from the edges of the chicken thighs and rub evenly with 1/2 tsp salt.修去鸡腿肉边缘多余的脂肪，正反面抹匀半茶匙盐。
  2. Place the chicken thighs, smashed ginger, and knotted green onions in a pot, cover with cold water by 2 cm, and heat over medium-high until just below a boil.将鸡腿肉、姜片和葱结放入煮锅，加冷水刚没过鸡肉约 2 厘米，中大火煮至将开未开。
  3. Skim off any foam, reduce to the lowest heat, cover, and poach gently for 12 to 15 minutes until an instant-read thermometer in the thickest part reads 74°C.撇净表面浮沫，立刻转最小火并盖上锅盖，小火浸煮 12 至 15 分钟，直到鸡肉最厚处中心温度达到 74°C。
  4. Turn off the heat and leave the chicken covered in the hot broth for 5 minutes.关火后保持盖盖，让鸡肉留在热汤里再焖 5 分钟。
  5. Transfer the cooked chicken straight into a bowl of ice water for 10 minutes until completely cold and the skin feels firm.捞出鸡腿肉立刻放进准备好的冰水盆中，浸泡 10 分钟直到彻底凉透、鸡皮收紧。
  6. Strain 1 cup (240 mL) of the warm poaching broth, stir in the remaining 1 1/2 tsp salt and the sugar until dissolved, cool it quickly in an ice bath, then refrigerate until completely cold (do not leave it cooling at room temperature).过滤出 1 杯（240 mL）温热的原味鸡汤，放入剩下的 1 茶匙半盐和糖搅匀融化，坐进冰水盆快速降温，再放进冰箱冷藏至彻底变凉（不要留在室温慢慢等凉）。
  7. Stir the Shaoxing wine and rinsed goji berries into the completely chilled broth.向彻底冰凉的鸡汤中倒入花雕酒和洗净的枸杞，搅匀成醉鸡汁。
  8. Drain and pat the chilled chicken dry, pack it into a lidded container that fits it snugly, and pour over the wine marinade until fully submerged (if the container is too wide for the marinade to cover the chicken, top up with more broth and wine in roughly a 1:1 ratio, and add a little extra salt and sugar to match).取出冰透的鸡腿擦干表面水分，码入贴合鸡肉大小的保鲜盒，倒入醉鸡汁至完全没过鸡肉（容器太宽、醉汁没过不了鸡肉时，按鸡汤:花雕酒约 1:1 的比例补足液体，并按比例多加一点盐和糖）。
  9. Cover and refrigerate for at least 8 hours or overnight, then slice crosswise and spoon a little marinade over the top to serve.加盖放入冰箱冷藏至少 8 小时或过夜，食用前斜切成条并淋上少许醉汁。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Keep the broth at a gentle poach rather than a hard boil, and confirm doneness with a thermometer — this helps prevent the chicken from toughening from excess heat.全过程避免沸水剧烈翻滚，保持温和加热并用温度计确认熟度，能减少鸡肉因过度受热而发柴。
  • Plunging the hot poached chicken into ice water cools it quickly and firms up the skin and gelatin, making it easier to slice neatly once chilled.热鸡立刻进冰水能快速降温，让鸡皮和胶质更紧实，冷藏后切片也更容易保持完整。
  • Wait until the broth is completely cool before adding the wine so heat does not drive off the aroma or leave a bitter edge.花雕酒一定要等鸡汤凉透再倒，热汤会把酒香蒸散，还可能带出苦味。
  • A 1-to-1 ratio of Shaoxing wine to broth gives deep flavor all the way through without tasting overly harsh.酒与汤保持 1 比 1 左右，既能充分浸透肉里，又不会酒精味过冲。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The chicken is dry and stringy, or still pink inside鸡肉发柴、口感发干，或者切开中间还带血丝
    Stringiness comes from boiling too hard. Color alone cannot establish doneness — check the thickest part with a food thermometer, and if it has not reached 74°C, keep poaching gently and check again before chilling and marinating.发柴多半是水开太猛或煮过头；颜色不能单独判断熟没熟，一定要用食品温度计量最厚处，没到 74°C 就继续小火浸煮，再测一次确认达标才冰镇泡酒。
  • The wine tastes raw and flat, or slightly bitter只有生酒味不入味，或者带着苦味
    Salted cooking wine can turn harsh; use unsalted Shaoxing wine next time. If a finished batch tastes too strongly of wine, drain the marinade before slicing and spoon on less of it.普通加盐料酒加热或久泡容易发苦，下次换未加盐的花雕酒；已经做好且酒味过重的，沥去醉汁后切片，淋汁量减半即可。
  • The skin slips off while slicing切片时鸡皮滑脱散开
    Marinating chicken that is not fully chilled and dried can cause the skin to slip; make sure it is thoroughly chilled and patted dry, then slice cleanly across the grain with a sharp knife without dragging the blade.鸡肉没凉透、没沥干就泡酒容易脱皮；确保鸡肉充分冷藏、表面擦干，用锋利的刀垂直纤维方向一刀切下，不要来回拉扯鸡皮。

Chicken thighs provide solid protein with moderate fat and no added cooking oil. The marinade is high in sodium, and because the Shaoxing wine is never heated, the chicken can retain a meaningful amount of alcohol — avoid serving it to children, pregnant people, or anyone who needs to avoid alcohol.去骨鸡腿脂肪适中、蛋白质丰富，整道菜几乎不额外加油；但醉汁含较多钠，且花雕酒全程没有加热，鸡肉吸收的醉汁里仍可能带有实际意义的酒精含量，儿童、孕妇及需要避酒人群不要食用。
