Beef and Potato Stew土豆炖牛肉

A Dongbei winter staple. Beef chuck is blanched and braised slowly until tender, and the potatoes soak up the broth. It makes two meals, and tastes better the next day.东北人冬天的一锅暖饭。牛肩肉焯水后慢炖到筋软，土豆吸饱汤汁，一锅能吃两顿，第二天更入味。

120 min total分钟 (20 + 100)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine另购：老抽、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Beef chuck / stewing beef牛肩肉(炖牛肉用) 800 g · chuck or stewing beef, cut in 3 cm cubes牛肩肉/炖牛肉用，切 3 厘米方块
  • Potatoes土豆 600 g · 3 medium potatoes, cut in chunks3 个中等土豆，切滚刀块
  • Carrots胡萝卜 200 g · 2 carrots, cut in chunks2 根胡萝卜，切滚刀块
  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 medium onion, cut in chunks1 个中等洋葱，切块
  • Tomatoes西红柿 200 g · 1 large tomato, chopped1 个大番茄，切块
  • Ginger姜 20 g · 6 slices6 片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks, in pieces3 根，切段
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 30 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Salt盐 5 g · about 1 tsp, added in stages约 1 茶匙，分次加
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Put the beef in a pot of cold water with 2 slices of ginger and 1 tbsp wine and bring to a boil over high heat.牛肉冷水下锅，加 2 片姜和料酒 1 汤匙，大火烧开。
  2. Skim off the scum, cook 3 minutes, lift the beef out and rinse with warm water.撇净浮沫，煮 3 分钟，捞出用温水冲洗，沥干。
  3. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven and fry the green onion, remaining ginger and the onion for about 2 minutes.炖锅放油烧热，下葱段、剩下的姜片和洋葱，炒出香味约 2 分钟。
  4. Add the beef and stir over medium heat for 3 minutes until the surface is dry.放入牛肉，中火翻炒 3 分钟，炒干表面水汽。
  5. Add the soy sauces, sugar and the remaining wine, and stir 1 minute to colour the meat.加生抽、老抽、糖和剩下的料酒，翻炒 1 分钟上色。
  6. Add the tomato and cook until it releases its juice, about 2 minutes.加入番茄块，炒到出汁，约 2 分钟。
  7. Pour in boiling water to 3 cm above the beef.倒入开水，水面超过牛肉约 3 厘米。
  8. Bring to a boil, turn to low, cover and simmer 60 minutes.烧开后转小火，盖盖炖 60 分钟。
  9. Add the potatoes, carrots and 3 g salt and simmer 25 more minutes, until a chopstick slides into the potato.放入土豆和胡萝卜，加盐 3 克，再炖 25 分钟，直到筷子能轻松插进土豆。
  10. Taste, add up to 2 g more salt if needed, boil down 3-5 minutes over high heat and serve.尝味，需要时再加 2 克盐，大火收汁 3–5 分钟后装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cut the beef large (3 cm or more); it shrinks as it cooks and small pieces fall apart.牛肉块要大（3 厘米以上），炖久了会缩小，切太小会炖碎。
  • Start the beef in cold water so the scum comes out and the broth stays clear.牛肉要冷水下锅焯水，血沫才能出得干净，汤才清爽。
  • Add boiling water, never cold; cold water tightens the meat.加水一定用开水，冷水会让肉收紧变老。
  • Salt after the potatoes go in, and add the potatoes late so they do not disintegrate.盐在土豆下锅后再加，肉先加盐会收紧；土豆下得太早会煮烂。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The beef is still tough after a long time肉炖了很久还是硬
    Cuts vary; keep it at a low simmer another 20-30 minutes, or use a pressure cooker for about 25 minutes.牛肉各部位老嫩不同，继续小火炖 20–30 分钟；用高压锅可缩短到 25 分钟。
  • The potatoes fell apart and clouded the broth土豆烂成泥、汤很浑
    Use a waxy yellow potato in big chunks, simmer only 25 minutes; if they have broken down, call it a thick stew and serve.选不易烂的黄土豆，切大块，只炖 25 分钟；已经烂了就当浓汤，捞出肉另放。
  • Too salty太咸
    Add a cut potato and simmer 10 minutes, or thin with hot water; next time add salt in two stages.加一个切块的土豆再煮 10 分钟，或加热水稀释；下次盐分两次加。
