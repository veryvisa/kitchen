Liangpi Cold Noodles凉皮

Liangpi is an appetite-whetting northwest Chinese cold dish: slippery, springy noodles tossed with cucumber, bean sprouts, garlic-vinegar sauce and chili oil. At home the dough is washed and the starch settles; batter thickness decides the texture, and each sheet is done only when big bubbles appear on top.凉皮是夏天最开胃的西北凉食：薄滑筋道的面皮配上黄瓜丝、豆芽和蒜醋辣油。家里做要先洗面沉淀淀粉，面糊稠稀决定凉皮是否筋道，蒸的时候每张要起大泡才算熟。

Ingredients食材

Method做法

  1. Mix the flour and salt, add water gradually and knead a firm, smooth dough; cover and rest 30 minutes.面粉加盐拌匀，分次加清水，揉成偏硬的光滑面团，盖盖醒 30 分钟。
  2. Wash the dough in a bowl of clean water until the water turns milky white; lift out the gluten and let the starch water settle for 2 hours.面团放入大碗，加清水反复揉洗，直到洗出的水变得很白；捞出面筋，淀粉水静置 2 小时。
  3. Steam the gluten over high heat for 15 minutes, then cut it into small pieces.面筋放入蒸锅，大火蒸 15 分钟，取出切成小块。
  4. Pour off the clear water on top and stir the settled starch into a batter that pours in a steady stream.倒掉淀粉水表层的清水，把沉淀淀粉搅匀成可连续滴落的面糊。
  5. Using a flat-bottomed pan about 20-22 cm across, brush it with oil and pour in about 60-80 mL of batter, spreading it thin (this batch makes about 5-6 sheets, enough for 2 servings); steam over boiling water 2-3 minutes until big bubbles rise on the sheet.用一个直径约 20–22 厘米的平底盅盘，刷一层薄油，舀入约 60–80 毫升面糊摊匀（这批面糊大约可以摊 5–6 张，够 2 人份）；蒸锅水沸后放入，大火蒸 2 到 3 分钟，看到凉皮鼓起大泡即熟。
  6. Lift the pan out, cool the edge in cold water, peel off the sheet, brush lightly with oil, stack them and cut into wide strips.取出盘子，沿边缘在凉水中降温，揭下凉皮，表面刷薄油，叠放后切成宽条。
  7. Blanch the sprouts 30 seconds, drain and rinse with cold water; shred the cucumber.豆芽焯水 30 秒，捞出过凉水沥干；黄瓜切细丝。
  8. Put the chili powder and sesame in a small bowl; heat the oil until it shimmers, let it cool slightly, then pour it over to make chili oil.辣椒面和芝麻放入小碗；油烧到微微冒烟，稍凉后倒入碗中制成辣椒油。
  9. Mix the mashed garlic with 2 tbsp water, then stir in the soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, salt, chili oil and sesame oil for the sauce.蒜泥加 2 汤匙清水调成蒜水，加生抽、醋、糖、盐、辣椒油和香油调成酱汁。
  10. Pile the noodles, cucumber, sprouts and gluten into bowls, pour on the sauce and toss to serve.凉皮、黄瓜丝、豆芽和面筋装碗，浇酱汁，拌匀即食。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Pour off all the clear water after settling: a thin batter breaks when lifted, while a thick batter makes stiff, heavy sheets.沉淀后要倒净表层清水，面糊太稀凉皮一夹就断，太稠则厚硬不滑。
  • Oil the pan and start with boiling water. Each sheet is done when big bubbles appear; do not open the lid halfway, or the sheets will turn pitted.蒸盘刷油、水沸再放；每张蒸到起大泡才熟，中途不要开盖，否则凉皮会起孔。
  • Brush the sheets with oil to keep them separate, and toss just before serving; they clump if left standing.凉皮表面刷油防粘，现拌现吃，放置久了会坨在一起。
  • Garlic water is milder than raw mashed garlic; add the vinegar little by little and taste before adding more.用蒜水代替生蒜末，辣感柔和不呛；醋先少量加入，尝过再补。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The sheets break and will not hold strips凉皮易碎、不成条
    Usually a thin batter or under-steaming. Drain more water after settling, stir well and steam each sheet a full 3 minutes.多为面糊太稀或蒸制不足；下次沉淀后多倒清水、搅匀，每张蒸足 3 分钟。
  • The noodles are stiff and not springy凉皮发硬、不筋道
    Weak flour or too little water. Use all-purpose flour, keep the batter pouring in a stream, and brush oil on right after steaming to stop the surface drying.面粉筋度偏低或水量偏少；用中筋面粉，面糊舀起能连续滴落；蒸好立刻刷油防止表面发干。
  • The dish is bland or too sour味道寡淡或太酸
    Add vinegar and soy in small amounts and taste as you go. If bland, add a little salt and soy; if too sour, balance with half a teaspoon of sugar and extra cucumber.酱汁先少量加醋和酱油拌匀尝味；寡淡补一点盐和生抽，过酸加半勺糖和黄瓜丝缓冲。

  • About 7 g fibre per serving, a good amount (Canadian adult reference 25-38 g/day).每份膳食纤维约 7 g，较多（加拿大成人每日参考 25–38 g）。
  • About 1475 mg sodium per serving (about 64% of the 2,300 mg daily reference), on the high side; use less soy sauce or salt, and pair with unsalted sides.每份钠约 1475 mg（约占每日参考 2300 mg 的 64%），偏高；少放酱油或盐，或搭配无盐主食与蔬菜。
