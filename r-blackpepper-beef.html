Black Pepper Beef Stir-fry黑椒牛柳

Restaurant black pepper beef is tender because the meat first absorbs water, then is sealed with starch and oil before a short, fiercely hot stir-fry. Pepper should be freshly cracked and coarse, added twice: once in the marinade for depth and once at the end for aroma.餐馆里黑椒牛柳又嫩又香，关键是牛肉要「吸水」再「封油」：先让肉吸进水分，再用淀粉和油锁住，大火快炒到刚熟。黑胡椒要现磨、碾粗，分两次放，一次入味，一次留香。

43 min total分钟 (15 + 8)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Striploin steak西冷牛排 300 g · about 300 g striploin steak西冷牛排约 300 克 tenderloin also works; slice across the grain也可用牛里脊，横向逆纹切条
  • Onions洋葱 100 g · half a medium onion, cut in wide strips半个中等洋葱，切宽条
  • Bell peppers彩椒 150 g · 1 bell pepper, cut in strips1 个青椒或彩椒，切条
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 3 cloves, minced3 瓣，切末
  • Ginger姜 5 g · minced, about 1 tsp切末，约 1 茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 3 g · about 1.5 tsp coarsely cracked (1/2 tsp in the marinade, 1 tsp at the end)约 1.5 茶匙，现磨粗粒（腌肉半茶匙，起锅前 1 茶匙）
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (1/2 for the beef, 1/2 for the sauce)1 汤匙（腌肉半汤匙，调汁半汤匙）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL), for the sauce1 汤匙（15 mL），调汁用
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 9 g · 3 tsp (2 for the beef, 1 for the sauce)3 茶匙（腌肉 2 茶匙，调汁 1 茶匙）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 56 g · 4 tbsp (60 mL), 1 for the marinade and 3 for cooking4 汤匙（60 mL），1 汤匙腌肉、3 汤匙炒制

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Cut the beef across the grain into strips about 0.8 cm thick and 6 cm long.牛肉逆着纹理切成约 0.8 厘米粗、6 厘米长的条。
  2. Work 2 tbsp water into the beef by hand until absorbed, then add 1/2 tbsp soy sauce, the wine and 1/2 tsp pepper.牛肉加 2 汤匙水，用手抓拌到水分被吸收，再加生抽半汤匙、料酒、半茶匙黑胡椒。
  3. Mix in 2 tsp cornstarch and 1 tbsp oil, cover and marinate for 20 minutes.拌入淀粉 2 茶匙和 1 汤匙油，盖好腌 20 分钟。
  4. Stir the oyster sauce, 1/2 tbsp soy sauce, sugar, 1 tsp cornstarch and 3 tbsp water into a sauce.蚝油、生抽半汤匙、糖、淀粉 1 茶匙和 3 汤匙水调成汁。
  5. Heat a wok until smoking, add 1.5 tbsp oil, spread the beef out and leave it for 40 seconds.炒锅烧到冒烟，倒 1.5 汤匙油，放入牛肉摊开，不动煎 40 秒。
  6. Flip and cook 30 seconds more until about 80% done and browned outside, then remove.翻面再炒 30 秒，表面变色约八成熟，盛出。
  7. Add 1.5 tbsp oil and stir-fry the onion and pepper over high heat for about 1 minute so they stay crisp.锅里补 1.5 汤匙油，大火炒洋葱和青椒约 1 分钟，仍保持脆。
  8. Add the garlic and ginger and stir for 10 seconds.放入蒜末和姜末炒 10 秒。
  9. Return the beef, pour in the sauce and toss about 30 seconds until it thickens.牛肉回锅，倒入调好的汁，翻炒约 30 秒到汁变稠。
  10. Sprinkle on the remaining pepper, toss once and serve.撒上剩下的黑胡椒，翻匀出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Water first, then starch and oil: the meat soaks in moisture, and the coating holds it in under high heat.先吸水后锁油：肉吸进水才嫩，淀粉加油在表面成膜，大火时水分不会跑掉。
  • Always cut across the grain so the fibres are short and the beef is not stringy.牛肉一定要逆着纹理切，纤维切短，咬起来才不塞牙。
  • Crack the pepper coarsely and fresh; coarse bits release aroma in the heat, while fine pepper turns bitter.黑胡椒要现磨且颗粒粗，高温会让粗粒在锅里出香，而不是糊掉；胡椒粉遇热容易苦。
  • Cook the beef in two stages: out at 80%, and let the final toss finish it.牛肉分两步熟：八成熟就盛出，回锅时汁一裹就好。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The beef is chewy牛肉嚼不烂
    Usually it was cut with the grain or overcooked. Slice thinner across the grain, cut it to 30 seconds a side, and marinate up to 40 minutes if you have time.多半是顺纹切了或炒过头。换成逆纹更细的条，缩短到每面 30 秒；有时间的话腌肉可以延长到 40 分钟。
  • It is watery and the vegetables are limp出水很多，菜不脆
    The pan was not hot enough or crowded. Cook the beef and vegetables separately, give the vegetables just 1 minute and never cover the pan.锅不够热或一次放太多。牛肉和蔬菜分开炒，蔬菜炒 1 分钟就够，大火不要盖盖。
