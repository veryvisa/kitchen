Steamed Garlic Eggplant蒜泥蒸茄子

A quick home-style dish that turns eggplant tender without deep-frying. The keys are steaming long enough for the flesh to soften completely and using both oil-sizzled and raw garlic so the savory sauce tastes bright and layered.家常快手菜，不用过油也能把茄子蒸得绵软入味。重点在于蒸的时间和铺在上面的热油蒜蓉酱：茄子要蒸透才不发硬，蒜末分生熟两半用热油激出香味再淋酱汁，口感顺滑不腻。

22 min total分钟 (10 + 12)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)另购：香醋/陈醋

Ingredients食材

  • Eggplant茄子 400 g · 2 to 3 long Chinese or Japanese eggplants, about 400 g total2–3 根长茄子，共约 400 克 thin purple Chinese or Japanese eggplants steam best选紫皮长茄子，肉嫩籽少
  • Garlic大蒜 30 g · about 8 cloves, finely minced约 8 瓣，切细末
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 1 to 2 stalks, thinly sliced1–2 根，切葱花
  • Canola oil菜籽油 21 g · 1.5 tbsp (22 mL)1 汤匙半（22 mL）
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)香醋/陈醋 8 g · 1/2 tbsp半汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Trim the eggplant stems, cut into 7 cm lengths, and split lengthwise into finger-thick strips.茄子去蒂切成约 7 厘米长段，再纵切成食指粗细的长条。
  2. Arrange the strips skin-side down in a heatproof plate with small gaps between them.把茄条皮朝下平铺进耐热深盘，之间留一点空隙。
  3. Bring water in a steamer to a rolling boil over high heat, cover, and steam the eggplant 10-12 minutes until a chopstick slides in easily.蒸锅大火把水烧开，放入茄子加盖蒸 10–12 分钟，直到筷子能轻松扎透。
  4. While steaming, heat the canola oil in a small pan over medium-low heat until it ripples and just starts to smoke, then remove from the heat right away (about 1-2 minutes).蒸茄子时另取小锅，中小火把菜籽油烧热至油面明显波动、刚冒细烟就立刻离火（约 1–2 分钟）。
  5. Place half the minced garlic in a heatproof bowl, pour the off-heat hot oil over it, and stir 10 seconds until fragrant without browning.取一半蒜末放进耐热小碗，离火后浇上热油拌匀 10 秒，激出香味且不炸焦。
  6. Stir in the remaining raw garlic, soy sauce, oyster sauce, black vinegar, sugar, and sesame oil until the sugar dissolves.拌入剩下一半生蒜末、生抽、蚝油、香醋、糖和香油，搅至白糖溶化。
  7. Lift out the steamed eggplant plate and carefully pour off any excess liquid that has collected in the plate.取出蒸好的茄子盘，小心地倒掉盘底积出的多余汤汁。
  8. Spoon the garlic sauce evenly over the hot eggplant, scatter the green onions on top, and serve warm.把蒜泥酱汁均匀淋在热茄子上，撒上葱花即可上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Splitting the garlic between hot-oil-sizzled and raw adds toasty aroma from one half while the other keeps a sharper, fresh garlic bite.蒜末分生熟两半，一半用热油激出焦香，另一半保留生蒜的辛香，吃起来层次更丰富。
  • Setting the strips skin-side down with gaps lets steam circulate evenly and helps keep the purple skin brighter.茄条皮朝下留缝铺放，能让蒸汽均匀穿透，同时减缓紫皮发黑。
  • Draining the pooled steaming water keeps the garlic sauce concentrated rather than watery.淋酱前倒掉盘底蒸出的水，酱汁才不会被稀释。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The eggplant turned dark and stayed firm in the center茄子发黑发柴，中间没软
    Wait for a full rolling boil before adding the plate, cut even strips, and steam 10-12 minutes on high without lifting the lid.蒸锅必须水滚后再放茄子，大火足汽蒸够 10–12 分钟；切条粗细尽量一致，加盖后中途别开盖。
  • The garlic tastes bitter and burnt蒜末发苦，吃起来有焦味
    The oil was too hot; pull the pan off the heat at the first faint wisps and stir immediately after pouring.油温太高把蒜炸糊了；看到油微微起烟就关火，浇油后立刻搅拌散热。
  • The dish is too salty or watery and bland成品太咸或水汪汪没味道
    Pooled water dilutes the sauce while heavy soy and oyster sauce turns salty; drain the plate first and taste the dressing before spooning it on.盘底蒸水没倒会让味道变淡，酱油蚝油过量又会偏咸；下次先沥干盘底水，酱汁尝一口再淋。
