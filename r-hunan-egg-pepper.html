Hunan Chili and Egg Stir-fry尖椒炒蛋

Ready in about ten minutes, Hunan chili and egg stir-fry is a weeknight staple that tastes far bigger than its short ingredient list. The trick is dry-blistering the chilies until wrinkled before cooking the eggs into fluffy curds, so the final toss stays aromatic and never turns watery.尖椒炒蛋十分钟就能端上桌，是湖南人家常备的快手下饭菜。做法看似简单，难点在于先把尖椒干煸出虎皮香、再把鸡蛋滑炒得蓬松嫩滑，合炒才不会出水碎成渣。

14 min total分钟 (8 + 6)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Long green chilli peppers, Shaoxing wine另购：青尖椒（螺丝椒）、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Eggs鸡蛋 200 g · 4 large eggs4 个大鸡蛋
  • Long green chilli peppers青尖椒（螺丝椒） 180 g · 4 long green chilies or jalapenos, thinly sliced diagonally4 根青尖椒或螺丝椒，斜切薄片 scrape out the seeds and ribs for less heat想减辣可以剖开刮掉籽和白筋
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 3 cloves, minced3 瓣，切末
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp light soy sauce (15 mL)生抽 1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 8 g · about 1/2 tbsp, beaten into the eggs约半汤匙，打入蛋液
  • Salt盐 3 g · about 1/2 tsp, divided约半茶匙，分两次用
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add the cooking wine and half of the salt, and beat briskly until foamy on top.鸡蛋打入碗中，加料酒和一半盐，用筷子充分抽打到表面起小泡。
  2. Slice the green chilies thinly on a diagonal and finely mince the garlic.青尖椒斜切成薄片，大蒜剁成细末。
  3. Heat a dry wok over medium heat, add the chilies and remaining salt, and dry-blister for 2 minutes until charred spots form and they soften, then transfer out.干锅烧热不放油，倒入尖椒和剩下的盐，中火干煸 2 分钟到起焦斑变软后盛出。
  4. Heat the oil over medium-high until shimmering, pour in the eggs, let the edges puff for 20 seconds, then fold gently into large soft curds and plate.锅里倒油烧到微微冒纹路，转中大火倒入蛋液，等边缘鼓泡凝固 20 秒后轻推成大块盛出。
  5. In the residual oil over low heat, fry the garlic for 15 seconds until fragrant, then return the chilies and eggs.利用锅底余油转小火，下蒜末炒香 15 秒，倒回尖椒和炒蛋。
  6. Raise to high heat, splash the soy sauce around the rim of the wok, toss for 20 seconds to coat evenly and serve right away.转大火沿锅边淋入生抽，快速翻炒 20 秒让蛋块均匀挂味后立刻出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Dry-blistering the chilies with salt removes raw grassiness and unlocks sweet roasted chili aroma.先干锅加盐把尖椒煸软起焦皮，既能去掉生椒青涩味，又能让椒香充分释放出来。
  • A little cooking wine in the beaten eggs masks egginess and creates steam that puffs the curds.蛋液里拌一点料酒能压住蛋腥味，下锅受热时酒气蒸发还能让蛋块更蓬松。
  • Pouring into shimmering oil and folding into large curds keeps the eggs tender rather than shredded.油温够热再倒蛋液、凝固后推成大块盛出，最后再回锅合炒，鸡蛋才滑嫩不碎。
  • Splashing soy sauce against the hot wok rim releases toasted aroma instantly without making the eggs soggy.生抽沿着热锅边淋下去，瞬间受热激发酱香，又不会把蛋块泡湿。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The eggs broke into dry little pieces鸡蛋炒得又碎又老，发干发硬
    Wait until the oil shimmers before pouring in the eggs, push gently into large curds once set, and limit the final toss to under 30 seconds.别在锅还不够热时倒蛋液，底部定型就轻推成块并及时盛出，最后合炒不超过 30 秒。
  • The chilies taste raw and sharp, or unseasoned inside尖椒发硬生辣，或者表面有味里面没味
    Slice the chilies thinner and always dry-blister them with salt first until wrinkled and soft so seasoning penetrates.尖椒切薄一些，务必先干锅加盐煸到表面起皱变软，让盐分提前渗入辣椒。
