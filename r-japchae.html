Japchae韩式杂菜

Japchae centers on chewy sweet potato noodles tossed with beef and colorful vegetables. The noodles can turn mushy if overcooked, and too much sauce can weigh down the dish, so aim for a light, glossy coating.韩式杂菜以有弹性的红薯粉丝为主角，牛肉和蔬菜各自炒香后再一起拌匀。常见失误是粉丝煮过头或酱汁太多，做出清爽有光泽的口感更重要。

45 min total分钟 (20 + 25)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Sweet potato glass noodles另购：红薯粉条

Ingredients食材

  • Sweet potato glass noodles红薯粉条 180 g · sweet potato noodles, dry weight红薯粉丝，干重
  • Striploin steak西冷牛排 180 g · thinly sliced, then cut into strips切薄片，再切成细条
  • Onions洋葱 100 g · 1/2 onion, thinly sliced半个，切薄丝
  • Carrots胡萝卜 80 g · 1 small carrot, cut into matchsticks1 根小胡萝卜，切细丝
  • Bell peppers彩椒 100 g · 1/2 pepper, cut into matchsticks半个，切细丝
  • Spinach菠菜 100 g · a small bunch一小把
  • Shiitake香菇 80 g · thinly sliced切薄片
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · about 3 cloves, minced约 3 瓣，切末
  • Soy sauce生抽 45 g · 3 tbsp (about 45 g): 15 g for the beef marinade, the remaining 30 g for the noodle dressing3 汤匙（约 45 克）：15 克腌牛肉，剩余 30 克拌粉丝
  • Sugar白糖 18 g · about 1 1/2 tbsp (18 g): 4 g for the beef marinade, the remaining 14 g for the noodle dressing约 1 汤匙半（18 克）：4 克腌牛肉，剩余 14 克拌粉丝
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 18 g · a little over 1 tbsp (18 g): 5 g for the beef marinade, 3 g for the spinach, the remaining 10 g for the noodle dressing约 1 汤匙多（18 克）：5 克腌牛肉、3 克拌菠菜，剩余 10 克拌粉丝
  • Canola oil菜籽油 21 g · 1 1/2 tbsp, divided1 汤匙半，分次使用
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 8 g · about 2 tsp约 2 茶匙
  • Salt盐 2 g · a pinch, divided少许，分次使用

Method做法

  1. Cut the beef into strips and mix with 15 g soy sauce, 4 g sugar, half the garlic, and 5 g sesame oil.牛肉切成細条，加入生抽 15 克、糖 4 克、蒜末一半和芝麻油 5 克拌匀。
  2. Slice the onion, carrot, pepper, and shiitake separately.洋葱、胡萝卜、彩椒和香菇分别切丝或切片。
  3. Bring water to a boil, blanch the spinach for 30 seconds, drain it, and gently squeeze out excess water.锅中烧开水，菠菜焯 30 秒，捞出沥干并轻轻挤去多余水分。
  4. Season the spinach with a little salt and sesame oil and set it aside.菠菜拌入少许盐和芝麻油，放在一旁。
  5. Cook the sweet potato noodles according to the package directions until pliable with no hard center, usually about 6 to 8 minutes.红薯粉丝按包装说明煮至柔韧、中心不硬，通常约 6 至 8 分钟。
  6. Drain the noodles and snip them into easy-to-eat lengths with kitchen scissors.粉丝沥干，用剪刀剪成方便入口的长度。
  7. Stir together the remaining soy sauce, sugar, and sesame oil for the noodle dressing.将剩余生抽、糖和芝麻油搅匀，作为粉丝调味汁。
  8. Heat a little oil in a skillet over medium heat and cook the beef until browned and at least 63°C, then set it aside.炒锅中火加少许油，牛肉炒至变色并达到 63°C，盛出备用。
  9. Add a little more oil and cook the onion for 2 minutes until softened and translucent.锅中再加少许油，洋葱炒 2 分钟至变软透亮。
  10. Add the carrot and pepper and stir-fry over medium heat for 2 minutes until bright in color.加入胡萝卜和彩椒，中火炒 2 分钟至颜色鲜亮。
  11. Add the shiitake and remaining garlic and cook for 3 minutes until the mushrooms soften.放入香菇和剩余蒜末，炒 3 分钟至香菇变软。
  12. Add the noodles and dressing and toss over medium-low heat for 2 minutes until the liquid is absorbed.把粉丝和调味汁倒入锅中，中小火翻拌 2 分钟至汁水被吸收。
  13. Return the beef and spinach to the pan and toss for 1 minute until evenly warmed.放回牛肉和菠菜，翻拌 1 分钟至食材均匀受热。
  14. Sprinkle with sesame seeds, adjust with a little salt if needed, and serve hot or warm.撒上芝麻，尝味后按需加少许盐，趁热或温热上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Drain the noodles while still chewy; they will absorb the dressing in the pan, and overcooked noodles break easily.粉丝煮到柔韧就捞出，后面还要在锅里吸收调味汁，煮软了会断碎。
  • Cook the vegetables in batches to preserve their color and avoid releasing too much liquid at once.蔬菜分批炒能保留颜色，也避免一次下锅后大量出水。
  • Adding the dressing in stages makes the salt and sweetness easier to control; the noodles need a coating, not a pool of sauce.调味汁分次加更容易掌握咸甜度，粉丝只需均匀裹上一层，不必浸在汁里。
  • Slice the beef thinly across the grain for tenderness and use a thermometer to confirm it reaches at least 63°C.牛肉逆纹切薄，炒熟后更容易咀嚼；用温度计确认至少 63°C。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The noodles clumped or turned mushy粉丝黏成一团或软烂
    Cook the noodles for less time and drain them well next time; for this batch, add a little sesame oil and gently separate them with chopsticks.下次缩短煮粉丝时间并充分沥干；这次可加少许芝麻油，用筷子轻轻分开再拌。
  • The japchae is watery and bland杂菜出水、味道寡淡
    Cook vegetables in batches over medium heat and drain the noodles thoroughly; if the pan is watery, toss over medium heat until the extra liquid evaporates.蔬菜分批用中火炒干，粉丝沥透后再加调味汁；已出水时开中火翻拌至多余水分蒸发。
  • The beef is dry and tough牛肉又干又硬
    Slice the beef thinly across the grain, sear it quickly to a safe temperature, and return it only at the end.牛肉切薄并逆纹，热锅快速炒到安全温度后及时盛出，最后再回锅拌匀。
