Store and portion储存与分装

Refrigerate at 0-4°C for 3-5 days; freeze for 4-6 months. Ground meat keeps only 1-2 days chilled.冷藏 0–4°C 3–5 天；冷冻可放 4–6 个月。碎肉（肉馅）只放 1–2 天。

Split into meal-sized portions (300-500 g); freeze ground meat pressed flat so you can snap off what you need.按一餐的量分装（300–500 克），肉馅压成薄片冷冻，用时掰一块就够。
