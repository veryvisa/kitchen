Sichuan Dry-Fried Green Beans干煸四季豆

Dry-frying is an old Sichuan method: the beans lose moisture slowly over moderate heat until the skin wrinkles and browns and the flavor concentrates. Then they meet crisped pork, chilies and peppercorns. Patience matters, because high heat burns the outside while the inside stays raw.「干煸」是川菜的老手法：用中小火让食材慢慢失水，表皮起皱发焦，味道集中。豆子煸到表皮皱起、里面软熟，再和肉末、辣椒、花椒一起炒香。关键是耐心，火大了外面糊里面生。

26 min total分钟 (12 + 14)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dried chili, Sichuan peppercorn另购：干辣椒、花椒

Ingredients食材

  • Green beans四季豆 350 g · about 350 g green beans, trimmed and snapped into 5 cm pieces四季豆约 350 克，掐去头尾，掰成 5 厘米段
  • Ground pork猪肉馅 80 g · about 80 g ground pork猪肉末约 80 克
  • Dried chili干辣椒 5 g · 5 pieces, snipped5 个，剪段
  • Sichuan peppercorn花椒 1.5 g · about 1 tsp whole Sichuan peppercorns约 1 茶匙花椒粒
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 3 cloves, minced3 瓣，切末
  • Ginger姜 6 g · minced, about 2 tsp切末，约 2 茶匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp半汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 2 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙
  • Salt盐 1.5 g · about 1/4 tsp约 1/4 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）

Method做法

  1. Rinse the beans and dry them completely with paper towels, since wet beans spatter in hot oil.四季豆洗净，用厨房纸彻底擦干，表面有水会溅油。
  2. Heat a wok over medium-high heat and add 2 tbsp oil.中大火烧热锅，倒入 2 汤匙油。
  3. Add the beans and toss every half minute for 6 to 8 minutes.放入四季豆，每隔几十秒翻一下，煸约 6 到 8 分钟。
  4. Remove them when the skins wrinkle and show brown blisters and a bean tastes cooked with no raw edge.豆子表皮起皱、出现焦斑、尝一根断生不带豆腥味，盛出。
  5. Add the last tablespoon of oil over medium heat and fry the pork for about 2 minutes until dry and lightly crisped.锅里放 1 汤匙油，中火下猪肉末，炒到水分干、微微焦香，约 2 分钟。
  6. Add the chilies, peppercorns, ginger and garlic and stir about 20 seconds until fragrant and the chilies darken.放入干辣椒、花椒粒、姜和蒜，炒约 20 秒，香味出来、辣椒变深。
  7. Return the beans, turn the heat to high and toss.倒入煸好的四季豆，转大火翻炒。
  8. Add the soy sauce, sugar and salt, toss about 30 seconds until evenly coated and serve.加生抽、糖和盐，翻炒约 30 秒，让调料均匀裹上，出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Green beans must be cooked through; raw or half-cooked ones can upset your stomach, so taste one and make sure the raw flavor is gone.四季豆必须煸熟，生的或半熟含有会让人不适的天然成分，咬一根确认没有豆腥味才算好。
  • Dry beans blister in oil instead of steaming, which gives the concentrated, dry-fried texture.擦干再下锅：水分少，豆子起皱靠的是油煎而不是水煮，口感才干香。
  • Fry the pork until crisp at the edges, not just until it changes color, so it contrasts with the soft beans.肉末要炒到焦香酥脆，才会跟豆子形成对比；不要只炒到变色。
  • Dry-frying uses much less oil than deep-frying and tastes just as good at home.家里做不用炸，煸比油炸更省油，口感同样好。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • Charred outside, raw inside外面焦黑、里面还生
    The heat was too high. Reduce to medium and give it 2 to 3 more minutes; pick out the burnt ones.火太大。降到中火，多煸 2 到 3 分钟；已经焦的豆子别硬救，挑出来。
  • The beans are soft and yellow豆子软烂、颜色发黄
    They cooked too long over low heat. Stop when the skin wrinkles, before the whole bean turns yellow, and keep the final high-heat toss short.煸太久或火太小。看到表皮起皱就够，别等整条都变黄；最后大火炒调料时间要短。
  • Too salty or too hot味道太咸或太辣
    The pork and soy sauce already add salt, so the added salt is optional; shake out chili seeds to calm the heat.肉末和酱油都有咸味，盐是最后才放的，可以不放；辣椒籽先抖掉一些，辣度就降下来。
