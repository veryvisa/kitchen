Greek Chicken Souvlaki with Tzatziki希腊柠檬烤鸡串配酸奶黄瓜酱

The most ordered dish at Vancouver's Greek restaurants. Chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, lemon, garlic and oregano are skewered and grilled until charred at the edges, then served with homemade tzatziki and pita. No grill? Use the oven broiler or a grill pan.温哥华希腊餐馆最受欢迎的一道。鸡腿肉用橄榄油、柠檬、蒜和牛至腌过，串起来烤到边缘焦香，配自己做的酸奶黄瓜酱和口袋饼。没有烤架就用烤箱上火或平底锅。

65 min total分钟 (20 + 15)4 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 800 g · 800 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into 3 cm pieces去骨鸡腿肉 800 克，切 3 厘米块
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 42 g · 3 tbsp3 汤匙
  • Lemons柠檬 100 g · 1 lemon, zested and juiced1 个，擦皮屑、挤汁
  • Garlic大蒜 20 g · 6 cloves: 4 for the marinade, 2 for the sauce6 瓣：4 瓣腌肉，2 瓣做酱
  • Dried oregano干牛至/香草 3 g · 1.5 tsp dried oregano干牛至 1.5 茶匙
  • Plain yogurt原味酸奶 200 g · 3/4 cup plain yogurt (Greek yogurt is best)原味酸奶 3/4 杯（希腊酸奶最好）
  • English cucumber黄瓜 150 g · half a cucumber, grated and squeezed dry半根黄瓜，擦丝挤干
  • Salt盐 6 g · about 1 tsp (split between marinade and sauce)约 1 茶匙（腌肉和酱分开放）
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · a few grinds少许
  • Sandwich bread面包 240 g · 4 pitas (about 240 g)口袋饼 4 张（约 240 克）
  • Tomatoes西红柿 200 g · 1 large tomato, cut in wedges1 个大番茄，切块
  • Onions洋葱 75 g · half a small red onion, sliced半个小红洋葱，切丝

Method做法

  1. Mix 2 tbsp oil, half the lemon juice, the zest, 4 minced garlic cloves, the oregano, half the salt and the pepper.2 汤匙橄榄油、一半柠檬汁、柠檬皮屑、4 瓣蒜末、牛至、一半盐和胡椒调成腌料。
  2. Toss the chicken in the marinade and refrigerate 30 minutes to 4 hours.鸡块倒进腌料拌匀，冷藏腌 30 分钟到 4 小时。
  3. Stir the yogurt with the cucumber, 2 minced garlic cloves, the rest of the lemon juice, 1 tbsp oil and the rest of the salt, and chill.酸奶加黄瓜丝、2 瓣蒜末、剩下的柠檬汁、1 汤匙橄榄油和剩下的盐，拌成黄瓜酱冷藏。
  4. Thread the chicken onto skewers (soak wooden ones for 20 minutes first).鸡块穿到竹签上（竹签先泡水 20 分钟）。
  5. Grill or broil on high for 10-12 minutes, turning twice, until charred at the edges and 74°C inside.烤架或烤箱上火开到最高，烤 10–12 分钟，中途翻两次，到边缘焦香、中心 74°C。
  6. Warm the pitas on the grill for 30 seconds and serve with the skewers, tzatziki, tomato and onion.口袋饼放烤架上烤 30 秒，配鸡串、黄瓜酱、番茄和洋葱上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Thighs can take high heat and char without drying; breast needs 2-3 minutes less.用鸡腿肉，高温烤到焦也不会干；鸡胸肉要少烤 2–3 分钟。
  • Squeeze the cucumber hard or the sauce turns runny as it sits.黄瓜丝一定挤干水，否则酱会越放越稀。
  • Lemon tenderises the chicken, but past 4 hours the texture turns mealy, so do not leave it overnight.腌料里的柠檬汁会让肉变软，但超过 4 小时肉质会发粉，别隔夜。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The chicken sticks to the grill鸡肉粘烤架
    The grill was not hot or oiled. Preheat 10 minutes and oil the grates; once seared, it releases on its own.烤架没烧热或没刷油。先预热 10 分钟，刷油后再放，表面焦了自然会松开。
  • The tzatziki is thin黄瓜酱太稀
    Use Greek yogurt, or drain regular yogurt in a paper-lined sieve for 30 minutes.换希腊酸奶，或把普通酸奶放在铺了厨房纸的筛子里沥 30 分钟。
