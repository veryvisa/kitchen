Lamb Soup with Bread羊肉泡馍（家常版）

Lamb soup with bread is a warming northwest classic: flatbread broken into small pieces soaks up boiling lamb broth with sliced lamb, radish and vermicelli. This home version skips the aged master stock; the keys are blanching away the lamb smell, simmering the meat tender over low heat, drying the bread on the griddle, and soaking it only at serving time.羊肉泡馍是西北的暖身汤食：白吉馍掰成碎块吸满滚烫羊汤，配羊肉片、萝卜和粉丝。家常版省去老汤吊制，重点是羊肉焯水去膻、小火炖软，饼烙到偏干，临上桌前再泡汤。

Method做法

  1. Cut the lamb into 5-6 cm chunks and soak in cold water for 30 minutes (changing the water once) to draw out blood, then start it in cold water with 2 slices of ginger and the wine, bring to a boil, skim off the scum, cook 5 minutes, then rinse with warm water.羊肉切成 5–6 厘米的大块，冷水浸泡 30 分钟（中途换一次水）去血水，再冷水下锅，加 2 片姜和料酒，大火烧开撇净浮沫，煮 5 分钟捞出，用温水冲净。
  2. Place the lamb in a pot with the green onion, remaining ginger, garlic, star anise and bay leaves; pour in about 1.5 L boiling water (to 3 cm above the meat).炖锅放入羊肉、葱段、剩余姜片、蒜瓣、八角和香叶，加约 1.5 升开水（没过肉约 3 厘米）。
  3. Bring to a boil, turn to low with the lid slightly ajar, keep it at a gentle simmer for 60-70 minutes until a chopstick slides through; add 9 g salt in the last 10 minutes, skimming off foam now and then for a clearer broth.大火烧开转小火，盖子留一点缝，微沸炖 60 到 70 分钟，至筷子能扎透；出锅前 10 分钟加盐 9 克，期间不时撇去浮沫让汤更清。
  4. Lift out the lamb, slice it once slightly cooled; strain the broth and skim the surface fat.捞出羊肉稍凉后切片；汤滤去料渣，撇掉表层浮油。
  5. Mix the flour and salt, add warm water and knead a dough; rest 20 minutes, divide into 4 pieces and roll each into a thin round.面粉加盐，加温水揉成面团，醒 20 分钟，分成 4 个剂，擀成薄圆饼。
  6. Griddle the rounds dry 3-4 minutes per side until spotted; cool them, then break by hand into nail-sized crumbs.平底锅干烙，每面 3 到 4 分钟至两面有焦斑，取出放凉，用手掰成指甲盖大小的碎块。
  7. Add the daikon to the broth for the last 15 minutes of simmering; soak the vermicelli in warm water for 20 minutes, then boil it only 1-2 minutes and drain so it does not go mushy.白萝卜片在汤快炖好前 15 分钟下锅同煮；粉丝温水泡软 20 分钟，下锅煮 1–2 分钟捞出，避免泡得太烂。
  8. Bring the broth back to a rolling boil before serving; put the bread crumbs, vermicelli, daikon and lamb slices in bowls, ladle on the boiling broth, top with cilantro and white pepper (black pepper is fine if that is what you have), and finish with sesame oil.上桌前把汤重新烧开；碗中放馍块、粉丝、萝卜片和羊肉片，舀入滚烫肉汤，撒香菜和白胡椒（没有可用黑胡椒），滴香油。
  9. Offer chili oil and sweet pickled garlic on the side for those who want them.辣椒油和糖醋蒜可以配在一边，喜欢的人自己加。

Keys to success成功要点

  • This pot simmers for over an hour at a steady boil, so the lamb is already well past any safe temperature; judge doneness with the chopstick test rather than a thermometer. Soaking out the blood first and blanching thoroughly from cold water is what keeps the broth clear.这锅肉要炖 60 分钟以上、全程微沸，中心温度早已远超安全线；判断熟没熟看筷子能不能扎透，不用再测温。先浸泡去血水、冷水焯透，汤才清亮。
  • Break the bread by hand rather than cutting it; rough surfaces soak up more broth. A drier, firmer griddle bread does not turn mushy in the soup.馍要手掰成小块而不是刀切，断面粗糙更能吸汤；饼烙到偏干发硬，泡汤后不会立刻烂糊。
  • Soak the bread only at serving time; left too long it turns to porridge, and the broth should be boiling hot when it reaches the table.馍块临吃前再泡汤，泡久了会软成糊；上桌时汤一定要滚烫。
  • Adding the radish near the end of simmering gives a little sweetness; do not overcook the vermicelli so it stays slippery, and skim the fat for a lighter broth.萝卜炖汤快好前再下，能增加一点清甜，粉丝别久煮，保持爽滑；浮油撇净后汤头更清爽。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The broth smells strongly of lamb汤膻味重
    Usually under-blanching or too little spice. Soak the lamb in cold water 30 minutes first, blanch thoroughly, use enough ginger, onion and star anise, and skim both scum and surface fat.焯水不彻底或香料不足；下次羊肉先冷水浸泡 30 分钟再焯水，加足姜葱八角，并撇净浮沫和浮油。
  • The bread turns to mush in the soup馍块泡后烂成糊
    The bread was too soft; roll it thin and griddle it dry next time, keep the soaking to a few minutes, and break it small before pouring the broth.饼烙得太暄软；下次擀薄、烙到偏干，泡汤时间控制在几分钟内，先掰小块再盛汤。
  • The sliced lamb is dry and tough羊肉片发柴
    High heat or too little simmering. Keep a gentle boil for 60 minutes or more, and keep the sliced lamb warm in the broth rather than leaving it dry.火太大或炖的时间太短；保持小火微沸，炖够 60 分钟以上，切片后放回汤里保温，不要干放。
