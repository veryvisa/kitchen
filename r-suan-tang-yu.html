Guizhou Sour Soup Fish酸汤鱼

Guizhou sour soup fish pairs a vivid, tangy tomato-chili broth with tender fillets, tofu and bean sprouts. Starting with jarred Guizhou red sour soup base and fresh tomatoes builds deep flavor in twenty minutes. The tricky part is handling the fish gently: slicing it too thin or boiling it hard breaks the pieces apart and dries them out.酸汤鱼讲究汤色红亮、酸香开胃、鱼片滑嫩不断。家里用市售贵州红酸汤底料搭配新鲜番茄炒出浓郁汤底，二十分钟就能连汤带菜端上桌。做这道菜的难点在煮鱼的火候：鱼片切得太薄或大火猛滚容易碎掉，煮太久又会发干。

Method做法

  1. Slice the fish on an angle into 7 mm pieces, pat dry, and toss with the cooking wine, half the shredded ginger and 1 g salt for 1 minute.鱼柳斜刀切成约 7 毫米厚的片，擦干水分，加料酒、一半姜丝和 1 克盐抓拌 1 分钟。
  2. Toss the fish with the cornstarch, then drizzle on 1 tsp oil to seal the surface; cover and refrigerate for 10 minutes (take it out when you start cooking the base).鱼片再拌入玉米淀粉抓匀，最后淋 1 茶匙食用油封住表面，盖好放冰箱冷藏腌 10 分钟（炒底料时再取出）。
  3. Heat 1.5 tbsp oil in a deep pot over medium heat and stir-fry the minced garlic, ginger and scallion whites for 30 seconds until fragrant.深锅放 1.5 汤匙油中火烧热，下蒜末、姜末、剩余姜丝和葱白炒香约 30 秒。
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes and salted chili and cook over medium heat for 3 minutes, mashing into a saucy puree.放入番茄碎和剁椒中火翻炒 3 分钟，边炒边压碎至出红汁。
  5. Stir in the Guizhou red sour soup base over medium-low heat for 1 minute until aromatic.倒入贵州红酸汤底料，转中小火炒 1 分钟激出酸香。
  6. Pour in 700 mL boiling water and the sugar; taste for saltiness first, since jarred sour soup base is usually already salted, then bring to a boil, add the tofu slabs and simmer 5 minutes.倒入 700 毫升开水和糖，先尝一下咸淡（市售酸汤底料通常已经带盐），大火煮沸后放老豆腐片，中小火煮 5 分钟。
  7. Add the bean sprouts and simmer over medium heat for 1 minute until tender-crisp.下入豆芽中火煮 1 分钟至断生。
  8. Lift the tofu and bean sprouts out with a slotted spoon into a large serving bowl that has been pre-warmed with hot water.用漏勺把豆腐和豆芽捞出，铺入提前用热水烫过的大汤碗底。
  9. Taste the broth, add up to 2 g salt if needed, and reduce the heat to a gentle simmer.尝一下汤味并按需要补入剩余 2 克盐，把火调成微沸小滚。
  10. Slide the fish slices in one by one, leave undisturbed for 20 seconds to set the coating, then nudge gently.将鱼片逐片滑入微沸酸汤中，静置 20 秒定型后再轻推散开。
  11. Simmer gently for 2 to 3 minutes until the fish turns opaque and reaches 70°C inside; lift the fish out gently with a slotted spoon onto the tofu first, then ladle the broth over.保持微沸煮 2 至 3 分钟，至鱼片变白不透明、内部达到 70°C；先用漏勺把鱼片轻轻捞出铺在豆腐上，再把汤浇入碗中。
  12. Scatter with chopped green onion and serve hot.撒上葱花即可上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cooking fresh tomatoes into a puree before adding the sour soup base builds body and softens sharp acidity.新鲜番茄炒成浓汁后再下酸汤底料，能让汤底更醇厚，酸味也更圆润。
  • Slicing the fish thicker, coating with starch and oil, and waiting 20 seconds before nudging keep the fillets intact.鱼片切厚一点并用淀粉和油封住表面，下锅头 20 秒不动，淀粉定型后鱼片才完整。
  • Keep the broth at a bare simmer once the fish goes in, as a rolling boil breaks the pieces and toughens the edges.下鱼片后只保持微沸，翻滚的大火不仅会把鱼肉打碎，还会让边缘变老。
  • Placing the tofu and sprouts at the bottom of the bowl cushions the fish, and they keep soaking up the tangy broth once everything is assembled.豆腐和豆芽先捞出垫碗底，既托住鱼片不压碎，装碗后又能继续泡在酸汤里吸味。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The fish broke into small flakes in the broth鱼片煮碎成小块沉在汤里
    If it has already broken up, turn off the heat right away and stop stirring; ladle it out with the broth and scatter on scallions to eat over rice. Next time cut thicker 6-8 mm slices and keep the simmer gentle.已经碎了就马上关火、别再搅动，连汤盛出撒葱花，当酸汤鱼碎拌饭吃；下次切到 6–8 毫米厚并在微沸状态下轻推。
  • The soup tastes sharply sour or harsh汤味太酸、入口发呛
    Simmer with 1/2 to 1 tsp extra sugar for 2 minutes to round out the acidity, or thin with 100 mL hot water.加半茶匙到 1 茶匙糖再煮 2 分钟柔化酸度，或兑入 100 毫升热水稀释。
  • The fish tastes bland or slightly dry鱼肉内部偏淡或发干
    Season the fish with a pinch of salt before velveting, and pull from the heat right when it turns opaque and hits 70°C.腌鱼时先抓入少许盐入底味，下锅煮到鱼片刚刚变白达到 70°C 就立刻起锅。
