Korean Seasoned Spinach (Sigeumchi Namul)韩式凉拌菠菜

A side dish on Korean tables almost every day. Blanch spinach for 30 seconds, squeeze it dry and toss with garlic, sesame oil, sesame seeds and a little soy; good warm or cold. Dress half a big Costco bag at a time and it keeps three days in the fridge.韩国饭桌上几乎天天有的小菜。菠菜焯水 30 秒，挤干，用蒜、香油、芝麻和一点酱油拌开，冷热都好吃。Costco 一大袋菠菜，一次拌一半，冷藏可吃三天。

8 min total分钟 (5 + 3)3 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Spinach菠菜 450 g · 450 g spinach (half a large bag)菠菜 450 克（大袋的一半）
  • Garlic大蒜 5 g · 1-2 cloves, grated1–2 瓣，磨蓉
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 10 g · 1 stalk, finely sliced1 根，切葱花
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp1/2 汤匙
  • Salt盐 1 g · a small pinch一小撮
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 10 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 4 g · 1 tsp, crushed between your fingers1 茶匙，用手指捻碎

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Blanch the spinach in a large pot of boiling water for 30 seconds, just until wilted.大锅水烧开，下菠菜烫 30 秒到刚变软。
  2. Drain and plunge straight into cold water.捞出马上过冷水。
  3. Gather into a ball and squeeze out the water firmly.抓成团，用力挤干水分。
  4. Cut the ball in half and shake the leaves loose.切成两段，抖散。
  5. Add the garlic, green onion, soy, salt, sesame oil and sesame seeds and mix with your hands.加蒜蓉、葱花、生抽、盐、香油和芝麻，用手抓匀。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Thirty seconds and a cold rinse keep it bright green and not mushy.焯水只要 30 秒，再过冷水，颜色翠绿、口感不烂。
  • Squeeze it hard, or the water dilutes the seasoning and pools in the fridge.一定要挤干，不然调料被水冲淡，放冰箱也会出水。
  • Crushing the sesame releases much more aroma than whole seeds.芝麻捻碎再撒，香味比整粒出来得多。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The spinach is mushy and dark菠菜烂糊发黑
    It was blanched too long. Use rapidly boiling water, lift it out as soon as it wilts and cool it at once.焯太久了。水要大滚，菠菜一软就捞，马上过冷水。
  • It tastes bland味道淡
    It was not squeezed enough. Squeeze again and add a pinch of salt and a few drops of sesame oil.水没挤干。再挤一次，补一小撮盐和几滴香油。
