Cantonese Steamed Sea Bass清蒸鱼

This Cantonese-style fish lets its delicate flavor lead without a heavy sauce, while ginger, scallion and hot oil bloom the aromatics and finish the fish. Timing is the key: take it out as soon as the flesh separates easily from the bone, or it can turn dry.清蒸鱼靠鱼本身的鲜味，不用厚重调味，葱姜和热油用于激发香气、润泽鱼身。火候是关键：蒸到鱼肉刚刚离骨就取出，过火会发干。

27 min total分钟 (15 + 12)3 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Sea bass, Shaoxing wine另购：鲈鱼、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Sea bass鲈鱼 800 g · 1 whole branzino (European sea bass), about 800 g, scaled and gutted整条鲈鱼（branzino / 海鲈鱼）约 800 克，去鳞去内脏
  • Ginger姜 25 g · about 8 slices, with some finely shredded约 8 片，部分切极细丝
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 35 g · about 3 stalks, cut into lengths and finely shredded约 3 根，切段和极细丝
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL) Shaoxing wine绍兴料酒 1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1 1/2 tbsp (about 22 mL) soy sauce生抽 1.5 汤匙（约 22 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 2 g · 1/2 tsp, mixed with the soy sauce and 2 tbsp hot water1/2 茶匙，与生抽和 2 汤匙热水调匀
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp约 1/3 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Pat the fish dry inside and out, and make 3 diagonal slashes down to the bone on each side.用厨房纸把鱼内外擦干，在两面各斜划 3 刀，深度约至鱼骨。
  2. Rub the salt over and inside the fish, then place the ginger slices in its cavity.把盐抹在鱼身内外，并将姜片塞入鱼腹。
  3. Lay 2–3 scallion white lengths (or set a small heatproof rack) on a heatproof plate to raise the fish slightly so steam circulates underneath. Set the fish on top, drizzle with Shaoxing wine and lay a few ginger slices over it.在耐热盘底铺 2–3 段葱白（或放上耐热蒸架）把鱼略微垫高，让蒸汽从鱼底通过，再放上鱼身，淋上绍兴料酒并铺几片姜。
  4. Bring the steamer water to a full boil, add the fish plate and cover the pot.蒸锅里的水烧到大滚，放入鱼盘并盖好锅盖。
  5. Steam over high heat for 9–12 minutes, until a chopstick or thin knife pierces the thickest part with no resistance, the flesh turns opaque milky white and flakes easily from the bone, and the thickest part reaches 70°C on a thermometer.大火蒸 9–12 分钟，直到用筷子或细刀插入最厚处无阻力、鱼肉呈不透明乳白色并容易成片剥离离骨；如使用温度计，最厚处应达到 70°C。
  6. Remove the plate, pour off the steaming liquid, and discard the ginger slices and bottom scallion pieces.取出鱼盘，倒掉盘中蒸出的汁水，挑去姜片和垫底的葱段。
  7. Stir the soy sauce, sugar and 2 tbsp hot water together until dissolved. Top the fish with finely shredded scallion and ginger (julienne), then pour the soy sauce mixture around the rim of the plate.将生抽、白糖和 2 汤匙热水拌匀成较柔和的豉油；将极细的葱丝和姜丝铺在鱼身上，再将调好的豉油沿盘边淋入。
  8. Heat the oil in a small pan until shimmering and just beginning to smoke; do not let it burn, then pour it evenly over the scallion and ginger.小锅把油烧至微微冒烟、立即离火，不要烧到明显冒烟或发黑，均匀淋在葱姜上。
  9. Serve while hot.趁热上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Drying the fish and elevating it on scallion pieces or a rack keeps the underside from sitting in fishy steaming liquid.鱼身擦干并用葱段或蒸架垫高再蒸，盘底不容易浸在带腥味的汤水里。
  • Start with vigorously boiling water so strong steam cooks the fish evenly.蒸鱼要等水大滚再入锅，蒸汽足，鱼肉熟得更均匀。
  • Thickness matters more than weight; check the thickest part near 9 minutes to avoid overcooking.鱼的厚度比重量更影响时间；接近 9 分钟时检查最厚处，避免蒸过头。
  • Pouring hot oil over the scallion and ginger brings out their aroma; lukewarm oil leaves them tasting raw.热油淋在表面葱姜上才有香气，油温不足会让葱姜显得生硬。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The fish is dry, or the flesh near the bone is still bloody鱼肉发柴、鱼骨边还有血色
    If the fish is dry, shorten the steaming time to suit its thickness next time; if flesh by the bone is still underdone (resists a chopstick or is below 70°C), return it to the steaming pot for 1–2 minutes until the thickest part reaches 70°C and the opaque flesh flakes easily.如果鱼肉已发干，下次按鱼身厚度缩短蒸制时间；若骨边仍不熟（筷子插入有阻力或未达 70°C），把鱼放回滚水蒸锅再蒸 1–2 分钟，直到最厚处达 70°C 且鱼肉呈不透明乳白色、能轻松成片剥离。
  • The fish tastes noticeably fishy鱼有明显腥味
    Make sure the gills and cavity are cleaned, and dry and elevate the fish before seasoning with ginger and Shaoxing wine; fresh julienned scallion, ginger and hot oil can brighten a cooked fish.确认鱼鳃和腹腔清理干净，蒸前擦干、垫高并用姜和绍兴料酒；已经蒸好的鱼可多加极细的新鲜葱姜丝和热油提香。
  • The plate is watery and the flavor is weak盘里汤水太多、味道寡淡
    Pour off the steaming liquid before adding the soy sauce mixture around the rim so it does not dilute the flavor, and dry and elevate the fish next time.蒸好后倒掉盘汁再沿盘边淋入调好的豉油，避免用鱼汁稀释味道；下次也要把鱼身擦干并垫高。
