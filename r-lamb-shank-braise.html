Braised Lamb Shanks红焖羊腿

Lamb shanks are packed with connective tissue that melts into fork-tender meat during a slow braise. Searing the shanks first, turning them as they simmer in a dark soy-and-spice broth, and reducing the glaze at the end creates a glossy sauce that pairs well with rice.羊小腿筋膜丰厚，最适合用酱油和香料小火慢焖到软烂脱骨。先把羊腿煎出焦香，再加酱油和香料小火慢焖，中途翻两次面，最后开盖收出浓亮的汤汁，配米饭或馒头、面条都很合适。

140 min total分钟 (15 + 125)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dried chili, Star anise, Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine另购：干辣椒、八角、老抽、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Lamb shank羊小腿 1200 g · 4 lamb shanks (about 1200 g bone-in)4 根羊小腿（带骨约 1200 克）
  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 medium onion, cut into chunks1 个中等洋葱，切大块
  • Carrots胡萝卜 200 g · 2 carrots, cut into large chunks2 根胡萝卜，切滚刀块
  • Garlic大蒜 25 g · 6 cloves, lightly smashed6 瓣，拍扁
  • Ginger姜 20 g · 5 slices5 片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks, cut into 5 cm (2-inch) pieces3 根，切 5 厘米长段
  • Dried chili干辣椒 3 g · 2-3 dried chilies2–3 个干辣椒 adds gentle warmth; omit if preferred提香微辣，不吃辣可省
  • Star anise八角 2 g · 2 whole star anise2 颗八角
  • Bay leaves月桂叶 1 g · 2 bay leaves2 片香叶
  • Soy sauce生抽 45 g · 2 1/2 tbsp (38 mL)2 汤匙半（38 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 45 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Salt盐 3 g · 1/2 tsp, added to taste near the end of the reduction半茶匙，收汁时按咸淡加
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Put the lamb shanks in a large pot with enough cold water to cover, add 2 ginger slices and 1 tbsp cooking wine, and bring to a boil over high heat.羊小腿放进大锅，加冷水没过肉面，放入 2 片姜和 1 汤匙料酒，大火烧开。
  2. Boil for 4 minutes while skimming off scum, then lift out the shanks, rinse with warm water, and pat completely dry with paper towels.滚煮 4 分钟并撇净浮沫，捞出用温水冲净，再用厨房纸彻底擦干表面。
  3. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat, sear the shanks for 2-3 minutes per side until well browned, and set aside.炖锅烧热倒油，中大火把羊小腿煎到四面呈金黄微焦，每面约 2–3 分钟后盛出。
  4. Turn to medium-low and fry the remaining ginger, garlic, green onion, onion, dried chilies, star anise, and bay leaves for about 2 minutes until fragrant and softened.转中小火，下剩下的姜片、蒜瓣、葱段、洋葱、干辣椒、八角和香叶，炒约 2 分钟到洋葱变软出香。
  5. Return the shanks to the pot, pour in the remaining 2 tbsp cooking wine, add the soy sauces and sugar, and turn to coat for 1 minute.放回羊小腿，淋入剩下的 2 汤匙料酒，加生抽、老抽和白糖，翻动裹匀约 1 分钟。
  6. Pour in boiling water to reach two-thirds up the shanks, bring to a boil, cover tightly, and simmer on low for 75-90 minutes, turning the shanks twice. If the liquid drops below a third of the shank height, top up with more boiling water.倒入开水至没过羊小腿约三分之二高度，烧开后转小火盖严锅盖焖 75–90 分钟，中途翻面两次；若汤汁低于三分之一高度，及时补开水。
  7. Test with a chopstick to see if the meat is mostly tender; if it still resists, cover and braise 20-30 more minutes before continuing.用筷子试一下肉是否基本软烂，如果还紧，加盖再焖 20–30 分钟后再继续。
  8. Once tender, add the carrot chunks, cover, and braise 20 more minutes until a chopstick slides easily into the meat and tendons.确认软烂后放入胡萝卜块，加盖再焖 20 分钟，直到筷子能轻松扎透肉和筋膜。
  9. Fish out the star anise, bay leaves, dried chilies, ginger, and green onion lengths (leave the onion and garlic). Lift out the shanks and carrots, skim the fat off the surface, then boil the sauce down uncovered over high heat for 5-8 minutes. Return the shanks, spoon the sauce over them as it reduces, taste and add salt if needed, and plate once thick and glossy.夹出八角、香叶、干辣椒、姜片和葱段（洋葱和蒜瓣留着），把羊腿和胡萝卜先盛出，撇去汤面浮油，开盖转大火收汁 5–8 分钟，再放回羊腿，一边用勺把酱汁不断淋在羊腿上，尝味补盐，直到汤汁浓稠发亮后装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Patting the blanched shanks dry before searing prevents oil splatter and helps the surface brown deeply.焯水后把羊小腿表面彻底擦干再下锅煎，不仅不会四处溅油，还能快速煎出焦糖色和肉香。
  • If you need to top up the braise, use boiling water only, never cold; cold water shocks the meat and interrupts the simmer. Starting with the liquid only two-thirds up the shanks also means the final reduction does not take too long.焖煮中途如果要补水，只能用开水，不能用冷水，冷水会让肉温骤降、煮制中断；水量起步只到羊腿三分之二高度，最后收汁才不会花太久。
  • Turning the shanks twice during the braise lets the upper halves spend time submerged so the whole shank softens evenly.小火焖炖中途翻面两次，让露出汤面的那一半也轮流泡进酱汁里，整根羊腿才能同步软烂入味。
  • Basting the shanks as the sauce reduces builds a glossy coat from the natural gelatin so the meat stays moist.收汁时反复把锅底胶质浓汁淋到肉面上，成品才会亮泽挂汁、吃起来不干。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The meat is still clinging tightly to the bone肉还紧紧粘在骨头上咬不动
    Shanks vary in size and tendon thickness; add a splash of boiling water, cover, and simmer 20-30 more minutes until chopstick-tender before reducing.羊小腿大小不同、筋膜较厚；补一点开水继续盖盖小火焖 20–30 分钟，确认筷子一扎就透再收汁。
  • The sauce stayed thin and watery汤汁太多太稀、挂不住肉
    It needs a longer uncovered reduction; remove the lid and boil over high heat for several minutes until large bubbles form and the glaze thickens.最后没开盖收汁到位；拿掉锅盖转大火煮几分钟蒸发水分，酱汁起大泡变稠即可。
  • The reduced glaze turned out too salty收汁后酱味太咸
    Over-reducing concentrates sodium; stir in 1/2 cup hot water to loosen the sauce, and next time taste and salt only near the end of the reduction, or trim the soy sauce.收得太干让盐分浓缩了；加半杯热水把酱汁搅开稀释，下次收汁快结束时才尝味放盐，或少加半汤匙生抽。
