Chicken Caesar Salad with Homemade Dressing and Croutons凯撒沙拉配烤鸡（自制酱汁和面包丁）

What makes a Caesar is the homemade dressing and truly crisp croutons. Garlic, mustard, lemon, Parmesan and olive oil are whisked into a thick emulsion and served with chicken cooked just through. It takes ten minutes more than a bottled dressing and tastes nothing like one.凯撒沙拉的好吃全在自制酱汁和烤得脆的面包丁：蒜、芥末、柠檬、帕玛森和橄榄油打成浓稠的乳状酱，配煎到刚熟的鸡胸。比买现成的瓶装酱汁多花 10 分钟，味道差得很远。

45 min total分钟 (25 + 20)2 servings份Medium中等Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless skinless chicken breast鸡胸肉 450 g · 2 chicken breasts (about 225 g each), butterflied or pounded to 2 cm thick2 块鸡胸肉（约 225 克一块），片成 2 厘米厚
  • Romaine lettuce罗马生菜 300 g · 2 romaine hearts, torn in large pieces2 棵罗马生菜心，撕成大片
  • Sandwich bread面包 60 g · about 3 slices of bread, cut in 2 cm cubes (day-old is best)约 3 片面包，切 2 厘米丁（放过夜的更好）
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 60 g · 4 tbsp in total: 1 for the croutons, 1/2 for the chicken, 2 1/2 for the dressing共 4 汤匙：面包丁 1 汤匙、鸡胸 1/2 汤匙、酱汁 2 又 1/2 汤匙
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 3 cloves: 1 for the croutons, 2 for the dressing3 瓣：1 瓣给面包丁，2 瓣给酱汁
  • Eggs鸡蛋 50 g · 1 large egg (dropped into boiling water for 1 minute; the yolk goes in the dressing)1 个大鸡蛋（放进沸水煮 1 分钟，取蛋黄做酱） for anyone pregnant, elderly, young or immunocompromised, swap the yolk for 2 tbsp plain yogurt孕妇、老人、小孩或免疫力低的人，蛋黄换成 2 汤匙原味酸奶
  • Lemons柠檬 80 g · 1 lemon: about 2 tbsp juice and a little zest1 个：约 2 汤匙汁，加一点皮屑
  • Dijon mustard第戎芥末酱 10 g · 2 tsp Dijon mustard2 茶匙法式芥末
  • Soy sauce生抽 6 g · 1 tsp, standing in for the umami of anchovy and Worcestershire sauce1 茶匙，代替鳀鱼和伍斯特酱的鲜味 if you have anchovies, mash 3 fillets in instead of the soy sauce有鳀鱼的话用 3 片，捣碎代替生抽
  • Parmesan帕玛森奶酪 40 g · 40 g: 20 g finely grated into the dressing, 20 g shaved on top40 克：20 克磨细进酱汁，20 克刨片撒面
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp: 2 g chicken, 1 g croutons, 1 g dressing约 2/3 茶匙：鸡胸 2 克、面包丁 1 克、酱汁 1 克
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 2 g · 1 tsp freshly ground pepper, used in stages1 茶匙现磨黑胡椒，分次用

Method做法

  1. Preheat the oven to 190 C (375 F).烤箱预热到 190°C（375°F）。
  2. Toss the bread cubes with 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 smashed clove of garlic and 1 g salt and spread them on a tray.面包丁加 1 汤匙橄榄油、1 瓣拍碎的蒜和 1 克盐拌匀，平铺烤盘。
  3. Bake 10-12 minutes, turning once, until golden and dry, then cool.烤 10–12 分钟，中途翻一次，直到金黄干脆，取出放凉。
  4. Pat the chicken dry and season both sides with 2 g salt and 1/2 tsp pepper.鸡胸用厨房纸擦干，两面撒 2 克盐和半茶匙黑胡椒。
  5. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with 1/2 tbsp oil and sear the chicken 5 minutes until golden underneath.中大火烧热平底锅，加 1/2 汤匙油，放入鸡胸煎 5 分钟到底面金黄。
  6. Flip and cook 4-5 minutes more until a thermometer in the centre reads 74 C (165 F).翻面再煎 4–5 分钟，用温度计量到中心 74°C（165°F）。
  7. Rest the chicken on a board for 5 minutes, then slice on the diagonal.鸡肉放在砧板上静置 5 分钟，再斜切成片。
  8. Lower the egg into boiling water for 1 minute, cool it in cold water and crack out the yolk.鸡蛋放进沸水 1 分钟，捞出过冷水，剥开取蛋黄。
  9. Mash the remaining 2 garlic cloves with 1 g salt into a paste, then whisk in the soy sauce, mustard, lemon juice and yolk.剩下的 2 瓣蒜加 1 克盐磨成泥，加生抽、芥末、柠檬汁和蛋黄，搅匀。
  10. Whisking constantly, add the remaining 2 1/2 tbsp olive oil, first drop by drop and then in a thin stream, until thick.一边不停搅，一边先一滴一滴再成细线地倒入 2 又 1/2 汤匙橄榄油，直到变稠。
  11. Stir in 20 g finely grated Parmesan, loosen with a teaspoon or two of water until pourable, and taste.拌入 20 克磨细的帕玛森，再加一点水调到能流动的稠度，尝味。
  12. Dry the lettuce well, put it in a large bowl and toss gently by hand with most of the dressing until every leaf is coated.生菜撕好甩干，放进大碗，倒入大部分酱汁，用手轻轻拌到每片都沾上。
  13. Plate with the chicken and croutons, shave the remaining Parmesan over and add a little more dressing.装盘，放上鸡肉片、面包丁，刨上剩下的帕玛森，再淋一点酱汁。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Start the oil drop by drop; the dressing thickens by emulsifying, and too much at once separates it into oil and liquid.油要一滴一滴开始加：酱汁靠乳化变稠，一次倒太多会分离成油和水。
  • Bake the croutons rather than frying them; they use less oil and stay crunchy when they meet the dressing.面包丁用烤的而不是炸的：用油少，而且烤干后吸不进酱汁，吃起来才脆。
  • Pound the chicken to an even thickness; uneven pieces dry out at the thin end while the thick end is still raw, the commonest cause of dry chicken breast.鸡胸片到同样的厚度：厚薄不均，薄处煎老时厚处还不熟，是鸡胸变柴最常见的原因。
  • Dry the lettuce thoroughly; water on the leaves stops the dressing clinging and pools in the bowl.生菜要彻底甩干：叶子上有水，酱汁就挂不住，沙拉底下会积一层水。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The dressing split and turned thin酱汁分层变稀
    Put 1 tsp mustard in a clean bowl and whisk the split dressing into it a little at a time to re-emulsify.另取一个碗放 1 茶匙芥末，把分层的酱汁一点一点搅进去重新乳化。
  • The chicken is dry鸡胸干柴
    Slice thin and toss with a little dressing; next time pull it at 74 C and rest it.斜切薄片后拌进一点酱汁，酱汁的油和柠檬会补一些；下次到 74°C 立刻离火并静置。
  • The croutons are soft面包丁发软
    Return them to a 190 C oven 5 minutes and cool them completely; add them to the salad at the last moment.放回 190°C 烤箱再烤 5 分钟，放凉后才变脆；上桌前最后一刻再放。
