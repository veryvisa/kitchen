Sesame Cold Noodles麻酱凉面

A cooling, nutty noodle bowl that comes together in about twenty minutes when it is too hot to cook a big meal. The classic pitfall is dumping soy sauce straight into sesame paste, which makes it seize into a stiff lump; instead, whisk in warm water a little at a time until silky before adding the seasonings.夏天胃口不好时最解馋的一碗凉面，浓香的芝麻酱拌上脆黄瓜丝，从煮面到上桌大约二十分钟。很多人直接把生抽倒进芝麻酱里导致越搅越结块，秘诀是先用温水或凉白开分次把麻酱泄开，再拌入调味料和蒜水，面条才顺滑挂汁。

17 min total分钟 (12 + 5)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Mung bean sprouts, Chinese sesame paste, Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)另购：绿豆芽、纯芝麻酱、香醋/陈醋

Ingredients食材

  • Wheat noodles挂面/面条 200 g · 200 g dried medium-thin wheat noodles中细干面条或鲜切面 200 克 use about 280 g if buying fresh noodles若用鲜切面约需 280 克
  • English cucumber黄瓜 150 g · 1/2 English cucumber or 1 Persian cucumber, julienned半根英国黄瓜或 1 根小黄瓜，切细丝
  • Mung bean sprouts绿豆芽 100 g · 100 g mung bean sprouts (about 1 cup)绿豆芽 100 克（约 1 杯）
  • Carrots胡萝卜 60 g · 1/2 small carrot, julienned半根小胡萝卜，切细丝
  • Chinese sesame paste纯芝麻酱 45 g · 3 tbsp Chinese sesame paste3 汤匙，纯芝麻酱或二八酱 roasted Chinese sesame paste is richest; tahini works if unavailable, but it is thinner and less toasted-tasting, so use a little less water and consider a touch of sesame oil for closer flavor中式烘焙芝麻酱最香；买不到可用中东 tahini 代替，但 tahini 质地更稀、烘香味更淡，水量要相应减少，也可补一点香油找回风味

  • Salt盐 2 g · 1/3 tsp1/3 茶匙

  • Roasted peanuts花生 20 g · 2 tbsp roasted peanuts, crushed2 汤匙，烤熟压碎

Method做法

  1. Place the sesame paste in a bowl and whisk in about 4 tbsp warm water in three or four additions, stirring in one direction until smooth and pourable like yogurt.芝麻酱放进碗里，分三到四次加入约 4 汤匙温开水，每次朝同一方向搅匀至顺滑挂勺的酸奶状。
  2. Stir the mashed garlic with 1 tbsp cooled boiled water, then whisk it into the thinned sesame paste along with the soy sauce, black vinegar, sugar, salt, and half the sesame oil.把蒜泥加 1 汤匙凉白开拌成蒜水，连同生抽、香醋、糖、盐和一半香油拌进泄好的麻酱里。
  3. Bring a pot of water to a boil, blanch the bean sprouts and shredded carrots for 45 seconds until crisp-tender, then plunge them straight into cold or ice water to stop the cooking, and drain very well.烧一锅沸水，先下绿豆芽和胡萝卜丝焯烫 45 秒至断生脆嫩，捞出立刻过冷水或冰水定住脆感，再彻底沥干放凉。
  4. Keep the water at a vigorous boil, add the noodles, and cook 3 to 5 minutes (adjust for the noodle thickness and package timing) until tender with no hard center, then drain.同一锅水保持大滚，下入面条煮 3–5 分钟（按面条粗细和包装时间调整）至刚熟无硬芯，捞出。
  5. Plunge the noodles into ice water or cooled boiled water for 30 seconds until completely chilled, then drain thoroughly.把面条放入冰水或凉白开中过凉 30 秒至完全降温，再彻底沥干。
  6. Toss the noodles with the remaining sesame oil so they stay separate, and divide them into two bowls.面条拌入剩下的一半香油抖散防粘，分装进两个碗里。
  7. Top with the shredded cucumber, cooled bean sprouts, and carrots, then spoon the sesame sauce over everything.铺上青瓜丝、放凉的豆芽和胡萝卜丝，淋上芝麻酱汁。
  8. Scatter the scallions, toasted sesame seeds, and crushed peanuts on top, and toss well before eating.撒上葱花、熟白芝麻和碎花生，吃前拌匀。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Always thin sesame paste with water before adding soy sauce or vinegar; salt and acid tighten the paste on contact, so water must emulsify it first.芝麻酱一定先加水再加生抽和醋；咸味和酸味会立刻让麻酱絮凝结块，先用水泄到顺滑才不容易油水分离。
  • Chilling the boiled noodles in ice water or pre-boiled water washes off sticky surface starch and firms up their bite; drain them well, and refrigerate any leftovers promptly.面条煮熟后用冰水或凉白开过凉，能迅速洗去表面糊化的淀粉、让口感更爽滑弹牙；过凉后要沥干，没吃完的尽快冷藏。
  • If the tossed noodles feel too thick to coat evenly, stir in 1 or 2 tbsp cooled boiled water to loosen the sauce.拌好的面条如果觉得太干拌不开，往碗里加一两汤匙凉白开即可把浓酱化开。
  • Adults who want heat can spoon chili oil over their own bowl at the table while keeping the base nutty and kid-friendly.喜欢吃辣的大人可以在上桌后按自己口味淋一勺红油辣椒，孩子吃不辣原味就很香。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The sesame paste seized into a thick, lumpy mass芝麻酱越搅越干、结成一团疙瘩
    Avoid dumping in liquid all at once or adding soy sauce first; whisk in warm water a teaspoon at a time in one direction, using a small whisk if needed to smooth out lumps.不要一次倒进太多水或先放酱油；分次加一茶匙温水慢慢朝同一方向搅拌，或者用小蛋抽搅开就能恢复顺滑。
  • The noodles tasted watery and the sauce slid off凉面吃起来稀水寡淡、挂不住酱
    Drain the chilled noodles and blanched sprouts thoroughly before assembling; aim for a thick yogurt consistency in the sauce, whisking in another tablespoon of sesame paste if it turned out thin.过凉后的面条和焯过的豆芽必须彻底沥干水分；酱汁调成稠酸奶状最合适，太稀的话可再加 1 汤匙芝麻酱搅匀。
  • The noodles clumped together in the bowl面条坨在一起挑不开
    Toss the drained noodles with sesame oil right after chilling; if they still stick before serving, loosen them with a splash of cold water and the sauce.过凉沥干后立刻拌入香油抖散；吃的时候先夹起面条淋两勺凉白开和麻酱一起挑匀。
