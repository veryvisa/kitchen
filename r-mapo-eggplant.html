Minced Pork Eggplant肉末茄子

Minced pork with eggplant is a favorite over steamed rice, with tender eggplant soaking up savory chili-bean sauce and garlic. The classic home-cooking traps are eggplant drinking up a cup of oil like a sponge, or simmering until it collapses into mush.肉末茄子是最下饭的家常川菜之一，吸饱蒜香和豆瓣酱汁的茄条软糯入味。家里做最怕茄子像海绵一样吸满油，或者焖煮过头烂成一盘泥。

26 min total分钟 (12 + 14)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Doubanjiang, Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)另购：豆瓣酱、老抽、料酒(绍兴)、香醋/陈醋

Ingredients食材

  • Eggplant茄子 450 g · 2-3 long Asian eggplants (about 450 g)紫长茄 2–3 根（约 450 克） cut into 6 cm finger-thick batons切 6 厘米长、手指粗的条
  • Ground pork猪肉馅 150 g · about 150 g ground pork猪肉末约 150 克 medium-lean pork gives better flavour选三分肥七分瘦更香
  • Doubanjiang豆瓣酱 22 g · 1 generous tbsp Pixian doubanjiang郫县豆瓣酱约 1 汤匙多 mince finely so it releases red oil smoothly提前剁细更容易出红油
  • Garlic大蒜 20 g · 5 cloves, minced5 瓣，切末
  • Ginger姜 10 g · about 3 slices, minced约 3 片，切末
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 25 g · 2 stalks, thinly sliced2 根，切葱花
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp (7.5 mL)半汤匙（7.5 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)香醋/陈醋 8 g · 1/2 tbsp (about 7.5 mL)半汤匙（约 7.5 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 6 g · 1 1/2 tsp1 茶匙半

  • Canola oil菜籽油 45 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙

Method做法

  1. Cut the eggplants into 6 cm batons, toss with salt, let sit 10 minutes, then press gently (no need to squeeze hard) and pour off the liquid that collects.茄子切成 6 厘米长的粗条，撒盐拌匀静置 10 分钟，轻轻按压（不用使劲挤）倒掉渗出的水。
  2. Stir together the soy sauce, dark soy sauce, black vinegar, sugar, cornstarch, and 85 mL water in a small bowl.碗里把生抽、老抽、香醋、糖、玉米淀粉和 85 毫升清水搅匀成碗汁。
  3. Heat 2 tbsp oil over medium-high heat and pan-fry the eggplant for about 5 minutes, tossing often, until lightly charred and tender, then transfer to a plate.炒锅放 2 汤匙油烧热，中大火下茄条翻煎约 5 分钟，不时翻动，至表面微焦变软，盛出。
  4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil over medium heat and stir-fry the ground pork for about 3 minutes until no pink remains and dry, lightly browned (center about 71 °C/160 °F).锅里加剩下 1 汤匙油，中火下猪肉末炒散约 3 分钟，至水分收干、肉末不再是粉红色（中心约 71°C/160°F）。
  5. Reduce to low heat, push the pork aside, and fry the minced doubanjiang for about 1 minute until the oil runs red.转小火把肉末拨到一边，下剁细的豆瓣酱炒约 1 分钟至出红油。
  6. Add the ginger, garlic, and half the scallions, stir 20 seconds until fragrant, mix with the pork, then splash in the cooking wine and let it sizzle for 10 seconds.放入姜末、蒜末和一半葱花炒 20 秒出香，与肉末拌匀，淋入料酒呛香 10 秒。
  7. Return the eggplant to the wok and toss gently to combine.倒回煎好的茄条轻轻翻匀。
  8. Stir the sauce slurry again, pour it in, and simmer over medium heat for 2 minutes while gently nudging the pan until a glossy sauce coats the eggplant.碗汁搅匀倒入锅中，中火煮 2 分钟并轻推锅底，至汤汁红亮浓稠裹住茄条。
  9. Turn off the heat, drizzle with sesame oil, scatter the remaining scallions on top, and serve.关火，淋入香油，撒上剩下的葱花装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Salting draws out some water so the eggplant browns better, but it does not replace oil; to use less oil, soften the batons in the microwave or a steamer for 3-4 minutes first, then pan-fry briefly just to colour them.茄条先用盐渍去掉一部分水分，煎的时候更容易上色，但盐渍不能代替用油——想少油就先用微波炉或蒸锅把茄条软化 3-4 分钟，再下锅略煎上色。
  • Long purple Asian eggplants have thinner skin and fewer seeds than globe eggplants, so batons hold their shape better.紫长茄比粗圆茄皮薄籽少，切条炒制后更软糯，也不容易散烂。
  • Finely mincing the doubanjiang and frying it gently on low heat draws out smooth red oil that coats every piece evenly.豆瓣酱提前剁细、小火煸出红油，酱香才会均匀挂在茄条上，不会吃出一块块咸酱。
  • A splash of black vinegar and a pinch of sugar brighten the rich sauce without making the dish taste sweet-and-sour.少许香醋和糖用来提鲜解腻，成品吃不出酸甜味，但层次感会明显更好。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • Eggplant soaked up too much oil and tastes greasy茄子吸油太多、吃起来很腻
    Always salt and squeeze first and pan-fry over medium-high heat next time; blot excess oil on paper towels or serve with steamed greens and rice.下次一定先盐渍挤干再中高火煎；已做好的可先垫厨房纸吸油，再配烫青菜和白米饭。
  • Eggplant turned mushy or muddy brown茄子烂成泥，或者颜色发黑
    Cut thicker batons next time and simmer only 2 minutes at the end, nudging with the back of the spatula instead of stirring hard. Cut eggplant also browns from oxidation if it sits too long, so salt and cook it soon after cutting.茄条切太细且煮太久会烂；切好的茄子放久了也会氧化发黑，下次切手指粗、切完尽快盐渍下锅，最后下碗汁只煮 2 分钟，用铲背轻推即可。
  • The dish came out too salty整盘偏咸
    Doubanjiang and both soy sauces carry plenty of sodium; use a little less doubanjiang next time and skip extra salt on the eggplant. To rescue an over-salty batch, serve with more plain rice or stir in a little more unsalted eggplant rather than adding sugar.豆瓣酱、生抽、老抽都含盐；下次豆瓣酱少放一点，煎茄步骤不额外加盐；已做好的配多一点白米饭，或加一点清茄子块稀释，而不是加糖。
