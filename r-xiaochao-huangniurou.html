Hunan Stir-fried Beef小炒黄牛肉

Hunan stir-fried beef often turns watery and tough on a home stove when beef releases moisture or chilies scorch before the meat cooks. Slicing thinly against the grain, velveting with water and oil, dry-searing the chilies first, and stir-frying in quick stages keeps the beef tender and punchy.湘菜馆必点的小炒黄牛肉，在家做最容易出错的是牛肉出水发柴、或者辣椒炒糊而肉还没熟。把牛肉逆纹切薄抓水封油，青椒先干煸去水，再用高油温分步快炒，普通家用灶也能炒出香辣嫩滑的口感。

Method做法

  1. Slice the beef against the grain into 2 mm thin pieces and place in a bowl.牛肉逆着纹理切成约 2 毫米的薄片，放入大碗。
  2. Work in 1/2 tbsp soy sauce, dark soy sauce, cooking wine, cornstarch, and 1 tbsp water for about 1 minute until absorbed, then rest 10 minutes.加入半汤匙生抽、老抽、料酒、玉米淀粉和 1 汤匙清水抓揉约 1 分钟至水分完全吸收，静置 10 分钟。
  3. Stir 1 tbsp canola oil into the beef to seal in moisture.拌入 1 汤匙菜籽油把牛肉表面封匀锁水。
  4. Stir the remaining soy sauce, oyster sauce and sugar together in a small bowl ahead of time; roughly chop the fermented black beans.把生抽、蚝油和糖提前在小碗调匀备用；另把豆豉粗略剁碎。
  5. Heat a dry wok over high heat, add the green chilies and salt, and dry-sear about 2 minutes, pressing occasionally until charred, blistered spots appear, then remove.干锅大火烧热不放油，放入青线椒和盐煸炒约 2 分钟，不时按压至表皮起虎皮焦斑，盛出备用。
  6. Heat 2 tbsp oil over high heat until faintly smoking, spread the beef in the wok, sear 40 seconds undisturbed, then toss about 20 seconds until about 80% browned (a little pink still showing), then transfer out at once.锅中倒入 2 汤匙油大火烧至微微冒烟，下牛肉迅速摊平滑炒约 40 秒不要翻动，再翻炒约 20 秒至八成变色（还留一点粉红），立刻盛出。
  7. Add the last 1 tbsp oil over medium heat and fry the ginger, garlic, red chilies and fermented black beans for 15 seconds until fragrant.原锅加剩余 1 汤匙油转中火，放姜丝、蒜末、小米辣和豆豉炒 15 秒至辛香扑鼻。
  8. Toss in the celery and stir-fry 30 seconds until bright green.放入香芹段快炒 30 秒至颜色翠绿发亮。
  9. Return the beef and green chilies, drizzle the pre-mixed sauce around the edge of the wok, and toss over high heat for 20 seconds until no pink remains on the beef.倒回牛肉和青线椒，沿锅边淋入调好的酱汁，大火快速翻匀 20 秒至牛肉不再有粉红色。
  10. Turn off the heat, fold in the cilantro with the residual heat, and serve immediately.关火放入香菜段，用余温拌匀后立即装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cutting across the grain shortens tough muscle fibers, and absorbing water with starch before coating in oil keeps the beef juicy over high heat.牛肉逆纹切薄片能切断肌肉纤维，配合「先抓水加淀粉、再拌油封住」的顺序，下锅不易脱水变老。
  • Dry-searing the green chilies with a pinch of salt drives off moisture and raw sharpness so they do not water down the final toss.青椒先干煸并撒少许盐，能逼出多余水分和生涩草味，最后回锅合炒才不会泄水把酱汁冲淡。
  • Because home burners run cooler than restaurant woks, searing the beef separately and combining everything for the final 20 seconds prevents overcooking.家用灶火力不如餐厅猛火，把牛肉单独滑熟盛出、爆香配料后再合炒 20 秒，是保证嫩度的关键。
  • Folding in cilantro off the heat preserves its fresh aroma and bright color without wilting it.香菜最后关火才放，既保留清香气，又不会遇高温变黑发软。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The beef turned out tough and chewy牛肉炒老、嚼不烂
    Slicing with the grain or searing too long makes beef tough; cut thinly against the grain and pull it out while about 80% browned (still a little pink) — the final 20-second toss finishes the cooking.常见原因是顺纹切肉或滑炒太久；下次一定逆纹切薄，八成变色（还留一点粉红）就盛出，回锅合炒的 20 秒会让它刚好熟透。
  • Too much liquid pooled in the wok锅底出了很多水、味道变淡
    Overcrowding a lukewarm wok steams the meat; sear the beef in two smaller batches if needed and always dry-sear the green chilies first.油温不够或一次下太多肉会变成低温煮肉；家用小灶可把牛肉分两批滑炒，青椒也必须先干煸去水。
  • The dish is too spicy辣味太重、家人吃不了
    Omit or reduce the red Thai chilies, and halve and seed the green chilies to keep the pepper aroma with gentler heat.减少或去掉小米辣，把青线椒纵向剖开刮去辣椒籽和白筋再切段，就能只留椒香减少辣度。
