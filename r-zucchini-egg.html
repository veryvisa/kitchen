Zucchini and Egg Stir-fry西葫芦炒蛋

One of the most common quick dishes in northern Chinese homes: tender-crisp zucchini with fluffy eggs in 15 minutes. Zucchini releases water easily, so cook the eggs first and combine them only at the end.北方家里最常见的快手菜之一：西葫芦脆嫩、鸡蛋蓬松，15 分钟上桌。西葫芦容易出水，先把蛋炒好盛出、最后合炒，是不变汤的关键。

15 min total分钟 (8 + 7)2 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Zucchini西葫芦 400 g · 2 medium zucchini, cut into thin half-moons2 根中等西葫芦，切半月形薄片
  • Eggs鸡蛋 150 g · 3 large eggs3 个大鸡蛋
  • Garlic大蒜 8 g · 3 cloves, sliced3 瓣，切片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 5 g · a little chopped green onion葱花少许
  • Salt盐 3 g · about 1/2 tsp (1 g for the eggs, 2 g for the zucchini)约 1/2 茶匙（蛋液 1 克，菜 2 克）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Beat the eggs with 1 g salt.鸡蛋加 1 克盐打散。
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil and pour in the eggs.锅里放 1 汤匙油烧热，倒入蛋液。
  3. When the eggs puff up, break them into large curds and take them out at 80% set.蛋液鼓起后用铲子划成大块，八成熟时盛出。
  4. Add the second tablespoon of oil and fry the garlic over high heat until fragrant.锅里补 1 汤匙油，大火炒香蒜片。
  5. Add the zucchini and stir-fry on high for 2 minutes until the edges turn translucent.放西葫芦片，大火翻炒 2 分钟到边缘透明。
  6. Add 2 g salt, return the eggs and toss for 30 seconds.加 2 克盐，鸡蛋回锅，翻炒 30 秒。
  7. Add the green onion and serve at once.撒葱花，立刻出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cook zucchini fast over high heat; long cooking turns it limp and watery.西葫芦大火快炒，炒久了会软塌出水。
  • Salt the zucchini at the very end; early salt draws out its water.盐在快出锅时才放，早放会把西葫芦里的水逼出来。
  • Take the eggs out at 80% so they finish in the pan without toughening.鸡蛋八成熟就盛出，回锅时再熟透，才嫩。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • A puddle on the plate盘底一摊水
    Low heat, long cooking or early salt; use high heat, finish within 2 minutes and salt at the end.火小、炒太久或盐放早了；下次大火、2 分钟内炒完、出锅前放盐。
  • The eggs are tough and crumbly鸡蛋又老又碎
    They cooked too long the first time; break them into big curds as soon as they puff and take them out.第一次炒蛋时炒太久；鼓起划大块就盛出。
