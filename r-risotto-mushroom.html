Mushroom Risotto蘑菇烩饭

The rice slowly absorbs warm stock, then gets finished with butter and Parmesan for a creamy but not heavy texture. Add the stock gradually and keep stirring so the grains turn tender while retaining a little bite.米粒慢慢吸收热高汤，最后拌入奶油和帕玛森，口感浓润却不厚重。成败关键是分次加汤并持续搅拌，米心要熟而仍带一点嚼劲。

45 min total分钟 (15 + 30)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Short-grain rice短粒米(寿司米) 300 g · 300 g risotto rice (Arborio or Carnaroli)意大利烩饭米（Arborio 或 Carnaroli）300 克 Do not rinse; the surface starch helps create a creamy texture. Plain sushi short-grain rice absorbs liquid differently and will turn out gummier不淘洗，米表面的淀粉有助于形成浓稠口感；普通寿司短粒米吸水性不同，成品会偏黏
  • White mushrooms蘑菇 300 g · 300 g mushrooms, sliced蘑菇 300 克，切片
  • Onions洋葱 120 g · 1 small onion, finely diced1 个小洋葱，切细丁
  • Garlic大蒜 8 g · 2 cloves, minced2 瓣，切末
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 1000 g · about 4 cups chicken stock鸡汤约 4 杯
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 90 g · 90 ml dry white wine; use an actual drinking wine, not a salted Chinese cooking wine干白葡萄酒 90 毫升，用真正的干白酒，不要用含盐的中式料酒
  • Parmesan帕玛森奶酪 50 g · Parmesan, finely grated帕玛森奶酪，细刨
  • Butter黄油 35 g · unsalted butter无盐黄油
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 ml)1 汤匙（15 毫升）
  • Salt盐 5 g · about 3/4 tsp, adjusted for the stock's saltiness约 3/4 茶匙，按鸡汤咸度调整
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · about 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper现磨黑胡椒约 1/2 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Warm the stock in a small pot over medium heat until steaming, then keep it over low heat.鸡汤倒入小锅，中火加热到微微冒热气后转小火保温。
  2. Heat the olive oil in a heavy pot over medium heat and sear the mushrooms for 6–8 minutes until their liquid evaporates and the edges turn golden.厚底锅中火烧热橄榄油，放入蘑菇煎 6–8 分钟，直到出水蒸发且边缘金黄。
  3. Transfer the mushrooms to a plate and cook the onion in the pot over medium-low heat for 4 minutes until soft and translucent.把蘑菇盛出，锅中放洋葱，中小火炒 4 分钟至透明变软。
  4. Add the garlic and cook over medium-low heat for 30 seconds until fragrant.加入蒜末，中小火炒 30 秒至闻到香味。
  5. Add the rice and stir over medium heat for 2 minutes until the edges of the grains look translucent.倒入生米，中火翻炒 2 分钟，直到米粒边缘变透明。
  6. Pour in the white wine and stir over medium heat for 1–2 minutes until almost evaporated.倒入白葡萄酒，中火搅拌 1–2 分钟至酒液基本收干。
  7. Ladle in about 120–180 mL hot stock and stir continuously over medium-low heat until the rice has mostly absorbed it before adding the next ladle.舀入约 120–180 毫升热鸡汤，中小火持续搅拌至米粒大致吸干汤汁再加下一勺。
  8. From the first addition of stock, continue this way for a total of 18–22 minutes, tasting the rice often, until the grains are tender with a slight bite in the center.从第一次加鸡汤开始算，继续这样每次加汤并搅拌，共煮 18–22 分钟，期间不断尝米粒，直到柔软但中心仍有轻微嚼劲。
  9. Stir in the mushrooms, salt, and black pepper and cook over medium heat for 1 minute until hot.拌入煎好的蘑菇、盐和黑胡椒，中火煮 1 分钟至热透。
  10. Take the pot off the heat and immediately stir in the butter and Parmesan, mixing briskly to emulsify; adjust with a little more hot stock if needed so it stays thick but still flows slowly. Do not cover and let it sit, or it will keep thickening.关火后立刻拌入黄油和帕玛森，用力搅拌使其融入乳化；视情况补一点热鸡汤，调整到浓稠但仍能缓慢流动，不要盖锅静置，以免越来越稠。
  11. Confirm the rice is tender with a little bite and the risotto is thick but still flows slowly, then serve immediately while hot.确认米粒熟而带一点嚼劲、整锅呈浓稠但能缓慢流动的状态，趁热立即盛出。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Keep the stock warm; cold stock drops the pot's temperature and slows the cooking.鸡汤保持温热，冷汤会让锅内温度骤降，延长烹煮时间。
  • Let the rice mostly absorb each addition before adding more, which makes the texture easier to control.每次加汤后等米粒大致吸收再添，才能把握浓稠度，也不容易煮成稀粥。
  • Sear the mushrooms separately until their liquid evaporates for deeper flavor without watering down the rice.蘑菇先单独煎到水分蒸发，香味更集中，也不会让米饭出水。
  • Add the butter and cheese off the heat; residual warmth melts them without separating the fats.关火后再加黄油和奶酪，余温能融化它们，避免油脂在锅里分离。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The rice is porridge-like with no bite米饭煮成稀粥，米粒没有嚼劲
    Stop adding stock and cooking at once, then serve; overcooked grains cannot regain their bite, so shorten the cooking time next time.立刻停止加汤和烹煮，直接盛出；软烂的米粒无法恢复嚼劲，下次缩短煮制时间。
  • The center is still hard but the pot is dry米心仍硬，锅里却没有汤汁
    Add a little hot stock and stir over medium-low heat for another 2–3 minutes, then taste again.每次补入少量热鸡汤，继续中小火搅拌 2–3 分钟，再尝一次。
  • The risotto tastes greasy or has separated口感发腻或油水分离
    Stir in the butter and cheese off the heat; if it has separated, add a small spoonful of hot stock and stir briskly until smooth.关火后再拌入黄油和奶酪；已经分离时加一小勺热鸡汤并快速搅拌至重新顺滑。
