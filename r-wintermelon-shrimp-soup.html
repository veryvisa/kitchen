Winter Melon and Shrimp Soup冬瓜虾仁汤

A light, everyday soup. The winter melon is stir-fried briefly with ginger, then simmered until translucent, and the shrimp go in only at the end so they stay springy. Twenty minutes from start to finish.夏天和饭桌上最常见的清汤：冬瓜先用姜和一点油炒出香味，再加水煮到半透明，虾仁最后才下，吃起来脆弹而不老。整道汤二十分钟，很适合当一餐里的汤菜。

40 min total分钟 (20 + 20)4 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Winter melon, Shaoxing wine另购：冬瓜、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Winter melon冬瓜 1000 g · about 1 kg winter melon with skin and seeds, cut into 4 cm x 0.5 cm slices冬瓜约 1 公斤（带皮带籽），切 4 厘米 × 0.5 厘米薄片 about 700 g after peeling and seeding; supermarkets often sell a cut wedge去皮去籽后约 700 克，超市常卖切好的半块
  • Frozen raw shrimp冷冻生虾 300 g · 300 g peeled, deveined raw shrimp去壳去虾线的生虾仁 300 克 thaw frozen shrimp in cold water first冷冻虾仁先在冷水中解冻
  • Ginger姜 15 g · 5 slices5 片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 2 stalks, sliced2 根，切葱花
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 3 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Salt盐 6 g · about 1 tsp, added in stages约 1 茶匙，分次加
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · 1/2 tsp white or black pepper白胡椒或黑胡椒 1/2 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Peel and seed the winter melon and cut it into slices 4 cm long and 0.5 cm thick.冬瓜去皮去籽，切成 4 厘米长、半厘米厚的薄片。
  2. Pat the shrimp dry and toss with the wine, cornstarch, 1 g salt and the pepper; leave 10 minutes.虾仁用厨房纸吸干水，加料酒、淀粉、盐 1 克和胡椒拌匀，腌 10 分钟。
  3. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat and fry the ginger for 30 seconds until fragrant.锅里放油，中火烧热，下姜片煸 30 秒到出香。
  4. Add the winter melon and stir 2-3 minutes until the edges start to turn translucent.放入冬瓜片，翻炒 2–3 分钟，直到边缘开始变得半透明。
  5. Pour in about 900 mL boiling water and bring to a boil over high heat.倒入约 900 毫升开水，大火烧开。
  6. Reduce to medium-low, cover and cook 10-12 minutes, until the slices are translucent and a chopstick slides through.转中小火，盖盖煮 10–12 分钟，直到冬瓜整片半透明、用筷子能轻松戳穿。
  7. Stir in 4 g of salt and taste.加盐 4 克，搅匀，尝一口。
  8. Turn the heat to high, add the shrimp and separate them with chopsticks.转大火，下虾仁，用筷子拨散。
  9. Cook 1.5-2 minutes, until the shrimp are pink and curled into a C; do not cook until they curl into tight rings.煮 1.5–2 分钟，虾仁变粉红并卷成 C 形就好了，不要煮到卷成圈。
  10. Turn off the heat, drizzle with the sesame oil, scatter the green onion and serve hot.关火，淋香油，撒葱花，趁热盛出。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Slice the melon about 0.5 cm: thicker pieces stay raw in the middle and the broth cannot get in, thinner ones collapse.冬瓜要切薄片，约半厘米：太厚煮不透、汤味进不去，太薄一煮就烂。
  • Stir-fry the melon with ginger first; winter melon is very mild, and this step builds the base so it tastes far better than just boiling.先用油和姜炒冬瓜：冬瓜本身没什么味道，这一步给汤打底，比直接用水煮鲜很多。
  • Add the shrimp last and cook only to a C shape; shrimp curled into a ring are overcooked.虾仁一定最后下，而且只煮到卷成 C 形；煮成圈的虾已经老了。
  • Dry the shrimp before marinating so the cornstarch sticks, which keeps them tender and springy.虾仁腌前先擦干，淀粉才附得上，虾口感嫩脆。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The soup tastes like water汤没味道，只有水味
    Add 1 g more salt and a few drops of sesame oil; next time fry the melon with ginger first, or swap half the water for chicken stock.再加 1 克盐，并放几滴香油；下次一定先把冬瓜用姜炒一下，也可以用一半鸡汤代替水。
  • The melon cooked down to mush冬瓜煮烂成糊
    Call it a thick soup and mash it lightly with the back of a spoon; next time cut thicker and stop when translucent.把虾仁和少量汤盛出，其余用勺背压碎当浓汤；下次切厚一点，煮到半透明就关火。
  • The shrimp are rubbery虾仁变老、不脆
    They are overcooked; lift them out and serve them on the side. Next time switch off as soon as they turn pink.已经煮过头了，只能捞出沥汤当配菜吃；下次煮到变粉红就马上关火。
