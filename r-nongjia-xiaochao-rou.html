Hunan Farmhouse Stir-fried Pork农家小炒肉

Hunan farmhouse stir-fried pork is built for eating with steamed rice. The secret is dry-blistering the chilies first, rendering fat from the pork belly to fry the garlic and black beans, flashing the lean shoulder over high heat right after, and tossing the chilies back in at the very end so nothing turns soggy or tough.农家小炒肉是一道非常下饭的湖南家常菜。做好它的关键是先干煸青椒去水汽、再用五花肉逼出猪油炒香蒜豆豉，然后下腌好的瘦肉猛火快炒，最后把青椒回锅一起拌匀，既香辣入味又不老不腻。

Ingredients食材

  • Pork belly五花肉 180 g · about 180 g pork belly, thinly sliced五花肉约 180 克，切薄片 chilling 20 minutes in the freezer makes slicing easier放冷冻室冻 20 分钟更好切薄
  • Pork shoulder猪梅花肉 150 g · about 150 g pork shoulder, thinly sliced猪梅花肉或前腿瘦肉约 150 克，切薄片 cooking lean and fatty cuts separately keeps it tender肥瘦分开下锅更嫩不腻
  • Long green chilli peppers青尖椒（螺丝椒） 200 g · 4-5 long green chilies (Anaheim, Cubanelle or Chinese long hot peppers), sliced diagonally, plus 1-2 jalapenos or serranos if you want more heat4–5 根青尖椒或螺丝椒，斜切片 remove the seeds and ribs for milder heat怕辣可去掉辣椒籽和白筋
  • Garlic大蒜 20 g · 5 cloves, smashed and sliced5 瓣，拍扁切片

Method做法

  1. Slice the pork belly and pork shoulder separately into 2 mm pieces.五花肉和梅花肉分别切成约 2 毫米厚的薄片。
  2. Toss the pork shoulder slices with the cooking wine, dark soy sauce, 2 tsp of the soy sauce and the cornstarch, then marinate for 5 minutes.梅花肉片加料酒、老抽、2 茶匙生抽和淀粉抓匀上浆，腌 5 分钟。
  3. Slice the green chilies diagonally, roughly chop the black beans and smash and slice the garlic.青尖椒斜切成片，豆豉粗略剁几刀，大蒜拍裂切片。
  4. Heat a dry wok over medium-high heat, add the chilies, and blister for 3-4 minutes, pressing and turning them occasionally, until the skin wrinkles with dark spots. Sprinkle on the salt only in the last 30 seconds, then transfer out.干锅烧热不放油，下青尖椒，中大火煸炒 3–4 分钟并不时按压翻动，直到表皮起虎皮焦斑变软，最后 30 秒再撒盐，盛出。
  5. Place the pork belly in the same wok and render over medium heat for 3-4 minutes, stirring constantly and spreading it flat, until the fat renders and the edges curl golden. If more than about 2 tbsp of fat collects, pour off the excess and keep about 1 tbsp.把五花肉放进同一口锅，中火平铺煸炒 3–4 分钟并持续翻动，直到肥肉出油、边缘微卷发黄；如果出油超过 2 汤匙，倒掉多余的留约 1 汤匙。
  6. Push the belly to one side, add the garlic, ginger and black beans, and fry for 20-30 seconds until fragrant, then immediately raise to high heat and add the marinated pork shoulder (add the 1 tsp oil only if the pan looks dry). Stir-fry 1.5-2 minutes until no pink remains, the edges brown, and the center reaches 71°C; cook in two batches if the wok is crowded.把五花肉拨到锅边，放入蒜片、姜丝和豆豉，炒香 20–30 秒，随即转大火倒入腌好的梅花肉片（锅太干才补 1 茶匙油），快炒 1.5–2 分钟直到完全没有粉红色、边缘微焦且内部达到 71°C 以上；锅太小就分两批炒。
  7. Stir the remaining soy sauce and oyster sauce with 1 tbsp water in a small bowl, then return the chilies and pour in the sauce. Toss vigorously over high heat for about 30 seconds, making sure the pork and chilies are evenly mixed before serving.生抽和蚝油先在小碗里加 1 汤匙水调开，倒回青尖椒和调好的酱汁，大火快速翻炒约 30 秒拌匀，确认肉和菜都已拌匀再出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Dry-blistering the chilies with salt draws out moisture and builds roasted aroma so the stir-fry never turns watery.青椒先干锅加盐煸炒，能逼出多余水分并带出焦香，回锅后才不会水塌塌的。
  • Rendering lard from the pork belly first, then frying the garlic, ginger and black beans in it before the lean pork goes in, gives much deeper flavor than cooking oil alone.先煸出五花肉自带的猪油，用来炒香蒜末、姜丝和豆豉，再趁热下瘦肉，风味比只用植物油厚实得多。
  • Marinating the lean pork with a light starch coating and searing it right after the aromatics (rather than letting them linger over low heat) keeps both the pork juicy and the garlic from turning bitter.瘦肉单独上浆腌制、炒香料后立刻转大火快炒，避免肉片在锅里等太久变柴，也避免蒜末豆豉在小火里炒太久发苦。
  • Slicing semi-frozen pork into even 2 mm pieces makes it easier to cut evenly and lets it cook through in a minute or two.把肉稍微冻硬再切 2 毫米薄片，既容易切匀，下锅一两分钟内就能熟透。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The wok turned watery and the chilies went limp锅里出很多水，辣椒软烂不香
    Dry-blister the chilies first until charred, set them aside, and toss everything together uncovered over high heat at the end.下次一定先干锅把辣椒煸到起虎皮再盛出，最后回锅全程开大火且不要盖锅盖。
  • The lean pork turned chewy and dry瘦肉炒老了咬不动
    Cut the slices thinner and marinate them with wine, soy sauce and cornstarch beforehand; add them right after the aromatics and stir-fry over high heat for about 2 minutes total.肉片要切得足够薄并提前用料酒、生抽和淀粉抓匀上浆，炒香料后立刻下锅，大火快炒总共约 2 分钟即可。
  • The dish tastes too salty or slightly bitter成菜偏咸或有焦苦味
    Black beans, soy sauce and oyster sauce add plenty of salt, so use only a small pinch on the chilies. Fry the garlic and black beans for just 20-30 seconds before adding the pork; do not let them sit in the pan longer.豆豉、生抽和蚝油都带咸度，盐只需一小撮给辣椒底味；炒蒜末豆豉只需 20–30 秒就该下肉，别让它们在锅里多待。
