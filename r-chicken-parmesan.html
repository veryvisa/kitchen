Chicken Parmesan意式帕玛森鸡排

A golden, crispy chicken cutlet topped with melted cheese and a rich tomato sauce, this is a favourite Italian-American family dinner. The main challenges are keeping the coating crisp and attached, keeping the chicken juicy, and getting the tomato sauce thick rather than watery.鸡排外壳金黄酥脆、内里多汁，上铺融化芝士和慢煮番茄酱，是北美家庭餐桌上很受欢迎的一道意大利菜。家里做的难点是外壳不脱落、鸡肉不柴，以及番茄酱要收得浓而不水。

45 min total分钟 (20 + 25)4 servings份Medium中等Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless skinless chicken breast鸡胸肉 600 g · 2 chicken breasts (about 600 g)鸡胸肉 2 块（约 600 克）
  • All-purpose flour面粉 60 g · about 1/2 cup约半杯
  • Eggs鸡蛋 150 g · 3 large eggs3 个大鸡蛋
  • Sandwich bread面包 120 g · about 4–5 stale slices (depending on size), made into about 120 g crumbs干硬面包约 4–5 片（视面包大小），搓成约 120 克面包屑
  • Parmesan帕玛森奶酪 50 g · about 1/2 cup freshly grated现磨帕玛森 约半杯
  • Mozzarella马苏里拉 150 g · mozzarella, torn or shredded马苏里拉，撕片或切丝
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 55 g · about 4 tbsp (60 mL), shared between frying the cutlets and the tomato sauce—set aside 1 tbsp for the sauce first约 4 汤匙（60 mL），煎鸡排和炒番茄酱蒜末共用，先留 1 汤匙给酱汁
  • Canned diced tomatoes番茄罐头(碎) 400 g · 1 can (400 g) tomatoes罐装番茄 1 罐
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 2 cloves, minced2 瓣，切末
  • Fresh parsley欧芹 10 g · chopped, about 2 tbsp切碎，约 2 汤匙
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 1/2 tsp, in stages约半茶匙，分次用
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 2 g · cracked black pepper现磨黑胡椒

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Slice each breast horizontally into two cutlets and pound them to about 1.5 cm thick.鸡胸肉从中间横切成两片厚排，用刀背或肉锤拍松到约 1.5 厘米厚。
  2. Season both sides lightly with salt and pepper and rest for 10 minutes.两面撒少许盐和黑胡椒，静置腌 10 分钟。
  3. Set three dishes: flour; eggs beaten with 1 tbsp water; crumbs mixed with half the parmesan and a little salt.准备三个浅盘：一盘面粉，一盘打散的鸡蛋加 1 汤匙水，一盘面包屑拌入一半帕玛森和少许盐。
  4. Dredge in flour, shake off excess, dip in egg, then press crumbs onto both sides.肉排先沾面粉，抖掉多余粉，再裹蛋液，最后按压上面包屑，两面都按实。
  5. Heat the olive oil about 3 mm deep in a pan over medium; a crumb dropped in should sizzle right away.平底锅倒橄榄油约 3 毫米深，中火加热，丢一粒面包屑进去能立刻滋啦作响。
  6. Fry the cutlets 2-3 minutes per side just until the crust is golden and set; it does not need to be fully cooked yet, then drain.肉排入锅，每面煎 2–3 分钟到外壳金黄定型即可，这一步不必测熟度，捞出沥油。
  7. In a small pot with the reserved 1 tbsp oil, fry the garlic over low heat for 30 seconds, add the canned tomatoes and crush them with a spoon, simmer 12-15 minutes over medium-low until thick, and season.另取小锅放留出的 1 汤匙油，小火下蒜末炒 30 秒出香，加入罐装番茄并用勺子压碎，中小火煮 12–15 分钟收浓，加盐和胡椒调味。
  8. Place the cutlets in a baking dish and spread only a thin layer of sauce on top (not pooled underneath), then add the mozzarella and remaining parmesan; serve the rest of the sauce alongside or over pasta.鸡排放入烤盘，表面只铺薄薄一层番茄酱（不要铺满盘底），再铺马苏里拉和剩余帕玛森；其余番茄酱留着上桌时配意面或放在旁边。
  9. Preheat the oven to 200°C and bake 5-10 minutes until the cheese melts, bubbles and browns at the edges; confirm with an instant-read thermometer that the thickest part of the chicken reaches 74°C, continuing to bake and rechecking if it has not.烤箱预热至 200°C，烤 5–10 分钟至芝士融化起泡、边缘微微焦黄；用食品温度计确认鸡肉最厚处达到 74°C，没到就继续烤并复测。
  10. Sprinkle with parsley and serve hot with pasta or bread.出炉撒欧芹碎，趁热配意面或面包食用。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Keep the order right: flour gives the egg something to grip, and the egg holds the crumbs. Press firmly and rest a few minutes so the crust stays on.裹粉顺序不能乱：面粉让蛋液附着，蛋液再粘住面包屑；按压实后静置几分钟，煎的时候外壳才不会脱落。
  • Add the cutlet only when the oil is hot; cold oil makes the crust greasy and soft. Let the first side set and brown before turning.油温到位再下肉，冷油会让外壳吸油变软；煎的时候不要翻动太勤，等底面定型金黄再翻。
  • The pan stage only sets and browns the crust; the oven finishes the cooking. The chicken only needs to reach 74°C at the center—further heat will dry it out.煎锅阶段只让外壳定型上色，之后进烤箱完成加热；最终鸡肉中心达到 74°C 即可，避免过度加热变柴。
  • Mozzarella melts and stretches, while parmesan adds a savory depth to the breadcrumb coating and the cheesy topping; the two cheeses do different jobs.马苏里拉负责拉丝融化，帕玛森用在面包屑和表面芝士层增香，两种芝士分工不同。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The coating falls off or goes soggy外壳脱落或者软塌
    Rest the coated cutlets 5 minutes so the crumbs bond, and fry with the oil properly hot without crowding the pan. If the crust is already a bit soft, go straight into the oven rather than re-crisping it over high heat.裹好粉后静置 5 分钟让面包屑粘牢；煎的时候油温要够热，锅别太挤，炸衣受潮后别再回锅大火补脆，直接进烤箱完成加热即可。
  • The chicken is undercooked or dry鸡肉夹生冷或者过柴
    Pound the cutlet evenly so no spot stays thick. Let the pan stage only brown and set the crust, then check the thickest part with a thermometer during the oven stage until it reaches 74°C instead of judging by colour.肉排厚度要拍均匀，厚的地方用锤压开；煎锅阶段只让外壳上色定型，最后在烤箱阶段用食品温度计测鸡肉最厚处达到 74°C，不要凭外观判断。
  • The tomato sauce is thin and watery番茄酱稀薄出水
    Simmer uncovered a few more minutes and add half a tbsp of tomato paste to thicken; if it is already thin, serve it beside the cutlet instead of pouring it on thick.开盖中小火多收几分钟，再加半汤匙番茄膏提味增稠；已经做好的可以把酱淋在鸡排旁而不是厚厚盖住。
