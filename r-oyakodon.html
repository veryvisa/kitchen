Staple主食 · Japanese日

Oyakodon (Japanese Chicken and Egg Rice Bowl)亲子丼（日式鸡肉滑蛋盖饭）

Oyako means parent and child, meaning the chicken and the egg. Chicken and onion simmer in dashi, soy sauce, mirin and sugar, then beaten egg is poured in two stages and cooked only until half-set and silky, over hot rice. The one skill is not overcooking the egg.「亲子」指鸡肉和鸡蛋。用高汤、酱油、味醂和糖把鸡肉和洋葱煮到入味，蛋液分两次淋入，只煮到半熟、像丝绸一样滑，再盖在热饭上。关键是蛋别煮老。

50 min total分钟 (15 + 15)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dashi powder, Mirin另购：日式鲣鱼高汤粉、味啉

Ingredients食材

  • Short-grain rice短粒米(寿司米) 150 g · about 3/4 cup (150 g) Japanese short-grain rice, cooked for 2 bowls日本米或寿司米约 3/4 杯（150 克），煮成 2 碗饭
  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 250 g · 250 g boneless skin-on chicken thigh, cut in 2.5 cm pieces去骨带皮鸡腿肉 250 克，切 2.5 厘米块
  • Onions洋葱 120 g · half a large onion, sliced 5 mm thick半个大洋葱，切 5 毫米薄片
  • Eggs鸡蛋 150 g · 3 large eggs3 个大鸡蛋
  • Dashi powder日式鲣鱼高汤粉 3 g · 1 small tsp dashi powder dissolved in 200 mL hot water日式高汤粉 1 小茶匙，兑 200 毫升热水
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1 1/2 tbsp (22 mL)1 又 1/2 汤匙（22 mL）
  • Mirin味啉 33 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 10 g · 1 green onion, sliced (mitsuba in Japan)1 根青葱，切葱花（日本做法用鸭儿芹）

Method做法

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs nearly clear, soak 20 minutes, cook it and keep it warm.米洗到水基本清澈，在水中泡 20 分钟，然后煮成饭，保温。
  2. Dissolve the dashi powder in 200 mL hot water and stir in the soy sauce, mirin and sugar.高汤粉用 200 毫升热水化开，加酱油、味醂和糖，搅匀。
  3. Crack the eggs into a bowl and stir gently with chopsticks about 10 times, leaving some streaks of white.鸡蛋打进碗里，用筷子轻轻搅 10 下，留一点蛋白的纹路。
  4. Bring the seasoned dashi to a boil over medium heat in a 20 cm skillet.20 厘米的小平底锅里倒入调味汁，中火烧开。
  5. Add the onion and simmer 2 minutes until soft.放入洋葱煮 2 分钟，煮到变软。
  6. Add the chicken skin-side down and simmer 5 minutes, flip and cook 2 more minutes until cooked through.鸡肉皮朝下放入，中火煮 5 分钟，翻面再煮 2 分钟，直到完全熟透。
  7. Cut into the thickest piece to check there is no pink, or confirm 74 C (165 F).鸡肉切开最厚的一块确认没有粉红色，或测温到 74°C（165°F）。
  8. Turn the heat to high and pour two-thirds of the egg in a circle.转大火，绕圈淋入三分之二的蛋液。
  9. Cover and cook 40 seconds until the edges of the egg just set.盖盖煮 40 秒，蛋边缘刚凝固。
  10. Pour in the remaining egg, add the green onion, cover and turn off the heat for 30 seconds.淋入剩下的蛋液，撒葱花，盖盖关火，焖 30 秒。
  11. Fill two bowls with hot rice and slide the contents of the pan, juices and all, over the top.两个碗里盛热饭，用锅铲把锅里的料连汁整个滑到饭上。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Pour the egg in two stages; the first sets into body while the second stays soft and silky, so you get two textures in one bite.蛋液分两次淋：先下的一部分煮熟成形，后下的保持半熟滑嫩，一口里有两种口感。
  • Beat the egg only about 10 times; streaks of white give the finished egg texture rather than uniform scrambled egg.蛋液只搅 10 下，不要打匀：保留的蛋白纹路让蛋煮出来有层次，不会变成均匀的炒蛋。
  • Use a small pan so the liquid is shallow and the egg spreads evenly; cooking one portion at a time is the most reliable.用小锅：汁浅，鸡肉和蛋铺得均匀，一次煮一份效果最稳。
  • Cook the chicken through before adding egg; the egg needs only a few seconds, so undercooked chicken leads to overcooked egg.鸡肉先煮熟再放蛋：蛋只需要半熟，鸡肉没熟透就加蛋会煮过头。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The egg is overcooked and rubbery蛋煮老了、变成硬块
    Spoon it over the rice with a little sauce and call it egg rice; next time turn the heat off after the second pour and let the residual heat finish it.无法补救，盖在饭上淋一点汤汁当炒蛋盖饭；下次第二次蛋液入锅后关火焖 30 秒就够。
  • The sauce is too salty or too sweet汤汁太咸或太甜
    If too salty add 2 tbsp hot water; if too sweet add 1/2 tsp soy sauce. Taste the sauce before the chicken goes in.太咸加 2 汤匙热水；太甜加半茶匙酱油。下次放鸡肉前先尝一口调味汁。
  • The chicken is still pink inside鸡肉还有粉红色
    Lift out the egg, simmer the chicken 2 more minutes; next time cut the chicken smaller and more evenly.把蛋液先盛出，鸡肉继续煮 2 分钟；下次鸡肉切得小而均匀。
