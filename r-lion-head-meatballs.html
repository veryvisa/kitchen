Lion's Head Meatballs清炖狮子头

Braised Lion's head meatballs are prized for their cloud-soft texture and clear, sweet cabbage broth. The catch is that lean-only ground pork turns dense and firm, so this recipe combines fatty ground pork with hand-diced pork belly for fat and texture, then adds beaten ginger-scallion water and a bare simmer under cabbage leaves to keep the meatballs tender and intact.清炖狮子头讲究肉质松软、汤色清亮，咬开不柴也不散。难点在于不能只用纯瘦的机器绞肉，要用肥瘦三七开的猪绞肉搭配手切五花肉粒增加脂肪和颗粒感，再分次打水摔打上劲，下锅后全程微火捂熟，这样既抱团又入口即化。

Method做法

  1. Soak 10 g smashed ginger and 2 green onions in 80 mL warm water for 10 minutes, squeeze hard and strain the liquid.10 克拍碎的姜和 2 根葱用 80 毫升温水浸泡 10 分钟，用力抓捏后滤出葱姜水。
  2. Hand-dice the skinless pork belly into 4-5 mm cubes and finely mince the lotus root.五花肉切成 4–5 毫米细粒，莲藕剁成细末。
  3. Combine ground pork, diced pork belly, lotus root, egg, cornstarch, soy sauce, wine, sugar, black pepper, sesame oil and 2/3 tsp salt in a large bowl.大碗里放猪绞肉、五花肉粒、莲藕末、鸡蛋、玉米淀粉、生抽、料酒、糖、黑胡椒、香油和 2/3 茶匙盐拌匀。
  4. Beat in the ginger-scallion water in three additions, stirring briskly in one direction for 3-4 minutes until sticky and cohesive.分三次倒入葱姜水，朝同一方向快速搅打 3–4 分钟，直到肉馅发黏抱团。
  5. Wet your hands, scoop a quarter of the mixture and toss it gently between your palms 10-12 times into a smooth, crack-free ball; make 4 meatballs.双手沾水，取四分之一肉馅在掌心轻柔来回摔打 10–12 下，团成光滑无裂缝的大肉丸，共做 4 个。
  6. Heat a wide pot of water to a bare simmer with tiny bubbles (about 85°C), lower the meatballs in on a large spoon and poach 3 minutes until pale and set.宽口锅烧水到冒细密小泡但未大滚（约 85°C），用大勺托着肉丸轻放入锅，微火定形 3 分钟至表面泛白。
  7. Lift the set meatballs out onto a plate and discard the poaching water.小心捞出肉丸沥水，倒掉焯水的水。
  8. Line a Dutch oven or clay pot with half the napa cabbage leaves, set the meatballs on top and add chicken stock, sliced ginger, scallion knot and the remaining 2/3 tsp salt.砂锅或厚底炖锅底铺一半大白菜叶，摆入 4 个肉丸，倒入清鸡汤、姜片、葱结和剩下 2/3 茶匙盐。
  9. Lay the remaining cabbage leaves directly over the meatballs, bring just to a gentle simmer over medium heat and cover with the lid.用剩下的大白菜叶盖住肉丸表面，中火加热到汤面刚起小泡，盖上锅盖。
  10. Simmer on the lowest heat for 45 minutes, keeping the broth at a quiet tremble, until the center of a meatball reaches 71°C.转最小火慢炖 45 分钟，保持汤面微微颤动但不翻滚，直到肉丸中心温度达到 71°C。
  11. Lift off the top cabbage leaves, tuck the baby bok choy around the edges and simmer uncovered 3 minutes until bright green and tender.揭掉盖在上面的菜叶，在锅边放入小油菜，开盖微火煮 3 分钟至翠绿断生。
  12. Serve the meatballs, braised cabbage, bok choy and clear broth together.连肉丸、白菜、小油菜和清汤一起盛盘上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Mixing hand-diced pork belly into ground pork creates tiny pockets as the fat slowly renders, keeping large meatballs loose and juicy.五花肉手切小粒配猪绞肉，慢炖时肥肉粒慢慢化开，肉丸内部才会留下细小空隙、松软多汁。
  • Stirring in ginger-scallion water in batches in one direction develops the meat's binding texture, and gently tossing it between wet palms helps shape the meatballs so they hold together without deep-frying.分次打入葱姜水并顺一个方向搅打，能让肉馅产生黏性抱团；再用湿手轻摔成形，肉丸不靠油炸也能保持完整。
  • A quick gentle poach removes scum, and covering with cabbage at a bare simmer keeps the broth clear and stops vigorous bubbles from breaking the meatballs.先微水定形能去掉血沫，炖煮时再盖白菜叶、全程不滚锅，汤汁才会清澈甘甜，肉丸也不会被沸水冲散。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The meatballs broke apart in the pot肉丸下锅散开
    The meat was under-mixed or the broth boiled too hard. Stir longer in one direction next time and keep the pot at the gentlest simmer.肉馅没搅打上劲或汤水煮得太滚；下次多朝同一方向搅打 2 分钟，炖煮全程保持水面微颤即可。
  • The meatballs feel dense and dry口感发硬发柴
    Too little fat or liquid was used. Aim for roughly 30 percent fat via pork belly and beat in the full amount of ginger-scallion water.瘦肉比例太高或打水太少；要保证约三成肥肉，并确实分次打入葱姜水。
  • The broth is cloudy and greasy汤很浑浊而且浮油厚
    Skipping the initial poach or boiling rapidly emulsifies scum and fat. Skim surface fat before serving, and poach for 3 minutes first next time.跳过定形焯水或大火猛煮会把肉沫和油脂搅浑；上桌前先撇净表面浮油，下次记得先微火定形 3 分钟。
