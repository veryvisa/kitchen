Cumin Beef孜然牛肉

Cumin beef captures the toasty, peppery spice of Northwest Chinese street barbecue in a weeknight skillet. Two pitfalls trip up home cooks: crowding a lukewarm pan so the beef steams gray and tough, and scorching the ground cumin and chili powder over blasting heat.孜然牛肉讲究肉嫩焦香、孜然和辣椒的干香扑鼻。家里做容易翻车在两处：火不够旺、一次下太多肉把牛肉煮出水变柴，或者旺火直接撒粉把孜然辣椒炒糊发苦。

Method做法

  1. Chill the striploin in the freezer for 15 minutes if soft, then slice across the grain into 3 mm thick strips.牛排若偏软先冷冻 15 分钟定型，再逆着肌肉纹理切成 3 毫米薄片。
  2. Toss the beef with both soy sauces, cooking wine, sugar, salt, cornstarch and 1 tbsp water until absorbed, then coat with 1 tbsp oil and marinate 10 minutes.牛肉加生抽、老抽、料酒、糖、盐、淀粉和 1 汤匙水抓匀至水分吃进肉里，再淋 1 汤匙油拌匀腌 10 分钟。
  3. Lightly crush all the whole cumin seeds with the flat of a knife or in a mortar, then combine with the ground cumin, chili flakes and sesame seeds in a small bowl so they can go in together later.把全部整粒孜然用刀面轻压或略捣碎，和孜然粉、辣椒面、白芝麻一起放进小碗备用，炒的时候一把下锅。
  4. Heat 1.5 tbsp oil in a wok, cast-iron or carbon-steel pan over high heat until shimmering and lightly smoking (if using a non-stick pan, drop to medium-high as soon as the first wisp of smoke appears — it should not be pushed this hot for long).大铁锅或宽平底锅大火烧热 1.5 汤匙油，至油面起波纹并冒出微烟（用不粘锅的话，烧到微微起烟就转中高火，不要一直猛烧，不粘涂层受不了太高的温度）。
  5. Sear the beef in two batches so it sits in a single layer each time: 30 seconds undisturbed until browned underneath, then another 30 seconds tossing until no pink remains on the outside, then remove. Do not add it all at once — a crowded pan steams the beef instead of searing it.牛肉分两批下锅，每批平铺一层，静置煎 30 秒至底面焦香，再快速翻炒 30 秒至表面不再是粉红色，盛出备用；不要一次全下，肉堆太厚会出水变柴。
  6. Add the onion to the pan and stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute until the edges brown, then reduce to medium, add the garlic and ginger for 20 seconds until fragrant, and finally add the green onion for 15 seconds.锅里下洋葱丝，大火翻炒 1 分钟至边缘微焦，转中火放蒜末和姜末炒 20 秒出香，再放葱段炒 15 秒。
  7. Return the beef along with the bowl of cumin, chili and sesame seeds, toss over medium heat for about 20 seconds until the spices are fragrant and the beef's centre is no longer pink (about 63°C).倒回牛肉和备好的孜然辣椒芝麻碗，转中火快速翻匀 20 秒至香料出香、牛肉刚好中心也不再是粉红色（中心约 63°C）。
  8. Turn off the heat, fold in the cilantro for 10 seconds until just wilted, and serve hot.关火放入香菜段，利用余温拌匀 10 秒至香菜刚断生，立刻装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Slicing perpendicular to the grain shortens the muscle fibers so the beef chews tender rather than stringy.牛肉一定要逆纹切，把长纤维切断，炒出来才容易咬断不塞牙。
  • Spread the marinated strips in a smoking-hot pan and let them sear before stirring so moisture flashes off instead of stewing the meat.锅烧热再下肉并铺平先煎定型，这样表面迅速脱水产生焦香，不会积出一锅水把肉煮老。
  • Crushing the cumin seeds releases more aroma than leaving them whole; pairing crushed seeds with ground cumin gives both bursts of toasted flavour and even coverage.孜然粒压碎后比整粒更容易出香，和孜然粉搭配用，爆发香和均匀挂味兼顾。
  • Ground spices scorch in seconds over maximum heat, so drop to medium when tossing them in and pull from the burner promptly.干香料粉耐不住猛火，最后转中火快速翻匀就关火，才不会发黑发苦。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The beef released liquid and turned chewy牛肉炒出一锅水、口感发柴
    The pan was too cool or overcrowded. If it has already flooded, lift the beef out, boil the liquid down over high heat, then return the beef with the spices.火力不够或一次下太多肉；已经出水的话，先把肉盛出，大火把锅里的汁水收干，再回锅和香料拌匀。
  • The cumin and chili powder turned dark and bitter孜然和辣椒面发苦发黑
    The wok was too hot when the dry spices went in; next time lower to medium or turn off the burner before tossing them.干粉下锅时锅温太高，下次关火或转中小火再撒料，靠余温翻匀即可。
  • Spices stuck to the pan instead of coating the beef香料粘在锅底、肉上没味道
    Dry spices bounce off meat that has dried out; next time keep the oil left in the pan after searing instead of wiping it out, so the spices have something to cling to.肉炒得太干时粉料挂不住；下次牛肉盛出后别把锅底的油擦掉，让香料在原来的油里翻匀就能裹住肉。
