Pan-Fried Pork Buns生煎包

Shanghai pan-fried pork buns balance a crisp golden bottom, tender crumb and hot broth inside. Instead of simmering pork skin for hours, a quick gelatin chicken-stock jelly (chilled at least 1 hour, or made the night before) melts inside the filling while steaming. Beating chilled liquid into the pork and controlling the short rise and pan-steaming timing keeps the bottoms crisp and the buns juicy.上海生煎包一口下去底脆、皮软、肉馅带着滚烫汤汁，在家做其实不必熬几个小时猪皮冻。用鸡汤和明胶粉调出快手高汤冻（需冷藏至少 1 小时，也可前一晚做好），再控制半发面时间、肉馅打水和煎锅加水焖煎的火候，就能做出焦底爆汁的生煎。

Ingredients食材

  • All-purpose flour面粉 300 g · about 300 g all-purpose flour (about 2.4 cups; weighing is recommended, or fluff flour before spooning and leveling)中筋面粉约 300 克（约 2.4 杯；建议用秤称量，用杯量时先松散面粉再平刮）
  • Active dry yeast干酵母 3 g · 1 tsp instant or active dry yeast (about 3 g)即发干酵母 1 茶匙（约 3 克）
  • Unflavoured gelatin powder明胶粉 7 g · 1 packet (about 7 g) unflavored gelatin无味明胶粉 1 小包（约 7 克） makes quick broth jelly代替猪皮冻做高汤冻
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 240 g · 1 cup (240 mL) unsalted or low-sodium chicken stock (180 mL for jelly, 60 mL chilled for filling)无盐或低盐鸡汤 1 杯（240 毫升；180 毫升做汤冻，60 毫升冷用打馅）
  • Ground pork猪肉馅 350 g · about 350 g ground pork (around 70/30 lean-to-fat)肥瘦三七开的猪绞肉约 350 克
  • Ginger姜 12 g · 12 g, finely minced (or crushed with green onion in 50 mL cold water and strained)12 克，切极细末（或拍碎与葱加 50 毫升冷水捏出葱姜水滤渣用）
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 40 g · 4 stalks, half for filling and half chopped for garnish4 根，一半拌馅，一半切葱花出锅前撒面
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (18 g)1 汤匙（18 克）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 6 g · 1 tsp (about 6 g)1 茶匙（约 6 克）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 10 g · 2 tsp (about 10 g)2 茶匙（约 10 克）
  • Sugar白糖 10 g · 2.5 tsp (divided between dough and filling)2.5 茶匙（一半和面，一半拌馅）
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp约 2/3 茶匙
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 8 g · 1 tbsp (about 8 g) toasted white sesame seeds熟白芝麻 1 汤匙（约 8 克）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL), divided for two batches2 汤匙（30 mL），分两锅煎包用

Method做法

  1. Sprinkle the gelatin over 60 mL chicken stock for 3 minutes, warm 30 seconds until dissolved, stir in 120 mL cold stock (180 mL total) and chill in a shallow dish for at least 1 hour until firm (or make the night before). Reserve the remaining 60 mL cold stock for the filling.明胶粉撒在 60 毫升鸡汤上静置 3 分钟，微火或微波加热 30 秒融化后拌入 120 毫升冷鸡汤（共 180 毫升），倒入平盘冷藏至少 1 小时至凝固成冻（可前一晚做好）；剩余 60 毫升冷鸡汤留着打馅。
  2. Dissolve the yeast and 5 g sugar in 160 mL lukewarm water (about 38°C), add to the flour and knead 6-8 minutes until smooth.酵母和 5 克糖溶于 160 毫升温水（约 38°C），倒入面粉中揉 6–8 分钟成光滑面团。
  3. Cover the dough and rest at room temperature for 35 minutes until slightly puffed to about 1.5 times its size.面团盖紧保鲜膜或湿布，室温醒发 35 分钟至略微鼓起（约 1.5 倍大，未完全发满）。
  4. In a bowl, mix the ground pork with the salt, soy sauce, dark soy, cooking wine, remaining 5 g sugar and minced ginger, beating in one direction for 2 minutes until sticky. Beat in the reserved 60 mL cold chicken stock in 2-3 additions until absorbed (alternatively, crush the ginger and filling green onions in 50 mL cold water, strain out the solids and beat the infused water into the pork instead of minced ginger). Finally stir in the sesame oil and half the chopped green onions.大碗里放猪绞肉、盐、生抽、老抽、料酒、剩下 5 克糖和姜末，顺一个方向搅打 2 分钟至上劲发黏；再分 2–3 次加入预留的 60 毫升冷高汤搅打至完全吸收（可选：也可用葱和姜加 50 毫升冷水捏出味后滤出葱姜水，分次打入肉馅代替直接拌入姜末），最后拌入香油和一半葱花。
  5. Dice the firm stock jelly into 5 mm cubes without crushing them and fold gently into the pork mixture so each bun gets about 10-12 g of jelly. Cover and refrigerate the filling while you work on the dough.凝固的高汤冻切成 5 毫米小丁（不要切碎），轻拌入肉馅中，确保每个包约能包入 10–12 克汤冻丁。馅料盖好放入冰箱冷藏，同时开始处理醒发好的面团。
  6. (Dough shaping) Knead the rested dough briefly until smooth, roll into a log, divide into 12 pieces (about 38 g each) and keep covered. Roll each into a 9 cm circle thinner at the edge and slightly thicker in the center.【面团整形】取出面团揉光滑后搓成长条，均分 12 个剂子（每个约 38 克），盖保鲜膜防干；逐个擀成中间稍厚、边缘薄的约 9 厘米圆片。
  7. Place about 40 g of filling (including 10-12 g jelly cubes) in each wrapper. Cup it in your left hand and use your right thumb and index finger to pleat 12-18 folds around the rim as you turn, then gather and twist the top tightly shut with no gaps. Set seam-side down, flatten the bottoms gently, cover and rest 10 minutes.每个面皮包入约 40 克馅料（含约 10–12 克汤冻丁），左手托皮，右手拇指和食指边转边沿边缘推出 12–18 个细褶，收拢后拧紧封口不留孔隙；收口朝下摆放并将底部轻轻压平，盖好静置再醒 10 分钟。
  8. Cook in 2 batches of 6 buns: heat 1 tbsp oil in a wide non-stick skillet over medium-low heat and arrange 6 buns seam-side down, spaced 1.5-2 cm apart so they do not stick or tear as they puff.分两锅煎，每锅 6 个：宽底不粘平底锅加 1 汤匙油开中小火，把 6 个生煎包收口朝下摆入锅中，彼此间隔约 1.5–2 厘米，防止焖煎膨胀后粘连撕破底。
  9. Fry over medium-low heat for 2 minutes until the bottoms turn pale golden.中小火干煎 2 分钟，至包子底部变成淡金黄色。
  10. Slowly pour 100-120 mL (about 1/2 cup) of 80-90°C hot water down the side of the pan without splashing the buns (reaching about one-third up their sides), cover tightly at once and cook over medium heat 7-8 minutes until the water is nearly dry.取约 100–120 毫升（约半杯）80–90°C 的热水，沿锅边缓缓倒入（切勿直接淋到包子上，水没过包子约三分之一高），立刻盖紧锅盖，中火焖煎 7–8 分钟至水分将干。
  11. When the water is nearly dry, about 1 minute before uncovering to crisp, lift the lid briefly to sprinkle on half the toasted white sesame seeds and half the remaining green onions, then re-cover for about 1 minute so the steam helps them stick. Once bubbling turns to sharp sizzling, uncover and fry over medium-low heat 1-2 minutes until the bottoms are deep golden-brown and crisp and the pork reaches 71°C inside.水将烧干、开盖煎底前约 1 分钟时，快速揭盖撒上该锅份量（一半）的熟白芝麻和剩下葱花，盖回锅盖借水汽粘住约 1 分钟；待锅里水声变成滋滋油响后彻底开盖，转中小火敞盖煎 1–2 分钟，至底部深金黄酥脆且肉馅中心温度达 71°C。
  12. Turn off the heat, wait 1 minute, lift out carefully with a thin spatula and repeat with the second batch. Serve hot with Chinkiang black vinegar and shredded ginger for dipping; bite a small hole first to release the hot broth before eating.关火静置 1 分钟，用薄铲贴锅底小心铲出，按同样方法煎好第二锅。趁热配镇江香醋和少许姜丝蘸食，先咬小口放出汤汁再吃（小心烫口）。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Use a half-leavened dough (risen to only 1.5 times its size, then rested 10 minutes after shaping) so the skin stays springy instead of turning fluffy like a steamed bao.生煎包讲究半发面，面团发到约 1.5 倍大就包，成形后再醒 10 分钟，外皮才会紧实有韧劲、吸汤不塌，发过头会变成大包子口感。
  • Keep the diced stock jelly cold until wrapping; if it melts on the counter, the wet filling will stop the pleats from sealing.高汤冻要切小丁并全程保持冷藏，拌完立刻包，室温放久化水会让收口黏不住而漏汤。
  • Listen for the sound shift: when bubbling softens and water is nearly gone, sprinkle on the white sesame seeds and green onions and re-cover 1 minute so steam sets them; uncover once you hear sharp sizzling to crisp the bottom.听声音判断撒料与开盖时机：锅里的水声变小、水将烧干时先撒白芝麻和葱花并盖回 1 分钟借水汽粘牢，听到清脆油响再彻底开盖煎出脆壳。
  • Cooking the buns seam-side down turns the pleated seam into a thick, crisp base, while the smooth top holds the sesame and scallions.收口朝下煎能把捏褶处封成厚实的脆底，正面圆润光滑，芝麻和葱花也更容易挂住。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The bottoms scorched, or stayed soft and soggy instead of crisp.底部焦糊发苦，或者底部发软不脆
    Too little water scorches the bottom and too much makes it soggy. Pour about 100-120 mL (1/2 cup) of 80-90°C hot water down the pan side per batch of 6 buns, then finish uncovered for 1-2 minutes to crisp.加水太少会提前烧干焦底，加水太多则发软；每锅 6 个包按约 100–120 毫升（约半杯）80–90°C 热水沿锅边倒入，最后开盖干煎 1–2 分钟收脆。
  • The broth leaked out during cooking包好的生煎出锅漏汤
    Unsealed pleats, thin centers or crowded buns tearing apart cause leaks. Keep wrapper centers thicker, twist pleats tightly shut, and space 6 buns 1.5-2 cm apart per batch.收口没捏紧、面皮中间擀太薄或间距太小粘连拉破；下次面皮中间留厚一点、沿边缘捏褶拧紧，每锅仅放 6 个并留足 1.5–2 厘米间距。
  • The filling is dry with no broth inside里面只有干肉团，没有汤汁
    Too little jelly, jelly cubes crushed too fine, or no liquid beaten into the pork leaves a dry center. Beat cold stock into the pork in batches first, then gently fold in intact 5 mm jelly cubes (about 10-12 g per bun).汤冻量不够或切得太碎被肉馅压烂，且肉馅没打水；每个包约放 10–12 克高汤冻丁（保持 5 毫米完整小丁），并先给肉馅分次打入少量冷高汤。
