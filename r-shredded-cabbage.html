Hand-Torn Cabbage Stir-fry手撕包菜

Tearing the cabbage by hand leaves rough edges that soak up flavor and char nicely. With rendered pork belly, dried chilies and a splash of vinegar, ten minutes gives a crisp, smoky, mildly spicy side dish that disappears fast.用手撕而不是刀切，菜叶的断面粗糙，容易吸味、边缘更容易焦香。再配上煸出油的五花肉片、干辣椒和一圈醋，十分钟就是一盘脆、香、微辣的下饭菜。

18 min total分钟 (10 + 8)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dried chili另购：干辣椒

Ingredients食材

  • Green cabbage包菜 500 g · about half a small head of green cabbage约半个小圆白菜
  • Pork belly五花肉 60 g · about 60 g pork belly, sliced thin五花肉约 60 克，切薄片 bacon works instead可换成培根
  • Dried chili干辣椒 5 g · 5 pieces, snipped5 个，剪成段
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 4 cloves, smashed and sliced4 瓣，拍碎切片
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp半汤匙
  • Rice vinegar米醋 8 g · 1.5 tsp1.5 茶匙
  • Sugar白糖 2 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙
  • Salt盐 1.5 g · about 1/4 tsp约 1/4 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Remove the outer leaves and thick core, and tear the cabbage by hand into pieces about 4 cm across.包菜去掉最外层和硬梗，用手撕成约 4 厘米的块。
  2. Rinse the pieces and drain them thoroughly, since extra water will keep them from crisping.撕好的包菜洗净，彻底沥干水，水分多了会炒不脆。
  3. Slice the pork belly thin, slice the garlic and snip the chilies.五花肉切成薄片，蒜切片，干辣椒剪成段。
  4. Heat a dry wok, add the pork belly over medium heat and cook 2 minutes until the edges curl and the fat renders.锅烧热，不放油，下五花肉片中火煸 2 分钟，到边缘卷起、出油。
  5. Add the tablespoon of oil, then the chilies and garlic, and stir for 15 seconds until fragrant.加 1 汤匙油，放干辣椒和蒜片，炒约 15 秒，香味出来。
  6. Turn the heat to high, add the cabbage and keep tossing for about 1.5 minutes until the edges blister but the leaves are still crisp.转大火，倒入包菜，不停翻炒约 1.5 分钟，边缘微微发焦，菜叶还保持脆。
  7. Drizzle the vinegar down the side of the wok and add the soy sauce, sugar and salt.沿锅边淋入醋，加生抽、糖和盐。
  8. Toss for 30 seconds more and serve.再翻炒 30 秒，出锅装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Tearing breaks along the leaf veins and leaves rough edges that absorb the seasoning and brown better than clean knife cuts.用手撕：叶脉自然断开，断面粗，吸汁、也容易炒出焦边；刀切的口感会齐整但没那个香。
  • Stir-fry hard for only about 2 minutes; longer and the cabbage weeps and turns into a soft stew.大火快炒，只炒 2 分钟；炒久了菜出水，就成了软烂的炖菜。
  • Vinegar poured down the hot side vaporizes and sharpens the dish without tasting sour.醋沿锅边淋入，遇热气化，提鲜又不喧宾夺主。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The cabbage is watery and soft菜出水、发软
    It was not drained, the heat was low or the pan was crowded. Dry the leaves, cook only this much at a time on the highest heat, and let a watery pan reduce for a minute.菜没沥干、火不够大或一次炒太多。擦干菜叶，一次只炒这个量，火开到最大；已出水的就大火再收一下。
  • It tastes flat味道平淡
    The pork was not rendered, the garlic not bloomed or the vinegar skipped. Render the pork, bloom the aromatics and finish with vinegar.肉片没煸香、蒜没爆香，或醋没加。肉要煸出油边，蒜辣椒要炒出香，最后淋醋。
