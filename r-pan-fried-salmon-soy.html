Pan-Seared Salmon with Soy Butter酱油黄油煎三文鱼

This quick dinner, about fifteen minutes start to finish, delivers crisp salmon skin coated in a savory garlic soy-butter glaze that pairs effortlessly with steamed rice or greens. The classic pitfalls are scorching butter at high heat or flipping too soon and tearing the skin. Starting with neutral oil and building the sauce gently at low heat solves both.约十五分钟就能上桌的快手硬菜：先用植物油把鱼皮煎得酥脆，再用黄油、蒜末和生抽淋出浓亮酱汁，配白米饭或烫青菜都下饭。最常出岔子的是全程用黄油猛煎导致焦黑发苦，或者翻面太早把鱼皮粘破；分两步用油并离火下酱汁就能稳稳成功。

15 min total分钟 (5 + 10)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Mirin另购：味啉

Ingredients食材

  • Salmon fillet三文鱼排 340 g · 2 skin-on salmon fillets (about 170 g each, 2.5 cm thick)带皮三文鱼柳 2 块（每块约 170 克，厚约 2.5 厘米）
  • Butter黄油 28 g · 2 tbsp (about 28 g) unsalted butter无盐黄油 2 汤匙（约 28 克）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL) canola oil菜籽油 1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1 1/2 tbsp (22 mL) soy sauce生抽 1.5 汤匙（22 mL）
  • Mirin味啉 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL) mirin味霖 1 汤匙（15 mL） or substitute 1 tbsp cooking wine plus 1 tsp sugar没有味霖可用 1 汤匙料酒加 1 茶匙糖替代
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 2 cloves garlic, smashed and minced蒜 2 瓣，拍扁切末
  • Lemons柠檬 30 g · 1/2 lemon, half juiced and half cut into wedges半个柠檬，一半挤汁、一半切瓣配食
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 1 stalk scallion, thinly sliced1 根小葱，切葱花
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp salt盐约 1/3 茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · about 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper黑胡椒约 1/4 茶匙，现磨

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Let the salmon sit at room temperature for 10-15 minutes, then run a finger along the flesh to check for pin bones and pull out any with tweezers. Pat both sides completely dry with paper towels and season evenly with the salt and black pepper.三文鱼柳提前室温放置 10–15 分钟，用手指顺着鱼肉摸一遍检查细刺，有的话用镊子拔掉；两面用厨房纸彻底按干，均匀抹上盐和黑胡椒。
  2. Heat the canola oil in a stainless or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.平底锅或铸铁锅放菜籽油，中大火烧到油面微起波纹。
  3. Lay the fillets in skin-side down, press gently with a spatula for 10 seconds to keep the skin flat, and sear 4 minutes until crisp and opaque color climbs halfway up the sides.鱼皮朝下入锅，用锅铲轻轻按压 10 秒防卷曲，中大火煎 4 分钟到鱼皮酥脆、侧面变白熟度升到一半。
  4. Flip the fillets, lower the heat to medium-low, and cook about 2-3 minutes (roughly what a 2.5 cm fillet needs); confirm with a thermometer that the thickest center reaches 70°C and the fish flakes easily with a fork.翻面转中小火再煎约 2–3 分钟（2.5 厘米厚的鱼柳大致需要这个时间），用温度计确认最厚处中心温度达到 70°C、鱼肉用叉子轻拨能成瓣散开。
  5. Transfer the salmon skin-side up to a warm plate (skin-side down lets steam from the plate soften it), and pull the skillet off the heat for 15 seconds to cool slightly.把鱼柳鱼皮朝上盛到温热的盘里（鱼皮朝下会被盘里的蒸汽闷软），锅端离火源静置 15 秒略降温。
  6. Return the pan to low heat, let the butter melt fully until it just starts to bubble, then add the garlic and stir for 10-15 seconds until fragrant, without browning.把锅放回小火，先把黄油完全融化冒小泡后再下蒜末，炒 10–15 秒出香，不要炒焦。
  7. Add the soy sauce, mirin, and juice from half the lemon piece, swirling 30 seconds until glossy and slightly thickened.倒入生抽、味霖和半个柠檬的半边汁，晃锅煮 30 秒到酱汁微稠发亮。
  8. Spoon the soy-butter sauce over the salmon, scatter with sliced scallions, and serve with the remaining lemon wedges.把酱油黄油汁淋在鱼块上，撒葱花并配剩下的柠檬瓣上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Sear in neutral canola oil first and save the butter for the sauce, because butter milk solids burn bitter during a 4-minute high-heat sear.煎鱼第一步只用高烟点的菜籽油而不用黄油，因为黄油乳固体在高温煎鱼的 4 分钟里一定会焦黑发苦。
  • Drying the skin thoroughly and pressing it flat for 10 seconds ensures full pan contact for crisp, even skin.下锅前把鱼皮表面擦到完全干爽，再按压 10 秒，鱼皮才能贴紧锅面煎脆而不回缩。
  • Pull the fillets as soon as the center hits 70°C; residual heat will carry it a little higher as it rests. Letting the pan cool briefly before adding the butter and garlic also helps them not scorch.鱼一到中心 70°C 就盛出，静置期间余温还会再升一点；锅稍降温再下黄油蒜末，也更不容易糊锅。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The skin stuck to the pan and tore鱼皮粘锅撕破、翻面散架
    Wait until the oil is shimmering and the skin releases naturally after about 4 minutes before flipping; if it tore, slide a thin spatula underneath and cover with the glaze.下次务必等油烧热且鱼皮煎满 4 分钟自然脱离锅底再翻；已经粘破时用薄铲贴锅底铲起，鱼肉照样好吃，淋酱汁能遮住破口。
  • The butter and garlic burned dark and bitter黄油和蒜末发黑发苦
    Never drop butter and garlic into a roaring-hot skillet; if they scorch, wipe out the pan and rebuild the sauce over low heat with fresh butter.锅温过高时不要直接下黄油和蒜；若是刚焦掉，倒掉焦黑底油擦净锅，用新黄油重新小火煮酱汁。
  • The sauce turned too salty or separated酱汁太咸或油水分离
    Whisk in 1 tbsp hot water off the heat to dilute the salt and bring the emulsion back together. Next time season the fish more lightly and start the sauce with just 1 tbsp soy sauce, adding more to taste.加 1 汤匙热水进锅里大力晃匀就能乳化并冲淡咸度；下次鱼身少撒点盐，酱汁里先放 1 汤匙生抽，尝过再按需补。
