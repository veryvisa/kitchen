Dan Dan Noodles担担面

Sichuan Dan Dan noodles build big flavor in the bowl from aromatic chili oil, rich sesame paste and crisp savory pork with preserved mustard greens. The keys are frying the pork dry and crumbly, thinning the sesame paste smoothly and tossing the hot noodles right before eating.正宗川味担担面靠碗底的红油、芝麻酱与芽菜肉臊撑起层次，红油香辣、麻酱浓香、微麻回甜。难点在于肉臊要煸到干香酥松、芝麻酱澥得顺滑不结块，煮好面趁热拌匀立刻吃。

Method做法

  1. Toast the Sichuan peppercorns in a dry pan over low heat for about 1 minute until fragrant, then grind to a powder.花椒放进干锅小火焙约 1 分钟至出麻香，盛出碾成细粉。
  2. Put the chili powder and sesame seeds in a heatproof bowl. Heat 3 tbsp (45 mL) oil with the scallion whites over medium heat until fragrant, remove the scallions, and bring the oil to about 160°C (a pinch of chili powder should sizzle gently without scorching). Remove from heat, wait 10 seconds, pour half over the chili mixture and stir, then pour in the rest.辣椒面和白芝麻放进耐热小碗。小锅中火烧热 3 汤匙（45 mL）油并下葱白段炸香后捞出，油温升至约 160°C（撒一小撮辣椒面能温和起泡而不发焦），离火静置 10 秒，先泼一半油进碗拌匀，再泼入另一半成红油。
  3. Divide the sesame paste evenly between two serving bowls and whisk in 2 tbsp hot stock per bowl over 2-3 additions, stirring steadily until smooth and pourable.把芝麻酱平均分进两个面碗，每碗分两三次共加入 2 汤匙热鸡汤，用筷子持续搅拌至顺滑呈流质。
  4. Divide 1 tbsp soy sauce, the black vinegar, minced garlic, sugar, sesame oil, ground Sichuan pepper and the fresh chili oil (including a little of its sediment) evenly into the bowls and stir.往两个碗里平均加入 1 汤匙生抽、镇江香醋、蒜末、糖、香油、花椒粉和刚泼好的红油（连同少许辣椒渣），拌匀备用。
  5. Set a large pot of water over high heat to boil while cooking the pork. Heat 1 tsp oil in a wok over medium-high heat, add the ground pork and a pinch of salt, press and break it up, and fry 6-8 minutes until the moisture evaporates, the fat renders and the pork is golden and crumbly (internal temperature at least 71°C).先另起一锅足量清水大火加热备用。炒锅烧热 1 茶匙油，中高火下猪绞肉并撒少许盐，边按压边炒散，煸炒 6–8 分钟至水分收干、油脂析出、肉末金黄酥松（中心温度达 71°C 以上）。
  6. Push the crisp pork to the side, lower the heat to medium-low, and fry the sweet bean sauce in the rendered fat for 20-30 seconds until fragrant. Add the cooking wine and let it evaporate, then add the minced ginger, yacai, remaining 1/2 tbsp soy sauce and dark soy, stir-frying with the pork for 1-1.5 minutes until fragrant and glossy.把炒酥的肉末推到锅边，转中小火，在析出的猪油里下甜面酱炒 20–30 秒至出酱香，烹入料酒炒至酒气挥发，再下姜末、碎米芽菜、剩余半汤匙生抽和老抽，与肉末翻炒 1–1.5 分钟至芽菜出香、肉臊油亮。
  7. Once the pork topping is nearly done, cook the noodles in the boiling water until slightly firm (3-4 minutes for dried thin noodles, 1.5-2 minutes for fresh thin noodles, following package directions). Add the bok choy for the last 30-45 seconds, then drain very well so excess water does not dilute the sauce.肉臊快炒好时水已沸腾，下面条煮至中心略带咬劲（干细面约 3–4 分钟，鲜细面约 1.5–2 分钟，具体按包装说明），最后 30–45 秒下小油菜同焯，捞出彻底沥干水分。
  8. Transfer the well-drained noodles and bok choy into the sauced bowls, spoon over the pork and yacai, top with crushed unsalted peanuts and scallion greens, and toss thoroughly while hot. Splash in 1-2 tbsp hot noodle water if the noodles look dry, then taste and adjust with a pinch of salt or soy sauce.把沥干的面条和油菜放进调好底料的碗里，浇上芽菜肉臊，撒无盐花生碎和葱花，趁热拌匀；若觉得偏干可淋 1–2 汤匙热煮面水拌开，再尝味按需加少许盐或生抽调整。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Fry the ground pork until all moisture evaporates; wet pork stays soft and muted instead of giving that crisp, nutty contrast.猪绞肉一定要多煸几分钟把水分逼干，水汽没走净的肉臊软塌不香，也撑不起干拌面的口感。
  • Sesame paste seizes if you dump cold water in all at once; whisking in warm stock in small additions turns it silky.芝麻酱遇冷或一次加太多水会「澥不开」反而更稠，用温热鸡汤分次慢慢澥开最顺滑。
  • Traditional Dan Dan noodles are saucy rather than soupy; the hot noodles coat smoothly in the paste and chili oil without flooding the bowl.老派担担面偏重碗底干拌，热面条靠自身余温裹住芝麻酱和红油，拌匀前不要急着加太多汤。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The sesame paste seized into thick clumps that will not coat the noodles芝麻酱结成厚块，怎么拌都拌不开
    Thin the paste with about 1 tbsp of hot liquid at a time, stirring steadily between additions; if already clumped on the noodles, splash in 1-2 tbsp hot cooking water and toss.每次只加约 1 汤匙热鸡汤或热面汤，用筷子持续搅拌均匀再加下一次；如果已经拌进面里结块，淋 1–2 汤匙热面汤趁热抖散。
  • The pork topping tastes flat or soggy肉臊寡淡或软塌不香酥
    Avoid ultra-lean ground pork, and fry over medium to medium-high heat until the moisture evaporates and the pork sizzles in clear oil before adding sweet bean sauce, wine and yacai.猪绞肉别选太瘦的里脊肉末，下锅后用中火到中高火耐心煸炒，直到水汽收干、肉末在清油中滋滋作响再下甜面酱、料酒和芽菜。
  • The yacai made the whole bowl too salty芽菜太咸，整碗面齁咸
    Preserved yacai varies in saltiness by brand; taste a pinch first and rinse and squeeze dry quickly if it is heavily salted.不同牌子的碎米芽菜咸度差别很大，下锅前可抓一小撮尝下，偏咸就快速过一遍清水再挤干炒。
