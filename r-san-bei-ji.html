Taiwanese Three-Cup Chicken三杯鸡

Three-cup chicken is braised in equal-ish parts sesame oil, soy sauce and rice wine, so the meat carries a glossy, savory sauce with a whiff of basil. At home, fry the ginger and garlic over low heat first, keep skin-on thigh meat, and add the basil off the heat so its aroma does not boil away.三杯鸡以麻油、酱油、米酒各一杯焖烧，鸡肉裹着浓稠发亮的酱汁，带一点九层塔的清香。家里做的关键是姜片和蒜粒先用小火逼出香气，鸡块选带皮鸡腿肉，最后才放九层塔，避免香气挥发。

35 min total分钟 (10 + 25)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, Star anise, Fresh basil另购：老抽、料酒(绍兴)、八角、九层塔/罗勒

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 500 g · 2-3 skin-on thighs, cut into 4 cm pieces带皮鸡腿 2–3 只，剁成约 4 厘米块
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 36 g · about 2.5 tbsp (35 mL)约 2.5 汤匙（35 mL）
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp (8 mL), for colour半汤匙（8 mL），上色用
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 60 g · 4 tbsp (60 mL)4 汤匙（60 mL）
  • Ginger姜 20 g · about 6 thin slices约 6 片，切薄片
  • Garlic大蒜 30 g · 6 cloves, whole6 瓣，整粒不去膜
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 2 stalks, in pieces2 根，切段
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Star anise八角 2 g · 1 star anise1 颗
  • Fresh basil九层塔/罗勒 15 g · 1 small bunch Thai basil, leaves picked九层塔 1 小把，摘叶
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · a pinch少许

Method做法

  1. Rinse the chicken pieces twice under cold water and pat the surface dry.鸡块用冷水冲洗两遍，沥干表面水分。
  2. Slice the ginger thinly, keep the garlic whole, cut the scallion into pieces and strip the basil leaves from tough stems.姜切薄片，蒜保留整粒，葱切段，九层塔摘去老梗。
  3. Heat the sesame oil in a pan over low heat and fry the ginger until its edges curl and it smells fragrant, about 2 minutes.锅中下麻油，开小火，放入姜片慢慢煎到边缘微卷、透出香气，约 2 分钟。
  4. Add the whole garlic cloves and stir over low heat for 30 seconds until lightly coloured.加入整粒蒜瓣，小火翻炒 30 秒到蒜皮微微上色。
  5. Turn to medium heat, lay the chicken skin-side down in a single layer and leave it undisturbed for about 2 minutes, then stir until the surface changes colour and the skin turns golden, about 4 minutes in all.转中火，鸡块皮朝下平铺入锅，不要急着翻动，煎约 2 分钟，再翻炒到表面变色、鸡皮微黄，共约 4 分钟。
  6. Pour in the cooking wine and let it bubble for 30 seconds to drive off the sharp alcohol.淋入料酒，煮 30 秒让酒味散开。
  7. Add the light and dark soy sauce, sugar, star anise and pepper, and toss until the sauce coats every piece.加入生抽、老抽、糖、八角和少许黑胡椒，翻炒均匀，让酱汁裹住鸡块。
  8. Cover, lower to medium-low and braise 12 minutes until the chicken reaches 74°C internally.盖上锅盖，转中小火焖 12 分钟，直到鸡块内部达到 74°C。
  9. Uncover, raise to medium-high and reduce the sauce, swirling or stirring constantly so it does not scorch, for 2-3 minutes until about 2 tbsp of liquid remains, thick, glossy and clinging to the meat.开盖转中大火收汁，不停晃锅或翻动防止糊底，煮 2–3 分钟到汤汁剩约 2 汤匙、浓稠发亮能挂在肉块上。
  10. Turn off the heat, fold in the basil leaves and scallion with the residual warmth, then serve at once.关火，放入九层塔叶和葱段，用锅铲借余温翻匀，即刻装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Fry the ginger and sesame oil low and slow; a hot pan makes the oil bitter and the ginger burn black.麻油和姜片必须小火慢煎，油温过高麻油会发苦，姜片也会焦黑。
  • Skin-on thigh stays juicy through the braise, while breast dries out and does not work well here; bone-in thighs, if you can get them, soak up even more flavour.带皮鸡腿肉焖后仍然软嫩，鸡胸肉容易柴，不适合这道菜；有条件用带骨鸡腿更入味。
  • Add basil only after turning off the heat; long heat kills its aroma and turns the leaves dull.九层塔在关火后再下，高温久煮会把罗勒香气煮散，颜色也会发黄。
  • A little more wine than soy keeps the balance; otherwise the dish reads only salty and savoury.酒的比例略高于酱油，酸甜咸的平衡才不会只剩咸和酱香。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The sesame oil tastes bitter麻油吃起来发苦
    Start the ginger in cool oil over low heat next time. If it is already bitter, remove the burnt ginger, discard the excess sauce and add a little fresh sesame oil and sugar to balance.下次先冷油下姜片，全程小火；已经发苦就挑出焦姜片，多余酱汁倒掉，补一点新麻油和糖中和。
  • The chicken is dry鸡肉发柴
    This usually means breast was used, or the covered braise ran longer than 12 minutes. Use skin-on thighs next time and do not extend the braising time.大概率用了鸡胸，或者盖盖焖的时间超过了 12 分钟；下次换带皮鸡腿，焖煮时间不要拖长。
  • The sauce boiled away汤汁烧干
    Keep the lid on tight and braise over medium-low heat throughout. Stay by the pan during the reduction, stirring constantly, and turn off the heat as soon as about 2 tbsp of sauce remains — do not let it reduce all the way dry.全程盖紧锅盖、中小火焖煮；收汁阶段守在锅边不停翻动，看到汤汁剩约 2 汤匙就立刻关火，别等到完全收干。
  • It is too salty太咸
    Use less light soy (about 1.5 tbsp) next time and keep the dark soy only for colour, not extra saltiness. To rescue an oversalted batch, splash in 2-3 tbsp hot water with half a teaspoon of sugar and simmer a minute, then serve with rice or plain greens to balance it.下次生抽减到 1.5 汤匙，老抽只用来上色，不额外加盐；已经偏咸时沿锅边加 2–3 汤匙热水，配半茶匙糖再烧 1 分钟稀释，配白饭或青菜食用。
