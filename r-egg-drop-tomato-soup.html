Tomato Egg Drop Soup番茄蛋花汤

A quick weeknight soup, ready in about 15 minutes, that pairs bright, savoury-sweet tomato broth with silky ribbons of egg. The secret is frying the tomatoes until saucy, thickening lightly with starch, and streaming in beaten egg only after turning the boil down.十几分钟能端上桌的家常快手汤，酸甜开胃、蛋花轻盈如云朵。关键在于番茄要炒出红沙浓汁，勾薄芡后再沿锅边细流淋蛋液，蛋花才不会散成浑汤。

17 min total分钟 (5 + 12)2 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Tomatoes西红柿 350 g · 2 ripe medium tomatoes (about 350 g), cored and chopped2 个中等熟透番茄（约 350 克），去蒂切小块
  • Eggs鸡蛋 150 g · 3 large eggs, beaten smooth with 1 tbsp water stirred in3 个大鸡蛋，打散成蛋液，加 1 汤匙清水拌匀
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 1 stalk, chopped with white and green parts separated1 根，切葱花（葱白葱绿分开）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 3 g · 1 tsp, mixed with 1 tbsp water into a slurry1 茶匙，加 1 汤匙清水调成水淀粉
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp约 2/3 茶匙
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp, to balance acidity1 茶匙，中和酸味
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 2 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙
  • Water水 750 g · 750 mL750 毫升

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Beat the eggs with a pinch of the salt and 1 tbsp water until smooth.鸡蛋加少许盐和 1 汤匙清水打匀至顺滑。
  2. Heat the oil in a soup pot over medium heat and fry the scallion whites for 15 seconds until fragrant.汤锅放油中火烧热，下葱白爆香 15 秒。
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes with a pinch of salt and stir over medium heat for 3-4 minutes, pressing with the spatula, until the tomatoes collapse and release a red, oily juice.倒入番茄块并加少许盐，中火翻炒 3–4 分钟，边炒边用铲压碎，至番茄塌软出红油红汁。
  4. Pour in 750 mL hot water with the sugar and remaining salt, bring to a boil, and simmer over medium-low heat for 4-5 minutes so the tomato flavour infuses the broth.倒入 750 毫升热水，加糖和剩下的盐，大火烧开后转中小火煮 4–5 分钟让番茄味融进汤里。
  5. Mix the cornstarch with 1 tbsp water into a slurry, stir it well, then pour it in slowly while stirring for about 10-15 seconds until the broth turns lightly glossy and thickened.玉米淀粉加 1 汤匙清水调成水淀粉，搅匀后缓缓倒入锅中，边倒边搅约 10–15 秒至汤汁微微变稠。
  6. Keep the soup at a bare simmer (small bubbles at the edge, not a hard boil), drizzle the beaten egg in a thin circular stream, wait 10 seconds before nudging gently so the ribbons set, and cook until the egg is fully opaque and set, not translucent.保持汤面微沸（不是猛滚），绕圈细流淋入蛋液，静置 10 秒等蛋花凝成薄片再轻推，煮到蛋花完全凝固变白、不再透明。
  7. Turn off the heat, drizzle in the sesame oil, scatter on the scallion greens, and serve.关火淋入香油，撒上葱绿即可出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Stir-frying the tomatoes in oil with a pinch of salt draws out their juice and colour, so the broth tastes rich rather than watery.先用油炒番茄并加少许盐能把汁水和颜色逼出来，汤才浓郁有层次，直接水煮汤色寡淡、酸味生硬。
  • A light cornstarch slurry gives the broth just enough body for the egg ribbons to spread into even sheets; too much starch makes the soup gluey.淋蛋前先勾一点薄芡，汤稍微有点稠度能让蛋丝均匀铺开成薄片，淀粉太多反而会让汤变得黏糊。
  • Never pour egg into a hard boil, because vigorous bubbles shred it into cloudy fragments.蛋液不能在大火猛滚时倒，大沸泡会把蛋花冲成细碎浑浊的蛋渣。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The egg broke into tiny bits and clouded the soup蛋花碎成渣、汤变浑浊
    The broth was boiling too hard or stirred right away; next time turn down to a gentle simmer, stream the egg slowly, and wait 10 seconds before nudging it.通常是因为淋蛋时火太猛或立刻大力搅拌；下次关到微沸、细流慢淋并等 10 秒定形后再轻搅。
  • The soup tastes too sour汤喝起来太酸
    The tomatoes were not ripe enough; stir in another 1/2 tsp sugar and simmer 1 minute, and pick softer, riper tomatoes next time.番茄不够熟；现在补半茶匙糖再煮 1 分钟，下次选捏起来微软的熟番茄。
  • The soup tastes bland汤喝起来寡淡没味道
    Not enough salt, or the tomatoes were not cooked down enough; add salt a little at a time, tasting after each addition, and simmer the tomatoes a bit longer next time.盐放少了或番茄没炒透；少量多次补盐并每次尝一口，再多炒一会番茄能让味道更浓。
