Teriyaki Chicken Bento照烧鸡腿便当

Golden-seared chicken thighs get a glossy glaze of soy, mirin and sugar, with rice and vegetables alongside. Cook the chicken through before reducing the glaze so its sugar does not scorch.鸡腿排煎出金黄表皮，再用酱油、味醂和糖收成亮亮的照烧汁，配米饭和蔬菜就是一盒便当。火候的关键是先煎熟鸡肉，再把酱汁收浓，避免糖分过早烧焦。

37 min total分钟 (12 + 25)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Mirin另购：味啉

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 400 g · 2 boneless, skin-on chicken thighs去骨带皮鸡腿肉 2 块
  • Short-grain rice短粒米(寿司米) 150 g · 150 g dry Japanese short-grain rice, about 3/4 cup日本短粒米 150 克，约 3/4 杯
  • Broccoli西兰花 250 g · about 1/2 a head, cut into florets约 1/2 棵，切小朵
  • Carrots胡萝卜 100 g · 1 carrot, thinly sliced1 根，切薄片
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Mirin味啉 45 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 12 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Rinse the rice and cook it with water according to the rice cooker's markings, about 20 minutes.淘洗大米，按电饭锅刻度加水煮熟，约 20 分钟。
  2. Pat the chicken thighs dry and trim them to an even thickness.将鸡腿肉擦干，两面修整至厚薄大致均匀。
  3. Stir the soy sauce, mirin and sugar together in a bowl.把生抽、味醂和糖放入碗中搅匀。
  4. Bring water to a boil in a steamer, add the broccoli and carrot, and steam for 5 minutes until bright and just tender.蒸锅水烧开后放入西兰花和胡萝卜，蒸 5 分钟至颜色鲜亮、刚刚变软。
  5. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat, lay in the chicken skin-side down and sear for 5 minutes until the skin is golden.平底锅中火烧热油，鸡腿肉鸡皮朝下平铺下锅，煎 5 分钟至鸡皮金黄。
  6. Turn the chicken over, reduce the heat to medium-low and cook covered for 5 minutes.翻面后转中小火，盖锅煎 5 分钟。
  7. Check the thickest part with a thermometer; if it has not reached 74°C, cover and continue cooking over medium-low heat, rechecking until it reaches 74°C before transferring to a plate to rest for 3 minutes.用温度计测量最厚处；如果尚未达到 74°C，继续加盖以中小火加热，每次检查确认达到 74°C 后再盛出静置 3 分钟。
  8. Before adding the sauce, check the fat in the skillet and pour off any excess chicken fat if needed, leaving just a thin film of oil and the browned bits; pour in the sauce and simmer over medium heat for 2 minutes until slightly thickened and gently bubbling.倒入酱汁前先检查锅中油脂，必要时倒掉多余鸡油，只保留少量锅底油和煎出的焦香物，再倒入调好的酱汁，中火煮 2 分钟至略微浓稠、起细泡。
  9. Slice the chicken, return it to the pan and turn the pieces in the glaze for about 30 seconds.鸡腿肉切成条，放回锅中翻面裹匀酱汁约 30 秒。
  10. Portion the rice, chicken and vegetables into shallow containers to help them cool quickly; serve hot right away, or refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking and carry in an insulated lunch bag with ice packs to keep the food at 4°C or below.将米饭、鸡肉和蔬菜分装到浅便当盒中帮助快速散热，热食立即享用；如需带走，煮熟后两小时内冷藏，携带时使用保冷午餐袋和冰袋，将食物保持在 4°C 或以下。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Dry the chicken before searing so its surface browns instead of steaming.鸡腿肉擦干后再煎，表面水分少才容易煎出金黄色。
  • Even out the chicken so thin sections do not dry out before the thick sections cook through.鸡肉尽量厚薄一致，薄处不容易先变干，厚处也能及时熟透。
  • Teriyaki sauce contains sugar, so reduce it after the chicken cooks and keep it at a gentle bubble.照烧汁含糖，鸡肉熟后再收汁，锅里持续轻轻冒泡即可。
  • Steam the vegetables just until tender; overcooking can make them watery in the lunchbox.便当配菜蒸到刚软即可，过熟会出水并影响装盒口感。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The glaze tastes bitter or looks scorched酱汁发苦或焦黑
    Do not boil the sugary sauce hard for long; cook the chicken first and reduce the glaze afterward. Discard and remake a bitter sauce.糖汁不要大火久煮；下次先煎熟鸡肉再收汁，已发苦的酱汁需倒掉重调。
  • The chicken is browned outside but undercooked inside鸡肉外焦里面还生
    Cover and continue cooking over medium-low heat, checking that the thickest part reaches 74°C; even out the thighs next time.转中小火加盖继续煎，并测最厚处是否达到 74°C；下次把鸡肉修整至厚薄均匀。
  • The chicken is dry and tough鸡肉煎得又干又柴
    Remove the chicken as soon as it reaches a safe temperature instead of leaving it in the pan while reducing the glaze.煎到安全温度后及时离火，避免为了收汁而让鸡肉一直留在锅里。
