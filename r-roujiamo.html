Roujiamo Chinese Burger肉夹馍

Roujiamo is the classic northwest Chinese street staple: slow-braised pork belly is chopped fine and stuffed into a dry-griddled flatbread, with meat aroma and braising juice in every bite. At home the meat must be tender and deeply seasoned, the dough well rested and griddled until puffed, and a spoonful of braising juice kept for the filling.肉夹馍是西北街头的经典主食：卤到软糯的五花肉剁碎，夹进干烙的热饼里，咬开有肉香也有卤汁。家里做的关键是肉要卤得酥烂入味，饼要醒透、干烙鼓起，夹馅时保留一勺卤汤。

Method做法

  1. Cut the pork belly into 8 cm strips, put them in cold water with 2 slices of ginger and 1 tbsp wine, and bring to a boil over high heat.五花肉切成约 8 厘米宽的条，冷水下锅，加 2 片姜和 1 汤匙料酒，大火烧开。
  2. Skim off the scum, cook 3 minutes, then lift the pork out and rinse with warm water; boil a kettle of water to have ready, since the braise needs boiling water, not cold, or the meat will seize up and turn tough.撇净浮沫，煮 3 分钟，捞出用温水冲净沥干；另烧一壶开水备用，炖肉要用开水不要用冷水，否则肉会收缩变柴。
  3. Place the pork in a braising pot with the green onion, remaining ginger, garlic, star anise, bay leaves, five-spice powder, soy sauce, dark soy, sugar and the boiling water you just made, enough to cover the pork.卤锅放入肉块，加葱段、剩余姜片、蒜瓣、八角、香叶、五香粉、生抽、老抽、糖和刚烧好的开水，没过肉面。
  4. Bring to a boil, turn to low, cover and braise 80-90 minutes until a chopstick slides easily through the skin; add the salt in the last 10 minutes.大火烧开后转小火，盖盖炖 80 到 90 分钟，直到筷子能轻松扎透肉皮；最后 10 分钟加盐。
  5. While the pork braises, mix the flour and salt, add warm water gradually and knead into a smooth, soft dough; cover and rest 20 minutes.炖肉时和面：面粉加盐，分次加入温水，揉成光滑偏软的面团，盖好醒 20 分钟。
  6. Divide the dough into 8 pieces and roll each into a flat round about 12 cm across.面团分成 8 个剂子，逐个擀成直径约 12 厘米的圆饼。
  7. Heat a dry pan over medium-low, add the rounds and cook 3-4 minutes per side until puffed with brown spots; stack and keep warm.平底锅中小火烧热，不放油，放入饼坯，每面烙 3 到 4 分钟，鼓起、两面出现焦斑后叠放保温。
  8. Turn off the heat and let the pork rest in the braising liquid for 10 minutes, then lift it out, chop it into roughly 5 mm pieces, and mix in 2-3 tbsp braising liquid with the chopped cilantro.关火后让肉块在卤汤里浸 10 分钟，捞出稍沥，剁成约 5 毫米的小粒，拌入 2–3 汤匙卤汁和香菜碎。
  9. Split each round open about two-thirds from one side, stuff in the chopped pork and spoon in a little braising juice.饼从侧面剖开三分之二，填入肉末，浇一勺卤汁即成。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Skin-on belly gives melted fat that keeps the chopped filling moist; whole pork is safe once the internal temperature reaches 71°C.选带皮五花肉，卤后油脂融出，剁馅润而不柴；整块猪肉中心温度达到 71°C 即为安全。
  • Leftover pork and braising liquid keep refrigerated for up to 3 days, or frozen for up to 1 month; bring it back to a boil before eating again and confirm the pork reaches 74°C at the centre before stuffing it into a bun.剩下的肉连卤汤冷藏不超过 3 天，冷冻可存 1 个月；再吃前把卤汤连肉烧开，确认肉中心重新达到 74°C 再夹馍。
  • Keep the braise at a gentle simmer; a rolling boil breaks the meat and clouds the broth. Enough low heat makes the skin meltingly tender.卤肉保持小火微沸，大火会把肉煮散、汤变浑；时间足够长，皮才会软糯粘唇。
  • Griddle the rounds dry without oil; a rested dough rolls easily and puffs on the pan. Stuff them while warm, because they stiffen as they cool.饼干烙不刷油，面团醒透才擀得开、烙时会鼓；饼趁热夹馅，放凉后会发硬。
  • The juiciness comes from the braising broth, so keep some juice when chopping instead of adding plain water.肉夹馍的汁来自卤汤，剁肉时要保留少量卤汁，而不是额外加水。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The filling is dry and tough肉馅发干发柴
    Lean meat dries out; use skin-on belly and rest it in the broth 10 minutes before chopping. If it is already dry, simmer it back in the broth for 5 minutes.偏瘦的肉卤后容易柴，下次用带皮五花肉，炖好后留在卤汤里浸 10 分钟再剁；已经偏柴可回卤汤煮 5 分钟。
  • The buns are hard and do not puff饼硬、烙时不鼓
    Usually too little water or not enough resting. Keep the dough soft, rest it at least 20 minutes, set the shape over medium heat then finish on low.多是水加得少或醒面不足；面团应偏软，醒面不少于 20 分钟，先中火定型再转小火烙透。
  • The braise tastes bitter or looks dark卤味发苦或颜色发黑
    Too much spice or dark soy; cut back on the star anise, bay leaves and dark soy next time, and rely on a small amount for colour.香料或老抽放多了；下次减少八角、香叶和老抽，酱色由少量老抽即可调出。
