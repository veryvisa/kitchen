Pork and Napa Cabbage Dumplings猪肉白菜水饺

The pot of dumplings northern families make for New Year, the winter solstice or any weekend. Mixing dough, seasoning filling, rolling and folding is best done around the table together. The secret of pork and napa filling is the cabbage water: squeeze it all out and the filling is dry, leave it in and it floods; coating the cabbage in oil first is the fix.北方人过年、过冬至、过周末都离不开的一锅饺子。和面、调馅、擀皮、包，一家人围着桌子一起做最有意思。白菜猪肉馅的关键是白菜的水：挤干会柴，不挤会出汤，正确做法是先用油封住白菜。

Ingredients食材

  • All-purpose flour面粉 500 g · 4 cups (500 g) all-purpose flour中筋面粉 4 杯（500 克）
  • Ground pork猪肉馅 500 g · 500 g ground pork (about 30% fat)猪肉馅 500 克（三成肥）
  • Napa cabbage大白菜 600 g · half a napa cabbage (about 600 g), finely chopped大白菜半棵（约 600 克），剁碎
  • Ginger姜 10 g · a knob, minced1 块，切末
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks, minced3 根，切末
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1.5 tbsp1.5 汤匙
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (optional)1 汤匙（可不放）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 14 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp, for the cabbage1 汤匙，拌白菜用
  • Salt盐 7 g · about 1 tsp (1/4 tsp in the dough, the rest in the filling)约 1 茶匙（和面 1/4 茶匙，其余调馅）
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · a pinch (white pepper is traditional)少许（传统用白胡椒）

Method做法

  1. Add the 1/4 tsp salt to the flour and pour in about 250 mL cold water a little at a time, stirring to shaggy flakes.面粉加 1/4 茶匙盐，分次倒入约 250 mL 冷水，搅成棉絮状。
  2. Knead into a fairly firm dough, cover and rest 30 minutes.揉成稍硬的面团，盖上醒 30 分钟。
  3. Season the pork with the soy, wine, ginger, salt and pepper, then beat in 60 mL water in three parts in one direction until sticky.肉馅加生抽、料酒、姜末、盐和胡椒，分三次加 60 mL 水，朝一个方向搅到起胶。
  4. Toss the chopped cabbage with the canola and sesame oils first.剁碎的白菜先拌上菜籽油和香油。
  5. Mix the cabbage and green onion into the pork and start wrapping right away.把白菜和葱末倒进肉馅拌匀，马上包，不要放久。
  6. Knead the dough 2 more minutes, roll into ropes and cut into pieces of about 10 g.面团再揉 2 分钟，搓成长条，切成约 10 克一个的剂子。
  7. Flatten each piece and roll it into a round that is thicker in the middle and thin at the edge.剂子按扁，擀成中间厚、边缘薄的圆皮。
  8. Add a spoonful of filling, pinch the middle shut, then squeeze the edges together with both hands.放一勺馅，对折捏紧中间，再用两手虎口一挤成元宝形。
  9. Drop the dumplings into boiling water, stir once so they do not stick, and add a cup of cold water each time it boils, three times.大锅水烧开下饺子，推一下防粘，水开后加一碗冷水，共点三次。
  10. Lift them out when they are puffed and all floating.饺子鼓起、全部浮起后捞出。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Oiling the cabbage before it meets the meat seals its water in, so the filling does not flood but is still juicy.白菜先裹油再拌肉，油把水分封在菜里，馅不会出汤，咬开还多汁。
  • Salt draws water out of cabbage the moment they meet, so combine late and wrap straight away.盐最后接触白菜、拌好马上包，盐一碰白菜就开始出水。
  • A round thicker in the middle and thin at the edge keeps the seam from being doughy and the base from tearing.皮擀成中间厚边缘薄，捏合处不会太厚，底部也不容易破。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The filling turned watery and is hard to wrap馅出了很多汤、包不住
    It sat too long. Stir 1 tbsp flour or cornstarch into the rest and wrap quickly.拌好放太久了。剩下的馅加 1 汤匙干面粉或淀粉拌匀，尽快包完。
  • Dumplings split while boiling饺子煮破了
    Thin wrappers, poor seals or a hard boil. Add cold water to keep it gentle and press out air as you seal.皮太薄、没捏紧或大滚煮。点冷水让水保持微滚，捏的时候挤出空气。
  • The dough is stiff and springs back面皮硬、擀不开
    It needs more rest. Cover it for another 15 minutes until the gluten relaxes.醒的时间不够。盖好再醒 15 分钟，筋松了就好擀。
