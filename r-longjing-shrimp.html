Longjing Tea Shrimp龙井虾仁

A delicate Hangzhou stir-fry where crisp-tender white shrimp carry the fresh aroma of green tea. Common missteps at home are applying the egg-white-and-cornstarch velvet to wet shrimp so the coating slips off, cooking too hot so the shrimp turn rubbery, or frying the tea leaves too long until bitter.杭州清雅名菜，虾仁白嫩脆弹，带着一缕鲜爽的龙井茶香。家里常做不好，多半是虾仁没擦干就挂不住蛋清淀粉浆、油温过高把虾炒老，或者茶叶下早了发黑发苦。

Ingredients食材

  • Frozen raw shrimp冷冻生虾 450 g · 450 g (1 lb) peeled, deveined raw shrimp去壳去肠线生虾仁 450 克（1 磅） choose untreated shrimp without phosphate soaks so they stay snappy and dry选未泡磷酸盐的纯虾仁，炒出来更脆也不出水
  • Longjing (Dragon Well) green tea leaves龙井茶叶 4 g · 3–4 g (or about 1 tbsp loosely packed leaves)3–4 克（或约 1 汤匙蓬松茶叶） steep in 120 mL water; reserve the leaves and use only 3 tbsp of the strained liquor in the sauce用 120 毫升热水冲泡，茶叶留用，炒制时只取 3 汤匙茶汤
  • Eggs鸡蛋 55 g · 1 large egg (use about half the egg white, ~15 g)1 个大鸡蛋（只取约半个蛋清，约 15 克）
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 12 g · 4 tsp (3 tsp for velveting, 1 tsp for slurry)4 茶匙（3 茶匙用于上浆，1 茶匙调稀淀粉水）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp, divided约 2/3 茶匙（分两次用）
  • Sugar白糖 2 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙

Method做法

  1. Steep the Longjing tea leaves in 120 mL (1/2 cup) of 85°C hot water for 3 minutes, then strain and set the tea liquor aside to use warm or at room temperature; reserve the steeped leaves separately.茶叶放进碗里，倒入 120 毫升约 85°C 的热水浸泡 3 分钟后滤出茶汤备用（温热或室温即可），泡开的茶叶另留备用。
  2. Pat the shrimp completely dry with paper towels, then massage with half the salt (about 1/3 tsp) for 1 minute until tacky.虾仁用厨房纸彻底吸干水分，放进碗里加一半盐（约 1/3 茶匙）抓揉 1 分钟至表面发黏。
  3. Stir in half the egg white (~15 g) in one direction until absorbed, mix in 3 tsp of the cornstarch to complete the egg-white-and-cornstarch velvet, and chill covered for 15 minutes.拌入约半个蛋清（15 克）顺一个方向搅匀，再抓入 3 茶匙玉米淀粉完成上浆，盖好冷藏静置 15 分钟。
  4. Pour 120–180 mL (1/2 to 3/4 cup) oil into a wok—enough to generously coat and come partway up the shrimp—and heat over medium-low heat to 110–120°C, verified with a thermometer (or drop in one test shrimp; it should turn gently opaque without sputtering violently).炒锅倒入 120–180 毫升（约 1/2 至 3/4 杯）油（能充分润裹并没过部分虾仁），中小火加热至 110–120°C，用温度计确认，或放入 1 只虾仁测试：周围仅温和转白、不剧烈溅油即可。
  5. Slip in the shrimp and stir gently for 30–45 seconds until just opaque at the edges, then lift out and drain.滑入虾仁，轻柔拨散滑油 30–45 秒，至边缘刚刚变白转熟时捞出控油。
  6. Pour off all but about 1 tsp of the oil and raise the heat to medium-high; meanwhile, mix the remaining 1 tsp cornstarch with 1 tbsp cold tea liquor or water into a thin slurry.倒出锅里多余的油，只留约 1 茶匙底油，开中大火烧热；同时将剩下的 1 茶匙玉米淀粉与 1 汤匙凉茶汤或冷水调匀成稀淀粉水。
  7. Return the shrimp to the hot wok, add the cooking wine alone around the rim and let it sizzle for about 5 seconds, then add 3 tbsp of the tea liquor, the remaining salt, and sugar. Stir in the cornstarch slurry and toss for 10–20 seconds just until the sauce lightly glosses the shrimp without turning heavy or gravy-like.倒回虾仁，先沿锅边单独淋入料酒，滋啦烹出酒香约 5 秒；随后加入 3 汤匙茶汤、剩下的盐和糖，再搅匀淋入稀淀粉水，快速翻炒 10–20 秒至薄芡轻亮地裹住虾仁，切忌炒成浓稠厚芡。
  8. Toss in the reserved tea leaves over high heat for 15 seconds until fragrant and the shrimp are snowy white and cooked through to 74°C, then plate immediately.撒入沥干的茶叶，大火翻匀 15 秒至虾仁洁白熟透（中心温度达 74°C）后立刻出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Drying the shrimp first and massaging them with salt creates a tacky surface so the egg-white-and-cornstarch velvet clings evenly.虾仁上浆前必须彻底擦干，再先加盐揉出黏性，蛋清淀粉浆才会稳稳裹住虾肉不掉浆。
  • Gentle low-heat velveting in ample oil brings the shrimp most of the way without squeezing out moisture, keeping them far more tender than high-heat searing.先低温「滑」油把虾仁弄到八分熟，能锁住水分，比直接大火爆炒嫩脆得多。
  • Brewing with 85°C water instead of boiling water preserves the sweet, grassy notes of Longjing without extracting bitterness.用约 85°C 热水泡龙井而不是滚水，茶汤才清香回甘，不会带出涩味。
  • Adding the steeped leaves only for the final 15 seconds releases their aroma without scorching them bitter.茶叶只在最后 15 秒下锅借锅气烘出香味，炒久了不仅变黑还会发苦。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The shrimp stuck and shed their coating in the pan虾仁粘锅、白浆掉了一锅底
    Pat the shrimp drier, chill them 15 minutes before cooking, use enough oil (120–180 mL) heated to 110–120°C verified with a thermometer before gently stirring, or use a nonstick pan.下次虾仁擦得更干并先冷藏 15 分钟定浆，滑油时用足 120–180 毫升油并用温度计确认达到 110–120°C 后再下锅轻拨，也可用不粘锅降低难度。
  • The shrimp wept water and turned rubbery, or taste bitter虾仁出水发柴，或者带着苦味
    Check ingredient labels next time to avoid phosphate-treated frozen shrimp, shorten cooking times, and brew the tea at 85°C.看配料表避开加了磷酸盐的急冻虾，缩短滑油和复炒时间，泡茶水温也不要用滚水。
  • The flavor feels too mild觉得味道偏淡
    This dish is meant to taste subtle and naturally sweet; serve it as is with steamed rice, or offer a very small amount of the reserved tea liquor at the table if desired.正宗做法本身讲究清鲜回甜，原味直接配白米饭吃最好；如喜欢更明显的茶香，上桌时可另附极少量留下的茶汤佐味。
