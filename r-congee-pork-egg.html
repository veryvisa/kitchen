Century Egg and Pork Congee皮蛋瘦肉粥

As the rice opens, the porridge turns soft and creamy, while pork and century egg add savory flavor and contrast. A low simmer and occasional stirring help prevent sticking, and thin slices of pork cook quickly and stay tender.米粒熬开后，粥底绵滑，瘦肉和皮蛋带来鲜香与不同口感。小火慢煮并适时搅动，能减少粘锅；瘦肉切薄后短时间煮熟，口感更嫩。

80 min total分钟 (20 + 60)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Century egg (preserved duck egg), Shaoxing wine另购：皮蛋、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Short-grain rice短粒米(寿司米) 150 g · about 3/4 cup short-grain rice (weigh by rice variety for accuracy)约 3/4 杯短粒米（以实际米种称重为准）
  • Pork loin猪里脊 180 g · lean pork (tenderloin or lean shoulder), thinly sliced猪瘦肉（里脊或梅头瘦肉），切薄片
  • Century egg (preserved duck egg)皮蛋 120 g · about 2 eggs, peeled and cut into chunks约 2 枚，去壳后切块
  • Ginger姜 15 g · cut into fine strips切细丝
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · about 2 stalks, finely sliced约 2 根，切葱花
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · about 1/2 tbsp (7.5 mL)约 1/2 汤匙（7.5 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 3 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp, adjust to taste约 2/3 茶匙，按口味调整

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Rinse the short-grain rice twice, until the water is mostly clear.把短粒米淘洗两次，直到水基本清澈。
  2. Soak the rice in 1800 g (about 7 1/2 cups) water for 15 minutes.将米浸泡在 1800 g（约 7.5 杯）水中 15 分钟。
  3. Slice the pork thinly and place it in a bowl.把猪肉切成薄片，放入碗中。
  4. Mix the soy sauce, cooking wine and cornstarch into the pork, then marinate for 10 minutes.在猪肉里拌入生抽、料酒和淀粉，腌 10 分钟。
  5. Cut one century egg into small pieces and the other into larger chunks for adding at the end.将一枚皮蛋切成小块，另一枚切成较大的块留作最后加入。
  6. Pour the rice and its soaking water into a heavy pot, add the ginger and bring to a boil.把泡米连同浸泡水倒入厚底锅，加入姜丝并烧开。
  7. Turn the heat to low and keep a gentle simmer, partly covered, for about 40–50 minutes, stirring the bottom often and checking the consistency until the grains bloom and the porridge is creamy; if the grains have not opened yet, continue simmering and add hot water as needed.转小火半盖锅盖，保持轻微沸腾煮约 40–50 分钟，期间经常搅动锅底并观察浓稠度，直到米粒开花、粥底浓稠；如果米粒尚未开花，可继续煮，并适量补充热水。
  8. Add the finely cut century egg and salt, then stir gently.加入切碎的皮蛋和盐，轻轻搅匀。
  9. Slide the pork slices one by one into the gently simmering porridge.将猪肉片逐片放入微沸的粥中。
  10. Simmer gently for about 2–3 minutes, until the thickest pork slices reach an internal temperature of 71°C.小火保持微沸煮约 2–3 分钟，直到最厚肉片中心温度达到 71°C。
  11. Add the remaining century egg chunks and simmer for 2 minutes.加入剩下的皮蛋块，再煮 2 分钟。
  12. Turn off the heat and stir in the sesame oil and scallion.关火后拌入芝麻油和葱花。
  13. Ladle into bowls and serve hot.趁热盛碗。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Short-grain rice helps create a creamy base; keep the soaking water in the pot instead of draining it.短粒米更容易熬出绵滑粥底；泡米时保留浸泡水，不必沥干。
  • A heavy pot and a low simmer reduce sticking; sweep the spoon across the bottom as you stir.用厚底锅并保持小火，锅底更不容易粘住；搅动时要刮过锅底。
  • Slice the pork thinly and add it in separate pieces so it does not clump and cooks through evenly.猪肉尽量切薄并分开下锅，避免结成一团，也更容易熟透。
  • Add the century egg in two stages: the first batch flavors the porridge, while the later pieces keep their shape.皮蛋分两次加入：先放的融入粥底，后放的保留形状和口感。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The porridge is thin and the rice is still firm粥底太稀，米粒还硬
    Simmer another 10–15 minutes, stirring and scraping the bottom often; next time soak the rice for 15 minutes and keep a gentle simmer.继续小火煮 10–15 分钟并经常刮底搅动；下次确保米泡足 15 分钟且维持轻微沸腾。
  • The porridge sticks or tastes scorched粥底糊锅或有焦味
    Transfer the unscorched porridge to a clean pot without scraping up the burned layer; continue over low heat and stir more often.立刻把未糊的部分转到干净锅中，不要刮起锅底焦层；后续保持小火并更频繁搅动。
  • The pork is tough and clumped together瘦肉又柴又结团
    Slice the pork thinly, add it piece by piece and stop cooking once it reaches 71°C; chop overcooked pieces and stir them back into the porridge.猪肉要切薄并逐片放入，微沸时煮至达到 71°C 即停；已经煮老的肉可切碎拌回粥中。
