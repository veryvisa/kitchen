Oven Lamb Skewers烤箱羊肉串

You can make crisp, juicy lamb skewers at home without a charcoal grill. Cut boneless lamb leg with a little fat into even cubes, marinate with onion and aromatics to mellow the gaminess, and roast on a wire rack at high heat so the meat browns instead of steaming.家里没有炭火也能烤出外焦里嫩的羊肉串。选带少许肥肉的去骨羊腿肉切小块，用洋葱和姜蒜腌去膻味，架在烤网上高温快烤，才能留住肉汁不发柴。

Method做法

  1. Soak bamboo skewers in cold water for 20 minutes, and wrap the exposed ends in small strips of foil so they do not scorch in the oven.竹签放进冷水浸泡 20 分钟，并用小块锡纸包住签子外露的一端，防止烤的时候焦黑。
  2. Cut the lamb into even 2 cm cubes, keeping both lean and fatty pieces.羊腿肉切成约 2 厘米见方的小块，肥肉和瘦肉都保留。
  3. Toss the lamb with the onion, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, cooking wine, 1/2 tsp salt, sugar, and half the cumin, kneading for 1 minute until tacky.羊肉加洋葱丝、姜片、蒜瓣、生抽、料酒、半茶匙盐、糖和一半孜然拌匀，抓揉 1 分钟到表面发黏。
  4. Stir in 1 tbsp oil to coat the surface and slow down moisture loss, cover, and refrigerate for 30 minutes.拌入 1 汤匙油让表面裹一层薄油，减少表面水分蒸发，盖好冷藏腌制 30 分钟。
  5. Preheat the oven to 230°C (450°F), place a wire rack over a foil-lined baking sheet, and lightly oil the rack so the skewers do not stick.烤箱预热到 230°C（450°F），烤盘铺锡纸并架上烤网，烤网表面刷一点油防止肉串粘连。
  6. Discard the onion, ginger, and garlic, then thread the lamb onto skewers alternating lean and fat with slight gaps between cubes.挑掉洋葱、姜和蒜，把羊肉按肥瘦相间串上签子，块与块之间留一点空隙。
  7. Brush the skewers with the remaining 1 tbsp oil, arrange on the rack, and roast on the upper-middle rack for 6 minutes.肉串两面刷上剩下的 1 汤匙油，摆在烤网上，放进中上层烤 6 分钟。
  8. Flip the skewers without adding spices yet and roast 4 more minutes. Then sprinkle on the remaining cumin, chili powder, 1/4 tsp salt, ground Sichuan peppercorn, and sesame seeds, and roast 1-2 more minutes until the edges brown and the center reaches at least 71°C (cutting the lamb into small cubes and kneading it by hand increases surface exposure, so this higher target is safer).取出翻面，先不撒香料，再烤 4 分钟；最后撒上剩下的孜然、辣椒粉、剩下的 1/4 茶匙盐、花椒粉和白芝麻，继续烤 1–2 分钟，直到边缘微焦、肉块中心至少达到 71°C（切成小块并经手抓揉后表面接触面增大，按此熟度判断更稳妥）。
  9. Transfer to a platter and serve immediately while hot.取出装盘，趁热上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Include a little fat on every skewer; pure lean lamb dries out fast in the oven, whereas melting fat keeps it juicy and aromatic.一定要带少许肥肉一起串，纯瘦肉在烤箱里很容易烤干变柴，油脂融化才能带出羊肉串香味。
  • Elevating the skewers on a wire rack lets dripping fat and juices fall away so the bottoms roast crisp instead of steaming.用烤网把肉串架高悬空，滴落的油水不会把底部泡软，四面都能受热微焦。
  • Save half the spices for only the last 1-2 minutes of roasting; a long stretch at high heat scorches them bitter.孜然和辣椒粉一半留到接近烤好的最后 1–2 分钟才撒，高温久烤容易把香料烤糊发苦。
  • Coating the marinated lamb with oil at the end slows surface moisture loss and helps it brown faster in a hot oven.腌肉抓匀后再拌油，能在表面形成薄油膜减少水分蒸发，进高温烤箱也更容易上色。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The lamb turned out dry and chewy烤出来又干又硬
    This usually means it was overcooked, or had too little fat. Pull the skewers as soon as the center reaches 71°C on a thermometer, instead of roasting longer for colour; keep about 20 percent fat on the cubes.多半是烤过头了，或没留肥肉。下次按时间一到就用温度计确认中心已到 71°C 并立刻出炉，别为了上色多烤；切块时留约两成肥肉。
  • Liquid pooled in the pan and steamed the meat烤盘积水很多、像水煮肉
    Overcrowding or roasting flat on the pan traps moisture; space the cubes slightly on a wire rack and make sure the oven is fully preheated.没有架高烤网或肉块挤得太紧；肉之间留出空隙、全程用烤网架空，烤箱要充分预热到位。
  • The cumin burned and tasted bitter孜然发黑带苦味
    The spices went on too early and spent too long at high heat. Next time save all the spices for the last 1-2 minutes, or sprinkle them on right after the skewers come out of the oven.香料撒得太早，在高温里待太久。下次全部留到最后 1–2 分钟才撒，或者直接出炉后趁热撒上。
