Tofu Miso Soup豆腐味噌汤

The soup Japan drinks every day, ready in ten minutes. Bring dashi to a boil, add tofu, dissolve miso off the heat, and that is all. The one rule is never to boil the miso, which drives off its aroma. It goes with almost any Japanese or Chinese main.日本人每天都喝的汤，十分钟就好。高汤烧开放豆腐，关火后化开味噌，就这么简单；唯一的规矩是味噌不能滚，一滚香味就跑了。配任何一道日式或中式主菜都合适。

15 min total分钟 (5 + 10)4 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Soft / silken tofu, Miso paste, Dashi powder另购：嫩豆腐、味噌（白味噌/混合味噌）、日式鲣鱼高汤粉

Ingredients食材

  • Soft / silken tofu嫩豆腐 200 g · half a box of soft tofu (about 200 g), cut into 1.5 cm cubes嫩豆腐半盒（约 200 克），切 1.5 厘米丁
  • Miso paste味噌（白味噌/混合味噌） 50 g · 3 tbsp miso味噌 3 汤匙 brands vary in saltiness; start with 2 tbsp and taste各品牌咸度不同，先放 2 汤匙再尝
  • Dashi powder日式鲣鱼高汤粉 4 g · 1 tsp dashi powder日式高汤粉 1 茶匙
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 2 stalks, sliced2 根，切葱花

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Bring 800 mL water and the dashi powder to a boil.锅里放 800 mL 水和高汤粉，烧开。
  2. Add the tofu and simmer 2 minutes.放入豆腐丁，小火煮 2 分钟。
  3. Turn off the heat, hold the miso in a ladle or small sieve in the soup and stir it in with chopsticks until dissolved.关火，把味噌放在汤勺或小漏网里，用筷子在汤中慢慢化开。
  4. Add the green onion and serve straight away.撒葱花，马上盛碗。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Add miso off the heat; boiling it drives off the aroma and makes it taste harsh and salty.味噌最后关火再放，滚煮会让香味散掉、变得发咸发苦。
  • A pinch of dried wakame cooked with the tofu makes the classic combination.家里有干裙带菜，放一小撮和豆腐一起煮，就是最经典的组合。
  • White miso is sweet and red is salty; a mixed (awase) miso suits everything, so buy that first.白味噌甜、红味噌咸，混合味噌最百搭，第一次买选它。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • Too salty太咸
    Thin it with hot water; next time start with 2 tbsp miso and add after tasting.加热水稀释；下次味噌先放 2 汤匙，尝过再加。
  • There are lumps of miso汤里有味噌疙瘩
    Dropped straight in it will not dissolve; use a sieve or loosen it in a bowl with a little broth first.直接丢进锅里不容易化开，用漏网或先在碗里用少量汤调开再倒回。
