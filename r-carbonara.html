Spaghetti Carbonara培根蛋酱意面

The Roman classic uses only pork, eggs, cheese and pepper, no cream; starchy pasta water emulsifies a silky sauce. The home challenge is temperature: over heat the eggs curdle into scrambled bits, so work off the flame to coat every strand.罗马经典意面只用培根、鸡蛋、芝士和黑胡椒，无需加入奶油，靠面汤乳化出顺滑浓稠的酱汁。家里做最大的难点是控制温度：蛋酱遇大火会结成蛋花，离火操作才能裹住每一根面条。

25 min total分钟 (10 + 15)4 servings份Medium中等Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Spaghetti意大利面 400 g · 400 g spaghetti意大利直面 400 克
  • Bacon培根 160 g · about 160 g bacon, diced培根 约 160 克，切小丁
  • Eggs鸡蛋 200 g · 4 large eggs4 个大鸡蛋
  • Parmesan帕玛森奶酪 90 g · about 1 cup freshly grated现磨帕玛森 约 1 杯
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 4 g · about 3–5 g freshly cracked black pepper, to taste现磨黑胡椒 约 3–5 克，按口味调整
  • Salt盐 6 g · about 1 tsp for the pasta water, adjust to the water volume and the saltiness of the cheese and bacon面汤用盐，约 1 茶匙，按煮面水量及芝士、培根咸度调整

Method做法

  1. Bring a large pot with about 3–4 L water to a boil, salt it until fairly salty, cook the pasta 2 minutes short of the package time, and reserve about 1 cup of hot pasta water.大锅装约 3–4 升水烧开，加盐到尝起来偏咸，下意面煮到比包装时间少 2 分钟，留约 1 杯热面汤。
  2. Dice the bacon and render it in a pan over low heat for about 5–8 minutes until the fat turns translucent and the edges of the bacon are golden and crisp; adjust the time for the thickness of the dice, and do not burn it.培根切小丁，平底锅小火慢慢煸出油脂，约 5–8 分钟至脂肪变透亮、肉丁边缘金黄酥脆，根据肉丁厚度调整时间，不要煎焦。
  3. Whisk the eggs, mix in the parmesan and plenty of cracked pepper into a smooth paste.鸡蛋打散，拌入帕玛森和大量现磨黑胡椒，搅成均匀糊状。
  4. Lift the pasta into the bacon pan, turn off the heat, and toss with the residual warmth for 1 minute.意面捞入培根锅，关火，用余温把面和培根拌匀 1 分钟。
  5. Wait about 1 minute off the heat, pour in the egg-cheese paste, toss fast, and add hot pasta water gradually until the sauce clings smoothly to every strand.等锅稍凉约 1 分钟，倒入蛋液芝士糊，快速翻动面条，分次加入热面汤，让酱汁顺滑裹住每根面。
  6. Taste; the water and bacon are already salty, so add only a pinch if needed. Serve at once with extra pepper and parmesan on top.尝味，面汤和培根都有咸味，必要时再补一小撮盐；即刻装盘，表面再磨黑胡椒和帕玛森。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Add the egg mixture only off the heat; a hot pan sets the white into curds. Residual warmth is enough to melt the cheese and emulsify the sauce.蛋酱必须在离火后再下，锅温太高蛋白会凝固成蛋花；余温足够让芝士融化、酱汁乳化。
  • The reserved starchy water is what makes the sauce glossy. Add it in small stages rather than pouring it all in at once.保留的面汤含淀粉，是酱汁乳化的关键；分次少量加入，比一次倒完更容易控制浓稠度。
  • Grate cheese fresh; pre-shredded cheese has anti-caking agents that do not melt smoothly and leave a grainy sauce.芝士现磨口感最好；预磨粉裹有抗结剂，下锅后不容易化开，酱汁会发粉发涩。
  • Render the bacon over low heat until just crisp. Burnt edges turn the whole pasta bitter.培根小火出油，煸到微脆即可；一旦焦黄发苦，整盘面都会带苦味。
  • The egg sauce is only warmed by residual heat and hot pasta water, never fully cooked; for pregnant women, young children, older adults or anyone with a weaker immune system, use pasteurized eggs.蛋液只靠余温和热面汤乳化，不会被加热到全熟；孕妇、幼儿、老人或免疫力较弱者食用时，建议用巴氏杀菌蛋。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The egg sauce curdles into scrambled bits蛋酱结块，像炒蛋
    Heat is the usual cause. Move off the stove and add hot pasta water a little at a time, tossing fast, to smooth out what you can; clumps that have already set firm will not fully loosen. Next time wait a minute for the pan to cool before adding the eggs.锅温太高是主因；立刻离火，少量多次加入热面汤快速翻拌，能让剩余酱汁恢复顺滑，但已经明显结块的部分无法完全回软。下次等锅凉一分钟再倒蛋液。
  • The sauce is too dry to coat the pasta酱汁太干，裹不住面条
    Not enough pasta water was used. Add another hot ladleful while warm and keep tossing until the sauce shines on the strands.面汤加得不够；趁热再补一勺热面汤，连续翻拌到酱汁发亮挂在面上。
  • The whole dish is too salty整盘太咸
    Bacon and cheese are already salty, so salt the pasta water lightly next time. If it is over-salted, add more cooked pasta and a little pasta water to dilute it, and no extra salt.培根和芝士本身含盐，面汤先少加盐；已经偏咸就多加一份煮好的意面和一点面汤稀释，不再补盐。
