Cantonese Shrimp and Silky Eggs滑蛋虾仁

A Cantonese café classic: snappy shrimp in eggs as soft as custard. It is all about heat; the eggs come off the stove while still half-set and finish in their own warmth.粤式茶餐厅的经典：虾仁爽脆、鸡蛋嫩滑得像奶油。全部秘诀在火候，鸡蛋要在半凝固时就离火，用余温把它推熟。

21 min total分钟 (15 + 6)2 servings份Medium中等Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Frozen raw shrimp冷冻生虾 250 g · about 250 g peeled raw shrimp去壳生虾仁约 250 克
  • Eggs鸡蛋 250 g · 5 large eggs5 个大鸡蛋
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 6 g · 2 tsp (1 for the shrimp, 1 for the eggs)2 茶匙（腌虾 1 茶匙，蛋液 1 茶匙）
  • Salt盐 3 g · about 1/2 tsp (half for the shrimp, half for the eggs)约 1/2 茶匙（腌虾和蛋液各一半）
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 0.5 g · a pinch (white pepper is traditional)少许（有白胡椒更好）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 3 g · a few drops几滴
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 10 g · 1 stalk, chopped1 根，切葱花

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Devein the shrimp and blot them dry with paper towel.虾仁挑去虾线，用厨房纸吸干水分。
  2. Toss the shrimp with half the salt, 1 tsp cornstarch and the pepper, and leave for 10 minutes.虾仁加一半盐、1 茶匙淀粉和胡椒抓匀，放 10 分钟。
  3. Mix 1 tsp cornstarch with 1 tbsp water and add it to the eggs.1 茶匙淀粉和 1 汤匙水调匀，倒进鸡蛋里。
  4. Beat the eggs with the remaining salt and the sesame oil until no streaks of white remain.鸡蛋加剩下的盐和香油，打到没有成块的蛋白。
  5. Cook the shrimp in 1 tbsp oil over medium heat until pink on both sides, about 1.5 minutes.锅里放 1 汤匙油，中火把虾仁煎到两面变红，约 1 分半钟。
  6. Tip the shrimp into the beaten eggs and stir once.虾仁倒进蛋液里拌一下。
  7. Wipe the pan, add the remaining oil and turn the heat to medium-low.锅擦干，加剩下的油，转中小火。
  8. Pour in the eggs and, once the edges just set, push them slowly from the outside in with a spatula.倒入蛋液，等边缘刚凝固，用铲子从外往里慢慢推。
  9. Turn off the heat when the eggs are about 70% set and still a little runny, push twice more, plate and scatter the green onion.蛋液七成凝固、还有一点流动时关火，再推两下装盘，撒葱花。

Keys to success成功要点

  • A little cornstarch slurry in the eggs keeps them tender and stops them weeping.蛋液里加一点水淀粉，鸡蛋更嫩、不容易出水。
  • Push slowly in large folds; do not scramble fast into small curds.推蛋的动作要慢、要大片，不要像炒蛋那样快速搅碎。
  • It should look underdone when you turn off the heat; it sets on the way to the plate.关火时看起来还没熟才是对的，装盘时余温会让它刚好凝固。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The eggs are dry and broken up鸡蛋又老又碎
    Too much heat, too much stirring or off the stove too late; use medium-low, push slowly and stop at 70% set.火太大、推太快、关火太晚；下次中小火、慢推，七成熟就离火。
  • There is liquid on the plate盘底有一摊水
    The shrimp were wet or under-cooked; blot them before seasoning and cook them alone until pink before mixing.虾仁没擦干或煎得不够；虾仁要吸干再腌，先单独煎到变色再和蛋液混合。
