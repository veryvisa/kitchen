Taiwanese Braised Pork Rice卤肉饭

The heart of lu rou fan is crispy fried shallots and slow-braised diced pork belly in a glossy soy sauce, poured over hot rice with a braised egg. At home, render the pork fat first, add seasonings and hot water, then simmer low and long; leftovers taste deeper the next day.卤肉饭的灵魂是煸到金黄的红葱酥和慢炖到软糯的五花肉丁，卤汁浇在热米饭上，配一颗卤蛋。家里做要把肉丁先煸出油脂，再加调料和热水小火炖足时间，第二天回锅味道更浓。

  • Garlic大蒜 20 g · 4 cloves, minced4 瓣，切末
  • Ginger姜 15 g · 3 slices3 片
  • Eggs鸡蛋 200 g · 4, hard-boiled and peeled4 个，煮熟剥壳
  • Short-grain rice短粒米(寿司米) 280 g · about 1.5 cups short-grain rice, cooked短粒米约 1.5 杯，煮熟

  • Dark soy sauce老抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL), for colour1 汤匙（15 mL），上色

Method做法

  1. Dice the pork belly into about 1.2 cm cubes; do not grind it into mince. Pat the dice dry with paper towel after cutting.五花肉切成约 1.2 厘米的小丁，不要剁成肉糜；切好后用厨房纸吸干表面水分。
  2. Slice the shallots thinly, mince the garlic, slice the ginger, and peel the hard-boiled eggs.红葱头切薄片，蒜切末，姜切片，鸡蛋煮熟后剥去外壳。
  3. Heat the oil over medium-low, fry the shallot slices in batches until light golden and crisp, lift out to drain, and set half aside.锅中放油，中小火下红葱头片，分批炸至浅金黄酥脆，捞出沥油，盛出一半备用。
  4. Add the dried pork dice to the same pan and stir over medium heat for about 5 minutes until fat renders and the edges brown lightly.原锅放入沥干的肉丁，中火翻炒到油脂渗出、边缘微微焦黄，约 5 分钟。
  5. Add the ginger and garlic and stir until fragrant, then add the star anise, bay leaf and five-spice, and stir 20 seconds.下姜片、蒜末炒香，再加入八角、香叶和五香粉，翻炒 20 秒。
  6. Add both soy sauces, the cooking wine and the sugar, and toss 1 minute to colour the meat.加入生抽、老抽、料酒和糖，翻炒 1 分钟让肉丁上色。
  7. Pour in the stock or hot water just to cover the pork, add the peeled eggs, bring to a boil, cover and simmer low for 50-60 minutes, keeping an eye on the liquid level.倒入鸡汤或热水，水量刚没过肉丁，放入剥壳鸡蛋，烧开后转小火盖盖炖 50–60 分钟，中途留意汤汁量。
  8. Simmer until the dice are tender enough to crush with chopsticks and the internal temperature is at least 71°C (the safe pork temperature, which long slow braising reaches naturally).炖到肉丁软糯、筷子能轻松压散，内部温度不低于 71°C（猪肉安全温度，长时间慢炖自然会达到）。
  9. Taste and add a little salt if needed, then uncover and reduce over high heat for 3-5 minutes until the sauce coats the pork; no need to reduce it to one third beforehand.尝味后补少许盐，开盖大火收浓 3–5 分钟，汤汁能挂在肉丁上即可，不必提前收到只剩三分之一。
  10. Spoon hot rice into bowls, top with the pork and sauce, add 1 whole braised egg per bowl, and finish with scallion and the reserved shallot crisp.热米饭盛入碗中，浇上卤肉和卤汁，每碗放 1 个卤蛋，撒葱花和预留的红葱酥。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Keep the cubes chunky; they shrink while braising, and tiny pieces fall apart and lose texture.肉丁保留颗粒感，炖后会收缩，切太小会变成碎肉，口感差一截。
  • Fry the shallots low until crisp; that sweetness and crunch are the dish's aroma source. Too dark is bitter, underdone is bland.红葱头必须小火炸到酥脆，这是卤肉饭浓香的来源；炸过头会苦，炸不够不香。
  • Render the belly fat before braising so the sauce is not greasy; skim some of the surface oil if you like.先把五花肉的油脂煸出来，卤汁才不腻；表面浮油可以撇掉一些。
  • Braise the eggs alongside so they absorb the sauce; let them keep soaking in the braise after cooking for deeper flavour.鸡蛋一起下锅慢卤才会入味，炖好后可以继续泡在卤汁里，吸味更足。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The braise is greasy with a thick oil layer卤汁肥腻、油层厚
    Pork belly is naturally high in fat, so a layer of oil on the braise is normal. Skim the surface oil after cooking, and render out a bit more fat when stir-frying next time.五花肉本身脂肪含量高，炖好后浮一层油很正常；撇去表层浮油即可，煸炒时也可以多煸出一些油脂。
  • The sauce is thin and does not cling to the rice卤汁太稀、不挂饭
    Reduce uncovered over high heat a few more minutes to thicken naturally. Do not use a starch slurry, which masks the meaty flavour.开盖大火多收几分钟，让酱汁自然变稠；不要用淀粉勾芡，会盖住肉香。
  • It is too salty太咸
    Use half the soy and add salt last next time. If it is already salty, thin it with hot water or simmer in another boiled egg and some tofu.下次酱油减半、盐最后再加；已经偏咸可加热水或多加一个煮蛋和豆腐同煮稀释。
