Bok Choy with Shiitake Mushrooms香菇扒油菜

A vegetable dish Jiangnan families put out for guests: a ring of bright green bok choy around glossy shiitake. The greens are blanched and arranged, and the mushrooms are simmered in their soaking liquid with oyster sauce, lightly thickened and spooned over. Easy, and it looks the part.江南人家宴客常见的素菜，碧绿的青菜围一圈，中间是油亮的香菇。青菜先焯水摆盘，香菇用泡香菇的水和蚝油烧一下勾薄芡浇上去，颜色好看，做法也不难。

21 min total分钟 (15 + 6)3 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Bok choy另购：小白菜

Ingredients食材

  • Bok choy小白菜 450 g · 450 g Shanghai or baby bok choy, halved上海青或小白菜 450 克，对半切开
  • Shiitake香菇 15 g · 6-8 dried shiitake, soaked in warm water (keep the liquid)干香菇 6–8 朵，温水泡软，泡香菇的水留着
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 18 g · 1 tbsp oyster sauce蚝油 1 汤匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 6 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Sugar白糖 2 g · 1/2 tsp1/2 茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 3 g · 1 tsp, mixed with 1 tbsp water1 茶匙，加 1 汤匙水调匀
  • Garlic大蒜 6 g · 2 cloves, minced2 瓣，切末
  • Canola oil菜籽油 21 g · 1.5 tbsp, used in two parts1.5 汤匙，分两次用
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp, for blanching约 1/3 茶匙，焯菜用

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Bring water to a boil with the salt and 1 tsp oil and blanch the bok choy for 1 minute.锅里水烧开，加盐和 1 茶匙油，下青菜烫 1 分钟。
  2. Drain and arrange cut side up in a ring on a plate.捞出沥干，切面朝上围着盘子摆一圈。
  3. Squeeze the mushrooms, trim the stems and halve any large ones.香菇挤干、去蒂，大的切半。
  4. Fry the garlic and mushrooms in the rest of the oil over medium heat for 1 minute.锅里放剩下的油，下蒜末和香菇中火炒 1 分钟。
  5. Add the oyster sauce, soy, sugar and 100 mL of the soaking liquid and simmer 3 minutes.加蚝油、生抽、糖和 100 mL 泡香菇的水，小火煮 3 分钟。
  6. Stir in the cornstarch mixture and cook until glossy and thick.倒入水淀粉，煮到汁变亮变稠。
  7. Pile the mushrooms in the centre and spoon the sauce over the greens.香菇放在盘中间，汁浇在青菜上。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Salt and a few drops of oil in the blanching water keep the greens bright.焯水时加盐和几滴油，青菜颜色更绿更亮。
  • The soaking liquid holds the most flavour; pour it off and leave the grit at the bottom.泡香菇的水鲜味最浓，倒的时候留下碗底的沙。
  • Keep the sauce light, just thick enough to cling and still flow.芡要薄，能挂在菜上、流得动就好。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The greens turned yellow青菜发黄
    Blanched too long or left sitting. One minute, out at once, sauce at the end.焯太久或焯完一直放着。焯 1 分钟马上捞出，最后再浇汁。
  • The mushrooms have hard centres香菇有硬心
    Not soaked long enough. Soak in warm water for an hour or cold water overnight.没泡透。提前用温水泡 1 小时，或冷水泡一夜。
