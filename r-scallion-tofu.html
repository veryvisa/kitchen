Braised Tofu with Green Onions葱烧豆腐

A home version of Shandong-style scallion braising. Tofu fried to golden edges, a big handful of green onion fried until toasty, then a short simmer with soy, oyster sauce and a little sugar. Mild, cheap, and one block of tofu feeds two.鲁菜「葱烧」的家常版：豆腐煎出焦边，一大把葱段煸到焦香，再用酱油、蚝油和一点糖烧一会儿。不辣、不贵，一盒豆腐两个人吃一顿。

20 min total分钟 (5 + 15)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Firm tofu, Dark soy sauce另购：老豆腐、老抽

Ingredients食材

  • Firm tofu老豆腐 450 g · 1 block firm tofu (about 450 g), cut into 1.5 cm slices老豆腐 1 盒（约 450 克），切 1.5 厘米厚片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 60 g · 6 stalks, cut into 4 cm lengths, whites and greens apart6 根，切 4 厘米段，葱白葱绿分开
  • Garlic大蒜 6 g · 2 cloves, sliced2 瓣，切片
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 9 g · 1/2 tbsp半汤匙
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 3 g · 1/2 tsp (for colour, optional)半茶匙（上色，可不放）
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 3 g · 1 tsp, mixed with 1 tbsp water1 茶匙，加 1 汤匙水调匀
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Blot the tofu slices dry.豆腐片用厨房纸吸干。
  2. Fry the tofu in the oil over medium heat until golden on both sides, about 6 minutes, and take it out.不粘锅放油，中火把豆腐两面煎到金黄，约 6 分钟，盛出。
  3. In the oil left in the pan, fry the onion whites and garlic gently until the whites brown at the edges, about 2 minutes.用锅里的余油小火煸葱白和蒜片，到葱白边缘焦黄，约 2 分钟。
  4. Add the soy sauce, oyster sauce, dark soy, sugar and 120 mL hot water and bring to a boil.加生抽、蚝油、老抽、糖和 120 mL 热水，煮开。
  5. Return the tofu and simmer on medium-low for 3 minutes, turning once.豆腐回锅，中小火烧 3 分钟，中途翻一次面。
  6. Add the onion greens and the slurry and toss until the sauce thickens, then serve.放葱绿，淋水淀粉，翻匀到汤汁浓稠出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • The green onion is the star; fry the whites slowly until browned for the flavour.葱是这道菜的主角，葱白要小火煸到焦黄才香。
  • The more the tofu is browned, the more flavour and sauce it takes up.豆腐煎得越干越香，也越能吸收汤汁。
  • Stop when the sauce clings to the tofu; any further and it gets salty.汤汁收到能挂住豆腐就停，太干会咸。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The onion tastes raw葱一股生味
    The whites were not fried long enough; cook them gently until the edges brown before adding the sauce.葱白煸的时间不够；小火煸到边缘发黄再加调料。
  • Too salty太咸
    The sauce reduced too far; leave more liquid, or cut the soy sauce to 2 tsp.汤汁收得太干；下次多留一点汁，或生抽减到 2 茶匙。
