West Lake Beef Soup西湖牛肉羹

A Hangzhou soup that is also a fixture of Cantonese restaurants. Minced beef is blanched loose, simmered with tofu and mushrooms, lightly thickened, then streaked with egg white like mist on a lake. Fifteen minutes for a pot that sits quietly beside any main.杭州菜，后来在粤菜馆里也很常见。牛肉末先烫散，再和豆腐、蘑菇一起入羹，勾一层薄芡，最后淋蛋清拉出白色的蛋花，看起来像湖面起雾。十五分钟一锅，配任何主菜都不抢味。

25 min total分钟 (10 + 15)4 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Soft / silken tofu, Shaoxing wine另购：嫩豆腐、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Ground beef牛肉馅 200 g · 200 g ground beef (as lean as you can get)牛肉馅 200 克（越瘦越好）
  • Soft / silken tofu嫩豆腐 150 g · 150 g soft tofu, finely diced嫩豆腐 150 克，切小丁
  • White mushrooms蘑菇 60 g · 4 mushrooms, finely diced蘑菇 4 朵，切小丁
  • Eggs鸡蛋 66 g · 2 eggs, whites only2 个鸡蛋，只要蛋清
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 500 g · 2 cups (500 mL) chicken stock plus 2 cups water鸡高汤 2 杯（500 mL），另加 2 杯水
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 24 g · 3 tbsp, mixed with 4 tbsp water3 汤匙，加 4 汤匙水调匀
  • Ginger姜 5 g · a small piece, minced1 小块，切末
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 10 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Salt盐 3 g · about 1/2 tsp约 1/2 茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · 1/4 tsp (white pepper is traditional)1/4 茶匙（白胡椒最地道）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 3 g · a few drops几滴
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 2 stalks, sliced (coriander is more traditional)2 根，切葱花（有香菜更传统）

Method做法

  1. Loosen the beef with the wine, ginger and 2 tbsp cold water.牛肉末加料酒、姜末和 2 汤匙冷水抓散。
  2. Drop the beef into a small pot of boiling water, stir it apart with chopsticks and drain as soon as it changes colour.小锅水烧开，倒入牛肉末用筷子搅散，变色立刻捞出沥干。
  3. Bring the stock and 2 cups water to a boil and simmer the mushrooms and tofu for 3 minutes.高汤加 2 杯水烧开，放蘑菇和豆腐煮 3 分钟。
  4. Add the beef and salt and bring back to a boil.放入牛肉末，加盐，再次烧开。
  5. Stir the cornstarch mixture again and pour it in slowly while stirring, until the soup is lightly thick and glossy.水淀粉再搅一下，一边搅汤一边慢慢倒入，煮到汤变得稍稠透亮。
  6. Lower the heat, drizzle the beaten whites in a thin circle, wait 10 seconds, then nudge gently.关小火，蛋清打散，细细地绕圈淋入，等 10 秒再轻轻推开。
  7. Finish with pepper, green onion and a few drops of sesame oil.撒胡椒、葱花，滴香油出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Blanching the beef first keeps the soup clear, free of grey scum and lumps.牛肉末先单独烫一下，羹才清亮，不会浮一层灰沫，也不会结成肉团。
  • Keep it lightly thickened, so it runs slowly off a spoon; too much starch turns it to paste.芡要薄，舀起来能缓缓流下就对了，太稠就成糊了。
  • Leave the whites alone until they set before stirring, for wisps rather than crumbs.蛋清淋进去先别动，定型后再推，蛋花才是一片片白雾，不是碎末。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The soup is too thick汤太稠
    Stir in some hot water; next time add the starch in two parts and judge as you go.加一点热水搅匀；下次水淀粉分两次加，看稠度再决定。
  • The beef formed little lumps牛肉结成小团
    Loosen the mince with cold water first and break it up with chopsticks the moment it hits the water.牛肉末先加冷水抓散，下锅马上用筷子打散。
