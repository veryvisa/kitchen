Sichuan Chili Beef Salad夫妻肺片（牛肉版）

This home version swaps offal for a single beef shank, keeping the prep practical while delivering the characteristic Sichuan mala flavour, with a crisp contrast from celery. The key is braising the shank until fully tender, briefly resting it in the broth, then chilling it fully in the fridge so it slices paper-thin, and pouring hot oil over the chilies at a controlled temperature in two stages so they turn fragrant without scorching.家庭版的夫妻肺片用整条牛腱代替牛杂，备料简单很多，吃起来依然麻辣鲜香、爽脆弹牙。想做得漂亮，重点在于牛腱卤到软透、短暂泡汤后转冰箱彻底冷透，冷透再切才能片得薄而不散，热油泼红油时也要控制油温、分两次下才香而不糊。

Method做法

  1. Put the beef shank in a pot of cold water with 1 tbsp cooking wine and half the ginger, and bring to a boil over high heat.牛腱冷水下锅，加 1 汤匙料酒和一半姜片，大火煮沸。
  2. Skim off the foam, boil for 3 minutes, then lift out the beef and rinse with warm water.撇净浮沫后煮 3 分钟，捞出用温水冲净表面。
  3. Submerge the beef in fresh boiling water by 2 cm and add the remaining ginger, knotted green onion, star anise, bay leaves, half the Sichuan pepper, 2 dried chilies, 1/2 tbsp soy sauce, dark soy, remaining wine, and salt.锅中重新加开水没过牛腱约 2 厘米，放入剩余姜片、葱结、八角、香叶、一半花椒、2 个干辣椒、半汤匙生抽、老抽、剩余料酒和盐。
  4. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce to low, and simmer for 90-110 minutes (adjust for the shank's thickness) until a chopstick pierces the thickest part easily and the sinew is tender.大火烧开后盖盖转小火，慢炖 90–110 分钟（视牛腱粗细调整），直到筷子能轻松扎透最厚处且筋膜软烂。
  5. Turn off the heat and let the beef rest in the braising liquid for 20-30 minutes, reserving 3 tbsp broth. Then transfer the beef with a little broth to a shallow covered container and refrigerate for at least 2 hours until fully chilled (do not leave the whole pot of broth at room temperature for over an hour).关火后牛腱留在卤汤里浸泡 20–30 分钟，留 3 汤匙卤汤备用；然后把牛腱连少量卤汤移入浅容器，加盖放入冰箱冷藏至少 2 小时到完全冷透（不要让整锅卤汤在室温下放 1 小时以上）。
  6. Toast the remaining Sichuan pepper in a dry pan over low heat for 1 minute until fragrant, grind it, and place in a heatproof bowl with the chili flakes, snipped chilies, and sesame seeds.剩余花椒放干锅小火炒 1 分钟出香后碾碎，与粗辣椒面、剪碎的干辣椒、白芝麻一起放进耐热碗。
  7. Heat the canola oil in a small pan over medium-high heat to about 160-180°C, until it shimmers (do not wait for smoke), then remove from the heat.小锅倒入菜籽油，中高火加热到约 160–180°C（油面起细纹，不要等到冒烟）后离火。
  8. Pour half the hot oil into the chili bowl, stir, wait 10 seconds, and pour in the rest; the two stages release the aroma gradually and keep the chili flakes from scorching.先倒一半热油进辣椒碗拌匀，等 10 秒再倒另一半，分两次释放香气并控制温度，避免辣椒面炸焦。
  9. Once the chili oil cools slightly, stir in the minced garlic, remaining 1-1/2 tbsp soy sauce, black vinegar, sugar, sesame oil, and 3 tbsp reserved broth.红油略降到温热后，拌入蒜末、剩下的 1.5 汤匙生抽、陈醋、糖、香油和 3 汤匙卤汤。
  10. Slice the chilled beef across the grain into thin 2 mm pieces.凉透的牛腱逆着肉纹切成约 2 毫米的薄片。
  11. Toss the beef slices with the celery, cilantro, and chili dressing, transfer to a plate, and scatter crushed peanuts on top.牛肉片与西芹片、香菜和红油汁拌匀装盘，表面撒拍碎的花生米。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Rest the beef in the braising liquid for 20-30 minutes, then move it to the fridge to chill fully; the gelatin firms up as it cools so it slices cleanly without crumbling. Do not leave the whole pot of broth at room temperature for long.牛腱卤好后先在汤里泡 20–30 分钟再转冰箱冷透，胶质冷却收紧，切片才会完整透明、不散架；别让整锅汤在室温下放太久。
  • Pour the hot oil into the chili bowl in two stages, keeping it around 160-180°C (not smoking); this releases the aroma gradually without scorching the chili flakes bitter.热油分两次泼进辣椒碗，并控制在约 160–180°C（不等冒烟），分阶段释放香气又不把辣椒面炸糊发苦。
  • Whisking a few spoonfuls of reserved beef broth into the dressing rounds out the sharp heat and helps the sauce cling to the slices.拌汁里加几勺原味牛肉卤汤，能把辣油的燥气柔化，鲜味也更挂得住肉片。
  • If the shank still feels soft when slicing, chill it in the fridge for 20 minutes first so even a home knife makes paper-thin slices.切牛肉前如果还不够凉，放冰箱冷藏 20 分钟再切，刀工普通的也能切得很薄。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The beef falls apart into shreds while slicing牛肉一刀切下去全碎成肉丝
    Slicing warm or under-chilled beef, or over-braising, causes crumbling. Chill the beef thoroughly, then slice across the grain, angling the knife slightly if needed. Shorten the braising time next time if the beef was overcooked.通常是趁热切、没冷透或炖过头了。把剩下的牛腱彻底冷藏后，始终逆着肉纹切、必要时略微斜切；下次适当缩短炖煮时间，避免牛肉过度软烂。
  • The chili oil turned dark and bitter红油发黑发苦，不够红亮
    Oil that is too hot, or poured after it starts smoking, scorches the chili flakes and the bitterness cannot be fixed afterward. Discard the batch and remake it; next time pull the oil off the heat at 160-180°C, just as it shimmers, and pour it in two stages.油温太高或直接等到冒烟才泼会把辣椒面炸糊，焦苦味补不回来。整碗倒掉重做；下次把油烧到 160–180°C 刚起纹就离火，分两次泼。
  • The center of the beef is tough while the outside is salty牛肉中间硬、外面咸
    This usually means the shank was not simmered long enough. Return the uncut beef to the pot with more boiling water and keep simmering over low heat until a chopstick pierces the thickest part easily; the exact time depends on the size of the shank. If already sliced and too salty, cut back on or remake the dressing and pair it with unsalted vegetables rather than relying on extra celery to fix the saltiness.通常是没炖够时间。还没切的牛腱回锅加适量开水，小火继续炖到筷子能轻松扎透最厚处，具体时间看肉块大小；已经切好的如果太咸，减少或重新调配拌汁并搭配不额外加盐的蔬菜，不要只靠多拌西芹解咸。
