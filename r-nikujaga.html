Nikujaga土豆炖肉

Nikujaga is a staple of Japanese home cooking, simmering thinly sliced beef and potatoes in dashi, mirin and soy sauce for a lighter, gently sweet broth than heavy braises. The usual pitfalls are potatoes breaking down into a cloudy mash or an overly sweet broth.日式土豆炖肉是日本家常餐桌的代表菜，用日式高汤、味淋和生抽炖出清甜带鲜的汤汁，和重酱香的中式炖牛肉风味不同。做这道菜最怕土豆炖散浑汤，或者甜咸比例没调好发腻。

55 min total分钟 (15 + 40)3 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dashi powder, Mirin, Shaoxing wine另购：日式鲣鱼高汤粉、味啉、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Beef chuck / stewing beef牛肩肉(炖牛肉用) 300 g · 300 g thinly sliced beef (chuck or hotpot slices)薄切火锅牛肉片或肥牛片 300 克 a chuck roast partially frozen can be sliced paper-thin整块牛肩肉冻到微硬后切薄片也可
  • Potatoes土豆 450 g · 3 medium yellow potatoes (about 450 g), cut into large chunks3 个中等黄皮土豆（约 450 克），切大块 waxy yellow potatoes hold their shape best黄皮蜡质土豆不易炖散
  • Onions洋葱 200 g · 1 large onion, sliced into thick wedges1 个大洋葱，切厚月牙片
  • Carrots胡萝卜 150 g · 1 medium carrot, cut into bite-sized chunks1 根中等胡萝卜，切滚刀块
  • Green beans四季豆 80 g · a small handful of green beans (about 80 g), trimmed and halved1 小把四季豆（约 80 克），去筋切段
  • Dashi powder日式鲣鱼高汤粉 4 g · 1 tsp dashi powder (for 350 mL water)1 茶匙日式高汤粉（配 350 毫升清水）
  • Soy sauce生抽 54 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Mirin味啉 55 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 30 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL) sake清酒 2 汤匙（30 mL） mild Chinese cooking wine works in a pinch but will not taste quite the same没有清酒可用淡味中式料酒代替，但风味会不一样
  • Sugar白糖 12 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）

Method做法

  1. Peel the potatoes, cut into 4 cm chunks, soak in cold water for 5 minutes to rinse off surface starch and drain.土豆去皮切 4 厘米大块，泡在冷水里 5 分钟洗去表面淀粉后沥干。
  2. Cut the carrots into chunks and the onion into thick wedges, and blanch the green beans in boiling water for 2 minutes before draining.胡萝卜切滚刀块，洋葱切厚月牙片，四季豆在沸水里焯 2 分钟捞出沥干。
  3. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat, spread out the sliced beef and stir for about 1 minute until just browned, then set aside (this only colors the surface; the beef is not fully cooked yet).炖锅放油中火烧热，铺开牛肉片翻炒约 1 分钟至刚变色，盛出备用（这一步只是上色，牛肉还没有完全熟透）。
  4. Cook the onion in the same pot for 2 minutes until translucent at the edges, then toss in the potatoes and carrots for 1 minute to coat with oil.同锅下洋葱炒 2 分钟至边缘微透明，再放土豆和胡萝卜翻炒 1 分钟裹上油。
  5. Pour in 350 mL water with the dashi powder, sake, mirin, soy sauce and sugar, bring to a boil and skim off any scum, then lay a round of parchment paper directly on the surface of the ingredients as a cartouche.倒入 350 毫升清水、高汤粉、料酒、味淋、生抽和糖，大火煮开后撇净表面浮沫，铺一张大小合适的圆形烘焙纸贴在食材表面作落盖。
  6. Simmer over medium-low heat for 12 to 15 minutes until a chopstick slides easily into the center of a potato.转中小火盖着落盖炖 12–15 分钟，直到筷子能轻松插进土豆中心。
  7. Remove the parchment, return the beef to the top, add the blanched green beans, gently swirl the pot to mix and simmer over medium-high heat for 2 to 3 minutes to reduce the broth slightly, until the beef is no longer pink and fully cooked through; keep it at a gentle simmer rather than a hard boil.揭掉烘焙纸，把牛肉片放回锅面，放进焯好的四季豆，轻晃锅子拌匀，中大火收汤 2–3 分钟至牛肉不再呈粉红色、完全熟透；收汁时保持微沸，不要剧烈翻滚。
  8. Turn off the heat, cover and rest for about 10 minutes to let the ingredients absorb more of the broth's flavor off the heat, then serve with the broth.关火盖盖静置约 10 分钟，让食材在余温中进一步吸收汤汁风味，连汤装盘上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Coating the potato edges briefly in oil helps keep their corners intact during simmering.土豆下锅先用油轻炒 1 分钟，表面形成薄油膜，炖煮时边角不容易碎烂。
  • A parchment cartouche helps distribute the shallow cooking liquid over the ingredients while keeping them from moving around too much.贴面盖一张烘焙纸作落盖，能让不多的汤汁较均匀地接触食材，也减少翻动造成的土豆破碎。
  • Resting the pot off the heat for 10 minutes is a common technique in Japanese simmered dishes, giving the ingredients more time to absorb the seasoned broth.关火后焖 10 分钟是日式炖菜常用的做法，有助于食材在静置中进一步吸收汤汁风味。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The potatoes broke apart and thickened the broth土豆碎了、汤汁变得糊稠
    Floury potatoes or vigorous boiling and stirring caused them to break; use waxy yellow potatoes and simmer gently without stirring.用了淀粉太高的粉质土豆或滚沸时反复翻搅；下次换黄皮蜡质土豆、保持微沸不搅拌。
  • The broth tastes too sweet or flat汤汁太甜腻或味道发平
    Sweetness varies by mirin and soy sauce brand; taste first, then stir in 1 tsp extra soy sauce if too sweet, or a little more dashi if the broth tastes flat.不同牌子味淋和生抽甜咸度不同；先尝味，太甜就补 1 茶匙生抽，太淡就补少量高汤粉或清水稀释后的高汤。
