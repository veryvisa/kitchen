Twice-Cooked Pork回锅肉

Twice-cooked pork is a cornerstone of Sichuan home cooking, prized for its curled, rendered fat, tender lean meat and a glossy, savoury sauce made with fermented broad bean and chilli paste, with tender pork and fragrant Chinese garlic shoots (or thick green onions). Getting it right comes down to simmering the pork until cooked through yet firm, chilling it so it slices evenly and cooking the greens just until wilted.回锅肉是川菜家常桌上的头牌，讲究肥肉煸出油后卷成「灯盏窝」，瘦肉不柴、酱香裹匀。难点在于煮肉要煮透但不散、放凉冷藏后切片均匀，以及青蒜苗（或代用的粗大葱）下锅后断生即出锅。

Method做法

  1. Put the whole piece of pork belly in a pot of cold water with the knotted green onions, 8–10 g ginger slices, peppercorns and cooking wine, and bring to a boil over high heat.整块带皮五花肉冷水下锅，加入葱结、8–10 克姜片、花椒和料酒，大火烧开。
  2. Skim off any scum once boiling, reduce to low to maintain a gentle simmer, cover and cook for 20–30 minutes depending on thickness, until a chopstick pierces the meat with slight springiness remaining and a food thermometer at the thickest part reads at least 71°C.水沸后撇去浮沫，转小火保持微沸，加盖煮约 20–30 分钟（视肉块厚度调整），直到筷子能穿透肉块但仍带适当弹性，并用食品温度计测得最厚处中心温度至少达 71°C。
  3. Lift out the pork and let it cool until easy to handle, then refrigerate for 30–60 minutes until firm before slicing evenly about 2–3 mm thick.捞出五花肉放凉至不烫手，冷藏约 30–60 分钟待肉块定型，再切成约 2–3 毫米厚的均匀薄片。
  4. Heat a wide wok over medium heat with no oil or just a thin film. Spread the pork slices out in batches without crowding so they sear rather than steam, rendering until the fat releases some oil and the slices begin to curl, then combine.使用足够大的炒锅烧热，先不放油或仅放极少量底油，中火将五花肉片分批铺开煸炒以免拥挤出水，待肥肉部分出油、肉片微卷后再合并继续。
  5. Push the pork to the side, drop to low heat and fry the doubanjiang in the rendered pork fat (adding a small amount of oil first if the pan is too dry) until fragrant and red oil separates, then add the fermented black beans, sweet bean sauce, remaining 5–7 g ginger and garlic and stir-fry until aromatic without scorching the garlic.把肉拨到锅边，转小火，利用锅中逼出的猪油（若油不足可补少量底油）先炒香豆瓣酱至红油析出，再加入豆豉、甜面酱、剩余 5–7 克姜片和蒜片炒香，注意蒜片不要炒焦。
  6. Turn to medium-high, toss the pork with the sauces, add the soy sauce and sugar and stir-fry 30 seconds until evenly coated.转中大火把肉和酱料翻匀，淋入生抽并撒白糖，炒 30 秒让每片肉挂满酱色。
  7. Add the white green-onion stems (or Chinese garlic shoot stems), cut diagonally into 3–4 cm lengths, and stir-fry over high heat until slightly softened, then add the optional bell pepper and toss 30–45 seconds until the edges blister slightly.下斜切约 3–4 厘米的葱白（或青蒜白），大火翻炒至略微软化，再加入可选的青椒块快炒约 30–45 秒至边缘微起皱。
  8. Finally add the green-onion tops (or leaves), cut to a similar length, and toss quickly over high heat for about 15 seconds just until wilted and glossy, then serve.最后下切成相近长度的葱叶（或青蒜叶），大火快速翻炒约 15 秒至刚断生变软发亮即装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Simmer the pork whole until cooked through (71°C internal) yet still holding its shape, then cool and chill 30–60 minutes to firm up before slicing; hot pork shreds under the knife and will not slice evenly.五花肉一定要整块煮熟透（中心达 71°C）仍保持形状，放凉后再冷藏 30–60 分钟定型切片；热切容易散碎，也切不出一致的 2–3 毫米厚度。
  • Render the pork belly gently over medium heat until the fatty edges curl into small cup-shaped pockets, without drying out the lean meat.煸肉片只用很少或不用底油，靠中火慢煸把猪油逼出来，肥肉才会卷成凹窝状的「灯盏窝」而不过柴油腻。
  • Add the white stems first so they soften slightly while retaining some crunch, then add the leaves and cook just until wilted.葱白（或蒜白）和绿叶分两次下锅，先炒白段至略微软化仍带脆度，最后下绿叶断生就起锅，香味最浓且不出水。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The pork slices turned dry and stiff instead of curling肉片炒得干硬发柴，或者没有卷起来
    Slice 2-3 mm thick next time. Adjust simmering time to the thickness of the pork so the center reaches a safe 71°C; when rendering the slices, add the sauces as soon as the fat begins to curl and the lean is just cooked through, avoiding prolonged frying that dries out the lean meat.下次肉片切 2–3 毫米厚；煮肉时间按肉块厚度调整，确保中心达到 71°C 安全温度；煸肉片时看到肥肉开始卷曲、瘦肉刚熟即可加入酱料，避免持续煸炒导致瘦肉变干。
  • The sauce turned dark and bitter酱味发苦、颜色发黑
    Both doubanjiang and sweet bean sauce scorch quickly, so lower the heat before frying them, and add a small amount of oil before frying the pastes if the pan lacks oil. If the paste has already scorched and turned bitter, discard the burnt portion and fry fresh paste rather than trying to rescue it with more oil.豆瓣酱和甜面酱含糖和酱渣，下锅前务必把火调小；锅中油不足时，在炒酱料前补少量油。若酱料已经焦苦，应停止使用焦糊部分，重新炒制，不能靠补油补救。
  • Too salty太咸
    All three fermented sauces are salty, so keep soy sauce minimal; to rescue a salty batch, toss in extra blanched bell pepper or onion.豆瓣酱、豆豉和甜面酱都偏咸，生抽只起提鲜作用；已做好可补一把焯过水的青椒或洋葱块大火拌炒几下分担咸味。
