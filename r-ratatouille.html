Ratatouille普罗旺斯炖菜

Eggplant, zucchini, peppers, and tomatoes slowly soften into a sweet, savory vegetable stew that works warm or chilled. Sear the vegetables in batches and reduce the tomato juices just enough to keep each piece distinct rather than mushy.茄子、西葫芦、甜椒和番茄慢慢炖出清甜的蔬菜汁，热吃冷吃都合适。关键是蔬菜分批煎、番茄汁收得适度，让每种蔬菜保留形状而不是煮成一锅糊。

80 min total分钟 (25 + 55)4 servings份Medium中等Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Eggplant茄子 450 g · 1 eggplant, cut into 2 cm cubes茄子约 1 根，切 2 厘米方块
  • Zucchini西葫芦 400 g · about 2 zucchini, cut into thick slices西葫芦约 2 根，切厚片
  • Bell peppers彩椒 250 g · 1 bell pepper, cut into pieces甜椒 1 个，切块
  • Tomatoes西红柿 500 g · about 4 ripe tomatoes, chopped熟番茄约 4 个，切块
  • Onions洋葱 180 g · 1 medium onion, diced1 个中等洋葱，切丁
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · 3 cloves, minced3 瓣，切末
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 ml), divided3 汤匙（45 毫升），分次使用
  • Dried thyme干百里香 1 g · about 1 tsp dried thyme干百里香约 1 茶匙
  • Salt盐 7 g · about 1 1/4 tsp, divided约 1 又 1/4 茶匙，分次使用
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · about 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper现磨黑胡椒约 1/2 茶匙
  • Fresh parsley欧芹 10 g · a little parsley, chopped, optional欧芹叶少许，切碎，可选

Method做法

  1. Cut the eggplant, zucchini, and pepper into similarly sized pieces.把茄子、西葫芦和甜椒切成大小接近的块。
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a heavy pot over medium heat and sear the eggplant in batches for 5–6 minutes until golden, adding a splash more oil if the pan looks dry, then set it aside.厚底锅中火烧热 1 汤匙橄榄油，分批煎茄子 5–6 分钟至表面金黄，锅底变干可以再补一点油，盛出备用。
  3. Add another tablespoon of olive oil and sear the zucchini over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until the edges are lightly browned, then set it aside.锅中再加 1 汤匙橄榄油，中火煎西葫芦 3–4 分钟至边缘微黄，盛出备用。
  4. Add 1 teaspoon of olive oil and cook the pepper over medium heat for 3 minutes until brightly colored and slightly softened, then set it aside.锅中再加 1 茶匙橄榄油，中火煎甜椒 3 分钟至颜色鲜亮、略微变软，盛出备用。
  5. Add the remaining olive oil and onion and cook over medium-low heat for 5 minutes until translucent and soft.锅中加入剩余橄榄油和洋葱，中小火炒 5 分钟至透明变软。
  6. Add the garlic and cook over medium-low heat for 30 seconds until fragrant.加入蒜末，中小火炒 30 秒至闻到香味。
  7. Add the tomatoes and thyme and cook over medium heat for 8–10 minutes until the tomatoes release their juices and begin to collapse.加入番茄和百里香，中火煮 8–10 分钟至番茄出汁并开始塌软。
  8. Stir in the eggplant, zucchini, pepper, salt, and black pepper and cook over medium heat until bubbling again.拌入茄子、西葫芦、甜椒、盐和黑胡椒，中火煮至重新冒泡。
  9. Cover and simmer over low heat for 15 minutes until the vegetables are tender but still hold their shape.盖锅转小火炖 15 分钟，直到蔬菜变软但仍能看出形状。
  10. Uncover and simmer over low heat for 5–8 minutes until excess liquid has reduced.开盖小火再煮 5–8 分钟至锅底没有多余水汁。
  11. Sprinkle with parsley and serve warm. For later serving, divide into shallow containers, refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking, and serve chilled or reheated.撒上欧芹后趁热盛出；要留着冷食，分装进浅容器，烹调后 2 小时内放入冰箱冷藏，吃前可冷食也可重新加热。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Searing the eggplant separately in batches until golden adds a deeper, browned flavor and helps it color evenly, rather than steaming with the other vegetables.茄子先单独分批煎至表面金黄，能增加焦香风味、让颜色更均匀上色，而不是和别的蔬菜混在一起闷煮。
  • Sear in batches without crowding the pot; crowded vegetables release water and steam instead of browning.蔬菜分批煎，不要堆满锅底；拥挤会让蔬菜出水，变成焖软而不是煎香。
  • Keep the pieces similar in size so they finish at roughly the same time.切块大小尽量一致，成熟时间才接近，端上桌时不会有的烂、有的生。
  • Tomatoes vary in juiciness, so judge the final reduction by the liquid in the pot rather than time alone.番茄的水分差异很大，最后开盖收汁时看锅底状态，不要只按时间判断。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The vegetables have collapsed into mush蔬菜炖成一锅软烂的糊
    Next time, shorten the covered simmer by 5 minutes and cut larger pieces; reduce this batch uncovered and serve it as a thick vegetable stew.下次缩短加盖炖煮时间 5 分钟，并把蔬菜切大块；这锅可开盖收汁后作为浓稠配菜食用。
  • There is too much liquid and the flavor is weak锅底汤汁很多，味道寡淡
    Simmer uncovered for another 5–10 minutes, turning gently now and then, until excess liquid evaporates.开盖小火继续煮 5–10 分钟，偶尔轻轻翻动，直到多余水分蒸发。
  • The eggplant absorbed too much oil and tastes greasy茄子吸油过多，吃起来油腻
    Sear the eggplant in batches and use a nonstick or heavy pot; spoon off any visible oil before serving.茄子分批煎并使用不粘锅或厚底锅；成菜可用勺子撇去表面浮油。
