Cantonese-Style Steamed Cod清蒸鳕鱼

Cantonese steamed fish lets the fish taste like itself, with only ginger, scallion, a seasoned soy sauce and a ladle of hot oil. Cod fillets are thick and nearly boneless, so they are easier than a whole fish. The only real skill is timing, because one extra minute turns the flesh dry.广式蒸鱼靠的是鱼本身的鲜，调味只有姜、葱、蒸鱼豉油和一勺滚油。鳕鱼肉厚、刺少，比整条鱼好上手，难点只有一个：蒸的时间要准，多蒸一分钟肉就发柴。

22 min total分钟 (10 + 12)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Cod fillet鳕鱼排 400 g · 2 cod fillets, about 200 g and 2.5 cm thick each2 块鳕鱼排，每块约 200 克、厚约 2.5 厘米 thaw frozen fillets in the fridge and pat dry冷冻鳕鱼先在冰箱里解冻，并擦干
  • Ginger姜 25 g · a thumb-sized piece, half sliced and half cut into thin threads约 1 块拇指大，一半切片，一半切细丝
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 40 g · 3 stalks, 2 shredded and 1 cut in sections for the rack3 根，2 根切丝、1 根切段垫底
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1.5 tbsp, ideally seasoned soy sauce for steamed fish1.5 汤匙，最好用蒸鱼豉油
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Salt盐 1 g · 1 g, for the fish1 克，腌鱼用

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Pat the fillets dry, rub both sides with the salt and wine, and let them sit for 5 minutes.鳕鱼用厨房纸吸干，两面抹上盐和料酒，静置 5 分钟。
  2. Stir the soy sauce, sugar and 2 tbsp hot water together in a small bowl until the sugar dissolves.小碗里放生抽、糖和 2 汤匙热水，搅到糖化开。
  3. Lay the scallion sections and ginger slices on a plate and set the fish on top so steam can circulate underneath.盘子里垫上葱段和姜片，鱼放在上面，让蒸汽能从底下透上来。
  4. Wait until the steamer water is at a full boil and sending up steam before you put the plate in.蒸锅水烧到大滚冒出蒸汽，再放入盘子。
  5. Cover and steam on high for 8 minutes; a 2.5 cm fillet is done when a chopstick slides through the thickest part and the flesh is opaque.盖上盖大火蒸 8 分钟，厚度 2.5 厘米的鱼排用筷子戳最厚处，肉变白、能轻松戳透就是熟了。
  6. Turn off the heat, carefully pour away the liquid on the plate and lift off the cooked ginger and scallion.关火，小心倒掉盘里蒸出的汤汁，挑掉葱姜。
  7. Pile the shredded scallion and ginger on top of the fish.鱼面铺上葱丝和姜丝。
  8. Heat the oil in a small pan until it just smokes and pour it over the threads; it should sizzle loudly.油在小锅里烧到冒细烟，趁热浇在葱丝和姜丝上，会听到滋啦声。
  9. Pour the soy mixture around the edge of the plate and serve immediately.把调好的酱汁沿盘边淋入，立刻上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Always put the fish in once the water is at a full boil. The surface sets instantly and holds in moisture; starting from cold water gives mushy flesh.水一定要滚了再放鱼，鱼表面瞬间定型，鲜味和水分才被锁住；冷水上锅蒸出来的肉会松散。
  • Discard the steaming liquid, which carries the fishy smell, and use fresh seasoning so the dish tastes clean.蒸出来的汤汁要倒掉，那里面有鱼腥味；换新调味汁，味道才清爽。
  • The ginger and scallion bed also lifts the fish off the plate so it does not sit in liquid and cooks evenly.垫姜葱不只是调味，让鱼悬空，底部不会泡在水里，受热均匀。
  • The oil must really smoke; only that heat releases the aroma of the raw scallion and ginger, lukewarm oil does nothing.滚油要真的热到冒烟，才能把姜葱的香味逼出来，油温不够只是温油淋上去，没有香气。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The fish is dry and crumbly鱼肉发柴、发散
    It was overcooked. Time from the moment the plate goes in; thinner fillets need 6 to 7 minutes, so check at 8.蒸过头了。下次从放入蒸锅就计时，薄一点的鱼排 6 到 7 分钟，8 分钟时就开盖检查。
  • It tastes fishy有腥味
    The fish was not patted dry, the wine step was skipped, or the steaming liquid stayed on the plate. Do all three and add more ginger threads.鱼没擦干、料酒腌得不够或没有倒掉蒸出的汤汁；这三步都要做，再多放姜丝。
  • The sauce is too salty酱汁太咸
    Seasoned steamed-fish soy is already salty. With regular soy sauce add more hot water, and taste before adding any more salt since the fish was already seasoned.蒸鱼豉油本身已调味，用普通生抽时就要多兑热水，直接加盐的话鱼已经腌过，要先尝再补。
