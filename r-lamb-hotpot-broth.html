Clear Lamb Hot Pot清汤羊肉锅

A comforting clear lamb hot pot made for cold evenings at home. Bone-in lamb leg is blanched from cold water, then simmered long and gently for a clean, gamy-free broth and tender meat. Daikon, shiitake and tofu go in next to soak up the flavour, and Napa cabbage and vermicelli finish cooking at the table.冬天一家人围坐吃最暖和的清汤羊肉锅。带骨羊腿块冷水焯净血沫再微火慢炖足够久，汤色清亮不膻、肉炖到软烂，白萝卜、香菇和豆腐依次下锅吸满肉香，端上桌后再下白菜和粉条，边煮边吃。

Ingredients食材

  • Daikon radish白萝卜 450 g · 1 medium daikon radish, peeled and cut into chunks1 根中等白萝卜，去皮切滚刀块
  • Carrots胡萝卜 120 g · 1 medium carrot, cut into chunks1 根中等胡萝卜，切滚刀块
  • Napa cabbage大白菜 300 g · 1/2 small Napa cabbage (about 300 g), torn into large pieces半颗小大白菜（约 300 克），撕大块
  • Firm tofu老豆腐 350 g · 1 block firm tofu, thickly sliced1 盒老豆腐，切厚片
  • Shiitake香菇 100 g · 6 fresh shiitake mushrooms, stemmed and scored6 朵鲜香菇，去蒂划十字
  • Sweet potato glass noodles红薯粉条 100 g · 1 bundle sweet potato vermicelli, soaked in warm water until pliable1 把红薯粉条，温水泡软
  • Ginger姜 25 g · 6-7 slices, smashed6–7 片，拍松
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 40 g · 4 stalks (2 knotted for broth, 2 chopped)4 根（2 根打结、2 根切葱花）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 30 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL) Shaoxing cooking wine绍兴料酒 2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Bay leaves月桂叶 1 g · 2 bay leaves2 片香叶
  • Sichuan peppercorn花椒 2 g · 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns, in a spice sachet1 茶匙花椒粒，装香料袋
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper半茶匙现磨黑胡椒粉
  • Salt盐 9 g · 1 1/2 tsp, adjusted to taste1 茶匙半，按咸淡调整

Method做法

  1. Place the lamb chunks in a large pot with enough cold water to cover, add half the ginger and the cooking wine, and bring to a boil over high heat.羊肉放进大锅，加足量冷水没过肉面，放入一半姜片和料酒，大火烧开。
  2. Boil for 5 minutes while skimming off all grayish scum, then lift out the lamb and rinse with warm water.滚沸煮 5 分钟并撇净表面浮沫，捞出羊肉用温水冲洗干净。
  3. Rinse the pot, return the lamb with the remaining ginger, knotted green onions, bay leaves, and spice sachet, and pour in about 2.5-3 L hot water, enough to cover the lamb by about 3 cm.汤锅洗净后放入羊肉、剩下的姜片、葱结、香叶和花椒袋，倒入约 2.5–3 升热水，水面没过羊肉约 3 厘米。
  4. Bring to a boil, skim any lingering foam, cover partially, and simmer gently over medium-low heat for 70 minutes. If the liquid drops below the meat partway through, top up with more boiling water.大火烧开后撇净残余浮沫，转中小火半盖锅盖，保持微沸炖 70 分钟；中途汤面如果低于肉面，只能补开水。
  5. Discard the aromatics and spice sachet, add the daikon, carrot and shiitake, and simmer 20 more minutes until the daikon turns translucent and a chopstick pierces the thickest part of the lamb easily.捞出葱结、姜片和香料袋，放入白萝卜、胡萝卜块和香菇，继续小火炖 20 分钟到萝卜变半透明、筷子能轻松扎透羊肉最厚处。
  6. Add the tofu and salt, and simmer 10 more minutes so the tofu takes on flavour; skim off the surface fat now if you prefer a lighter broth.放入老豆腐和盐，再煮 10 分钟让豆腐吸味；喜欢清爽的话，这时撇掉表面一层浮油。
  7. Move the pot to a tabletop burner, bring it back to a gentle simmer, then add the Napa cabbage and the drained, soaked vermicelli and cook 3-5 minutes until the cabbage wilts and the noodles turn translucent. Make sure the tabletop burner is stable, and if children are at the table, keep in mind the lid and pot stay very hot.连锅端上餐桌小火加热，先让汤重新微沸，再下入大白菜和泡软沥干的红薯粉条，煮 3–5 分钟到白菜变软、粉条透明；餐桌用炉子要放稳，有小孩在场时留意锅盖和锅身都很烫。
  8. Sprinkle with black pepper and chopped green onion, and ladle out the clear broth along with the meat and vegetables; a small dish of sesame paste thinned with water, fermented tofu and chopped cilantro also makes a good dip.撒上黑胡椒粉和葱花，先盛清汤再吃肉和菜；也可配一碟芝麻酱加水澥开、腐乳和香菜碎做蘸料。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Starting from cold water lets blood and impurities seep out gradually. Rinsing in warm water washes off the scum clinging to the meat without shocking it the way cold water would.冷水下锅能让血水和杂质慢慢渗出，比直接用热水更干净；捞出后用温水冲掉表面浮沫，不会像冷水那样激得肉收紧，汤色才会清亮。
  • Maintain a gentle simmer rather than a hard boil; vigorous boiling emulsifies fat into a cloudy broth and sharpens the gamy aroma.全过程保持微沸而不是大火猛滚，猛火会把油脂打混让汤发白，还会放大羊膻味。
  • Steeping the Sichuan peppercorns in a sachet for the first 50 minutes removes gaminess without leaving whole peppercorns in the soup.花椒装进小布袋或茶包袋里炖 50 分钟就捞出，既能去膻提鲜，吃的时候也不会咬到整粒花椒。
  • Add salt near the end, once the broth has reduced and the bones and meat have released their own savouriness; it is easier to get the seasoning right in one go at that point.盐留到快出锅前再放，这时汤已经炖得差不多浓，骨头和肉本身也出了鲜味，更容易一次调准咸淡。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The broth still tastes noticeably gamy汤喝起来有明显羊膻味
    Some scum remained or aromatics were light; next time blanch a minute longer, and for this pot simmer 5 more minutes with 2 extra ginger slices and extra black pepper.焯水浮沫没撇净或姜椒不够；下次延长焯水 1 分钟并冲净，这锅可补 2 片姜和少许黑胡椒再滚 5 分钟。
  • The lamb is still tough羊肉炖完还是咬不动
    Toughness varies by cut; hold off on adding the cabbage and noodles and simmer the lamb another 20-30 minutes, checking with a chopstick at the thickest part before moving on.羊腿不同部位软烂速度不同；先别下白菜和粉条，把肉留在锅里小火多炖 20–30 分钟，用筷子扎最厚处确认软烂再继续。
  • The broth grew too salty at the table汤越吃越咸
    Tabletop simmering concentrates salt; stir in a cup of boiling water to dilute, and start with slightly less salt next time.餐桌边煮边蒸发会让咸度浓缩；冲入 1 杯开水稀释即可，下次一开始盐少放一点。
