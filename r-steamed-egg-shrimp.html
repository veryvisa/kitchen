Steamed Egg with Shrimp虾仁蒸水蛋

A good steamed egg custard with shrimp should be as smooth as silken tofu, with bouncy shrimp resting neatly on top. Although fast to make, wrong water temperature, excess heat, or adding the shrimp too early easily leaves you with pitted egg and rubbery shrimp.一盘合格的虾仁蒸水蛋要像嫩豆腐一样滑溜无孔，虾仁弹牙又正好留在蛋面不沉底。看似简单，实际最常出错的是水温、比例和火候没控制好，蒸出满面蜂窝或者虾仁老成橡皮。

27 min total分钟 (15 + 12)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Eggs鸡蛋 200 g · 4 large eggs (about 200 g without shells)4 个大鸡蛋（去壳约 200 克）
  • Frozen raw shrimp冷冻生虾 150 g · 150 g raw peeled shrimp, deveined去壳生虾仁 150 克，去虾线洗净
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 8 g · 1/2 tbsp (7.5 mL)半汤匙（7.5 mL）
  • Ginger姜 5 g · 2 slices, smashed for marinating2 片，拍扁腌虾用
  • Salt盐 3 g · 1/2 tsp, divided半茶匙，分两次用
  • Soy sauce生抽 12 g · 2 tsp (10 mL), for drizzling2 茶匙（10 mL），出锅淋面用
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp (5 mL)1 茶匙（5 mL）
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 2 stalks, chopped2 根，切葱花

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Toss the shrimp with the cooking wine, smashed ginger, and 1 g salt for 5 minutes, discard the ginger, then pat the shrimp dry with paper towels.虾仁加料酒、拍扁的姜片和 1 克盐抓匀腌 5 分钟，挑出姜片弃用，再用厨房纸把虾仁表面水分吸干。
  2. Beat the eggs smoothly with the remaining 2 g salt and 290 mL warm water (about 40°C, close to a 1 to 1.5 egg-to-water volume ratio).鸡蛋打散，加入剩下的 2 克盐和 290 毫升温水（约 40°C，蛋水体积比约 1:1.5），轻手拌匀。
  3. Strain the egg mixture through a fine mesh sieve into a wide, shallow heatproof dish and skim off any surface bubbles.把蛋液过细网筛倒入宽口浅盘中，用勺撇去表面浮沫。
  4. Cover the dish with pierced heatproof wrap or an inverted plate (leave a small gap for steam to escape), place over simmering water in a steamer, and steam over medium-low heat for 7 minutes until the edges set and the center wobbles slightly.在盘口盖上扎有几个小孔的耐热保鲜膜或倒扣一只平盘（留一条小缝透气），放进水已烧开的蒸锅，转中小火蒸 7 分钟至边缘凝固、中心微微晃动。
  5. Uncover, arrange the marinated, pat-dried shrimp over the set custard, re-cover, and steam for 3-5 more minutes until the shrimp turn opaque pink, curl, and reach 74°C internally.揭盖将腌好并擦干的虾仁平铺在凝固的蛋面上，重新加盖中小火续蒸 3–5 分钟，直到虾仁完全变红卷曲且内部温度达到 74°C。
  6. Lift the dish from the steamer, stir the soy sauce, sesame oil and 1 tbsp hot water together, pour it around (not directly over) the shrimp, scatter the green onions on top, and serve.关火取出，把生抽、香油和 1 汤匙热水调匀，绕着虾仁周围淋上，撒上葱花即可上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Using 40°C warm water at a 1 to 1.5 ratio helps the custard heat evenly without prematurely cooking the eggs, yielding a smoother texture than cold water.使用约 40°C 的温水按 1:1.5 的体积比调蛋液，能让蛋白质受热更均匀，比冷水更容易蒸出滑嫩质感，又不会把蛋直接冲熟。
  • Straining, skimming, and covering remove stubborn chalazae and air pockets while keeping lid condensation from pitting the custard.过筛、撇泡和盖膜三步一起做，既能滤掉打不散的系带和气泡，又能挡住锅盖冷凝水滴进蛋面。
  • Gentle simmering steam is essential because violent boiling overcooks the edges and forces air bubbles through the setting egg.必须用中小火保持微沸蒸汽，猛火滚水会让蛋液在凝固前剧烈翻动，形成蜂窝孔洞。
  • Adding the pat-dried shrimp only after the custard surface sets keeps them from sinking and limits their cooking time to about 3-5 minutes so they stay snappy.等蛋面刚凝固定形再放擦干的虾仁，既能托住虾仁不沉底，又能把受热时间控制在 3–5 分钟左右，保持弹嫩。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The custard surface is full of honeycomb holes and feels spongy蒸出来满面蜂窝小孔，口感粗糙
    Caused by excessive steam heat, trapped air bubbles, or dripping condensation. Keep the steamer at a gentle medium-low simmer, strain the mixture, and cover the dish next time.火力太大、没盖盘或蛋液没过滤；下次水开后调到中小火，只保持微弱蒸汽，蛋液过筛并盖好再蒸。
  • The center stayed watery and failed to set中间水汪汪没凝固，一挖就散
    Deep bowls slow heat penetration and excess water weakens the gel. Use a wide shallow dish next time, or cover and steam for 2 to 3 more minutes now.深碗导热慢或水放太多；下次改用宽口浅盘，水蛋比例别超过 1:1.5。当前情况可加盖中小火补蒸 2 至 3 分钟。
  • The shrimp shrank and turned rubbery虾仁缩水发硬，不够嫩
    The shrimp cooked too long. Wait until the custard sets at the 7-minute mark before laying the shrimp on top, then pull the dish as soon as they reach 74°C, without steaming longer.虾仁放得太早或整盘蒸太久；一定要等水蛋蒸满 7 分钟定形后再铺虾仁，刚达到 74°C 就立刻关火出锅，不要多蒸。
