Chongqing Chilli Chicken重庆辣子鸡

Hunting for chicken among a heap of dried chillies is half the fun. Thigh meat cut small is fried crisp outside and juicy inside, then tossed with lots of chillies and Sichuan pepper; the chillies give aroma, the chicken is what you eat. Boneless thigh in place of bone-in pieces is easier at home and just as good.满盘干辣椒里找鸡块，是这道菜的乐趣。鸡腿肉切小丁，先炸到外酥里嫩，再和大量干辣椒、花椒一起翻炒，辣椒只是给香味，吃的是鸡。用去骨鸡腿肉代替带骨小块，在家更好做也更好吃。

40 min total分钟 (20 + 20)3 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine, Dried chili, Sichuan peppercorn另购：料酒(绍兴)、干辣椒、花椒

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 600 g · 600 g boneless chicken thigh, cut into 2 cm pieces去骨鸡腿肉 600 克，切 2 厘米丁
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Salt盐 3 g · 1/2 tsp1/2 茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 15 g · 5 tsp5 茶匙
  • Dried chili干辣椒 30 g · a big handful of dried chillies (about 30 g), snipped and seeded干辣椒一大把（约 30 克），剪段去籽
  • Sichuan peppercorn花椒 6 g · 1.5 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns花椒 1.5 汤匙
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · 5 cloves, sliced5 瓣，切片
  • Ginger姜 10 g · a knob, sliced1 块，切片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 3 stalks, cut into lengths3 根，切段
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 45 g · about 500 mL for frying (roughly 3 tbsp is absorbed, which is what is counted)炸用约 500 mL（实际吸油约 3 汤匙，按此计）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Toss the chicken with soy, wine, salt and ginger, marinate 15 minutes, pick out the ginger and mix in the cornstarch.鸡丁加生抽、料酒、盐、姜片抓匀，腌 15 分钟，拣出姜片后拌入淀粉。
  2. Heat the oil to 170°C (dense bubbles around a chopstick) and fry the chicken in two batches for 4 minutes until pale gold.油烧到 170°C（筷子插进去冒密集小泡），分两批下鸡丁炸 4 分钟到浅黄，捞出。
  3. Raise the oil to 190°C and refry all the chicken for 1 minute until crisp and golden.油温升到 190°C，鸡丁全部回锅复炸 1 分钟到金黄酥脆，捞出。
  4. Leave 2 tbsp oil in the wok and fry the Sichuan pepper and garlic on low for 30 seconds.锅里留 2 汤匙油，小火下花椒、蒜片炒 30 秒。
  5. Add the chillies and stir on low until deep red and fragrant, about a minute.下干辣椒小火翻炒到颜色变深红、发出香味，约 1 分钟。
  6. Return the chicken, add the sugar and green onion, and toss on high for a minute.鸡丁回锅，加糖和葱段，转大火翻炒 1 分钟。
  7. Finish with the sesame seeds.撒芝麻出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Double frying is the key: the first cooks the chicken, the hot second fry drives off moisture for crispness.复炸是酥脆的关键：第一次炸熟，第二次高温逼出水分。
  • Fry the chillies and pepper on low; on high they scorch in a second and make everything bitter.干辣椒和花椒一定小火炒，火大一秒就糊，糊了整盘都苦。
  • Thigh stays juicy through two fries where breast would dry out.鸡腿肉比鸡胸嫩，炸两次也不会柴。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The chicken is not crisp or goes soft鸡块不脆、回软
    The oil was not hot enough or there was no second fry. Get the oil hot for the refry and toss with the chillies straight away.油温不够或没复炸。复炸时油要够热，炸完马上和辣椒炒，别放太久。
  • Too hot and numbing太辣太麻
    Seed the chillies and halve the pepper; the chillies are not meant to be eaten, just pick out the chicken.辣椒去籽、花椒减半；辣椒本身不必吃，挑鸡块即可。
