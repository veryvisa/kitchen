Lunchbox Egg Fried Rice便当蛋炒饭

Egg fried rice uses everyday ingredients and packs neatly for lunch. Keep the grains separate, coat them evenly with egg, and cool the cooked rice promptly before packing.蛋炒饭用料简单，做成便当也方便，米粒分明、鸡蛋均匀裹住米饭是关键。米饭要摊薄并及时冷藏，炒好后也要尽快冷却再装盒。

95 min total分钟 (10 + 25)2 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Jasmine rice茉莉香米 150 g · 150 g dry rice, about 3/4 cup生米 150 克，约 3/4 杯
  • Eggs鸡蛋 150 g · 3 large eggs3 个大鸡蛋
  • Frozen mixed vegetables冷冻混合蔬菜 120 g · about 1 cup, thawed and drained约 1 杯，解冻沥干
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 2 stalks, sliced2 根，切葱花
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp (5 mL)1 茶匙（5 mL）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 21 g · 1 1/2 tbsp (22.5 mL)1 汤匙半（22.5 mL）
  • Salt盐 1 g · a pinch, to taste少许，按口味调整

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Rinse the rice and cook it with water according to the rice cooker's markings, about 20 minutes.淘洗大米，按电饭锅刻度加水煮熟，约 20 分钟。
  2. Spread the hot rice in a shallow tray and refrigerate it within 1 hour until cool.把热米饭摊在浅盘上，1 小时内放入冰箱冷藏至凉。
  3. Beat the eggs with a small pinch of salt in a bowl.鸡蛋打入碗中，加一小撮盐打匀。
  4. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok over medium heat and scramble the eggs until just set but still soft, then transfer them to a plate.炒锅中火烧热 1 汤匙油，倒入鸡蛋炒至刚凝固，仍然柔软时盛出。
  5. Add the remaining oil and cold rice, then stir-fry over medium-high heat for 3 minutes, breaking up any clumps.锅中加入剩下的油，放入冷米饭，中大火炒 3 分钟并压散结块。
  6. Add the vegetables and stir-fry over medium-high heat for 3 minutes until hot throughout and excess moisture has evaporated.加入蔬菜，中大火炒 3 分钟，直到蔬菜热透、锅中水汽散去。
  7. Pour in the soy sauce and toss for about 30 seconds until the rice is evenly coloured.倒入生抽，快速翻炒约 30 秒，让米饭均匀上色。
  8. Return the eggs to the wok with the green onion and sesame oil, then cook over medium heat for 1 minute until the eggs are fully cooked.倒回鸡蛋，加入葱花和香油，中火炒 1 分钟至鸡蛋全熟。
  9. Spread the fried rice in a clean shallow tray, cool it promptly, then pack it in a lunchbox and refrigerate.将炒饭薄薄铺在干净的浅盘上，尽快冷却后装入便当盒并冷藏。
  10. Reheat thoroughly until steaming before eating, and carry the chilled lunchbox in an insulated bag with an ice pack.食用前彻底加热至热气腾腾，冷藏便当用保冷袋和冰袋携带。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Spread and chill the rice promptly; cold, firm grains separate more easily than warm, moist rice.米饭煮好后及时摊薄冷藏，结实的冷米粒比温热湿饭更容易炒散。
  • Drain thawed vegetables well so excess water does not make the rice soggy.蔬菜解冻后要沥干，避免多余水分让炒饭变湿软。
  • Remove the eggs as soon as they set so they do not overcook when returned to the wok.鸡蛋先炒到刚凝固再盛出，最后回锅不容易变老。
  • Avoid crowding the wok; too much rice lowers the heat and can turn the grains sticky.锅不要装得太满；米饭过多会降温，炒出来容易发黏。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The rice clumped together米饭炒成一团
    Next time, chill the rice spread out until cool, loosen it gently before frying, and work in batches.下次把米饭摊薄冷藏至凉，炒前用手轻轻拨散，并分批炒。
  • The fried rice is soggy炒饭湿软
    Drain the vegetables and cook off their moisture before seasoning; briefly stir-fry the finished rice over medium-high heat to dry it out.蔬菜要沥干，先炒到水汽散去再调味；已炒好的可继续中大火翻炒片刻。
  • The eggs are dry and overcooked鸡蛋又干又老
    Remove the eggs when just set, then cook them only until fully done after returning them to the pan.第一次炒到刚凝固就盛出，回锅后只需炒到全熟。

Rice, eggs and vegetables make this easy to portion ahead. Refrigerate the rice promptly, keep the lunchbox chilled during transport, and reheat thoroughly before eating.炒饭含有米饭、鸡蛋和蔬菜，适合提前分装；米饭冷却后要及时冷藏，携带时保持低温，食用前彻底加热。
