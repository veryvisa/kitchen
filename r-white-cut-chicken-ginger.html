Sand Ginger Chicken沙姜鸡

Sand ginger chicken follows the classic Cantonese poached-chicken technique, lifted by a sizzling oil sauce made from aromatic sand ginger. Gentle sub-boil steeping followed by an ice bath brings the chicken safely up to temperature while keeping the skin snappy and the meat juicy.沙姜鸡的做法承袭广式白切鸡，灵魂在于用热油激发出沙姜特有的辛芳清香。鸡肉全程用微沸浸熟再过冰水，既能稳稳达到安全温度，又能保持皮爽肉嫩不发柴。

80 min total分钟 (15 + 25)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Sand ginger, Shaoxing wine另购：沙姜、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Whole chicken整鸡 1300 g · 1 whole chicken (about 1.3 kg)1 只整鸡（约 1.3 千克） a young yellow-skin chicken is best, or substitute bone-in leg quarters三黄鸡或走地小母鸡最嫩，也可用带骨大鸡腿
  • Sand ginger沙姜 50 g · about 50 g fresh sand ginger, peeled and finely minced新鲜沙姜约 50 克，去皮剁成细末 available fresh or frozen in Asian markets; soak dried slices first华人超市冷柜常见；干沙姜片需泡发后使用
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 34 g · 3 stalks (2 knotted for poaching, 1 finely minced for the sauce, so sand ginger stays the dominant flavor)3 根（2 根打结煮鸡，1 根切末做蘸料，让沙姜味更突出）
  • Ginger姜 20 g · 4 slices, for poaching4 片，煮鸡用
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 30 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Salt盐 9 g · 1.5 tsp, divided1.5 茶匙，分次用
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 7 g · 1/2 tbsp, for brushing the skin半汤匙，抹鸡皮用
  • Canola oil菜籽油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL), for sizzling the dipping sauce3 汤匙（45 mL），浇沙姜蘸料用

Method做法

  1. Rinse the chicken inside and out, clear any remaining clots from the cavity, and drain well.整鸡清洗干净，掏净腹腔内残余血块，沥干水分。
  2. Fill a tall pot with enough water to submerge the chicken, add the ginger, knotted scallions, wine, and 1 tsp salt, and bring to a boil over high heat.大深汤锅加足量能没过整鸡的清水，放入姜片、2 根打结的葱、料酒和 1 茶匙盐，大火煮沸。
  3. Holding the neck, dunk the chicken into the boiling water for 5 seconds, lift to drain the cavity for 5 seconds, and repeat 3 times to tighten the skin and warm the inside.提着鸡脖将整鸡浸入沸水 5 秒再提起沥去腹腔水，如此「三提三放」让鸡皮收紧、内外受热均匀。
  4. Lower the chicken breast-side down into the pot, drop to low so only tiny edge bubbles appear (about 85-90 °C), cover, and poach gently for 18 minutes.将鸡胸朝下完全浸入锅中，立刻转小火让水面维持几乎不翻滚的微泡状态（约 85–90 °C），加盖慢浸煮 18 分钟。
  5. Turn off the heat and steep covered for 25 minutes; probe the thickest part of the thigh without touching bone — a whole bird needs to reach the Health Canada safe internal temperature of at least 82 °C.关火不开盖，利用余温在热汤里再浸泡 25 分钟；用探针测鸡腿最厚处不碰骨头，整只鸡须达到卫生部对整禽的安全温度至少 82 °C。
  6. If under 82 °C, bring back to a bare simmer for 8-10 minutes and check again, repeating until it reaches temperature; once there, transfer straight into an ice-water bath for 15 minutes until cool and snappy.如温度未达 82 °C，再开小火微沸焖 8–10 分钟后重新测温，直到达标；达标后立刻把鸡捞进预先准备的大盆冰水里，完全浸凉 15 分钟至鸡皮紧弹。
  7. While the chicken chills, place the minced sand ginger, minced scallions, remaining 1/2 tsp salt, and sugar in a heatproof bowl.冰鸡的同时，把沙姜末、葱花末、剩下半茶匙盐和糖放进耐热小碗中。
  8. Heat the 3 tbsp canola oil in a small pan over medium-high heat for about 2 minutes until shimmering and just smoking.小锅烧热 3 汤匙食用油，中大火加热约 2 分钟至油面微微起波纹、微冒烟。
  9. Pour the hot oil slowly in two or three batches over the sand ginger and scallions to sizzle them fragrant, then stir in the soy sauce.把热油分两三次缓缓浇到沙姜葱末上，边浇边用勺拌开激香，稍凉后拌入生抽调成蘸料。
  10. Lift the chicken from the ice bath, pat dry, brush lightly with sesame oil, chop into serving pieces, and serve with the sand ginger sauce.把鸡从冰水捞出彻底擦干，外皮薄刷一层香油，斩成适口块状摆盘，淋一半沙姜汁或另碟蘸食。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Never boil the chicken hard; a rolling boil tears the skin and dries the breast, whereas gentle steeping safely reaches the 82 °C whole-bird standard while keeping the meat tender.全程不能大火猛煮：滚水会让鸡胸迅速收紧变柴、鸡皮破裂；靠微沸浸煮加上盖焖泡，鸡胸也能鲜嫩又稳妥达到整禽安全标准 82 °C。
  • Plunging the hot chicken into ice water tightens the skin and sets the thin sub-skin fat into a pleasant, snappy layer.过冰水能让鸡皮遇冷迅速收缩紧致，皮下脂肪凝结出淡淡冻感，口感才会爽脆不腻。
  • Sand ginger has an herbal, piney warmth unlike regular ginger; sizzle it in hot oil first and stir in soy sauce afterward so the soy never scorches.沙姜与普通老姜风味完全不同，带微辛和淡淡药草清香；用热油先烫香沙姜再加生抽，香气才不会被酱油盖住，生抽也不易焦苦。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The chicken breast turned dry and stringy鸡肉嚼起来发柴、鸡胸像木屑
    The broth boiled too hard or cooked too long; keep the pot at a bare simmer next time, and rescue dry meat by shredding it and tossing it well with the oil sauce.水沸太猛或煮得太久；下次下鸡后立刻把火调到水面几乎不起泡，若这次已经偏老，就把鸡肉手撕成丝，彻底拌进沙姜油汁里回润。
  • The thigh joint looked bloody or read under 82 °C切开鸡腿关节处还带血水、温度不到 82 °C
    A heavier bird needed a little more steeping; return undercooked pieces to the simmering broth for 8-10 minutes until they hit 82 °C, then chill briefly again.整鸡偏大或焖泡时间算少了；把切开未熟的鸡块放回热鸡汤微沸多煮 8–10 分钟直到测温达 82 °C，再过一次冰水即可。
  • The dipping sauce tastes bitter with blackened scallions蘸料苦涩发焦、葱末变黑
    The oil was too hot and dumped in all at once; discard charred bits, stir in fresh minced scallions and a little more sand ginger, and pour oil more gently next time.油温太高且一次全倒进去；先倒一半热油试一下微冒小泡即可，把烧黑的部分挑掉，重新切少许葱花与沙姜拌入热酱汁补香。
