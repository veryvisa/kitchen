New England Clam Chowder新英格兰蛤蜊汤

Milk and a little cream give this New England classic its gentle richness, while potatoes and clams make it hearty. The keys are cooking the flour before adding liquid and heating the clams gently at the end so they stay tender.新英格兰蛤蜊汤的奶香来自牛奶和少量奶油，土豆与蛤蜊则撑起浓实口感。关键是面粉炒熟后再加液体，并在最后轻煮蛤蜊，避免汤糊底或蛤蜊变韧。

60 min total分钟 (20 + 40)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Bottled clam juice, Canned chopped clams另购：蛤蜊汁、罐装蛤蜊肉

Ingredients食材

  • Bacon培根 100 g · about 4 slices, diced培根约 4 片，切小丁
  • Butter黄油 28 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 medium onion, diced1 个中等洋葱，切小丁
  • Celery西芹 100 g · about 2 stalks, diced约 2 根，切小丁
  • All-purpose flour面粉 35 g · about 1/4 cup约 1/4 杯
  • Potatoes土豆 500 g · about 3 medium potatoes, cut into 1.5 cm dice约 3 个中等土豆，切 1.5 厘米丁
  • Bottled clam juice蛤蜊汁 240 g · 1 cup bottled clam juice1 杯瓶装蛤蜊汁
  • Milk 2%牛奶 500 g · about 2 cups约 2 杯
  • Whipping cream淡奶油 120 g · about 1/2 cup约 1/2 杯
  • Canned chopped clams罐装蛤蜊肉 250 g · about 250 g drained chopped clams沥干蛤蜊肉约 250 克
  • Dried thyme干百里香 0.6 g · about 1/2 tsp约 1/2 茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · about 1/2 tsp约 1/2 茶匙
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp, adjust for the clam juice约 1/3 茶匙，按蛤蜊汁咸度调整

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Drain the canned clams and set the meat aside.把罐装蛤蜊沥干，保留蛤蜊肉。
  2. Cook the bacon in a heavy pot over medium heat for 6–8 minutes until golden and crisp around the edges and some fat has rendered into the pot.厚底锅中火煎培根 6–8 分钟，至边缘金黄酥脆、油脂渗出积在锅底。
  3. Add the butter, onion and celery and cook over medium heat for 5 minutes until softened but not browned.加入黄油、洋葱和芹菜，中火炒 5 分钟，至洋葱变软但不焦黄。
  4. Sprinkle in the flour and stir over low heat for 2 minutes until it no longer smells raw.撒入面粉，小火搅炒 2 分钟，至闻不到生面粉味。
  5. Gradually add the clam juice and milk, stirring constantly and incorporating each addition before adding more, until smooth.一边持续搅拌，一边分次慢慢倒入蛤蜊汁和牛奶，每次搅匀后再继续加入，避免面粉结团。
  6. Add the potatoes and thyme and bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat.加入土豆和百里香，中火加热至微沸。
  7. Partly cover and simmer over low heat for 15–20 minutes until a fork easily pierces the potatoes.转小火半盖锅盖煮 15–20 分钟，至土豆能被叉子轻松刺穿。
  8. Add the cream and black pepper and simmer for 3 minutes until small bubbles appear at the edges.倒入奶油和黑胡椒，小火煮 3 分钟，锅边冒小泡即可。
  9. Add the drained canned clams and warm gently for 1–2 minutes, just until heated through. Do not boil.加入沥干的罐装蛤蜊肉，小火轻轻加热约 1–2 分钟，至蛤蜊热透即可，不要煮沸。
  10. Taste, add a little salt if needed, and take the pot off the heat.尝味后少量加盐，关火盛出。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cooking the flour over low heat before adding liquid removes its raw taste and helps prevent lumps.面粉用小火炒熟再加液体，能减少生粉味，也较不容易结团。
  • Cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same rate; they are ready when a fork slides in easily.土豆切得大小一致，熟成时间才接近；叉子能轻松刺入就是合适的口感。
  • Canned clams are often salty, so taste the chowder before adding salt.罐装蛤蜊通常已有咸味，最后再尝味加盐，避免整锅过咸。
  • Add the clams near the end and heat them gently; a long boil can make them tough.蛤蜊最后下锅并轻轻加热，沸腾太久会让肉质变韧。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The chowder is lumpy or tastes of raw flour汤底结团或有生面粉味
    Next time, cook the flour over low heat for 2 minutes and add the liquid gradually while stirring; gently whisk any lumps that have already formed.下次先把面粉用小火炒足 2 分钟，再一边搅拌一边慢慢倒入液体；已经结团时可用打蛋器轻轻搅散。
  • The clams are tough and chewy蛤蜊肉又硬又韧
    Add the clams at the end and warm gently just until heated through. Avoid prolonged boiling; if they become tough, stop cooking immediately.蛤蜊最后加入，小火加热至热透即可，不要长时间煮沸；已经变韧时应立即关火盛出。
  • The chowder is too thin汤太稀
    Mash a few potato pieces into the pot and simmer for 2–3 minutes; next time, make sure the potatoes are tender before adding the cream.取出几块土豆压碎后拌回汤里，小火再煮 2–3 分钟；下次确认土豆煮软后再加入奶油。
