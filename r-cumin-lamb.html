Cumin Lamb Stir-fry孜然羊肉

All the flavour of a cumin lamb skewer from one wok. Costco's boneless lamb leg slices well; a hot pan and a fast stir-fry wake up the cumin and chilli, browning the lamb outside while it stays tender inside, with no gamey edge.烤羊肉串的味道，用一口炒锅就能做出来。Costco 的去骨羊腿肉正合适，切片后大火快炒，孜然和辣椒在热油里一激，香气出来，羊肉外焦里嫩，不膻也不柴。

23 min total分钟 (15 + 8)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Ground cumin, Dried chili, Shaoxing wine另购：孜然、干辣椒、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless leg of lamb去骨羊腿肉 400 g · 400 g boneless leg of lamb, sliced thin去骨羊腿肉 400 克，切薄片 half-frozen meat slices more easily冻到半硬更好切
  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 small onion, cut into thick strips1 个小洋葱，切粗丝
  • Ground cumin孜然 8 g · 1 tbsp ground cumin (about 8 g)孜然粉 1 汤匙（约 8 克） half whole seeds, half ground is even better有整粒孜然更好，一半粒一半粉
  • Dried chili干辣椒 3 g · 4-6 dried chillies, snipped (leave out if you avoid heat)干辣椒 4–6 个，剪段（不吃辣可不放）
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL), for the marinade1 汤匙（15 mL），腌肉用
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL), for the marinade1 汤匙（15 mL），腌肉用
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 6 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp约 1/3 茶匙
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 2 stalks, cut into lengths2 根，切段
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 4 g · 1 tsp sesame seeds1 茶匙白芝麻

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Slice the lamb across the grain about 3 mm thick.羊肉逆着纹理切成约 3 毫米厚的薄片。
  2. Toss the lamb with the soy sauce, wine, cornstarch and 1 tsp of the oil, and leave for 10 minutes.羊肉片加生抽、料酒、淀粉和 1 茶匙油抓匀，腌 10 分钟。
  3. Heat a wok until it smokes, then add the rest of the oil.炒锅烧到冒烟，倒入其余的油。
  4. Spread the lamb out in the pan and leave it for 40 seconds until the underside browns.羊肉片摊开下锅，不要翻动，煎 40 秒到底面焦黄。
  5. Stir-fry until the outside has changed colour, about 1 minute.翻炒到羊肉表面变色，约 1 分钟。
  6. Push the lamb to one side and fry the chillies and onion for 30 seconds.羊肉推到一边，放干辣椒和洋葱炒 30 秒。
  7. Sprinkle in the cumin and salt and toss everything over high heat for 1 minute.撒入孜然和盐，大火和羊肉一起翻炒 1 分钟。
  8. Turn off the heat, add the green onion and sesame seeds, toss and serve.关火，撒葱段和芝麻，翻匀装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • The pan must be very hot and the meat in one layer; crowding makes it steam instead of sear.锅一定要够热、肉不要堆，一次放太多会出水，变成煮羊肉。
  • Add the cumin near the end; early in the pan it burns and turns bitter.孜然最后放，太早下锅会焦苦。
  • A little fat on the leg meat adds flavour, so do not trim all of it off.羊腿肉带一点肥更香，不要把白色脂肪全部修掉。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The lamb released a lot of liquid羊肉出了很多水
    Cook in two batches, letting the pan smoke again each time; thaw frozen lamb fully and pat it dry.分两批炒，每批都等锅重新冒烟再下肉；冷冻羊肉要完全解冻并擦干。
  • It tastes gamey有膻味
    Do not skip the wine in the marinade, add more green onion and cumin at the end, and blot the raw meat dry first.腌肉时料酒不要省，出锅前多放葱和孜然；买回来的羊肉先用厨房纸吸干血水。
  • The cumin turned dark and bitter孜然发黑发苦
    Cook the cumin for a minute at most; if the heat is fierce, turn it off before adding the cumin and toss.孜然下锅后最多炒 1 分钟就出锅，火太大时先关火再撒孜然翻匀。
