Kimchi Fried Rice泡菜炒饭

Kimchi fried rice turns fridge staples into a complete meal in about twenty minutes, pairing rendered pork belly with tangy fermented kimchi and crisp-edged rice topped with a fried egg. The key is squeezing excess brine from the kimchi before frying so the rice stays separate instead of soggy.泡菜炒饭是十分钟就能炒好的快手主食，五花肉煎出猪油香，配上酸辣发酵的泡菜和粒粒分明的冷饭，盖上一颗煎蛋特别满足。做好它的诀窍是把泡菜挤干汁水再下锅炒香，米饭才不会湿黏成团。

20 min total分钟 (10 + 10)2 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Short-grain rice短粒米(寿司米) 185 g · 1 cup dry short-grain rice (cooked and chilled ahead, about 400 g cooked)1 杯生短粒米（提前煮熟冷藏，约 400 克熟饭） day-old chilled rice fries best隔夜冷藏米饭水分更少
  • Kimchi泡菜 180 g · about 1 cup sour napa kimchi, chopped into roughly 1 cm pieces, plus 2 tbsp reserved juice发酵白菜泡菜约 1 杯，切成约 1 厘米小块，留出 2 汤匙泡菜汁
  • Pork belly五花肉 120 g · about 120 g skinless pork belly, diced into 1 cm pieces去皮五花肉约 120 克，切 1 厘米小丁
  • Eggs鸡蛋 100 g · 2 large eggs, for topping2 个大鸡蛋，煎蛋铺面用
  • Onions洋葱 60 g · 1/3 medium onion, finely diced1/3 个中等洋葱，切碎
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 25 g · 2 stalks, sliced with whites and greens separated2 根，葱白与葱绿分开切花
  • Garlic大蒜 8 g · 2 cloves, minced2 瓣，切末
  • Gochujang韩式辣酱 15 g · 1 generous tsp (about 3/4 tbsp)1 满茶匙（约 3/4 汤匙）
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp1/2 汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 7 g · 1/2 tbsp1/2 汤匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 3 g · 1 tsp toasted white sesame seeds1 茶匙，熟白芝麻

Method做法

  1. Put the chopped kimchi in a sieve over a bowl and press out the juice with the back of a spoon or your hand; reserve 2 tbsp of the juice in the bowl and set the squeezed kimchi aside.把切碎的泡菜放进小碗上方的滤网里，用勺背或手压出汁水，汁水接在碗里留出 2 汤匙备用，泡菜碎放在一边。
  2. Heat 1/2 tbsp canola oil in a large skillet or wok over medium heat, add the diced pork belly, and cook 3 to 4 minutes until browned, crisp, and cooked above 71°C.大炒锅放 1/2 汤匙菜籽油，中火下五花肉丁煸炒 3–4 分钟至出油、边缘微焦且熟透（71°C 以上）。
  3. Add the onion, garlic, and green onion whites, and stir-fry 1 minute until softened and fragrant.放入洋葱碎、蒜末和葱白，翻炒 1 分钟至变软出香。
  4. Push the pork aside, add the squeezed kimchi, gochujang, and sugar, and fry over medium-high heat for 2 minutes until the kimchi darkens slightly and smells caramelized.把肉拨到锅边，放入挤干的泡菜、韩式辣酱和糖，中高火煸炒 2 分钟至泡菜色泽变深、带出焦香。
  5. Wash your hands after handling the raw pork, then break up the chilled rice with a rice paddle or spatula, add it to the pan, and stir-fry 2 to 3 minutes until every grain separates and heats through.切完生肉后先把手洗净，用饭勺或饭铲把冷藏米饭压散，倒入锅中大火翻炒 2–3 分钟至米粒松散受热均匀。
  6. Drizzle the soy sauce and reserved 2 tbsp kimchi juice around the edge of the pan and toss over high heat for 1 minute until dry and glossy.沿锅边淋入生抽和预留的 2 汤匙泡菜汁，大火快炒 1 分钟至锅底干爽、饭粒油亮。
  7. Turn off the heat, stir in the sesame oil and half the green onion greens, and divide between two bowls.关火拌入香油和一半葱绿，盛入两个碗中。
  8. Heat the remaining 1/2 tbsp canola oil in a small nonstick pan over medium heat and fry the eggs 2 to 3 minutes until the whites are completely set.小平底锅放剩下 1/2 汤匙菜籽油，中火把两个鸡蛋煎 2–3 分钟至蛋白完全凝固。
  9. Slide one fried egg onto each bowl of rice, garnish with the remaining green onions and sesame seeds, and serve hot.每碗饭放上一颗煎蛋，撒上剩余葱绿和熟白芝麻，趁热上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Wet kimchi steams the rice into a paste; squeezing it dry and frying it first drives off moisture and concentrates savory depth.泡菜不挤水直接下锅会把炒饭煮成焖饭；先把挤干的泡菜炒到微焦，能浓缩鲜味并减少水汽。
  • Spread cooked rice out and refrigerate it within 1 hour, and use it within a day; do not use rice that cooled at room temperature overnight.煮好的米饭要摊开，1 小时内放入冰箱冷藏，冷藏不超过 1 天；不要用在室温放了一夜的饭。
  • Well-fermented sour kimchi gives the best flavor; the 1 tsp sugar already in the recipe is there to round off that sharp acidity, and you can add another 1/4 tsp if your kimchi is especially sour.用发酵偏酸的老泡菜风味最足；配方里的 1 茶匙糖正是用来磨圆这股酸味的，如果泡菜特别酸可以再加 1/4 茶匙。
  • Pouring soy sauce onto the hot pan rim briefly chars it for toasted aroma without pooling liquid in the rice.生抽沿着烧热的锅边淋入，能瞬间激出酱香味，也不会在米饭里积水。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The fried rice came out wet and clumpy炒饭湿软成团、粒粒黏在一起
    Freshly cooked rice or un-squeezed kimchi adds too much water; next time chill the rice and squeeze the kimchi dry. Spread this batch flat over medium-high heat for 2 minutes to dry out.用了刚出锅的热饭或泡菜汁加太多；下次用冷藏隔夜饭并把泡菜挤干，这次可开中高火摊平多烘煎 2 分钟收干水汽。
  • It tastes sharply sour without rich depth吃起来只有生酸味、不够香浓
    The kimchi was not fried long enough before the rice went in, or the kimchi was too young; stir 1/2 tsp sugar and a few drops of sesame oil into this batch to balance it.泡菜下锅炒的时间不够，或没有用老泡菜；下次把泡菜炒到微焦再放饭，这次可补拌半茶匙糖和几滴香油平衡。
