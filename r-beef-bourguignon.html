Beef Bourguignon法式红酒炖牛肉

The classic Burgundy stew. Beef is browned deeply and braised in red wine and stock in the oven for over two hours, while the bacon, small onions and mushrooms are cooked separately and join at the end. No single step is hard; it just takes patience, and it is better the next day.勃艮第的家常名菜：牛肉煎到深褐，用红酒和高汤在烤箱里慢炖两个多小时，培根、小洋葱和蘑菇各自先煎好，最后才回到锅里。每一步都不难，只是需要耐心，隔夜再热更好吃。

225 min total分钟 (30 + 195)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Red wine另购：红酒(烹饪用)

Ingredients食材

  • Bacon培根 100 g · thick-cut bacon, cut into 1.5 cm strips (lardons)厚切培根，切 1.5 厘米宽条
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL), for browning the beef1 汤匙（15 mL），煎牛肉用

  • Carrots胡萝卜 200 g · 2 carrots, cut in 1 cm rounds2 根，切 1 厘米厚圆片

Method做法

  1. Preheat the oven to 160 C (325 F) with a rack in the lower middle.烤箱预热到 160°C（325°F），架子放中下层。
  2. Pat the beef thoroughly dry and season with 3 g of salt and the pepper.牛肉用厨房纸擦干，撒盐 3 克和黑胡椒。
  3. Fry the bacon in a large Dutch oven over medium heat for about 6 minutes until crisp, lift it out and leave the fat in the pot.大号铸铁锅中火放培根条，煎到油出来、变脆，约 6 分钟，捞出备用，锅里留油。
  4. Add the olive oil and brown the beef over high heat in 3 batches, 2-3 minutes a side, until deep brown all over.锅里加橄榄油，大火分 3 批煎牛肉，每面 2–3 分钟，直到四周都是深褐色。
  5. Remove the beef, add the diced onion and carrots to the pot and cook over medium heat 6 minutes until soft and lightly coloured.牛肉盛出，锅里放洋葱丁和胡萝卜，中火炒 6 分钟，炒软并略微上色。
  6. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste for 1 minute, sprinkle over the flour and cook 1 more minute until it no longer smells raw.加蒜和番茄酱炒 1 分钟，撒面粉再炒 1 分钟，把面粉炒到没有生粉味。
  7. Pour in the wine, scrape up the browned bits and boil 5 minutes to cook off some of the alcohol and sharpness.倒入红酒，刮起锅底的褐色渣，大火煮 5 分钟，让酒气和酸味散掉一部分。
  8. Return the beef and bacon with the stock, thyme and bay leaves so the liquid just covers the beef.牛肉和培根倒回锅里，加高汤、百里香和月桂叶，汤汁刚好没过牛肉。
  9. Bring to a simmer, cover and braise in the oven 2 hours 15 minutes, until a fork pulls the beef apart easily.烧开后盖盖，放进烤箱炖 2 小时 15 分钟，直到牛肉用叉子轻松拨开。
  10. While it braises, blanch the pearl onions in boiling water for 1 minute, cool in cold water and peel them.炖肉的同时，小洋葱在沸水里烫 1 分钟，冲冷水，剥皮。
  11. Melt 2 tsp of butter in a skillet over medium heat, brown the pearl onions 8 minutes until golden all over and set aside.平底锅中火放 2 茶匙黄油，小洋葱煎 8 分钟到四面金黄，盛出。
  12. Add the rest of the butter and the mushrooms to the same skillet and sauté over high heat 6-8 minutes until the liquid is gone and the edges golden, seasoning with 1 g salt.同一个锅加剩下的黄油和蘑菇，大火煎 6–8 分钟，水分收干、边缘金黄，撒盐 1 克。
  13. Take out the pot, stir in the onions and mushrooms and return to the oven 20 minutes.炖锅出炉，放入小洋葱和蘑菇，再放进 160°C 烤箱 20 分钟。
  14. Skim the fat from the surface, taste, adjust with the remaining salt and finish with parsley.撇去表面浮油，尝味，用剩下的盐调整，撒欧芹后上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Dry the beef and brown it in batches; a crowded pot steams and turns grey, losing the deep flavour that browning gives.牛肉要擦干、分批煎、不拥挤：一锅挤满会出水变灰，不上色就没有那层深色的风味。
  • Cook the onions and mushrooms separately; stewed with the meat they turn grey and mushy, while sautéing keeps their shape and caramel flavour.洋葱和蘑菇单独煎再加回：和肉一起炖会变烂变灰，分开煎才保留形状和焦香。
  • Boil the wine first; those minutes drive off raw alcohol and harshness so the finished sauce tastes round.红酒要先大火煮开：生酒的酸涩和酒精味靠这几分钟去掉一部分，成品才圆润。
  • Braise in the oven rather than on the stove; heat surrounds the pot evenly so the bottom does not scorch and it stays at a gentle simmer.放进烤箱而不是炉子：四周加热更均匀，不会糊底，小火自己保持住微沸。
  • It improves overnight; chilling lets you lift off the set fat and the flavours meld.隔夜再热更好吃：冷却后撇掉凝固的油，味道也融合得更好。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The sauce is thin酱汁太稀
    Lift out the meat, boil the sauce down 10 minutes over high heat and pour it back; next time cook the flour a full minute.捞出肉，把汤汁大火收浓 10 分钟再倒回；下次面粉要炒足 1 分钟。
  • The beef is still tough牛肉还是硬
    Return it covered to the oven for another 30 minutes; chuck is tender only after its collagen melts, so it is time, not more heat.继续盖盖在烤箱里炖 30 分钟再试；牛肩肉要炖到胶质融化才软，不是时间不够就是火太大。
  • The sauce is sharp and tastes of raw wine汤汁发酸、有酒的生味
    Simmer uncovered 15 minutes and add a pinch of sugar or a little tomato paste; next time reduce the wine for 5 minutes first.开盖微沸 15 分钟，加 1 茶匙糖或少许番茄酱圆润一下；下次红酒先煮 5 分钟。
