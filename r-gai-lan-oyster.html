Gai Lan in Oyster Sauce蚝油芥兰

A Cantonese dim sum and dinner staple, gai lan in oyster sauce should be vivid green, glossy, and crisp-tender from stem to leaf. Home versions often falter when thick stalks and delicate leaves cook for the same time, or when lingering water dilutes the sauce on the plate.茶楼和粤菜馆里几乎每桌必点的蚝油芥兰，讲究菜色翠绿油亮、茎脆叶嫩。家里常做不好，往往是因为粗茎和嫩叶同时下锅导致熟度不均，或者沥水不干把蚝油汁冲淡。

  • Salt盐 3 g · 1/2 tsp, for blanching water半茶匙，焯水用

Method做法

  1. Trim off the bottom 2 cm woody ends of the gai lan, peel the tough outer skin off thick stalks with a vegetable peeler, then split them in half.芥兰切掉底部约 2 厘米木质老梗，较粗的茎部用削皮刀削去外层粗皮，再对半剖开。
  2. Bring a wide pot with plenty of water (about 2-3 L) to a rolling boil over high heat and add the salt and 1 tbsp of the canola oil.宽口锅加足量清水（约 2–3 升）大火烧至大沸，放入盐和 1 汤匙植物油。
  3. Using tongs, hold the thick stem ends in the boiling water for 30 seconds first, then submerge the whole stalks for 60-90 more seconds, until bright green and the thickest stem is crisp-tender when pierced.用夹子把芥兰粗茎一端先浸入沸水烫 30 秒，再把整棵推入水中继续焯烫 60–90 秒，至茎叶翠绿发亮、用筷子轻掐最粗的茎部微软即可。
  4. Lift the gai lan out, drain in a colander for 30-60 seconds, shaking gently or blotting with paper towels, then arrange neatly on a platter.捞出芥兰放在漏勺里沥干 30–60 秒，轻轻抖动或用厨房纸吸干水分，再整齐码入长盘中。
  5. In a dry small saucepan over medium-low heat, warm the remaining 1 tbsp canola oil and fry the minced garlic for 15-20 seconds until fragrant and just starting to turn golden.取小锅擦干，倒入剩下的 1 汤匙植物油，中小火煸炒蒜末 15–20 秒至散出香味、刚开始泛黄即可。
  6. Add the oyster sauce, soy sauce, and sugar, bring to a simmer over medium-low heat, re-stir the cornstarch slurry and pour it in, then stir for about 15 seconds until glossy and lightly thickened.加入蚝油、生抽和糖，中小火煮开后重新搅匀水淀粉再淋入，推匀约 15 秒至酱汁微稠发亮。
  7. Remove from the heat, stir in the sesame oil, and spoon the hot garlic oyster sauce evenly over the gai lan.关火拌入香油，把热蚝油蒜蓉汁均匀浇在芥兰上即可。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Peeling thick stalks and giving them a 30-second head start lets the dense stems catch up with the tender leaves by the time they both come out of the water.粗茎削皮并且先下锅焯 30 秒，让最耐煮的部位先受热，整棵菜才能茎脆叶嫩地同时出锅。
  • A little salt in the blanching water seasons the stems, and a little oil gives the leaves a glossy sheen; the bright green color mainly comes from a quick blanch in plenty of boiling water.焯水时加一点盐能让茎部更入味，加一点油能让菜面更油亮；翠绿的颜色主要靠宽水大火快速焯烫得来。
  • A light cornstarch slurry lets the sauce coat the leaves in a thin, glossy layer instead of pooling in one spot or running off.用少量薄芡让蚝油汁均匀挂在菜上一层薄薄的亮膜，不会结块也不会流成一滩水。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The stems taste woody and stringy菜茎咬不动、有一层粗纤维皮
    Thick stems have a fibrous skin and need longer heat. Peel the thick stalks with a vegetable peeler, split them, and blanch them 30 seconds before adding the leaves.没有削皮，或整棵焯水时间太短；下次用削皮刀削掉粗茎外皮，剖开后先单独焯 30 秒再放入叶子。
  • The gai lan turned dull olive-yellow and mushy芥兰发黄发暗、叶子软烂
    Covering the pot or overcooking dulls the green color. Boil uncovered in plenty of water over high heat for about 1.5-2 minutes total, then spread out to cool.水量太少、加了锅盖或焯太久；一定要宽水大火敞口快焯，总时间控制在 1.5–2 分钟左右，捞出后摊开散热。
  • Water pooled at the bottom of the platter and washed off the flavor盘底积满浑水，吃起来没味道
    Drain the blanched stalks in a colander for 30-60 seconds before plating, and blot any stray water from the platter prior to spooning on the sauce.芥兰带水直接装盘稀释了酱汁；捞出后先在漏勺里沥干 30–60 秒再摆盘，或用厨房纸轻吸一下盘底再淋汁。
