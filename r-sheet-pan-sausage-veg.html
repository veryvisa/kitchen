Main主菜 · Western西式

Sheet-Pan Sausage and Vegetables香肠烤蔬菜

Pork sausage, potatoes and peppers roast together for a simple meal with very little cleanup. Evenly cut potatoes, space between the ingredients and a thermometer check for the sausage make the difference. For lunchboxes, cool completely, refrigerate within 2 hours, eat within 3 days, and reheat to 74°C (165°F).一盘烤出猪肉香肠、土豆和彩椒，备料简单，烤好后也省去多余锅具。成功的关键是让土豆切得均匀、食材在烤盘上留出空隙，并用温度计确认香肠熟透；带便当的话，彻底放凉后 2 小时内冷藏，3 天内吃完，加热到 74°C（165°F）。

60 min total分钟 (20 + 40)4 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Pork sausage德式香肠 500 g · about 4 raw Western-style pork sausages (such as Italian or breakfast sausage, not Chinese cured sausage)约 4 根西式生猪肉香肠（如意式或早餐香肠，不是中式腊肠）
  • Potatoes土豆 700 g · about 4 medium potatoes, cut into 2 cm chunks约 4 个中等土豆，切 2 厘米块
  • Bell peppers彩椒 300 g · about 2 peppers, seeded and cut into chunks约 2 个，去籽切块
  • Onions洋葱 200 g · 1 large onion, cut into wedges1 个大洋葱，切成粗瓣
  • Broccoli西兰花 250 g · about 1/2 head, cut into florets约 1/2 颗，切成小朵
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · about 3 cloves, minced约 3 瓣，切碎
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 28 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Dried oregano干牛至/香草 2 g · about 1 tsp约 1 茶匙
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp约 2/3 茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · about 1/2 tsp约 1/2 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F) with a rack in the center.烤箱预热至 220°C（425°F），并把烤架放在中层。
  2. Toss the potatoes, peppers and onion in a large bowl with the olive oil, garlic, oregano, salt and black pepper.把土豆、彩椒和洋葱放入大碗，加入橄榄油、蒜、牛至、盐和黑胡椒拌匀。
  3. Spread the vegetables on a rimmed baking sheet with space between the pieces.把蔬菜铺在带边烤盘上，彼此留出空隙。
  4. Place the sausages among the vegetables and roast for 18 minutes until the potatoes begin to soften and the sausages start to brown.将香肠放在蔬菜之间，烤 18 分钟，至土豆开始变软、香肠表面上色。
  5. Remove the pan, flip the vegetables and sausages once, then scatter the broccoli on top.取出烤盘，把蔬菜和香肠都翻动一次，再铺上西兰花。
  6. Roast for another 12–17 minutes until the potatoes are golden and the vegetables have lightly browned edges.继续烤 12–17 分钟，至土豆金黄、蔬菜边缘微焦。
  7. Check the center of the thickest sausage with a thermometer and remove the pan once it reaches 71°C (160°F). Without a thermometer, cut into the thickest sausage: the center should show no pink and the juices should run clear — but a thermometer is still the more reliable check.用温度计测量最粗香肠的中心，达到 71°C（160°F）后取出；没有温度计时，切开最粗的一根检查，中心应没有粉红色且流出的汁水清澈——但还是建议备一支温度计更可靠。
  8. Rest for 3 minutes before serving.静置 3 分钟后盛出。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cut the potatoes evenly; oversized pieces may stay firm while tiny pieces can dry out.土豆切成相近大小，太大的块还没熟透，太小的块则容易烤干。
  • Leave space on the pan so the vegetables can brown; crowding encourages them to steam.烤盘上留出空隙，让蔬菜烤出焦边；铺得太满会更像焖煮。
  • Broccoli cooks faster than potatoes, so add it for the second part of roasting to keep it intact and prevent scorching.西兰花比土豆熟得快，后半段再加入，能保留花朵形状并避免焦黑。
  • Sausage thickness and oven heat both vary, so color alone is not reliable — always check that the center reaches 71°C (160°F).香肠粗细和烤箱火力都有差异，看颜色不可靠，务必测中心温度达到 71°C（160°F）。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The potatoes are still firm but the sausages are dry土豆中心还硬，香肠已经太干
    Next time cut the potatoes to about 1.5 cm, keeping the same approach of roasting them together with the sausages from the start; use a thermometer and pull the sausages as soon as they hit 71°C (160°F) rather than waiting on the potatoes.下次把土豆切到约 1.5 厘米，和香肠一起入炉的做法不变；用温度计及时确认香肠一到 71°C（160°F）就取出，不要等土豆。
  • The vegetables released liquid and did not brown蔬菜出水、没有焦边
    Do not crowd the pan; use two pans if needed, and pat the vegetables dry before tossing them with oil.烤盘不要铺得太满，必要时分成两盘；蔬菜表面水分擦干后再拌油。
  • The sausage is browned outside but undercooked inside香肠外面焦了但里面没熟
    Lower the oven to 200°C (400°F) and continue roasting until the center reaches 71°C (160°F); next time place the sausages toward the top or center of the pan, flip once for even heat, and tent loosely with foil if the skins are already dark.把烤箱温度调低至 200°C（400°F），继续烤至中心达到 71°C（160°F）；下次把香肠放在烤盘上层或靠中间的位置，翻面一次让受热均匀，外皮已经深色就松松盖一层锡纸继续烤。
