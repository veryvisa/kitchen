Tofu Skin Rolls腐皮卷

A Cantonese dim sum and home-cooking favorite with a crisp golden wrapper and a savory tofu-shiitake filling. Two habits keep it clean: cook the filling dry and let it cool before rolling, and soften the dried tofu skin with a damp cloth and a short rest rather than soaking it, so it folds without tearing.粤式茶楼和家常餐桌都常见的腐皮卷，外皮金黄酥脆，内里是鲜香软弹的豆腐香菇馅。做好的关键有两点：馅料要提前炒干水汽再放凉包，干腐皮用湿布擦过再静置回软而不能泡水，否则下锅一煎就破。

Ingredients食材

  • Dried tofu skin sheets (yuba)干腐皮 100 g · 3 to 4 thin round dried tofu skin sheets (about 30-40 cm across), about 100 g total, trimmed to about 18 cm squares圆形薄干腐皮（鲜竹腐皮一类）3–4 张，约 100 克，按包装直径裁成约 18 厘米见方 choose plain unseasoned thin round sheets; thicker stick-type dried yuba is too brittle for this method选原味无盐的圆形薄干腐皮；较厚的腐竹类不适合这个做法，容易裂
  • Firm tofu老豆腐 200 g · 1/2 block firm tofu (about 200 g), crumbled and pressed半块老豆腐（约 200 克），捏碎挤水
  • Shiitake香菇 120 g · about 6 fresh shiitake caps (about 120 g), finely diced to about 3 mm鲜香菇约 6 朵（约 120 克），去蒂切 3 毫米细丁 rehydrated dried shiitake give deeper aroma用泡发干香菇香味更浓
  • Carrots胡萝卜 80 g · 1 small carrot (about 80 g), finely diced to about 3 mm1 根小胡萝卜（约 80 克），切 3 毫米细丁
  • Celery西芹 60 g · 1 rib celery (about 60 g), finely diced to about 3 mm1 根西芹（约 60 克），切 3 毫米细丁
  • Ginger姜 6 g · finely minced, about 1 tbsp切细末，约 1 汤匙
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 2 stalks, finely chopped2 根，切葱花
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL); not vegetarian — swap in a vegetarian mushroom sauce if needed1 汤匙（15 mL），非素食，吃素可换素食蘑菇酱
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 10 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Salt盐 1.5 g · 1/4 tsp四分之一茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · 1/4 tsp四分之一茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 12 g · 4 tsp, split for filling and sealing paste4 茶匙，分馅料和封口糊
  • Canola oil菜籽油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL), split for stir-frying and pan-frying3 汤匙（45 mL），炒馅和煎卷用

Method做法

  1. Crumble the firm tofu into small curds and squeeze firmly in a clean cloth or paper towel to press out moisture.把老豆腐捏成细碎状，包进干净纱布或厨房纸里用力挤干水分。
  2. Heat 1 tbsp canola oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and stir-fry the ginger and shiitake for 2 minutes until the moisture evaporates and the edges brown, then add the carrot and celery and stir-fry 2 more minutes.炒锅放 1 汤匙菜籽油，中大火下姜末和香菇丁翻炒 2 分钟至水汽收干、边缘微焦，再加胡萝卜丁和西芹丁续炒 2 分钟。
  3. Add the squeezed tofu, black pepper, and sesame oil. Mix the soy sauce, oyster sauce, and 2 tsp cornstarch with 1 tbsp water into a slurry, pour it in, and stir 2 more minutes until cohesive; season with the salt and sugar.加入挤干的豆腐、黑胡椒、香油，把 1 汤匙生抽、1 汤匙蚝油和 2 茶匙玉米淀粉加 1 汤匙水调成浆汁倒入，继续炒 2 分钟至抱团，加盐和糖调味。
  4. Fold in the green onions, spread the filling on a plate, and cool to room temperature (about 10-15 minutes) or chill 10 minutes, so steam does not soften the wrapper; if making ahead, refrigerate the filling or rolled rolls and fry within 24 hours.拌入葱花后盛到平盘里摊开，放凉至室温（约 10–15 分钟）或冷藏 10 分钟，避免热气把腐皮焖软；如要提前做好，馅料和卷好的生卷都要冷藏，并在 24 小时内下锅煎。
  5. Whisk the remaining 2 tsp cornstarch with 1 tbsp water to form a thick sealing paste.把剩下的 2 茶匙玉米淀粉加 1 汤匙清水拌匀，调成浓稠的封口糊。
  6. Wipe each dried tofu skin sheet on both sides with a wrung damp cloth, then let it rest 20-30 seconds until pliable (if it is brittle, a quick 2-3 second dip in warm water helps); cut into 18 cm squares only after it softens.用拧干的湿毛巾在干腐皮两面轻擦，静置 20–30 秒至回软变得柔韧（偏脆的可用温水快速蘸 2–3 秒），回软后再裁成约 18 厘米见方的片。
  7. Near the edge closest to you, spoon 2 tbsp cooled filling shaped into a 10 cm log. Fold the bottom edge up and over, fold in both sides, roll to the far edge, and dab starch paste on the flap to seal (the finished roll is about 10 cm long and 3 cm thick).每张腐皮靠近自己的一边放 2 汤匙放凉的馅料，整成约 10 厘米长条，向上折起底边，再折两侧，卷到对边，封口处涂淀粉糊粘牢（卷好约 10 厘米长、3 厘米粗）。
  8. Heat the remaining 2 tbsp oil in a wide skillet over medium heat, place the rolls seam-side down, fry 1-2 minutes to set the seal, then turn a quarter-turn every 1.5-2 minutes, for 6-8 minutes total until evenly golden and crisp on all sides.平底锅放剩下的 2 汤匙油烧热，中火把腐皮卷收口朝下排入，先煎 1–2 分钟定型，之后每隔 1.5–2 分钟转动一次，共煎 6–8 分钟至四面金黄酥脆。
  9. Transfer to a wire rack to drain, rest 1-2 minutes, then slice diagonally in half with a sharp knife and serve hot.夹出放在网架上沥油，静置 1–2 分钟后用快刀斜切成两段，趁热装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Pressing the tofu and frying the vegetables dry prevents moisture from turning the crisp wrapper gummy.老豆腐挤干并把蔬菜提前炒干，包好的腐皮卷内部不出水，表皮才能保持酥脆。
  • Wiping dried yuba with a damp cloth and letting it rest gives just enough flexibility to roll, without soaking up enough water to turn fragile and soggy.干腐皮用湿布擦过后静置回软即可，直接下水浸泡会吸饱水分变得极易破损。
  • Starting seam-side down sets the seal right away, so the cornstarch paste holds once you begin turning the roll.下锅先将收口朝下煎定型，淀粉糊受热立刻封住接口，之后再转动翻面就不容易散开。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The wrapper cracks when rolling or unravels in the pan腐皮一卷就裂，或者下锅散开
    If a sheet is too dry and brittle, wipe it again and let it rest a little longer before rolling; keep the sealing paste thick, and start seam-side down for 1-2 minutes before turning.太干发脆就再擦一遍湿布，多静置一会儿再卷；封口糊要调稠，下锅收口朝下先煎 1–2 分钟定型后再翻动。
  • The wrapper scorches or turns soggy soon after cooking外皮煎焦了，或者放一会儿就软塌
    Tofu skin browns quickly, so stick to medium or medium-low heat; drain on a wire rack rather than a flat plate, so steam does not soften the bottom.腐皮含豆蛋白极易上色，火候保持中火或中小火；出锅后架在烤网上沥油，避免放在平盘上让底部被蒸汽闷软。
  • The filling falls out when sliced切开时馅料散落出来
    Chop the tofu and vegetables finer and more evenly, press out more moisture, roll snugly, and let the cooked rolls rest 1-2 minutes before slicing with a sharp knife.把豆腐和蔬菜切得更细碎均匀、挤得更干，卷的时候压紧实，煎好后静置 1–2 分钟再用快刀下刀。
