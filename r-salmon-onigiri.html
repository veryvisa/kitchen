Salmon Onigiri三文鱼饭团

Salmon onigiri needs no special mold—just warm short-grain rice, salted pan-seared salmon and crisp nori—making it a dependable lunchbox staple. The usual mistakes are soggy rice that falls apart or flaking the fish too fine.日式三文鱼饭团不用复杂模具，一碗温热短粒米饭、一块盐煎三文鱼和几条烤海苔就能捏出四个三角饭团，是学生和上班族带午饭的省心选择。常见失误是米饭太湿捏不成团，或把鱼肉拨得太碎失去口感。

40 min total分钟 (10 + 30)2 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Short-grain rice短粒米(寿司米) 280 g · 1.5 cups dry Japanese short-grain rice (about 280 g)日本短粒米或东北大米 1.5 量杯（约 280 克生米） short-grain rice has the starch needed to hold together短粒米黏性足才捏得紧
  • Salmon fillet三文鱼排 180 g · about 180 g salmon fillet三文鱼柳约 180 克
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp total (for salting the fish and hands)共约 2/3 茶匙（腌鱼和抹手分别用）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 7 g · 1/2 tbsp (7.5 mL)半汤匙（7.5 mL）
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 8 g · 1 tbsp toasted white sesame seeds1 汤匙熟白芝麻
  • Roasted nori seaweed烤海苔 6 g · 2 full sheets roasted nori seaweed, halved into strips2 大张烤海苔，每张剪成两条

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Rinse the short-grain rice twice, drain, cook with 330 mL water in a rice cooker, rest 10 minutes and fluff until warm enough to handle.短粒米轻淘两次后沥干，加 330 毫升清水用电饭煲煮熟，焖 10 分钟后打散晾到温热不烫手。
  2. Pat the salmon dry, rub both sides with 1/3 tsp salt, rest 5 minutes and blot away any released moisture.三文鱼擦干表面，两面均匀抹上 1/3 茶匙盐，静置 5 分钟后吸干渗出的水分。
  3. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat and sear the salmon for 3 to 4 minutes per side until lightly crisp and the center reaches 70°C.平底锅放油中火烧热，放入三文鱼每面煎 3–4 分钟，至表面微脆、中心温度达到 70°C。
  4. Let the salmon cool slightly, run a finger along the fillet to find and pull out any pin bones, then discard the skin and flake the fish with a fork into rough 1 cm chunks rather than a paste.取出三文鱼放凉一会儿，用手指沿着鱼柳摸一遍挑出鱼刺，去皮后用叉子拨成约 1 厘米的小块，不要捣成泥。
  5. Fold the toasted sesame seeds into the warm rice, and set out a small bowl of water.把熟白芝麻拌进温热米饭里，另备一小碗清水。
  6. Wet your palms, rub in a small pinch of salt (about 1/8 tsp), flatten a quarter of the rice, place a quarter of the salmon in the center and fold the rice over it.双手沾湿，再抹上一小撮盐（约 1/8 茶匙）搓匀，取四分之一米饭在掌心压扁，中间放四分之一三文鱼块再合拢包住。
  7. Cup your hands into a triangle and press lightly three or four times as you rotate the rice to shape four compact triangles.两手掌窝成三角，每转一次轻压三四下，塑成边缘紧实但不死硬的 4 个三角饭团。
  8. Wrap a strip of nori around the base right before eating, or cool the onigiri completely, wrap in plastic, pack with an ice pack, and keep the nori separate for lunchboxes; eat within 4 hours at room temperature or refrigerate and eat the next day.现吃直接用海苔条包住底部；带便当则把饭团彻底晾凉后用保鲜膜包好、配保冷袋和冰袋，海苔单独装袋，4 小时内吃完或冷藏到次日。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Drain the rinsed rice well and cook at the standard ratio (about 1:1.2 by weight); short-grain rice is sticky enough on its own once well drained.淘米后沥干水再按标准比例（约 1:1.2 米水重量比）煮，煮好的短粒米本身黏性就够，捏好后不容易散塌。
  • Wet your palms first to keep the rice from sticking, then rub in a pinch of salt; this seasons the outside more reliably than salted water alone.手心先沾水防粘，再抹一小撮盐搓匀，这样饭团外层才有均匀的底咸，比靠盐水更可靠。
  • Shape with firm cupping rather than heavy squeezing so the outside holds while the inside stays airy.捏饭团靠合拢塑形而不是使劲揉死，外紧内松吃起来才蓬松。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The rice ball crumbles when picked up饭团一拿起来就散架
    Long-grain rice lacks stickiness, or the rice cooled too much; use warm short-grain rice and shape it with gentle, even pressure inside a sheet of plastic wrap — no need to squeeze hard.可能用了长粒米，或米饭凉透了才捏；换短粒米并趁温热塑形，可借助一张保鲜膜轻轻压合定型，不必使劲揉死。
  • The nori turned chewy and tough海苔变韧咬不动
    Steam or sitting time softened the seaweed; cool the rice balls completely and wrap the nori just before eating.热气或放置时间太久让海苔吸湿；带饭时把饭团完全晾凉，海苔分开装，吃前再包。
