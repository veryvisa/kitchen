Yuxiang Shredded Pork鱼香肉丝

There is no fish in Yuxiang. The name describes a Sichuan sauce built from chili paste, scallion, ginger and garlic, where salty, sweet, sour and spicy all arrive together. Success comes from a balanced sauce and chili paste fried until the oil turns red, while starch keeps the pork silky.鱼香肉丝里没有鱼，「鱼香」是川菜里用泡辣椒、葱姜蒜调出的复合味：咸、甜、酸、辣一起到。它的成败在碗汁的平衡和豆瓣酱炒出红油，肉丝则靠上浆保持滑嫩。

38 min total分钟 (20 + 8)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Doubanjiang, Shaoxing wine另购：豆瓣酱、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Pork loin猪里脊 250 g · about 250 g pork loin, cut into matchsticks里脊肉约 250 克，切丝
  • Carrots胡萝卜 80 g · half a carrot, julienned半根，切丝
  • Celery西芹 80 g · 1 stalk, julienned1 根，切丝 classically bamboo shoots and wood ear are used原版用竹笋和木耳，可换
  • Bell peppers彩椒 80 g · half a bell pepper, cut into strips半个青椒，切丝
  • Doubanjiang豆瓣酱 24 g · a generous tablespoon (about 24 g)1 汤匙多（约 24 克） pickled chilies are traditional; doubanjiang is the easy substitute原版用泡椒，买不到就用豆瓣酱
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · 4 cloves, minced4 瓣，切末
  • Ginger姜 10 g · minced, about 1 tbsp切末，约 1 汤匙
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 2 stalks, chopped2 根，切葱花
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp, for the pork1 汤匙，腌肉用
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp (split between marinade and sauce)半汤匙（腌肉和调汁共用）
  • Sugar白糖 12 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Rice vinegar米醋 15 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 9 g · 3 tsp (2 for the pork, 1 for the sauce)3 茶匙（腌肉 2 茶匙，调汁 1 茶匙）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Slice the pork across the grain into strips about 5 mm thick.里脊肉逆着纹理切成约 5 毫米粗的肉丝。
  2. Mix the pork with the wine, 1 tsp soy sauce, 2 tsp cornstarch, 1 tbsp water and 1 tsp oil, and marinate 10 minutes.肉丝加料酒、生抽 1 茶匙、淀粉 2 茶匙、1 汤匙水和 1 茶匙油，抓匀腌 10 分钟。
  3. Stir the sugar, vinegar, remaining soy sauce, 1 tsp cornstarch and 3 tbsp water into a sauce.糖、醋、剩下的生抽、淀粉 1 茶匙和 3 汤匙水调成碗汁。
  4. Cut the carrot, celery and pepper into matchsticks about the same thickness as the pork.胡萝卜、芹菜、青椒都切成跟肉丝差不多粗的丝。
  5. Heat the wok, add 2 tbsp oil, scatter in the pork and stir quickly for about 40 seconds until it turns white, then remove.锅烧热，倒 2 汤匙油，下肉丝快速划散，炒到变白，约 40 秒，盛出。
  6. Turn the heat to medium-low and fry the doubanjiang in the leftover oil for about 40 seconds until the oil turns red and smells rich.锅里余油转中小火，下豆瓣酱炒约 40 秒，油变红、酱香冒出来为止。
  7. Add the ginger and garlic and stir for 10 seconds.放入姜末、蒜末炒 10 秒。
  8. Turn the heat to high, add the carrot, celery and pepper and stir-fry for about 1 minute.转大火，倒入胡萝卜、芹菜和青椒，炒约 1 分钟。
  9. Return the pork, pour in the sauce and keep tossing for about 30 seconds until it turns thick and glossy.肉丝回锅，倒入碗汁，不停翻炒约 30 秒，汁变得浓稠亮泽。
  10. Scatter the scallion, toss once and serve.撒葱花，翻匀出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Fry the doubanjiang on low heat until the oil turns red; raw paste just tastes salty, while fried paste tastes deep and rounded.豆瓣酱一定要小火炒出红油，生豆瓣只有咸味，没有香；油变红是信号。
  • Equal parts sugar and vinegar is the baseline; the sweet-sour sits over the salt so no single flavor dominates.糖和醋 1:1 是基准，酸甜要「在咸之上」，吃起来像什么都有一点，没有哪个特别冲。
  • Cook the pork first and return it later; stir-frying it with the vegetables makes it watery and tough.肉丝先滑炒再回锅，和蔬菜一起炒会出水又老。
  • Mix and taste the sauce before you start. The doubanjiang is already salty, so the soy sauce is kept low.碗汁提前调好，先尝一下，豆瓣酱本身有咸味，所以酱油只放一点点。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • It is too salty太咸
    Brands of doubanjiang vary a lot. Start with 2 tsp next time and add more after tasting; for the finished dish add a pinch of sugar and half a teaspoon of vinegar.豆瓣酱品牌差异大。下次先少放到 2 茶匙，尝了再补；已做成的加一小撮糖和半茶匙醋。
  • The pork clumps together肉丝粘成一团
    Separate the strips as they go in, use enough oil and break them up quickly with chopsticks. Too much starch also makes them clump, so stay at 2 tsp.下锅前要抓散，锅里油足、一开始用筷子快速划散；淀粉腌得过多也会粘，控制在 2 茶匙。
  • The sauce is thin and pools at the bottom汁太稀，盘底有一滩水
    Keep the heat high and never cover the pan so vegetables do not weep. If thin, stir half a teaspoon of cornstarch into a spoonful of water and add it while stirring.蔬菜不要出水：大火快炒，不要盖盖；汁稀就加半茶匙淀粉加一勺水，边倒边搅。
