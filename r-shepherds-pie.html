Main主菜 · Western西式

Shepherd's Pie牧羊人派

Savory lamb and vegetables sit beneath a golden layer of mashed potato in this family-style bake. Reduce the filling before assembling, or excess liquid can seep into the topping.羊肉馅在浓郁的蔬菜肉汁下铺底，土豆泥烤成金黄的盖层，是适合一家人分食的家常烤盘菜。馅料要先收浓再入烤盘，否则汁水会浸透土豆泥。

105 min total分钟 (30 + 75)6 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Ground lamb另购：羊肉末

Ingredients食材

  • Potatoes土豆 1100 g · about 6 potatoes, weighed before peeling土豆约 6 个，去皮前称重
  • Carrots胡萝卜 180 g · 2 carrots, diced2 根，切小丁
  • Onions洋葱 180 g · 1 large onion, diced1 个大洋葱，切丁
  • Frozen mixed vegetables冷冻混合蔬菜 180 g · 180 g frozen mixed vegetables (about 1 1/4 cups, volume varies by mix)180 克冷冻杂菜（约 1 又 1/4 杯，视菜量而不同）
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 3 cloves, minced3 瓣，切末
  • Tomato paste番茄膏 30 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • All-purpose flour面粉 20 g · about 2 tbsp约 2 汤匙
  • Beef broth牛肉汤 300 g · about 1 1/4 cups beef stock, low-sodium if possible约 1 又 1/4 杯牛肉汤，选低钠的更好控盐

  • Salt盐 8 g · about 1 1/3 tsp, divided约 1 又 1/3 茶匙，分次使用

Method做法

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C and cut the peeled potatoes into 4 cm chunks.烤箱预热至 200°C，土豆去皮切成约 4 厘米块。
  2. Put the potatoes in a pot of cold water with 5 g salt, bring to a boil over high heat and simmer over medium heat for 15–18 minutes until easily pierced.土豆放入冷水锅中，加 5 克盐，大火煮开后转中火煮 15–18 分钟，至刀尖轻松穿过。
  3. Drain the potatoes, return them to the warm pot and stir over low heat for 1 minute until surface moisture evaporates, then mash until smooth.土豆沥干后放回热锅，小火翻动 1 分钟，至表面水汽散去，先压成顺滑土豆泥。
  4. Gradually stir in the butter and warm milk, season with some of the black pepper, and avoid overworking the mash or it can turn gluey.逐步拌入黄油和温牛奶，调入部分黑胡椒，不要过度搅打以免土豆泥发黏。
  5. Heat the oil in a pan wide enough for the lamb over medium heat and break it up for 7-9 minutes until no pink remains, spot-checking several larger pieces to confirm 71°C; brown in two batches if the pan is crowded, or the lamb will release liquid and steam instead of browning.用够大的宽锅中火加热油，放入羊肉末炒散 7–9 分钟，至不见粉红且多点抽查中心达到 71°C；锅不够宽就分两批炒，避免肉末出水变闷煮。
  6. Add the onion and carrot and cook over medium heat for 6 minutes until the onion softens.加入洋葱和胡萝卜，中火炒 6 分钟，至洋葱变软。
  7. Add the garlic and tomato paste and cook for 1 minute until the paste darkens slightly.加入蒜末和番茄膏，炒 1 分钟至番茄膏颜色变深。
  8. Sprinkle in the flour, rosemary and thyme and stir constantly for 1-2 minutes until no raw flour taste remains.撒入面粉、迷迭香和百里香，持续翻拌 1–2 分钟，炒到没有生面粉味。
  9. Pour in the stock and cook over medium heat for at least 5 minutes, stirring often, until the gravy is visibly thick and coats the back of a spoon — the exact time varies with the pan, heat and moisture from the vegetables.倒入牛肉汤，中火煮至少 5 分钟并不断搅拌，看到肉汁明显变浓、能挂在勺背才停，锅宽、火力或蔬菜出水都会影响实际收汁时间。
  10. Stir in the frozen vegetables and remaining salt and pepper, then cook for 2 minutes until heated through.拌入冷冻杂菜和剩余盐、黑胡椒，煮 2 分钟至刚刚热透。
  11. Spread the piping-hot lamb filling in a baking dish about 23x33 cm and cover it evenly with the mashed potatoes, sealing it to the edges so the filling cannot bubble up through gaps.把滚热的羊肉馅铺进约 23×33 厘米的烤盘，再将土豆泥均匀盖在馅料上并抹到边缘封住，防止馅料从缝隙冒出来。
  12. Rake shallow lines across the mash with a fork and bake for 25-30 minutes until bubbling at the edges, golden on top, and hot in the center. If the filling and mash had cooled or been refrigerated before assembly, bake considerably longer and confirm the center reaches 74°C.用叉子在土豆泥表面划出浅纹，烤 25–30 分钟至边缘冒泡、表面金黄、中心摸起来烫手；如果馅料和土豆泥在装盘时已经放凉或提前冷藏过，烤的时间要明显延长，并确认中心达到 74°C。
  13. Rest the pie for 10 minutes after baking so the filling can settle before slicing.出炉静置 10 分钟，让馅料稍稍定形后分切。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Drying the cooked potatoes helps keep the mash fluffy and lets it brown in the oven.土豆煮好后先蒸干表面水分，土豆泥才不会稀软，烤后也更容易上色。
  • Reduce the filling until it coats a spoon so it does not turn soupy in the dish.馅料收浓到能挂住勺背，才不会在烤盘里变成一层汤。
  • Break up and brown the lamb (center 71°C) before adding the vegetables and gravy ingredients, so no raw chunks hide in the filling; the whole pot then keeps simmering for several more minutes, and it's that full process — not just the initial browning — that makes it safely cooked.羊肉末先炒散炒透（中心达 71°C）再加蔬菜和肉汁材料，能避免大块生肉藏在馅里；之后整锅还要继续小火煮几分钟，真正的熟透是靠这整个过程，不是煎炒那一步就定了。
  • Fork ridges give the topping more surface area to brown and make its color easy to judge.土豆泥用叉子划纹会增加上色的表面积，也方便观察烘烤程度。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The filling is watery and the mash is soggy underneath馅料出水，土豆泥底部湿软
    Return the filling to the pan and reduce it before assembling; do not skip the reduction next time.把馅料倒回锅中小火收浓后再铺盘；下次不要省略收汁步骤。
  • The mashed potato is runny and collapses after baking土豆泥太稀，烤后塌陷
    Return the potatoes to the warm pot to dry, then add milk sparingly; drain them thoroughly next time.把土豆放回热锅小火蒸干水汽，再少量加入牛奶；下次确保土豆沥干。
  • The top is dark but the filling is not hot表面焦黑但馅料还不热
    Tent the top loosely with foil and continue baking until hot throughout; make sure the filling is piping hot when assembling next time.用锡纸松松盖住表面并继续烤至中心热透；下次确认馅料入盘时仍然滚热。
