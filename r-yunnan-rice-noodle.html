Crossing-the-Bridge Rice Noodles过桥米线（家常版）

Crossing-the-Bridge Rice Noodles rely on a piping hot broth sealed under a thin layer of oil to keep the noodles and sliced chicken tender. The traditional way serves raw slices for diners to cook in the boiling broth at the table; this home version cooks everything in the kitchen first for food safety, trading a little of the tableside drama for certainty. Simmering bone-in drumsticks with chicken stock builds rich body in a bit over 40 minutes. Common pitfalls are serving into a cold bowl that drops the broth temperature too fast, or slicing the chicken too thick to cook evenly.云南过桥米线讲究滚烫高汤与油膜锁温，让米线和鸡肉片保持滑嫩烫口；传统吃法是生片上桌、滚汤现烫，这个家常版出于食品安全考虑改成厨房里先烫熟再分碗，少了一点仪式感但更稳妥。家常做法不必熬一整天大骨汤，用带骨鸡腿配合鸡高汤提鲜，四十多分钟就能上桌。最容易失手的是碗不够热导致汤温骤降，或者鸡片切太厚而煮老、没熟透。

Ingredients食材

  • Dried round rice noodles干米线 200 g · 200 g dried round rice noodles, soaked first according to the package (usually 30 minutes to a few hours, depending on thickness)干米线 200 克，按包装说明先泡软（通常 30 分钟到几小时，看粗细）
  • Chicken drumsticks鸡腿(带骨) 300 g · 2 bone-in chicken drumsticks (about 300 g, for broth)带骨鸡腿 2 只（约 300 克，熬汤用）
  • Boneless skinless chicken breast鸡胸肉 180 g · 1 boneless skinless chicken breast (about 180 g, for slicing)去皮鸡胸肉 1 块（约 180 克，切片用）
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 750 g · about 750 mL (about 3.2 cups) chicken stock; a low-sodium version is better if you can find it — if not, halve the added salt below约 750 mL（约 3.2 杯）鸡高汤；买得到低钠款更好，普通款就把下面的盐减半
  • Eggs鸡蛋 100 g · 2 large eggs2 个大鸡蛋
  • Mung bean sprouts绿豆芽 100 g · 1 handful bean sprouts (about 100 g)豆芽 1 把（约 100 克）
  • Garlic chives (Chinese chives)韭菜 40 g · 1 small bunch garlic chives (about 40 g), cut into lengths韭菜 1 小把（约 40 克），切段
  • Shiitake香菇 60 g · 3 fresh shiitake mushrooms (about 60 g), sliced鲜香菇 3 朵（约 60 克），切片
  • Firm tofu老豆腐 100 g · about 100 g firm tofu, thinly sliced; tofu skin (yuba) is closer to the traditional topping if you have it, torn into pieces instead老豆腐约 100 克，切薄片；有豆腐皮（yuba）更接近传统配菜，撕小片代替
  • Ginger姜 15 g · 4 slices (for broth)4 片（熬汤用）
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 25 g · 2 stalks, 1 knotted for broth and 1 chopped2 根，1 根打结熬汤，1 根切葱花
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 3 g · 1 tsp (for coating chicken slices)1 茶匙（拌鸡片用）

  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · a pinch of white pepper (ground black pepper is fine if that is what you have)白胡椒粉少许（没有可用现磨黑胡椒代替）

Method做法

  1. Chill the chicken breast in the freezer for 15 minutes until firm around the edges.鸡胸肉放入冷冻室微冻 15 分钟至边缘微硬。
  2. Slice the chicken breast against the grain into about 1-2 mm thin slices.取出鸡胸肉，逆着肉纹切成约 1–2 毫米的薄片。
  3. Toss the chicken slices with the cornstarch and 1 tsp oil, then set aside.鸡片加玉米淀粉和 1 茶匙食用油拌匀，静置备用。
  4. Bring the drumsticks from cold water to a boil with 2 ginger slices and the wine for 2 minutes, then rinse in warm water.鸡腿冷水下锅，加 2 片姜和料酒大火煮沸 2 分钟后捞出用温水冲净。
  5. Combine the chicken stock, 500 mL water, drumsticks, remaining ginger and knotted scallion, bring to a boil and simmer over medium-low heat for 25 minutes until the flavours meld and the drumsticks are cooked through.汤锅倒入鸡高汤和 500 毫升清水，下鸡腿、剩余姜片和葱结，大火烧开转中小火熬 25 分钟至汤味融合、鸡腿熟透。
  6. Lift out the drumsticks to shred the meat once cool, and discard the ginger and scallion.捞出鸡腿稍凉后拆成鸡丝备用，弃去姜片和葱结。
  7. Season the broth with salt and pepper and keep it at a gentle simmer.高汤中加入盐和胡椒粉调味，保持小火微滚。
  8. Drop the pre-soaked noodles into boiling water and cook over medium heat for 3 to 5 minutes until tender with no hard core, then rinse in cold water and drain.泡软的米线下沸水，中火煮 3 到 5 分钟至掐断无硬芯，捞出过凉水沥干。
  9. Using the same pot of boiling water, blanch the shiitake and tofu for 1.5 to 2 minutes first, then add the bean sprouts and garlic chives for another 20 to 30 seconds until just tender, and drain everything together.利用同一锅沸水，先下香菇片和豆腐片焯 1.5 到 2 分钟，再加豆芽和韭菜续煮 20 到 30 秒至刚断生，一起捞出沥干。
  10. Warm two large ceramic bowls with boiling water for 1 minute, then pour the water out.烧开水倒入两个大瓷碗中烫热 1 分钟后倒掉。
  11. Crack the eggs into a small bowl first, let the broth drop to a gentle simmer, then slide the eggs in close to the surface and poach over low heat for 3-4 minutes until both the white and the yolk are fully set (if anyone eating is a young child, pregnant or immunocompromised, make sure the yolk is completely set).鸡蛋先打入小碗，高汤转小火至微沸后，贴着汤面把蛋滑入，小火煮 3 到 4 分钟至蛋白和蛋黄都凝固（家中有幼儿、孕妇或免疫力弱者，蛋黄务必煮到完全凝固）。
  12. With the chicken sliced 1-2 mm thin, add it in small batches to the gently simmering broth and separate the pieces with a slotted spoon; cook until fully opaque with no pink left, then another 10-20 seconds (well past 74°C by this point) — no need to check each slice with a thermometer.鸡片切 1–2 毫米薄，分小批下微沸汤中用漏勺拨散，烫到完全变白、看不到粉红后再煮 10–20 秒（此时远超 74°C），不用逐片测温。
  13. Heat the remaining oil in a small pan just until it looks glossy and faintly rippled (not smoking), then remove from the heat and pour it slowly down the side of the broth pot to form an oil seal; keep your face back from the pot so rising steam or a splash does not burn you.小锅将剩余食用油加热到表面发亮、微微起纹即可（不要烧到冒烟），离火后沿锅边缓慢倒入高汤锅形成油面，不要俯身在锅上方，避免热汤溅起烫伤。
  14. Divide the noodles, blanched toppings and shredded chicken into the warm bowls, ladle over the boiling broth, chicken slices and eggs, and top with chopped green onion.将米线、焯好的配菜和鸡丝分装入预热好的大碗，浇入滚汤、鸡片和鸡蛋，撒上葱花。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Pre-warming the bowls and sealing the broth with hot oil slows heat loss so the soup stays piping hot through the meal.先用开水烫碗再靠油面锁热，能显著减慢散热，米线吃到最后都还是滚烫的。
  • Chilling the chicken breast makes paper-thin slicing easy, allowing it to hit 74°C quickly without drying out.鸡胸肉微冻后再切更容易切得薄且均匀，下锅不到 1 分钟就能达到 74°C 并保持鲜嫩。
  • Rinsing the cooked rice noodles removes surface starch so the broth stays clear instead of turning cloudy.干米线煮好后过一下凉水并充分沥干，能洗去多余米浆，让高汤清亮不浑浊。
  • Blanching the vegetables beforehand keeps them from cooling the broth and removes any raw flavor.配菜提前焯水断生再入碗，既不会吸走高汤热量，也能去除豆芽和香菇的生涩味。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The noodles turned mushy and clouded the broth米线泡一会儿就坨了，汤还变浑
    Rinse the boiled noodles in cold water and drain thoroughly; keep them separate until right before ladling the hot broth.煮好的米线一定要冲凉水洗去米浆并控干水分；如果分批吃，米线和配菜留在碗外，吃之前再浇滚汤。
  • The chicken slices turned dry and tough鸡胸肉片发柴发硬
    Slice across the grain paper-thin, coat with cornstarch and oil, and ladle out as soon as the meat turns opaque and hits 74°C.确保鸡片横纹切薄并用淀粉和油抓匀，烫至变白达到 74°C 就立刻连汤盛碗，不要久煮。
  • The broth tastes salty but flat汤头只有咸味、不够鲜浓
    Simmering bone-in drumsticks adds body and savory depth; use less water next time and finish with a pinch of pepper.带骨鸡腿与高汤同熬能补足鲜味与胶质；下次减少清水比例，出锅前补少许胡椒粉提香。
