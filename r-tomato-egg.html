Tomato and Egg Stir-fry番茄炒蛋

The most everyday Chinese dish, with two things to get right, soft eggs and a rich red sauce. Cook the eggs just until half-set and set them aside, cook the tomatoes down until they release their juice, then bring them together.最家常的一道菜，难在两点：蛋要嫩、汤汁要红亮不寡淡。蛋先炒到半熟盛出，番茄炒出汁再合并，三分钟就能救回一盘"炒坏的"。

18 min total分钟 (8 + 10)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Eggs鸡蛋 150 g · 3 large eggs3 个大鸡蛋
  • Tomatoes西红柿 400 g · 2 medium tomatoes (about 400 g)2 个中等番茄（约 400 克） ripe and soft is best选熟透偏软的
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL): 1 for the eggs, 1 for the tomatoes2 汤匙（30 mL），蛋用 1 汤匙、番茄用 1 汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 6 g · 1.5 tsp1.5 茶匙
  • Salt盐 3 g · about 1/2 tsp (1 g for the eggs, 2 g for the tomatoes)约 1/2 茶匙（蛋 1 克，番茄 2 克）
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 1 stalk, chopped1 根，葱花
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 5 g · 1 tsp (5 mL), optional1 茶匙（5 mL），可省

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Score a cross in the top of each tomato and drop them in boiling water for 30 seconds.番茄顶部划十字，放入沸水烫 30 秒。
  2. Lift them out, peel, and cut into 2 cm pieces.捞出剥皮，切成 2 厘米的块。
  3. Beat the eggs in a bowl with 1 g salt and the wine.鸡蛋打入碗中，加盐 1 克和料酒 1 茶匙，用筷子打散。
  4. Heat a wok or skillet, add 1 tbsp oil, and pour in the eggs when the oil just starts to shimmer.锅烧热，倒 1 汤匙油，油面微微冒烟时倒入蛋液。
  5. When the edges set, push from the outside in to make big soft curds; at about 30 seconds, when they are 70% set, take them out.等边缘凝固，用锅铲从外向内推，炒成大块的嫩蛋，约 30 秒，七成熟即盛出。
  6. Add the second tablespoon of oil and the tomatoes over medium heat.锅里补 1 汤匙油，倒入番茄块，中火翻炒。
  7. Add the sugar and 2 g salt and cook for about 3 minutes until the tomatoes are soft and juicy.加糖和盐 2 克，炒到番茄软烂出汁，约 3 分钟。
  8. Return the eggs and fold gently two or three times so they take up the sauce.倒回鸡蛋，轻轻翻两下，让蛋吸上汤汁。
  9. Scatter the green onion, turn off the heat and serve.撒葱花，关火装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Pour the eggs into properly hot oil so they puff up; cold oil makes them greasy and dense.蛋液下锅前油要热，蛋会很快鼓起变蓬松；油凉了蛋会吸油变硬。
  • Take the eggs out at 70% set; they finish cooking when they return to the pan and stay tender.蛋炒到七成熟就盛出，回锅后余热会把它带到全熟，这样蛋是嫩的。
  • Peeling the tomatoes avoids tough skin strands in the sauce.番茄烫皮去皮后更好吃，皮在汤汁里会卷成一缕缕。
  • The sugar balances the acid rather than making it sweet; 1.5 tsp is enough.糖不是为了甜，是为了压住番茄的酸，放 1.5 茶匙就够。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The sauce is too sour汤汁太酸
    Add half a teaspoon more sugar and cook one more minute; next time use fully ripe tomatoes.再加半茶匙糖，继续炒一分钟让酸味挥发；下次选熟透的番茄。
  • It is watery汤汁太稀、盘底一摊水
    Boil it down over high heat for a minute or two; cook the tomatoes down fully before the eggs go back; or add half a teaspoon of starch slurry.开大火收汁 1–2 分钟；或者番茄先炒透再倒回鸡蛋；也可加半茶匙水淀粉。
  • The eggs are overcooked and crumbly鸡蛋炒老了、一粒粒的
    Pull the eggs at 70% set, fold only two or three times on return, and avoid stirring them into small bits.蛋炒到七成就起锅，回锅时只翻两下；炒的时候不要一直拨散。
