Bulgogi (Korean Marinated Beef)韩式烤牛肉 bulgogi（自制腌料）

Thin-sliced beef marinated in soy sauce, sugar, garlic, sesame oil and grated apple, then seared hot in minutes until the edges caramelise. The fruit tenderises the meat, but do not marinate too long. Serve with rice or wrapped in lettuce.牛肉切成薄片，用酱油、糖、蒜、芝麻油和磨碎的苹果腌过，大火几分钟煎熟，边缘微焦带点甜。腌料里的水果让肉更嫩，但别腌过头。配米饭或生菜叶包着吃。

80 min total分钟 (25 + 10)4 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Mirin另购：味啉

Ingredients食材

  • Ribeye steak肋眼牛排 600 g · 600 g ribeye (or sirloin), partly frozen, sliced 2-3 mm thin肋眼牛排（或西冷）600 克，部分冷冻后切 2–3 毫米薄片 or ask the butcher for hot-pot slices and cut them to 5 cm lengths不会切就请肉铺切火锅片，再按 5 厘米长度剪短
  • Soy sauce生抽 54 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 24 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Mirin味啉 33 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Garlic大蒜 24 g · 6 cloves, grated6 瓣，磨成蒜泥
  • Apples苹果 120 g · half an apple (or Asian pear), cored and grated, about 1/3 cup半个苹果（或亚洲梨）去核磨泥，约 1/3 杯
  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 small onion, thinly sliced1 个小洋葱，切丝
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 40 g · 4 stalks, cut in 4 cm lengths4 根，切 4 厘米段
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · 1/2 tsp1/2 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL) for cooking1 汤匙（15 mL），煎肉用
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 6 g · 2 tsp toasted sesame seeds2 茶匙烤过的白芝麻
  • Short-grain rice短粒米(寿司米) 240 g · about 1 1/3 cups dry rice, cooked for 4 bowls米约 1 又 1/3 杯，煮成 4 碗饭

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Freeze the beef about 40 minutes, until the edges are firm and the centre is still soft.牛肉放进冰箱冷冻室 40 分钟，到边缘变硬、中间还软。
  2. Slice against the grain into 2-3 mm slices.逆着肌肉纹理，切成 2–3 毫米的薄片。
  3. Grate the apple and garlic into a bowl, add the soy sauce, sugar, mirin, sesame oil and pepper and stir until the sugar dissolves.苹果和蒜磨成泥，加入酱油、糖、味醂、芝麻油和黑胡椒，搅到糖化开。
  4. Add the beef, onion and green onion to the marinade and mix by hand.牛肉、洋葱和葱段放进腌料，用手抓匀。
  5. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes, up to 4 hours.盖好放冷藏腌 30 分钟，最多 4 小时。
  6. Meanwhile cook the rice.同时煮好米饭。
  7. Heat a large cast-iron or heavy pan over high heat for 2 minutes and add half the oil.大号铸铁锅或平底锅大火烧 2 分钟，加一半的油。
  8. Add half the beef in a single layer and leave it alone for 1 minute.放入一半的牛肉，摊开成一层，不要动 1 分钟。
  9. Stir-fry 1-2 minutes until all the meat has changed colour and the edges caramelise, then remove.翻炒 1–2 分钟，直到肉全部变色、边缘焦糖化，盛出。
  10. Wipe the pan and repeat with the remaining oil and beef.擦一下锅，用剩下的油和牛肉重复一次。
  11. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve with rice.撒芝麻，配米饭上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Freeze the beef first; a partly frozen block does not slip, so you can cut thin even slices.牛肉先冻 40 分钟再切：半冻的肉不会滑，才切得出薄而均匀的片。
  • Cook in two batches over high heat; crowding releases juice and the beef boils instead of caramelising.分两批、大火煎：一次放太多会出水，肉变成煮的而不是焦的，也失去烤肉的香味。
  • The apple or pear contains enzymes that tenderise the meat, but beyond half a day the texture turns mushy, so do not marinate much longer.腌料里的苹果或梨含酶，能让肉更嫩，但超过半天肉质会变得软烂，所以不要腌过夜以上。
  • For a shortcut, buy ready-marinated bulgogi beef; Korean supermarkets sell it and some BC Costco stores carry it at the meat counter. Skip the marinade steps and start at the hot pan, and do not pour the bag liquid into the pan.赶时间可以买现成腌好的 bulgogi 牛肉：韩国超市有，部分 BC 的 Costco 门店肉柜也会卖；跳过前 6 步，直接从大火煎肉开始，腌肉的汁不要倒进锅里。
  • Never spoon leftover marinade over cooked meat; it has touched raw beef, so boil it first if you want a sauce.用剩下的腌料不要直接淋在熟肉上：它碰过生牛肉，要煮开才能当酱。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The beef is swimming in liquid and will not brown牛肉煮出很多汤、不上色
    Lift out the meat, boil the liquid down in the pan until syrupy and return the meat; next time cook in smaller batches in a hotter pan.把肉捞出，继续把汤汁大火收到起泡再倒回肉，或者下次分更多批、锅烧得更热。
  • It is too salty or too sweet肉太咸或太甜
    Serve with plenty of rice and lettuce; next time cut the soy by 1 tbsp and let it marinate a little longer.配大量米饭和生菜叶吃；下次酱油减 1 汤匙，让肉多腌一会儿让味道进去。
  • The beef is chewy牛肉嚼不烂
    The slices were too thick or cut with the grain; next time cut against the grain thinner than 3 mm and cook under 3 minutes.说明切得太厚或顺纹切；下次逆着纹理切到 3 毫米以下，煎的时间也不要超过 3 分钟。
