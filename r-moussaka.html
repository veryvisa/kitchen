Moussaka希腊茄子肉酱千层

Layers of tender eggplant, warmly spiced lamb and creamy white sauce bake into a golden centerpiece. Roasting the eggplant until soft with browned spots, reducing the meat sauce well and cooking the topping until it coats a spoon all help the slices hold their shape.茄子、香料羊肉酱和奶香白酱层层叠起，烤成表面金黄、切面分明的一盘主菜。茄子烤至柔软并略带焦斑，肉酱充分收浓，白酱煮至能挂在勺背，有助于减少成品出水，让切面更整齐。

145 min total分钟 (35 + 90)6 servings份Needs care需要用心Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Ground lamb另购：羊肉末

Ingredients食材

  • Eggplant茄子 1200 g · about 3 large eggplants, cut into 1 cm slices约 3 个大茄子，切成 1 厘米厚片
  • Ground lamb羊肉末 500 g · ground lamb羊肉末
  • Onions洋葱 180 g · 1 large onion, diced1 个大洋葱，切丁
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · about 4 cloves, minced约 4 瓣，切末
  • Canned diced tomatoes番茄罐头(碎) 400 g · 1 can chopped tomatoes1 罐切碎番茄
  • Tomato paste番茄膏 30 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 60 g · about 4 tbsp, divided约 4 汤匙，分次使用
  • Ground cinnamon肉桂粉 1 g · about 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon约 ½ 茶匙肉桂粉
  • Dried oregano干牛至/香草 2 g · about 2 tsp dried oregano约 2 茶匙干牛至
  • Salt盐 8 g · about 1 1/3 tsp, divided约 1⅓ 茶匙，分次使用
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 2 g · about 1 tsp, divided约 1 茶匙，分次使用
  • Butter黄油 60 g · unsalted butter无盐黄油
  • All-purpose flour面粉 60 g · all-purpose flour, about 1/2 cup中筋面粉，约 ½ 杯
  • Milk 2%牛奶 600 g · about 2 1/2 cups约 2½ 杯
  • Eggs鸡蛋 50 g · 1 large egg, beaten1 个大鸡蛋，打散
  • Parmesan帕玛森奶酪 60 g · grated; a substitute for the traditional Greek hard cheese Kefalotyri, used here because it's easier to find in BC磨碎；是北美容易买到的替代品，传统希腊做法用 Kefalotyri 等硬质芝士

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C.烤箱预热至 220°C。
  2. Arrange the eggplant slices on baking sheets and brush both sides with about 35 g of the olive oil.茄子片铺在烤盘上，两面刷上约 35 克橄榄油。
  3. Season the eggplant with 3 g salt and roast at 220°C for 22–26 minutes, turning once, until soft with browned spots.茄子撒上 3 克盐，以 220°C 烤 22–26 分钟，中途翻面一次，直到柔软并出现褐色斑点。
  4. Heat the remaining olive oil in a skillet over medium heat and cook the onion for 5 minutes until translucent.平底锅中火加热剩下的橄榄油，炒洋葱 5 分钟至透明。
  5. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.加入蒜末炒 30 秒，直到闻到香味。
  6. Add the ground lamb and brown it over medium-high heat for 7–9 minutes, breaking it up until browned and its center reaches 71°C.加入羊肉末，中大火炒散 7–9 分钟，直到肉色变褐且中心达到 71°C。
  7. Add the tomatoes, tomato paste, cinnamon, oregano, 3 g salt and black pepper, then bring the mixture to a boil.加入番茄、番茄膏、肉桂、牛至、3 克盐和黑胡椒，拌匀煮开。
  8. Simmer uncovered over low heat for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking or scorching, until thick, leaving a brief trail when a spoon crosses the pan.转小火不加盖煮 20–25 分钟，期间不时搅拌防止粘底焦糊，直到肉酱浓稠、勺划过锅底留下短暂痕迹。
  9. Melt the butter in a separate saucepan over medium heat, then whisk in the flour for 2 minutes until foamy.另取锅中火融化黄油，加入面粉搅拌 2 分钟至起泡。
  10. Gradually whisk in the milk and cook over medium heat until it thickens noticeably and starts to bubble gently, then simmer 1-2 minutes more over low heat — about 8-10 minutes total — so the flour fully cooks out and leaves no raw taste.逐渐倒入牛奶并持续搅拌，中火煮到明显变稠并开始轻微冒泡，再转小火继续煮 1–2 分钟，约共 8–10 分钟，确保面粉完全糊化、没有生面粉味。
  11. Take the sauce off the heat, add the egg, Parmesan and remaining salt, and whisk quickly until smooth.白酱离火后加入鸡蛋、帕玛森芝士和剩下的盐，快速搅匀。
  12. Reduce the oven temperature to 190°C. Let the meat sauce and eggplant cool for a few minutes (no need to cool completely) so the hot food does not thin the white sauce too early.烤箱调至 190°C。肉酱和茄子稍微放凉几分钟（不必完全冷却），避免滚烫食材让白酱提前变稀。
  13. Layer half the eggplant in a baking dish at least 23 x 33 cm with sides about 5-6 cm deep, then spread on all the meat sauce and the remaining eggplant.把一半茄子铺进至少 23 × 33 厘米、深约 5–6 厘米的烤盘，接着铺上全部肉酱和剩下的茄子。
  14. Spread the white sauce evenly over the top, leaving a little headroom below the rim, and bake at 190°C for 35–40 minutes until golden and bubbling at the edges with the center reaching at least 74°C.把白酱均匀铺在顶层，预留一点顶部空间，以 190°C 烤 35–40 分钟，直到表面金黄、边缘冒泡、中心至少达到 74°C。
  15. Rest the dish on a rack for 20 minutes before cutting into portions.把烤盘放在架上静置 20 分钟再切块。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Roast the eggplant in a single layer; crowded slices steam instead of developing browned spots.茄子片铺开烤，不要叠放；挤在一起会蒸出水，难以烤出褐色斑点。
  • Reduce the meat sauce until thick; excess liquid seeps out and makes the layers slump.肉酱必须煮到浓稠，水分太多会从切面渗出，令千层散开。
  • Take the white sauce off the heat before adding the egg, then whisk promptly to reduce the chance of curdling.白酱离火后再加蛋，并快速搅拌，能减少蛋液结块的风险。
  • Let the bake rest before slicing so the sauces settle and thicken slightly, making cleaner slices easier.出炉后静置再切，让热的白酱和肉酱稍微降温、变得黏稠，切面会更整齐。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The bake releases lots of liquid when sliced切开后大量出水
    Reduce the meat sauce further and roast the eggplant until soft with browned spots; rest the finished bake for 20 minutes before slicing.肉酱回锅小火再收浓，茄子也要烤到柔软并有褐色斑点；成品静置 20 分钟再切。
  • The white sauce is lumpy or has scrambled egg bits白酱结块或有蛋花
    Add the milk gradually while whisking; take the pan off the heat and let it cool slightly before adding the egg. A little lumpiness can be smoothed with an immersion blender, but if it has visibly scrambled, remake the sauce — straining will not restore the texture.加牛奶时分次倒入并持续搅拌；加蛋前务必先离火让酱稍降温，若只是轻微结块可用手持搅拌器短暂打匀，明显结块出现蛋花就要重新做一锅，过筛救不回质地。
  • The top is dark while the center is still cool表面很深但中心仍凉
    Loosely cover the top with foil and continue baking at 175°C until the center is hot, then rest before cutting.用铝箔松松盖住表面，继续以 175°C 烘烤至中心热透，再静置后切。
