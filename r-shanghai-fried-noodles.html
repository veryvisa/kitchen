Shanghai Fried Noodles上海粗炒面

A classic Shanghai noodle shop staple built on a glossy dark soy glaze, springy thick wheat noodles, tender pork strips and crisp greens. The usual home problems are sticking and clumping. Boil the noodles only until 80% done, rinse them cold, toss with oil, and lift them with chopsticks as they fry so every strand stays separate.上海面馆里常见的「浓油赤酱」主食：粗面弹牙入味，搭配滑嫩猪里脊丝与小青菜，咸中带一点点甜。家里炒面最怕面条粘锅或碎成一团，只要把面煮到八分熟就过凉拌油，进锅后用筷子抖散快炒就能根根分明。

Method做法

  1. Toss the pork strips with 1/2 tbsp soy sauce, the cooking wine, cornstarch and 1 tsp canola oil and marinate 10 minutes.肉丝加半汤匙生抽、料酒、玉米淀粉和 1 茶匙食用油抓匀，腌 10 分钟。
  2. Whisk the remaining 1.5 tbsp soy sauce, dark soy, sugar and 2 tbsp water in a small bowl.小碗把剩下的 1.5 汤匙生抽、老抽、糖和 2 汤匙清水拌匀备用。
  3. Boil the noodles in a large pot of water for about 3 to 4 minutes until 80% cooked with a thin white core remaining when you bite it (separate the strands with chopsticks in the water so they do not clump).大锅烧开水，放入粗面煮约 3 至 4 分钟至八分熟、咬开还有极细白芯（用筷子在水里把面条挑散，避免成团）。
  4. Drain the noodles immediately, rinse under cold water, shake and drain well for a few minutes, then toss with 2 tsp canola oil.捞出面条立刻用冷水冲凉，摇散沥干几分钟后拌入 2 茶匙食用油防粘。
  5. Heat the remaining 2 tbsp oil in a wok over high heat until just shimmering (no need for it to smoke), stir-fry the pork for about 90 seconds until opaque and cooked through (71°C), then transfer out.炒锅大火烧热，倒入剩余 2 汤匙油，油面刚起波纹（不用冒烟）时下肉丝滑炒约 1 分半，变白熟透（达 71°C）后盛出。
  6. Add the ginger and white scallion pieces to the wok and stir over medium heat for 15 seconds until fragrant.原锅下姜丝和葱白段，中火煸香 15 秒。
  7. Add the cabbage and the thicker bok choy stems with the salt and stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute, then add the leafy parts and stir 20-30 seconds more until wilted yet bright green.倒入圆白菜丝和上海青梗部，加盐大火快炒 1 分钟，再放上海青叶片炒 20–30 秒至塌软仍翠绿。
  8. Add the noodles, pour the sauce around the edge of the wok, and toss with chopsticks and a spatula over medium-high heat for 2 minutes to coat evenly.放入面条，沿锅边淋入调好的酱汁，中高火用筷子配合锅铲抖散翻炒 2 分钟。
  9. Return the pork and green scallion tops, turn off the heat, then drizzle in the sesame oil and toss for 10-15 seconds (sesame oil turns bitter over high heat, so add it off the burner) and serve.倒回肉丝和葱绿，关火后淋入香油拌匀 10–15 秒（香油高温会发苦，关火再加），装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Stopping the boil at 80% done, rinsing cold and oiling the noodles keeps them firm enough to finish in the wok without turning mushy.粗面煮到八分熟就捞出冲凉并拌油，进锅后还要吸酱汁再炒 2 分钟，全熟下锅一定会糊成一团。
  • Lift and shake the noodles with a long pair of chopsticks instead of only pressing with a spatula; it is easier and keeps the strands from breaking.炒面时用一双长筷子挑散面条，比只用锅铲更省力，也不容易把面条铲断。
  • Dark soy builds the mahogany sheen while light soy seasons, and a touch of sugar rounds out the savoury edges.老抽负责红亮酱色、生抽负责咸鲜，加少许白糖能把酱香和青菜的清甜带出来。
  • Shake the greens dry and stir-fry over high heat so excess water does not steam the noodles soft.蔬菜洗净务必甩干水分再大火快炒，锅里不出水，面条才不会越炒越软。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The noodles stuck to the wok and clumped面条粘锅底、结成一团
    Preheat the dry wok before adding oil; loosen stuck bits with 1 tbsp hot water, and next time shake and drain the rinsed noodles a little longer before oiling them.干锅先烧热再倒油润锅；若已经粘底可加 1 汤匙热水轻铲解开，下次冲凉后多摇散、沥干几分钟再拌油。
  • Uneven colouring with pale patches面条颜色一块深一块浅
    Never pour straight dark soy onto a pile of noodles; whisk the sauce with a splash of water first and toss continuously with chopsticks.不要把老抽直接淋在面堆上，要先在碗里加少许水调稀，倒下去时同时用筷子把面抖开。
  • The pork strips turned dry and tough肉丝炒老发柴
    Slice the loin thinly across the grain, coat with soy, cornstarch and oil, pull it from the wok as soon as it hits 71°C, and return it only for the final 30-second toss.猪里脊逆纹切薄丝并用生抽、淀粉和油锁水，变色达 71°C 就立刻盛出，最后回锅只拌 30 秒。

A filling one-wok meal combining wheat noodles, lean pork loin and two vegetables, though it runs fairly high in both calories and sodium. Because light and dark soy sauces add significant sodium, cut the light soy by half a tablespoon if watching salt, or use a smaller portion of noodles.一盘集齐精制面条、猪里脊瘦肉和两种蔬菜，饱腹感强，但热量和钠都不低；生抽与老抽带来较高钠含量，控盐人士可把生抽减半汤匙，也可以减少面条份量。
