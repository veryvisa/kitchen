Braised Chicken with Potatoes土豆烧鸡

A one-pot home dish with sauce for spooning over rice. Costco's boneless thighs mean no chopping through bones and half the time of a whole bird; the potatoes soak up the chicken juices and soften at the edges, thickening the sauce on their own.一锅出、汤汁拌饭的家常菜。用 Costco 去骨鸡腿肉，不用剁骨头，比整鸡快一半；土豆吸饱鸡汁，边缘烧得微微化开，汤汁自然浓稠。

45 min total分钟 (10 + 35)3 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine, Dark soy sauce另购：料酒(绍兴)、老抽

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 600 g · about 600 g boneless chicken thighs, in large pieces去骨鸡腿肉约 600 克，切大块
  • Potatoes土豆 450 g · 2-3 medium potatoes, in chunks2–3 个中等土豆，切滚刀块
  • Carrots胡萝卜 100 g · 1 carrot, in chunks1 根胡萝卜，切滚刀块
  • Ginger姜 10 g · 4 slices4 片
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 3 cloves, smashed3 瓣，拍扁
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 2 stalks, cut into lengths2 根，切段
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1 1/2 tbsp1.5 汤匙
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 6 g · 1 tsp (for colour)1 茶匙（上色）
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp (taste first)约 1/3 茶匙（最后尝过再加）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Pat the chicken pieces dry.鸡腿肉块用厨房纸吸干。
  2. Brown the chicken skin side down in the oil over medium-high heat until golden and the fat runs, about 5 minutes.锅里放油，中大火把鸡块皮朝下煎到金黄出油，约 5 分钟。
  3. Add the ginger, garlic and white parts of the onion and stir for about 30 seconds.放姜片、蒜和葱白炒香，约 30 秒。
  4. Pour the wine around the edge of the pan, then add both soy sauces and the sugar and toss.沿锅边淋料酒，加生抽、老抽和糖翻匀。
  5. Add the potato and carrot, and hot water to come halfway up.放土豆和胡萝卜，加热水到食材的一半高。
  6. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer on medium-low for 20 minutes.煮开后盖盖，中小火焖 20 分钟。
  7. Uncover, taste and add the salt.开盖，尝味后加盐。
  8. Reduce over high heat, stirring, until the sauce thickens, then add the green parts of the onion and serve.大火收汁到浓稠，边收边翻，撒葱绿出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Brown the chicken first to render fat and build flavour before adding the seasoning.鸡块先煎出油和焦香，再加调料，味道才浓。
  • Cut the potatoes large so 20 minutes leaves them soft but whole.土豆块切大一点，焖 20 分钟正好软糯不散。
  • The potato starch thickens the sauce as it reduces; no slurry needed.收汁时土豆的淀粉会让汤自然变稠，不用勾芡。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The potatoes fell apart土豆烂成泥
    They were cut too small or cooked too long; cut bigger chunks and check at 20 minutes.切得太小或焖太久；切大块，焖 20 分钟先查一次。
  • Pale and bland颜色太浅、不香
    The chicken was not browned; brown it well before going on, and let the teaspoon of dark soy do the colour.鸡块没煎上色；先把鸡块煎到金黄再往下做，老抽 1 茶匙负责颜色。
