Pickled Cabbage Fish酸菜鱼

Sichuan pickled cabbage fish balances a bright, tangy broth with silky fish slices and a sizzling chili-peppercorn finish. Three things make or break it at home: slice the fillets at an even bias so they cook together, velvet them with egg white and starch to stay tender, and keep the broth at a gentle simmer once the fish goes in so the slices hold whole.酸菜鱼讲究酸汤开胃、鱼片滑嫩、最后那一勺滚油激出椒香。家里做的难点在三处：鱼片要斜刀片得均匀才不会老嫩不齐，上浆时加蛋清和淀粉锁住水分，鱼片下锅后只能微沸养熟，大火翻滚会把鱼肉煮散。

Method做法

  1. Pat the tilapia fillets dry and slice at a 45-degree angle into 0.5 cm thick pieces.罗非鱼柳用厨房纸吸干，斜刀 45 度片成约 0.5 厘米厚的鱼片。
  2. Toss the fish slices with cooking wine, 1/4 tsp salt, black pepper and the julienned ginger for 1 minute until lightly tacky.鱼片加料酒、1/4 茶匙盐、黑胡椒和姜丝，抓拌 1 分钟至表面微粘。
  3. Mix in the egg white and cornstarch for 30 seconds until every slice is coated, then rest for 10 minutes.加入蛋清和淀粉抓匀 30 秒让每片裹浆，静置腌制 10 分钟。
  4. Rinse the pickled mustard greens once to remove excess brine, squeeze dry, slice the stems on a bias and cut the leaves into strips.四川酸菜用清水冲去表面盐分后挤干，梗斜切片、叶切段。
  5. Heat 1.5 tbsp oil in a deep wok over medium heat and stir-fry the pickled greens for 3-4 minutes until dry, glossy and very fragrant, with the stems only lightly coloured (do not let the edges char, or they turn bitter).深锅中火烧热 1.5 汤匙油，放入酸菜翻炒 3–4 分钟至水汽收干、梗部微微上色、酸香浓郁（不要炒到边缘焦黑，会发苦）。
  6. Add the sliced ginger, half the garlic, scallion whites and half the dried chilies, frying over medium heat for 30 seconds until aromatic.加入姜片、一半蒜末、葱白和一半干辣椒，中火炒 30 秒出香。
  7. Pour in the chicken stock and sugar, bring to a boil over high heat, then cover and simmer over medium-high heat for 6 minutes until milky white and deeply tangy.倒入鸡汤和糖，大火煮沸后加盖转中高火熬 6 分钟，至汤色奶白、酸香浓郁。
  8. Taste the broth, add the remaining salt only if needed, and transfer the solids with a slotted spoon into a deep serving bowl.尝一下汤味，按咸度补剩下的盐，再用漏勺把酸菜捞进深汤碗铺底。
  9. Lower the heat to a gentle simmer, slide the fish slices in one by one without stirring, and poach 2 to 3 minutes until opaque and 70°C at the thickest part.转中小火让汤面保持微沸不翻滚，逐片滑入鱼片，不要搅动，煮 2–3 分钟至全部变白且最厚处达 70°C。
  10. Pour the fish and hot broth gently over the pickled greens in the serving bowl, then scatter the remaining scallion greens and sesame seeds over the fish.连汤带鱼轻倒进装有酸菜的大碗里，撒上剩下的葱花和白芝麻铺在鱼片上。
  11. Wipe the wok dry, warm the remaining 1.5 tbsp oil over medium heat, and fry the remaining chilies and peppercorns for 10-15 seconds until sizzling; turn off the heat and stir in the remaining minced garlic off the heat so it blooms in the residual warmth instead of scorching.锅擦干，放剩下的 1.5 汤匙油中火烧热，下剩余干辣椒和花椒炸 10–15 秒至滋滋作响，关火后拌入剩余蒜末（余温烘香，不下锅炸，避免蒜末焦苦）。
  12. Pour the hot oil and spices straight over the fish and its scallion-sesame topping, and serve immediately.把滚油和香料直接浇在撒好葱花芝麻的鱼片上即可上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Bias-slicing widens each piece at an even thickness so all the fish cooks through at the same pace.斜刀片鱼能增大受热面并保持厚度一致，鱼片一起熟透，不会外面煮烂里面还生。
  • Dry-frying the squeezed pickled greens drives off raw brine and unlocks deep aroma before the stock goes in.酸菜下锅先干炒几分钟，把生腌水汽逼出来，煮出的汤才会香浓发亮，不会水垮垮的。
  • Keep the broth at a bare simmer and avoid stirring after adding the fish, or the starch coating washes off and the slices break.鱼片下锅后最忌大火猛煮和频繁翻搅，微沸养熟才能保住滑嫩的淀粉浆衣。
  • Packaged pickled mustard greens vary widely in saltiness, so rinse once and taste the simmered broth before salting.不同牌子的袋装酸菜咸度差异很大，先冲水挤干，熬好底汤尝过咸淡再决定是否补盐。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The fish broke apart and clouded the broth鱼片碎在汤里、汤色浑浊
    Do not stir the fish in a rolling boil; cut slices 0.5 cm thick next time and let the starch-egg coating set for 10 minutes before cooking.不要直接翻炒鱼片，用微沸水温浸熟；下次鱼片切稍厚一点（0.5 厘米），淀粉上浆后再静置 10 分钟更贴牢。
  • The fish turned dry and tasted muddy鱼肉发柴还带土腥味
    Pull the fish as soon as it turns opaque and hits 70°C (under 3 minutes), and next time make sure the wine and ginger fully coat the slices during marinating to mask any muddy flavour.罗非鱼柳下锅别超过 3 分钟，达到 70°C 变白即关火；下次腌制时料酒和姜丝量给足、裹匀，去腥效果更好。
  • The broth is too salty or not tangy enough汤太咸或不够酸
    Thin an overly salty broth with half a cup of boiling water and a pinch of sugar; if it lacks tang, stir in 1 tsp rice vinegar off the heat.太咸就兑半杯开水再加半茶匙糖煮开；不够酸可临出锅滴 1 茶匙米醋提酸，下次多炒一会儿酸菜再煮汤。
