Watercress Soup西洋菜汤

Watercress and pork rib soup is a staple Cantonese home broth with a clean sweetness, a pale golden colour, and a little natural sweetness from candied dates. The order matters: the ribs simmer alone for about an hour to build the base, and the watercress — stems and all — goes in only for the last 20-30 minutes, so the greens never turn into dark, bitter mush.西洋菜煲猪骨是广东家庭常喝的老火汤，菜香清甜，汤色微黄清亮，蜜枣提供回甘。做好这锅汤的关键次序反过来：排骨要先煲够一小时打底，西洋菜连茎一起最后下锅煲 20–30 分钟，才不会把菜煲成发黑的烂泥。

Ingredients食材

  • Watercress西洋菜 300 g · 1 large bunch (about 300 g), cut into 2-3 sections1 大把（约 300 克），切 2–3 段 choose crisp stems and bright green leaves; keep the stems, do not discard them选叶片翠绿、茎脆嫩的，茎部保留不要摘掉
  • Pork back ribs猪肋排 500 g · 500 g pork ribs, cut into pieces猪排骨 500 克，斩段 bone-in pork shoulder also works也可用带骨猪肩肉
  • Carrots胡萝卜 150 g · 1 medium carrot, cut into thick rounds1 根中等胡萝卜，切厚片
  • Candied red dates蜜枣 20 g · 2-3 candied dates2–3 颗蜜枣
  • Ginger姜 15 g · 4 slices, lightly smashed4 片，拍松
  • Salt盐 5 g · about 3/4 tsp, added at the end约 3/4 茶匙，出锅前加

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Put the pork ribs in cold water with 2 ginger slices, bring to a boil over high heat, and boil 5 minutes until scum rises; discard this water and rinse the ribs clean under hot running water.排骨冷水下锅，加 2 片姜，大火烧开煮 5 分钟至浮沫涌出，然后倒掉焯水，用热水冲净排骨表面浮沫。
  2. Add 2 litres of water, the ribs, carrot, candied dates, and remaining 2 ginger slices to a soup pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce to low, cover, and simmer for 60 minutes.汤锅加 2 升清水，放入排骨、胡萝卜、蜜枣和剩下 2 片姜，大火烧开后转小火加盖慢煲 60 分钟。
  3. Wash the watercress, trimming only the yellowed leaves and roots — keep the stems — and cut into 2-3 sections.洗净西洋菜，只摘掉发黄的叶子和根部，茎部保留，切成 2–3 段。
  4. Uncover, submerge the watercress in the broth, re-cover, and keep simmering gently on low for 20-30 more minutes, until the leaves and stems are soft, the broth turns pale gold, and the ribs are tender.揭盖放入西洋菜压入汤中，加盖转小火继续煲 20–30 分钟，至菜叶和茎都变软、汤色微黄清亮、排骨筋膜软烂。
  5. Stir in the salt, turn off the heat, and ladle into bowls.加盐拌匀，关火盛碗。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Give the ribs a full hour to build the broth first; adding the watercress — stems included — only for the last 20-30 minutes lets its flavour infuse the soup without turning it into dark mush.排骨先煲够一小时打底，西洋菜连茎带叶最后下锅煲 20–30 分钟，菜香才能融进汤里又不会煲成黑泥。
  • Blanching the ribs from cold water and rinsing them keeps the finished broth clear and free of grey foam.排骨先冷水焯水并冲净浮沫，煲出来的汤才会清澈不腥不腻。
  • Keep the pot at a gentle simmer throughout, not a hard boil, which emulsifies the fat and clouds the broth.全程保持小火慢煲而不是大滚，大滚会把油脂打散乳化，汤色容易变浑浊。
  • Candied dates add natural sweetness, so no extra sugar is needed; salt only at the end, since adding it too early tightens the meat and slows flavour extraction.蜜枣带来自然回甘，不用额外加糖；盐要最后放，煲太早会让肉纤维收紧，也影响鲜味融进汤里。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The watercress turned dark and the soup tastes bitter西洋菜煲得发黑、汤味发苦
    The watercress went in too early or simmered too long; next time let the ribs build the broth for a full hour first, and give the watercress only 20-30 minutes at the end.西洋菜下锅太早、煲太久了；下次一定要等排骨先煲满一小时打好底，西洋菜最后只煲 20–30 分钟就关火。
  • The broth is cloudy and greasy汤色浑浊、表面浮油多
    Incomplete blanching or a hard boil throughout emulsifies the fat; skim the surface oil and keep the simmer gentle and covered.焯水不彻底或全程大火猛滚会把油脂乳化；用勺撇去表面浮油，改最小火微沸状态加盖慢煲。
