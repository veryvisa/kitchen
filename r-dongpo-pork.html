Dongpo Braised Pork东坡肉

A classic Hangzhou braise of large pork belly squares simmered low and slow over aromatics until the skin turns tender, the fat melts, and the lean yields to chopsticks. Home cooks mostly run into trouble by scorching the sugary liquid over high heat or skipping the twine so the squares fall apart.杭州传统名菜，整块五花肉切大块后垫着葱姜小火慢焖，肉皮软糯、肥肉化而不腻，瘦肉用筷子一夹就散。家里做最容易出岔子的是火太猛烧糊锅底，或者没捆绳导致肉块焖散。

140 min total分钟 (20 + 120)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine, Dark soy sauce, Bok choy另购：料酒(绍兴)、老抽、小白菜

Ingredients食材

  • Pork belly五花肉 900 g · about 900 g skin-on pork belly (choose an even, square-cut slab)带皮五花肉约 900 克（挑肥瘦分层均匀的方肉）
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 80 g · about 6 stalks, folded to line the pot约 6 根，整根对折铺锅底
  • Ginger姜 40 g · about 10 thick slices约 10 厚片
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 360 g · 1 1/2 cups (360 mL)1 又 1/2 杯（360 mL） Use as the main braising liquid; Shaoxing or Huadiao rice wine gives the traditional aroma作为主要炖煮液体，用绍兴黄酒或花雕酒风味最正
  • Soy sauce生抽 54 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 36 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 45 g · 45 g (yellow rock sugar, or 38 g granulated sugar)45 克（黄冰糖，或普通白砂糖 38 克） crushed yellow rock sugar gives a clearer gloss敲碎的黄冰糖上色更亮
  • Bok choy小白菜 200 g · 4 baby bok choy (blanched for garnish, optional)4 棵小油菜或上海青（焯水围边，可选）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Place the whole pork belly in a pot of cold water with 2 ginger slices and 1 tbsp cooking wine, bring to a boil over high heat, and boil for 5 minutes to firm up the pork.五花肉整块冷水下锅，加 2 片姜和 1 汤匙料酒，大火烧开后煮 5 分钟定形。
  2. Lift the pork out, rinse with warm water, pat dry, and cut into 4 even squares about 6 cm wide.捞出五花肉用温水冲净擦干，切成 4 块约 6 厘米见方的方肉。
  3. Tie each square crosswise with cotton kitchen twine, snug enough to hold its shape without cutting into the fat.用棉厨房绳在每块肉上十字捆扎，松紧刚好兜住肉块不勒陷即可。
  4. Line the bottom of a heavy clay pot or Dutch oven with the green onions and remaining ginger, then pack the pork squares tightly skin-side down.厚底砂锅或铸铁锅底部铺满葱段和剩下的姜片，把肉块猪皮朝下紧挨着码上去。
  5. Pour in the remaining Shaoxing wine, light soy sauce, and dark soy sauce, and add the sugar. Keep Shaoxing wine as the main braising liquid, adding only a little hot water to bring the level halfway up to just covering the pork (if the level drops significantly later, top up only with hot water, never cold).倒入剩下的绍兴黄酒、生抽、老抽，撒入糖；黄酒应占炖煮液体的大部分，再补充少量热水至约肉块一半高度到刚没过肉块（中途若液面明显下降，只补热水，不加冷水）。
  6. Bring to a boil over high heat, skim off the scum, drop immediately to the lowest simmer, cover tightly, and braise for 60 minutes. Uncover to check: the pork should be noticeably softer and the liquid still at least halfway up the squares; add hot water if it has dropped below that.大火烧开撇去浮沫，立刻转最小火并盖严锅盖，慢焖 60 分钟后开盖检查：此时肉块应已明显变软，液面仍约在肉块一半以上，若不足则加热水补足。
  7. Gently flip the squares skin-side up, cover, and simmer 20–40 minutes more (extending as needed based on texture), checking the liquid level periodically so the pot does not run dry, until the skin is tender and wobbly, a chopstick slides easily through the pork, and the center reaches at least 71°C.小心地把肉块翻面成猪皮朝上，盖盖再最小火焖 20–40 分钟（总焖制时间按实际状态延长），中途留意液面防止烧干，直到猪皮软糯颤滑、筷子能轻松穿透且中心温度达到 71°C 以上。
  8. In a separate pot of boiling water, blanch the baby bok choy for 1 minute until bright green, drain, and arrange around the rim of a deep plate.另锅烧沸水把小油菜焯烫 1 分钟至翠绿断生，捞出沥干围摆在深盘边缘。
  9. Transfer the braised pork squares onto the plate and snip off the twine.把焖好的肉块夹出摆入盘中，剪去棉绳。
  10. With the pork out of the pot, skim the rendered fat from the braising liquid, then reduce over medium to medium-high heat for 3–8 minutes depending on the volume—stirring constantly and avoiding high heat—until glossy and thick enough to coat the back of a spoon; remove from heat immediately and ladle over the pork.捞出肉后，先撇净锅内汤汁表面的浮油，视汤汁量开中大火收 3–8 分钟，期间不停搅动并避免大火，至能挂在勺背、呈亮泽浓汁即离火，浇在肉块上。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Blanching the slab whole before cutting firms the layers for clean squares and clears out impurities.整块先焯水定形再切块，切面平整不容易碎，还能预先去掉血水和腥味。
  • Braising skin-side down for the first stretch keeps the rind submerged so its collagen softens before flipping to glaze.前大半程猪皮朝下泡在汤里焖，胶质才会充分软化，翻面后再给猪皮上亮色。
  • A thick bed of scallions and ginger perfumes the braise and lifts the meat off the pot floor so the sugary sauce does not scorch.锅底铺厚葱姜既是增香底料，也能隔热防粘，避免含糖汤汁贴底烧糊。
  • Relying on plenty of Shaoxing wine as the main braising liquid cuts the richness, while dark soy and sugar build color and shine without burning caramel.黄酒用量大且不炒糖色，靠酒香去腻、老抽定色、冰糖提亮，成品味道更醇厚。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The squares fell apart or the fat separated from the lean肉块焖碎了或者皮肉脱层
    Tie the twine snugly, pack the pieces tight, and support them with a wide spatula when flipping next time; serve broken pieces shredded over rice with the glaze.下次绳子扎稳并在锅里摆紧凑减少晃动，翻面用宽铲托底；已经散了就拆成碎肉连浓汁拌米饭。
  • The pork tastes greasy or the skin is still chewy肥肉还是腻、猪皮嚼不动
    Return the pork to the pot with half a cup of hot water, cover, and simmer 25 more minutes so the collagen breaks down; always skim the surface fat.说明焖制火候不够长，把肉放回锅里加半杯热水，盖盖小火再焖 25 分钟；出锅前务必撇净浮油。
  • The bottom scorched and tastes slightly bitter锅底粘糊发苦
    Do not scrape the burnt bottom; move the meat to a clean pot with a splash of hot water and 1/2 tbsp wine to reheat, and keep the heat at a bare bubble next time.不要刮锅底糊层，把肉夹进干净锅里加少量热水和半汤匙料酒热透即可；下次火调到只冒微泡。
