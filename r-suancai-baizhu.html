Pickled Cabbage and Pork Belly Stew酸菜白肉

A comforting Dongbei winter soup-stew where poached, thinly sliced pork belly simmers with pickled napa cabbage and glass noodles until bright, rich and light on the palate (if you can find Dongbei potato-starch glass noodles, they are the traditional choice, though sweet potato glass noodles work well too). Common pitfalls are not tasting, rinsing as needed and squeezing the pickled cabbage dry—leaving the broth too salty, sour or flat—or slicing the pork too thick.东北冬天最暖胃的汤菜之一：整块五花肉煮透再切薄片，和酸菜、粉条同炖后肥而不腻，汤色清亮酸鲜（如果买得到东北土豆淀粉粉条最为地道，买不到也可用红薯粉条替代）。最常踩坑的地方是没先尝酸菜咸酸度就处理或没攥干水分，导致整锅过咸过酸或寡淡，或者五花肉切得太厚、吃起来腻口。

Method做法

  1. Soak the glass noodles in warm water for 20-30 minutes until supple (if you can find Dongbei potato-starch glass noodles, they are the traditional choice and can be soaked per package directions; sweet potato glass noodles are chewier, so extend soaking per package directions if needed).粉条用温水浸泡 20–30 分钟至软（如果买得到东北土豆淀粉粉条最为地道，按其包装说明浸泡即可；若用红薯粉条，因更耐煮，可按包装适当延长浸泡时间）。
  2. Taste a small piece of the pickled cabbage first: if overly salty or sharp, rinse quickly in cold water 1-2 times, or rinse lightly (or skip rinsing) if well balanced; slice any thick stems lengthwise into thinner pieces, then cut into 3 mm shreds and squeeze handfuls firmly dry.先尝一小条酸菜：偏咸或酸涩时用冷水快洗 1–2 遍，本身咸酸适中时只需轻轻冲洗或免洗；将较厚的菜帮横刀片薄，再切成约 3 毫米细丝，最后双手用力攥干水分。
  3. Put the whole pork belly in cold water covering it by 4 cm with the ginger, green onion pieces, cooking wine, star anise, bay leaves and peppercorns, and bring to a boil over high heat.整块五花肉冷水下锅（水没过肉约 4 厘米），加姜片、葱段、料酒、八角、香叶和花椒，大火烧开。
  4. Skim off the scum, partially cover, reduce to low and simmer 40-50 minutes until a food thermometer inserted into the thickest center reads at least 71°C; if you do not have a thermometer, cook until the slab has shrunk noticeably, a chopstick slides through with no resistance and the juices run clear.撇净浮沫后转小火半盖煮 40–50 分钟，并用食品温度计确认最厚处中心温度至少达到 71°C；若没有温度计，应煮到肉块整体明显收缩、筷子插入无阻力且流出的汁水清亮。
  5. Lift out the pork belly to cool for about 10 minutes, and strain the pork broth free of whole spices to save.捞出五花肉放凉约 10 分钟，原汤过滤掉香料渣后留用。
  6. Slice the cooled pork belly across the grain into thin 3 mm slices.把稍凉的五花肉顶刀切成约 3 毫米薄片。
  7. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a casserole or deep pot over medium heat and stir-fry the squeezed cabbage for 3 minutes until fragrant and glossy.另起砂锅或深锅，中火烧热 1 汤匙油，倒入攥干的酸菜丝炒 3 分钟至出酸香且表面油润。
  8. Pour in the strained pork broth—just enough to cover the cabbage and pork slices, about 1.2-1.5 L (top up with boiling water if short, or simmer uncovered briefly to reduce if there is too much)—lay the pork slices on top, bring to a boil and simmer over medium-low heat for 15 minutes.倒入过滤原汤，汤量以刚好没过酸菜和肉片、约 1.2–1.5 升为宜（不足时补开水，过多则敞盖稍收），把五花肉片铺在上面，烧开后转中小火炖 15 分钟。
  9. Tuck in the soaked noodles and simmer 8-12 minutes until tender with no hard core (if using Dongbei potato-starch glass noodles, adjust to about 6-10 minutes depending on thickness and package directions); taste before adding salt as needed, and finish with black pepper and chopped green onion.放入泡软的粉条再煮 8–12 分钟至中心无硬芯、口感滑软（若用的是东北土豆淀粉粉条，可按粗细和包装说明调整，通常煮约 6–10 分钟）；先尝汤再按需补盐，撒黑胡椒和葱花。
  10. Mix the mashed garlic, soy sauce and sesame oil in a small dish to serve as a dipping sauce alongside the hot pot.小碗中拌匀蒜泥、生抽和香油，盛出汤锅并配蒜泥蘸料一起上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Taste commercial pickled cabbage before rinsing: saltiness and acidity vary widely by brand, so taste a strand first to decide between 1-2 quick cold rinses or a light rinse before squeezing dry so you do not wash away its fermented aroma; stir-frying it in oil lets the strands absorb rich pork flavor.袋装酸菜先尝再洗并过油炒一下：不同品牌咸度和酸度差异很大，先尝一小条再决定快洗 1–2 遍还是轻冲后直接攥干，免得洗掉发酵酸香；攥干后过油炒过才更耐炖、更容易吸进肉香。
  • Poaching the slab whole keeps the meat juicy while enriching the broth; resting it slightly firms the fat so you can cut clean 3 mm slices.整块煮好放凉再切薄片：整煮能锁住肉汁并逼出多余油脂到汤里，稍微放凉让脂肪变紧，才切得出 3 毫米不散的薄片。
  • Wait until the noodles go in and taste the broth before salting, because sodium levels vary widely across brands of pickled cabbage.盐一定等粉条下锅尝完汤再放：不同品牌酸菜含盐量差别很大，贸然按常规加盐很容易整锅过咸。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The broth tastes too sour, salty or harsh汤喝起来太酸或者太咸发涩
    Thin the pot immediately with 200-300 mL boiling water and add 1/2 tsp sugar to round out the acidity; next time taste the cabbage first, give it an extra cold rinse if salty or sharp, and squeeze drier.立即兑入 200–300 毫升开水稀释，再加半茶匙白糖中和；下次酸菜先尝一下，偏咸偏酸时冷水多淘一遍并彻底攥干。
  • The pork slices feel greasy or chewy五花肉吃着肥腻或发硬
    The slices were either cut too thick or under-simmered; simmer 10-15 minutes longer on low heat, skim the surface oil and dip each slice in garlic soy sauce.多半是肉切得太厚或初煮时间不够；把肉片留在锅里继续小火多炖 10–15 分钟，出锅前撇净浮油并配蒜泥生抽蘸食。
  • The soup tastes thin and lacks savory depth汤头清淡没有白肉的醇香
    Always reuse the strained pork-poaching broth instead of plain water; do not dilute with water, and let the stew bubble gently uncovered for 5-10 minutes to concentrate the flavor.不要倒掉煮五花肉的第一锅原汤，一定要滤净香料后用来炖酸菜；不要加水稀释，优先用原汤并再小火敞盖微滚 5–10 分钟收浓。

Good to know适合 / 需注意

  • About 1550 mg sodium per serving (about 67% of the 2,300 mg daily reference), on the high side; use less soy sauce or salt, and pair with unsalted sides.每份钠约 1550 mg（约占每日参考 2300 mg 的 67%），偏高；少放酱油或盐，或搭配无盐主食与蔬菜。
  • About 23 g saturated fat per serving (daily reference about 20 g), on the high side; use a leaner cut or remove skin.每份饱和脂肪约 23 g（每日参考约 20 g），偏高；可换瘦肉部位或去皮。
  • Contains chilli; reduce or leave it out for children and anyone who avoids heat.含辣，不吃辣的人和小朋友可减量或不放。
  • Contains allergens: Sesame, Soy, Wheat / gluten.含过敏原：芝麻、大豆、小麦/麸质。

Pork belly is high in total and saturated fat, and both pickled cabbage and the dipping sauce add significant sodium. Skim surface fat, go light on the broth and dipping sauce, and pair with plain vegetables or whole grains.五花肉的总脂肪与饱和脂肪较高，东北酸菜和蘸料也含较多钠；建议吃肉控量、少喝汤底并撇去浮油，搭配清淡时蔬或杂粮主食。
