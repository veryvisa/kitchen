Lamb and Daikon Soup白萝卜羊肉汤

The pot northern families keep simmering all winter. Blanched lamb cooks low with ginger, and the daikon goes in for the last half hour to soak up the broth, which stays clear and clean-tasting. Finish with green onion and white pepper for a warming bowl.北方冬天最常喝的一锅汤。羊肉焯水后和姜一起小火慢炖，最后半小时放白萝卜，萝卜吸饱羊肉的鲜味，汤清不膻。出锅撒葱花和白胡椒，一碗下去全身暖和。

110 min total分钟 (20 + 90)4 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Daikon radish, Shaoxing wine另购：白萝卜、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless leg of lamb去骨羊腿肉 600 g · 600 g boneless leg of lamb, cut into 3 cm chunks去骨羊腿肉 600 克，切 3 厘米块 bone-in lamb ribs or neck give more flavour带骨羊排或羊蝎子更香
  • Daikon radish白萝卜 500 g · half a daikon (about 500 g), cut into chunks白萝卜半根（约 500 克），切滚刀块
  • Ginger姜 20 g · a large knob, thickly sliced1 大块，切厚片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks: 2 tied in a knot, 1 sliced3 根：2 根打结，1 根切葱花
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Salt盐 6 g · about 1 tsp, added at the end约 1 茶匙，出锅前放
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · 1/4 tsp (white pepper is most authentic)1/4 茶匙（白胡椒最地道）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Soak the lamb in cold water for 30 minutes, changing the water once, to draw out the blood.羊肉块泡冷水 30 分钟，中间换一次水，泡出血水。
  2. Put the lamb in a pot of cold water with half the ginger and the wine, bring to a boil, skim and boil 2 more minutes.羊肉冷水下锅，加一半姜片和料酒，烧开后撇沫再煮 2 分钟。
  3. Lift out the lamb and rinse off the scum in warm water.捞出羊肉，用温水冲净浮沫。
  4. Put the lamb in a clean pot with about 2 L boiling water, the rest of the ginger and the onion knot.羊肉放入砂锅，加约 2 升开水、剩下的姜片和葱结。
  5. Bring to a boil, then cover with the lid ajar and simmer 1 hour.大火烧开转小火，盖盖留缝炖 1 小时。
  6. Add the daikon and simmer 30 more minutes until it is translucent and soft.加白萝卜块，再炖 30 分钟到萝卜透明软烂。
  7. Remove the onion knot, season with salt and pepper and scatter the green onion.捞出葱结，加盐和胡椒，撒葱花。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Soaking and a cold-water blanch are the two steps that remove gaminess; rinse in warm water, as cold water tightens the meat.泡水和冷水焯，是去膻的两道关；焯完要用温水洗，冷水一激肉会紧。
  • Add all the water at the start, and only boiling water if you must top up, for a clear broth and tender meat.炖的水一次加足，中途要加只加开水，汤才清、肉才不柴。
  • Salt at the end; early salt tightens the lamb so it takes longer to soften.盐最后放，早放羊肉会紧、不容易炖烂。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The soup tastes gamey汤有膻味
    Not soaked or blanched enough. Add more ginger and white pepper now; next time soak fully and blanch.没泡透或没焯水。这次多放姜和白胡椒压一压；下次泡足时间、焯水撇沫。
  • The broth is cloudy汤浑
    It boiled too hard. Keep it at a bare simmer with just a few bubbles.炖的时候火太大。保持表面微微冒泡，别大滚。
