Black Pepper Beef Rice Bowl黑椒牛肉饭

Slice a grocery-store steak into strips and prep while the rice cooker runs, and you can put this restaurant-style black pepper beef rice bowl on the table in half an hour. Three habits keep the beef tender: slice across the grain and velvet with cornstarch, sear fast over high heat and pull it early, then toss it back with the onions and sauce for just half a minute.在北美超市买一块牛排切条，电饭煲煮饭的同时备料炒菜，半小时内就能端出餐厅风味的黑椒牛肉饭。成败关键在三点：逆纹切条加淀粉上浆、大火快滑到八分熟先盛出、最后才与洋葱彩椒回锅裹汁，牛肉才会嫩滑不柴。

30 min total分钟 (12 + 18)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine另购：老抽、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Striploin steak西冷牛排 300 g · 1 beef striploin steak (about 300 g)牛外脊牛排 1 块（约 300 克） ribeye or beef tenderloin also works也可用肋眼牛排或牛里脊
  • Jasmine rice茉莉香米 185 g · 1 cup dry jasmine rice (about 185 g)1 杯生茉莉香米（约 185 克）
  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 medium onion (about 150 g), cut into strips1 个中等洋葱（约 150 克），切条
  • Bell peppers彩椒 150 g · 1 bell pepper (about 150 g), cut into chunks1 个彩椒或青椒（约 150 克），切块
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 3 cloves, minced3 瓣，切末
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 3 g · about 1.5 tsp coarsely cracked black pepper约 1.5 茶匙粗磨黑胡椒碎
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1.5 tbsp (about 22 mL), divided for marinade and sauce1.5 汤匙（约 22 mL），腌肉和调汁分用
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 6 g · 1 tsp (5 mL)1 茶匙（5 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 11 g · 3.5 tsp, 2 tsp for beef and 1.5 tsp for sauce3.5 茶匙，腌肉用 2 茶匙、调汁用 1.5 茶匙
  • Butter黄油 14 g · 1 tbsp (14 g)1 汤匙（14 克） adds richness to the pepper sauce; swap for oil if preferred黄油让黑椒汁更香浓，也可全换植物油
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 120 g · 1/2 cup chicken stock or water半杯鸡汤或清水

Method做法

  1. Rinse the jasmine rice and cook it in a rice cooker with 1.3 cups water for about 20 minutes until tender (adjust slightly for your cooker or the package instructions).茉莉香米淘洗干净，加 1.3 杯清水放进电饭煲煮熟（约 20 分钟，水量可按电饭煲或包装说明微调）。
  2. Slice the beef steak across the grain into 5 mm thick strips.牛排逆着肉纹切成约 5 毫米厚的条。
  3. Toss the beef with 1/2 tbsp soy sauce and cooking wine first, then the 2 tsp cornstarch until absorbed, and finally the 1 tbsp canola oil to seal in moisture; marinate 10 minutes.牛肉条先加半汤匙生抽和料酒抓匀，再加 2 茶匙淀粉抓至吸干，最后拌入 1 汤匙植物油锁住水分，腌制 10 分钟。
  4. Whisk the remaining 1 tbsp soy sauce, oyster sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, 1.5 tsp cornstarch, cracked black pepper, and stock in a small bowl.小碗中把剩下的 1 汤匙生抽、蚝油、老抽、糖、1.5 茶匙淀粉、粗黑胡椒碎和鸡汤搅匀。
  5. Heat the remaining 1 tbsp canola oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat until very hot and shimmering, just before it starts to smoke.炒锅或大平底锅加剩下 1 汤匙植物油，大火烧到热而起油纹、尚未冒烟。
  6. Spread the beef in a single layer (sear in two batches if the pan is crowded, so the strips do not steam), sear undisturbed 45 seconds, then stir-fry 1 minute until browned outside and about 63°C inside — a little pink in the center is fine — and transfer to a plate.平铺放入牛肉（锅小就分两批，避免挤在一起出水），先煎 45 秒不动，再翻炒 1 分钟至表面焦黄、中心约 63°C（切面仍可带一点粉红），盛出。
  7. Melt the butter in the pan over medium heat and stir-fry the onion and bell pepper for about 1.5 minutes until the onion edges turn translucent, then add the garlic for 15 seconds until fragrant.锅中转中火放黄油融化，下洋葱条和彩椒块翻炒约 1.5 分钟至洋葱边缘半透明，再下蒜末炒 15 秒出香。
  8. Stir the pepper sauce well, pour it into the pan, and cook over high heat for 30 to 45 seconds until bubbling and thickened.把黑椒料汁再次搅匀倒入锅中，大火煮 30–45 秒至冒泡变稠。
  9. Return the beef and any juices to the pan and toss over high heat for 30 seconds until evenly coated.倒回牛肉连同盘中肉汁，大火快速兜匀 30 秒让牛肉裹满酱汁。
  10. Divide the hot rice into two bowls, spoon the beef, vegetables, and sauce on top, and serve right away.热米饭盛入两个碗中，铺上黑椒牛肉和配菜趁热吃。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Slicing across the muscle fibers shortens them, while cornstarch and oil lock in moisture under high heat.逆纹切条能切断长肉纤维，淀粉和油则锁住水分，高温快炒也不易发柴。
  • Letting the beef sear undisturbed for the first 45 seconds builds browning instead of steaming the meat in its own juices.下锅前先让牛肉贴锅煎 45 秒再翻动，能迅速煎出焦香而不出汤。
  • Coarsely cracked black pepper keeps far more aroma than pre-ground fine pepper and blooms in the hot sauce.用现磨或粗碎黑胡椒比细胡椒粉香气更冲，随酱汁受热后味道更立体。
  • Removing the beef before cooking the vegetables stops it from overcooking while the sauce reduces.牛肉提前盛出，等蔬菜炒软、汤汁收浓再回锅，能防止牛肉久煮变老。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The beef turned out tough and chewy牛肉咬着发硬发柴
    Make sure you slice against the grain, keep the heat high, and sear in two batches if the pan is crowded so the beef browns fast.检查下次是否逆纹切肉，并且不要一次塞满锅；锅不够大就分两批煎，缩短炒制时间。
  • The beef released gray liquid and steamed牛肉出水变成灰白水煮感
    Pat the steak dry before slicing and wait until the pan is smoking hot before adding the meat.牛排切前先用厨房纸吸干表面，锅要烧到足够烫再下肉。
  • The black pepper flavor is too mild黑椒香味不够浓
    Use freshly cracked coarse black pepper instead of old ground pepper, and add another 1/2 tsp fresh cracks just before serving.改用现磨粗黑胡椒碎，出锅前再现磨半茶匙拌一下即可提香。
