Shakshuka番茄烩蛋（Shakshuka）

A home dish from around the Mediterranean and Middle East: tomatoes and peppers simmered into a thick sauce, eggs nestled on top and covered until the whites set and the yolks still run. Bring the pan to the table and mop it up with bread. Think of it as a distant cousin of tomato and egg stir-fry.地中海和中东一带的家常菜，番茄彩椒炖成浓酱，在上面卧几个蛋，盖盖焖到蛋白凝固、蛋黄还流心。整锅端上桌，用面包蘸着吃。中国胃会觉得像番茄炒蛋的远房亲戚。

35 min total分钟 (10 + 25)3 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Ground cumin另购：孜然

Ingredients食材

  • Eggs鸡蛋 300 g · 6 large eggs6 个
  • Canned diced tomatoes番茄罐头(碎) 800 g · 1 can (796 mL) crushed tomatoes番茄碎罐头 1 罐（796 mL）
  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 small onion, sliced1 个小洋葱，切丝
  • Bell peppers彩椒 200 g · 1 red bell pepper, cut in strips1 个红彩椒，切条
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 3 cloves, sliced3 瓣，切片
  • Ground cumin孜然 3 g · 1 tsp ground cumin孜然粉 1 茶匙
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 28 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp (if the tomatoes are sharp)1 茶匙（番茄太酸时放）
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp约 2/3 茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · a few grinds少许
  • Feta菲达奶酪 60 g · 60 g feta, crumbled (optional)菲达奶酪 60 克，捏碎（可不放）
  • Fresh parsley欧芹 5 g · a small handful, chopped一小把，切碎
  • Sandwich bread面包 200 g · 200 g crusty bread or pita, to serve面包或口袋饼 200 克，配着吃

Method做法

  1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat and soften the onion and pepper, about 6 minutes.大平底锅中火热橄榄油，下洋葱和彩椒炒软，约 6 分钟。
  2. Add the garlic and cumin and cook 1 minute until fragrant.加蒜片和孜然炒 1 分钟出香。
  3. Pour in the tomatoes, season with salt, sugar and pepper and simmer 10 minutes until thick.倒入番茄碎，加盐、糖和黑胡椒，小火煮 10 分钟到变稠。
  4. Make 6 hollows in the sauce with a spoon and crack an egg into each.用勺子在酱里压出 6 个小坑，每个坑打入一个蛋。
  5. Cover and cook on low for 5-7 minutes until the whites are set and the yolks still soft.盖上锅盖，小火焖 5–7 分钟到蛋白凝固、蛋黄仍软。
  6. Scatter the feta and parsley and serve from the pan with bread.撒菲达奶酪和欧芹，整锅上桌配面包。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Thicken the sauce before adding the eggs; in a thin sauce they spread and it turns watery.酱要先煮稠再打蛋，太稀的话蛋会散开、酱也水汪汪。
  • The lid sets the tops of the whites; for runny yolks stop at 7 minutes.盖盖焖是为了让蛋白表面也熟，蛋黄要流心就别超过 7 分钟。
  • Canned tomatoes are more reliable than fresh, especially in winter.罐头番茄比新鲜番茄味道稳定，冬天尤其如此。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The whites will not set蛋白一直不熟
    No lid or too little heat. Cover tightly and nudge the sauce away from the whites so they heat through.盖子没盖或火太小。盖紧锅盖，把蛋白周围的酱用勺子拨开一点让它受热。
  • Too acidic太酸
    Add 1 tsp sugar and cook 3 minutes more, or stir in a little feta.加 1 茶匙糖多煮 3 分钟，或拌入一点菲达奶酪。
