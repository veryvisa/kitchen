Kung Pao Chicken宫保鸡丁

The soul of Kung Pao is not heat but a balance of salty, sweet and sour wrapped in the smoky aroma of chilies fried to dark red. The peanuts go in last so they stay crunchy. The tricky part is oil temperature, because chilies need heat to bloom but burn in seconds.宫保鸡丁的灵魂不是辣，而是「糊辣荔枝味」：辣椒和花椒在热油里炸到暗红，再用糖和醋把咸、甜、酸压在一起，最后才放花生，保住脆。难点在火候，油温太低辣椒出不了香，太高一秒就苦。

45 min total分钟 (20 + 10)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dried chili, Sichuan peppercorn, Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine另购：干辣椒、花椒、老抽、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 300 g · about 4 boneless skinless thighs去骨去皮鸡腿肉约 4 块
  • Roasted peanuts花生 40 g · about 1/4 cup, raw or roasted约 1/4 杯，熟花生或生花生均可 raw ones are fried in cold oil first生花生要先用冷油炸熟
  • Dried chili干辣椒 8 g · 8 to 10 dried red chilies8 到 10 个干辣椒
  • Sichuan peppercorn花椒 2 g · about 1 tsp whole Sichuan peppercorns约 1 茶匙花椒粒
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 60 g · about 4 stalks, white and pale green parts约 4 根，取葱白
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 4 cloves4 瓣
  • Ginger姜 8 g · 4 slices4 片
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1.5 tbsp (1/2 tbsp for the chicken, 1 tbsp for the sauce)1.5 汤匙（腌肉半汤匙，调汁 1 汤匙）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 6 g · 1 tsp, for the sauce1 茶匙，调汁用
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp, for the marinade1 汤匙，腌肉用
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 9 g · 3 tsp (2 for the chicken, 1 for the sauce)3 茶匙（腌肉 2 茶匙，调汁 1 茶匙）
  • Sugar白糖 12 g · 1 tbsp, for the sauce1 汤匙，调汁用
  • Rice vinegar米醋 23 g · 1.5 tbsp, for the sauce1.5 汤匙，调汁用 Chinkiang black vinegar is even better有镇江香醋更好
  • Canola oil菜籽油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Cut the thighs into cubes of about 1.5 cm.鸡腿肉切成约 1.5 厘米的方丁。
  2. Mix the chicken with 1/2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp wine, 2 tsp cornstarch and 1 tbsp water, working it until the liquid disappears into the meat.鸡丁加生抽半汤匙、料酒 1 汤匙、淀粉 2 茶匙和 1 汤匙水，抓到水分被吸进去。
  3. Cover and let it marinate for 15 minutes.盖好腌 15 分钟。
  4. Snip the chilies into 2 cm pieces and shake out most of the seeds.干辣椒剪成 2 厘米的段，抖掉大部分辣椒籽。
  5. Cut the scallion whites into 1.5 cm sections and slice the garlic and ginger small.葱白切成 1.5 厘米的段，蒜和姜切成小片。
  6. In a small bowl stir 1 tbsp soy sauce, the dark soy, sugar, vinegar, 1 tsp cornstarch and 1 tbsp water until the sugar dissolves.小碗里放生抽 1 汤匙、老抽 1 茶匙、糖、醋、淀粉 1 茶匙和 1 汤匙水，搅到糖化开。
  7. Put 1 tbsp oil and the peanuts in a cold wok over medium-low heat, stir until pale gold and set aside; skip this if yours are already roasted.冷锅放 1 汤匙油和花生，中小火炒到花生微黄，盛出，熟花生可省略这一步。
  8. Heat 1 tbsp oil over high heat, stir-fry the chicken until white outside and about 80% cooked, then take it out.锅里加 1 汤匙油烧热，下鸡丁大火炒到表面变白、约八成熟，盛出。
  9. Add the last tbsp of oil over medium heat and fry the chilies and peppercorns for about 20 seconds, moving on as soon as the chilies turn dark red.锅里补 1 汤匙油，转中火，下辣椒和花椒炒约 20 秒，辣椒变成暗红色就要立刻往下走。
  10. Add the ginger, garlic and scallion whites and stir for about 10 seconds until fragrant.放入姜蒜和葱白，炒出香味，约 10 秒。
  11. Return the chicken, turn the heat to high and toss a few times.鸡丁回锅，转大火翻炒几下。
  12. Stir the sauce bowl once more and pour it in, then toss for about 20 seconds until the glaze clings to the chicken.把调好的碗汁搅一下再倒入，翻炒约 20 秒，汁变稠裹住鸡丁。
  13. Turn off the heat, fold in the peanuts and serve at once.关火，倒入花生拌匀，立刻装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Fry the chilies and peppercorns over medium heat for only about 20 seconds; the point is to put their aroma into the oil. Dark red is right, black means bitter.辣椒和花椒要在中火的热油里炒，只有 20 秒，目的是把香味炸进油里，颜色暗红即可，发黑就苦了。
  • The peanuts go in last because they soften once they sit in sauce, so leftovers will never be crunchy again.花生最后才放，沾上汁以后再久放会回软；吃不完的菜第二天花生一定不脆，这是正常的。
  • Mix the sauce before you start. Everything happens in seconds, and hunting for bottles mid-stir is how chicken overcooks.碗汁一定提前调好：糖、醋、酱油要在几秒内进锅，边炒边找调料，鸡丁早就老了。
  • Working a little water and starch into the chicken locks in moisture and keeps the cubes juicy.腌肉时加水抓匀，淀粉锁住水分，鸡腿肉丁才会嫩滑。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The chilies burned and the dish is bitter辣椒炒糊发苦
    If the oil is too hot, pull the pan off the heat for 10 seconds before adding chilies. If they already blackened, pick them out and fry a fresh pinch to bring the aroma back.油太热就先关火等 10 秒再下辣椒；已经发黑的辣椒挑出来，换新辣椒重炒一小撮，用来补香。
  • The balance is too sweet or too sour味道偏甜或偏酸，不平衡
    Kung Pao should taste salty, sweet and sour at once. If too sweet, add half a teaspoon of vinegar; if too sour, half a teaspoon of sugar. Next time stick to the bowl ratios.宫保讲究咸、甜、酸同时到舌头：太甜多加半茶匙醋，太酸加半茶匙糖；下次按碗汁的比例，不要凭手感乱加。
  • The chicken is dry and tough鸡肉又老又干
    Take the chicken out at 80% cooked and give it only 30 seconds on its return. Do not crowd the wok, or the meat steams and toughens.鸡丁先炒到八成熟就盛出，最后回锅只加热 30 秒；一次不要放太多，锅里挤了会出水煮老。
