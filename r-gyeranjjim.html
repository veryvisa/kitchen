Korean Steamed Egg韩式蒸蛋

Korean steamed egg is tender and softly puffed, making an easy side dish for a home meal. Gentle heat and warm broth help it set evenly; high heat or overcooking can make it spongy and watery.韩式蒸蛋细嫩蓬松，鸡蛋和温热高汤搅匀后小火慢蒸，适合作为家常配菜。火太大或蒸得过久，容易让蛋液变蜂窝、析出水分。

23 min total分钟 (8 + 15)2 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Eggs鸡蛋 200 g · 4 large eggs4 个大鸡蛋
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 180 g · about 3/4 cup, warmed约 3/4 杯，温热
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 1 stalk, finely chopped1 根，切细葱花
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · about 1 tsp约 1 茶匙
  • Salt盐 3 g · about 1/2 tsp, adjust for the stock's saltiness约半茶匙，按高汤咸度调整
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 3 g · 1 tsp, for garnish1 茶匙，装饰用

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Warm the stock until pleasantly warm but not hot, about 40-50°C.把高汤加热到温热、不烫手，约 40–50°C。
  2. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add the salt and warm stock, and whisk gently until the whites and yolks are combined.鸡蛋打入碗中，加盐和温热高汤，轻轻搅打至蛋白与蛋黄融合。
  3. Strain the egg mixture into a small lidded pot or heatproof bowl and skim off large surface bubbles.将蛋液过筛倒入带盖的小锅或耐热碗，撇去表面大泡。
  4. Set a rack and about 2 cm water in a pot, bring to a boil over high heat and reduce to low.锅中放入蒸架和约 2 厘米水，大火烧开后转小火。
  5. Place the egg container on the rack, cover the pot and leave a small gap.把装蛋液的容器放在蒸架上，盖上锅盖并留一道小缝。
  6. Steam over low heat for 10-12 minutes (a guide only—container depth and heat affect the actual time), until the edges are set and the center still jiggles gently, then turn off the heat.小火蒸 10–12 分钟（只作参考，视容器深度和火力而定），直到边缘凝固、中心仍轻轻颤动即可关火。
  7. Cover fully and rest for 2 minutes, then confirm with a thin instant-read thermometer that the center has reached 74°C (Health Canada's safe temperature for egg dishes); if it has not, cover and steam on low for 2 to 3 more minutes, then recheck.关火盖严焖 2 分钟后，用细探针温度计确认中心已达到 74°C（加拿大卫生部对鸡蛋类菜肴的安全温度）；未到 74°C 就盖回继续小火蒸 2–3 分钟再复测。
  8. Drizzle with sesame oil, top with scallion and sesame seeds, and serve at once.淋上芝麻油，撒葱花和芝麻后立即上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Whisk just until combined; excess foam can leave a rough surface.蛋液轻轻搅匀即可，打出太多泡沫会让成品表面粗糙。
  • Straining removes stubborn strands of egg white for a more even texture.过筛能滤掉未散开的蛋白，使蒸蛋组织更均匀。
  • Start the eggs after reducing the steamer to low; gentle steam helps prevent large holes.蒸锅转小火后再放蛋液，温和的蒸汽能减少蜂窝孔。
  • Turn off the heat while the center still jiggles slightly; residual heat finishes the set without overcooking.中心还有一点颤动时关火焖熟，余温会完成凝固而不易蒸老。
  • For pregnant women, young children, older adults or anyone with a weaker immune system, steam until the center is fully set and confirmed at 74°C, with no runny egg left.孕妇、幼儿、老人或免疫力较弱者食用时，建议蒸到中心完全凝固、确认达到 74°C，不留流动蛋液。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The steamed egg has large holes蒸蛋有蜂窝孔
    Use low heat next time and keep the water from boiling vigorously; skim large bubbles from the egg mixture first.下次用小火并避免水沸腾翻滚，蛋液表面的大泡也要先撇掉。
  • The bottom is overcooked while the top is still loose底部太老、上面还没凝固
    Use a wider, shallower container and turn off the heat when the center jiggles, then cover fully for 2 minutes.换用浅而宽的容器，蒸到中心轻颤时关火，再盖严焖 2 分钟。
  • Water has separated from the egg蒸蛋出水
    Lower the heat and shorten the steaming; next time avoid vigorous steam and stop before the center turns firm.把火调低并缩短蒸制时间；下次不要用滚沸的蒸汽，也不要蒸到中心发硬。
