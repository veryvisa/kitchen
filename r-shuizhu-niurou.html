Sichuan Boiled Beef水煮牛肉

Sichuan boiled beef features tender, silky beef slices poached in a fiery broth and finished with sizzling oil over crushed chilies and Sichuan peppercorns. The main traps at home are overcooking the beef so the coating slips off, watery vegetables thinning the broth and scorched spices at the end.水煮牛肉不是用水白煮，而是先用蛋清淀粉腌好牛肉片再汆熟，再借着一勺热油泼出满碗麻辣鲜香。家里做容易踩的坑是肉片煮老脱浆、配菜出水冲淡汤味，或者辣椒花椒在泼油时糊掉。

Ingredients食材

  • Striploin steak西冷牛排 400 g · 400 g beef striploin, sliced 2 mm thin against the grain牛外脊（striploin）约 400 克，逆纹切 2 毫米薄片 beef tenderloin or flank steak also works牛里脊或侧腹牛排（flank steak）也合适
  • Napa cabbage大白菜 250 g · 4-5 napa cabbage leaves (about 250 g), stems sliced and leaves torn into bite-size pieces大白菜 4–5 片（约 250 克），菜帮切条、菜叶撕成巴掌大小块
  • Mung bean sprouts绿豆芽 150 g · about 150 g bean sprouts, trimmed黄豆芽或绿豆芽约 150 克，摘去根须
  • Celery西芹 80 g · 1 stalk celery (about 80 g), sliced thinly on the diagonal1 根西芹（约 80 克），斜切薄片
  • Eggs鸡蛋 30 g · 1 large egg (white only)1 个大鸡蛋（只用蛋清）
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 6 g · 2 tsp, for velveting2 茶匙（腌肉用） use 3 tsp (9 g) for thicker slices肉片切得较厚时可用 3 茶匙（9 克）
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp light soy sauce (1 tbsp for marinade, 1 tbsp for broth)2 汤匙生抽（腌肉 1 汤匙，调汤 1 汤匙）

  • Fermented black beans (douchi)豆豉 8 g · 1.5 tsp fermented black beans, chopped1.5 茶匙豆豉，切碎
  • Dried chili干辣椒 12 g · 10-12 dried chilies, snipped and seeded10–12 个干辣椒，剪段去籽
  • Chinese coarse chili flakes (la jiao mian)中式粗辣椒面（辣椒碎） 10 g · 2 tbsp coarse chili flakes (Sichuan-style)2 汤匙川式粗辣椒面（辣椒碎）
  • Sichuan peppercorn花椒 6 g · 1 tbsp whole Sichuan peppercorns (half toasted and chopped, half lightly crushed for topping)1 汤匙整粒花椒（一半焙香剁碎，一半略捣碎最后泼油）
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · 5 cloves, minced5 瓣，切末
  • Ginger姜 15 g · about 4 slices (2 slices shredded for marinade, 2 slices minced for the sauce)约 4 片（2 片切丝腌肉，2 片切末炒底料）
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks, whites cut in pieces and greens chopped3 根，葱白切段，葱绿切花

  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 5 g · 1.5 tsp toasted white sesame seeds1.5 茶匙熟白芝麻

Method做法

  1. Knead the sliced beef with 1 tbsp light soy sauce, the Shaoxing wine, 2 slices shredded ginger and 2 tbsp water for 2-3 minutes until the liquid is absorbed and the beef feels sticky.牛肉片加 1 汤匙生抽、绍兴料酒、2 片切丝的姜和 2 汤匙清水，用力抓揉 2–3 分钟直到水分被完全吸收、肉片发粘。
  2. Mix in the egg white until frothy, then coat with the cornstarch and 1/2 tbsp oil; refrigerate for 10-15 minutes while preparing the other ingredients.加入蛋清抓匀起泡，再拌入玉米淀粉和半汤匙油封住水分，放入冰箱冷藏腌制 10–15 分钟，同时准备其他食材和汤底。
  3. Put 1 tbsp oil in a cold wok over low heat, add the dried chilies and half the peppercorns, and stir constantly for 1-2 minutes until the chilies darken slightly to deep red and smell toasty; remove immediately, cool and chop coarsely. Lightly crush the remaining peppercorns.冷锅放 1 汤匙油，小火放入干辣椒和一半花椒，不停翻炒 1–2 分钟至辣椒略变暗红、闻到焦香后立刻盛出，放凉后剁成碎末；另一半生花椒略捣碎备用。
  4. Heat 1 tbsp oil over high heat, stir-fry the celery and napa cabbage stems for 1 minute, then add the cabbage leaves, bean sprouts and 1/4 tsp salt for 1 minute until just tender (cook in two batches if your wok is small). Drain well as you transfer them to a large deep bowl, leaving any liquid behind.锅中放 1 汤匙油大火烧热，先炒西芹和白菜帮 1 分钟，再下白菜叶、豆芽和 1/4 茶匙盐炒 1 分钟至断生（锅小可分两批）；捞入大深碗底时充分沥干，把菜汁留在外头。
  5. Add 1 tbsp oil over low-medium heat and fry the Pixian doubanjiang and fermented black beans for about 1 minute until red oil separates, then add the 2 slices of minced ginger, half the garlic and the scallion whites for 20-30 seconds until fragrant.锅中放 1 汤匙油，中小火下郫县豆瓣酱和豆豉炒约 1 分钟至出红油，再加入 2 片姜切成的姜末、一半蒜末和葱白段炒香 20–30 秒。
  6. Pour in the stock, remaining 1 tbsp light soy sauce and sugar, bring to a boil and simmer 3-4 minutes over medium heat. Taste and adjust salt or soy sauce if needed (you may strain out the solids for a cleaner broth, as in the traditional method), then cook the beef immediately.倒入鸡汤、剩余 1 汤匙生抽和糖，大火煮开后转中火熬煮 3–4 分钟；试味后按需调整咸淡（也可按传统做法滤去汤渣），汤底一熬好就立刻煮牛肉。
  7. Keep the broth at a gentle simmer (small bubbles, about 90-95°C), drop the beef slices in separately (in two batches if needed), wait 15-20 seconds for the coating to set, separate with chopsticks and cook 30-40 seconds more until no pink remains (at least 63°C internally).把火调到微沸（水面保持小泡，约 90–95°C），牛肉片一片片抖散下锅（需要时可分两批），静置 15–20 秒定形后用筷子拨散，再煮 30–40 秒至不见粉色、熟透（中心达 63°C 以上）。
  8. Pour the beef and broth over the vegetables, scatter the remaining raw minced garlic on top, then mound the chopped toasted chilies and peppercorns, coarse chili flakes, lightly crushed peppercorns, sesame seeds and scallion greens over the garlic layer.连肉带汤倒入装菜的大碗，先撒上剩余生蒜末，再铺上切碎的刀口辣椒花椒末、粗辣椒面、略捣碎的剩余花椒、熟白芝麻和葱花。
  9. Heat the final 3 tbsp (45 mL) oil in a small pan to about 170-180°C (until it barely wisps smoke) and pour in two stages over the aromatics: ladle half over the chili flakes first, wait for the sizzling to subside, then pour over the rest.另用小锅把最后 3 汤匙（45 mL）油烧到约 170–180°C（微微冒白烟），分两次淋在香料堆上：先淋一半在辣椒面上，等滋啦声略平息后再淋入另一半即可上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Work water into the beef before the egg white, cornstarch and oil; that order locks moisture inside and keeps the slices from clumping.腌牛肉先打水再下蛋清和淀粉，最后封油，肉片才会又滑又嫩，下锅也不易粘连。
  • Poach the beef at a gentle simmer rather than a rolling boil so the velvety coating stays intact and the meat stays tender.牛肉下锅时汤面只能微微冒泡，大火滚煮会把外层淀粉冲散，肉也会变硬。
  • Toasting and chopping the chilies with half the peppercorns gives deep roasted notes, while lightly crushing the remaining raw peppercorns for the topping adds bright numbing aroma when the hot oil hits.先把干辣椒和一半花椒焙香剁碎做成「刀口辣椒」，另一半花椒略捣碎生撒在表面，热油一泼既有焙焦香又有鲜麻味。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The beef turned chewy or its starch coating washed into the broth牛肉片又老又柴，或者淀粉浆脱落混汤
    Keep the broth at a gentle simmer, let the slices sit undisturbed for 15-20 seconds before separating with chopsticks, pull them off within a minute and slice thinner against the grain next time.保持微沸不要大火滚煮，下肉后静置 15–20 秒定形再用筷子拨散，总煮制时间控制在 1 分钟内；下次切得更薄并确保逆纹切。
  • The chili flakes scorched and turned bitter after the hot oil pour泼油后辣椒面发黑发苦
    Heat the oil only to 170-180°C until a faint wisp of smoke appears rather than heavily smoking, use a thicker mound of coarse chili flakes and pour the oil in two stages.油温控制在 170–180°C 微微冒烟即可，不要烧到大烟滚滚；可用厚一点的粗辣椒面堆并分两次泼油。
  • The broth tastes watery and flat汤底发淡、不够香浓
    Always stir-fry the base vegetables over high heat and drain off excess liquid before plating, and taste the broth before cooking the beef; if the finished bowl tastes thin, stir a splash of light soy sauce into the broth.铺底蔬菜一定要大火炒干并沥干菜汁再盛碗，不要直接生垫；下肉前先试味调整，已做好的话可在碗边补少许生抽拌匀。

Beef and vegetables provide protein and fiber, but the dish is high in oil and sodium; eat mostly the beef and vegetables and leave most of the red oil and broth.牛肉和蔬菜提供蛋白质与膳食纤维，但整道菜油脂和钠含量较高；吃的时候以吃牛肉和配菜为主，尽量少喝红油和汤底。
