Lemon Chicken Orzo柠檬鸡肉米粒面

Lemon, oregano and chicken stock give the orzo a bright, savory flavor, and the chicken cooks in the same pan. The key is to brown the chicken first and watch the pan as the pasta absorbs the broth.柠檬、牛至和鸡汤把米粒面煮得清香入味，鸡肉也在同一锅里完成。关键是把鸡肉煎上色后再焖熟，并看住锅底，避免米粒面吸干汤汁后粘锅。

45 min total分钟 (15 + 30)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Orzo pasta另购：米粒面

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 700 g · about 4–5 boneless chicken thighs (about 700 g total)去骨鸡腿肉约 4–5 块（共约 700 克）
  • Orzo pasta米粒面 250 g · 1 1/4 cups dry orzo干米粒面 1 又 1/4 杯
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 850 g · about 3 1/2 cups chicken stock约 3 又 1/2 杯鸡汤
  • Lemons柠檬 160 g · 1 large lemon (about 160 g whole), zested and juiced1 个大柠檬（约 160 克整果），取皮屑和汁
  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 medium onion, diced1 个中等洋葱，切丁
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 4 cloves, minced4 瓣，切末
  • Spinach菠菜 120 g · about 4 cups baby spinach约 4 杯嫩菠菜
  • Dried oregano干牛至/香草 2 g · 1 tsp dried oregano1 茶匙干牛至
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Salt盐 7 g · about 1 tsp, divided约 1 茶匙，分次使用
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · about 1/2 tsp约 1/2 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Pat the chicken dry and season both sides with salt and black pepper.鸡腿肉擦干，两面撒上盐和黑胡椒。
  2. Heat the olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat and sear the chicken for about 3–4 minutes per side until golden, then transfer it to a plate; it does not need to be cooked through yet.宽锅中火加热橄榄油，鸡肉每面煎约 3–4 分钟至金黄后盛出，这时不需要煎熟透，后面还会焖煮。
  3. Keep the pan over medium heat and cook the onion for 4 minutes until soft and translucent.原锅转中火，放入洋葱炒 4 分钟，至变软透亮。
  4. Add the garlic and oregano and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.加入蒜末和牛至，炒 30 秒至闻到香味。
  5. Add the orzo and stir over medium heat for 1 minute to coat the grains with oil.倒入米粒面，中火翻炒 1 分钟，让面粒均匀裹油。
  6. Pour in the stock and lemon zest, scrape up the browned bits and bring to a gentle boil.倒入鸡汤和柠檬皮屑，刮起锅底焦香，煮至微沸。
  7. Return the chicken and any plate juices to the pan, keep it at a gentle simmer rather than a rolling boil, cover and cook for 8 minutes.把鸡肉连同盘中汁水放回锅里，保持小火微沸（不要大滚），盖盖煮 8 分钟。
  8. Uncover and stir the orzo, checking frequently from here; cook for about 4–6 minutes more until tender and the chicken reaches 74°C in the center. Add hot stock if the orzo is absorbing liquid too quickly.揭盖后翻动米粒面，从这时起勤查看，再煮约 4–6 分钟，至面粒柔软且鸡肉中心达到 74°C；汤汁吸得太快就加一点热鸡汤。
  9. Fold in the spinach until just wilted, then stir in the lemon juice off the heat.拌入菠菜炒至刚塌软，离火后再拌入柠檬汁。
  10. Turn off the heat and rest for 3 minutes, then taste and adjust the salt before serving.关火静置 3 分钟，尝味后调整盐量再上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Pat the chicken dry before it goes in the pan to help it brown; an overcrowded pan makes it release moisture and stay pale instead.鸡肉下锅前擦干表面水分，有助于煎出金黄色；如果锅太挤，鸡肉容易出水，影响上色。
  • A wide pan spreads the orzo in a thinner layer, helping it cook evenly and making the liquid level easier to monitor.用宽锅让米粒面铺得较薄，受热均匀，也更容易观察汤汁是否快收干。
  • Stock varies in saltiness, so start with less salt and adjust after cooking.鸡汤的咸度各有不同，先少放盐，最后尝味再补。
  • Orzo keeps absorbing liquid quickly, so leave a little loose sauce in the pan before resting.米粒面继续吸汁很快，关火时保留一点松润汤汁，静置后口感正好。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The orzo sticks or scorches on the bottom米粒面粘锅或底部烧焦
    Lower the heat, add a splash of hot stock and loosen the bottom while stirring gently; stir more often as it thickens to avoid scorching, and use a heavy pan next time.转小火，加入少量热鸡汤，刮松锅底并轻轻翻动；煮至浓稠时增加搅拌频率，避免锅底烧焦。下次用厚底锅并避免大火久煮。
  • The chicken is browned outside but undercooked inside鸡肉外面焦了，里面还没熟
    Cover and continue cooking on low, checking for 74°C at the center; choose thighs of similar size next time.盖盖后转小火继续煮，并用温度计确认中心达到 74°C；下次选大小接近的鸡腿肉。
  • The orzo turns out too dry米粒面煮得太干
    Stir in a little hot stock until loose; next time turn off the heat while some liquid remains so resting can finish the absorption.拌入少量热鸡汤至顺滑；下次在锅中仍有些汤汁时就关火，让静置过程完成吸收。
