Store and portion储存与分装

Whole heads keep 2-3 months in a cool, airy place; peeled cloves 1 week in the fridge.整头放通风阴凉处约 2–3 个月；剥好的蒜瓣冷藏 1 周。

Peel and mince a big pack at once and freeze it flat to snap off small portions.大包装的蒜瓣一次剥好、磨成泥，平铺冷冻成小块，用时掰一块。
