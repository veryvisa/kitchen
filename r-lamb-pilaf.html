Xinjiang Lamb Pilaf新疆手抓饭

Lamb, carrots and rice cook together in one pot, with the grains taking on the savoury meat juices and carrot sweetness. The important part is to soften the lamb first and control the liquid so the rice is neither hard nor soggy.羊肉、胡萝卜和米饭在一口锅里慢慢焖熟，米粒吸进肉香与胡萝卜的甜味。成败在于先把羊肉炖到软，再控制加水量，避免米饭夹生或湿黏。

140 min total分钟 (20 + 90)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Ground cumin另购：孜然

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless leg of lamb去骨羊腿肉 700 g · boneless lamb leg, cut into about 3 cm cubes去骨羊腿肉，切约 3 厘米块
  • Jasmine rice茉莉香米 370 g · about 2 cups (US 240 mL cup, about 185 g/cup), dry; long-grain or basmati-style rice gives firmer separate grains if available — reduce the water by about 10% if you switch约 2 杯（US 量杯，约 185 克/杯），干米；也可用长粒米或巴斯马蒂米代替，米粒更分明——如果用，水量减少约 10%
  • Carrots胡萝卜 500 g · 4 large carrots (about 500 g), cut into thick matchsticks; use 6 medium carrots if yours are smaller, to reach the same weight4 根大胡萝卜（约 500 克），切粗条；胡萝卜偏小就用 6 根中等的凑够重量
  • Onions洋葱 200 g · 1 large onion, thinly sliced1 个大洋葱，切丝
  • Ground cumin孜然 4 g · cumin seeds, lightly crushed, about 1 1/4 tsp孜然籽，略压碎，约 1¼ 茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · ground black pepper, about 1/2 tsp现磨黑胡椒，约 ½ 茶匙
  • Salt盐 10 g · about 1 1/2 tsp fine salt, added in two stages (6 g with the lamb, 4 g before steaming), taste and adjust at the end约 1½ 茶匙细盐，分两次加入（炖肉时 6 克＋焖饭前 4 克），收尾尝味再调
  • Canola oil菜籽油 55 g · about 4 tbsp (60 mL)约 4 汤匙（60 mL）
  • Water水 850 g · up to about 3 1/2 cups, used in two stages: about 500-600 g to simmer the lamb, then another 250-300 g before steaming so the water sits about 1 cm above the rice; you may not need all of it最多约 3½ 杯，分两次用：炖肉约 500–600 克，焖饭前再加约 250–300 克使水面高出米约 1 厘米；不必全部用完

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Rinse the rice until the water is mostly clear, soak it in cool water for 20 minutes, then drain and keep it covered (do not let the surface dry out).大米淘洗至水基本清澈，冷水浸泡 20 分钟后沥干，盖好备用（不要让表面风干）。
  2. Pat the lamb dry and cut the carrots into thick matchsticks and the onion into thin slices, about 8 minutes.羊肉用厨房纸擦干，胡萝卜切粗条、洋葱切丝，约需 8 分钟。
  3. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat and brown the lamb in batches (3-4 minutes per batch, about 8 minutes total) until browned on 2-3 sides, then set it aside.厚底锅中大火烧热油，羊肉分批煎（每批 3–4 分钟，共约 8 分钟）至两三面焦黄，盛出备用。
  4. Add the onion to the pot and fry over medium heat for 3 minutes until soft and lightly golden.原锅加入洋葱，中火炒 3 分钟至变软并略带金色。
  5. Return the lamb and add the cumin, black pepper and 6 g salt; stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.放回羊肉，加入孜然、黑胡椒和 6 克盐，翻炒 30 秒至闻到香味。
  6. Pour in 500-600 g water, bring to a boil over high heat, then cover and simmer on low for 35 minutes.倒入 500–600 克水，大火煮开后转小火，加盖炖 35 分钟。
  7. Lay the carrots on top of the lamb without stirring, cover and simmer on low for another 10 minutes until a fork easily enters the lamb and the carrots are just softened but still firm; the pot should now hold about 1-1.5 cm of liquid — boil it down a little if there is too much, or top up with hot water if there is too little.把胡萝卜铺在羊肉上（不要搅动），加盖继续小火炖 10 分钟，至羊肉用叉子容易刺入、胡萝卜刚变软但仍挺括；此时锅里应留约 1–1.5 厘米汤汁，水多就开盖收一下，水少就补热水。
  8. Spread the drained rice evenly over the lamb and carrots without stirring.把沥干的大米均匀铺在羊肉和胡萝卜上，不要搅动。
  9. Pour the remaining hot water (about 250-300 g) and 4 g salt around the edge until the water sits about 1 cm above the rice; you may not need all of it.沿锅边加入剩余热水（约 250–300 克）和 4 克盐，使水面高出米约 1 厘米；水不必全用完。
  10. Boil over medium-high heat (not full high, to avoid scorching the bottom) for about 8-10 minutes until no water is visible on top, then poke 5-6 holes through the rice layer with a chopstick.中大火（不要全开大火，避免锅底烧焦）煮至表面看不见水，约 8–10 分钟，再用筷子在米层表面戳 5–6 个孔。
  11. Cover tightly (on a gas stove, use a heavy lid or wrap a clean tea towel inside the lid to catch condensation) and steam over the lowest heat for 20 minutes; on an electric or induction stove, turn off the heat for the last 5 minutes and let residual heat finish it.盖紧锅盖（燃气灶上最好用厚重锅盖，或在盖子内侧包一层干净茶巾吸走水汽），转最小火焖 20 分钟；电陶炉可以在最后 5 分钟直接关火靠余温焖。
  12. Turn off the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes, then fluff the rice gently: the lamb should be fork-tender and steaming hot throughout.关火后加盖静置 10 分钟，再轻轻翻松米饭：羊肉应该叉子一戳就透、整锅摸起来都很热。
  13. After this long a simmer the lamb is well above 74°C at the centre; refrigerate any leftover rice and lamb within 2 hours in shallow containers, and reheat to 74°C before eating again.经过这么久的炖煮，羊肉中心温度早已远超 74°C；剩饭连肉要在 2 小时内分浅盘冷藏，吃前回热到 74°C。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Drain the rinsed rice well and avoid stirring once it is layered so the grains stay intact.淘洗后把米充分沥干，铺米时不搅动，米粒才容易保持完整。
  • Brown the lamb before simmering it tender to build savoury flavour in the pot and cooking liquid.羊肉先煎出焦黄，再加水炖软，能让肉香留在锅底和汤汁里。
  • Use the rice surface as your guide and aim for water about 1 cm above it; rice type and pot width affect the amount needed.加水以米面高出约 1 厘米为准，米的品种和锅的宽窄都会影响用量。
  • Keep the heat very low and the lid tight during steaming so escaping moisture does not leave hard rice.焖饭期间保持最小火并盖紧锅盖，避免水汽跑掉造成夹生。
  • For a closer-to-restaurant version, scatter 50 g raisins (soaked 10 minutes) and half a head of garlic (cut side down) over the rice before steaming for extra sweetness and garlic aroma.想要更接近馆子味道，可以加 50 克葡萄干（泡软 10 分钟）和半头大蒜（横切面朝下）一起铺在米上焖，带一点甜味和蒜香。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The rice is still hard and the pot has scorched dry米饭夹生、锅底已经烧干
    Add 2-3 tbsp hot water around the edge, cover tightly and steam on the lowest heat for 5-8 minutes; next time start with the water slightly above the rice.沿锅边加 2–3 汤匙热水，盖紧后最小火再焖 5–8 分钟；下次水面要略高于米面。
  • The rice is wet and sticky with liquid at the bottom米饭湿黏、底部有很多汤
    Uncover and cook over medium-low heat for 5-8 minutes without stirring, tilting the pot to check whether liquid remains at the bottom; once the steam has gone, rest it covered with a clean tea towel under the lid for 10 minutes. Next time use less water and drain the rice well.开盖中小火煮 5–8 分钟（不要搅动），倾斜锅看看底部还有没有汤；散完水汽后盖一层干净茶巾再静置 10 分钟。下次少加水并确保大米沥干。
  • The lamb is still tough after simmering羊肉久炖仍发硬
    Add a little hot water, cover and simmer on low for another 15 minutes; wait until a fork enters easily before layering the carrots and rice.补少量热水并加盖小火再炖 15 分钟，确认叉子能轻松刺入后再铺胡萝卜和米。
