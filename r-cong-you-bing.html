Scallion Pancakes葱油饼

A good scallion pancake is crisp on the outside, tender inside, and packed with distinct flaky layers. The keys are a half-scalded dough so it stays soft, a flour-oil paste to separate the layers, and enough resting time so the coils roll out without tearing.家常葱油饼讲究外皮酥脆、内层软韧且层层分明。成败的关键在于用半烫面保留柔软口感，再靠油酥隔开面层并充分松弛，擀起来才薄而不破。

84 min total分钟 (20 + 24)4 servings份Medium中等Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • All-purpose flour面粉 325 g · 325 g all-purpose flour (2 1/2 cups plus 2 tbsp; 300 g for dough, 25 g for oil paste)中筋面粉 325 克（约 2.5 杯加 2 汤匙；300 克和面，25 克调油酥）
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 80 g · 6 stalks green onion, finely chopped6 根香葱，切细葱花
  • Canola oil菜籽油 98 g · 7 tbsp (3 tbsp for oil paste, 4 tbsp for pan-frying)7 汤匙（3 汤匙调油酥，4 汤匙烙饼）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 7 g · 1/2 tbsp (optional, for the oil paste)半汤匙（调油酥增香，可选）
  • Salt盐 6 g · 1 tsp (for the oil paste)1 茶匙（调入油酥）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Put 300 g flour in a large bowl and slowly pour 95 mL boiling water into half of it while stirring with chopsticks into shaggy flakes.把 300 克面粉放进大盆，向其中一半面粉缓缓倒入 95 毫升刚烧开的沸水，边倒边用筷子搅成絮状。
  2. Pour 90 mL room-temperature water into the remaining dry flour, mix everything together, and knead into a fairly smooth dough.再向另一半干面粉倒入 90 毫升室温清水，搅匀后揉成表面较光滑的面团。
  3. Cover the dough with plastic wrap or a damp towel and rest for 30 minutes.盖上保鲜膜或湿布，静置松弛 30 分钟。
  4. Combine 25 g flour and the salt in a small bowl, then stir in 3 tbsp hot canola oil and the sesame oil to make a smooth paste.把 25 克面粉和盐放进小碗，淋入 3 汤匙烧热的菜籽油与香油拌成顺滑的油酥。
  5. Divide the rested dough into 4 equal balls, lightly oil the counter, and roll each piece into a 2 mm thick rectangle.松弛好的面团分成 4 等份滚圆，案板抹薄油，把每份擀成约 2 毫米厚的长方形薄片。
  6. Spread an even coat of oil paste across the dough sheet and scatter the thoroughly dried chopped green onions evenly on top.在面片表面均匀抹上一层油酥，铺满擦干水分的葱花。
  7. Roll up tightly from a long edge into a rope, then coil it into a spiral and tuck the end underneath the coil.从长边紧实卷起成长条，再盘成螺旋状并将收口压在底部。
  8. Cover the coiled rounds and let them rest for 10 more minutes.盖上保鲜膜，再次静置松弛 10 分钟。
  9. Press each coil flat with your palm and gently roll into an 18 cm round about 4 mm thick.用手掌按扁后轻轻擀成直径约 18 厘米、厚约 4 毫米的圆饼。
  10. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium heat, add one pancake, cover, and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the underside turns deeply golden and blisters.平底锅加 1 汤匙油中火烧热，放入一张饼胚，盖盖烙 3 至 4 分钟至底面深金黄起泡。
  11. Flip and cook uncovered for 2 to 3 minutes, adjusting for your pan, until both sides are deeply golden and crisp and the pancake is cooked through.翻面后开盖再烙 2 至 3 分钟，根据锅具火力微调，至两面深金黄酥脆且内部熟透。
  12. Tap the pancake lightly with a spatula to loosen the flaky layers, slice into wedges, and serve hot.用铲子轻拍饼面让层次松散，切块趁热上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Half-scalded dough balances the tenderness of hot-water dough with the structure of cold-water dough, so leftovers stay soft.半烫面兼顾烫面的柔软和冷水面团的筋度，饼放凉后也不容易发硬。
  • Brushing with a flour-oil paste rather than plain oil keeps each layer separated during rolling and cooking.用油酥代替纯清油刷面，油里的面粉能稳定隔开每一层，烙出来层次更清晰。
  • Mixing the salt into the oil paste instead of onto the scallions keeps the scallions from weeping and tearing the dough.盐调进油酥而不是直接拌进葱花，能防止葱花提前出水把面皮泡破。
  • Covering for the first side traps steam to keep the interior tender, while uncovering for the second side crisps the crust.先盖盖烙能留住内部水汽让饼心柔软，翻面后开盖则能把表皮收干变脆。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The dough snaps back or tears and leaks scallions when rolled饼胚擀不薄、一擀就回缩或破皮漏葱
    Do not skip either rest period; roll gently outward from the center rather than pressing back and forth with heavy force.两次静置松弛时间不能省，擀饼时从中心向四周轻推，不要用力来回死压。
  • The pancake turns out tough, dry, or dense without layers烙出来发硬发干、没有分层
    Use half-scalded dough and an even layer of oil paste next time, and cook over medium heat covered on the first side instead of drying it out over long low heat.下次改用半烫面并抹匀油酥，用中火快烙并在第一面加盖锁水，不要小火慢烤太久。
  • Burnt on the outside while raw in the center外面焦黑而饼心还生
    The pan was too hot or the dough too thick; lower to medium-low heat and roll each pancake to about 4 mm thick.锅温过高或饼擀得太厚，把火力降至中小火，饼胚厚度控制在 4 毫米左右。
