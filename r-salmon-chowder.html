Salmon Chowder三文鱼土豆浓汤

A Pacific coast winter soup. Potato and onion simmer soft, milk with a splash of cream gives body, and the salmon goes in last for just a few minutes. Half of a Costco salmon side makes a pot; with bread it is a meal.太平洋沿岸的冬天汤。土豆和洋葱炖软，牛奶加一点奶油调出浓度，三文鱼块最后放、煮几分钟就好。Costco 一块三文鱼正好分一半做汤，配面包就是一顿饭。

45 min total分钟 (15 + 30)6 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Salmon fillet三文鱼排 400 g · 400 g salmon, skinned and cut into 2 cm cubes三文鱼 400 克，去皮切 2 厘米块
  • Potatoes土豆 400 g · 2 medium potatoes, cut into 1.5 cm dice2 个中等土豆，切 1.5 厘米丁
  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 small onion, diced1 个小洋葱，切丁
  • Celery西芹 100 g · 2 celery stalks, diced2 根西芹，切丁
  • Carrots胡萝卜 100 g · 1 carrot, diced1 根胡萝卜，切丁
  • Butter黄油 28 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • All-purpose flour面粉 16 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 500 g · 2 cups (500 mL)2 杯（500 mL）
  • Milk 2%牛奶 500 g · 2 cups (500 mL)2 杯（500 mL）
  • Whipping cream淡奶油 60 g · 1/4 cup (60 mL)1/4 杯（60 mL）
  • Bay leaves月桂叶 1 g · 1 leaf1 片
  • Dried thyme干百里香 1 g · 1/2 tsp1/2 茶匙
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp, adjust to the stock约 2/3 茶匙，按高汤咸度调
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · a few grinds少许
  • Fresh parsley欧芹 5 g · a small handful, chopped一小把，切碎

Method做法

  1. Melt the butter in a pot over medium heat and soften the onion, celery and carrot, about 5 minutes.汤锅中火熔化黄油，下洋葱、西芹、胡萝卜炒软，约 5 分钟。
  2. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for a minute until no dry flour shows.撒入面粉，翻炒 1 分钟到看不见干粉。
  3. Pour in the stock slowly while stirring, until there are no lumps.一边搅一边慢慢倒入高汤，搅到没有面疙瘩。
  4. Add the potato, bay leaf and thyme, cover and simmer 12-15 minutes until the potato is tender.加土豆、月桂叶和百里香，盖盖小火煮 12–15 分钟到土豆软。
  5. Pour in the milk and cream and heat until small bubbles appear; do not let it boil hard.倒入牛奶和奶油，加热到冒小泡，不要大滚。
  6. Add the salmon and cook on low for 4-5 minutes, just until it is cooked through.放入三文鱼块，小火煮 4–5 分钟到鱼肉刚熟。
  7. Remove the bay leaf, season with salt and pepper and add the parsley.取出月桂叶，加盐和黑胡椒调味，撒欧芹。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cooking the flour in butter before adding liquid thickens the soup without a raw-flour taste.面粉先在黄油里炒熟再加液体，汤才会稠而且没有生粉味。
  • Once the milk is in, keep it gentle; a hard boil makes it split and grainy.加奶以后只能小火，大滚会让奶分离起颗粒。
  • Add the salmon last and stir gently so the pieces stay whole.三文鱼最后放，搅动要轻，鱼块才完整不散。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The soup is too thin汤太稀
    Crush a few potato pieces against the pot with a spoon and simmer 2 more minutes.用勺背压碎几块土豆搅进汤里，再煮 2 分钟就会变稠。
  • The milk curdled奶起了颗粒
    The heat was too high. Take it off, whisk in a splash of cold milk and keep it low from now on.火太大了。离火加一点冷牛奶快速搅匀，之后保持小火。
