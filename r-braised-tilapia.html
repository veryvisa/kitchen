Soy-Braised Fish (Hong Shao Yu)红烧鱼块

The fish dish most families cook. Traditionally a whole fish; in North America Costco's frozen tilapia loins make it easier. Pan-fry until golden, then braise a few minutes with soy, sugar, vinegar, ginger and garlic until the sauce is glossy and perfect over rice.家里最常做的鱼。传统用整条鱼，在北美用 Costco 冷冻罗非鱼柳更省事：先煎到两面金黄，再用酱油、糖、醋和姜蒜焖几分钟，汤汁浓稠，拌饭正好。

30 min total分钟 (10 + 20)3 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine, Dark soy sauce另购：料酒(绍兴)、老抽

Ingredients食材

  • Tilapia fillet罗非鱼 500 g · about 500 g tilapia loins (4-5 pieces), cut into large chunks罗非鱼柳约 500 克（4–5 块），切大块 thaw overnight in the fridge冷藏过夜解冻
  • All-purpose flour面粉 15 g · 2 tbsp, for dusting2 汤匙，拍粉用
  • Canola oil菜籽油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Ginger姜 15 g · 5 slices5 片
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · 5 cloves, smashed5 瓣，拍扁
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 3 stalks, cut into lengths3 根，切段
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1.5 tbsp (22 mL)1.5 汤匙（22 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 6 g · 1 tsp (for colour)1 茶匙（上色）
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Rice vinegar米醋 10 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Salt盐 1 g · a small pinch一小撮

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Pat the fish completely dry with paper towel.鱼块用厨房纸彻底吸干水分。
  2. Season the fish with the pinch of salt and dust both sides thinly with flour.鱼块撒一小撮盐，两面薄薄拍一层面粉。
  3. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat, add the oil and lay in the fish.不粘锅中火烧热，倒油，放入鱼块。
  4. Leave it for 3 minutes until the underside is golden.不要动，煎 3 分钟到底面金黄。
  5. Turn gently, cook 2 minutes more and take the fish out.轻轻翻面再煎 2 分钟，盛出。
  6. In the oil left in the pan, fry the ginger, garlic and white parts of the onion for about 30 seconds.锅里留底油，放姜片、蒜和葱白炒香，约 30 秒。
  7. Add the wine, both soy sauces, sugar, vinegar and 150 mL hot water and bring to a boil.加料酒、生抽、老抽、糖、醋和 150 mL 热水，煮开。
  8. Return the fish and simmer on medium-low for 6 minutes, spooning the sauce over now and then.鱼块放回锅里，转中小火焖 6 分钟，中途舀汤汁淋在鱼上。
  9. Turn up the heat to reduce the sauce until glossy, add the green parts of the onion and serve.开大火收汁到浓稠，撒葱绿出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Dry fish, a dusting of flour and a hot pan with hot oil keep the fish from sticking and breaking.鱼要擦干再拍粉，锅和油都要热，鱼皮才不粘锅不碎。
  • Move the fish as little as possible while braising; baste it instead of turning it.焖的时候少翻动，用勺子淋汁代替翻面。
  • The vinegar cooks off its sharpness and just freshens the fish and removes fishiness.醋在高温下会挥发掉酸味，只留下去腥提鲜的作用。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The fish broke up and stuck鱼块煎碎了、粘锅
    The fish was wet or the pan not hot enough; use a non-stick pan, wait for the oil to heat, and do not move the fish until the first side is set.鱼没擦干或锅不够热；下次用不粘锅、等油热再下鱼，煎好一面之前不要动它。
  • Too dark and too salty颜色发黑、味道太咸
    Too much dark soy or the sauce over-reduced; use just 1 tsp dark soy and stop when the sauce coats a spoon.老抽放多了或收汁过头；老抽只要 1 茶匙，汁收到能挂住勺背即可。
