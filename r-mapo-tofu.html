Mapo Tofu麻婆豆腐

Mapo tofu is meant to be numbing, hot, fragrant and silky all at once. Three things matter at home: blanch the tofu so it holds its shape, fry the chili paste until the oil runs red, thicken in two stages, and add the Sichuan pepper at the very end to keep its aroma.麻婆豆腐讲究「麻、辣、烫、香、酥、嫩、鲜、活」。家里做的难点有三个：豆腐要先焯水定形，豆瓣酱要炒到出红油，芡汁分两次下，最后才撒花椒粉保住麻香。

27 min total分钟 (12 + 15)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Soft / silken tofu, Doubanjiang, Dried chili, Sichuan peppercorn另购：嫩豆腐、豆瓣酱、干辣椒、花椒

Ingredients食材

  • Soft / silken tofu嫩豆腐 400 g · 1 block (about 400 g) soft or silken-firm tofu嫩豆腐或南豆腐 1 盒（约 400 克）
  • Ground beef牛肉馅 100 g · about 100 g ground beef牛肉末约 100 克 ground pork is a fine substitute传统用牛肉末，也可换猪肉末
  • Doubanjiang豆瓣酱 24 g · a generous tablespoon约 1 汤匙多 Pixian brand is best最好用郫县豆瓣
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · 4 cloves, minced4 瓣，切末
  • Ginger姜 8 g · minced, about 2 tsp切末，约 2 茶匙
  • Dried chili干辣椒 3 g · 3 dried chilies, snipped3 个干辣椒，剪段
  • Sichuan peppercorn花椒 3 g · about 2 tsp, toasted and ground fresh约 2 茶匙，现炒现磨
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 2 stalks, chopped2 根，切葱花
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp半汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 2 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 200 g · 1 cup chicken stock or water1 杯鸡汤或清水
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 9 g · 3 tsp, used in two additions3 茶匙，分次勾芡
  • Canola oil菜籽油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp, for the tofu water约 1/3 茶匙，焯豆腐用

Method做法

  1. Cut the tofu into 2 cm cubes.豆腐切成约 2 厘米的方块。
  2. Bring salted water to a simmer, add the tofu for 2 minutes, turn off the heat and leave it in the water.水烧开，加盐，放入豆腐煮 2 分钟后关火，留在热水里。
  3. Toast the peppercorns in a dry pan over low heat for about 1 minute until fragrant, then crush to a powder.花椒放进干锅，小火炒约 1 分钟，闻到香味，碾成粉。
  4. Mix two starch slurries: 1 tsp with 1 tbsp water, and 2 tsp with 3 tbsp water.淀粉分两碗：1 茶匙加 1 汤匙水，另 2 茶匙加 3 汤匙水。
  5. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wok over medium heat, add the beef and break it up, cooking about 3 minutes until dry and lightly browned.锅里放 2 汤匙油，中火下牛肉末，炒散到水分炒干、微微焦黄，约 3 分钟。
  6. Push the beef aside, reduce to low and fry the doubanjiang and chilies for about 1 minute until the oil turns red and smells deep.把肉推到一边，转小火，放豆瓣酱和干辣椒，炒约 1 分钟，油变红、冒出酱香。
  7. Add the ginger and garlic for 15 seconds and mix everything with the beef.放姜末、蒜末炒 15 秒，和肉末拌匀。
  8. Pour in the stock, add the soy sauce and sugar, and bring to a boil.倒入鸡汤，加生抽和糖，煮开。
  9. Lift the tofu out with a slotted spoon into the wok, nudge it gently and simmer 4 minutes.用漏勺捞出豆腐放入锅里，轻轻推匀，中小火煮 4 分钟入味。
  10. Pour in the first, smaller slurry, swirl and cook another minute.先倒入第一碗水淀粉，晃匀，再煮 1 分钟。
  11. Add the second slurry, nudge gently and let the sauce thicken until it coats the tofu.再倒入第二碗水淀粉，轻轻推，让汤汁变浓稠，裹住豆腐。
  12. Transfer to a bowl, sprinkle on the Sichuan pepper and scallion and eat while hot.盛盘，撒花椒粉和葱花，趁烫吃。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Poaching the tofu in salted water firms its surface so it breaks less, and removes the raw bean taste.豆腐在盐水里焯过，表面收紧，不容易碎，还能去豆腥味。
  • Keep the paste on low heat, because a hot pan turns it bitter; red oil tells you it is ready.豆瓣酱只能小火炒，油温高会发苦发糊；红油出来才是好了的信号。
  • Thicken in two stages: the first adds body so flavor clings, the second finishes the gloss, and the tofu does not turn gluey.芡汁分两次下：第一次让汤汁有点稠，使味道挂住，第二次再收到浓稠发亮，豆腐才不会糊成团。
  • Add the Sichuan pepper last; long heat dulls the numbing aroma and makes it bitter.花椒粉最后撒，加热太久麻味会散，也会发苦。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The tofu fell apart豆腐碎了
    Push with the back of the spatula instead of stirring, cut the cubes a bit larger next time and keep blanching to 2 minutes.推的时候用锅铲背轻推，不用翻；下次豆腐切大一点，焯水时间别超过 2 分钟。
  • Hot but not numbing, or numbing and bitter只辣不麻，或者麻得发苦
    Old peppercorns have little numbness. Toast gently until fragrant but never dark, grind them and add at the end.花椒粒新鲜度很重要，旧的花椒没有麻味；要小火炒香再碾，不要炒黑，最后才撒。
  • Too salty or too hot太咸或太辣
    The paste is already salty, so use less and add no salt next time. To rescue it, add a little hot water, half a teaspoon of sugar and more tofu.豆瓣酱本身很咸，下次少放豆瓣酱、不加盐；已做好的加一点热水和半茶匙糖，再补一点豆腐。
