Baked Mac and Cheese烤芝士通心粉

Baked mac and cheese uses a béchamel (white sauce) to carry the cheese. Cook the roux until it loses its raw flour taste, thicken the milk, take the pot off the heat before adding the cheese, and finish with a crisp crumb topping. Common problems are grainy or oily sauce, mushy pasta and a dry top.焗烤芝士通心粉靠白酱把奶酪裹在面上：先把黄油面粉炒到没有生面味，牛奶煮到浓稠后离火再拌入奶酪，表面撒面包屑烤至金黄。常见问题是奶酪结块出油、面煮得太软，或烤得太干。

65 min total分钟 (15 + 50)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Ground nutmeg另购：肉豆蔻粉

Ingredients食材

  • Elbow macaroni弯管通心粉 300 g · 300 g elbow macaroni弯管通心粉 300 克
  • Butter黄油 60 g · 60 g unsalted butter (a bit over 1/4 cup), split: for the roux and for tossing the crumbs无盐黄油 60 克（1/4 杯稍多），分两份：炒酱与拌面包屑
  • All-purpose flour面粉 40 g · all-purpose flour, 1/3 cup (40 g)中筋面粉 1/3 杯（40 克）
  • Milk 2%牛奶 600 g · about 600 mL (2 1/2 cups) whole milk, warmed全脂牛奶约 600 毫升（2 1/2 杯），温的
  • Cheddar cheese切达奶酪 300 g · 300 g cheddar (about 3 cups, loosely packed), grated fresh, medium or sharp切达奶酪 300 克（约 3 杯松装），现刨丝，用中熟或浓郁口味
  • Parmesan帕玛森奶酪 60 g · 60 g parmesan (about 3/4 cup grated)帕玛森奶酪 60 克（约 3/4 杯刨丝）
  • Salt盐 6 g · about 1 tsp total, for the pasta water and the sauce, added in stages面汤及酱汁用盐，共约 1 茶匙，分次加
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 2 g · 1/2 tsp freshly ground1/2 茶匙现磨
  • Ground nutmeg肉豆蔻粉 1 g · about 1/8 tsp freshly grated nutmeg现磨肉豆蔻约 1/8 茶匙
  • Sandwich bread面包 30 g · 1 slice bread (or about 1/2 cup panko), torn into crumbs面包 1 片（或约 1/2 杯粗面包糠），撕成屑

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C and bring a large pot of water to a boil, salting it until lightly salty.烤箱预热到 190°C，大锅水烧开，加盐到尝起来略带咸味。
  2. Cook the macaroni 2 minutes less than the package time, then drain without rinsing, and wipe the pot dry to reuse.下通心粉煮到比包装时间少 2 分钟，捞出沥干，不要冲水，把锅擦干备用。
  3. Melt 40 g of the butter in the same pot over medium-low, whisk in the flour and cook 1-2 minutes until it smells nutty with no raw flour taste left.原锅中放 40 克黄油，中小火融化，撒入面粉炒 1–2 分钟，炒到闻到坚果香、没有生面味。
  4. Whisk in a splash of the warm milk to form a smooth paste, then add the rest gradually while whisking; keep it at a gentle simmer, scraping the corners, and cook about 5 minutes until the sauce coats the back of a spoon.先倒入一点温牛奶搅成顺滑糊状，再分次倒入剩余牛奶，边倒边搅，保持微沸、不断搅动锅角，煮到酱汁能挂在勺背上，约 5 分钟。
  5. Take the pot off the heat and let it sit 1 minute, then stir in the cheddar and parmesan a handful at a time along with the remaining salt, pepper and nutmeg, until melted.离火晾 1 分钟，分几把加入切达、帕玛森、剩余盐、胡椒和肉豆蔻，搅到奶酪融化。
  6. Fold in the drained pasta so every piece is coated, then pour into a greased 20x30 cm (9x13 inch) baking dish and spread level.倒入沥干的通心粉拌匀，让酱汁裹住每一根面；倒入抹油的 20×30 厘米焗盘中铺平。
  7. Melt the remaining 20 g butter, toss with the breadcrumbs, and scatter evenly over the top.剩余 20 克黄油融化，拌入面包屑，均匀撒在焗盘表面。
  8. Bake in the preheated oven 20-25 minutes until the top is golden and bubbling; tent with foil if it browns too fast.放入预热好的烤箱烤 20–25 分钟，表面金黄冒泡；如果上色太快可盖一层锡纸。
  9. Let it rest 5-10 minutes after baking so the sauce settles, then portion it out.出炉静置 5–10 分钟，酱汁稍收浓后再分份。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Grate cheese yourself: pre-shredded cheese is coated with anti-caking powder that makes the sauce grainy.奶酪现刨比预刨丝融化得顺；预刨奶酪表面裹有防结块粉，容易让酱汁发砂。
  • Add cheese off the heat; long high heat separates the fat and leaves a grainy, oily sauce.奶酪要离火再拌入，高温久煮会让油脂分离，酱变油、口感粗糙发砂。
  • Cook the pasta to a firm bite; it keeps absorbing sauce in the oven, and soft pasta turns mushy after baking.面煮到偏硬的咬口即可，入炉后还会继续吸酱变软，煮太软烤完会糊。
  • The sauce should look a little saucier than you want the final dish, since the pasta keeps absorbing it in the oven — but it should still coat the back of a spoon before baking.酱汁入炉前要比你想要的成品稀一点，面会在烤的时候继续吸酱，但仍要能挂住勺背。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The cheese clumps and oil separates out奶酪结块、酱里有油析出
    Add cheese after removing the pot from heat, once it is no longer scalding hot. If oil has split, whisk in 1-2 tbsp warm milk off the heat, or smooth it with an immersion blender — do not strain, which removes the cheese.奶酪在离火后拌入，酱温降到不烫手再加；已经出油的酱离火拌入 1–2 汤匙温牛奶，或用手持搅拌器打匀，不要过筛（会把奶酪滤掉）。
  • It comes out dry with hard edges烤完发干、边缘硬壳
    Use enough sauce to coat the pasta, and tent with foil if it browns too fast — shortening the rest time is not the fix for dry edges.焗碗别铺太薄，酱汁要裹住面；上色太快就盖锡纸，不是缩短静置时间。
  • The sauce is thin and will not set酱汁太稀、不成形
    Thicken the roux sauce until it coats the spoon before adding cheese. If it is already thin, simmer gently 2-3 minutes longer, or whisk in a little extra butter-flour paste (beurre manié) — do not stir in a raw flour slurry.白酱煮到挂勺再拌奶酪；已经偏稀就离火后再小火多煮 2–3 分钟，或用一点黄油面粉糊（beurre manié）搅入收稠，不要直接拌生面粉水。
