Cantonese Poached Shrimp with Ginger-Soy Dip白灼虾

The Cantonese way to taste shrimp at their best. Shell-on shrimp in boiling water with ginger and green onion for a minute or two, peeled at the table and dipped in ginger soy sauce. The only real rule is not to overcook them.广东人吃虾最讲究原味：带壳的虾在加了姜葱的开水里烫一两分钟，剥开蘸一碟姜葱酱油。做法简单到只剩一个要点，就是别煮老。

15 min total分钟 (10 + 5)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Frozen raw shrimp冷冻生虾 500 g · about 500 g raw shrimp in the shell (Costco frozen raw shrimp)带壳生虾约 500 克（Costco 冷冻生虾） shell-on is best; peeled works but is less juicy带壳的最好，去壳虾也能做但口感差一些
  • Ginger姜 20 g · a 20 g piece (half sliced for the water, half in fine shreds for the dip)一块约 20 克（一半切片煮虾，一半切细丝做蘸料）
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 25 g · 3 stalks (2 knotted for the water, 1 chopped for the dip)3 根（2 根打结煮虾，1 根切葱花做蘸料）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1.5 tbsp (22 mL), for the dip1.5 汤匙（22 mL），蘸料用
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp, for the dip1 茶匙，蘸料用
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL), heated and poured over the dip1 汤匙（15 mL），烧热浇蘸料

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Thaw the frozen shrimp in cold water for 15 minutes, then drain.冷冻虾放进冷水里浸 15 分钟解冻，沥干。
  2. Pull out the vein from the back of each shrimp with a toothpick at the second segment.用牙签从虾背第二节挑出虾线。
  3. For the dip, put the shredded ginger, chopped green onion, soy sauce, sugar and 1 tbsp hot water in a bowl.蘸料：碗里放姜丝、葱花、生抽、糖和 1 汤匙开水。
  4. Heat the oil until it smokes, pour it over the ginger and onion, and stir.油烧到冒烟，浇在姜丝葱花上，拌匀。
  5. Bring about 1.5 L water to a boil with the ginger slices, onion knots and wine for 1 minute.大锅烧开约 1.5 升水，放姜片、葱结和料酒煮 1 分钟。
  6. Add the shrimp and keep the heat high until they turn pink and curl, about 1.5-2 minutes.放入虾，保持大火，煮到虾身变红弯曲，约 1.5–2 分钟。
  7. Lift them out at once and serve with the dip.立刻捞出装盘，配蘸料上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Shrimp are done the moment they turn pink and curl; one extra minute makes them rubbery.虾一变红卷起就熟了，多煮 1 分钟肉就发硬。
  • Do not add too many at once or the water cools and they cook unevenly; work in two batches for a big amount.一次放的虾不要太多，水温降太多会煮得不均匀，量大就分两批。
  • The oil for the dip must be smoking so it releases the ginger and onion aroma.蘸料的热油一定要冒烟，浇下去姜葱的香味才出得来。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The shrimp are tough虾肉发硬发柴
    They cooked too long; lift them out as soon as all are pink, as carry-over heat keeps cooking them.煮久了；看到虾全部变红就捞，捞出后余温还会继续加热。
  • They taste fishy有腥味
    Do not skip the ginger and wine in the water, and thaw frozen shrimp in cold water rather than on the counter.水里姜和料酒不要省；冷冻虾要在冷水里解冻，不要放在台面上回温。
