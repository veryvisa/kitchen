Hunan Stir-fried Pork with Green Chillies农家小炒肉

To many in Hunan, the best dish ever to eat with rice. Pork belly renders its fat, the green chillies blister in that fat, and then lean and fatty pork and chillies are tossed together over fierce heat. Thin-skinned hot green chillies are essential; bell peppers will not give this flavour.湖南人心中的第一下饭菜。五花肉先煸出油，再用这股猪油把青辣椒煎出虎皮，最后肥瘦肉和辣椒一起爆炒。辣椒要选薄皮、有辣味的螺丝椒或尖椒，用甜椒做不出这股味。

18 min total分钟 (10 + 8)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Long green chilli peppers, Dark soy sauce另购：青尖椒（螺丝椒）、老抽

Ingredients食材

  • Pork belly五花肉 100 g · 100 g pork belly, thinly sliced五花肉 100 克，切薄片
  • Pork shoulder猪梅花肉 150 g · 150 g pork shoulder, thinly sliced猪梅花肉或前腿瘦肉 150 克，切薄片
  • Long green chilli peppers青尖椒（螺丝椒） 250 g · about 6 long green chillies, cut on the bias青尖椒或螺丝椒约 6 根，斜切段 seed them for less heat怕辣去籽
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 4 cloves, sliced4 瓣，切片
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp, half for the marinade1 汤匙，一半腌肉
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 3 g · 1/2 tsp (for colour)1/2 茶匙（上色）
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 3 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp约 1/3 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Toss the lean pork with half the soy and the cornstarch.瘦肉片加一半生抽和淀粉抓匀。
  2. Dry-fry the chillies in an oil-free pan over medium heat until blistered and wrinkled, salt lightly and set aside.干锅不放油，中火把辣椒煸到表皮起皱发白，撒一点盐，盛出。
  3. Fry the pork belly in 1 tsp oil over low heat until the fat renders and the slices curl.锅里放 1 茶匙油，下五花肉片小火煸到出油、微卷。
  4. Push it aside, add the rest of the oil and stir-fry the lean pork on high until it changes colour.推到一边，加剩下的油，下瘦肉片大火炒散变色。
  5. Add the garlic, then the rest of the soy and the dark soy down the side of the pan.下蒜片炒香，沿锅边淋剩下的生抽和老抽。
  6. Return the chillies and toss everything on high for a minute.辣椒回锅，大火一起翻炒 1 分钟出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Blistering the chillies first draws out their heat and aroma and keeps the dish from going watery.辣椒先干煸出虎皮，辣味和香味才出来，也不会炒出一锅水。
  • The rendered belly fat carries the flavour; do not skip it for lean meat only.五花肉煸出的猪油是香味来源，别图省事直接用瘦肉。
  • High heat throughout and under 4 minutes from meat to plate keeps the pork tender.全程大火快炒，从肉下锅到出锅不超过 4 分钟，肉才嫩。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • Far too hot辣得受不了
    Seed and devein the chillies or swap half for bell pepper; the heat is in the seeds and ribs.辣椒去籽去筋，或一半换成甜椒；辣度主要在籽和白筋。
  • The pork is tough肉老
    Coat the lean meat and cook it fast; add the chillies as soon as it changes colour.瘦肉要上浆、大火快炒；炒到刚变色就加辣椒，别炒到发干。
