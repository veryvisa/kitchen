Tomato Fish Soup番茄鱼片汤

Tomatoes cooked down until the oil turns red give a tangy, sweet broth; lightly coated cod slices go in last and stay silky. On the table in half an hour, an easy weeknight soup.番茄炒出红油再加水，汤底酸甜开胃；鳕鱼片腌一下再下锅，滑嫩不散。半小时内上桌，是工作日晚上最省心的一道汤。

30 min total分钟 (15 + 15)4 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Cod fillet鳕鱼排 400 g · about 400 g cod fillet, cut on the diagonal into 1 cm slices鳕鱼柳约 400 克，斜刀切 1 厘米厚片 thaw frozen fillets in the fridge冷冻的冷藏解冻
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 6 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 10 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Tomatoes西红柿 450 g · 3 medium tomatoes, diced3 个中等番茄，切小块
  • Tomato paste番茄膏 15 g · 1 tbsp tomato paste1 汤匙番茄膏
  • Ginger姜 8 g · 3 slices3 片
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Salt盐 5 g · about 3/4 tsp (1 g for the fish, the rest for the soup)约 3/4 茶匙（腌鱼 1 克，其余调汤）
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 0.5 g · a pinch少许
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 10 g · 1 stalk, chopped1 根，切葱花

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Toss the fish gently with 1 g salt, the wine, pepper and cornstarch and set aside.鱼片加 1 克盐、料酒、胡椒和淀粉轻轻拌匀，放一边。
  2. Heat the oil over medium and fry the ginger until fragrant.锅里放油，中火煸香姜片。
  3. Add the tomatoes and tomato paste and cook 4 minutes until soft and the oil turns red.下番茄块和番茄膏，炒 4 分钟到番茄软烂出红油。
  4. Add 1 L boiling water and the sugar, return to a boil and simmer 5 minutes.加 1 升开水和糖，煮开后小火煮 5 分钟。
  5. Season with the rest of the salt.加剩下的盐调味。
  6. Lower the heat so the soup barely bubbles and slip in the fish one slice at a time.转小火，让汤保持微微冒泡，把鱼片一片片放进去。
  7. Do not stir; cook 2-3 minutes until the fish turns white, then add the green onion and serve.不要搅动，煮 2–3 分钟到鱼片变白，撒葱花出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cook the tomatoes until soft and juicy before adding water for a rich, red broth.番茄一定要炒软出汁再加水，汤才浓、颜色才红。
  • Add the fish to a gentle simmer; a rolling boil breaks it apart.鱼片下锅时汤要微开，大滚会把鱼片冲碎。
  • The cornstarch coating makes the fish silkier and thickens the soup slightly.鱼片裹了一层淀粉，口感更滑，也能让汤稍微浓一点。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The fish fell apart鱼片碎成渣
    Slices too thin or heat too high; cut 1 cm thick, add to a gentle simmer and do not stir.切片太薄或火太大；下次切 1 厘米厚，小火下锅，下锅后不要搅。
  • The soup is too sour汤太酸
    Tomatoes vary; add half a teaspoon more sugar or simmer a few minutes longer to mellow it.番茄品种不同酸度不同；多加半茶匙糖，或多煮几分钟让酸味柔和。
