Salmon Fried Rice三文鱼炒饭

Rich, savory salmon turns fried rice into a satisfying weeknight meal that also packs well for lunch. Two problems can ruin it: leaving the salmon in the wok breaks it into dry shreds, and moist rice clumps together. Searing the salmon cubes separately and folding them back in at the very end helps keep the rice grains separate and the salmon tender in distinct pieces.三文鱼油脂丰润、鲜味足，做成炒饭很受大人小孩欢迎，带便当也很合适。这道饭最怕两件事：三文鱼一直留在锅里翻炒会碎成鱼泥、干柴发腥，米饭不够干爽则容易粘成团；把鱼丁先煎熟盛出、最后再轻手拌回热饭，让米饭粒粒分明，三文鱼保持完整嫩滑。

22 min total分钟 (12 + 10)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Salmon fillet三文鱼排 250 g · about 250 g salmon fillet (skinned, boned, and cut into 1.5 cm cubes)三文鱼柳约 250 克（做时去皮去刺切 1.5 厘米丁）
  • Jasmine rice茉莉香米 250 g · 250 g (about 1 1/3 cups) jasmine rice (cooked and chilled)茉莉香米 250 克（约 1 又 1/3 杯干米，煮熟冷藏备用）
  • Eggs鸡蛋 100 g · 2 large eggs, beaten2 个大鸡蛋，打散
  • Carrots胡萝卜 60 g · 1/2 medium carrot, finely diced半根胡萝卜，切 5 毫米小丁
  • Edamame毛豆 80 g · about 1/2 cup thawed shelled edamame, drained冷冻毛豆仁约 1/2 杯，解冻沥干
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 25 g · 2 scallions, whites and greens sliced separately2 根葱，葱白和葱绿分开切
  • Garlic大蒜 8 g · 2 cloves garlic, minced2 瓣蒜，切末
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL) soy sauce生抽 1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL) cooking wine, for the salmon料酒 1 汤匙（15 mL），腌鱼用
  • Salt盐 3 g · about 1/2 tsp salt, divided盐约 1/2 茶匙，分次加
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · about 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper黑胡椒约 1/4 茶匙，现磨
  • Canola oil菜籽油 20 g · 20 g (about 1 1/2 tbsp) canola oil, divided (adjust slightly for wok size and rice dryness)菜籽油 20 克（约 1 1/2 汤匙），分次使用（可根据锅具大小和米饭干湿微调）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp sesame oil, to finish香油 1 茶匙，出锅淋入

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Pat the salmon cubes dry with paper towels and toss with the cooking wine, black pepper, and 1 g salt.三文鱼丁用厨房纸吸干水汽，拌入料酒、黑胡椒和 1 克盐抓匀。
  2. For best results, prepare the rice the day before: spread cooked rice in a thin layer in shallow containers and refrigerate promptly, ideally within 1 hour of cooking, at 4°C or below rather than letting it cool completely at room temperature. Break up the chilled rice with clean hands or a spoon so no clumps remain.建议提前一天备好米饭：煮好的米饭摊成薄层装入浅容器，最好在煮好后 1 小时内放入 4°C 或以下冰箱冷藏，不要放在室温下彻底放凉；使用前用手抓散或用勺子压松，确保没有结块。
  3. Whisk the eggs with 1 g salt until uniform.鸡蛋打入碗中加 1 克盐充分打散。
  4. Preheat a wok or large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat, add 1 tsp (5 mL) oil, and sear the salmon cubes in a single layer, turning gently as needed, for about 3–4 minutes (time is approximate) until lightly browned. Avoid repeatedly probing or stirring; insert an instant-read thermometer into the center of one representative thick piece until it reaches 70°C internally, then transfer to a plate.先将炒锅或大不粘锅预热，倒入 1 茶匙（5 mL）油中大火烧热，铺入三文鱼丁单层煎约 3–4 分钟（时间为参考），中途轻翻一次，煎到表面微黄。不要反复翻搅或多次扎测，用速读温度计插入一块有代表性的较厚鱼丁中心确认达到 70°C 后盛出。
  5. Add 1 tsp (5 mL) oil over medium-high heat, scramble the eggs for about 45 seconds into soft curds, and transfer to a plate.锅中再加 1 茶匙（5 mL）油保持中大火，倒入蛋液快炒约 45 秒成嫩碎块，盛出备用。
  6. Preheat the wok again over medium-high heat and add the remaining oil (about 2 1/2 tsp, adjusting slightly for wok size and rice dryness). Stir-fry the carrot and scallion whites for 2–3 minutes until the carrot is tender-crisp and the scallions are fragrant, then add the garlic and edamame and stir-fry 30–45 seconds until aromatic. Transfer the vegetables to the plate with the eggs if frying the rice in batches.重新预热炒锅后倒入剩余的油（约 2 1/2 茶匙，可根据锅具大小和米饭干湿微调），放入胡萝卜丁和葱白中大火翻炒 2–3 分钟，直到胡萝卜断生脆嫩、葱香散出，再下蒜末和毛豆仁翻炒 30–45 秒出香；若分批炒饭，可先将蔬菜盛出与鸡蛋放在一起。
  7. Turn to high heat and add the chilled rice and remaining 1 g salt—if using a standard home wok or skillet, fry the rice in two batches so it sears rather than steams—tossing for about 2–3 minutes per batch until the grains separate and dance in the wok. Combine both batches and the vegetables back in the wok.转大火，倒入冷饭和剩下的 1 克盐——如果使用普通家用炒锅或平底锅，建议将米饭分两批下锅翻炒以免拥挤闷出水汽——每批炒约 2–3 分钟至米粒散开跳动，随后将两批米饭与蔬菜一起倒回锅中汇合。
  8. Pour the soy sauce around the hot edge of the wok and toss immediately for 30 seconds to distribute it evenly.沿滚烫的锅边淋入生抽，立刻快速翻炒约 30 秒使其均匀上色。
  9. Return the eggs and cooked salmon to the wok along with the scallion greens, add the sesame oil near the end to preserve its aroma, and gently fold together until steaming hot throughout (about 30 seconds) without breaking up the salmon, then serve.倒回鸡蛋和煎熟的三文鱼丁，撒入葱绿，临近出锅时再淋入香油以保留香气，轻拌约 30 秒至整锅炒饭热透冒气即可出锅，注意不要把鱼丁压碎。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Spread cooked rice in a thin layer in shallow containers and refrigerate promptly, ideally within 1 hour of cooking, at 4°C or below—do not leave it at room temperature to cool completely. Preparing it the day before gives the rice drier surfaces for separate grains.煮好的米饭要摊成薄层装入浅容器，最好在煮好后 1 小时内放入 4°C 或以下冰箱冷藏，不要在室温下慢慢放凉。提前一天备好隔夜饭，米粒表面更干爽，炒出来粒粒分明。
  • Cook the salmon fully before setting it aside, avoiding repeated stirring or probing. Check the center of one representative thick piece with an instant-read thermometer until it reaches 70°C so the fish stays safe and intact.三文鱼先单独煎熟再盛出，过程避免频繁翻动或反复扎测；只需用速读温度计测一块有代表性的较厚鱼丁中心达到 70°C 即可，既能确保安全熟透，又能保持鱼肉完整嫩滑。
  • In a standard home wok or skillet, frying the chilled rice in two batches prevents overcrowding and steaming; combine the batches before folding in the eggs and salmon.用普通家用炒锅或平底锅炒饭时，建议分两批下锅，避免一次堆入太多导致米饭变成闷蒸；等两批饭都炒干爽后再合锅拌入鸡蛋和三文鱼。
  • Pouring soy sauce around the hot edge of the wok and tossing immediately distributes it evenly and toasts its aroma without soggy spots; add sesame oil near the end to preserve its fragrance.生抽沿着热锅边淋入并立刻翻匀，高温能激出酱香且分布均匀、不会局部发湿；香油留到最后快出锅时再淋，才能保留馥郁香气。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The rice turned mushy and clumped together米饭黏糊成团、炒不散
    Next time, prepare the rice the day before by spreading it in a thin layer in shallow containers and refrigerating within 1 hour at 4°C or below; rescue a wet batch by frying in smaller portions over high heat for 2 extra minutes.下次务必提前一天煮好米饭，摊成薄层装入浅容器并在 1 小时内放入 4°C 或以下冰箱冷藏；若当餐米饭仍偏湿，可分小批用大火多翻炒 2 分钟蒸发水汽。
  • The salmon crumbled into tiny dry bits三文鱼碎成细渣且口感发干
    Sear the cubes in a single layer until the center reaches 70°C, remove them without repeated stirring or probing, and fold gently at the end; if it already crumbled, treat it as flaked salmon rice next time without pressing down.鱼丁先单层煎到中心 70°C 就先盛出，避免反复翻动或扎测，最后热透拌匀即可；已经碎掉就当作松散鱼松拌饭吃，下次别用锅铲猛压。
  • The fried rice smells fishy成品有鱼腥味
    Blot the fish dry before marinating with wine and pepper, and cook the garlic and scallion whites until aromatic; a squeeze of lemon fixes an already cooked bowl.鱼块一定要用厨房纸吸干再加料酒和黑胡椒，葱白蒜末也要爆香；吃时可挤一点柠檬汁解腥。
