Hong Kong Egg Tarts港式蛋挞

Hong Kong bakery-style cookie-crust egg tarts pair a crumbly shortbread shell with a custard as smooth as steamed milk. At home, success comes down to three details: do not over-whip the butter, strain and rest the custard to clear bubbles, and pull the tray while the centers wobble only faintly.茶餐厅里的港式曲奇皮蛋挞，外皮酥松掉渣，蛋液嫩滑像炖奶。家里烤制不难，关键在于黄油别打发过头、蛋液要过筛静置去气泡，以及烤到蛋心只有中心轻微晃动就及时出炉。

Ingredients食材

  • All-purpose flour面粉 220 g · about 1 3/4 cups all-purpose or cake flour约 1¾ 杯中筋或低筋面粉
  • Butter黄油 113 g · 1 stick (1/2 cup) unsalted butter, softened1 条无盐黄油（1/2 杯，室温软化）
  • Sugar白糖 115 g · 115 g sugar total (40 g for crust, 75 g for custard)115 克糖（40 克用于挞皮，75 克用于蛋液） powdered or caster sugar blends easily into the crust挞皮用糖粉或细砂糖更易拌匀
  • Eggs鸡蛋 200 g · 4 large eggs (2 tbsp beaten egg for crust, about 30 g; remaining eggs for custard, about 170 g)4 个大鸡蛋（约 2 汤匙蛋液用于挞皮，约 30 克；剩余约 170 克做蛋液）
  • Milk 2%牛奶 120 g · 1/2 cup whole milk1/2 杯全脂牛奶
  • Whipping cream淡奶油 75 g · 1/3 cup (80 mL, about 75 g) heavy whipping cream1/3 杯淡奶油（80 毫升，约 75 克）
  • Salt盐 1 g · 1 pinch salt, for the pastry1 小撮盐，用于挞皮提味

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Mash the softened butter with 40 g sugar and the salt until smooth, without whipping in air.软化黄油加 40 克糖和盐，用刮刀按压拌匀到顺滑即可，不要打发。
  2. Mix in 2 tbsp (about 30 g) beaten egg in two additions until fully absorbed.分两次加入约 30 克打散蛋液（约 2 汤匙），每次拌到完全吸收再加下一次。
  3. Sift in the flour and fold gently just until no dry flour remains.筛入面粉，用刮刀切拌压成均匀面团，看不到干粉就停手。
  4. Wrap the dough, flatten into a disc, and chill in the fridge for 30 minutes.把面团包好压扁成饼状，放进冰箱冷藏 30 分钟定形。
  5. Dissolve the remaining 75 g sugar in 1/2 cup (120 mL) hot water and let cool to lukewarm.剩下的 75 克糖加半杯（120 毫升）热水搅拌到完全融化，放凉到微温。
  6. Whisk the remaining eggs gently, about 170 g, then gently stir the cooled syrup, milk, and heavy cream along the side of the bowl until fully combined.剩余约 170 克鸡蛋轻轻打散，沿碗边轻轻搅拌至完全混合，再倒入凉糖水、牛奶和淡奶油。
  7. Strain the custard twice through a fine mesh sieve, rest 10 minutes, and skim off any surface bubbles.把蛋液用细网筛过滤两次，静置 10 分钟后撇去表面小泡。
  8. Divide the chilled dough into 12 equal balls, about 33 g each.冷藏好的面团分成 12 等份，每份约 33 克。
  9. Lightly grease the 12 tart tins, including the sides, with a thin layer of butter; do not flour them heavily.在 12 个蛋挞模内轻轻抹上一层薄黄油，包括四周内壁；不要厚厚地撒面粉。
  10. Press each ball evenly into a tart tin, bringing the rim 2 mm above the metal edge to allow for shrinkage.把面团按进蛋挞模里贴紧底壁，边缘略高出模具约 2 毫米。
  11. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and fill each shell about 85% full with custard.烤箱预热到 200°C（400°F），把过滤好的蛋液倒入挞壳至八九分满。
  12. Bake on the middle-lower rack for about 10–13 minutes total, watching closely until the pastry rims begin to color. If the tops dome early, immediately reduce the oven to 350°F (180°C) or briefly crack the oven door.送入烤箱中下层，烤约 10–13 分钟，密切观察挞皮边缘微微上色；如果蛋液上部过早鼓起，立即降温至 180°C（350°F），或短暂打开烤箱门让热量散出。
  13. Bake until the crust is golden and the custard is set at the edges, with only a faint wobble in the very center. It should not look liquid or distinctly jelly-like; remove it promptly once reached.烤至挞皮金黄、蛋液边缘已经凝固、模具轻晃时只有正中心略微晃动；蛋液不能呈明显液态或果冻状，达到后立即出炉。
  14. Cool the tarts in their tins for 5 minutes before unmolding and serving warm.在模具里放凉 5 分钟再脱模，趁温热享用。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cream the butter just until smooth rather than fluffy; trapped air causes the pastry to puff and shrink down the tin.黄油只需按压拌匀、不要打发成蓬松状，否则烤时挞皮会膨胀回缩并把蛋液挤出。
  • Dissolving the sugar in hot water first avoids vigorous whisking in the eggs, keeping the custard mirror-smooth.糖先用热水化开再放凉，蛋液就不必用力搅打，能大大减少气泡，烤面才会光滑如镜。
  • Double-straining and skimming after resting removes chalazae and tiny air pockets that cause pitting.蛋液过筛两遍并静置撇泡，烤出来才细腻无蜂窝。
  • Pull the tarts when the edges are set and the center still has a faint wobble; carryover heat finishes the set without curdling the eggs.边缘凝固、中心只有轻微晃动时就出炉，靠余温完成凝固，烤到完全不动就过老了。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The custard surface is pitted or full of bubbles蛋挞表面起蜂窝、凹凸不平
    Stir gently along the side of the bowl instead of whisking air in, strain twice, and lower the heat or crack the oven door slightly if the custard begins to dome.轻轻沿碗边搅拌，不要打出泡沫，入模前过筛两遍；若烤到后半段蛋液鼓起，把炉温调低或将烤箱门开一条小缝散热。
  • The crust shrank down the tin or stayed soggy on the bottom挞皮回缩严重、底部发软发湿
    Stop mixing as soon as no dry flour shows, lightly grease the tins, press the edges 2 mm above the rim, and bake on the middle-lower rack.面团拌到无干粉即可别揉出筋，模具内壁和底部薄薄抹油，压模时边缘略高出模具 2 毫米，并放烤箱中下层烤。
  • The custard collapsed and wept liquid蛋心塌陷、切开有水渗出
    This means the custard overcooked and curdled; next time pull the tray as soon as the edges set and the very center still has a faint wobble.这是烤过头导致蛋液析水；下次看到边缘凝固、正中心仅有轻微晃动就立刻出炉。
