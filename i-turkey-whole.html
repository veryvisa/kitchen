Store and portion储存与分装

Keep at 0-4°C and use raw within 1-2 days; freeze at -18°C for about 12 months. Thaw in the fridge; a 4.5 kg bird takes about 2-3 days.冷藏 0–4°C 1–2 天内用完；冷冻 −18°C 可放约 12 个月。解冻放冷藏，4.5 公斤约需 2–3 天。

After roasting, carve and refrigerate the breast and leg meat separately, and keep the carcass for stock.整只烤完拆骨，胸肉和腿肉分开冷藏，骨架留着熬高汤。
