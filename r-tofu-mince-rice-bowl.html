Pork Mince Tofu Rice Bowl肉末豆腐盖饭

Pork mince and a savory sauce turn tofu into a quick, satisfying rice bowl. The main challenge is keeping the tofu intact, so handle it gently rather than stirring it repeatedly.猪肉末炒出香气，再用酱汁煨豆腐，浇在米饭上就是省事的一餐。豆腐容易碎，关键是切块后轻推，不要在锅里反复翻炒。

37 min total分钟 (12 + 25)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Firm tofu, Dark soy sauce另购：老豆腐、老抽

Ingredients食材

  • Jasmine rice茉莉香米 150 g · 150 g dry rice, about 3/4 cup生米 150 克，约 3/4 杯
  • Firm tofu老豆腐 400 g · 1 block firm tofu, cut into 2 cm cubes1 盒硬豆腐，切 2 厘米方块
  • Ground pork猪肉馅 150 g · 150 g ground pork猪肉末 150 克
  • White mushrooms蘑菇 100 g · 100 g mushrooms, finely diced100 克，切小丁
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 2 cloves, minced2 瓣，切末
  • Ginger姜 8 g · minced, about 2 tsp切末，约 2 茶匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp (7.5 mL)1/2 汤匙（7.5 mL）
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 6 g · 2 tsp, mixed with a little water2 茶匙，另加少许水调开
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 1 stalk, sliced1 根，切葱花
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Salt盐 1 g · a pinch, to taste少许，按口味调整

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Rinse the rice, add water according to the rice cooker's markings, and cook on the white-rice setting (about 25-40 minutes depending on the cooker).淘洗大米，按电饭锅刻度加水，用白米程序煮熟（不同机型约需 25–40 分钟）。
  2. Stir the soy sauce, dark soy sauce and oyster sauce together in a small bowl.把生抽、老抽和蚝油放入小碗拌匀。
  3. Mix the cornstarch with 2 tbsp water until smooth.淀粉加 2 汤匙水调成均匀的水淀粉。
  4. Heat the oil in a wok over medium heat and fry the pork, breaking it up, for about 4 minutes until no pink remains anywhere, it is lightly browned, and the center reaches 71°C (160°F).炒锅中火烧热油，放猪肉末炒散约 4 分钟，直到完全没有粉红色、微微焦香，中心温度达到 71°C。
  5. Add the mushrooms, garlic and ginger and cook over medium heat for 2 minutes until the mushrooms soften.加入蘑菇丁、蒜末和姜末，中火炒 2 分钟至蘑菇变软。
  6. Add the mixed sauces and 120 g water, then bring to a boil.倒入调好的酱汁和 120 克水，煮沸。
  7. Add the tofu and simmer over low heat for 5-7 minutes until heated through, gently shaking the wok or nudging the pieces with the back of a spatula to coat them in sauce.放入豆腐，转小火煮 5–7 分钟至中心热透，轻轻晃锅或用锅铲背推动让豆腐裹上酱汁。
  8. Stir the slurry again, then add it gradually, gently nudging the ingredients for about 1 minute until the sauce looks glossy, lightly thickened, and clings to the tofu.水淀粉重新搅匀，分次少量淋入，边轻推边煮约 1 分钟，直到汤汁发亮并略微浓稠、能挂住豆腐。
  9. Taste and add salt if needed, then scatter over the green onion.尝味后按需加盐，撒上葱花。
  10. Divide the rice between bowls and spoon the pork and tofu over it.将米饭分入碗中，盛上肉末豆腐。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Firm tofu is easier to keep intact; softer tofu is delicate but breaks more readily in the pan.用硬豆腐更容易保持完整；嫩豆腐风味细滑，但在锅里更容易碎。
  • Light browning before adding the sauce deepens the pork flavor and prevents a raw-meat taste.肉末先炒到表面微焦再加酱汁，能带出肉香，也不容易留下生肉味。
  • Stir the slurry again just before adding it, since the starch settles as it stands.水淀粉下锅前再搅匀，静置时淀粉会沉底。
  • Soy sauce and oyster sauce are already salty, so taste before adding extra salt.酱油和蚝油都有咸味，先尝再决定是否加盐。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The tofu broke into small pieces豆腐碎成小块
    Nudge with the back of a spatula or shake the pan instead of stirring; use firm tofu and larger pieces next time.用锅铲背轻推或晃锅，不要来回翻炒；下次选硬豆腐并切大块。
  • The pork mince formed large clumps肉末结成大团
    Spread the pork out briefly once the oil is hot, then break it apart gradually with a spatula; cook in batches if the pan is small so it is not overcrowded.油热后肉末先稍微摊开，再用锅铲逐步拨散；锅较小时分批炒，别一次下太多。
  • The sauce is thin and will not cling to the tofu酱汁稀而不挂豆腐
    Stir the slurry again and add a little at a time; cook until the sauce coats the back of a spoon.先把水淀粉重新搅匀再少量淋入，煮到酱汁裹住勺背即可。
