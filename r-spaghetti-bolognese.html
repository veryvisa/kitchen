Spaghetti with Bolognese Ragu意式番茄肉酱面（博洛尼亚肉酱）

A real ragu is about slowness. A soffritto of onion, carrot and celery goes in first, the meat is browned properly, then wine and milk are each reduced, and finally tomato simmers gently for two hours. The sauce is meatier than tomatoey and clings to the pasta instead of pooling beneath it.真正的肉酱靠慢：洋葱、胡萝卜、芹菜先炒软，肉煎到出香，再依次收干红酒和牛奶，最后加番茄小火煮两个小时。酱汁里肉比番茄多，裹在面上而不是浮在上面。

Method做法

  1. Heat the oil and butter in a heavy pot over medium heat and fry the chopped bacon 4 minutes until it renders and turns lightly golden.厚底锅中火放橄榄油和黄油，下培根碎煎 4 分钟，到出油并微黄。
  2. Add the onion, carrot and celery with 1 g salt and cook over medium-low heat 10 minutes until very soft but not browned.加洋葱、胡萝卜和芹菜，加 1 克盐，中小火炒 10 分钟，炒到很软、不上色。
  3. Stir in the garlic for 30 seconds and push the vegetables to the side.加蒜炒 30 秒，把蔬菜推到锅边。
  4. Turn the heat to high, add both ground meats and break them up with a spatula.转大火，下牛肉馅和猪肉馅，用铲子压散。
  5. Cook 10-12 minutes until the liquid evaporates and the meat sizzles and turns brown.煎 10–12 分钟，直到水分蒸干、肉开始发出滋滋声并变成褐色。
  6. Pour in the wine and boil 4-5 minutes until nearly dry.倒入红酒，大火煮 4–5 分钟，收到几乎干锅。
  7. Add the milk and cook 5 minutes until almost no liquid remains.倒入牛奶，煮 5 分钟，收到几乎看不到液体。
  8. Stir in the tomato paste for 1 minute, then add the crushed tomatoes, bay leaf, 2 g salt and the pepper.加番茄酱炒 1 分钟，再倒入捏碎的番茄、月桂叶、2 克盐和黑胡椒。
  9. Bring to a bubble, turn the heat as low as it goes, partially cover and simmer 2 hours, stirring every 20 minutes.烧开后转最小火，半盖锅盖，咕嘟 2 小时，每 20 分钟搅一次。
  10. If it gets dry add half a cup of hot water; at the end it should be thick with red oil pooling on top.太干就加半杯热水，酱汁最后应该是浓稠、表面泛出红油。
  11. Taste, adjust with the last 3 g of salt and remove the bay leaf.尝味，加 3 克盐调整，拿掉月桂叶。
  12. Boil a large pot of salted water, cook the pasta 1 minute less than the pack says and scoop out a cup of the water.大锅烧水加盐，面煮到比包装时间少 1 分钟，舀出 1 杯煮面水。
  13. Add the pasta to the sauce with a splash of pasta water and toss over medium heat 1 minute until the sauce coats it.面放进酱汁里，加一小勺面水，中火翻拌 1 分钟，让酱汁裹住面。
  14. Plate and top with freshly grated Parmesan.装盘，撒现磨帕玛森奶酪。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Dice the soffritto very fine and cook it soft; it melts into the sauce and supplies sweetness and depth.洋葱、胡萝卜、芹菜要切得很细、炒得很软：它们会化进酱里，提供甜味和层次。
  • Brown the meat hard until it sizzles rather than just stewing it; browning gives depth and boiled meat stays grey.肉要在大火下煎到出声、变褐，不要一直炖：只有煎出的褐色才有深度，煮出来的肉只会发灰。
  • Reduce the wine, then the milk; the milk tenderises the meat and rounds the sauce, and the wine must lose its sharpness first.先收干红酒再收牛奶：牛奶让肉更嫩、酱更圆润，红酒的酸味先挥发掉才不会冲。
  • Finish the pasta in the sauce; the starch in the pasta water makes the sauce cling instead of sliding to the bottom of the plate.酱汁和面一起在锅里翻拌：面水里的淀粉让酱挂在面上，而不是沉在盘底。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The sauce is too acidic酱汁偏酸
    Simmer 20 minutes longer and add 1/2 tsp sugar; next time fry the tomato paste and fully reduce the wine.再小火煮 20 分钟，加半茶匙糖；下次番茄酱要炒 1 分钟，红酒要收干。
  • The ragu is too greasy肉酱太油
    Rest it off the heat 5 minutes and skim the oil off the top; next time use ground beef with about 20% fat, not more.关火静置 5 分钟，用勺子撇去表面的油；下次用 20% 脂肪的肉馅，不要太肥。
  • The sauce is watery and pools under the pasta酱汁太稀、面会泡在汤里
    Simmer uncovered another 15 minutes to thicken it and always toss the pasta in the sauce with a splash of pasta water.敞盖多煮 15 分钟收浓；面煮好后一定要带一点面水和酱一起翻拌。
