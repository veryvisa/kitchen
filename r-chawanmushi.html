Chawanmushi茶碗蒸

Chawanmushi is a delicate savory custard with a gentle dashi flavor and tender mushrooms. Strain the egg mixture and steam it gently; high heat can make the custard porous and overcook the edges before the center sets.茶碗蒸细嫩清爽，鸡蛋、昆布鲣鱼风味的高汤和香菇搭配很合适。成败在于蛋液过滤和小火慢蒸，火太猛容易起孔，中心也不易熟透。

32 min total分钟 (10 + 22)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dashi powder, Mirin另购：日式鲣鱼高汤粉、味啉

Ingredients食材

  • Eggs鸡蛋 150 g · 3 large eggs3 个大鸡蛋
  • Water水 900 g · 900 g; use 330 g for the custard and the rest in the steamer900 克；330 克调蛋液，其余用于蒸锅
  • Dashi powder日式鲣鱼高汤粉 3 g · about 1 tsp约 1 茶匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp1/2 汤匙
  • Mirin味啉 15 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Shiitake香菇 50 g · 2 caps, stems removed and thinly sliced2 朵，去蒂切薄片
  • Edamame毛豆 40 g · about 1/4 cup shelled edamame去壳毛豆仁约 1/4 杯
  • Salt盐 1 g · a pinch少许

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Warm 330 g water, stir in the dashi powder until dissolved, then let it cool.将 330 克水加热至温热，搅入柴鱼高汤粉至溶解，再放凉。
  2. Divide the shiitake and edamame between two heatproof cups.香菇片放入两个耐热小碗，各放一半毛豆仁。
  3. Gently beat the eggs with the soy sauce, mirin, salt, and cooled dashi.鸡蛋轻轻打散，加入生抽、味醂、盐和放凉的高汤水。
  4. Strain the egg mixture through a fine sieve and divide it between the cups, filling each about three-quarters full.将蛋液过细筛，分别倒入小碗至八分满。
  5. Cover the cups loosely with foil to keep steam droplets off the custard.用锡纸松松盖住小碗，避免蒸汽水滴落在蛋面。
  6. Set a rack in a pot and add the remaining water below the rack.锅中放蒸架和剩余的水，水面保持在蒸架下方。
  7. Cover the pot and bring the water to a boil over high heat until steady steam forms.盖锅用大火烧至水沸腾，锅内出现稳定蒸汽。
  8. Set in the cups, reduce to low, and steam for 15 minutes with only a gentle simmer.放入小碗，转小火蒸 15 分钟，水只需轻轻冒泡。
  9. Lift one corner of the foil and check the center with a thin instant-read thermometer, avoiding the bottom of the cup; remove once it reaches 74°C. The 15 minutes is only a guide—cup depth and heat affect the actual time.揭开一角锡纸，用细探针温度计测量中心（避免碰到碗底），达到 74°C 后取出；15 分钟只是参考，不同碗深和火力会影响实际时间。
  10. Rest the custards for 2 minutes and serve when the surface quivers slightly but the center is set; for pregnant women, young children, older adults or anyone with a weaker immune system, steam until the center is fully set with no runny egg left.让茶碗蒸静置 2 分钟，表面微微颤动、中心凝住时上桌；孕妇、幼儿、老人或免疫力较弱者食用时，建议蒸到中心完全凝固、不留流动蛋液。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cool the dashi before adding it so the eggs do not begin cooking in spots.高汤水先放凉再加蛋，能减少蛋液局部变熟的机会。
  • Beat gently and strain to remove foam and chalazae for a smoother surface.轻轻打蛋并过滤，泡沫和蛋筋少，蒸出来的表面更平滑。
  • Keep the steamer at a gentle simmer so the cups do not jostle in forceful steam.蒸锅保持小火轻沸，不要让杯子在猛烈蒸汽里晃动。
  • A foil cover shields the custard from lid condensation and helps prevent pockmarks.杯口盖锡纸可挡住锅盖凝结的水，减少表面坑洞。
  • 74°C is Health Canada's safe internal temperature for egg dishes. It runs a touch firmer than the silkiest versions, and a little surface pitting is the trade-off for that safety margin.74°C 是加拿大卫生部对鸡蛋类菜肴的安全内部温度，比追求嫩滑口感的做法略高，表面可能出现细孔，这是安全温度的代价。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The custard is full of holes表面全是蜂窝孔
    Lower the heat and beat gently next time; if this batch is cooked through, cover and rest it, though the texture will be firm.下次把火调低并轻轻打蛋；这次若已熟透，可盖好静置后食用，口感会偏实。
  • The center is still liquid中心仍是稀蛋液
    Replace the foil and steam on low for 2 to 3 minutes, then recheck for 74°C at the center.盖回锡纸，小火继续蒸 2 至 3 分钟，再测中心温度至 74°C。
  • The custard tastes eggy or too salty蛋液有腥味或味道太咸
    Dashi powder and soy sauce both add salt; use less of one next time and dissolve the dashi fully.高汤粉和生抽都有咸味，下次先减半茶匙高汤粉或少放生抽，并确保高汤充分溶解。
