Three-Treasure Dumplings三鲜水饺

The classic northern Chinese three-treasure dumplings combine pork, shrimp, and garlic chives in a juicy filling. The secret is beating aromatic water into the pork first for tenderness, then coating the chives with oil right before mixing so the chives do not release water into the filling.胶东和北方家庭最经典的「猪肉、虾仁、韭菜」肉三鲜水饺。做好不难，关键在肉馅要先打进葱姜水才嫩滑多汁，韭菜最后用油锁水再拌，下锅才不会一出水就破皮。

80 min total分钟 (40 + 10)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Garlic chives (Chinese chives), Shaoxing wine另购：韭菜、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • All-purpose flour面粉 300 g · 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour (about 300 g), for wrappers2½ 杯中筋面粉（约 300 克），做饺子皮用 or substitute 1 pack (about 40) store-bought dumpling wrappers赶时间可用 1 包（约 40 张）现成饺子皮代替面粉
  • Ground pork猪肉馅 300 g · 300 g ground pork (about 70/30 lean-to-fat)300 克三分肥七分瘦猪肉馅
  • Frozen raw shrimp冷冻生虾 200 g · 200 g raw peeled, deveined shrimp200 克去壳生虾仁，抽去虾线
  • Garlic chives (Chinese chives)韭菜 200 g · 1 bunch garlic chives (about 200 g), washed and dried thoroughly1 把韭菜（约 200 克），洗净彻底晾干
  • Ginger姜 15 g · 15 g ginger, half smashed for water and half minced15 克姜，一半拍碎泡水，一半切细末
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 2 scallions, cut for aromatic water2 根葱，切段泡水
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1 1/2 tbsp soy sauce1½ 汤匙生抽
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 18 g · 1 tbsp oyster sauce1 汤匙蚝油
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp cooking wine1 汤匙料酒
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 14 g · 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil1 汤匙香油
  • Canola oil菜籽油 21 g · 1 1/2 tbsp neutral oil, to coat the chives1½ 汤匙食用油，拌韭菜锁水用
  • Salt盐 5 g · about 3/4 tsp total (1 g for dough, 4 g for filling)约 ¾ 茶匙（面团 1 克，馅料 4 克）
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 0.5 g · 1/4 tsp ground black or white pepper¼ 茶匙黑胡椒或白胡椒粉

Method做法

  1. Mix the flour and 1 g salt with 160–165 mL room-temperature water (adjusting slightly by flour brand so the dough is soft, non-sticky, and rolls out without springing back), knead into a smooth dough, and rest covered for 30 minutes.面粉加 1 克盐，分次倒入约 160–165 毫升室温水揉成光滑面团，以面团柔软不粘手、能擀开不回缩为准（不同品牌面粉吸水性不同可微调水量），盖湿布醒 30 分钟。
  2. Steep the scallions and about 7–8 g smashed ginger in 100–120 mL hot water for 15 minutes, let cool, and strain out the solids.葱段和拍碎的约 7–8 克姜用 100–120 毫升热水泡 15 分钟，放凉后捞出葱姜留下葱姜水。
  3. Lightly flatten the shrimp with the flat of a knife and chop into 1 cm pieces.虾仁用刀背拍松后切成约 1 厘米小段，保留颗粒感。
  4. Beat the pork with minced ginger, soy sauce, oyster sauce, wine, pepper, and 4 g salt in one direction for 2–3 minutes until sticky and resistant.猪肉馅加姜末、生抽、蚝油、料酒、胡椒粉和 4 克盐，顺同一方向搅打 2–3 分钟，至肉馅发黏、有阻力。
  5. Beat the cooled aromatic water into the pork in three additions, stirring in the same direction until glossy and absorbed.把放凉的葱姜水分三次倒入肉馅，每次顺同一方向搅打到水分完全吃进去、肉馅发亮上劲。
  6. Fold in the shrimp and sesame oil, then chill the mixture for 15 minutes to firm up.拌入虾仁和香油，送进冰箱冷藏 15 分钟让肉馅更抱团。
  7. Finely chop the completely dry chives into 5 mm bits and toss them with 1 1/2 tbsp neutral oil to coat.彻底晾干的韭菜切约 5 毫米细段，先拌入 1½ 汤匙食用油裹匀表面。
  8. Right before folding, gently combine the oiled chives into the pork mixture without squeezing.临包之前再把韭菜倒进肉馅里轻轻拌匀，不要用力揉捏。
  9. Roll the rested dough into a rope, cut into 40 pieces, and roll each into a round wrapper thicker in the center.醒好的面团搓成粗长条，切成约 40 个剂子，擀成中间厚、边缘薄的圆皮。
  10. Place about 1 tablespoon (15–18 g) of filling in each wrapper, fold in half, and pinch the edges tightly to seal.每张皮放约 1 汤匙（15–18 克）馅料，对折捏紧边缘包成月牙饺。
  11. Drop the dumplings into a large pot of boiling water and gently swirl along the bottom with the back of a ladle to prevent sticking.大锅水沸后下入饺子，立刻用勺背贴锅底轻推两圈防粘。
  12. When boiling hard, add 1/2 cup cold water, bringing it back to a boil between splashes for 3 additions in total, until the dumplings puff up, float, look slightly translucent, and the filling is no longer pink and reaches 71°C for the pork (74°C for the shrimp).大火煮开后点入半杯凉水，每次再次煮沸后再点水，共点凉水 3 次，至饺子鼓肚浮起、表皮微透，且内馅熟透不再呈粉红色（猪肉中心温度达 71°C，虾仁达 74°C）即可捞出。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Beating aromatic water into the pork in stages keeps the cooked filling juicy instead of dense and tough.肉馅分次打入葱姜水，煮熟后咬开才有鲜美汤汁，不会干硬成一坨。
  • Dry the chives completely, seal the cut edges in oil, and fold them in only right before wrapping so salt never draws out water.韭菜务必洗后彻底晾干，先用食用油裹住切口、包前才拌入肉馅，才能锁住水分不杀出汤。
  • Keeping the shrimp in small chunks rather than mincing it into a paste gives a pleasant bouncy bite.虾仁切成小段而不是剁成泥，吃起来更有弹脆口感。
  • Adding splashes of cold water in stages slows a violent boil so the wrappers stay intact while the pork and shrimp cook through.分次点凉水（共 3 次）能缓和滚沸水温，避免外皮煮破而内馅还没熟透。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The filling turned watery halfway through and the edges will not seal包到一半馅里出了很多水，收口捏不住
    Damp chives or early contact with salt releases liquid; next time dry them well and oil them before mixing, or drain a wet batch through a fine strainer.韭菜带水或过早接触盐都会杀出菜水；下次韭菜擦干先裹油再后拌，已出水的可把馅料倒进细筛沥掉多余汤汁。
  • Dumplings burst open or stuck to the bottom of the pot煮的时候破皮漏馅，或者粘在锅底
    Nudge along the pot bottom right after dropping them in, and splash in cold water or ease the heat slightly when at a rolling boil.下锅后马上用勺背贴锅底轻推，水大开时及时点水或转中大火，不要一直猛滚冲破饺皮。
  • The pork filling feels dry and crumbly inside饺子煮完肉馅干硬不抱团
    Use 70/30 ground pork rather than extra-lean, and beat the aromatic water in gradually in one direction until sticky before adding the chives.选三分肥七分瘦的猪肉馅，顺同一方向分次搅打进足量葱姜水，打到肉馅发黏再拌菜。
