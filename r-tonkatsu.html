Tonkatsu日式炸猪排

Tonkatsu pairs a crisp coating and juicy pork with shredded cabbage, steamed rice, miso soup and a tangy-sweet sauce. Cutting the sinew, evenly pounding the chops, letting the breading set and keeping steady oil temperature help the pork cook through before the crust gets too dark.日式炸猪排外壳酥脆、里层多汁，配卷心菜丝、米饭和味增汤以及酸甜酱汁就很完整。关键是给猪排断筋拍匀、裹粉后静置定型并控制油温，让猪肉熟透时外壳仍然金黄。

50 min total分钟 (30 + 20)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Mirin另购：味啉

Ingredients食材

  • Pork loin猪里脊 600 g · 4 boneless pork loin chops with the fat cap left on, about 2 cm thick as bought, about 150 g each (pork tenderloin medallions are an alternative)4 块带脂肪边的去骨猪里脊排，买时厚约 2 厘米，每块约 150 克（也可用猪柳厚片代替）
  • All-purpose flour面粉 100 g · about 3/4 cup (only part adheres), for dredging约 3/4 杯（仅部分实际裹附），用于裹粉
  • Eggs鸡蛋 100 g · 2 large eggs, beaten (about 100 g in shell, about 90 g shelled)2 个大鸡蛋（带壳约 100 克，去壳约 90 克），打散
  • Panko breadcrumbs日式面包糠 115 g · about 2 cups (110–120 g) panko breadcrumbs日式面包糠约 2 杯（110–120 克）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 60 g · have about 1 L (about 900 g) canola, vegetable or peanut oil on hand for frying, but most stays in the pot—the breading absorbs only about 60 g炸制需备约 1 升（约 900 克）菜籽油、植物油或花生油，但大部分留在锅里，面包糠实际吸收约 60 克
  • Green cabbage包菜 300 g · about 1/4 small cabbage, finely shredded约 1/4 个小卷心菜，切细丝
  • Lemons柠檬 100 g · 1 lemon, cut into wedges1 个，切成角
  • Tomato ketchup番茄酱 68 g · 4 tbsp4 汤匙
  • Worcestershire sauce伍斯特酱 34 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 6 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Mirin味啉 5 g · 1 tsp (or substitute rice vinegar)1 茶匙（或用米醋代替）
  • Brown sugar红糖 9 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 5 g · about 1.5 tsp toasted sesame seeds (optional, for the sauce)约 1.5 茶匙熟白芝麻（可选，拌入酱汁）
  • Dijon mustard第戎芥末酱 15 g · about 1 tbsp (optional, on the side)约 1 汤匙（可选，蘸食）
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 3/4 tsp fine salt (use 1 1/4 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt)约 3/4 茶匙细盐（如用 Diamond Crystal 粗盐则用 1 1/4 茶匙）
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · about 1/2 tsp约 1/2 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Use commercial tonkatsu sauce as a first choice, or stir together the ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce, mirin (or rice vinegar), brown sugar and toasted sesame seeds if using, then set aside.优先可直接准备市售日式猪排酱，或把番茄酱、伍斯特酱、酱油、味淋（或米醋）、红糖和熟白芝麻（如用）搅匀备用。
  2. Finely shred the cabbage, soak it in ice water for 5 minutes and drain well; cut the lemon into wedges and set aside with optional Dijon mustard.卷心菜切细丝，用冰水浸泡 5 分钟后充分沥干；柠檬切角，连同可选的第戎芥末酱一起放在一旁备用。
  3. Pat the chops dry, cut through the sinew and fat edge in 3–4 places so they do not curl, then lightly pound to an even 1.5–2 cm thick without pounding thin like schnitzel.猪排擦干，在猪排边缘的筋膜与肥油交界处断筋 3–4 刀以防卷曲，再轻拍至厚薄均匀的 1.5–2 厘米（不要像维也纳炸肉排那样拍得太薄）。
  4. Season both sides of the chops with salt and black pepper, rest for 10 minutes, then pat the surface dry again before dredging.猪排两面撒盐和黑胡椒，静置 10 分钟后，裹粉前再用厨房纸把表面水分擦干。
  5. Put the flour, beaten eggs and panko in three separate shallow dishes.把面粉、打散的鸡蛋和面包糠分别放进三个浅盘。
  6. Using one hand for dry and one for wet, coat each chop lightly in flour, dip it in egg and press panko onto both sides; let the breaded chops sit on a rack or tray for 5 minutes so the coating sets, and discard any leftover panko that touched raw egg and pork.一只手管干料、另一只手管湿料：每块猪排先薄薄裹面粉，再蘸蛋液，最后两面压上面包糠；放到架子或托盘上静置 5 分钟让外壳定型，接触过生蛋和生猪肉的剩余面包糠需丢弃。
  7. Pour oil to a depth of about 4 cm in a 24 cm deep pot or wide Dutch oven, attach a clip-on thermometer and heat over medium to medium-high to 170°C; never leave hot oil unattended, and test with a panko crumb if without a thermometer (it should sizzle immediately and rise within 2–3 seconds).在 24 厘米深锅或宽口铸铁锅中倒入约 4 厘米深的油，夹上油温计，中火至中高火加热到 170°C（切勿让热油无人看管；如无温度计，可丢入一粒面包糠测试，立刻冒泡并在 2–3 秒内浮起即可）。
  8. Fry 2 chops at a time, raising the heat briefly after adding them to recover to 170°C and keep the oil at 165–175°C; fry for about 3 minutes until the underside is golden, then flip.每次炸 2 块猪排，下锅后略微调大火力把油温拉回 170°C 并维持在 165–175°C；先炸约 3 分钟至底面金黄，再翻面。
  9. Fry for about 2 minutes more, until the crust is golden and the pork reaches 71°C in the center.翻面后再炸约 2 分钟，直到外壳金黄且猪肉中心温度达到 71°C。
  10. Transfer the chops to a rack to drain and rest for 3 minutes, then cut with a sharp knife using a single downward press, not a sawing motion, into 2 cm strips.把猪排移到架子上沥油并静置 3 分钟，再用锋利的刀一刀直压下去（不要来回锯切）切成 2 厘米宽的条。
  11. Serve the cutlets with steamed rice, miso soup, shredded cabbage, lemon wedges and optional Dijon mustard on the side; drizzle the sauce over the cutlet and cabbage, or serve it in a small dish.猪排配白米饭、味增汤、卷心菜丝、柠檬角和可选的第戎芥末酱上桌，酱汁淋在猪排和卷心菜丝上或盛在小碟中。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Pound the thicker edges until the chops are even, so they cook at a similar rate.猪排厚薄尽量一致，较厚的边缘先拍平，受热更均匀，熟得更一致。
  • Press the panko on firmly so large patches are less likely to fall off in the oil.面包糠要用手轻压贴牢，入油后不容易大块脱落。
  • Avoid crowding the pot; a sharp temperature drop can leave the coating greasy and soft.炸锅不要一次塞满，温度下降太多会让外壳吸油、变软。
  • Resting the cutlets on a rack rather than covering them lets steam escape and helps preserve the crisp coating.出锅后放在架子上而不是直接盖住，蒸汽散去，外壳更脆。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The crust is dark before the pork is cooked through外壳颜色很深，猪肉中心还没熟
    Lower the oil to about 165°C and continue frying until the center reaches 71°C; pound the chops evenly and monitor the oil next time.把油温降到约 165°C，继续炸至中心达到 71°C；下次把猪排拍薄并用温度计控油温。
  • The breadcrumbs fell off面包糠脱落
    Dry the pork first, use only a thin flour layer, and press the panko on after dipping in egg; let the coated chops rest for 5 minutes before frying.猪排表面先擦干，面粉只薄薄一层，蘸蛋液后再把面包糠压实；裹好后静置 5 分钟再炸。
  • The crust is soft and greasy猪排外壳软塌、吃起来油腻
    The oil may be too cool or the pot overcrowded; fry in batches, keep the oil at 165–175°C and drain on a rack.油温可能过低或锅里放得太满；分批炸并维持在 165–175°C，出锅后放架子沥油。
