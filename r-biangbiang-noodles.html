Shaanxi Oil-Splashed Noodles油泼面

Freshly stretched wide noodles are cooked and topped with chili, garlic and scallions, then finished with hot oil that releases their aroma. The dough needs the right firmness and resting time to stretch without tearing, and the oil must be hot without scorching the toppings.宽面条现拉现煮，辣椒、蒜和葱花遇上热油，香气一下子散开。面团的软硬和醒面时间决定能不能拉得长而不断，泼油则要够热但不能把配料炸苦。

90 min total分钟 (45 + 15)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Chinese coarse chili flakes (la jiao mian), Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)另购：中式粗辣椒面（辣椒碎）、香醋/陈醋

Ingredients食材

  • All-purpose flour面粉 300 g · about 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour约 2½ 杯，中筋面粉
  • Water水 165 g · about 2/3 cup, for the dough约 ⅔ 杯，揉面用
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp, used in stages约 ⅔ 茶匙，分次使用
  • Canola oil菜籽油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL), for coating the dough and splashing3 汤匙（45 mL），抹面和泼油用
  • Chinese coarse chili flakes (la jiao mian)中式粗辣椒面（辣椒碎） 6 g · about 2 tsp, adjust to taste约 2 茶匙，按口味调整
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · about 3 cloves, minced约 3 瓣，切末
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · about 2 stalks, finely sliced约 2 根，切葱花
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)香醋/陈醋 30 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 6 g · about 2 tsp约 2 茶匙

Method做法

  1. Mix the flour and 3 g salt in a bowl for about 30 seconds.面粉和 3 克盐放入盆中拌匀，约 30 秒。
  2. Add 165 g water in stages and mix with chopsticks for about 2 minutes until even, shaggy clumps form.分次加入 165 克水，用筷子拌成均匀面絮，约 2 分钟。
  3. Knead for 8 minutes until the surface is fairly smooth and the dough feels firm but has no dry flour.揉面 8 分钟至表面较光滑、手感偏硬但没有干粉。
  4. Divide the dough into 6 pieces, roll each into a ball, then shape each into a short log.把面团分成 6 份，每份揉圆后搓成长条。
  5. Coat each log lightly with oil, cover and rest for 30 minutes until more pliable.用少量油抹匀每条面团，盖好静置 30 分钟至延展性变好。
  6. Oil the work surface and press one log into a long strip about 3-4 cm wide and 3-5 mm thick.案板抹油，将一条面团压成约 3–4 厘米宽、3–5 毫米厚的长条（牛舌形）。
  7. Press a long groove down the centre of the dough with a chopstick.用筷子沿面片中间压出一道长痕。
  8. Hold both ends and stretch the dough, lightly slapping it against the board, until about 60 cm long.双手托住面片两端，边拉边轻摔到案板上，拉至约 60 厘米长。
  9. Pull the noodle apart along the centre groove, keeping one end joined.沿中间压痕把面条撕开，保留一端相连。
  10. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil and add the remaining 1 g salt.大锅水烧至翻滚，加入剩余 1 克盐。
  11. Boil the noodles in batches over high heat for 2-3 minutes until floating, translucent at the edges and without a white centre.面条分批下锅，大火煮 2–3 分钟至浮起、边缘透明且中心没有白芯。
  12. Put the soy sauce and black vinegar in serving bowls, then lift in the cooked noodles.碗中放入生抽和黑醋，捞入煮好的面条。
  13. Top the noodles with the minced garlic, chili powder, scallions and sesame seeds, so the hot oil lands directly on the garlic.在面条上放蒜末、辣椒粉、葱花和芝麻，让热油能直接浇到蒜末上。
  14. Heat the remaining oil in a small pan over medium heat to about 180°C (355°F): test with a pinch of scallion — it should sizzle into fine bubbles immediately, then remove from the heat; do not let it smoke.小锅中火加热剩余油至约 180°C：丢一撮葱花试油，立刻密集冒细泡就离火；不要等到冒烟。
  15. Pour the hot oil evenly over the chili, scallions and garlic; mix after the brief sizzling subsides.把热油均匀淋在辣椒粉和葱蒜上，听到短促滋啦声后拌匀。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Keep the dough firm; it may look rough at first, but a full rest makes it easier to stretch.面团要偏硬，初揉时粗糙是正常的；充分醒面后会更容易拉长。
  • Oil and cover the dough to prevent a dry skin and help it stretch smoothly.面团表面抹油并盖住，能避免风干，也能让面条在拉伸时更顺滑。
  • Stretch and boil one portion at a time in a large pot so the water stays hot and the noodles do not clump.每次只拉一条并用大锅水煮，水温回升快，面条不容易粘成团。
  • Pour the oil over the chili and aromatics while it is shimmering; smoking-hot oil can make the chili bitter.热油要淋到辣椒和葱蒜上，微微闪亮即可；油冒烟容易让辣椒发苦。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The noodles tear as soon as they are stretched面条一拉就断
    Cover and rest the dough another 15 minutes before trying again; next time let the oiled dough rest a full 30 minutes and increase the stretch gradually.把面团盖好再醒 15 分钟后重拉；下次揉面后抹油静置足 30 分钟，拉的时候逐渐加力。
  • The noodles clump together or stick to the pot面条煮成一团或黏锅
    Separate them gently with chopsticks and keep the water boiling; next time use plenty of water and cook one portion at a time.马上用筷子轻轻分开并保持大火；下次用足量翻滚的水，一次只煮一份。
  • The chili turns dark and bitter after the oil is poured辣椒泼油后发黑发苦
    Use fresh chili and let the oil cool slightly before pouring; next time turn off the heat when it shimmers, before it smokes.换新辣椒粉并把油稍微放凉再淋；下次看到油微微闪亮就关火，不要等到冒烟。
