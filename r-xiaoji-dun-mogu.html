Chicken and Mushroom Stew小鸡炖蘑菇

A classic Dongbei stew built on widely available dried shiitake for deep mushroom umami, while dried hazel mushrooms are the traditional Dongbei choice if you can find them. Sear the chicken pieces until lightly golden and render some of the skin fat before braising; if you can find dried sweet potato glass noodles, add them near the end to soak up the rich broth.东北经典炖菜，用干香菇作日常基底，浓郁菌香在慢炖中融进鸡肉油脂；如果买得到干榛蘑（东北传统用料），风味更正宗。鸡块先煸炒至表皮微黄、煸出鸡油再炖才香；如果买得到红薯粉条，也可以在最后十来分钟下锅吸饱汤汁。

Method做法

  1. Rinse the dried shiitake (or hazel mushrooms) briefly and soak in 600 mL warm water (about 50°C) for 30 minutes; if using optional dried sweet potato glass noodles (80–120 g), soak them separately in warm water until pliable.干香菇（或干榛蘑）轻冲一遍，用 600 毫升约 50°C 温水泡发 30 分钟；如用可选的干红薯粉条（80–120 克），另用温水泡软备用。
  2. Lift the mushrooms from the top of the soaking bowl and rinse the stems and gills well.从水面上方捞出泡好的蘑菇，洗净根部与菌褶。
  3. Strain the soaking liquid through a paper-towel-lined sieve and reserve it.泡菇水用垫了厨房纸的细筛过滤，留用。
  4. Put the chicken in cold water with 2 ginger slices and 1 tbsp cooking wine, bring to a boil, skim the foam, boil 2 minutes and drain well.鸡块冷水下锅，加 2 片姜和 1 汤匙料酒，大火烧开撇净浮沫，煮 2 分钟后捞出沥干。
  5. Heat the oil over high heat in a wide heavy pot. Sear the drained chicken in an uncrowded layer or in batches for 3–4 minutes per side (about 6–8 minutes total per batch) until lightly golden on both sides and some skin fat renders; if the chicken releases liquid, pour off the excess water before continuing to sear.宽底炖锅大火烧热油，鸡块分批或平铺不拥挤地下锅煎，每面煎 3–4 分钟（每批共约 6–8 分钟），至两面微黄且煸出鸡皮油脂；若鸡块出水，先倒掉多余水分再继续煎。
  6. Return all seared chicken to the pot over medium heat, add the remaining ginger, green onion, garlic, star anise, bay leaves, and dried chilies, and stir-fry about 1 minute until fragrant.鸡块全部回锅转中火，放入剩余姜片、葱段、蒜瓣、八角、香叶和干辣椒，煸炒约 1 分钟至香味散出。
  7. Pour in the remaining 1 tbsp cooking wine, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, and sugar around the rim, tossing 1 minute until the chicken is evenly coated.沿锅边淋入剩余 1 汤匙料酒、生抽、老抽和糖，翻炒 1 分钟让鸡块均匀上色。
  8. Pour in the stock and the strained soaking liquid to just cover the chicken, topping up with hot water if needed.加入鸡汤和过滤后的泡菇水，汤汁大致没过鸡块，不够就补热水。
  9. Add the mushrooms and most of the salt, keeping a pinch for the end, and bring to a boil.放入蘑菇和大半的盐（留少许最后调整），大火烧开。
  10. Cover, reduce to a gentle simmer, and cook about 30 minutes, stirring or turning the pieces gently once or twice for even cooking. Check the thickest part near the bone of the thickest piece with an instant-read thermometer; it should reach at least 74°C.转小火加盖保持微沸炖约 30 分钟，中途可轻轻翻动一两次使鸡块受热均匀；用温度计测最厚鸡块最靠近骨头的部位，达到至少 74°C 且肉质软嫩即达标。
  11. Once the chicken reaches 74°C, stop stewing: uncover and reduce over medium-high heat for 5–10 minutes, scraping the bottom so nothing catches.鸡肉达到 74°C 后不要再久炖：开盖转中大火收汁 5–10 分钟，期间不断推动锅底防糊。
  12. If using the soaked sweet potato noodles, push them into the liquid 10–12 minutes before the end and stir often.如用泡软的红薯粉条，关火前 10–12 分钟压入汤中同煮，勤翻动。
  13. Taste, adjust with the reserved salt and serve.尝味，用预留的盐微调后装盆。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Mushroom soaking water packs deep umami but can hold grit; lift the caps off the top and strain the liquid through a paper towel so the broth stays clean.泡菇水既提鲜又可能藏沙：一定要从上方捞出蘑菇，再把水用厨纸过滤留作底汤，香气足又不牙碜。
  • Searing the well-drained chicken in batches renders excess skin fat and builds golden fond, giving the finished stew rich aroma without greasiness.先大火煸出鸡油再下汤：鸡块沥干分批煎透，能把鸡皮油脂和焦香逼出来，炖出来的汤才浓而不腻。
  • Keep at a gentle simmer; stir or turn the pieces gently once or twice if needed. Chicken shreds mainly from boiling too hard or overcooking, not from gentle stirring.保持微沸，避免持续剧烈翻滚；中途可轻轻翻动一两次，使鸡块受热均匀。鸡肉发散多半是火太大或炖过头所致。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The stew feels gritty or overly earthy汤里咬到细沙，或者蘑菇有土腥气
    Lift out the chicken and mushrooms, let the broth settle briefly, and ladle only the clear top liquid back in; next time strain the soaking water through a paper towel.把鸡块和蘑菇先捞出，汤静置沉淀后只取上层清汤继续收汁；下次务必把泡菇水用滤纸或细纱布过滤。
  • The chicken tastes dry or bland inside鸡肉咬起来发柴、不够入味
    Use bone-in dark meat rather than breast meat, add salt before simmering, and if the thickest piece is below 74°C or still firm, cover and simmer gently for 5–10 minutes longer.选带骨带皮的整鸡或鸡腿肉，不要用鸡胸；若最厚处尚未达到 74°C 或仍偏紧实，盖盖微沸再多炖 5–10 分钟，并确保盐在炖前下锅。
  • The broth is too watery or scorched after adding noodles汤汁太稀挂不住味，或加粉条后糊底
    If the broth is thin, boil uncovered over medium-high heat for 5–10 minutes while scraping the bottom; if noodles scorch, move the unburnt top portion to a clean pot with a splash of hot water right away.汤太稀时开盖中大火收汁 5–10 分钟并勤推锅底；若加粉条糊底，立刻把上层未糊部分换锅并补少量热水，下次只煮 10–12 分钟且勤翻动。
