Shanghai Sweet and Sour Spare Ribs上海糖醋小排

The headline cold dish of Shanghai cooking that is just as good hot. Home cooks skip the deep fryer: blanch, brown, braise in wine, vinegar, sugar and soy until tender, then reduce to a dark glaze that coats every piece. It tastes even better cold, so it suits lunchboxes and making ahead for guests.本帮菜的冷盘头牌，也是热菜。上海家庭做法不油炸：小排焯水后煎香，用料酒、醋、糖、酱油焖软，再大火收成裹满排骨的深色酱汁。冷了更入味，适合带饭和请客提前做。

60 min total分钟 (10 + 50)3 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine, Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang), Dark soy sauce另购：料酒(绍兴)、香醋/陈醋、老抽

Ingredients食材

  • Pork back ribs猪肋排 600 g · 600 g pork spare ribs, cut into 4 cm pieces猪肋排 600 克，斩 4 厘米小段 ask the butcher counter to cut them请肉柜帮忙斩段
  • Ginger姜 10 g · 4 slices4 片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 2 stalks, knotted2 根，打结
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)香醋/陈醋 30 g · 2 tbsp black vinegar, plus 1 tsp to finish香醋 2 汤匙，另备 1 茶匙出锅时用
  • Sugar白糖 36 g · 3 tbsp (rock sugar gives more shine)3 汤匙（冰糖更亮）
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 6 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 3 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙

Method做法

  1. Put the ribs in cold water, bring to a boil, skim, boil 2 more minutes, then rinse and pat dry.排骨冷水下锅，烧开撇沫再煮 2 分钟，捞出洗净擦干。
  2. Brown the ribs in the oil over medium heat, about 6 minutes.锅里放油，中火把排骨煎到两面金黄，约 6 分钟。
  3. Add the ginger and onion knot, then splash the wine down the side of the pan.放姜片和葱结炒香，沿锅边淋料酒。
  4. Add the vinegar, sugar, soy, dark soy and enough boiling water to just cover.加醋、糖、生抽、老抽和刚好没过排骨的开水。
  5. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer 35 minutes until tender.烧开后盖盖小火焖 35 分钟到排骨软。
  6. Remove the ginger and onion, turn up the heat and keep turning the ribs as the sauce reduces.拣出姜葱，开大火不停翻动收汁。
  7. When the sauce is syrupy and coats the ribs, add the extra teaspoon of vinegar, toss and scatter the sesame.汁变得黏稠、裹住排骨时，淋 1 茶匙香醋翻匀，撒芝麻。

Keys to success成功要点

  • The old Shanghai rhyme of one part wine, two vinegar, three sugar, four soy is a handy guide; cut the soy to taste and add a splash of vinegar at the end for a bright tang.老上海口诀「一酒二醋三糖四酱油」可以记比例，酱油按口味减，醋最后再补一点，酸味才鲜明。
  • Keep turning while reducing; a sugary glaze can burn in the final minute.收汁时要一直翻动，糖多的汁最后一分钟很容易焦。
  • A night in the fridge deepens the flavour, and cold is how Shanghai likes it best.冷藏一夜，排骨更入味，冷吃是上海人最爱的吃法。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The sauce will not thicken汁收不浓
    Too much water. Keep reducing on high until the sugar turns syrupy; do not use starch.水太多了。继续大火收，糖浓缩后就会变黏；不要勾芡。
  • The ribs are tough排骨发柴咬不动
    They needed longer. Add half a cup of boiling water and simmer 15 more minutes.焖的时间不够。加半杯开水再焖 15 分钟。
