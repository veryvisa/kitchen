Vinegar Stir-fried Napa Cabbage醋溜白菜

The most common winter vegetable dish in the north: a cheap napa cabbage and a hot wok make a crisp, tangy plate. Use only the thick stems, sliced thin on the bias, and add the vinegar in two goes for a bright sour aroma. Save the leaves for soup or dumplings.北方冬天最常见的素菜，几块钱一棵的大白菜，大火一炒就是一盘爽口下饭菜。只用菜帮、斜刀片薄，醋分两次下，酸香才明亮。白菜叶留着煮汤或包饺子。

13 min total分钟 (8 + 5)3 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)另购：香醋/陈醋

Ingredients食材

  • Napa cabbage大白菜 600 g · half a napa cabbage (about 600 g), stems sliced thin on the bias大白菜半棵（约 600 克），取菜帮斜刀片薄
  • Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)香醋/陈醋 30 g · 2 tbsp black vinegar, used in two parts香醋或陈醋 2 汤匙，分两次用
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp1/2 汤匙
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp约 1/3 茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 3 g · 1 tsp, mixed with 2 tbsp water1 茶匙，加 2 汤匙水调匀
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 3 cloves, sliced3 瓣，切片
  • Canola oil菜籽油 21 g · 1.5 tbsp1.5 汤匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Mix half the vinegar with the sugar, soy, salt and cornstarch slurry.一半醋、糖、生抽、盐和水淀粉调成碗汁。
  2. Heat the wok until smoking, add the oil and garlic for 10 seconds.炒锅烧到冒烟，下油和蒜片炒 10 秒。
  3. Add the cabbage stems and toss on high for 90 seconds until the edges turn translucent.下白菜帮，大火翻炒 1.5 分钟到边缘透明。
  4. Splash the other half of the vinegar down the side of the wok and toss.沿锅边淋入另一半醋，翻炒几下。
  5. Add the sauce and toss 20 seconds until glossy and clinging, then plate at once.倒入碗汁，翻炒 20 秒到汁变亮裹住白菜，马上出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Thin bias slices cook through in seconds and stay crisp.斜刀片薄，菜帮受热面大，一炒就熟又保持脆。
  • Vinegar poured down the hot side of the wok sizzles and releases its aroma, the heart of the technique.醋沿着热锅边淋下去，「呲」一声激出醋香，这是醋溜的关键。
  • High heat and under 3 minutes keeps the cabbage from going watery.全程大火、不超过 3 分钟，白菜才不出水。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • It is watery and limp出了很多水、软塌塌
    Not enough heat or too long. Cook in two batches with the wok smoking first.火不够或炒太久。分两次炒，锅一定先烧到冒烟。
  • It is sour but not fragrant酸味不香、只是酸
    All the vinegar went in the sauce. Keep half to splash on the hot wok.醋全放在碗汁里了。留一半直接淋锅边。
