Korean Soy-braised Potatoes (Gamja Jorim)韩式酱烧土豆

A Korean lunchbox regular that children love. Small potato cubes are fried, then braised in soy and sugar until the liquid is gone and they shine. Korean cooks use rice syrup for the gloss; honey stands in here. It stays tender cold, so make it ahead for packed lunches.韩国便当里的常客，小孩子最爱。土豆切小块，先煎一下再用酱油和糖烧到汁收干、表面亮晶晶。韩国用米糖浆出光泽，这里用蜂蜜代替。冷吃不硬，适合提前做好带饭。

30 min total分钟 (10 + 20)3 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Potatoes土豆 500 g · 2 medium potatoes, cut into 2 cm cubes2 个中等土豆，切 2 厘米块
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Honey蜂蜜 14 g · 2 tsp (in place of Korean rice syrup)2 茶匙（代替韩式米糖浆）
  • Garlic大蒜 6 g · 2 cloves, minced2 瓣，切末
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 3 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 10 g · 1 stalk, chopped1 根，切葱花

Method做法

  1. Soak the potato cubes in cold water for 10 minutes and drain.土豆块泡冷水 10 分钟，沥干。
  2. Fry the potatoes in the oil over medium heat for 5 minutes until lightly golden.锅里放油，中火煎土豆 5 分钟到表面微黄。
  3. Add the soy, sugar, garlic and 200 mL water and bring to a boil.加生抽、糖、蒜末和 200 mL 水，烧开。
  4. Cover and simmer 10 minutes until a chopstick goes through the potato.盖盖小火煮 10 分钟到土豆能用筷子扎透。
  5. Uncover, raise to medium and turn the potatoes as the liquid reduces almost completely.开盖转中火，翻动收汁到几乎收干。
  6. Add the honey and sesame oil and toss until shiny, then scatter the sesame seeds and green onion.加蜂蜜和香油翻匀，到表面发亮，撒芝麻和葱花。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Soaking removes surface starch so the potatoes neither stick nor turn to mush.土豆泡水去掉表面淀粉，烧的时候不会粘锅、也不会煮成泥。
  • Frying first sets the outside so the cubes hold their shape through the braise.先煎一下，土豆外层定型，长时间烧也保持完整。
  • Add the honey at the end; early on it scorches and loses its shine.蜂蜜最后放，早放容易焦，光泽也不好。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The potatoes fell apart土豆煮烂了
    Floury potatoes or overcooked. Choose waxy yellow potatoes and start reducing as soon as they are tender.用了粉质土豆或煮太久。选黄肉、蜡质的土豆，扎透就开盖收汁。
  • It caught while reducing收汁时粘锅焦了
    Keep turning for the final two minutes on medium-low.最后两分钟要一直翻动，火调到中小。
