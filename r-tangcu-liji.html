Sweet and Sour Pork Tenderloin (Tangcu Liji)糖醋里脊

The dish northern Chinese children love most. Strips of tenderloin in an egg batter are fried crisp and golden, then tossed in a sweet-sour tomato sauce. No deep-fryer needed: a skillet with a shallow layer of oil gives the same crisp outside and tender inside.北方孩子最爱的一道菜。里脊肉条裹蛋糊煎到金黄酥脆，再翻进酸甜的番茄酱汁里。家里不用一大锅油，平底锅半煎半炸，同样外酥里嫩。

40 min total分钟 (20 + 20)3 servings份Needs care需要用心Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Pork loin猪里脊 450 g · about 450 g pork tenderloin, cut into finger-thick strips猪里脊（tenderloin）约 450 克，切小指粗的条
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 10 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp约 1/3 茶匙
  • Eggs鸡蛋 50 g · 1 large egg1 个大鸡蛋
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 60 g · about 1/2 cup cornstarch (60 g: 55 for the batter, 5 for the sauce)淀粉约半杯（60 克，糊用 55 克，汁用 5 克）
  • All-purpose flour面粉 15 g · 2 tbsp flour面粉 2 汤匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 70 g · 5 tbsp (75 mL), for shallow-frying5 汤匙（75 mL），半煎半炸
  • Tomato ketchup番茄酱 50 g · 3 tbsp ketchup番茄酱 3 汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 30 g · 2 1/2 tbsp2.5 汤匙
  • Rice vinegar米醋 30 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Garlic大蒜 6 g · 2 cloves, minced2 瓣，切末
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 3 g · 1 tsp sesame seeds1 茶匙白芝麻

Method做法

  1. Toss the pork with the wine and salt and leave for 10 minutes.肉条加料酒和盐抓匀，腌 10 分钟。
  2. Mix the egg, 55 g cornstarch, the flour and about 3 tbsp water into a thick batter that coats a chopstick.鸡蛋、55 克淀粉、面粉和约 3 汤匙水调成能挂住筷子的稠糊。
  3. Make the sauce: stir the ketchup, sugar, vinegar, 5 g cornstarch and 80 mL water together.调汁：番茄酱、糖、醋、5 克淀粉和 80 mL 水搅匀。
  4. Add the pork to the batter and coat every strip.肉条倒进糊里，每条都裹匀。
  5. Heat 4 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium until small bubbles form around a chopstick.平底锅放 4 汤匙油，中火烧到筷子插入冒小泡。
  6. Lay the strips in apart and fry 2 minutes a side until golden, then take them out.肉条分开放入，每面煎 2 分钟到金黄，盛出。
  7. Raise the heat, return the pork and fry 1 minute more until crisp, then drain.油温调高，肉条回锅再煎 1 分钟到酥脆，盛出沥油。
  8. Wipe the pan, add the last tablespoon of oil and fry the garlic gently.锅洗净，放剩下的 1 汤匙油，小火炒香蒜末。
  9. Pour in the sauce and cook until it bubbles and turns thick and glossy.倒入调好的汁，煮到冒大泡、变稠发亮。
  10. Turn off the heat, toss in the pork quickly, scatter the sesame and serve at once.关火，倒入肉条快速翻匀，撒芝麻马上上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • The second, hotter, shorter fry is what makes it crisp.复炸一次是酥脆的关键，第二次油温要高、时间要短。
  • Cook the sauce to big bubbles before adding the pork so it clings without making the coating soggy.糖醋汁要先熬到冒大泡再下肉，汁浓才挂得住、不让面衣变软。
  • Tenderloin is delicate; do not cut it too thin or fry it more than 5 minutes in all.里脊很嫩，切条不要太细，煎的时间也不要超过 5 分钟。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The coating is soft or goes soggy面衣不脆、一会儿就软
    No second fry or a thin sauce; fry again for a minute, thicken the sauce first and serve as soon as it is tossed.没有复炸或汁太稀；复炸 1 分钟，汁熬浓再下肉，翻匀就上桌。
  • The batter slides off面糊挂不住、一煎就掉
    The batter was thin or the meat wet; blot the pork and make the batter thick enough to coat a chopstick.糊太稀或肉太湿；肉条先吸干，糊要稠到能挂住筷子。
