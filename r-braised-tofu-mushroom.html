Braised Tofu with Mushrooms香菇烧豆腐

Pan-searing firm tofu until golden before braising with shiitake mushrooms lets it soak up a savory glaze without crumbling. Two common pitfalls at home are putting wet tofu into the pan where it sticks and tears, or skipping the brief covered simmer so the center stays bland.老豆腐煎到金黄定形再和香菇同烧，吸饱鲜浓汤汁还不易碎。家里做常失败在两处：豆腐没擦干下锅粘破，或者没先焖够时间导致外面咸、里面淡。

25 min total分钟 (10 + 15)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Firm tofu, Dark soy sauce另购：老豆腐、老抽

Ingredients食材

  • Firm tofu老豆腐 400 g · 1 block (about 400 g) firm tofu1 盒老豆腐（约 400 克）
  • Shiitake香菇 150 g · 8-10 fresh shiitake mushrooms (about 150 g)新鲜香菇 8–10 朵（约 150 克） if using dried shiitake, soak ahead and reserve the soaking liquid用干香菇需提前泡发并保留泡菇水
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 3 cloves, sliced3 瓣，切片
  • Ginger姜 6 g · 3 slices3 片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 2 stalks, whites in pieces and greens chopped2 根，葱白切段、葱绿切花
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp (7.5 mL)半汤匙（7.5 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 6 g · 2 tsp, mixed with 2 tbsp water2 茶匙，加 2 汤匙水调匀
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 150 g · about 2/3 cup chicken stock约 2/3 杯鸡汤 use water or mushroom soaking liquid for a vegetarian dish素食可用清水或香菇泡发水代替
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙

Method做法

  1. Cut the firm tofu into 4 by 4 cm squares, 1.5 cm thick, and pat dry with paper towels.老豆腐切成约 4 厘米见方、1.5 厘米厚的块，用厨房纸吸干表面水分。
  2. Remove the tough stems from the shiitake and slice the caps about 5 mm thick.香菇去掉硬蒂，伞盖切成约 5 毫米厚的片。
  3. Stir the soy sauce, oyster sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, and stock together in a small bowl.小碗中把生抽、蚝油、老抽、糖和鸡汤拌匀成料汁。
  4. Mix the cornstarch with 2 tbsp water in a separate bowl to make a slurry.另取一只小碗，把淀粉和 2 汤匙清水搅匀成水淀粉。
  5. Heat 1.5 tbsp oil in a nonstick skillet over medium heat and pan-fry the tofu in a single layer for about 3 minutes per side until golden and lightly crisp.不粘平底锅加 1.5 汤匙油中火烧热，单层摆入豆腐，每面煎约 3 分钟至金黄微脆。
  6. Transfer the seared tofu to a plate.把煎好的豆腐盛出备用。
  7. Add the remaining 1/2 tbsp oil over medium heat and stir-fry the ginger, garlic, and scallion whites for about 30 seconds until fragrant.锅中加剩下的半汤匙油，中火放入姜片、蒜片和葱白段爆香约 30 秒。
  8. Add the shiitake slices and stir-fry over medium-high heat for 3 to 4 minutes until softened, slightly shrunken, and most of the liquid they release has cooked off.倒入香菇片，转中高火翻炒 3–4 分钟，至香菇变软、边缘微缩、锅底水分基本收干。
  9. Pour in the sauce mixture and bring to a boil over high heat.倒入调好的料汁，大火烧开。
  10. Return the tofu to the pan, cover, and simmer over medium-low heat for 4 minutes.把豆腐放回锅中，加盖转中小火焖烧 4 分钟入味。
  11. Stir the slurry again, pour it around the pan, and cook 30 to 60 seconds while gently swirling until the sauce turns glossy and thick.把水淀粉再次搅匀，沿锅边淋入，轻轻晃锅煮 30–60 秒至汤汁浓稠发亮。
  12. Drizzle with sesame oil, scatter with chopped scallion greens, and serve hot.淋入香油，撒上葱花，出锅装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Patting the tofu dry prevents hot oil from splattering and helps a golden crust form quickly.煎前擦干豆腐表面水分，既不会剧烈溅油，也能更快形成金黄表皮。
  • Browning both sides forms a thin crust that holds together better when stirred and lets the tofu soak up the broth while braising.把豆腐两面煎黄能让表皮结一层薄壳，减少翻动时碎裂，焖烧时也更稳定地吸住汤汁。
  • Cooking the shiitake until tender before adding liquid brings out their savory aroma.香菇先下锅炒软再倒汤汁，菌香才能充分逼出来。
  • Swirling the pan instead of stirring aggressively with a spatula coats the tofu in glaze while keeping the pieces intact.勾芡时轻轻晃锅而不是用锅铲猛翻，能挂匀芡汁又保持豆腐完整。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The tofu stuck to the pan and broke apart豆腐粘锅碎掉
    Let the oil heat through before adding the tofu and wait a full 3 minutes until a crust forms before flipping; a nonstick pan is easiest.下锅前等油温升到位，底面煎够 3 分钟结壳再翻面；用不粘锅最稳妥。
  • The sauce is watery and slides off the tofu汤汁稀薄挂不住豆腐
    Boil off excess liquid uncovered for 1 minute first, then stir the settled starch and drizzle it in while bubbling.先开盖大火收掉多余汤汁 1 分钟，再把沉底的淀粉搅起，分次淋入煮滚。
  • The tofu is salty outside but bland in the middle豆腐外面咸、里面淡
    Keep the slices no thicker than 1.5 cm and simmer covered for at least 4 minutes so the broth penetrates.豆腐别切太厚（不超过 1.5 厘米），并且一定要加盖焖煮至少 4 分钟。
