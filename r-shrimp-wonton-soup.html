Shrimp Wonton Soup鲜虾云吞汤

The heart of a Hong Kong wonton is the springy bite of shrimp. Pork worked until sticky, shrimp folded in as large pieces, a little goldfish-tail shape, and they float up after one boil in clear broth. Make extra and freeze them for a ten-minute soup later.港式云吞的灵魂是馅里一颗颗弹牙的虾。猪肉馅打上劲，虾仁切大粒拌进去，包成小金鱼尾的样子，清汤一滚就浮起来。一次多包一些冻起来，以后十分钟就是一碗汤。

40 min total分钟 (30 + 10)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Wonton wrappers, Bok choy另购：馄饨皮/云吞皮、小白菜

Ingredients食材

  • Wonton wrappers馄饨皮/云吞皮 250 g · about 40 wonton wrappers (1 pack)云吞皮约 40 张（1 包） thin yellow Hong Kong style wrappers are best选薄的黄色碱水皮
  • Frozen raw shrimp冷冻生虾 250 g · 250 g peeled raw shrimp, half cut in large pieces and half chopped fine生虾仁 250 克，一半切大粒、一半剁碎
  • Ground pork猪肉馅 200 g · 200 g ground pork (about 30% fat)猪肉馅 200 克（三成肥）
  • Ginger姜 5 g · a small piece, grated1 小块，磨蓉
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 2 stalks, sliced2 根，切葱花
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp1/2 汤匙
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 3 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Salt盐 3 g · about 1/2 tsp (split between filling and broth)约 1/2 茶匙（馅和汤分开放）
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · a pinch (white pepper is traditional)少许（传统用白胡椒）
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 500 g · 2 cups (500 mL) chicken stock plus 2 cups water鸡高汤 2 杯（500 mL），另加 2 杯水
  • Bok choy小白菜 200 g · 200 g baby bok choy or choy sum小白菜或菜心 200 克

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Stir the pork with half the salt, the soy, the ginger and 2 tbsp water in one direction until it turns sticky.猪肉馅加一半盐、生抽、姜蓉和 2 汤匙水，朝一个方向搅到起胶发黏。
  2. Fold in both kinds of shrimp, the cornstarch, sesame oil and pepper.拌入剁碎和大粒的虾仁、淀粉、香油和胡椒。
  3. Chill the filling for 15 minutes so it is easier to wrap.馅放冰箱冷藏 15 分钟，更好包。
  4. Put 1 tsp filling in the centre of a wrapper, fold it into a triangle and press the wet edges shut.皮中间放 1 茶匙馅，对折成三角形，边缘蘸水捏紧。
  5. Bring the two bottom corners together and pinch to make a little ingot shape.两个底角往中间一折一捏，成小元宝形。
  6. Bring the stock and 2 cups water to a boil, season with the rest of the salt, blanch the greens and put them in the bowls.高汤加 2 杯水烧开，加剩下的盐调味，烫熟青菜盛到碗里。
  7. Boil the wontons in a separate large pot of water until they all float, then 1 minute more.另一锅大量水烧开，下云吞煮到全部浮起再煮 1 分钟。
  8. Lift the wontons into the bowls, ladle over the hot stock and add the green onion.捞出云吞放进碗里，浇上热高汤，撒葱花。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Working the pork until sticky binds the springy shrimp and tender pork so the filling holds together.肉馅搅到起胶，虾的弹和肉的嫩才连得在一起，煮的时候也不会散。
  • Boiling the wontons in plain water keeps the wrapper starch out of the clear broth.云吞用另一锅清水煮，皮上的面粉不会把汤弄浑。
  • Freeze extras on a floured tray until hard, then bag them; cook from frozen with 2 extra minutes.包好吃不完的铺在撒了面粉的盘上冻硬再装袋，冻的直接下锅，多煮 2 分钟。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The wontons burst煮破了
    Edges not sealed or the water was boiling hard. Press out the air as you seal, and add half a bowl of cold water to keep a gentle boil.边缘没捏紧或水大滚。捏的时候挤出空气；水开后加半碗冷水，保持微滚。
  • The filling is dry馅发柴不嫩
    The pork was too lean or no water was added. Use pork with some fat and beat in water a spoonful at a time.肉太瘦或没加水。用三成肥的肉馅，搅的时候分次加水，每次吸收再加。
