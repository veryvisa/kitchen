Ginger Scallion Crab姜葱炒蟹

Ginger scallion crab highlights the sweetness of fresh crab beneath a glossy, aromatic glaze. The shells are glossy and coated in a savory ginger-scallion sauce. At home, the meat easily turns watery or stays raw inside scorched shells. Dusting the cut edges with cornstarch before searing locks in moisture, and a covered braise cooks the thick claws evenly.姜葱炒蟹是最讲究本味的粤式海味菜，蟹壳油亮，裹着咸鲜姜葱汁，连姜片和葱段都格外抢手。家里做最怕蟹肉出水发散，或者壳焦了里面还没熟；切面薄拍干淀粉先煎封汁，再配高汤加盖短焖，肉质就会紧实鲜甜。

Method做法

  1. Scrub the crab under cold running water, pry off the top shell, discard the gray feathery gills and stomach sac and rinse clean.流水下刷净蟹壳，掀开背壳去掉灰色海绵状蟹鳃和蟹胃，冲洗干净。
  2. Chop the body into 6 to 8 pieces, crack the thick claws gently with the back of a cleaver and pat everything dry.蟹身斩成 6 到 8 块，用刀背把大蟹钳轻轻敲裂，全部擦干表面水分。
  3. Lightly dust only the exposed cut meat surfaces with about 5 tsp cornstarch, shaking off any excess.只在蟹块露出的肉切面上薄薄拍一层约 5 茶匙干淀粉，抖掉多余干粉。
  4. Stir the chicken stock, oyster sauce, soy sauce, sugar and remaining 1 tsp cornstarch together in a small bowl.把鸡汤、蚝油、生抽、白糖和剩下 1 茶匙淀粉在小碗里搅匀备用。
  5. Heat 2 tbsp canola oil in a wok or wide skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering but not smoking, then lay the crab cut-side down and sear undisturbed for 2 minutes until lightly golden.大炒锅或平底宽锅烧热 2 汤匙油至油面微微波动、未见冒烟，中大火把蟹块切面朝下煎 2 分钟至定形微黄。
  6. Toss for about 1 minute to coat the shells evenly and lightly brown the edges, then transfer to a plate.翻炒约 1 分钟，让蟹壳均匀沾油、边缘微焦，先盛出装盘。
  7. Add the remaining 1 tbsp canola oil over medium heat and stir-fry the ginger and white scallion segments for 45 seconds until fragrant.锅中补入剩下 1 汤匙油，转中火放入姜和葱白段炒 45 秒至出香味。
  8. Add the garlic and stir for 15 seconds until aromatic.放入蒜粒快炒 15 秒至微黄飘香。
  9. Return the crab and any juices to the wok, pour the wine down the side of the hot wok and stir-fry over high heat for about 30 seconds until the raw alcohol smell dissipates.倒回蟹块和盘中汁水，沿锅边淋入料酒，大火翻炒约 30 秒至酒气挥发。
  10. Stir the sauce bowl, pour it into the wok, cover and braise over medium heat for 5 to 6 minutes, adding 2 to 3 tbsp water or stock if the wok looks dry.搅匀调味芡汁倒入锅中，加盖中火焖煮 5 到 6 分钟；锅中偏干时可补入 2 到 3 汤匙水或高汤。
  11. Uncover and verify doneness: the thickest claw meat is opaque and firm and reaches 74°C / 165°F.开盖检查：最厚蟹钳肉呈不透明乳白色、肉质紧实，中心达到 74°C（165°F）。
  12. After the crab is fully cooked, remove it if needed and boil the sauce over high heat for 30 to 60 seconds until it lightly coats a spoon, then return the crab and scallion greens and toss briefly to coat.蟹肉熟透后视情况将蟹块盛出，汤汁转大火煮 30 到 60 秒至能轻薄地挂住勺子，再放回蟹块和葱绿段，翻炒数下裹匀即可。
  13. Turn off the heat, drizzle with sesame oil, toss once and serve hot.关火淋入香油拌匀，立即趁热上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Patting the crab dry and dusting only the cut edges creates a light seal that holds moisture inside and helps thicken the final glaze.蟹块擦干后只在切口拍淀粉，能锁住蟹肉原汁不流失，收汁也更容易浓稠挂壳。
  • Crack the big claws lightly before cooking; intact shells insulate the meat and block the sauce from getting inside.大蟹钳务必提前敲裂，否则高温下壳内蒸汽顶不开，肉既慢熟又吃不进鲜汁。
  • Searing the crab first and blooming the aromatics separately keeps the ginger and scallions sweet instead of scorched.先煎蟹盛出再单独爆香姜葱，可以避免姜葱从头跟着高温干炒而焦黑发苦。
  • Use a wide pan so the crab pieces sit mostly in one layer; crowding traps steam and boils the crab rather than searing it.尽量用宽锅让蟹块平铺受热，锅太小挤在一起会变成水蒸，失去煎香风味。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The pan is watery and the sauce slides off the shells锅底水汪汪，酱汁太稀挂不住蟹壳
    Usually from wet crab or too little reduction. Lift the crab out, boil the sauce down hard over high heat, then toss the crab back in.多半是蟹块没擦干或焖煮时间太短；先把蟹盛出，锅里汤汁大火收浓再拌回，下次切面可多拍半茶匙淀粉。
  • The shells scorched while the claw meat stayed translucent蟹壳炒焦了，但大钳里的肉还是半透明
    Add 2 to 3 tbsp hot water, cover and cook for another 2 to 3 minutes over medium heat. Next time crack the claws better and verify doneness in the thickest claw meat.加 2 到 3 汤匙热水，加盖中火再焖 2 到 3 分钟；下次记得把钳壳敲裂，用最厚蟹钳肉中心的温度确认熟透。
  • The ginger and scallions turned dark and bitter姜葱发黑发苦
    Keep ginger and scallions out during the high-heat crab sear, bloom them over medium heat and save the green tops for the final toss.不要把姜葱和生蟹一起全程猛火干煎；先煎蟹再中火炒姜葱，葱绿留到最后才下。
