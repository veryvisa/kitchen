Jianbing Breakfast Crepe煎饼果子

Freshly griddled jianbing wraps crisp fried wonton crackers, egg, and scallions inside a tender, nutty crepe for a hot breakfast that takes about 30 minutes start to finish. Getting the batter consistency right and keeping the pan temperature gentle are the keys to spreading thin crepes without tearing.现摊的煎饼果子外皮软韧带豆香，夹着现炸薄脆、鸡蛋和葱花，在家备料加摊制大约半小时就能吃上热乎早餐。难点在于面糊稀稠要恰当、锅温不能太高，否则摊不开还容易粘锅或破皮。

30 min total分钟 (15 + 15)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Mung bean flour, Wonton wrappers, Chinese sweet bean sauce (tianmianjiang)另购：绿豆面、馄饨皮/云吞皮、黄酱/甜面酱

Ingredients食材

  • Mung bean flour绿豆面 60 g · 60 g mung bean flour (about 1/2 cup)绿豆面 60 克（约半杯） if unavailable, mung bean starch or another grain flour can be used, but the flavor and texture will differ没有绿豆面时可用绿豆淀粉或其他杂粮粉代替，但风味和口感会不同
  • All-purpose flour面粉 60 g · 60 g all-purpose flour (about 1/2 cup)中筋面粉 60 克（约半杯）
  • Eggs鸡蛋 100 g · 2 large eggs (1 per crepe)2 个大鸡蛋（每张饼 1 个）
  • Wonton wrappers馄饨皮/云吞皮 80 g · 8 square wonton wrappers, for the crispy crackers方形馄饨皮 8 张，做薄脆用
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 2 stalks, finely chopped2 根，切细葱花
  • Cilantro (fresh coriander)香菜 15 g · 1 small handful cilantro, chopped1 小把香菜，切碎 omit if you dislike cilantro不吃香菜可省略
  • Chinese sweet bean sauce (tianmianjiang)黄酱/甜面酱 36 g · 2 tbsp sweet bean sauce甜面酱 2 汤匙
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 6 g · 2 tsp toasted sesame seeds熟白芝麻或黑芝麻 2 茶匙
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp, for thinning the sauce1 茶匙，调酱用
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp, for the batter约 1/3 茶匙，调面糊用
  • Canola oil菜籽油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL), for frying crackers and greasing the pan3 汤匙（45 mL），炸薄脆和润锅用

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Whisk the mung bean flour, all-purpose flour, and salt with 220 mL water in a bowl until smooth and pourable like thin yogurt. Let it rest for about 10 minutes, then check the consistency again and loosen with a little more water if it has thickened.把绿豆面、中筋面粉和盐放进大碗，分次加入 220 毫升清水拌匀，搅到成能流畅滴落、无颗粒的稀酸奶状面糊；静置约 10 分钟后再看一次稠度，太稠就再加少量水调开。
  2. Cut two small slits in the center of each wonton wrapper so they stay flat when fried.在每张馄饨皮中间用刀划两道小口，防止下锅后鼓成大泡。
  3. Heat 2.5 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium heat to about 165-170°C (reserve the remaining 1.5 tsp for greasing the crepe pan). Fry the wonton wrappers in batches for about 20-40 seconds per side until lightly golden and crisp, letting the oil temperature recover between batches, then drain on paper towels.平底锅加 2.5 汤匙油中火烧到约 165–170°C（剩下约 1.5 茶匙油留着煎饼时润锅用），分批放入馄饨皮每面煎炸约 20–40 秒到浅金黄酥脆即可，批次之间让油温回升，捞出放厨房纸上吸油。
  4. Stir the sweet bean sauce with 1 tbsp water and the sesame oil so it spreads easily.把甜面酱加 1 汤匙清水和香油调匀，方便均匀刷开。
  5. Lightly oil a 26 cm nonstick skillet over low heat, ladle in half the batter, and immediately tilt and swirl the pan so it coats the bottom thinly.另取约 26 厘米的不粘平底锅，小火微热后刷薄薄一层油，舀入一半面糊并立刻端锅转圈晃匀，铺满锅底。
  6. Cook over low heat for about 40 seconds, until the bottom is mostly set but the top is still slightly wet, then immediately crack 1 egg onto the crepe and spread it evenly over the whole surface with the back of a spatula.小火摊约 40 秒，等面糊底面基本定型但表面仍略湿润时，马上打入 1 个鸡蛋，用锅铲背把蛋液摊匀铺满整张饼皮。
  7. While the egg is still wet, scatter half the sesame seeds, green onions, and cilantro over it.趁蛋液未凝固撒上一半芝麻、葱花和香菜。
  8. Cook over low heat for 1 more minute until most of the egg is set, then flip egg-side down and keep cooking until the egg is fully set with no runny raw egg remaining; adjust the time to your pan rather than relying on a fixed count.小火再煎 1 分钟到蛋液大部分凝固，翻面让蛋朝下继续煎，直到蛋液完全凝固、没有流动的生蛋液为止（时间按锅温调整，不要只看固定秒数）。
  9. Flip back so the egg side faces up, spread half the sweet bean sauce evenly over the egg, and lay 4 crisp crackers in the center.翻回让鸡蛋面朝上，在鸡蛋表面均匀刷上一半甜面酱，把 4 片薄脆摆在正中间。
  10. Fold the crepe around the crackers into a packet, slice in half, and eat right away while hot.把饼皮折叠包住薄脆，切半趁热立刻食用。
  11. Before starting the second crepe, have the remaining batter, egg, sauce and crackers all ready so you can griddle it right away, rather than letting the first one go cold and the crackers soften while you wait.摊第二张之前先把剩下的面糊、蛋、酱料和薄脆都准备好，按同样步骤尽快摊好，避免第一张在等待中变凉、薄脆回软。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Mixing mung bean flour 1:1 with all-purpose flour balances the nutty aroma with the gluten's stretch. Keep the batter from getting too thick and avoid overcooking the crepe dry, so it stays pliable rather than cracking.绿豆面和面粉按 1 比 1 混合，能兼顾传统豆香和面粉带来的延展性；面糊别太稠、饼也别煎得太干，饼皮才更容易弯折不裂。
  • Keep the pan gently warm on low heat when pouring the batter; a hot pan sets the batter on contact before you can swirl it thin.倒面糊时锅温不能高，微温的小火能让面糊充分流平，锅太烫会一倒就凝结成厚块。
  • Thinning the sweet bean sauce with water and sesame oil lets you brush an even coat instead of leaving salty clumps.甜面酱先用少量清水和香油澥开，刷起来薄而均匀，不会一小块咸得发腻。
  • Slitting the wonton wrappers vents steam while frying so they stay flat and crisp as homemade crackers.馄饨皮划口后油炸能排出热气，成品平整又酥脆，在家做比买现成薄脆更省事。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The batter is too thick to swirl and makes a thick pancake面糊太稠摊不开、饼皮厚得像发面饼
    Whisk 1 tbsp of water at a time into the remaining batter until it pours freely like thin yogurt.往剩余面糊里每次加 1 汤匙清水拌匀再试，下次按面粉总重与清水约 1 比 1.8 的比例调糊。
  • The crepe sticks or tears when flipping翻面时饼皮粘锅或撕破
    Brush a light film of oil before pouring next time, and wait until the surface looks dry and the edges lift on their own before flipping.下次倒面糊前刷一薄层油，且等饼皮边缘自然翘起、底面呈干爽微黄再铲起翻面。
  • The crispy crackers go soggy before finishing薄脆还没吃完就软了
    Let fried crackers cool completely before assembling, eat right away, or pack crackers separately if taking to go.薄脆炸好要摊凉透，煎饼包好后立刻吃；带出门的话把薄脆单独装袋，吃前再夹进去。
