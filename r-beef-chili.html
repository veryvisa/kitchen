Beef Chili美式牛肉辣豆汤（Chili）

The North American cold-weather and game-day pot, enough for several meals and better the next day. Ground beef is browned well, then simmered an hour with onion, peppers, tomatoes and black beans, chili powder and cumin. Serve with rice, tortillas or baked potatoes.北美冬天和看球时的标配，一大锅能吃好几顿，第二天更好吃。牛肉末炒出焦香，加洋葱彩椒、番茄和黑豆，用辣椒粉和孜然慢炖一个小时。配米饭、玉米饼或烤土豆都行。

Method做法

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot, press in half the beef and sear on high until browned underneath, then break it up and lift out.大锅烧热油，下一半牛肉馅压平，大火煎到底面焦褐再翻散，盛出。
  2. Brown the rest the same way and lift it out, leaving about 1 tbsp fat in the pot.剩下的牛肉同样煎好盛出，锅里留 1 汤匙油。
  3. Soften the onion and pepper over medium heat, about 6 minutes.下洋葱和彩椒中火炒软，约 6 分钟。
  4. Add the garlic, chili powder, cumin, oregano and tomato paste and stir a minute until darker.加蒜末、辣椒粉、孜然、牛至和番茄膏，炒 1 分钟到颜色变深。
  5. Return the beef, add the tomatoes, stock and brown sugar and bring to a boil.牛肉回锅，倒入番茄碎、高汤和红糖，烧开。
  6. Simmer with the lid ajar for 40 minutes, stirring now and then.转小火，盖盖留缝炖 40 分钟，中途搅几次。
  7. Add the beans, simmer 15 more minutes and season with salt.加入黑豆再炖 15 分钟，加盐调味。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Browning the beef in two batches builds the base flavour; all at once it steams grey.牛肉分两批煎出焦褐色，这层焦香是整锅的底味；一次全倒进去只会煮成灰色。
  • Toasting the spices in fat for a minute before the liquid makes them far more fragrant.香料在油里炒一分钟再加液体，味道比直接撒进汤里浓得多。
  • North American chili powder is a mild blend of chilli, cumin and garlic, not pure ground chilli.北美超市的 chili powder 是辣椒加孜然、蒜粉的混合粉，不是中式纯辣椒粉，辣度温和。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • Too runny太稀
    Simmer uncovered 10-15 more minutes, or mash some beans into the pot.开盖再炖 10–15 分钟，或用勺子压碎一些豆子搅进去。
  • Too greasy太油
    The beef was fatty. Pour off excess fat after browning and skim the top once it has simmered.牛肉太肥。煎完把多余的油倒掉；炖好后撇去表面的油。
