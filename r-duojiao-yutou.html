Steamed Fish with Chopped Chili剁椒蒸鱼

Steamed fish with chopped chili is a signature Hunan dish where savory heat soaks right into tender white fish. While the classic Hunan version uses a fish head if you can find one, it works smoothly at home with sea bass fillets or a whole fish, as long as you briefly sauté the chili topping, steam over a rolling boil without overcooking, and finish with hot oil.剁椒蒸鱼是湘菜里最受欢迎的蒸菜之一，经典做法常用鱼头，如果买得到鱼头也很适合；在家用海鲈鱼柳或整条海鲈鱼同样能做出咸辣鲜香、嫩滑入味的口感。关键在于先炒香剁椒、足汽蒸熟不过头，最后再用热油泼出香味。

22 min total分钟 (10 + 12)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Sea bass, Hunan chopped salted chili, Shaoxing wine另购：鲈鱼、剁椒（瓶装）、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Sea bass鲈鱼 550 g · about 550 g boneless sea bass fillets (net flesh weight)约 550 克去骨海鲈鱼柳（净鱼肉重量） thick fillets around 2 cm hold up best during steaming; if using a whole sea bass, buy 700–900 g depending on whether the head and bones are kept鱼柳选 2 厘米左右厚的更不容易蒸散；若使用整条海鲈鱼，建议购买约 700–900 克的整鱼，具体取决于是否保留鱼头和鱼骨
  • Hunan chopped salted chili剁椒（瓶装） 70 g · about 4 tbsp (60–80 g) Hunan chopped salted chili约 4 汤匙湖南剁椒（60–80 克） saltiness varies by brand; rinse briefly if sharp不同品牌咸度差很多，太咸先冷水略冲
  • Ginger姜 25 g · about 25 g (4 slices for the plate, the rest finely minced for sautéing)约 25 克（切 4 片垫底，其余切细末炒香）
  • Garlic大蒜 20 g · 5 cloves, finely minced5 瓣，剁细末
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks, whites cut into pieces and greens shredded3 根，葱白切段、葱绿切丝
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Soy sauce生抽 17 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL, about 17 g), poured around the edge after steaming1 汤匙（15 mL，约 17 克），蒸后淋盘边
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp, to round out the salted chili1 茶匙，柔和剁椒咸辣味
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL), divided2 汤匙（30 mL），分两次用

Method做法

  1. Score both sides of a whole fish 2–3 times; leave fillets whole or cut them to fit the plate.整鱼在鱼身两侧各斜划 2–3 刀；鱼柳保持完整或按大小分切。
  2. Rub the fish with a little cooking wine and rest for 10 minutes.鱼身抹少量料酒，静置 10 分钟。
  3. Warm 1 tbsp oil in a small pan over low heat, sauté the minced garlic and reserved minced ginger until fragrant without scorching, then add the chopped chili and sugar and stir-fry for about 1 minute before transferring out.小锅烧热 1 汤匙油，小火将蒜末和预留的姜末炒出香味（避免炒焦），再加入剁椒和白糖翻炒约 1 分钟后盛出。
  4. Arrange the scallion whites and ginger slices on a heatproof plate, pat the fish dry with paper towels, and lay it on top.蒸盘底铺上葱白段和姜片，用厨房纸吸干鱼表面水分，再将鱼放在姜葱上。
  5. Spread the sautéed chopped chili and garlic mixture evenly across the top of the fish.把炒好的剁椒蒜蓉均匀铺在鱼肉表面。
  6. Bring the steamer to a rolling boil, put in the fish plate, cover and steam over high heat.蒸锅大火把水烧到翻滚，放入鱼盘，加盖大火蒸。
  7. Start checking 2 cm fillets at 7–9 minutes (a whole fish takes longer); it is done when the thickest part reaches 70°C and flakes easily.约 2 厘米厚的鱼柳从 7–9 分钟开始检查，整鱼更久；鱼肉最厚处中心达 70°C、能轻松戳透即出锅。
  8. Carefully remove the hot plate and steady the fish to drain off most of the accumulated liquid, leaving a little behind. Pour the soy sauce around the edge and scatter the scallion greens on top.小心端出热鱼盘，注意护住鱼身完整，倒掉盘中多余蒸汁、保留少量汤汁，再沿盘边淋入生抽，撒上葱绿丝。
  9. Heat the remaining 1 tbsp oil until barely smoking and pour it over the scallions and chili to sizzle.将剩下的 1 汤匙油烧到微微冒烟，迅速泼在葱丝和剁椒上激出香味。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Briefly frying the chopped chili with garlic, ginger and sugar tames raw garlic sharpness and mellows the salt.剁椒先和蒜蓉、姜末、糖小火炒过，能去掉生蒜的辛冲味，也让剁椒香气更圆润。
  • Resting the fish on scallion whites and ginger lets steam circulate underneath so the bottom cooks evenly.鱼身底下垫葱段和姜片架空，蒸汽能从底部穿过，整条鱼受热更均匀。
  • Wait for a full rolling boil before adding the fish and maintain steady high heat for consistent steam and even cooking. Avoid overcooking so the flesh stays tender instead of turning dry.水烧开后再放鱼，大火保持充足蒸汽，有助于稳定蒸制时间和鱼肉受热。避免蒸过头，以免鱼肉变干变柴。
  • Draining excess cooking liquid before adding soy sauce prevents the seasoning from becoming diluted.蒸后倒掉多余蒸汁，再淋生抽，可以避免盘底汤汁过多稀释剁椒的咸鲜味。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The fish turned firm and dry鱼肉蒸老发硬、不滑嫩
    Avoid slicing fillets too thin, adjust steaming time to thickness and cut (about 7–9 minutes for 2 cm fillets), and pull the dish as soon as the thickest part reaches 70°C.鱼柳别切太薄，按鱼的厚度和形态调整蒸制时间（2 厘米厚鱼柳约 7–9 分钟），最厚处刚到 70°C 就关火出锅。
  • The dish tastes overly salty or harsh整盘太咸发苦
    Taste the jarred chili first; if very salty, rinse briefly and squeeze dry before frying, and cut the soy sauce in half.开罐先尝一粒剁椒，如果太咸就用冷水冲一下挤干再用，蒸完后的生抽也可以减半。
  • There is a lingering fishy taste吃起来有土腥味
    Scrape away any dark belly membrane and bloodline before marinating, and drain most of the cloudy steaming liquid after cooking.下锅前彻底刮净鱼腹黑膜和贴骨血块，蒸完记得把盘底浑浊汤汁倒掉大半。
