Tomato Beef Brisket Noodles番茄牛腩面

Slow-braised beef brisket melts into a bright, sweet-tangy tomato broth that makes every bowl of noodles deeply comforting. Cooking the tomatoes down with tomato paste builds rich body, while boiling the noodles separately keeps the soup clean and glossy.番茄炖到融进牛腩浓汤里，酸甜开胃，配上一碗筋道热面和小油菜特别满足。想做好汤底，要先把番茄炒出红汁并借一点番茄膏增浓，面条另锅煮熟再淋汤才不会糊汤。

115 min total分钟 (20 + 95)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Bok choy, Star anise, Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine另购：小白菜、八角、老抽、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Beef brisket牛腩 700 g · 700 g beef brisket, cut into 3 cm cubes牛腩 700 克，切 3 厘米方块
  • Tomatoes西红柿 600 g · 4 medium ripe tomatoes (about 600 g), chopped4 个中等熟番茄（约 600 克），切块
  • Tomato paste番茄膏 30 g · 2 tbsp tomato paste番茄膏 2 汤匙
  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 medium onion, finely chopped1 个中等洋葱，切碎
  • Wheat noodles挂面/面条 400 g · 400 g dried wheat noodles干小麦面条 400 克
  • Bok choy小白菜 200 g · 4 baby bok choy, halved lengthwise4 棵小油菜（上海青），对半切开
  • Ginger姜 20 g · 6 slices6 片
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · 4 cloves, smashed4 瓣，拍扁
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks, whites cut into lengths and greens chopped3 根，葱白切段、葱绿切花
  • Star anise八角 2 g · 1 whole star anise1 颗
  • Bay leaves月桂叶 1 g · 2 bay leaves2 片
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp (7.5 mL)半汤匙（7.5 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 30 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Salt盐 6 g · about 1 tsp, added in stages约 1 茶匙，分次加
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Place the brisket in cold water with 2 ginger slices and 1 tbsp cooking wine, and bring to a boil over high heat.牛腩冷水下锅，加 2 片姜和 1 汤匙料酒，大火煮开。
  2. Skim off the scum, boil 3 more minutes, then lift out the brisket and rinse with warm water.撇掉表面血沫，继续煮 3 分钟后捞出牛腩，用温水冲净沥干。
  3. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat and saute the onion, green onion whites, remaining ginger, and garlic for about 3 minutes until translucent.炖锅倒油中火烧热，下洋葱碎、葱白、剩余姜片和蒜瓣炒约 3 分钟，直到洋葱变软呈半透明。
  4. Add the tomato paste and two-thirds of the chopped tomatoes, reduce to medium-low and mash and cook for 4 minutes into a thick red paste. Tomato paste scorches easily; if the pan bottom darkens or smokes, splash in 2 tbsp hot water along the edge and scrape it up before continuing.加入番茄膏和三分之二的番茄块，转中小火翻炒约 4 分钟，用锅铲压碎炒成浓稠红酱；番茄膏容易粘锅，若发现锅底变深或冒烟，沿锅边淋 2 汤匙热水刮底再继续炒。
  5. Add the brisket, star anise, and bay leaves, stirring 3 minutes over medium heat to coat the beef.放入牛腩、八角和香叶，中火翻炒 3 分钟让肉块裹上番茄酱汁。
  6. Pour in the remaining wine, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, and sugar, stirring 1 minute until evenly colored.倒入剩余料酒、生抽、老抽和糖，翻炒 1 分钟炒匀上色。
  7. Pour in about 1.2 L boiling water to cover the beef, bring to a boil, cover, and simmer over low heat for 60 minutes. Check the liquid level once partway through; if it has dropped below the beef, top up with more boiling water only, never cold water.倒入约 1.2 升开水没过牛腩，大火煮开后转小火加盖炖 60 分钟；中途检查一次汤面，低于牛腩时只能补开水，不要加冷水。
  8. Stir in the remaining tomatoes and 4 g salt, cover, and simmer 20 more minutes until the brisket is fork-tender (well above 63°C internally).加入剩下的番茄块和 4 克盐，加盖再炖 20 分钟，直到牛腩软烂能用筷子轻松扎透（中心温度远高于 63°C）。
  9. Taste the broth, adjust with up to 2 g more salt if needed, and keep warm.尝一下汤味按需补入剩余 2 克盐，关火保温。
  10. Bring a separate pot of water to a boil over high heat and blanch the bok choy for 40 seconds until bright green, then remove.另烧一锅沸水，放入小油菜大火烫 40 秒到翠绿后捞出。
  11. Cook the wheat noodles in the same boiling water according to the package directions (usually 4-6 minutes) until no hard core remains, then drain well and divide among 4 bowls.同一锅沸水下入小麦面条，按包装时间煮（通常 4–6 分钟）至无硬芯，捞出沥干后分入 4 个碗中。
  12. Ladle the brisket and hot tomato broth over the noodles (about 5-6 pieces of beef and 350-400 mL broth per bowl), top with bok choy and green onion greens, and serve hot.往碗里舀入牛腩和热番茄汤（每碗约 5–6 块牛腩、汤约 350–400 毫升），摆上小油菜并撒葱花趁热吃。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Adding the tomatoes in two batches gives both a rich, dissolved soup base from the first batch and fresh tomato pieces from the second.番茄分两次下：先下三分之二炒碎熬出浓汤，后下三分之一留出果肉块，既有汤底又有清爽口感。
  • Store-bought tomatoes can be mild; frying 2 tbsp of tomato paste deepens both the color and tangy richness of the broth.北美市售番茄有时偏淡不够酸甜，加 2 汤匙番茄膏小火炒香能立刻让汤色红亮、酸香更足。
  • Boil the noodles in a separate pot of water rather than directly in the broth so starch does not cloud or thicken the soup.面条一定另起一锅清水煮，再浇牛腩汤，这样汤头清亮顺滑，不会因为面汤里的淀粉变浑变黏。
  • If you need to top up the braise, use only boiling water and keep a gentle simmer so the connective tissue turns silky instead of tightening; cold water makes the meat seize up.炖牛腩如需补水只能加开水、保持微沸小火，牛腩筋膜才能慢慢化软、不发柴；冷水会让肉收紧变硬。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The broth tastes sharply sour汤头偏酸、喝起来尖锐
    Stir in 1/2 to 1 tsp sugar and simmer 2 minutes to round out the acidity.往汤里再加半茶匙到 1 茶匙糖小火煮 2 分钟，糖能立刻柔和番茄的酸度。
  • The brisket is still chewy牛腩炖完还是有点咬不动
    Cuts vary in toughness; cover and simmer 20-30 minutes longer, or use a pressure cooker for about 25 minutes.不同牛腩肥瘦和部位不同，继续加盖小火多炖 20 到 30 分钟即可，或改用高压锅压约 25 分钟。
  • Not enough broth for the noodles汤汁太浓太少不够浇面
    Stir in 150 mL boiling water, bring to a simmer, and adjust salt so each bowl gets plenty of soup.关火前兑入 150 毫升开水煮开并补一点盐，留足宽汤浇面最合适。

Beef brisket supplies protein and the noodles provide carbohydrates. Because brisket has moderate fat and the broth contains sodium, enjoy extra bok choy and go light on drinking the leftover soup.牛腩提供蛋白质，面条是主要碳水来源；牛腩带一定脂肪且汤底含钠，吃时可以少喝些汤、多配青菜。
