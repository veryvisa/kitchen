Mouthwatering Chicken口水鸡

Good mouthwatering chicken comes down to two things: poaching the thighs gently, steeping off the heat, and plunging them into ice water so the meat stays juicy and the skin firms up; and building a chili dressing where garlic, numbing pepper, sugar, and vinegar balance the heat.口水鸡成败看两点：鸡肉要靠「煮后再焖、立刻冰镇」做到骨边刚好熟透、肉质滑嫩、鸡皮爽脆；红油料汁则要蒜香、椒麻和一点微甜微酸互相托住，才不会只剩死辣。

47 min total分钟 (15 + 10)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Sichuan peppercorn, Chinese coarse chili flakes (la jiao mian), Shaoxing wine, Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)另购：花椒、中式粗辣椒面（辣椒碎）、料酒(绍兴)、香醋/陈醋

  • Garlic大蒜 20 g · 5 cloves, finely minced or grated5 瓣，剁成细蒜泥
  • Cilantro (fresh coriander)香菜 15 g · 1 small bunch, chopped1 小把，切段
  • Roasted peanuts花生 40 g · about 1/4 cup roasted skinless peanuts, coarsely chopped熟去皮花生米约 1/4 杯，切碎

Method做法

  1. Put the chicken thighs in a pot, cover with cold water by 3 cm, and add the ginger, knotted green onion, half the Sichuan pepper, and cooking wine.鸡腿放进深锅，加冷水没过鸡肉约 3 厘米，放入姜片、葱结、一半花椒和料酒。
  2. Bring just to a boil, skim off the foam, then immediately reduce to the lowest heat (do not keep it at a hard boil) and simmer covered for 8 minutes.大火煮沸后撇净浮沫，不要继续滚煮，随即转最小火加盖微沸 8 分钟。
  3. Turn off the heat and steep covered for 12 minutes, then check with a food thermometer in the thickest part of the meat (away from the bone) to confirm it reaches 74°C. If it has not, cover and continue over low heat and recheck; the times here are only a guide, the thermometer reading is what matters.关火不开盖再焖 12 分钟；用食品温度计插入鸡腿最厚处（避开骨头）确认达到 74°C，没达到就盖上盖子继续小火加热后再测，时间只作参考，温度才是标准。
  4. Transfer the chicken straight into a bowl of ice water for 10 minutes until completely chilled and the skin firms up.立刻把鸡腿捞入提前准备好的冰水盆中，冰镇 10 分钟到皮脆肉凉。
  5. Toast the remaining Sichuan pepper in a dry pan over low heat for 1 minute until fragrant, then grind to a powder.剩余花椒放干锅小火炒 1 分钟出香，碾成细粉。
  6. Put the chili flakes, sesame seeds, and ground Sichuan pepper in a heatproof bowl.把辣椒面、白芝麻和花椒粉放进耐热碗里。
  7. Heat the canola oil to about 160-180°C, until it shimmers (do not wait for smoke), then pour it over the chili bowl in two batches.小锅烧热菜籽油到约 160–180°C（油面起细纹，不要等到冒烟），分两次泼进辣椒碗里激出红油。
  8. Let the chili oil cool to warm, no longer bubbling hard, before stirring in the minced garlic, soy sauce, black vinegar, sugar, salt, sesame oil and 3 tbsp cooled, strained poaching broth. Raw garlic added to still-hot oil turns into fried-garlic flavour instead of bright pungent garlic.红油放凉至温热、不再剧烈冒泡后，加蒜泥、生抽、陈醋、糖、盐、香油和 3 汤匙放凉过滤的原味鸡汤搅匀；蒜泥不能遇到还很热的油，否则会变成炸蒜味。
  9. Lift out the chicken, drain and pat it fully dry. Deboning before cutting into 2 cm strips is the easier and safer home option; if keeping the bone in, use a proper cleaver and check carefully for bone splinters.鸡腿捞出充分沥干、擦干表面，去骨后切成约 2 厘米宽的条块更省心也更安全；如果保留骨头，用斩骨刀小心斩块并仔细挑净碎骨屑。
  10. Drain the smashed cucumber of excess water and layer it in a deep plate, arrange the well-drained chicken on top, spoon over the chili dressing, and garnish with green onion, cilantro, and chopped peanuts.拍黄瓜沥去多余水分后铺在深盘底，码上沥干的鸡肉，淋上红油料汁后撒葱花、香菜和碎花生。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Gentle simmering followed by covered steeping helps keep the meat from overcooking, but you must still confirm with a food thermometer that the thickest part reaches 74°C; a fixed time cannot guarantee that on its own.小火微沸加关火焖熟，有助于避免沸水把鸡肉煮老煮柴，但最终必须用食品温度计确认最厚处达到 74°C，固定时间不能替代测温。
  • Plunging the chicken into ice water cools it quickly, firms the skin, and shortens the time it spends in the warm danger zone. Drain and pat it dry before cutting so no water pools on the plate.冰水激冷能快速给鸡肉降温、让鸡皮收紧，并缩短鸡肉停留在温热区间的时间；捞出后沥干擦干再切块，盘子里才不会积水。
  • Bone-in skin-on thighs stay far juicier than breast meat when served chilled.带骨带皮鸡腿比鸡胸更适合做口水鸡，脂肪和皮下胶质让凉吃也不干硬。
  • Do not add the raw garlic to the freshly heated chili oil; wait until it has cooled to warm so you get bright pungent garlic aroma instead of fried garlic.蒜泥不要跟刚烧热的辣椒油一起泼，要等红油降到温热才下，蒜香才会是清亮辛香而不是炸蒜味。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • Pink liquid near the bone, or you are not sure it is fully cooked切开骨头周围还渗很多血水，或不确定有没有熟透
    Colour near the bone is not a reliable test; always confirm with a food thermometer. Return uncut thighs to simmering broth and keep rechecking the thickest part with a thermometer until it reaches 74°C. If already chopped, spread the pieces out, steam or microwave them, and check several of the thicker pieces individually before chilling again.骨边颜色不是可靠的判断依据，务必用食品温度计确认。还没切完的鸡腿放回热鸡汤里小火再煮，用温度计复测最厚处直到达到 74°C；已斩块的摊开，连汤进微波炉或蒸锅加热，并对较厚的几块逐一测温确认达标，再冰镇。
  • The chicken tastes stringy and the skin tore鸡肉吃起来柴、表皮破烂
    A hard boil tears the skin and dries the meat. Shred the dry pieces and toss with extra poaching broth and chili oil; next time keep the pot at a bare simmer.全程大火滚煮会把皮冲破、肉煮柴。改做鸡丝拌匀多淋一点鸡汤和红油润口，下次水开后立刻转最小火微沸。
  • The sauce is overly fiery or salty without depth料汁太辣太咸、不够鲜香
    Stir in 2 more tbsp cooled broth, half a teaspoon of sugar, and a splash of black vinegar to round out the salt and heat.多加 2 汤匙凉鸡汤和半茶匙糖，再挤一点陈醋，鲜甜和酸度会把辣度立刻拉圆润。

Chicken thighs provide solid protein, and removing the skin cuts down on fat. The chili dressing is rich in oil and sodium, so eat each piece with the cucumber underneath.鸡腿提供优质蛋白，去皮食用可明显减少油脂；红油料汁含油和钠较多，搭配底下的拍黄瓜一起夹着吃会更清爽。
