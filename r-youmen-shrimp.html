Shandong Braised Prawns油焖大虾

A Shandong classic built on frying the red fat out of the prawn heads and reducing it with sugar and soy into a glossy sauce. Costco frozen shrimp come without heads, so the head richness is missing, but large shell-on shrimp seared then braised are still glossy, sweet and savoury, meant to be sucked from the shell.鲁菜的看家菜，讲究把虾头里的红油逼出来，和糖、酱油一起收成红亮的汁。Costco 冷冻生虾没有头，做不出虾脑那份浓，但用带壳大虾、先煎后焖，照样油亮甜鲜，连壳吮着吃。

22 min total分钟 (10 + 12)3 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Frozen raw shrimp冷冻生虾 500 g · about 500 g large shell-on shrimp带壳大虾约 500 克 trim the legs and pick out the vein through the back with a toothpick, leaving the shell on剪掉虾须，用牙签从背部挑出虾线，壳保留
  • Canola oil菜籽油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Ginger姜 10 g · 4 slices4 片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 4 stalks, cut into lengths4 根，切段
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 16 g · 4 tsp4 茶匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Tomato ketchup番茄酱 15 g · 1 tbsp (for colour, optional)1 汤匙（帮助上色，可不放）
  • Salt盐 1 g · a small pinch一小撮

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Pat the shrimp completely dry.虾用厨房纸彻底擦干。
  2. Heat the oil, lay in the shrimp and fry on medium for a minute until the undersides turn red.锅烧热下油，虾平铺，中火煎 1 分钟到底面变红。
  3. Turn them, pressing gently with the spatula, until both sides are bright red and the oil is tinted.翻面，用锅铲轻压虾身，煎到两面红亮、锅里的油变红。
  4. Add the ginger and white parts of the onion and stir until fragrant.下姜片和葱白炒香。
  5. Splash the wine down the side of the pan, then add the sugar, soy, ketchup and 3 tbsp boiling water.沿锅边淋入料酒，再加糖、酱油、番茄酱和 3 汤匙开水。
  6. Cover and braise on low for 2 minutes.盖盖小火焖 2 分钟。
  7. Uncover, raise the heat and toss until the sauce thickens and coats the shells, then add the green onion tops.开盖转大火，翻动收汁到汁变稠、裹满虾壳，下葱绿翻匀。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Fry the shells until the oil turns red before adding any seasoning; that red oil reduced with sugar gives the colour and flavour.壳一定要煎到出红油再加调料，红油和糖一起收汁，颜色和香味才对。
  • There is more sugar than soy; the dish leans sweet, and the soy is only there for savouriness.糖比酱油多，这道菜是甜口为主，酱油只为提鲜。
  • Keep the braise short so the meat stays tender; the sauce thickens from the final high-heat toss, not from long cooking.焖的时间短，虾肉才嫩；汁靠最后大火收，不是靠长时间焖。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The shrimp look pale, not glossy red颜色发白不红亮
    Not fried long enough or too little oil. Press the shrimp as they fry; a little more ketchup helps the colour.煎得不够或油太少。煎时多压几下虾身；可以多放一点番茄酱补色。
  • The sauce will not thicken汁收不浓
    Too much water. Keep tossing over high heat and the sugar will turn it syrupy; avoid cornstarch, which goes gluey on the shells.水放多了。开大火继续翻，糖会让汁变黏；别加淀粉，会糊在壳上。
