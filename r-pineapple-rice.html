Dai Pineapple Rice菠萝饭

Yunnan Dai pineapple rice pairs soaked glutinous rice with fresh pineapple cubes, steamed inside the hollowed fruit shell. Steam drives the bright tropical aroma into the chewy grains for a gently sweet finish. The main traps are under-soaking the rice so the center stays firm, or leaving too much juice in the mix and turning the bottom soggy.云南傣味菠萝饭把泡透的糯米与新鲜菠萝丁拌匀，装回掏空的菠萝壳里同蒸。蒸汽把热带果香逼进软糯米粒中，甜中带着清爽微酸，端上桌也很亮眼。常见失误是糯米没泡透或没先蒸半熟就入壳，导致中心夹生，或者菠萝汁太多让米饭发水。

300 min total分钟 (20 + 40)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Glutinous rice另购：糯米

Ingredients食材

  • Pineapple菠萝 300 g · buy 1 medium-large fresh pineapple (about 1200 g whole, skin and core included); dice 300 g of the scooped flesh for the rice and save the rest for snacking买 1 个中等偏大新鲜菠萝（约 1200 克带皮带芯），挖出果肉后切 300 克小丁拌饭，其余果肉另装盘食用 pick one that smells sweet and yields slightly挑果香明显、微按有弹性的熟果
  • Glutinous rice糯米 350 g · 350 g white glutinous rice (about 2 cups)白糯米 350 克（约 2 杯）
  • Brown sugar红糖 35 g · about 2.5 tbsp packed brown sugar (35 g)红糖或黄糖约 2.5 汤匙（35 克）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp约 1/3 茶匙
  • Roasted peanuts花生 30 g · about 3 tbsp roasted crushed peanuts (30 g)熟花生碎约 3 汤匙（30 克）
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 5 g · 1 tsp toasted white sesame seeds熟白芝麻 1 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Rinse the glutinous rice twice, soak in plenty of cold water for 4 hours until a grain crushes easily (soak it in the fridge if the kitchen is warm, and change the water once if soaking longer than 4 hours), then drain.糯米淘洗两遍，加足量冷水浸泡 4 小时至米粒一捻就碎（厨房较热时放冰箱里泡，超过 4 小时要中途换一次水），沥干。
  2. Line a steamer with damp cheesecloth or perforated parchment, spread out the rice, and once the water is at a full boil steam for 12 to 15 minutes until the edges are translucent with a small opaque core left in the middle (about 80% done).蒸笼铺湿纱布或打孔烘焙纸，平铺糯米，水烧开上汽后蒸 12 到 15 分钟至边缘透明、中心还留一点白芯（八分熟）。
  3. While the rice steams, wash the pineapple and cut it lengthwise through the leafy crown into two halves ('pineapple boats'), or slice off the top third as a leafy lid and keep the bottom as the bowl; trim a thin slice off the base so it sits flat.趁蒸米时洗净菠萝外皮，从带叶冠的一端纵向对半切开成两只「菠萝船」（或横切下三分之一当带叶盖子，保留下半部分做碗），底部削平一小片让它能立稳。
  4. Score around the inside edge and scoop out the flesh with a spoon, cutting out the hard core, and leave a 1 cm shell wall; turn the hollowed shell upside down on paper towels for 5 minutes to drain off extra juice.用小刀沿皮内侧划圈并用勺子挖出果肉，去掉中间硬芯，留下约 1 厘米厚的菠萝壳碗；挖空后倒扣在纸巾上沥 5 分钟，倒掉多余果汁。
  5. Dice about 300 g of the pineapple flesh into 1 cm cubes and drain it in a sieve for 5 minutes (do not squeeze it by hand — save the juice to drink or use elsewhere), and keep the rest for snacking.取约 300 克菠萝肉切成 1 厘米小丁，放细筛沥 5 分钟（不要用手挤，果汁留着可以另外喝或做饮料），其余果肉另装盘食用。
  6. Stir the brown sugar, salt and oil with 2 tbsp hot water until dissolved, drizzle it over the hot rice and mix well, then fold in the diced pineapple last (this keeps the raw fruit from releasing too much juice into the hot rice).红糖、盐和食用油加 2 汤匙热水调匀化开，淋在蒸好的热糯米上拌匀，最后才拌入菠萝丁（避免生果肉被热米焖出太多汁水）。
  7. Spoon the rice mixture loosely into the hollowed pineapple shell without packing it down.把拌好的菠萝糯米松松地填进掏空的菠萝壳里，不要压实。
  8. Loosely tent the filled pineapple with foil (or set the leafy lid piece on top), set it on a heatproof plate, and once the water is boiling steam over medium-high heat for 18 minutes; the foil keeps condensation from dripping back onto the rice, and you can check the rice at the 15-minute mark.菠萝碗松松盖上锡纸（或盖子部分那一瓣果肉），架在耐热大盘上，水沸后入锅中大火蒸 18 分钟；盖锡纸能挡住锅盖水汽滴回饭里，15 分钟时可以打开看一下米粒熟度。
  9. Remove from the steamer, scatter with crushed peanuts and sesame seeds, and serve warm.取出撒上熟花生碎和白芝麻，趁热挖食。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Soaking and pre-steaming the rice before stuffing the shell prevents the thick pineapple wall from leaving the center undercooked.糯米先泡透再预蒸至八分熟，最后进菠萝壳复蒸，可以彻底解决果壳隔热导致的中间夹生问题。
  • Squeezing excess juice from the diced pineapple keeps the fruit aroma without making the rice soggy.菠萝丁提前轻挤汁水再拌米，既能渗出果香，又不会让米饭底部积水发糊。
  • Pack the rice loosely so steam can circulate evenly through the grains.米饭填入菠萝壳时保持蓬松不压实，蒸汽才能顺畅穿透整碗米粒。
  • A small pinch of salt balances the brown sugar and pineapple so the sweetness tastes bright.加少许盐能衬出红糖和菠萝的清甜，吃起来更有层次不发腻。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • Liquid pooled at the bottom and the rice turned mushy菠萝饭底部积水、米粒湿软发糊
    Press more juice out of the pineapple next time; to rescue this batch, toss the rice in a dry pan over low heat for 2 minutes to evaporate excess moisture.下次菠萝丁多挤一点汁水并保证预蒸后米粒干爽；这一锅可把米饭盛到干锅里小火烘炒 2 分钟收干水汽。
  • The rice in the center or bottom is still firm靠近中心或底部的糯米还是硬的
    Loosely tent the pineapple with foil and steam 10 minutes longer, or spread the rice on a plate with 2-3 tbsp hot water, cover tightly and steam 8-10 minutes.在菠萝表面松松盖一张锡纸大火补蒸 10 分钟，或将米掏出撒 2–3 汤匙热水、盖紧后蒸 8 到 10 分钟。
  • The rice tastes too tart and lacks sweetness吃起来偏酸、甜香不够
    An underripe pineapple brings extra tartness; fold in 1 tsp honey or a touch more brown sugar while the rice is still hot.买的菠萝熟度偏生；趁热淋入 1 茶匙蜂蜜或再拌一点红糖即可改善。
