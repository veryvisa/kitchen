Steamed Pork Ribs in Black Bean Sauce豉汁蒸排骨

These ribs steam in a savory sauce that clings to each piece. Cut them into similar-sized pieces and spread them without piling too high so they cook evenly and stay tender.豉汁蒸排骨的关键是让酱汁裹住小块排骨，同时蒸到骨边熟透而不发柴。排骨切得大小相近、盘里铺得不太厚，熟成更均匀。

60 min total分钟 (20 + 40)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Fermented black beans (douchi), Shaoxing wine另购：豆豉、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Pork back ribs猪肋排 700 g · 700 g pork ribs, cut into single ribs or small pieces排骨 700 克，切成单根或小段
  • Fermented black beans (douchi)豆豉 15 g · 1 tbsp (about 15 g), roughly chopped1 汤匙，约 15 克，略切碎
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 4 cloves, minced4 瓣，切末
  • Ginger姜 10 g · minced切末
  • Soy sauce生抽 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 g)1 汤匙，15 克
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 18 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 8 g · 3 tsp (8 g)3 茶匙，8 克
  • Canola oil菜籽油 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 g)1 汤匙，15 克
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 1 stalk, chopped1 根，切葱花

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Rinse the ribs; if desired, soak in cold water for 10 minutes to remove excess blood, then drain and pat very dry.将排骨冲洗干净，必要时用冷水浸泡10分钟去血水，捞出充分沥干并用厨房纸擦干。
  2. Mix the chopped fermented black beans with the garlic, ginger, soy sauce, oyster sauce, cooking wine, sugar and sesame oil.把豆豉略切碎，与蒜末、姜末、生抽、蚝油、料酒、糖和芝麻油拌匀。
  3. Add the cornstarch and toss until the ribs are evenly coated in a thin layer, then add the canola oil and toss again.加入玉米淀粉，抓匀至排骨表面均匀裹上一薄层淀粉，再加入菜籽油抓匀。
  4. Cover the ribs and refrigerate them for 20 minutes.盖好排骨并冷藏腌制20分钟。
  5. Bring the steamer water to a boil and arrange the ribs in a single layer on a wide, shallow heatproof plate. Leave clearance around the plate for steam to circulate, and keep the plate above the boiling water.蒸锅加水烧开，把排骨单层铺在宽而浅的耐热盘中，盘子周围留出空隙让蒸汽循环，盘底不可浸入沸水。
  6. Set the plate in the steamer, cover and steam over high heat for 35 to 40 minutes.把排骨连盘放入蒸锅，大火加盖蒸35至40分钟。
  7. Check the meat beside the bone in the thickest piece. If it has not reached 71°C (160°F), continue steaming in 5-minute increments until the meat beside the bone reaches 71°C (160°F).检查最厚一块骨边的肉，未达到71°C（160°F）时每次再蒸5至10分钟，直到肉旁边的骨边温度达到安全熟度。
  8. Turn off the heat, carefully remove the plate and scatter the scallion over the ribs.关火后小心取出盘子，撒上葱花。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Dry the ribs well so their moisture does not dilute the seasoning.排骨表面尽量擦干，腌料才不会被多余水分冲淡。
  • Fermented black beans are already salty and savory, so season as written before considering extra salt.豆豉本身咸香，先按配方调味，不急着额外加盐。
  • A light starch coating helps the juices thicken and cling to the ribs.淀粉薄薄裹住排骨，蒸出的汁水会更浓，能附在肉上。
  • A shallow plate and spaced-out ribs let steam circulate between the pieces for more even cooking.使用浅盘并把排骨铺开，蒸汽更容易穿过肉块之间，受热也更均匀。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The steamed ribs are tough and dry排骨蒸出来发柴
    Keep the steamer boiling without letting it run dry, and remove the ribs once they reach a safe temperature; cut the pieces smaller and more evenly next time.确认蒸锅水持续沸腾但没有烧干，并在达到安全温度后及时取出；下次把排骨切成较小且大小相近的块。
  • The center is undercooked while the outside is overdone排骨中间还生，外层已经老了
    Spread the ribs in one layer without stacking and steam 5 to 10 minutes more before checking the thickest piece; cut oversized pieces smaller next time.把排骨铺成单层并避免堆叠，继续加蒸5至10分钟后测量最厚处；下次先将大块切小。
  • Water pooled in the plate and diluted the sauce盘底积水，豉汁变稀
    Pat the ribs dry before marinating, start with boiling water and keep the plate above the waterline.腌制前把排骨擦干，蒸锅水沸后再入锅，并确保蒸盘没有泡在锅水里。
