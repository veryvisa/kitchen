Buttermilk Pancakes美式松饼

Buttermilk gives these pancakes a mild tang, with tender centers and golden surfaces; this batch makes about 12 pancakes, 10-12 cm each. Mix the batter only until the dry flour disappears; overmixing makes the pancakes tough, while excessive heat can brown them before the centers cook.酪乳（Buttermilk，常见超市里也叫白脱牛奶）让美式松饼带着淡淡酸香，中心松软、表面金黄，一份面糊大约能煎出 12 张 10–12 厘米的松饼。面糊只拌到没有明显干粉即可，过度搅拌会让饼变韧，火太大则容易外焦内生。

30 min total分钟 (10 + 20)4 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Buttermilk另购：酪乳

Ingredients食材

  • All-purpose flour面粉 250 g · about 2 cups, spooned into the cup and leveled (not scooped straight from the bag)约 2 杯（舀入量杯后刮平，不要直接拿杯挖取）
  • Sugar白糖 25 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Baking powder泡打粉 8 g · about 2 tsp约 2 茶匙
  • Baking soda小苏打 3 g · about 1/2 tsp约 1/2 茶匙
  • Salt盐 3 g · about 1/2 tsp fine table salt约 1/2 茶匙细盐
  • Eggs鸡蛋 100 g · 2 large eggs2 个大鸡蛋
  • Buttermilk酪乳 360 g · about 1 1/2 cups约 1 1/2 杯 no buttermilk? use 1 1/2 cups milk plus 1 1/2 tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar, rested 5-10 minutes没有酪乳可用 1 1/2 杯牛奶加 1 1/2 汤匙柠檬汁或白醋，静置 5–10 分钟代替
  • Butter黄油 42 g · 3 tbsp, melted and cooled slightly3 汤匙，融化后稍微放凉
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · about 1 tbsp, for the pan约 1 汤匙，用于煎锅

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt in a large bowl.在大碗中拌匀面粉、糖、泡打粉、小苏打和盐。
  2. Beat the eggs in another bowl and whisk in the buttermilk; let the melted butter cool until no longer hot, then drizzle it in slowly while whisking — butter that's too hot or very cold buttermilk can cause small clumps.在另一只碗中打散鸡蛋，拌入酪乳；黄油放凉到不烫手后再慢慢边搅边倒入，黄油太热或酪乳太凉都容易结出小油粒。
  3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir just until the dry flour disappears; a few small lumps are fine. Rest 5 minutes to let the flour hydrate for a more tender texture — do not rest longer than 10-15 minutes.把湿料倒入干料，轻轻拌至刚好看不见干粉，面糊有少量小颗粒也可以；静置 5 分钟让面粉吸水、面糊更蓬松，不要超过 10–15 分钟。
  4. Preheat a skillet over medium heat for 2-3 minutes, then wipe it with a paper towel dipped in oil for a thin film. Test with a small spoonful of batter — it should sizzle lightly and set at the edge within about 30 seconds; lower to medium-low if it browns too fast.平底锅中火预热 2–3 分钟，再用厨房纸蘸油薄薄抹一层；挑一小勺面糊试锅，应该轻微滋滋响并在约 30 秒内边缘定型，太快变深就调低到中小火。
  5. Pour about 60 ml of batter per pancake, leaving about 3 cm between them, and cook over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until bubbles appear and the edges look dry, then flip without pressing down with the spatula.每张倒入约 60 毫升面糊，彼此留约 3 厘米间距，中火煎 2–3 分钟，表面出现气泡且边缘变干时翻面，翻面后不要用锅铲按压。
  6. Cook the second side for 1-2 minutes until both sides are golden, the center springs back when pressed, and cutting one open shows no runny batter.另一面煎 1–2 分钟，至两面金黄、中心按压能回弹、切开没有流动的生面糊。
  7. Keep the cooked pancakes in a single layer on a wire rack in a 95°C oven, for up to 20-30 minutes, and repeat with the remaining batter.煎好的松饼单层摆在烤盘架上，放进 95°C 烤箱保温，最多 20–30 分钟；剩余面糊重复煎完。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Baking soda starts reacting as soon as it meets the buttermilk, so aim to cook the batter within 10-15 minutes of mixing. To check if baking powder is still active, stir 1/2 tsp into hot water — it should fizz.小苏打一接触酪乳就开始反应，面糊拌好后最好 10–15 分钟内煎完；泡打粉可以先取半茶匙倒进热水里试，冒泡就说明还有效。
  • Stop once the dry flour disappears; a few small lumps make a more tender pancake than a perfectly smooth batter.只需拌到干粉消失，留下少量小颗粒比把面糊搅得光滑更容易做出松软口感。
  • Use only a thin film of oil; pooled oil can make the pancakes brown unevenly.锅面只需薄油，油积成小洼会让松饼一面煎得不均匀。
  • Use the first pancake to judge the heat; if its underside darkens too quickly, lower the heat for the rest.第一张可用来判断火力，若底部很快变深色，就先把火调低再煎后续松饼。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The pancakes are tough and flat松饼又韧又扁
    Do not beat the batter smooth or mix it long in advance; check that the baking powder and baking soda are still active. Do not press down with the spatula after flipping, and stir in 1-2 tbsp more flour if the batter runs noticeably thin.面糊不要搅到光滑，也不要提前很久拌好；检查泡打粉和小苏打是否仍有效；翻面后别用锅铲按压，面糊明显偏稀可以补 1–2 汤匙面粉。
  • The bottoms are browned but the centers are wet底部焦了，中心还湿
    Lower the heat and let the pan cool slightly first, then make smaller pancakes and cook the first side longer; wait until the edges look dry before flipping.先把火调低、等锅稍降温，再缩小每张的份量并延长第一面的煎制时间；翻面时应看到边缘已变干。
  • The pancakes stick or brown in patches松饼粘锅或颜色花斑
    Preheat the pan evenly and apply a thin film of oil; wipe away burnt crumbs between batches.先让锅均匀预热，再薄薄抹一层油；每煎一批后擦掉焦屑。
