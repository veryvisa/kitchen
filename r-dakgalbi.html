Main主菜 · Korean韩

Dakgalbi春川辣炒鸡

Chicken, cabbage and sweet potato cook together in a glossy, sweet-spicy sauce that works with rice or on its own. The trick is to brown the chicken first, then let the vegetables soften without scorching the paste.鸡肉、卷心菜和红薯在宽锅里裹上甜辣酱汁，炒出香气后加盖焖熟，最后收浓酱汁，配饭或单吃都合适。鸡肉与蔬菜同步吸收酱味，红薯软糯、卷心菜带甜。

45 min total分钟 (20 + 25)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Korean chili flakes (gochugaru)另购：韩式辣椒粉（gochugaru）

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 700 g · about 700 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into 3 cm pieces去骨鸡腿肉约 700 克，切 3 厘米块
  • Green cabbage包菜 350 g · about 1/2 head cabbage, cut into large pieces卷心菜约半个，切大块
  • Sweet potato红薯 250 g · 1 medium sweet potato, cut into 5–7 mm slices1 个中等红薯，切 5–7 毫米厚片
  • Carrots胡萝卜 100 g · 1 small carrot, thinly sliced on the diagonal1 根小胡萝卜，斜切薄片
  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 medium onion, cut into thick strips1 个中等洋葱，切粗丝
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 2 stalks, cut into short lengths2 根，切段
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · about 4 cloves, minced约 4 瓣，切末
  • Ginger姜 8 g · a small piece, minced一小块，切末
  • Gochujang韩式辣酱 60 g · 4 tbsp (about 60 g)4 汤匙（约 60 克）
  • Korean chili flakes (gochugaru)韩式辣椒粉（gochugaru） 12 g · Korean red pepper flakes (gochugaru), about 2 tbsp; heat varies by brand, so start with half and add more to taste韩式辣椒粉（gochugaru）约 2 汤匙，辣度因品牌而异，初次可先放一半，尝味后再加
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp regular soy sauce (about 30 mL)生抽 2 汤匙（约 30 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 12 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 14 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 6 g · 2 tsp, for serving2 茶匙，装盘用

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Stir together the gochujang, chili powder, soy sauce, sugar, garlic and ginger.把辣椒酱、辣椒粉、生抽、糖、蒜末和姜末拌匀。
  2. Coat the chicken with the sauce, cover and marinate in the refrigerator for 10 to 20 minutes.将鸡肉与调好的酱料拌匀，盖好放入冰箱冷藏腌 10–20 分钟。
  3. Prepare the sweet potato, carrot, onion and cabbage, keeping the sweet potato slices about 5–7 mm thick.把红薯、胡萝卜、洋葱和卷心菜分别切好，红薯片保持约 5–7 毫米厚。
  4. Heat the canola oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the marinated chicken with its sauce and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes, until the outside is no longer pink.宽锅中火烧热菜籽油，放入腌好的鸡肉及酱料翻炒约 3–4 分钟，至鸡肉表面变色。
  5. Add the sweet potato and carrot and stir-fry for 2 minutes.加入红薯和胡萝卜，翻炒 2 分钟。
  6. Add the onion and cabbage and stir-fry for about 2 minutes, until the cabbage begins to wilt.加入洋葱和卷心菜，翻炒约 2 分钟，直到卷心菜开始塌软。
  7. Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover and cook for 8 minutes, turning every 2 minutes, until a chopstick pierces the sweet potato.转中小火，盖锅焖 8 分钟，每隔 2 分钟翻动一次，直到红薯能被筷子刺穿。
  8. Uncover and stir-fry over medium heat for 3-5 minutes, checking that the center of the thickest chicken piece reaches 74°C.揭盖转中火炒 3–5 分钟，确认最厚的鸡肉中心达到 74°C。
  9. Stir in the green onion and sesame oil for 30 seconds, then sprinkle with sesame seeds.拌入葱段和芝麻油，炒 30 秒后撒上芝麻。

Keys to success成功要点

  • A wide pan gives the chicken and vegetables room to touch the hot surface; crowding makes them steam before they brown.用宽锅让鸡肉和蔬菜接触锅面，锅太挤会先出水，鸡肉不容易煎香。
  • Even sweet potato slices cook alongside the chicken; thick pieces stay firm inside while very thin ones can break apart.红薯切得均匀，才能和鸡肉差不多同时熟；太厚会夹生，太薄则容易碎。
  • The paste can stick easily, so cover only after the vegetables release moisture and turn the food often.辣椒酱容易粘锅，蔬菜出汁后再加盖焖，并勤翻锅底。
  • Chili pastes and powders vary widely in heat; start with the listed amount and adjust to taste next time.辣椒酱和辣椒粉的辣度差别很大，先按配方的较低量做，再按口味调整。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The chicken is cooked but the sweet potato is still hard鸡肉熟了但红薯还是硬
    Lift out the chicken, add 2 tbsp water to the sweet potato and cover for 3-5 minutes, then return the chicken and confirm it reaches 74°C.把鸡肉先盛出，红薯加 2 汤匙水继续盖锅焖 3–5 分钟，再放回鸡肉确认中心达到 74°C。
  • The sauce tastes bitter or the pan has scorched酱汁发苦或锅底烧焦
    Lower the heat at once and add 1-2 tbsp water, loosening the pan with a wooden spoon; next time cover after the vegetables release moisture.立刻转小火并加 1–2 汤匙水，用木勺刮起锅底；下次等蔬菜出水后再盖锅。
  • The dish is watery and tastes diluted菜出水太多、味道寡淡
    Uncover and stir over medium heat until the liquid reduces; next time use a wide pan and add the vegetables in batches.揭盖中火翻炒至汁液收浓；下次用宽锅，蔬菜分批下锅。
