Pan-Seared Butter-Basted Steak平底锅牛排（黄油淋煎）

A steak with a deep brown crust and a juicy pink centre, made in an ordinary pan. The steak must be dry, the pan very hot, and at the end you spoon foaming butter with garlic and thyme over it. About ten minutes of cooking; the traps are fiddling with it and overcooking.家里也能煎出带深褐色脆壳、里面粉红多汁的牛排：肉要擦干、锅要够热，最后用黄油、蒜和百里香不断淋在肉上。整个过程十分钟出头，难点是别心急翻动和别煎过头。

67 min total分钟 (5 + 12)2 servings份Medium中等Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Striploin steak西冷牛排 520 g · 2 strip loin steaks, about 260 g each and 2.5-3 cm thick纽约客/西冷牛排 2 块，每块约 260 克、厚 2.5–3 厘米 thickness matters more than cut; thin steaks overcook before they crust厚度比品种更重要，太薄会在煎出壳之前就熟透
  • Salt盐 5 g · about 1 tsp fine salt, applied ahead约 1 茶匙细盐，提前涂抹
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper1/2 茶匙现磨黑胡椒
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL) of a high-smoke-point oil1 汤匙（15 mL），耐高温的油
  • Butter黄油 30 g · 2 tbsp, cut in pieces2 汤匙，切块
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · 4 cloves, smashed with skins on4 瓣，带皮拍扁
  • Dried thyme干百里香 2 g · 4 sprigs fresh thyme (or 1/2 tsp dried, added in the last minute)新鲜百里香 4 枝（或 1/2 茶匙干百里香，最后一分钟再放）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Take the steaks out of the fridge and pat the surface completely dry with paper towels.牛排从冷藏取出，用厨房纸把表面完全擦干。
  2. Salt both sides and the edges evenly and leave them on a plate 45 minutes to warm up and absorb the salt.两面和侧边均匀抹盐，放在盘里静置 45 分钟，回温并让盐渗入。
  3. Pat dry once more and add the pepper.再擦干一次，撒上黑胡椒。
  4. Heat a heavy cast-iron or stainless pan over high heat for 3 minutes, add the oil and start when it just begins to smoke.厚底铸铁锅或不锈钢平底锅开大火烧 3 分钟，加油，油面微微冒烟时开始煎。
  5. Lay the steaks in the pan away from you; you should hear a loud sizzle, then leave them alone.牛排朝远离自己的方向放入锅里，听到明显的滋滋声，之后不要动它。
  6. Cook 2 minutes until the bottom is a deep brown crust, then flip.煎 2 分钟，直到底部形成深褐色的壳，然后翻面。
  7. Cook 1.5 minutes more and turn the heat to medium.再煎 1.5 分钟，立刻转中火。
  8. Add the butter, smashed garlic and thyme, and tilt the pan once the butter foams.放入黄油、拍扁的蒜和百里香，黄油起泡后倾斜锅身。
  9. Spoon the foaming butter over the steaks continuously for 1.5-2 minutes.用勺子不停舀起黄油淋在牛排上，淋 1.5–2 分钟。
  10. Push a thermometer into the thickest part and pull the steaks at 57 C (135 F); they will climb to about 63 C (145 F) as they rest.用温度计插进最厚处，读数 57°C（135°F）就离火；静置后会升到约 63°C（145°F）。
  11. Rest the steaks on a rack or plate for 6-8 minutes, uncovered.牛排放在网架或盘子上静置 6–8 分钟，不用盖。
  12. Slice against the grain and spoon over the pan butter.逆着肌肉纹理切片，浇上锅里的黄油汁。

Keys to success成功要点

  • The surface must be dry; any water turns to steam first, so the meat steams instead of browning.牛排表面必须干：水分会在锅里先变成蒸汽，肉就煮而不是煎，煎不出褐色的壳。
  • Salt at least 45 minutes ahead; the salt draws out moisture and is then reabsorbed, seasoning the meat and drying the surface.盐提前 45 分钟以上涂：盐先把水拉出来再被吸回去，肉里调到味，表面也更干。
  • Get the pan properly hot first; a cooler pan makes the steak weep and turn grey without a crust.锅要先烧到够热再下肉：温度不够时肉会出水变灰，壳也出不来。
  • Drop to medium before the butter goes in; at high heat it burns fast and the burnt milk solids taste bitter.淋黄油要转中火：黄油在高温下很快烧焦，焦黑的乳固体会让汁发苦。
  • Rest before cutting; right off the heat the juices sit in the centre and flood the board if you slice early.切之前一定静置：肉汁刚煎完集中在中间，立刻切会流满砧板。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The steak is grey with no crust牛排灰灰的没有壳
    The surface was wet or the pan too cool. Finish this one; next time dry it and preheat 2 minutes longer, and do not crowd the pan.说明表面湿或锅不够热；这块已经煎了就继续煎好，下次擦干并多预热 2 分钟，一次别放太多块。
  • Overcooked and tough煎过头太老
    Slice thin and spoon the pan butter back over; next time pull it 3-5 C early and go by the thermometer, not the clock.切薄片，浇回锅里的黄油汁；下次提前 3–5°C 离火，用温度计而不是计时。
  • The kitchen is smoky and the butter went black满屋油烟、黄油发黑
    Turn off the heat, discard the burnt butter, wipe the pan and baste 1 minute with fresh butter on medium; next time lower the heat before adding butter.关火，倒掉黑黄油，擦锅，补 1 汤匙新黄油在中火下淋 1 分钟；下次放黄油前先降到中火。
