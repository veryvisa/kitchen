Silky Steamed Eggs with Minced Pork肉末蒸水蛋

A home dish found all over China: eggs as smooth as custard over a layer of seasoned pork. Only two things matter, the ratio of egg to water and gentle steaming.南北都有的家常菜，水蛋嫩得像布丁，底下一层肉末带出鲜味。难点只有两个：蛋液和水的比例，以及蒸的火候。

25 min total分钟 (10 + 15)2 servings份Medium中等Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Eggs鸡蛋 200 g · 4 large eggs4 个大鸡蛋
  • Ground pork猪肉馅 100 g · 100 g ground pork猪肉末 100 克
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp (for the pork)半汤匙（腌肉用）
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 2 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp (for the eggs)约 1/3 茶匙（蛋液用）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 3 g · a few drops几滴
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 5 g · a little chopped green onion葱花少许

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Stir the pork with the soy sauce, cornstarch and 1 tbsp water in one direction until sticky.肉末加生抽、淀粉和 1 汤匙水，朝一个方向搅到起胶。
  2. Spread the pork thinly over the bottom of a shallow dish.把肉末薄薄铺在一个浅盘底部。
  3. Beat the eggs with the salt and stir in 300 mL warm water (about 1 part egg to 1.5 parts water).鸡蛋加盐打散，加入 300 mL 温水搅匀（蛋和水约 1 比 1.5）。
  4. Strain the egg mixture through a fine sieve over the pork and skim off the bubbles.蛋液用细筛过滤到肉末上，撇掉表面的气泡。
  5. Cover the dish with a plate or heat-safe wrap.盘子盖一个盘子或耐热保鲜膜。
  6. Once the water boils, put the dish in the steamer and steam on medium-low for 12-15 minutes.水开后放入蒸锅，转中小火蒸 12–15 分钟。
  7. Shake the dish gently; it is done when the centre no longer ripples.轻晃盘子，中间不再流动即熟。
  8. Finish with a few drops of sesame oil and the green onion.淋几滴香油、撒葱花上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Warm water, not cold, helps the eggs set smooth and even.用温水不用冷水，蛋液更容易蒸得均匀光滑。
  • Straining and skimming are what give a mirror-smooth top.过筛和撇泡是表面像镜子一样的关键。
  • Always cover and use medium-low heat; high heat makes it full of holes.一定要盖盖子并用中小火，大火会蒸出蜂窝。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The surface is full of holes表面全是小孔，像蜂窝
    Too much heat or uncovered; steam covered on medium-low, with the lid slightly ajar if needed.火太大或没盖盖子；下次中小火、加盖，锅盖留一条缝也行。
  • Firm and watery蛋太老或出水
    It steamed too long; check at the shortest time and stop when the centre has just set.蒸太久了；按时间先查一次，中间刚凝固就关火。
