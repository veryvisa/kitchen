Japanese Chicken Curry日式咖喱鸡

Japanese curry gets its base from onions, sliced thin and cooked slowly to a caramel brown. Chicken and vegetables simmer until tender, and the roux goes in off the heat so it never clumps. A grated apple and a little soy sauce round out the sweetness.日式咖喱的底味靠洋葱：切成薄片小火炒到焦糖色，鸡肉和蔬菜炖软，最后关火再放咖喱块，不会结块。放了苹果和一点酱油，甜味和香味更圆。

90 min total分钟 (20 + 70)4 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Curry roux另购：咖喱块

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 700 g · 700 g boneless chicken thigh, cut in 4 cm pieces去骨鸡腿肉 700 克，切 4 厘米块
  • Onions洋葱 600 g · 3 medium onions, thinly sliced3 个中等洋葱，切薄片
  • Carrots胡萝卜 250 g · 2 large carrots, cut in rolling chunks2 根大胡萝卜，切滚刀块
  • Potatoes土豆 400 g · 2 medium potatoes, cut in 4 cm chunks2 个中等土豆，切 4 厘米块
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Butter黄油 15 g · 1 tbsp, for the onions1 汤匙，炒洋葱加香
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 3 cloves, grated3 瓣，磨泥
  • Ginger姜 15 g · a thumb-sized piece, grated一块拇指大，磨泥
  • Tomato paste番茄膏 15 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Apples苹果 120 g · half an apple, grated半个苹果，磨泥
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp1/2 汤匙

  • Short-grain rice短粒米(寿司米) 280 g · about 1 1/2 cups dry rice, cooked for 4 bowls米约 1 又 1/2 杯，煮成 4 碗饭

Method做法

  1. Rinse the rice, soak 20 minutes and cook it.米洗好泡 20 分钟，煮成饭。
  2. Heat the oil and butter in a heavy pot over medium heat, add the sliced onions and a pinch of salt.厚底锅中火放油和黄油，下洋葱薄片，加一小撮盐。
  3. Cook over medium-low 25-30 minutes, stirring now and then, until the onions shrink and turn deep caramel brown.中小火炒 25–30 分钟，不时翻动，直到洋葱缩成一小堆并变成深焦糖色。
  4. Stir in the garlic, ginger and tomato paste for 1 minute.加蒜泥、姜泥和番茄酱炒 1 分钟。
  5. Add the chicken, turn the heat to medium-high and stir 4-5 minutes until the outside is white with no raw colour.放入鸡肉，转中大火翻炒 4–5 分钟，到表面变白、没有生肉的颜色。
  6. Add the carrots and 800 mL hot water, bring to a boil and skim off the scum.放胡萝卜，倒入 800 毫升热水，烧开后撇掉浮沫。
  7. Reduce to low, cover and simmer 10 minutes, add the potatoes and simmer 15 more minutes until a chopstick slides through them.转小火，盖盖煮 10 分钟，再放土豆煮 15 分钟，直到土豆用筷子能轻松戳穿。
  8. Turn off the heat, stir in the grated apple and soy sauce and break the roux into pieces over the pot.关火，加入苹果泥和酱油，把咖喱块掰成小块放进汤里。
  9. Stir 2 minutes until the roux has fully dissolved with no lumps.搅 2 分钟，到咖喱块完全化开、没有颗粒。
  10. Return to the lowest heat and cook 10 minutes, stirring, until it thickens enough to coat a spoon.开最小火，边搅边煮 10 分钟，直到变稠，能挂在勺背上。
  11. Check the largest piece of chicken reads 74 C (165 F) and let the curry sit 10 minutes before serving.测一块最大的鸡肉，温度到 74°C（165°F），然后静置 10 分钟再吃。
  12. Spoon the rice into bowls and ladle the curry over it.盛饭，浇上咖喱。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cook the onions at least 25 minutes; the caramel sweetness is the base of the curry and the roux alone cannot supply it.洋葱要炒 25 分钟以上：炒出来的焦糖味是日式咖喱的底，咖喱块给不了这层甜。
  • Add the roux off the heat; in boiling liquid its starch and fat clump, but off the heat it dissolves smoothly.咖喱块要关火再放：沸腾的汤会让淀粉和油脂快速结块，关火后才能顺利化开。
  • Add the potatoes late; cooked too long they crumble and make the curry gluey.土豆晚一点放：煮太久会碎在汤里把咖喱变糊，晚放 15 分钟刚好。
  • It improves overnight as the flavours meld and thicken.放过夜更好吃：咖喱冷却后味道融合，第二天再热会更浓郁。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The curry is too thick咖喱太稠
    Stir in a few tablespoons of hot water over low heat; it thickens as it cools, so leave it a little loose.加几汤匙热水，小火搅匀；咖喱放凉后会更稠，所以煮的时候稍稀一点。
  • The roux clumped咖喱块结成颗粒
    Take it off the heat and press out the lumps with the back of a spoon, or whisk them with hot water in a cup and pour back; next time turn off the heat first.关火，用勺子压碎并搅匀，必要时把这部分舀出来加热水搅匀再倒回；下次一定先关火。
  • It is too salty味道太咸
    Add a cut potato and 200 mL hot water and simmer 10 minutes; the roux is salty already, so do not add salt.加 1 个切块的土豆和 200 毫升热水再煮 10 分钟；日式咖喱块本身含盐不少，所以锅里不要再加盐。
