Harbin-style Borscht with Beef and Cabbage罗宋汤（哈尔滨红汤）

Russian borscht travelled to Harbin and Shanghai and became a beetless red soup coloured with tomatoes and tomato paste. Beef, cabbage, potato and carrot simmer in one pot, thickened at the end with a butter-and-flour roux. Make a big pot at the weekend and it feeds you two or three times.俄罗斯的红菜汤传到哈尔滨和上海，变成了不放甜菜、用番茄和番茄膏煮红的「罗宋汤」。牛肉、卷心菜、土豆、胡萝卜一锅炖，最后用黄油炒面粉增稠，周末煮一大锅，能吃两三顿。

Ingredients食材

  • Beef chuck / stewing beef牛肩肉(炖牛肉用) 600 g · 600 g stewing beef (chuck), in 2 cm cubes炖牛肉（chuck）600 克，切 2 厘米块
  • Green cabbage包菜 400 g · about 1/3 head green cabbage (400 g), chopped卷心菜约 1/3 个（400 克），切块
  • Tomatoes西红柿 400 g · 3 tomatoes, diced3 个番茄，切块
  • Potatoes土豆 400 g · 2 medium potatoes, cubed2 个中等土豆，切块
  • Carrots胡萝卜 150 g · 1 large carrot, cubed1 根大胡萝卜，切块
  • Onions洋葱 200 g · 1 onion, chopped1 个洋葱，切块
  • Celery西芹 80 g · 2 celery sticks, sliced2 根西芹，切段
  • Tomato paste番茄膏 60 g · 4 tbsp tomato paste番茄膏 4 汤匙
  • Butter黄油 25 g · 2 tbsp butter黄油 2 汤匙
  • All-purpose flour面粉 20 g · 2 1/2 tbsp flour面粉 2.5 汤匙
  • Bay leaves月桂叶 1 g · 2 bay leaves香叶 2 片
  • Sugar白糖 12 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Salt盐 8 g · about 1 1/2 tsp (add in stages)约 1.5 茶匙（分次加）
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · black pepper to taste黑胡椒适量
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙

Method做法

  1. Put the beef in cold water, bring to a boil, skim, then drain and rinse.牛肉块冷水下锅，煮开后撇去浮沫，捞出洗净。
  2. Simmer the beef with the bay leaves in 2.5 L water for 60 minutes after it boils.汤锅里放牛肉、香叶和 2.5 升水，煮开后小火炖 60 分钟。
  3. In a frying pan, soften the onion and celery in the oil, about 4 minutes.另取炒锅放油，炒软洋葱和西芹，约 4 分钟。
  4. Add the tomatoes and tomato paste and cook until soft and deep red, about 5 minutes.加番茄和番茄膏，炒到番茄软烂、颜色深红，约 5 分钟。
  5. Tip the tomato mixture into the beef pot.把炒好的番茄洋葱倒进牛肉锅里。
  6. Add the potato and carrot and simmer 15 minutes.放土豆和胡萝卜，小火煮 15 分钟。
  7. Add the cabbage, sugar and half the salt and simmer 15 minutes more.放卷心菜、糖和一半盐，再煮 15 分钟。
  8. In a small pan melt the butter, add the flour and stir on low for 2 minutes until pale golden.小锅里化开黄油，加面粉小火炒 2 分钟到浅黄色。
  9. Whisk a ladle of hot soup into the roux, then stir it back into the pot.舀一勺热汤进面糊里搅匀，再倒回汤锅。
  10. Simmer 5 minutes until slightly thickened and season with the remaining salt and the pepper.煮 5 分钟到汤微稠，用剩下的盐和黑胡椒调味。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cook the tomatoes and paste on their own first; that is where the red colour and deep sweet-sour flavour come from.番茄和番茄膏要先单独炒透，汤才红、才有浓郁的酸甜味。
  • The butter roux is what gives the Harbin style its body.黄油面糊是哈尔滨风味的关键，让汤浓稠挂勺。
  • It tastes even better the next day; keeps 3 days chilled or 2 months frozen in portions.隔夜更好喝，冷藏可放 3 天，分装冷冻可放 2 个月。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The soup looks orange, not red汤颜色发黄不红
    Too little tomato paste or not cooked enough; fry the paste until it darkens before adding liquid.番茄膏不够或没炒透；番茄膏要炒到颜色变深再加汤。
  • The roux went lumpy面糊结块
    Poured straight into boiling soup it lumps; loosen it with a ladle of soup first.直接倒进滚汤会结块；先用一勺热汤把面糊搅开再倒回去。
  • The beef is chewy牛肉嚼不动
    It needs longer; chuck wants at least 90 minutes in total, until a chopstick slides in easily.炖的时间不够；chuck 至少要炖 90 分钟，筷子能轻松扎透再关火。
