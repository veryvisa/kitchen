Scallion Oil Noodles葱油拌面

A classic Shanghai noodle dish built from just scallions, neutral oil, soy sauce, sugar and wheat noodles in about 25 minutes. Success comes down to frying the scallions slowly over low heat until dry and golden to medium brown, then adding the soy sauce right away — residual heat keeps cooking them, so waiting until they look deep brown or black means they have already turned bitter.只靠小葱、食用油、酱油和细面就能做出的上海小吃，约二十五分钟就能吃上热面。整碗面的成败全在熬葱油：必须用小火慢慢榨干葱段水分，炸到金黄到中棕色就下酱汁关火——油余温还会继续加热，等炸到深棕甚至发黑就已经迟了，会发苦。

26 min total分钟 (8 + 18)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dark soy sauce另购：老抽

Ingredients食材

  • Wheat noodles挂面/面条 200 g · 200 g dried thin wheat noodles细干小麦面（或阳春面）200 克 use 280 g if buying fresh thin noodles; follow package time, usually 3-4 minutes若买鲜细面则用 280 克；按包装时间煮，通常 3-4 分钟
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 120 g · 1 large bunch scallions (about 120 g), washed, patted bone-dry and cut in 6 cm lengths小葱 1 大把（约 120 克），洗净彻底擦干后切 6 厘米段 keep whites and greens separate, and dry completely to prevent splatter; split any thick whites lengthwise so they fry evenly葱白与葱绿分开，水分擦干才不溅油；葱白较粗的纵向剖开，受热才均匀
  • Canola oil菜籽油 82 g · 6 tbsp (90 mL); use a small, narrow, deep saucepan so the scallions are mostly submerged6 汤匙（90 mL），选小而窄深的奶锅让葱段大半浸在油里 the pot should be at least 3 times the oil's volume so the sauce does not foam over when added锅要比油量宽至少三倍，倒酱汁时才不会泡沫溢锅
  • Soy sauce生抽 54 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Put a large pot of water on to boil for the noodles; wash the scallions, pat completely dry with paper towels, separate whites from greens and cut into 6 cm lengths.先烧一大锅水备煮面用；小葱洗净后用厨房纸彻底擦干，葱白和葱绿分开，切成约 6 厘米段。
  2. Stir the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce and sugar together in a small bowl.小碗把生抽、老抽和白糖拌匀备用。
  3. Using a small, narrow, deep saucepan, put the oil and scallion whites in cold and fry over medium-low heat for about 4 minutes until pale gold at the edges.选小而窄深的奶锅，冷锅倒油，放入葱白，开中小火慢炸约 4 分钟至边缘微黄。
  4. Add the green tops, reduce to low and fry for about 6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until all pieces shrivel and turn golden to medium brown (a shade lighter than the finished colour you want — residual heat will keep darkening them).放入葱绿转小火慢熬约 6 分钟，其间不时拨动，直到全部葱段干瘪、呈金黄到中棕色（颜色比炸好的样子浅一点，关火后余温还会继续加深）。
  5. Turn the heat to its lowest setting, carefully pour the soy-sugar mixture in from the side of the pot (the pot needs to be wide enough, as the sauce will bubble up when it hits the oil), simmer 20 to 30 seconds until the sugar dissolves, then shut off the heat.把火调到最小，沿锅边小心倒入酱油糖汁（锅要够宽，油遇酱汁会冒泡），微沸 20 至 30 秒至糖融化后立刻关火。
  6. The water should be boiling by now: add the dried thin noodles and cook over high heat for about 4 minutes (or per package time) until tender with no raw core — time this to finish around when the scallion oil does.烧开的水这时应该已经可以下面：放入干细面大火煮约 4 分钟（或按包装时间）至熟透无硬芯，与熬葱油的时间大致同步。
  7. Lift the noodles out and drain well (do not rinse), then divide between two bowls and toss right away while hot — waiting lets them clump.捞出面条沥干水分（不用过凉水），立刻分装进两个碗中趁热拌，拖久了面会黏成一坨。
  8. Divide the scallion oil sauce and crisp scallions evenly between the two bowls, toss well, and eat immediately.把葱油酱汁和脆葱段平均分到两碗，拌匀立刻食用。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Wet scallions splatter violently and steam instead of frying crisp, so drying them thoroughly is essential.小葱带水下锅不但会剧烈溅油，还会让葱段被水汽焖软、迟迟炸不香，因此擦干是第一步。
  • Dense scallion whites hold more water than the leafy greens; starting the whites 4 minutes ahead helps both finish golden-brown together.葱白厚实水分多、葱绿薄易焦，先炸葱白 4 分钟再下葱绿，才能让两段同时酥香而不焦黑。
  • Residual heat in the oil keeps cooking the scallions, so add the sauce once they are golden to medium brown; waiting until they look deep brown or black means the oil has already turned bitter.热油余温很高，葱段炸到金黄到中棕色就该倒酱汁并尽快关火，等炸到深棕甚至发黑就已经发苦了。
  • Toss the noodles the moment they leave the pot, because resting noodles clump and resist the oil.面条捞出立刻拌油最好吃，时间一拖面条会黏成一坨，酱汁也挂不匀。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The scallion oil tastes bitter葱油吃起来发苦
    The greens have burnt, and bitter oil cannot be rescued — strain it out and start over. Next time add the soy mixture once the scallions are golden to medium brown (a shade lighter than the finished colour you want), not deep brown or black.是葱绿熬黑了，发苦的油救不回来，整锅倒掉重做；下次炸到金黄到中棕色（比想要的成品颜色浅一点）就倒酱汁关火，别等到深棕或发黑。
  • The noodles clumped or felt dry面条干涩坨在一起
    Stir in 1 tbsp hot noodle cooking water to loosen and gloss the strands; next time time the noodles and scallion oil to finish together, and toss the noodles the moment they drain instead of rinsing or letting them sit.往碗里加 1 汤匙热煮面水拌开即可润泽顺滑；下次把煮面和熬葱油的时间对齐，面一出锅立刻拌，不要过凉水或放着等。
  • Too pale and mild味道偏淡、颜色不够深
    Usually the heat was too high when frying, the sauce was not reduced enough, or undrained noodles diluted it; stir in 1 tsp more dark soy and 1 tsp light soy, and make sure to drain the noodles well next time.多半是炸葱油时火太大、糖酱没熬够或面没沥干稀释了酱汁；直接补 1 茶匙老抽和 1 茶匙生抽拌匀，下次确认面控干水分。
