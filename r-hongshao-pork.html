Red-Braised Pork Belly红烧肉

Good red-braised pork is rich without being greasy, the cubes trembling and coated in a glossy amber glaze. Two things decide it: caramelising the sugar to amber, not black, and a long gentle simmer followed by a quick high-heat reduction. It is not hard, only patient.好的红烧肉肥而不腻，肉块颤巍巍、汤汁收成亮红的糖衣。成败在两处：糖色要炒到琥珀色而不是黑，以及小火慢炖够时间，最后大火收汁。步骤不难，主要是耐心。

110 min total分钟 (15 + 95)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, Dried chili另购：老抽、料酒(绍兴)、干辣椒

Ingredients食材

  • Pork belly五花肉 500 g · about 500 g skin-on pork belly带皮五花肉约 500 克 Costco sells skin-on bellyCostco 有带皮五花
  • Ginger姜 20 g · 6 slices, 3 of them for the blanching water6 片，其中 3 片焯水用
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 40 g · 3 stalks, tied into a knot3 根，打结
  • Sugar白糖 36 g · 3 tbsp, for the caramel3 汤匙，炒糖色用
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 12 g · 2 tsp, for color2 茶匙，给颜色
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 60 g · 4 tbsp (60 mL), half for blanching and half for the braise4 汤匙（60 mL），一半焯水一半炖煮
  • Bay leaves月桂叶 1 g · 2 leaves2 片
  • Dried chili干辣椒 2 g · 2 pieces, optional2 个，可不放
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp, adjusted at the end约 1/3 茶匙，最后按口味补

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Cut the belly into cubes of about 3 cm; slicing skin-side down gives neater pieces.五花肉切成约 3 厘米见方的块，皮朝下切更整齐。
  2. Put the pork, 3 ginger slices and 2 tbsp wine in a pot of cold water that just covers the meat.肉块、3 片姜和 2 汤匙料酒冷水下锅，水刚没过肉。
  3. Bring to a boil, simmer 3 minutes, skim the scum, then lift the pork out and rinse it with hot water.大火煮开后再煮 3 分钟，撇掉浮沫，捞出肉块用热水冲净。
  4. Drain the pork and pat it dry with paper towels.肉块沥干，用厨房纸吸干表面水分。
  5. In a clean heavy pot, add 1 tbsp oil and 3 tbsp sugar over medium-low heat and stir constantly.干净的锅里放 1 汤匙油和 3 汤匙糖，中小火不停搅动。
  6. The sugar melts, then bubbles hard; the moment it turns amber, add the pork, about 3 minutes in, because dark brown means bitter.糖先化开再冒大泡，变成琥珀色时马上下肉，约 3 分钟，颜色发深发黑就苦了。
  7. Toss for 2 minutes so every cube is coated.翻炒 2 分钟，让每块肉都裹上糖色。
  8. Add the remaining 2 tbsp wine, the soy sauce and dark soy, and stir.倒入料酒 2 汤匙、生抽、老抽，翻匀。
  9. Add the rest of the ginger, the scallion knot, bay leaves and chilies, then hot water to just cover the meat.加入剩下的姜片、葱结、香叶和干辣椒，倒入刚好没过肉的热水。
  10. Bring to a boil, cover, drop to the lowest heat and simmer for 60 minutes, stirring once midway.大火煮开，盖上锅盖，转最小火炖 60 分钟，中途翻动一次。
  11. Uncover, remove the scallion knot and bay leaves, and turn the heat to high.打开锅盖，捞出葱结和香叶，转大火收汁。
  12. Keep turning the pork for 8 to 10 minutes until the liquid is thick and glossy and clings to the meat; taste, then adjust the salt.不停翻动 8 到 10 分钟，汤汁变得浓稠发亮、能挂在肉上，尝一下再补盐。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Starting the pork in cold water draws out impurities gradually; rinse it with hot, not cold, water or the meat tightens.肉冷水下锅焯水，血水才能慢慢出来，腥味少；焯完再用热水冲，不要用冷水，免得肉收紧变硬。
  • Add hot water, not cold, so the temperature in the pot does not drop and the meat does not seize.炖煮时加热水而不是凉水，锅里温度不掉，肉不会越炖越紧。
  • Keep the heat low and keep stirring; sugar goes from pale to amber in seconds, so it is safer to add the pork a little early.糖色炒的时候火要小、手要勤，颜色从浅黄到琥珀只差几十秒，宁可早一点下肉。
  • Stay at the stove during the final reduction; once the sauce thickens it scorches quickly.最后收汁不要走开，汤汁变稠后很容易糊底。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The dish is dark and bitter做出来发苦发黑
    The caramel went too far. Add a few tablespoons of hot water and a little sugar to rescue the balance, and next time add the pork at amber.糖色炒过头了，没法挽救；加两三汤匙热水、少许糖，重新调到能接受的甜度，下次看到琥珀色就下肉。
  • The pork is still firm after long cooking肉炖了很久还是硬
    A hard boil tightens meat. Keep only tiny bubbles breaking the surface and give it another 20 to 30 minutes.火太大、汤沸腾得太厉害会让肉收紧；保持锅里只有细小气泡，再炖 20 到 30 分钟。
  • The sauce is thin and will not coat the pork汤汁太稀不挂肉
    Uncover, raise the heat and keep turning. Skip the starch; the collagen from the skin thickens it naturally.转大火不盖锅盖继续翻动收汁；不要加淀粉，靠肉皮里的胶质自然变稠。

Good to know适合 / 需注意

  • About 987 mg sodium per serving (about 43% of the 2,300 mg daily reference), on the high side; use less soy sauce or salt, and pair with unsalted sides.每份钠约 987 mg（约占每日参考 2300 mg 的 43%），偏高；少放酱油或盐，或搭配无盐主食与蔬菜。

Pork belly is high in fat, and the sugar adds more energy, so this is a celebration dish rather than an everyday one. Serve it with plenty of vegetables and rice, and skim the surface fat before serving to cut some of it.五花肉脂肪含量高，是一道偏重口味的聚餐菜，热量主要来自肥肉和糖，按四人份计，配大量蔬菜和米饭更均衡；吃之前撇掉浮油能减一些脂肪。
