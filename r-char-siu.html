Cantonese Char Siu Pork蜜汁叉烧

Char siu gets its color and flavor from a sweet, savory marinade, but the glaze can scorch before the pork is done. Pork shoulder and a hot oven make it easy to get browned edges while keeping the slices juicy.叉烧的家常难点是让肉入味、表面上色，又不把甜蜜汁烤焦。用烤箱和猪肩肉就能做出带焦香边缘、切开仍多汁的一盘。

535 min total分钟 (20 + 35)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Hoisin sauce, Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, Chinese five-spice powder另购：海鲜酱、老抽、料酒(绍兴)、五香粉

Ingredients食材

  • Pork shoulder猪梅花肉 900 g · 900 g pork shoulder, cut into strips about 4 cm wide猪肩肉约 900 克，切成宽约 4 厘米的长条
  • Hoisin sauce海鲜酱 45 g · 3 tbsp3 汤匙
  • Honey蜂蜜 63 g · 3 tbsp: 42 g for the marinade and 21 g for glazing3 汤匙：腌料用 42 克，刷面用 21 克
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp1/2 汤匙
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 30 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Brown sugar红糖 25 g · about 2 tbsp, packed约 2 汤匙，压实
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 4 cloves, minced4 瓣，切末
  • Ginger姜 10 g · minced切末
  • Chinese five-spice powder五香粉 2 g · about 1 tsp约 1 茶匙
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp约 1/3 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Stir together the hoisin, soy sauces, cooking wine, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, five-spice and salt. Reserve a clean portion of this sauce in a separate container and refrigerate it for glazing. Stir 42 g of honey into the remaining sauce to make the marinade.把海鲜酱、生抽、老抽、料酒、红糖、蒜、姜、五香粉和盐搅匀；取出一份干净酱汁放入单独容器冷藏备用，剩余酱汁中加入 42 克蜂蜜，作为腌料。
  2. Coat each pork strip with the marinade, cover and refrigerate for 8 hours. Use only the clean, reserved sauce for glazing, never marinade that has touched raw pork.把猪肉逐条放入腌料中抹匀，盖好冷藏 8 小时；只使用未接触生猪肉的酱汁进行刷面。
  3. Preheat the oven to 220°C and line a baking tray with foil before setting a rack on top.烤箱预热至 220°C，并在烤盘上铺锡纸、放上烤架。
  4. Set the pork on the rack and roast on the middle shelf for 15 minutes, until the edges begin to brown.把猪肉放在烤架上，中层烤 15 分钟，直到边缘开始变褐。
  5. Remove the refrigerated clean glazing sauce, turn the pork, brush it over the strips and roast for 10 minutes.取出冷藏的干净刷酱，翻面后刷在猪肉表面，再烤 10 分钟。
  6. Brush on another layer of the clean reserved sauce and roast for 5–10 minutes.再刷一层冷藏的干净刷酱，烤 5–10 分钟。
  7. Measure the thickest part with a food thermometer and roast until it reaches at least 71°C.用食物温度计测量肉最厚处，烤至达到至少 71°C。
  8. Switch the oven to Broil and, if necessary, move the rack farther from the heating element. Brush with the remaining 21 g honey and broil for 1–2 minutes until glossy, watching constantly to prevent burning.将烤箱切换至 Broil（上火烧烤）模式，必要时将烤架移至距离上方加热管较远的位置；刷上剩余 21 克蜂蜜，烤约 1–2 分钟至表面发亮，期间密切观察，避免烤焦。
  9. Transfer the char siu to a board, rest for 5 minutes and slice across the grain.把叉烧移到砧板上静置 5 分钟，再逆纹切片。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Pork shoulder has enough fat to stay moist; keep the strips a similar thickness so they cook evenly.猪肩肉带些脂肪，长条厚薄尽量一致，较容易同时烤熟。
  • Make sure every side touches the marinade; an overnight chill also gives the flavor time to even out.腌制时让每一面都接触酱汁，冷藏过夜也可以，入味会更均匀。
  • A rack lets hot air reach the strips all around, while foil catches drips for easier cleanup.烤架让热气接触肉条四周，锡纸接住滴汁，清理也更方便。
  • Brush on honey at the end and broil briefly; its sugars can go from caramelized to bitter and black quickly.蜂蜜最后才刷并短时间上火，糖分很容易在高温下从焦香变成苦黑。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The outside is black before the center is cooked叉烧表面焦黑，里面还没熟
    Move it to the middle shelf, turn off the broiler and finish at 200°C until the center reaches 71°C; next time broil only at the end and watch closely.立刻移到中层并取消上火，降至 200°C 继续烤到肉心达到 71°C；下次把上火留到最后并全程看着。
  • The char siu is dry and tough叉烧发干发柴
    Use marbled shoulder and a thermometer next time, remove it at 71°C and rest for 5 minutes, and slice across the grain.下次选带脂肪的猪肩肉并用温度计检查，达到 71°C 后取出静置 5 分钟；切片时逆着肉纹。
  • The char siu looks pale and the glaze is not glossy叉烧颜色浅、表面不黏亮
    Space the strips apart on the rack, then brush on a thin layer of honey and broil briefly rather than overcooking the whole strip.确认烤架上的肉条没有互相挨着，最后刷薄薄一层蜂蜜并短暂上火，避免把整条肉烤过头。
