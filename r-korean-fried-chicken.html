Korean Fried Chicken韩式炸鸡

Korean fried chicken stands out for its paper-thin crisp crust coated in a glossy sweet-and-spicy glaze over juicy meat. Making it well at home comes down to three details: a light cornstarch batter, a double fry for extra crunch, and a quick toss in sauce off the heat so the crust stays crisp.韩式炸鸡的魅力在于薄脆的外壳裹上甜辣亮泽的酱汁，咬开后依然多汁。家里做的关键有三点：用玉米淀粉和面粉调出轻薄面衣、先炸熟定型再高温复炸逼出多余水汽，以及在最后关火后再快速裹酱，避免脆壳变软。

55 min total分钟 (15 + 20)3 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Method做法

  1. Pat the chicken wings completely dry with paper towels and place them in a bowl.用厨房纸把鸡翅表面完全擦干，放入大碗。
  2. Toss the wings with the cooking wine, 3/4 tsp salt, and black pepper, then marinate for 20 minutes. Pat them dry again with paper towels afterward — this step decides whether the batter sticks.加入料酒、3/4 茶匙盐和黑胡椒拌匀，腌制 20 分钟；腌好后再用厨房纸按压吸干表面水分，这一步决定面衣会不会脱落。
  3. Whisk the gochujang, ketchup, honey, brown sugar, soy sauce, rice vinegar, garlic, and 2 tbsp water in a small bowl until smooth (save the sesame oil for the very end, off the heat).在小碗中把韩式辣酱、番茄酱、蜂蜜、红糖、生抽、米醋、蒜末和 2 汤匙清水搅匀（香油留到最后关火再加）。
  4. In a wide bowl, stir the cornstarch, flour, and the remaining 1/4 tsp salt with about 100 mL cold water into a thin, pourable batter that just coats a spoon.另取宽碗，将玉米淀粉、面粉和剩下 1/4 茶匙盐加约 100 毫升冷水搅成稀薄、能流动、刚好挂住勺子的面糊。
  5. Heat the canola oil in a deep pot over medium-high heat to 170°C (a deep-fry thermometer is most reliable; as a backup, a wooden chopstick tip should bubble steadily when dipped in).深锅倒入菜籽油，中高火加热至 170°C（有油炸温度计最准；没有就用木筷插入，周围冒出细密连续油泡作为参考）。
  6. The batter settles quickly, so stir it again before dipping; dip half the wings into the thin batter, let excess drip off, and fry 5 minutes without crowding until pale golden and set.面糊沉淀快，下鸡翅前重新搅一次；先取一半鸡翅挂一层薄面糊，分摊入锅中炸 5 分钟至外壳定形呈浅金黄色。
  7. Transfer the wings to a wire rack to rest while frying the second batch (the first batch will rest about 8 to 10 minutes).捞出放在晾网上静置，同时炸完第二批鸡翅（第一批大约会歇 8–10 分钟）。
  8. Skim any loose bits of batter off the oil, raise it to 185°C, and double-fry all the wings in two batches for 2 to 3 minutes until deep golden and crisp. Check the thickest drumette near the bone with a thermometer for 74°C, and fry 1 more minute if it has not reached that.用漏勺撇掉油面上的碎面衣渣，把油温升至 185°C，分两批复炸全部鸡翅 2–3 分钟，至深金黄酥脆；用温度计在最厚翅根的骨边确认达到 74°C，没到就续炸 1 分钟。
  9. Drain the double-fried wings back on the wire rack.把复炸好的鸡翅捞回晾网控油。
  10. Pour the mixed sauce into a small saucepan and simmer over medium-low heat for about 1 minute, stirring and watching the texture: keep cooking if it is thinner than ketchup, or stir in 1 tbsp water if it gets thicker than ketchup.另取一口小锅倒入调好的酱汁，中小火煮约 1 分钟，边搅边看，比番茄酱还稀就再煮一会儿，比番茄酱稠就加 1 汤匙水调开。
  11. Turn off the heat, stir in the sesame oil, add the hot wings, and toss briskly for 15 seconds until thinly glazed. Because the glaze is already thick and the toss is quick, the crust barely touches any liquid and stays crisp.关火，拌入香油，倒入热鸡翅快速翻拌 15 秒，让每块均匀裹上一层亮泽薄酱——酱汁本身收得浓、裹酱动作又快，水分接触脆壳的时间很短，壳才不会被泡软。
  12. Transfer to a platter, sprinkle with sesame seeds and green onion, and serve immediately. Korean pickled radish (치킨무) on the side is the traditional way to cut the richness.装盘撒上熟白芝麻和葱花，趁热立刻食用；配一点韩式甜萝卜（치킨무）解腻很地道。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cornstarch gives a shattering crust while a small amount of flour helps the batter cling; cold water limits gluten so the shell stays light.玉米淀粉负责酥脆感，少量面粉帮助面糊挂在鸡皮上；用冷水调糊能减少面筋形成，炸出来的壳更轻薄。
  • The first fry only sets the shape; the second fry is what actually cooks the wings through. Resting between fries lets internal moisture move outward so the hotter second fry drives it off, keeping the crust crisp under the glaze.初炸只负责定型，复炸才真正炸透炸熟；两次之间歇一会儿能让鸡肉内部水汽先散到表层再被高温瞬间逼出，裹酱后也不容易塌软。
  • Keep minced ginger and garlic out of the marinade; bits clinging to the skin tend to scorch and turn bitter at the 185°C second fry, so save all the garlic flavor for the sauce.腌料里不放姜蒜碎：带皮鸡翅复炸到 185°C 时，粘在表面的姜蒜碎很容易烧焦发苦，蒜味全部留在酱汁里更稳妥。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The crust turns soggy quickly外壳很快变软不脆
    Next time rest the wings long enough on a rack before the second fry; to re-crisp soggy pieces now, use a 220°C oven for about 8 to 10 minutes or a 190°C air fryer for about 4 to 5 minutes — watch closely if they are already sauced, since the sugar can scorch.下次初炸后在晾网上静置够时间再高温复炸；这次已软的炸鸡，烤箱复烤 220°C 约 8–10 分钟或空气炸锅 190°C 约 4–5 分钟都能脆回来，留意已裹酱的话糖分在高温下容易焦。
  • The coating browned too fast while the inside stayed pink表皮炸糊了但里面还没熟
    Usually the oil ran hot or the wings were on the large side; next time hold the first fry near 170°C and always verify 74°C at the bone with a thermometer after the second fry. Finish undercooked pieces now in a 190°C oven for 12 to 15 minutes, tenting loosely with foil if the crust is already dark.常见原因是油温偏高或鸡翅偏大；下次初炸稳定控制在 170°C 左右，复炸后一定用温度计确认骨边达到 74°C，这次没熟的进 190°C 烤箱续烤 12–15 分钟（表皮太深可松松盖一层锡纸）。
  • The batter peeled off in patches面衣大片脱落
    Moisture on the skin or overcrowding drops the oil temperature; pat the marinated wings dry again right before battering, and wait 1 minute before nudging them in the oil.鸡翅表面有水或一次下太多导致油温骤降；下锅前务必把腌好的鸡翅再擦干一次，入锅头 1 分钟不要急着拨动。
