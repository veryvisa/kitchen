Sheet-Pan Salmon with Potatoes and Broccoli三文鱼烤盘餐

A whole dinner on one tray. The potatoes go in first, the broccoli and salmon join later, and a honey-mustard glaze goes on the fish. The key is staggering the timing, because the salmon needs only about a dozen minutes.一个烤盘做完一餐：土豆先烤，西兰花和三文鱼后放，蜂蜜芥末汁刷在鱼上。重点是不同食材放进去的时间不同，三文鱼只要十来分钟，别一起放。

50 min total分钟 (15 + 35)4 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Salmon fillet三文鱼排 700 g · 4 skin-on salmon fillets, about 175 g each and 3 cm thick带皮三文鱼 4 块，每块约 175 克、厚约 3 厘米
  • Potatoes土豆 600 g · baby potatoes or yellow potatoes, cut in 2 cm pieces小土豆（或黄土豆）切 2 厘米块
  • Broccoli西兰花 400 g · 1 large head of broccoli, in small florets1 个大西兰花，切成小朵
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Dijon mustard第戎芥末酱 20 g · a generous tbsp of Dijon mustard1 汤匙多一点（法式芥末）
  • Honey蜂蜜 21 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 3 cloves, grated3 瓣，磨泥
  • Lemons柠檬 100 g · 1 lemon: half juiced, half sliced1 个：一半挤汁，一半切片
  • Salt盐 6 g · about 1 tsp, divided约 1 茶匙，分开用
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · 1/2 tsp1/2 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Preheat the oven to 220 C (425 F) with a rack in the middle and line a large sheet pan with parchment.烤箱预热到 220°C（425°F），烤架放在中层，烤盘铺烘焙纸。
  2. Toss the potatoes with 2 tbsp olive oil and 3 g salt and spread them over one half of the tray.土豆块加 2 汤匙橄榄油和 3 克盐拌匀，平铺在烤盘一侧。
  3. Roast the potatoes alone for 15 minutes.先烤土豆 15 分钟。
  4. Meanwhile toss the broccoli with the remaining 1 tbsp oil and 1 g salt.同时把西兰花加剩下的 1 汤匙油和 1 克盐拌匀。
  5. Whisk the mustard, honey, garlic, half the lemon juice and the pepper into a glaze.芥末、蜂蜜、蒜泥、一半柠檬汁和黑胡椒调成酱汁。
  6. Pat the salmon dry, season with 2 g salt, set it skin-side down and brush with the glaze.三文鱼用厨房纸擦干，撒 2 克盐，鱼皮朝下放在盘子里，刷上酱汁。
  7. Pull out the tray, turn the potatoes, push them to the edges, and put the salmon in the middle with the broccoli in the gaps.从烤箱取出烤盘，土豆翻面，推到两边，中间放三文鱼，西兰花放在空位。
  8. Return to the oven for 11-13 minutes, until the fish flakes easily with a fork.放回烤箱烤 11–13 分钟，直到鱼肉用叉子能轻松掰开。
  9. A thermometer in the thickest part should read 70 C (158 F) for fully cooked fish.温度计插进鱼最厚处，读数 70°C（158°F）就是熟透。
  10. Take it out, squeeze over the remaining lemon juice, add the lemon slices and serve straight from the tray.取出，淋剩下的柠檬汁，摆上柠檬片，直接在烤盘上分餐。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Give the potatoes a head start; they cook far slower than fish and broccoli, so putting everything in together leaves the potatoes raw.土豆先烤 15 分钟：土豆比鱼和西兰花慢熟得多，同时放，鱼熟了土豆还是生的。
  • Dry the fish before glazing; surface water dilutes the glaze and stops the top from browning.鱼要擦干再刷酱：多余水分会把酱汁冲淡，鱼表面也上不了色。
  • Honey scorches easily, so the glaze goes only on the fish and bakes for a short time.蜂蜜糖分容易焦，所以酱汁只刷在鱼上、烤的时间也短。
  • Pick similar-thickness fillets; thin ones finish first, so put them near the edge and take them out early.每块鱼的厚度尽量一致：薄的先熟，可以把尾部较薄的那块放在烤盘边缘早点取出。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The salmon is dry三文鱼太干
    Squeeze lemon and a little olive oil over it; next time check 2 minutes earlier and pull it as soon as it turns opaque.淋柠檬汁和一点橄榄油拌着土豆吃；下次提早 2 分钟看，鱼肉刚变成不透明就取出。
  • The potatoes still have a hard centre土豆还有硬心
    Take out the fish and broccoli and roast the potatoes 8-10 minutes longer; next time cut them smaller.先取出鱼和西兰花，土豆继续烤 8–10 分钟；下次切小一点。
  • The broccoli burned西兰花烤焦发黑
    Pick off the blackened florets; next time set the broccoli toward the middle of the tray or add it when the fish goes in.把焦的小朵挑掉；下次把西兰花放在烤盘中间，或者在鱼放进去的同时再加。
