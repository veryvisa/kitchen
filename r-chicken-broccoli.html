Chicken and Broccoli Stir-fry鸡胸肉炒西兰花

Chicken breast turns dry fast. A thin coat of starch and a little oil keeps it tender, and blanching the broccoli first keeps it bright and crisp. Twelve minutes at the stove, and it holds up well in a lunchbox.鸡胸肉最怕炒柴。这道菜用淀粉和一点油"上浆"，西兰花先焯再炒，十来分钟出锅，肉嫩菜脆，带饭凉了也好吃。

27 min total分钟 (15 + 12)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless skinless chicken breast鸡胸肉 300 g · about 1 large breast, sliced thin约 1 大块（切薄片）
  • Broccoli西兰花 350 g · 1 large head, cut into small florets1 大颗，掰成小朵
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 3 cloves, sliced3 瓣，切片
  • Ginger姜 8 g · 3 slices3 片
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL), for the marinade1 汤匙（15 mL），腌肉用
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL), for the marinade1 汤匙（15 mL），腌肉用
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 9 g · 3 tsp (2 for the chicken, 1 for the sauce)3 茶匙（腌肉 2 茶匙，调汁 1 茶匙）
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL), for the sauce1 汤匙（15 mL），调汁用
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Salt盐 2 g · 2 g (about 1/3 tsp, for the blanching water and seasoning)2 克（约 1/3 茶匙，焯水和调味）
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 0.5 g · a pinch少许

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Slice the chicken across the grain into pieces about 3 mm thick.鸡胸肉逆着纹理切成约 3 毫米厚的薄片。
  2. Mix the chicken with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp wine, 2 tsp cornstarch, the pepper and 1 tsp of the oil.鸡片加生抽 1 汤匙、料酒 1 汤匙、淀粉 2 茶匙、黑胡椒和 1 茶匙油，抓匀。
  3. Cover and marinate for 10 minutes.盖好腌 10 分钟。
  4. Make the sauce: stir 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tsp cornstarch and 3 tbsp water together.调汁：蚝油 1 汤匙、淀粉 1 茶匙、清水 3 汤匙搅匀。
  5. Boil a pot of water with 1 g salt and blanch the broccoli for 60 seconds.烧一锅水，加盐 1 克，水开后放入西兰花焯 60 秒。
  6. Lift the broccoli out, rinse it under cold water right away and drain well.捞出西兰花，立刻过冷水，沥干。
  7. Heat a wok or large skillet and add 1 tbsp oil, swirling to coat.炒锅烧热，倒 1 汤匙油，转一圈。
  8. Spread the chicken in the pan and leave it alone for 30 seconds.放入鸡片摊开，不要翻动，煎 30 秒。
  9. Flip and stir until it is about 80% cooked and white outside, then take it out.翻面炒到八成熟、表面发白，盛出。
  10. Add the second tablespoon of oil, then the garlic and ginger, and stir for about 15 seconds until fragrant.锅里补 1 汤匙油，下蒜片和姜片炒出香味，约 15 秒。
  11. Add the broccoli and stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute.倒入西兰花，大火翻炒 1 分钟。
  12. Return the chicken and pour in the sauce.鸡片回锅，倒入调好的汁。
  13. Stir for about 1 minute until the sauce thickens and coats everything and the chicken is no longer pink in the middle; season with the last 1 g of salt and serve.翻炒约 1 分钟，汁变稠裹住食材，鸡肉中心不再发粉，加盐 1 克调味后装盘。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Always slice across the grain and thin; shorter fibres mean tender meat.一定逆着纹理切薄片，纤维被切断，肉才嫩。
  • Take the chicken out when it is 80% cooked and let the final minute finish it, so it does not toughen.鸡肉先炒到八成熟就盛出，最后回锅再加热，这样不会老。
  • Shocking blanched broccoli in cold water keeps it green and crisp; too long in the pot turns it yellow and soft.西兰花焯水后过冷水，颜色翠绿、口感脆；焯太久会发黄变软。
  • Chicken is safe at 74°C (165°F) inside; if unsure, cut one piece open.鸡肉中心温度达到 74°C（165°F）才算熟，不确定就切开一块看。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The chicken is dry and chewy鸡肉又干又柴
    Next time slice thinner, never skip the starch and oil in the marinade, and take it out at 80% cooked; keep the pan hot and do not crowd it.下次切得再薄一点、腌的时候一定放淀粉和油，炒到八成熟就起锅；锅要够热但肉不要一次放太多。
  • The dish is watery汤汁很多、菜出水
    Drain the broccoli well, use higher heat and cook in batches; if the sauce is thin, add half a teaspoon of cornstarch mixed with a spoon of water.西兰花焯水后要沥干；锅不够热会出水，改大火并分批炒；汁稀就加半茶匙淀粉加一勺水的水淀粉。
  • The broccoli turned yellow and soft西兰花发黄发软
    Blanch no longer than 90 seconds, chill it at once, and stir-fry it for only a minute afterwards.焯水不要超过 90 秒并立刻过冷水；回锅后只炒 1 分钟。
