Irish Beef Stew爱尔兰炖牛肉

Beef chuck simmers slowly in dark Irish stout and beef stock, while carrots, onions and potatoes take on the rich broth. Browning the meat well and keeping the stew at a gentle simmer are the main keys.牛肩肉在深色爱尔兰啤酒和牛肉汤中慢炖，胡萝卜、洋葱与土豆吸收了醇厚的汤汁。先把肉煎出焦香、再耐心小火炖软，是这锅炖菜的关键。

210 min total分钟 (25 + 185)6 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Irish stout另购：爱尔兰黑啤

Ingredients食材

  • Beef chuck / stewing beef牛肩肉(炖牛肉用) 1000 g · about 1 kg beef chuck, cut into 4 cm cubes牛肩肉约 1 千克，切 4 厘米块
  • Irish stout爱尔兰黑啤 440 g · 1 can stout (about 440 mL)1 罐黑啤（约 440 mL）
  • Beef broth牛肉汤 700 g · about 3 cups beef stock约 3 杯牛肉汤
  • Potatoes土豆 500 g · about 3 medium potatoes, cut into large chunks约 3 个中等土豆，切大块
  • Carrots胡萝卜 250 g · 3 carrots, cut into thick slices3 根，切厚片
  • Onions洋葱 200 g · 1 large onion, cut into chunks1 个大洋葱，切块
  • Celery西芹 120 g · 2 stalks, sliced2 根，切片
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 4 cloves, minced4 瓣，切末
  • Tomato paste番茄膏 30 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • All-purpose flour面粉 30 g · about 1/4 cup约 1/4 杯
  • Bay leaves月桂叶 0.2 g · 1 bay leaf1 片月桂叶
  • Dried thyme干百里香 1.5 g · 1 tsp dried thyme1 茶匙干百里香
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Salt盐 10 g · about 1 2/3 tsp, divided约 1 又 2/3 茶匙，分次使用
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 2 g · about 1 tsp约 1 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Preheat the oven to 160°C, pat the beef dry and season it with salt and black pepper.烤箱预热至 160°C，牛肉用纸巾擦干并撒上盐和黑胡椒。
  2. Heat half the oil in a heavy pot over medium heat and brown the beef in batches for 2–3 minutes per side, then set it aside.厚底炖锅中火加热一半油，分批煎牛肉各面 2–3 分钟，至深褐色后盛出。
  3. Add the remaining oil, onion, carrots and celery to the pot and cook over medium heat for 6 minutes until the onion softens.锅中加入剩余油，放入洋葱、胡萝卜和芹菜，中火炒 6 分钟至洋葱变软。
  4. Add the garlic and tomato paste and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.加入蒜末和番茄膏，翻炒 1 分钟至香味出来。
  5. Sprinkle in the flour and thyme and stir for 1 minute until the vegetables are coated.撒入面粉和百里香，翻炒 1 分钟至面粉裹住蔬菜。
  6. Pour in the stout gradually while scraping up browned bits, then simmer for 3 minutes until the foam subsides.慢慢倒入黑啤，边倒边刮起锅底焦香，煮 3 分钟至泡沫减少。
  7. Pour in the stock and add the bay leaf, return the beef and its juices to the pot, and bring to a gentle simmer.倒入牛肉汤并放入月桂叶，把牛肉连同肉汁放回锅中，煮至微沸。
  8. Cover the pot and braise in the oven for 2 hours until a fork pierces the beef easily.盖锅放入烤箱炖 2 小时，至牛肉可被叉子轻易刺入。
  9. Add the potatoes, cover and braise for 35–45 minutes until the potatoes are tender but intact and the beef is fork-tender.加入土豆，盖盖继续炖 35–45 分钟，至土豆软而不散、牛肉用叉子能轻松分开。
  10. Remove the bay leaf, taste and adjust the salt, then serve in warm bowls.取出月桂叶，尝味后调整盐量，再盛入热碗。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Dry the beef and brown it in batches so the pan has room to sear rather than steam the meat.牛肉表面擦干并分批煎，锅里留出空间才能形成焦褐色，而不是出水煮白。
  • Scraping up the browned bits and briefly simmering the stout mellows its raw edge.加入黑啤后刮起锅底焦香并短暂煮开，能让啤酒的生涩气息散去。
  • A gentle simmer tenderizes the beef evenly and keeps too much liquid from evaporating.保持轻微沸腾而非大滚，牛肉会更均匀地变软，汤汁也不容易大量蒸发。
  • Adding large potato chunks later helps them keep their shape instead of breaking down in the stew.土豆后放，避免长时间炖煮后碎在汤里；切大块更容易保持形状。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The beef is still tough after a long braise牛肉炖很久仍然发硬
    Keep it covered at a gentle simmer for another 20–30 minutes and test with a fork; chuck cuts can take different amounts of time.继续盖盖小火炖 20–30 分钟，再用叉子检查；不同牛肩肉的软化时间会有差异。
  • The broth is thin and the stout tastes sharp汤汁稀薄、啤酒味尖
    Simmer uncovered for 10–15 minutes to reduce; next time let the stout cook briefly before adding the stock.开盖小火煮 10–15 分钟收浓；下次让黑啤先煮几分钟再加牛肉汤。
  • The potatoes break down and cloud the broth土豆煮碎，汤汁变浑
    If they have broken down, stir gently and avoid further reduction; next time use firm potatoes, cut large, and add them on schedule.已煮碎时轻轻搅拌并缩短后续收汁时间；下次选较结实的土豆、切大块并按时加入。
