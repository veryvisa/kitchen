Braised Beef Brisket with Daikon萝卜牛腩

Braised beef brisket with daikon is a Cantonese classic where tender beef with soft, gelatinous connective tissue meets sweet, broth-soaked radish. The key is blanching and searing the brisket first, then adding the daikon late so it turns tender without falling apart.萝卜牛腩是经典粤式焖菜，牛腩软糯带筋香，白萝卜吸饱肉汁清甜化渣。难点在于牛腩要先焯后炒香、分阶段焖炖，萝卜最后下锅才不会煮成一锅碎泥。

145 min total分钟 (20 + 125)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Daikon radish, Hoisin sauce, Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, Star anise另购：白萝卜、海鲜酱、老抽、料酒(绍兴)、八角

Ingredients食材

  • Daikon radish白萝卜 600 g · 1 medium daikon (about 600 g), peeled and cut into chunks白萝卜 1 根（约 600 克），去皮切滚刀块
  • Ginger姜 20 g · 6-8 slices, 3 for blanching and the rest for frying6–8 片，其中 3 片用于焯水，其余用于炒香
  • Garlic大蒜 16 g · 4 cloves, lightly smashed4 瓣，拍松
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks, whites cut in lengths and greens sliced3 根，葱白切段、葱绿切花
  • Hoisin sauce海鲜酱 32 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）

Method做法

  1. Place the brisket in cold water with 3 ginger slices and 1 tbsp cooking wine, bring to a boil over high heat, and cook 3 minutes until scum rises.牛腩冷水下锅，加 3 片姜和 1 汤匙料酒，大火烧开煮 3 分钟至血沫浮起。
  2. Lift out the brisket, rinse clean with warm water, and pat the surface dry with paper towels.捞出牛腩用温水冲洗干净，用厨房纸吸干表面水分。
  3. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium-low heat and fry the scallion whites, garlic, remaining ginger, star anise, and bay leaves for 1 minute until fragrant.炖锅烧热放油，中小火下葱白、蒜瓣、剩下的姜片、八角和香叶炒香约 1 分钟。
  4. Add the pat-dried brisket and stir over medium heat for 3 minutes until the surface dries and browns lightly.倒入擦干的牛腩转中火翻炒 3 分钟，炒至表面收干并微焦。
  5. Add the hoisin sauce, oyster sauce, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, and remaining 1 tbsp cooking wine, and stir 1 minute until glossy and fragrant.加海鲜酱、蚝油、生抽、老抽、糖和剩下 1 汤匙料酒，翻炒 1 分钟上色出香。
  6. Pour in boiling water to just cover the beef, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer over low heat for 75-90 minutes, until the connective tissue starts to soften.倒入刚没过牛腩的开水，烧开后转小火加盖焖 75–90 分钟，直到筋膜开始软化。
  7. Stir in the daikon chunks and simmer covered over low heat for 20-30 minutes, until a chopstick slides easily into the radish, the radish is cooked through but still holds its shape, and the beef is tender — go by texture, not a fixed time.放入白萝卜块拌匀，加盖小火再焖 20–30 分钟，直到筷子轻松扎透萝卜、萝卜熟透但仍保持形状，且牛腩软烂为准（不是固定时间）。
  8. Taste and add salt if needed, uncover to reduce over medium-high heat for 5 minutes until glossy, and top with sliced scallion greens.尝味后按需加盐，开盖中大火收汁 5 分钟至汤汁浓亮，撒葱花出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cut the brisket into pieces at least 3 cm wide, as it shrinks during braising and small cubes turn dry.牛腩切块不要小于 3 厘米，久焖后会明显缩水，块太小容易柴碎。
  • If you need to top up the liquid partway through, use hot water so the braising temperature does not drop.中途如需补水用热水，避免锅内温度骤降影响持续焖煮。
  • Daikon softens relatively quickly and releases moisture as it cooks, so adding it after the brisket has had time to become tender lets it absorb flavour without turning mushy.白萝卜软化较快、煮的时候会释出水分，先炖牛腩一个多小时再下萝卜，既入味又保持块形完整。
  • Keep the star anise and bay leaves restrained so they lift the broth without masking the beef and sweet radish.八角和香叶只放少量提香即可，香料过多会盖住牛腩和萝卜本身的鲜甜。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The brisket is still tough or chewy牛腩咬起来还是发硬塞牙
    The connective tissue needs more time; keep it covered on a low simmer for another 20 to 30 minutes until a chopstick pierces the tendons easily.说明胶质还没完全软化；别急着收汁，盖好锅盖继续小火焖 20–30 分钟，直到用筷子能扎入筋膜。
  • The braise is too salty or slightly bitter酱汁太咸或颜色太深发苦
    Hoisin and dark soy are already salty and scorch over high heat; rescue it by diluting with 1/2 cup hot water or unsalted stock, then simmer 10 minutes and taste again.海鲜酱与老抽本身偏咸，炒酱时火别太大；补救加半杯热水或无盐高汤稀释，再煮 10 分钟并重新尝味。
