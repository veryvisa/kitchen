Seafood Soft Tofu Stew海鲜嫩豆腐汤

Soft tofu, shrimp and mussels simmer in a garlicky chili broth with a savory seafood base. Frying the seasonings first and adding the seafood near the end keeps the shellfish from turning tough.嫩豆腐、虾和青口在热汤里煮到刚好熟，汤底用蒜、辣椒和海鲜高汤提味。先炒香调料再加汤，最后放海鲜，能避免虾和贝肉变老。

35 min total分钟 (15 + 20)3 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Soft / silken tofu, Korean chili flakes (gochugaru), Dashi powder另购：嫩豆腐、韩式辣椒粉（gochugaru）、日式鲣鱼高汤粉

Ingredients食材

  • Soft / silken tofu嫩豆腐 500 g · 1 package soft tofu (about 500 g)嫩豆腐约 1 盒（500 克）
  • Frozen raw shrimp冷冻生虾 200 g · about 200 g raw peeled shrimp去壳生虾约 200 克
  • Mussels青口贝 300 g · about 300 g mussels in shells, scrubbed带壳青口约 300 克，刷洗干净
  • Eggs鸡蛋 50 g · 1 large egg1 个大鸡蛋
  • Shiitake香菇 80 g · 2-3 shiitake mushrooms, sliced2–3 朵香菇，切片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 25 g · 2 stalks, chopped2 根，切葱花
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 3 cloves, minced3 瓣，切末
  • Gochujang韩式辣酱 20 g · a heaped tablespoon1 汤匙多
  • Korean chili flakes (gochugaru)韩式辣椒粉（gochugaru） 6 g · Korean red pepper flakes (gochugaru), about 1 tbsp, adjust to taste韩式辣椒粉（gochugaru）约 1 汤匙，按口味调整

Method做法

  1. Scrub the mussels, remove their beards, and discard any with cracked shells or that remain open after tapping.把青口刷洗干净，去掉足丝，壳体破裂或敲开后仍不闭合的丢弃。
  2. Scoop the soft tofu into large pieces, slice the mushrooms and separate the white and green parts of the scallions.将嫩豆腐用大勺分成大块，香菇切片，葱白和葱绿分开放。
  3. Heat the canola and sesame oils in a pot over medium heat, then fry the scallion whites, garlic and chili powder for 30 seconds until fragrant.锅中中火加热菜籽油和芝麻油，放入葱白、蒜末和辣椒粉炒 30 秒，闻到香味即可。
  4. Add the gochujang and soy sauce and fry over low heat for 30 seconds, until the paste darkens and coats the aromatics.加入辣椒酱和生抽，转小火炒 30 秒，酱料颜色变深并均匀裹住葱蒜。
  5. Add the mushrooms, dashi powder and 600 g water, then bring to a boil over medium heat.加入香菇、海带高汤粉和 600 克水，转中火煮开。
  6. Add the soft tofu, gently separate the pieces and simmer over medium-low heat for 5 minutes.放入嫩豆腐，轻轻推开，中小火煮 5 分钟。
  7. Add the mussels, cover and cook for 4 minutes, until the shells open.放入青口，盖锅煮 4 分钟，直到贝壳张开。
  8. Add the shrimp and salt and cook over medium heat for 3-5 minutes, until opaque and 74°C at the center.加入生虾和盐，中火煮 3–5 分钟，直到虾肉不透明且中心达到 74°C。
  9. Mussels are done once the shells open; cook for 1 more minute after they open and discard any that stay shut.青口壳张开即可判断熟透，壳开后再煮 1 分钟；仍未张开的丢弃。
  10. Crack the egg onto the soup and cook over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until the white and yolk reach your preferred doneness, then scatter over the scallion greens and turn off the heat; for pregnant women, young children, older adults or anyone with a weaker immune system, use a pasteurized egg or cook the yolk fully.把鸡蛋打入汤面，中火煮 2–3 分钟至蛋白和蛋黄都达到喜欢的熟度，撒上葱绿后关火；孕妇、幼儿、老人或免疫力较弱者食用时建议用巴氏杀菌蛋或把蛋黄煮至全熟。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Scoop the tofu into pieces rather than cutting it small to preserve its soft texture.嫩豆腐用勺子分块，比切小块更能保留柔软的口感。
  • Toast the chili powder over medium-low heat and add liquid as soon as it darkens; scorched powder tastes bitter.辣椒粉先用中低火炒香，颜色刚变深就加液体，炒焦会发苦。
  • Add mussels before shrimp, and cook the shrimp only until opaque and 74°C; prolonged cooking makes them tough.贝类先下、虾后下，虾只煮到不透明并达到 74°C，继续久煮会变硬。
  • Dashi powder and soy sauce are both salty, so taste the broth before adding more salt.高汤粉和生抽都带咸味，汤煮开后再尝味补盐。
  • A thermometer cannot read the centre of meat still in the shell, so "open plus one more minute" is the practical test at home; Health Canada's instruction for shellfish in shells is to discard any that do not open, not that an open shell proves a given temperature.带壳贝类在壳里测不准中心温度，壳开再多煮 1 分钟是家里可行的做法；加拿大卫生部对带壳贝类的要求是丢弃不张开的，不是「壳开即达到某个温度」。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The shrimp is tough and dry虾肉变硬发柴
    Turn off the heat as soon as the shrimp reaches 74°C; if the broth needs more cooking, lift the shrimp out and return it just before serving.虾一达到 74°C 就关火；若汤还要继续煮，先把虾捞出，盛汤时再放回。
  • The chili base tastes bitter辣椒底发苦
    Dilute with water and simmer with the tofu; next time fry the chili powder gently and add liquid as soon as it darkens.加水稀释并放入豆腐继续煮；下次用小火炒辣椒粉，看到颜色转深就立刻加液体。
  • The broth is too salty汤太咸
    Dilute with hot water and a little more soft tofu; next time use half the dashi powder and season at the end.加热水和少量嫩豆腐稀释；下次先减半高汤粉，最后再调盐。
