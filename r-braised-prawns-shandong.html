Shandong Braised Prawns油焖大虾

Traditional Jiaodong-style braised prawns get their vibrant red glaze not from ketchup, but from pressing the prawn heads in hot oil to release the rich orange juices from the heads before braising with sugar, Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, and a little water. Fast cooking is essential so the meat stays snappy instead of turning dry.胶东名菜油焖大虾不用番茄酱，靠热油煎出带头大虾本身的红亮虾脑虾油，再加白糖、料酒、生抽和少许热水焖收成浓汁。动作要快、火候要准，虾肉才脆嫩弹牙，焖太久就会发柴缩水。

17 min total分钟 (12 + 5)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks, whites cut in pieces, greens finely shredded3 根，葱白切段，葱绿切细丝
  • Ginger姜 20 g · 20 g ginger: half cut into slices, half finely julienned姜 20 克：一半切片，一半切细丝

Method做法

  1. Trim the antennae, sharp rostrum, and legs from the prawns, rinse, and pat completely dry.剪去大虾的长须、虾枪和虾脚，洗净后彻底擦干表面水分。
  2. Snip down the back shell of each prawn and pull out the dark vein.沿虾背剪开虾壳并挑出虾线。
  3. Heat 3 tbsp oil in a wok over medium heat and fry the ginger slices, green onion whites, and garlic for 30 seconds until fragrant.炒锅倒 3 汤匙油中火烧热，下姜片、葱白段和拍蒜爆香 30 秒。
  4. Arrange the prawns in a single layer and sear over medium-high heat for 45 seconds until the undersides turn bright red.把大虾平铺入锅，中大火煎 45 秒至底面虾壳变成鲜红色。
  5. Flip and sear 45 seconds longer while gently pressing the prawn heads with the back of your spatula to release the rich orange juices from the heads.翻面再煎 45 秒，同时用锅铲背轻压虾头挤出虾脑虾油。
  6. Splash the cooking wine around the rim of the wok, then stir in the sugar, soy sauce, and salt.沿锅边烹入料酒，加入白糖、生抽和盐快速翻匀。
  7. Pour in a little hot water (about 60 to 80 mL) to come partway up the prawns, cover, and braise over medium-low heat for 1 to 2 minutes until opaque throughout, just curled, and 74°C internally.倒入约 60 至 80 毫升热水至刚润及虾身，盖盖转中小火焖 1 至 2 分钟，直到虾肉完全变白不透明、虾身刚卷曲且中心温度达到 74°C。
  8. Uncover and boil over high heat for 30 to 60 seconds, tossing until a glossy red glaze clings to the shells.开盖转大火快速收汁 30 至 60 秒，翻炒至红亮浓汁裹满虾壳。
  9. Transfer to a serving plate and top with the julienned ginger and shredded green onion greens.出锅装盘，撒上姜丝和葱绿丝即可。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Drying the prawns thoroughly prevents oil splatter and helps the shells sear cleanly.大虾下锅前彻底擦干，不仅能避免热油四溅，虾壳也更容易煎出红油和焦香。
  • Pressing the heads into the hot oil releases the rich orange juices from the heads for natural red color and deep shellfish flavor without needing tomato paste.用铲背轻压虾头挤出虾脑虾油，这是鲁菜油焖大虾不用番茄酱也能色泽红亮的秘诀。
  • Keep total stove time for the prawns around 3 to 4 minutes; once they are opaque throughout, just curled, and reach 74°C internally, reduce the sauce right away so the meat stays tender.大虾下锅后的总烹调时间控制在 3 至 4 分钟左右；虾肉刚完全变白不透明、虾身刚卷曲且中心达到 74°C 就立刻开盖收汁，久焖会让虾肉变老。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The prawns turned dry and chalky or stuck to the shells虾肉发粉发柴、壳肉粘在一起难剥
    Keep covered braising to 1 to 2 minutes and reduce as soon as the prawns are opaque throughout, just curled, and reach 74°C; always thaw frozen prawns completely before cooking.盖盖焖煮控制在 1 至 2 分钟，看到虾肉刚完全变白不透明、虾身刚卷曲且中心达 74°C 就开盖大火收汁；冷冻大虾要先完全解冻再炒。
  • The glaze looks pale and tastes flatly sweet汤色发白、吃起来只有死甜味
    Sear hot enough to color the shells and press the heads to release the rich orange juices from the heads, season adequately with Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, and salt, and reduce well so savory depth balances the sugar.煎虾时火候要足并轻压虾头挤出虾脑虾油，料酒、生抽和盐要放够，并把汤汁收浓以鲜衬甜。
  • The sauce is watery and slides off the shells锅底水汪汪的，酱汁挂不住虾壳
    Add only a little hot water, then boil uncovered over high heat until the glaze thickens and bubbles tightly.只加少许热水，开盖后转大火快速翻炒收浓至冒密集小泡再起锅。
