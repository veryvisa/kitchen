Coq au Vin红酒炖鸡

Chicken simmers in red wine with herbs, mushrooms, bacon, and vegetables for a deeply flavored sauce. Browning the chicken and bacon first, then braising gently until the chicken is cooked through, are the two key steps.鸡肉在红酒与香料中慢炖，配上蘑菇、培根和蔬菜，酱汁浓而有层次。先把鸡肉和培根煎出焦香，再耐心小火炖到鸡肉熟透，是家常做法最重要的两步。

155 min total分钟 (25 + 130)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Red wine另购：红酒(烹饪用)

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 1400 g · about 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 220–250 g each)带骨带皮鸡大腿约 6 个（每个约 220–250 克）
  • Bacon培根 150 g · bacon, cut into small pieces培根，切成小段
  • White mushrooms蘑菇 250 g · mushrooms, thickly sliced蘑菇，切厚片
  • Onions洋葱 220 g · onion, cut into chunks洋葱，切块
  • Carrots胡萝卜 200 g · carrots, cut into 2 cm pieces胡萝卜，切约 2 厘米块
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 3 cloves, minced3 瓣，切末
  • Red wine红酒(烹饪用) 750 g · 1 bottle (750 ml) dry red wine干红葡萄酒 1 瓶（750 毫升）
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 300 g · about 1 1/4 cups chicken stock鸡汤约 1 又 1/4 杯
  • Tomato paste番茄膏 30 g · 2 tbsp tomato paste番茄膏 2 汤匙
  • All-purpose flour面粉 20 g · about 2 tbsp all-purpose flour普通面粉约 2 汤匙
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 ml)1 汤匙（15 毫升）
  • Dried thyme干百里香 1 g · about 1 tsp dried thyme干百里香约 1 茶匙
  • Bay leaves月桂叶 0.2 g · 1 dried bay leaf干月桂叶 1 片
  • Salt盐 7 g · about 1 1/4 tsp, divided约 1 又 1/4 茶匙，分次使用
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · about 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper现磨黑胡椒约 1/2 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Pat the chicken thighs dry and season them with about half the salt and pepper.用厨房纸把鸡腿表面擦干，撒上约一半盐和黑胡椒。
  2. Cook the bacon in a heavy pot over medium heat for 5–6 minutes until it renders fat and turns golden brown.厚底锅中火煎培根 5–6 分钟，直到出油并呈金褐色。
  3. Transfer the bacon to a plate, leaving about 1 tablespoon of fat in the pot.把培根盛出，锅中留约 1 汤匙油脂。
  4. Sear the thighs skin-side down in batches over medium heat for 4–5 minutes per side until golden, then set them aside.鸡皮朝下分批煎鸡腿，中火每面煎 4–5 分钟至表皮金黄，盛出备用。
  5. If the pot has more than about 2 tbsp of fat, pour off the excess, then sear the mushrooms over medium heat for 5 minutes until their liquid evaporates and the edges brown, then set them aside.锅中若油脂过多（超过约 2 汤匙），先倒掉多余部分，再放入蘑菇中火煎 5 分钟至水分蒸发、边缘上色后盛出。
  6. Add the onion and carrots and cook over medium heat for 5 minutes until the onion softens.锅中放洋葱和胡萝卜，中火炒 5 分钟至洋葱变软。
  7. Add the garlic and tomato paste and cook over medium-low heat for 1 minute until the paste darkens slightly.加入蒜末和番茄膏，中小火炒 1 分钟至番茄膏颜色变深。
  8. Sprinkle in the flour and stir over medium heat for 1 minute until no dry flour remains.撒入面粉，中火搅拌 1 分钟至看不见干粉。
  9. Pour in the wine and stock, scrape up the browned bits, and boil over medium heat for 3 minutes.倒入红酒和鸡汤，刮起锅底焦香，中火煮沸 3 分钟。
  10. Return the chicken and bacon to the pot, add the thyme, bay leaf, and remaining salt, and bring the liquid back to a boil over medium heat.放回鸡腿和培根，加入百里香、月桂叶及剩余盐，中火煮至汤汁重新沸腾。
  11. Cover and simmer over low heat for 55–65 minutes until the chicken is tender and nearly falling off the bone and the carrots are tender.盖锅转小火炖 55–65 分钟，直到鸡肉软嫩接近脱骨、胡萝卜变软。
  12. Add the browned mushrooms and simmer for 10–15 minutes more; confirm with an instant-read thermometer that the thickest part of the chicken reaches 74°C.放入煎好的蘑菇，小火再炖 10–15 分钟；用食品温度计确认鸡肉最厚处达到 74°C。
  13. Remove the bay leaf and the chicken, then simmer the sauce uncovered over medium heat for about 10–20 minutes until it lightly coats the back of a spoon, and return the chicken and mushrooms to warm through.取出月桂叶和鸡肉，酱汁不盖锅中火浓缩约 10–20 分钟至能薄薄裹住勺背，再把鸡肉和蘑菇放回锅里略煮热透。
  14. Taste the sauce and serve hot.尝味后趁热盛出。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Dry the chicken before searing so it browns instead of steaming in its own moisture.鸡肉下锅前擦干，表面才容易煎出焦色，而不是先蒸出水分。
  • Sear the chicken in batches with room between pieces; crowding lowers the heat and prevents browning.鸡肉分批煎，锅里留出空间，拥挤会让温度下降、难以形成焦香。
  • Brown the mushrooms separately and add them back later to preserve their shape and keep the sauce concentrated.蘑菇先单独煎好再回锅，能保留形状，也让酱汁不被多余水分冲淡。
  • Keep the braise at a gentle simmer; a hard boil can toughen the chicken and cloud the sauce.炖煮时保持小火轻轻冒泡，猛烈沸腾容易让鸡肉变柴、酱汁变浑。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The chicken skin is pale with no browned flavor鸡肉表皮灰白，没有焦香
    Dry the chicken, sear in batches, and wait for the skin to release before turning; crisp cooked pieces separately in a hot pan if needed.先把鸡肉表面擦干，分批煎并等表皮自然松开后再翻；已经炖好的鸡肉可单独放入热锅煎脆表皮。
  • The chicken is cooked but the sauce is thin鸡肉已经熟了，酱汁却很稀
    Remove the chicken and reduce the sauce uncovered over medium heat until it coats a spoon, then return the chicken.先把鸡肉盛出，酱汁不盖锅中火煮几分钟至能裹住勺背，再把鸡肉放回锅里。
  • The sauce has small flour lumps酱汁有面粉小疙瘩
    Stir while pouring in the wine and scrape the pot well; if lumps remain, remove the chicken, strain the sauce, and bring it back to a simmer.下红酒时边倒边搅并刮匀锅底；若已结团，可把鸡肉取出后将酱汁过滤再煮开。
