Korean Spicy Stir-fried Pork (Jeyuk Bokkeum)韩式辣炒猪肉

A favourite of Korean homes and cafeterias, as good over rice as any dish going. Thin pork slices marinated in gochujang, soy, sugar and garlic are stir-fried with onion until the edges char. Wrap in lettuce or pile on rice.韩国家庭和食堂的人气菜，下饭能力和回锅肉有一拼。猪肉薄片用辣酱、酱油、糖和蒜腌一下，和洋葱大火炒到边缘焦香。生菜包着吃，或者直接盖在米饭上。

25 min total分钟 (15 + 10)3 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

  • Gochujang韩式辣酱 40 g · 2.5 tbsp gochujang韩式辣酱 2.5 汤匙 Korean chilli flakes are traditional too; fine to skip传统还加韩式辣椒粉，没有可省

Method做法

  1. Mix the gochujang, soy, sugar, garlic, ginger and sesame oil.辣酱、生抽、糖、蒜末、姜蓉和香油调匀。
  2. Toss the pork in the sauce and leave 10 minutes.猪肉片倒进酱里抓匀，腌 10 分钟。
  3. Heat the oil in a large pan, spread out the pork and sear on high for a minute without moving it.大锅烧热油，下猪肉摊开，大火煎 1 分钟不翻动。
  4. Stir-fry until the meat changes colour, about 2 minutes.翻炒到肉变色，约 2 分钟。
  5. Add the onion and carrot and fry 3 more minutes until the onion softens and the meat chars at the edges.加洋葱和胡萝卜，再炒 3 分钟到洋葱软、肉边缘微焦。
  6. Toss in the green onion and finish with the sesame seeds.放葱段翻匀，撒芝麻出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Use a cut with some fat (shoulder or belly); lean meat dries out.要用带肥的部位（梅花肉或五花），纯瘦肉炒出来干柴。
  • The sugar in the marinade caramelises over high heat; those charred edges are the flavour.酱里有糖，肉一定要大火快炒出焦边，这股焦香是这道菜的味道核心。
  • Gochujang heat varies by brand; start with 2 tbsp and add more after tasting.辣酱各品牌辣度不同，第一次做先放 2 汤匙，尝过再加。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • It turned soupy炒出很多汤
    The pan was crowded or the heat too low. Cook in two batches or keep going on high to reduce it.锅不够大或火太小。分两批炒，或者开大火继续把汤收干。
  • It scorched and stuck粘锅焦黑
    The sugary sauce needs constant stirring on high; add 1 tbsp water if it darkens too fast.酱汁糖多，火大时要不停翻；焦得太快就加 1 汤匙水。
