Garlic Broccoli Stir-fry蒜蓉西兰花

The simplest green vegetable dish: blanch for color, bloom the garlic gently, then toss over high heat. The key is blanching exactly long enough and draining well, so the broccoli comes out bright, crisp and garlicky, not watery.最简单的一道青菜：焯水保色，蒜末小火炒香，大火快炒上味。要点是西兰花焯得刚好、沥得够干，再用热油激出蒜香，出来的菜翠绿、脆、有蒜味，不会水汪汪。

14 min total分钟 (8 + 6)2 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Broccoli西兰花 400 g · 1 large head in small florets, stem peeled and sliced1 大颗，切成小朵，梗削皮切片
  • Garlic大蒜 25 g · 6 cloves, minced6 瓣，切末
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL), 1 tsp of it for the blanching water2 汤匙（30 mL），其中 1 茶匙焯水用
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 9 g · 1/2 tbsp, optional半汤匙，可不放
  • Salt盐 3 g · about 1/2 tsp (1 g for the water, 2 g for seasoning)约半茶匙（焯水 1 克，调味 2 克）
  • Sugar白糖 2 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Cut the broccoli into evenly sized florets and peel and slice the stem thin.西兰花切成大小均匀的小朵，梗削掉硬皮、切成薄片。
  2. Bring a pot of water to a boil with 1 g salt and 1 tsp oil.烧一锅水，加 1 克盐和 1 茶匙油。
  3. Add the broccoli to the rolling water for 60 seconds, until bright green, and lift it out.水大滚后放西兰花，煮 60 秒，颜色变成亮绿就捞出。
  4. Chill it at once in cold water, drain thoroughly and pat dry with paper towels.立刻过冷水，彻底沥干，用厨房纸吸掉多余的水。
  5. Put the garlic in the cold pan with the oil, turn on low heat and warm it until just golden, about 40 seconds.冷锅冷油放入蒜末，开小火，慢慢加热到蒜刚发黄，约 40 秒。
  6. Turn the heat to high, add the broccoli and toss for 30 seconds.转大火，倒入西兰花，翻炒 30 秒。
  7. Add the salt, sugar and oyster sauce and toss another 30 seconds until coated, then serve.加盐、糖和蚝油，再翻炒 30 秒，拌匀出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • A little oil in the water gives a sheen, and the cold rinse stops cooking to keep the crunch and color.焯水时油能让西兰花表面有光泽；过冷水能停止加热，保住脆度和颜色。
  • Starting garlic in cold oil releases flavor gently and lowers the risk of scorching; black garlic is bitter and should be thrown out.蒜末冷油下锅，小火慢慢出香，蒜不容易糊；一旦发黑就苦，要倒掉重来。
  • Dry the broccoli well; wet florets steam in the pan and stay bland.西兰花一定要沥干，否则下锅会冒水，菜不脆也不入味。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The broccoli is yellow and mushy西兰花发黄、软烂
    It was blanched or cooked too long. Next time blanch 60 seconds and stir-fry just a minute; yellowed broccoli cannot be rescued, so turn it into soup.焯过头或炒太久。下次焯 60 秒就捞，回锅只炒 1 分钟；已发黄的补不回来，做汤就好。
  • The plate is watery盘子里出水
    It was not drained or the heat was too low. Drain, pat dry and use high heat.焯完没沥干，或火太小。沥干、吸干，用大火炒。
