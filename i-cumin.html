Store and portion储存与分装

Keep sealed, dark and cool; ground cumin loses most of its aroma in about 6 months, whole seeds keep over a year.密封避光放阴凉处；粉状约 6 个月香味明显变淡，整粒可放 1 年以上。

Decant a small jar for daily use and keep the rest sealed; toast in a dry pan for 30 seconds before using for more aroma.大包分出一小瓶日常用，其余密封；炒菜前用干锅小火焙 30 秒更香。
