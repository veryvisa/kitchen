Taiwanese Beef Noodle Soup台湾红烧牛肉面

This noodle soup gets its deep, savory flavor from beef slowly braised with soy and warming spices. The key is a gentle simmer and a separate pot for the noodles — cooking them apart keeps the noodles from soaking up the broth and keeps starch from clouding it, so the noodles also keep their bite.红烧牛肉面的好味道，来自牛肉炖软后融进汤里的酱香和香料味。家里做最要紧的是小火慢炖、另锅煮面：分开煮面能避免面条吸走汤汁、面汤的淀粉把汤弄浑，面条也不会泡软。

Method做法

  1. Put the beef in a pot of cold water and bring it to a boil over high heat.牛肉放入冷水锅，大火加热至沸腾。
  2. Boil the beef for 3 minutes, skim the foam, then lift it out, rinse off any remaining foam and blood under warm water, and drain.牛肉沸煮 3 分钟，撇去浮沫后捞出，用温水冲掉表面残留的浮沫和血水，沥干。
  3. Heat the canola oil in a heavy pot over medium heat.炖锅里的菜籽油用中火烧热。
  4. Add the onion, ginger, garlic, and green onion and fry for about 3 minutes until the onion edges turn golden.放入洋葱、姜、蒜和葱段，炒约 3 分钟至洋葱边缘微黄。
  5. Add the doubanjiang and fry over low heat for about 1 minute until the oil turns reddish.加入豆瓣酱，小火炒约 1 分钟至油色发红。
  6. Add the beef and sear over medium heat for 3-4 minutes until lightly browned, then add the tomato and cook 5-6 minutes more until it softens and releases its juice.放入牛肉，中火煸炒 3–4 分钟至表面微焦，再加入番茄，继续炒 5–6 分钟至番茄出汁变软。
  7. Pour in the water and cooking wine, then add the soy sauces, brown sugar, star anise, and bay leaves.倒入清水和料酒，加入生抽、老抽、红糖、八角和月桂叶。
  8. Bring the broth to a boil, then lower to a gentle simmer with the lid ajar for 2-2.5 hours, until a chopstick slides easily into the beef and the connective tissue turns tender; top up with hot water if the broth runs low.汤烧开后转小火，半盖锅盖（留一点缝）保持微沸炖 2–2.5 小时，至牛肉能被筷子轻松刺穿、筋膜软糯；汤太少可中途补热水。
  9. Taste the broth around the 1-hour mark and add salt as needed; if it tastes salty, dilute with hot water rather than relying on salt added only at the end.炖到 1 小时左右先尝一次汤，按需补盐；汤偏咸就加热水稀释，不要只靠最后加盐调味。
  10. Taste the broth one final time, adjust the seasoning, and remove the star anise and bay leaves.炖好后尝汤最终调味，捞出八角和月桂叶。
  11. Bring water to a boil in a separate pot with a little salt, blanch the bok choy 30-60 seconds until bright green and just tender at the stem, then lift it out right away.另锅烧开水，加少许盐，放入小白菜烫 30–60 秒至颜色翠绿、梗部刚断生，立即捞出。
  12. Cook the noodles in fresh water in a separate pot: fresh noodles until just a faint white core remains, dried noodles about 30-60 seconds less than the package time; drain and divide among four bowls.用干净的宽水另煮面条：鲜面煮到中心仅剩极细白心即可捞出，干面按包装时间减 30–60 秒，沥干后分入四碗。
  13. Ladle the beef and hot broth over the noodles, top with the bok choy, and scatter on a little extra scallion.将牛肉和热汤舀在面上，放上小白菜，撒一点葱花即可。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cut the beef into evenly sized, generously large chunks so they soften together without crumbling.牛肉切成大小相近的大块，慢炖后更容易同时软透，也不容易散成碎肉。
  • Frying the doubanjiang gently until the oil turns red gives it a rounder aroma; high heat can scorch it and make it bitter.豆瓣酱用小火炒出红油再加汤，香味更圆润；火太大容易炒焦发苦。
  • The soy sauce and doubanjiang already carry salt, so season lightly at first and taste again at the 1-hour mark and once more when done. If the broth has reduced and tastes stronger, that is evaporation — top it up with hot water rather than reducing it on purpose.汤里酱油和豆瓣酱已经带盐，先少量调味，炖到一小时和炖好时各尝一次；如果汤变少变浓了，是蒸发太多，补热水，不要刻意收汁。
  • Cook and store the noodles separately from the broth so they do not soak up the soup.面条和汤分开煮、分开盛，剩汤也不容易被面条吸干。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The beef is still tough after a long simmer牛肉炖了很久还是硬
    Keep the pot at a gentle simmer rather than a hard boil, then cook another 20-30 minutes; different cuts take different amounts of time to soften, and beef shank can take up to 3 hours.确认锅里只是轻轻冒泡而不是大滚，再继续小火炖 20–30 分钟；不同部位软化所需时间会不同，牛腱可能要到 3 小时。
  • The broth is too salty and concentrated汤又咸又浓
    Thin it gradually with hot water; next time use less soy sauce and salt, and taste at the 1-hour mark before deciding whether to add more.分次加热水调淡；下次先少放生抽和盐，炖到一小时先尝一次再决定要不要补。
  • The noodles turn soft in the broth面条泡在汤里变软
    Drain the noodles while still slightly firm and store them apart from the broth; replace noodles that have already gone soft.面条煮到略有嚼劲就捞出，汤和面分开保存；已泡软的面可另煮一份替换。
