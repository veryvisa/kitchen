Tomato and Egg Noodles西红柿鸡蛋打卤面

The most common noodle bowl in northern homes. Ripe tomatoes cook down into a red sauce, the eggs are scrambled in big fluffy curds, and it all goes over just-cooked noodles. No water and no starch: the tomatoes make the sauce on their own.北方家庭最常见的一碗面。熟透的西红柿炒出红汤，鸡蛋炒得蓬松大块，浇在刚出锅的面条上，酸甜开胃。不加水不勾芡，靠西红柿自己的汁做卤，味道最纯。

30 min total分钟 (10 + 20)3 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Wheat noodles挂面/面条 360 g · 360 g wheat noodles (hand-cut or wide)面条 360 克（手擀面或宽面）
  • Tomatoes西红柿 600 g · 3 large ripe tomatoes (about 600 g)3 个大番茄（约 600 克），熟透的
  • Eggs鸡蛋 200 g · 4 large eggs4 个
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 2 stalks, sliced2 根，切葱花
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp, used in two parts2 汤匙，分两次用
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp1/2 汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 6 g · 1.5 tsp1.5 茶匙
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp约 2/3 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Score the tomatoes, scald them for 30 seconds, peel and cut into small pieces.番茄顶部划十字，开水烫 30 秒，去皮切小块。
  2. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt.鸡蛋加一小撮盐打散。
  3. Heat 1 tbsp oil, pour in the eggs and lift them out as soon as they set in large curds.锅里 1 汤匙油烧热，倒入蛋液，凝固成大块就盛出。
  4. Fry the white part of the onion in the rest of the oil, then add the tomatoes over medium heat.剩下的油下葱白炒香，倒入番茄中火翻炒。
  5. Cover for 5 minutes, then press the tomatoes with the spatula to release their juice.盖盖焖 5 分钟，用铲子把番茄压出红汤。
  6. Season with sugar, soy and salt, return the eggs, stir and add the onion greens.加糖、生抽和盐，鸡蛋回锅拌匀，撒葱绿关火。
  7. Cook the noodles as the packet says, lift them into bowls and ladle the sauce over.面条按包装时间煮好，捞到碗里，浇上一大勺卤。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Peeling and covering the tomatoes releases enough juice to make sauce without water.番茄去皮、盖盖焖，汁才出得多，不用加水也有足够的卤。
  • Take the eggs out just set and return them at the end so they stay tender and in large pieces.鸡蛋炒到刚凝固就盛出，最后回锅，才嫩而且是大块。
  • The sugar rounds off the acidity rather than making it sweet; adjust to your tomatoes.糖不是为了甜，是把番茄的酸圆一圆，按番茄的酸度加减。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • Not enough sauce to coat the noodles卤太少、拌不开
    The tomatoes were not ripe enough. Simmer with half a cup of pasta water for a minute, or add another tomato.番茄不够熟。加半杯煮面水一起煮 1 分钟，或多加一个番茄。
  • The eggs are dry and crumbly鸡蛋老了、碎成渣
    They were overcooked. Pour into hot oil, push into large curds as the edges puff and lift them out barely set.鸡蛋炒太久。油热后下蛋，边缘一鼓起就推大块，七成熟就盛出。
