Rosemary Garlic Lamb Chops迷迭香煎羊排

Costco's lamb loin chops are thick and meaty, and a few minutes in a skillet make a proper dinner. The keys are letting them warm up, patting them dry and basting with butter, garlic and rosemary at the end.Costco 的羊里脊排（loin chops）厚实、肉多，平底锅煎几分钟就是一道像样的晚餐。关键是煎前回温、擦干，再用黄油和蒜、迷迭香淋一淋。

37 min total分钟 (5 + 12)2 servings份Medium中等Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Lamb rack/chops羊排 600 g · 6 lamb loin chops (about 600 g, bone in)羊里脊排 6 块（约 600 克，带骨）
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp约 2/3 茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · freshly ground black pepper现磨黑胡椒适量
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 14 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Butter黄油 14 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 4 cloves, smashed4 瓣，拍扁
  • Rosemary迷迭香 2 g · 1 tsp dried rosemary (or 2 fresh sprigs)干迷迭香 1 茶匙（或新鲜 2 枝）
  • Lemons柠檬 60 g · half a lemon, in wedges半个，切块

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Take the chops out of the fridge and leave them at room temperature for 20 minutes.羊排从冰箱取出，室温放 20 分钟。
  2. Pat the chops completely dry with paper towel.用厨房纸把羊排表面彻底擦干。
  3. Season both sides evenly with the salt and pepper.两面均匀撒盐和黑胡椒。
  4. Heat a cast-iron or heavy skillet over medium-high heat and add the olive oil.铸铁锅或厚底锅中大火烧热，倒入橄榄油。
  5. Lay the chops in and leave them for 3 minutes until the underside is deep brown.羊排平放入锅，不要移动，煎 3 分钟到底面深褐色。
  6. Turn them and cook 2 minutes more.翻面再煎 2 分钟。
  7. Lower to medium and add the butter, garlic and rosemary.转中火，放黄油、蒜和迷迭香。
  8. Tilt the pan and spoon the butter over the chops for 1 minute.锅略倾斜，用勺子把黄油反复淋在羊排上 1 分钟。
  9. Check the thickest part with a thermometer and take them out at 57°C (medium-rare) to 63°C (medium).用肉温计量最厚处，到 57°C（三分熟）至 63°C（五分熟）出锅。
  10. Rest for 5 minutes and serve with the lemon wedges.盛出静置 5 分钟，配柠檬块上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • The drier the surface, the better the crust; wet meat goes grey instead of brown.表面擦得越干，焦壳越好；湿的肉只会被蒸成灰色。
  • Stand the chops on their fat edge with tongs for a minute to render it; it tastes better and less greasy.羊排边上那条肥边可以用夹子立起来煎 1 分钟，把油逼出来，更香也不腻。
  • Resting lets the juices settle, so they stay in the meat when you cut it.出锅后静置，肉汁回流，切开不流一盘血水。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • Burnt outside, raw inside外面焦了里面还生
    The heat was too high or the chops too cold; warm them up first, and after searing both sides finish covered on low for 2 minutes.火太大或羊排太冷；下次先回温，煎完两面后转小火盖盖焖 2 分钟。
  • The meat is tough and dry肉又老又硬
    It was overcooked; go by a thermometer and take it off 2-3°C below your target, as it rises while resting.煎过头了；以肉温计为准，出锅温度比目标低 2–3°C，静置时还会升温。
