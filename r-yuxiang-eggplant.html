Fish-fragrant Eggplant鱼香茄子

There is no fish in fish-fragrant: it is a Sichuan balance of salty, sweet, sour and hot from chilli bean paste, sugar, vinegar, ginger, garlic and onion. Eggplant famously drinks oil, so instead of deep-frying it is salted, dusted with starch and pan-fried in a little oil before the sauce goes on, soft and well seasoned without the grease.「鱼香」里没有鱼，是川菜用豆瓣酱、糖、醋和葱姜蒜调出的咸甜酸辣味。茄子吸油是出名的，这里不油炸：先用盐水泡、拍一层薄淀粉，少油煎软，再裹鱼香汁，软糯入味又不腻。

27 min total分钟 (15 + 12)3 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Doubanjiang, Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)另购：豆瓣酱、香醋/陈醋

Ingredients食材

  • Eggplant茄子 500 g · 2 Chinese eggplants or 1 globe eggplant (about 500 g), cut into 8 cm batons长茄子 2 根或圆茄子 1 个（约 500 克），切 8 厘米长条
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 12 g · 4 tsp: 3 for the eggplant, 1 for the sauce4 茶匙：3 茶匙拍茄子，1 茶匙调汁
  • Canola oil菜籽油 42 g · 3 tbsp3 汤匙
  • Doubanjiang豆瓣酱 16 g · 1 tbsp doubanjiang, chopped fine豆瓣酱 1 汤匙，剁细
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · 5 cloves, minced5 瓣，切末
  • Ginger姜 10 g · a knob, minced1 块，切末
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 2 stalks, chopped2 根，切葱花
  • Sugar白糖 12 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)香醋/陈醋 15 g · 1 tbsp black vinegar香醋 1 汤匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp1/2 汤匙
  • Salt盐 3 g · 1/2 tsp, for soaking the eggplant1/2 茶匙，泡茄子用

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Soak the eggplant in cold salted water for 10 minutes, then drain and squeeze gently.茄子条泡进加了盐的冷水 10 分钟，捞出挤干。
  2. Mix the sugar, vinegar, soy, 1 tsp cornstarch and 4 tbsp water into the sauce.糖、醋、生抽、1 茶匙淀粉和 4 汤匙水调成鱼香汁。
  3. Toss the eggplant in 3 tsp cornstarch to coat thinly.茄子条拌上 3 茶匙淀粉，薄薄裹一层。
  4. Fry the eggplant in 2 tbsp oil over medium heat until golden and soft on all sides, about 6 minutes, then lift out.平底锅放 2 汤匙油，中火把茄子煎到每面金黄变软，约 6 分钟，盛出。
  5. Fry the doubanjiang in the remaining oil on low until red, then add the ginger, garlic and onion whites.剩下的油小火炒豆瓣酱出红油，加姜蒜末和葱白炒香。
  6. Return the eggplant, pour in the sauce and toss on high until glossy.茄子回锅，倒入鱼香汁，大火翻匀到汁变亮。
  7. Finish with the onion greens.撒葱绿出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Salt-soaked eggplant is already full of water, so it soaks up far less oil and does not darken.盐水泡过的茄子先吸了水，煎的时候就不会大量吸油，也不容易发黑。
  • Mixing the sauce ahead keeps the sweet-sour balance consistent and the cooking calm.鱼香汁先调好一次倒入，糖醋比例才稳定，不会手忙脚乱。
  • Fry the bean paste slowly until the oil turns red; raw doubanjiang tastes harsh.豆瓣酱要小火炒出红油，生豆瓣有一股生酱味。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The eggplant is oil-logged茄子吸了满满的油
    It was not soaked or squeezed. Next time soak 10 minutes, squeeze and coat before frying.没泡盐水或没挤干。下次泡足 10 分钟、挤干、裹淀粉再煎。
  • It turned dark and grey颜色发黑
    Cut eggplant was left in the air too long. Drop it into the salt water as you cut.切好后放在空气里太久。切完立刻泡进盐水。
