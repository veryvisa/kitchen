Karaage Fried Chicken日式炸鸡

Japanese karaage is crisp on the outside and juicy within. It is best served hot, but can also go into a chilled lunchbox if cooled completely first and kept cold during transport. Home batches usually go wrong when wet chicken sheds its starch, unstable oil turns the crust greasy, or overcrowding drops the oil temperature.日式炸鸡咬开外壳酥脆、里面多汁，趁热吃最酥脆；装进便当冷吃也可以，但要先彻底冷却并保冷携带。家里做容易翻车的地方在于鸡肉水分没吸干导致脱粉、油温不稳吸油发软，或是分批炸时一次下太多让油温骤降。

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 450 g · about 450 g boneless skin-on chicken thighs (about 3 thighs)去骨带皮鸡腿肉约 450 克（约 3 块） skin-on stays juicier and crisper, though skinless works带皮更酥脆多汁，也可去皮
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1.5 tbsp (22 mL)1.5 汤匙（22 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL） sake or mild cooking wine用清酒或淡味料酒
  • Mirin味啉 8 g · 1/2 tbsp (7.5 mL)半汤匙（7.5 mL）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp (5 mL)1 茶匙（5 mL）
  • Ginger姜 12 g · about 12 g, finely grated约 12 克，磨成姜蓉
  • Garlic大蒜 8 g · 2 cloves, finely grated2 瓣，磨成蒜蓉
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · 1/4 tsp1/4 茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 45 g · 5 tbsp5 汤匙
  • All-purpose flour面粉 16 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙

  • Lemons柠檬 60 g · 1/2 lemon, cut into wedges for serving半个柠檬，切角配着吃

Method做法

  1. Trim excess fat from the chicken thighs and cut into even 4 cm pieces.鸡腿肉修去多余肥油，切成约 4 厘米见方的均匀块。
  2. Whisk the soy sauce, cooking wine, mirin, sesame oil, grated ginger, grated garlic and black pepper in a bowl.碗里放生抽、料酒、味淋、香油、姜蓉、蒜蓉和黑胡椒拌匀。
  3. Toss the chicken until evenly coated with the marinade, cover and refrigerate for 20 minutes.放入鸡块抓匀，使每块都均匀沾上腌料，盖好放入冰箱冷藏腌制 20 分钟。
  4. Blot off excess marinade with paper towels and pick off any large bits of ginger or garlic clinging to the surface; it does not need to be bone dry.腌好后用厨房纸吸去表面多余腌汁，顺手去掉沾在表面的大块姜蒜碎屑，不必擦到完全干燥。
  5. Mix the cornstarch and flour in a separate bowl, coat the chicken pieces evenly and shake off any loose powder.另一个碗里把玉米淀粉和面粉拌匀，把鸡块分批滚上一层薄粉并抖掉多余浮粉。
  6. Heat at least 3–4 cm of oil in a small deep pan over medium heat to about 170°C; never leave hot oil unattended, and a thermometer is the most reliable way to check, or use fine bubbles streaming off an inserted wooden chopstick as a rough guide without one.小深锅倒油至少 3–4 厘米深，中火加热到约 170°C（切勿让热油无人看管；最好用油温计，没有时可插进木筷子，冒出细密小泡作粗略参考）。
  7. Fry the chicken in two uncrowded batches for 4 to 5 minutes until set and pale gold, then lift onto a wire rack in a single layer (not stacked) for 3 minutes.分两批放入鸡块，互不粘连，炸 4–5 分钟至表面定形呈浅金色，捞出单层放在晾网上静置 3 分钟，不要堆叠。
  8. Raise the oil to about 190°C over medium-high heat and refry the chicken in two batches, about 45 to 90 seconds each, until deep golden and crisp; check the center of the largest piece with an instant-read thermometer until it reaches 74°C, continuing to fry and rechecking if it has not.把油温升到中大火约 190°C，鸡块分两批放回锅里复炸，每批约 45–90 秒至外壳金黄酥脆；用食品温度计插入最大一块的中心确认达到 74°C，没到就继续复炸并复测。
  9. Drain on the wire rack for 1 minute and serve hot with lemon wedges.捞出放晾网上沥油 1 分钟，装盘配柠檬角趁热上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Evenly sized pieces finish at the same time instead of small bits burning while large ones stay raw.鸡块切大小一致，同一锅才会同时熟透，不会小的焦了大的还没熟。
  • Combining cornstarch with a little flour keeps the crust crisp while locking in juices so cooled leftovers stay tender.粉衣用淀粉加少量面粉，纯玉米淀粉外壳最脆，加一点面粉能锁住肉汁，放凉后也不容易发硬。
  • Double frying at 170°C then 190°C lets resting moisture move to the surface so the second fry drives it off for lasting crunch.两次炸（先 170°C 熟透定形、静置 3 分钟、再 190°C 复炸）能让内部水汽散到表皮再蒸发，外壳才会持久酥脆。
  • Grate the ginger and garlic finely so no coarse bits cling to the meat and scorch bitter in hot oil.姜蒜要磨成细蓉，避免大块蒜末沾在鸡肉表面，高温炸时容易先焦黑发苦。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The crust burned dark before the center cooked through外皮发黑发苦，里面还没熟透
    The oil was too hot or the aromatics were too coarse; lower to medium heat around 170°C for the first fry and grate the ginger and garlic fine.油温太高或姜蒜末太粗；下次调到中小火保持 170°C 初炸，并用细擦板磨姜蒜蓉。
  • The crust turned soggy within minutes炸完几分钟外壳就回软湿塌
    You skipped the second fry or stacked hot pieces on a plate where steam trapped them; crisp them back up in a 200°C air fryer or oven for 3 minutes.漏了复炸或刚出锅叠在盘子里闷出了水汽；放回烤网，用 200°C 空气炸锅或烤箱复烤 3 分钟可恢复酥脆。
  • The coating slid off in the oil粉衣一进油锅就脱落
    Wet chicken or premature stirring knocks off the starch; pat dry before dredging and wait 1 minute after dropping them in before nudging.鸡块裹粉前表面太湿或下锅后太早翻动；先吸干汁水再裹粉，下锅后等 1 分钟外壳定形再动。
