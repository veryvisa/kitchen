Stir-fried Greens清炒时蔬

Simple stir-fried greens depend on heat control: undercooked leaves taste raw, while overcooked ones turn limp and watery. Dry the washed greens well, stir-fry over high heat, give thick stems a head start, and you can have them crisp and bright in about two to three minutes.越简单的家常青菜越考验火候：火候不到，吃着生涩；炒过了头，又会发黄出水。关键是菜叶一定要沥干水分，全程旺火快炒，厚实的菜梗先下锅，两三分钟内就能翠绿脆嫩地出锅。

16 min total分钟 (10 + 6)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Bok choy另购：小白菜

Ingredients食材

  • Bok choy小白菜 400 g · about 400 g Shanghai baby bok choy (green-stemmed)上海青（绿柄小白菜）约 400 克 choy sum or you mai cai (A-choy) also work, but both cook faster than bok choy, so shorten the stir-fry time也可换用菜心或油麦菜，但两者比上海青更快熟，翻炒时间要相应缩短
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 3 cloves3 瓣
  • Canola oil菜籽油 21 g · 1.5 tbsp (22 mL) canola oil1 汤匙半（22 mL）芥花籽油
  • Salt盐 3 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙
  • Sugar白糖 2 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. For small heads, halve or quarter lengthwise without cutting all the way through the base, so the wedges stay attached; for larger heads, separate the stems from the leaves.小棵的上海青纵切对半或四瓣（根部不切断，让瓣保持相连）；个头较大的就把菜梗和菜叶分开切。
  2. Rinse thoroughly, especially around the base where grit hides, then spin dry or pat dry with paper towels.彻底冲洗，尤其是根部容易藏泥沙的地方，再甩干或用厨房纸吸干表面水分。
  3. Smash the garlic cloves lightly with the flat of a knife and peel off the skins.蒜瓣用刀面轻轻拍扁并剥去外皮。
  4. Heat a wok or wide skillet over high heat until a drop of water sizzles away instantly, then swirl in the canola oil; for a nonstick pan, add the oil first and heat over medium-high, without preheating it empty.炒锅或平底锅大火烧至滴水立刻蒸发，倒入芥花籽油晃匀润锅；用不粘锅则先放油再中大火加热，不要空烧。
  5. Add the garlic and stir over medium to medium-high heat for about 30 seconds, until fragrant and lightly golden in spots — go by these cues, not the clock.下蒜瓣，中火至中大火煸炒约 30 秒，至闻到蒜香、局部微黄即可，以状态而不是秒数为准。
  6. Return to high heat: if using attached wedges, put them in stem-side down and toss first; if the stems are already separated, stir-fry them alone for about 45 seconds until glossy green.调回大火：如果是带柄的瓣状小白菜，整瓣梗朝下放入，先翻炒让梗受热；如果茎叶已分开，先下菜梗翻炒约 45 秒至表面油亮翠绿。
  7. Add the leaves (or simply toss the whole wedges), salt, and sugar, and stir-fry over high heat for about 2-3 minutes, until the stems are crisp-tender and the leaves are just wilted and bright green — adjust the time to your stove and batch size.倒入菜叶（或把整瓣菜推匀）、盐和白糖，大火急炒约 2–3 分钟，至菜梗脆嫩、菜叶刚变软且保持翠绿，具体时间看火力和菜量调整。
  8. Transfer the greens to a plate right away, leaving any excess liquid behind in the pan.立刻把青菜夹入盘中，锅底多余的汤汁不用倒入。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Drying the greens well stops water from cooling the wok and turning a stir-fry into a steam.青菜下锅前尽量甩干或擦干，带水下锅会拉低油温，把快炒变成水煮。
  • Over high heat, smashed whole cloves are less likely to scorch and turn bitter than minced garlic.大火快炒时，拍蒜比蒜末更不容易炒焦发苦。
  • Adding salt and a pinch of sugar near the end slows water loss so the greens stay crisp.盐和少许糖在后期再放，能减少蔬菜提前出水，吃起来更脆甜。
  • A large pan can handle a modest amount in one batch; if you have more greens or a smaller pan, cook in two batches rather than piling them too deep.锅大、菜量不多时一锅就能炒好；菜量多或锅偏小就分两锅炒，别让菜堆得太厚。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The greens turned dull yellow and pooled with water青菜发黄发软、盘底一摊水
    Crowding a cool pan or cooking too long causes stewing; next time stir-fry in two batches over high heat and stop as soon as the leaves just wilt.火力不够、一次放太多菜或炒太久都会出水；下次分成两锅炒，保持大火，炒到刚变软就出锅。
  • The stems are raw while the leaves are overcooked菜帮还生硬，菜叶已经软烂
    Thick stems need a head start; separate the stems from the leaves before cooking, and stir-fry the stems alone for about 45 seconds before adding the leaves.较厚实的菜梗成熟慢，下锅前把茎叶分开，先单独炒菜梗约 45 秒再放菜叶。
  • The dish tastes a little flat after plating出锅后尝着偏淡
    Taste before plating, and add a pinch more salt if it needs it — do not also add dissolved salt water on top, which can make the seasoning uneven.出锅前尝一口，缺味就再补一点盐，别用化开的盐水重复加盐，以免咸淡失控。
