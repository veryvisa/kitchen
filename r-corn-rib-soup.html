Corn and Pork Rib Soup玉米排骨汤

An everyday Cantonese-style soup. The ribs are blanched, then simmered gently with corn and carrot until the broth is clear and sweet from the corn. The only real skill is keeping the heat low so the broth stays clear and the meat stays tender.广东人和很多家庭的日常靓汤：排骨焯水去血沫，和玉米、胡萝卜一起小火慢煲，汤清而带玉米的自然甜味。难点在火候，滚得太猛汤会浑、肉会柴。

110 min total分钟 (15 + 95)4 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Pork back ribs猪肋排 700 g · pork back ribs, have the butcher cut them into 4-5 cm pieces猪肋排（小排），请肉贩斩成 4–5 厘米段
  • Corn on the cob玉米 500 g · 2 ears of corn, cut into 3 cm rounds2 根玉米，切 3 厘米圆段 fresh is sweetest, frozen corn pieces also work越新鲜越甜，冷冻玉米段也可以
  • Carrots胡萝卜 200 g · 2 carrots, cut in chunks2 根胡萝卜，切滚刀块
  • Ginger姜 25 g · 6 slices6 片
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 20 g · 2 stalks, sliced, for garnish2 根，切葱花，出锅撒
  • Salt盐 7 g · a little over 1 tsp, added at the end约 1 茶匙多一点，最后加

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Soak the ribs in cold water for 20 minutes, changing the water once to draw out blood.排骨用冷水泡 20 分钟，中途换水一次，泡出血水。
  2. Put the ribs in a large pot, cover with cold water and add 3 slices of ginger and the wine.排骨放进大锅，加冷水没过，放 3 片姜和料酒。
  3. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, skim off the scum and boil 3 minutes more.中大火烧开，撇去浮沫，再煮 3 分钟。
  4. Lift out the ribs, rinse off any scum with warm water, drain, and rinse the pot.捞出排骨，用温水冲净表面的浮沫，沥干，锅洗净。
  5. Return the ribs to the pot with the other 3 slices of ginger and about 2.2 L of hot water.排骨放回锅里，放剩下的 3 片姜，倒入约 2.2 升热水。
  6. As soon as it boils, turn the heat to low and leave the lid ajar so the surface only just trembles with small bubbles.烧开后立刻转小火，盖子留一条缝，让汤面只是微微冒小泡。
  7. Simmer 45 minutes, skimming once or twice.煲 45 分钟，期间撇一两次浮沫。
  8. Add the corn and carrots and keep it at a low simmer for another 35 minutes.放入玉米和胡萝卜，保持小火再煲 35 分钟。
  9. Test a rib; the meat should pull easily off the bone.夹一块排骨，肉能轻松从骨头上拉下来就是好了。
  10. Skim the fat off the surface, stir in 6 g of salt, taste and add up to 1 g more.撇掉表面的浮油，加盐 6 克，搅匀尝味，不够再补 1 克。
  11. Ladle into bowls, scatter the green onion and serve hot.盛入碗中，撒葱花，趁热吃。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Start the ribs in cold water; hot water sets the surface protein at once and traps the scum, which leaves the soup cloudy and gamey.排骨一定冷水下锅焯水：热水会让表面蛋白马上收紧，血沫锁在里面，汤会腥而浑。
  • Keep it at a bare simmer; a rolling boil whips fat and protein into the broth, making it cloudy and the meat dry.全程小火：大滚会把脂肪和蛋白打散进汤里，汤变白变浑，肉也会变柴。
  • Salt at the end; the soup concentrates as it cooks, so early salt overshoots and tightens the meat.盐放在最后：汤煲久了会浓缩，早放盐容易过咸，也会让肉质发紧。
  • Add the corn late; 35 minutes gives its sweetness, much longer just turns it mushy.玉米不要太早放：煲 35 分钟刚好出甜味，更久只会变得没有口感。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The soup is cloudy and smells gamey汤很浑、有腥味
    Strain through a fine sieve or paper towel, add a slice of ginger and simmer gently 10 minutes; next time skim and rinse the ribs properly after blanching.用细筛或厨房纸过滤一次，补一小片姜再微沸 10 分钟；下次焯水要撇干净并冲洗排骨。
  • The soup tastes thin汤太淡没味
    Add salt first, as it is often just under-seasoned; if still thin, simmer uncovered 15 minutes to concentrate.先加盐再说：很多时候只是缺盐；还不够就敞盖煮 15 分钟浓缩。
  • The ribs are still tough排骨肉还是硬
    Keep simmering another 20 minutes; ribs need time to soften, and high heat only makes them dry.继续小火煲 20 分钟，肋排的肉要煲到软才从骨头上掉；别开大火赶时间。
