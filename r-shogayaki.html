Japanese Ginger Pork (Shogayaki)日式生姜烧猪肉

One of the most common Japanese home set meals. Thin pork slices dusted with flour are seared, then glazed in ginger, soy and mirin, served beside a heap of raw shredded cabbage. Costco pork loin, half-frozen and sliced at home, works well.日本家庭定食里最常见的一道。薄猪肉片沾一点面粉，大火煎香，再用姜汁、酱油、味啉一裹就好，旁边放一堆生的卷心菜丝。Costco 的猪里脊冻到半硬自己切片就行。

18 min total分钟 (10 + 8)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Mirin, Shaoxing wine另购：味啉、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Pork loin猪里脊 300 g · 300 g pork loin, sliced 3 mm thin猪里脊 300 克，切 3 毫米薄片 30 minutes in the freezer makes it easy to slice冻 30 分钟到半硬更好切
  • All-purpose flour面粉 8 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Onions洋葱 100 g · half an onion, sliced半个洋葱，切丝
  • Ginger姜 15 g · a thumb-sized piece, grated1 块（拇指大小），磨蓉
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1.5 tbsp1.5 汤匙
  • Mirin味啉 30 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (sake is more authentic)1 汤匙（清酒更地道）
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Green cabbage包菜 150 g · 1/6 head green cabbage, shredded very fine卷心菜 1/6 个，切极细丝

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Mix the grated ginger, soy, mirin, wine and sugar into a sauce.姜蓉、生抽、味啉、料酒和糖调成酱汁。
  2. Soak the shredded cabbage in ice water for 5 minutes, drain well and plate it.卷心菜丝泡冰水 5 分钟，沥干装盘。
  3. Dust the pork slices lightly on both sides with flour.猪肉片两面薄薄拍一层面粉。
  4. Heat the oil in a frying pan over high heat and lay in the pork for a minute until golden underneath.平底锅大火烧热油，肉片平铺，煎 1 分钟到底面金黄。
  5. Turn the slices and add the onion for 1 minute.翻面，加洋葱丝一起煎 1 分钟。
  6. Pour in the sauce and toss for a minute until it thickens and coats the meat.倒入酱汁，翻动 1 分钟到汁变稠、裹住肉片。
  7. Serve with the sauce beside the cabbage.连汁盛在卷心菜丝旁边。

Keys to success成功要点

  • The flour helps the sauce cling and keeps the slices from seizing up.肉片沾面粉，酱汁才挂得住，肉也不会一煎就缩成一团。
  • Grate the ginger fresh and use its juice too; ginger is the star.姜蓉要现磨，连汁一起用，姜香是这道菜的主角。
  • The sugar and mirin burn quickly, so take it off as soon as the sauce thickens.酱汁有糖和味啉，下锅后很快会焦，看到变稠就出锅。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The pork is tough肉片硬
    Sliced too thick or cooked too long. Cut 3 mm and keep the total cooking under 3 minutes.切太厚或煎太久。切到 3 毫米，总共煎不超过 3 分钟。
  • The sauce burned酱汁焦了发苦
    Drop to medium before adding the sauce, and add 1 tbsp water if it is reducing too fast.倒酱汁前把火调到中火；汁太少时加 1 汤匙水。
