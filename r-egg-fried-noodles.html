Egg Fried Noodles鸡蛋炒面

A fast weeknight pantry stir-fry using staples every home kitchen keeps on hand: eggs, cabbage, and carrots. The common mistakes are overcooking the eggs and breaking the noodles, so scramble the eggs softly first, remove them, and fold them back in after the noodles and vegetables are tossed together.北美华人家里最省事的快手炒面，冰箱常备的鸡蛋、卷心菜和胡萝卜十分钟就能上桌。新手常把鸡蛋炒老、面条炒碎，先用热油把蛋炒到嫩滑盛出，面和蔬菜炒匀后再回锅，蛋香和面香才不会混成一锅糊。

16 min total分钟 (8 + 8)2 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dark soy sauce另购：老抽

Ingredients食材

  • Wheat noodles挂面/面条 200 g · 200 g dried medium-thin wheat noodles中细干面条或挂面 200 克 choose a firm round or alkaline wheat noodle that holds its shape; fresh chow mein noodles from the supermarket also work, about 280 g, blanched per the package选不易煮烂的细圆干面或碱水面；也可用北美超市的新鲜炒面（chow mein noodles）约 280 克代替，按包装汆烫
  • Eggs鸡蛋 140 g · 3 large eggs, beaten3 个大鸡蛋，打散
  • Green cabbage包菜 120 g · 120 g cabbage, thinly shredded卷心菜 120 克，切细丝
  • Carrots胡萝卜 60 g · 1/2 medium carrot, julienned半根中等胡萝卜，切细丝
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 25 g · 2 stalks, sliced2 根，切葱花
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 2 cloves, minced2 瓣，切末
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1.5 tbsp (22 mL)1.5 汤匙（22 mL）
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp (7 mL)半汤匙（7 mL）
  • Sugar白糖 2 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 0.6 g · 1/4 tsp ground black or white pepper1/4 茶匙，现磨黑胡椒或白胡椒
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL), divided2 汤匙（30 mL），分次用
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 7 g · 1/2 tbsp (7 mL)半汤匙（7 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Whisk the soy sauce, oyster sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, and pepper together in a small bowl.把生抽、蚝油、老抽、糖和胡椒粉在小碗里调匀备用。
  2. Add the noodles to a pot of water at a full rolling boil, keep the heat high, stir with chopsticks to separate them, and cook 3 minutes until firm with a slight bite in the center; drain and rinse under cold water for 10 seconds.锅中水大滚后下面条，保持大火，用筷子抖散，煮 3 分钟至八分熟仍有嚼劲，捞出过凉水 10 秒。
  3. Drain the noodles thoroughly and toss them with the sesame oil to keep the strands separate.彻底沥干面条后拌入香油，抖散备用。
  4. Heat 1 tbsp canola oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat, pour in the beaten eggs, and scramble for 45 seconds until just set in soft curds, then transfer to a plate.炒锅中大火烧热，倒 1 汤匙菜籽油，下蛋液划散炒 45 秒至刚刚凝固成大块嫩蛋后盛出。
  5. Wipe any scorched egg bits from the pan, and wash any bowl or chopsticks that touched the raw egg before reusing them.用厨房纸擦净锅底焦蛋渣，碰过生蛋液的碗和筷子先洗净再用。
  6. Add the remaining 1 tbsp canola oil to the pan, reduce to medium heat (the pan is still very hot from the eggs), and stir-fry the garlic and half the scallions for about 10 seconds until fragrant but not browned.锅里补入剩下 1 汤匙菜籽油，转中火下蒜末和一半葱花爆香约 10 秒（刚炒完蛋的锅还很热，中火防止蒜末变焦）。
  7. Add the shredded carrots and cabbage and stir-fry over high heat for 90 seconds until softened and slightly translucent.倒入胡萝卜丝和卷心菜丝，大火翻炒 1 分半钟至菜丝变软、边缘微透亮。
  8. Add the noodles and pour in the sauce (whisk 2 tbsp water into the sauce first to help the oyster sauce and sugar dissolve), tossing with chopsticks and a spatula over medium-high heat for 90 seconds until evenly coated.放入面条并淋入调好的酱汁（调酱汁时加 2 汤匙水帮蚝油和糖化开），用筷子配合锅铲中大火翻拌 1 分半钟至上色均匀。
  9. Return the cooked eggs and remaining scallions to the pan and toss over high heat for 30 seconds until evenly mixed, then serve.倒回炒蛋和剩下葱花，大火兜匀 30 秒至蛋块均匀分布后出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Scramble the eggs quickly over medium-high heat and pull them out while still glossy and just set so they stay tender after reheating.鸡蛋下锅用中大火快炒，刚凝固、表面还带一点湿润光泽就盛出，回锅再热一次才不会又干又硬。
  • A brief cold rinse washes excess surface starch off everyday wheat noodles and firms the strands so they do not snap or stick.普通挂面煮好后过一下凉水能洗去表面多余淀粉并收紧面身，炒的时候不容易粘锅断碎。
  • Cut the cabbage and carrots into thin strips about the length of the noodles so they cook quickly and tangle neatly with every bite.卷心菜和胡萝卜切成和面条长度相近的细丝，尽量切细，既容易熟透，夹起来也能和面条裹在一起。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The noodles broke into short pieces and turned mushy面条炒断成小段，吃起来粘糊
    Pull the noodles out while still slightly firm and rinse and drain well; toss with chopsticks rather than chopping with a metal spatula.挂面煮到八分熟就得捞出过凉沥干；翻炒时多用筷子挑散，少用锅铲用力铲切。
  • The eggs turned dry, rubbery, and shredded into tiny bits鸡蛋炒得又老又碎，吃不出蛋香
    Whisk 1 tbsp water into the eggs next time for tenderness, remove them as soon as soft curds form, and fold them in only at the very end.下次打蛋时加 1 汤匙清水能让蛋更嫩，看到蛋液刚凝结成块就盛出，最后再回锅拌匀即可。
  • The pan was too dry and the seasoning coated unevenly锅底太干，面条颜色深一块浅一块
    Premix the sauce before adding it; if the noodles still look dry and patchy, splash 1 tbsp hot water around the pan rim to help the glaze distribute.酱汁提前拌匀，淋在面条上后如仍觉得干，沿着锅边淋 1 汤匙热水帮助酱汁散开。
