Chicken Noodle Soup美式鸡肉蔬菜面汤

The pot North American families make when someone has a cold. Onion, carrot and celery soften in butter, chicken thighs poach in the stock and are shredded, and egg noodles go in at the end. It makes several meals and freezes well; cook the noodles separately if you plan leftovers, or they drink up the broth overnight.北美家家户户感冒时都会煮的一锅汤。洋葱、胡萝卜、西芹先用黄油炒香，鸡腿肉在高汤里煮熟撕成丝，最后放鸡蛋面。一锅分几顿，冻起来也方便；面条最好吃之前另煮，不然隔夜会吸干汤。

50 min total分钟 (15 + 35)6 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 small onion, diced1 个小洋葱，切丁
  • Carrots胡萝卜 200 g · 2 carrots, sliced2 根胡萝卜，切片
  • Celery西芹 150 g · 3 celery stalks, sliced3 根西芹，切片

Method做法

  1. Heat the butter and oil in a large pot over medium heat and soften the onion, carrot and celery for 6 minutes.大锅中火加热黄油和橄榄油，下洋葱、胡萝卜、西芹炒 6 分钟到变软。
  2. Add the garlic and thyme for 30 seconds.加蒜末和百里香炒 30 秒。
  3. Pour in the stock and 4 cups water, add the bay leaf and the whole chicken thighs and bring to a boil.倒入高汤和 4 杯水，放月桂叶和整块鸡腿肉，烧开。
  4. Simmer 20 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.转小火煮 20 分钟到鸡肉熟透。
  5. Lift out the chicken and shred it with two forks.捞出鸡肉，用两把叉子撕成丝。
  6. Bring the soup back to a boil and cook the noodles for the time on the packet.汤烧开，下鸡蛋面煮到包装时间。
  7. Return the chicken, remove the bay leaf, season with salt and pepper and add the parsley.鸡丝放回锅里，取出月桂叶，加盐和胡椒，撒欧芹。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Thighs stay juicy through the simmer and flavour the broth better than breast.用鸡腿肉比鸡胸嫩，煮 20 分钟也不柴，汤也更香。
  • Keep it at a gentle simmer; a hard boil clouds the broth and toughens the chicken.汤要微滚，大滚会让汤浑、鸡肉变硬。
  • For leftovers, cook the noodles separately and add them per bowl so they do not soak up the broth.要留隔夜的，面条另锅煮、吃时再放，汤才不会被吸干。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The noodles went mushy面条煮糊了
    Overcooked or left sitting in the soup. Cook a minute less than the packet; the hot broth finishes them.煮太久或在汤里泡太久。按包装时间少煮 1 分钟，余温会继续煮软。
  • It tastes flat味道寡淡
    It needs salt. Season each bowl, and a squeeze of lemon brightens it right away.盐不够。按碗加一点盐，挤几滴柠檬汁会让味道立刻鲜亮。
