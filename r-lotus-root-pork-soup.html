Lotus Root and Pork Rib Soup莲藕排骨汤

A comforting Cantonese home soup where starchy lotus root, pork ribs, and peanuts simmer into a gently sweet, savory broth. It takes very little technique: blanch the ribs starting in cold water, choose mature, starchy lotus root, and let a gentle simmer do the work.广式家庭常煲的一锅暖汤，粉糯的莲藕配上排骨和红皮花生，汤色清润，带着莲藕和花生的自然香气，喝起来自然回甘。做法几乎没有门槛，只要排骨冷水焯透、选对粉藕，小火慢煲一小时出锅就很鲜。

Ingredients食材

  • Pork back ribs猪肋排 600 g · about 600 g pork spareribs or baby back ribs, cut into 4 cm pieces约 600 克猪肋排或小排，剁 4 厘米段
  • Lotus root莲藕 500 g · 1 thick starchy lotus root (about 500 g)1 节粗壮老藕（约 500 克） look for plump, brownish roots for a tender, floury texture选表皮偏黄褐、节短粗的粉藕，炖完更绵软
  • Roasted peanuts花生 50 g · about 1/3 cup (50 g) raw skin-on peanuts约 1/3 杯（50 克）生红皮花生
  • Ginger姜 20 g · 5 slices ginger5 片姜
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 2 scallions, 1 knotted for simmering and 1 chopped2 根葱，1 根打结炖汤，1 根切花
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL), for blanching1 汤匙（15 mL），焯水用
  • Salt盐 6 g · about 1 tsp, added at the end约 1 茶匙，最后调味

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Soak the raw peanuts in cold water for 30 minutes, then drain.生花生提前用清水浸泡 30 分钟，沥干备用。
  2. Place the ribs in a pot of cold water with 2 ginger slices, the knotted scallion, and the cooking wine, and bring to a boil over high heat.排骨冷水下锅，加 2 片姜、葱结和料酒，大火烧开。
  3. Boil for 3 minutes, skim off the scum, lift out the ribs, and rinse them under warm water.保持沸腾煮 3 分钟，撇净浮沫后捞出排骨，用温水冲净表面杂质。
  4. Peel the lotus root, trim the ends, cut into 2 cm chunks, and rinse any grit from the holes.莲藕削去外皮和两头蒂结，切成约 2 厘米厚的滚刀块并冲净藕孔里的泥沙。
  5. Put the ribs, lotus root, soaked peanuts, and remaining 3 ginger slices in a soup pot and pour in about 2 L boiling water.汤锅里放入排骨、莲藕块、泡好的花生和剩下的 3 片姜，倒入约 2 升开水。
  6. Bring to a boil, skim any foam, cover, and simmer over the lowest heat for 70 minutes.大火烧开后撇去表面细沫，转最小火加盖保持微沸慢炖 70 分钟。
  7. Season with the salt 5 minutes before turning off the heat and stir until dissolved.关火前 5 分钟加盐调味，搅匀至盐融化。
  8. Ladle into bowls and top with chopped scallion to serve.盛入汤碗，撒上葱花即可上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Choose mature, starchy, plump tan-skinned lotus root for a soft, tender texture after simmering; crisp-textured varieties tend to stay firmer even after long simmering.想要莲藕粉糯绵软，要挑节短粗壮、表皮偏黄褐的七孔老藕；脆嫩白藕更适合清炒，久煮也不易粉。
  • Use a clay, enameled, or stainless pot rather than bare cast iron, which reacts with lotus root tannins and darkens the broth.炖莲藕汤尽量用砂锅、陶瓷锅或不锈钢锅，避免铁锅与莲藕里的单宁反应导致汤色发黑发涩。
  • Season toward the end so you can adjust the salt level after the broth has reduced.盐留到最后调味，方便根据汤的浓淡调整咸度，也能避免煲汤过程中水分蒸发后汤味过咸。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The broth turned dark or the lotus root stayed crunchy汤色发黑、莲藕吃起来发硬发脆
    Cast iron discolors the broth, and crisp-textured varieties of lotus root tend to retain more firmness even after prolonged simmering; use a non-reactive pot and simmer starchy roots another 20 minutes if still firm.用铁锅煲或买到脆藕都会出现这种情况；下次改用砂锅或不锈钢锅，若藕偏硬再加盖小火多炖 20 分钟。
  • The soup looks greasy or tastes porky汤面浮油多、喝起来腥腻
    Blanch the ribs starting in cold water for a full 3 minutes and rinse thoroughly, then skim the surface fat before serving.排骨冷水下锅要焯够 3 分钟再洗净；喝之前用汤勺撇掉表层浮油就会清甜不腻。
