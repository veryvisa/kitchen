Roast Turkey烤火鸡

A whole roast turkey is the centrepiece of holiday meals, and the challenge is heat control rather than seasoning: the breast dries fast while the legs need longer. Butter and herbs go under the skin, the bird roasts low and slow with regular basting, the thickest part reaches a safe temperature, and it rests before carving.整只烤火鸡是节日餐桌的主角，难点不在调味，而在火候：胸肉容易先干，腿肉需要更长时间。先把黄油香料抹到皮下，低温慢烤、中途淋汁，最后在最厚处测到安全温度，出炉必须静置再切。

Method做法

  1. Thaw the turkey in the fridge for 2-3 days, remove the giblet bag, rinse and pat the inside and outside completely dry.火鸡提前在冷藏室解冻 2–3 天，取出内脏袋，冲洗后用厨房纸把里外彻底擦干。
  2. Mix the softened butter with the garlic, salt, pepper, thyme and rosemary to make herb butter.软化黄油拌入蒜碎、盐、黑胡椒、百里香和迷迭香，调成香料黄油。
  3. Slide your fingers under the breast and thigh skin and rub half the herb butter onto the meat.手指从胸皮与肉之间伸入，把一半香料黄油抹到胸肉和大腿的皮下。
  4. Rub the rest of the butter over the skin, and pack the cavity with the onion, carrots, celery and lemon halves.剩下的黄油抹在鸡皮表面，膛内填入洋葱、胡萝卜、芹菜和柠檬两半。
  5. Set a rack in a roasting pan, place the turkey breast side up and tuck the wing tips behind the back.烤架放在烤盘上，火鸡胸朝上放好，翅膀尖折到背后。
  6. Preheat the oven to 160°C and roast covered with foil for the first 90 minutes.烤箱预热到 160°C，先盖锡纸烤 90 分钟。
  7. Remove the foil and baste the skin with pan juices every 30 minutes for about another 90 minutes.揭开锡纸，每 30 分钟舀烤盘里的汁淋在皮上，继续烤约 90 分钟。
  8. Insert a thermometer into the thickest part of the thigh, away from the bone, and take the turkey out once it reaches 82°C.温度计插入大腿最厚处，不碰骨头，达到 82°C 即取出。
  9. Transfer the turkey to a platter, tent loosely with foil and let it rest for 30 minutes before carving.火鸡转到大浅盘，松松盖上锡纸，静置 30 分钟再切。
  10. Set the roasting pan on the stovetop, stir in the flour for 1 minute, whisk in the stock gradually and simmer 5 minutes for gravy.烤盘留在炉头上，撒面粉炒 1 分钟，分次倒入鸡汤搅化，小火煮 5 分钟成肉汁。
  11. Carve the breast and legs and serve with the gravy.切下胸肉、腿肉，配肉汁上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • A dry skin is what turns crisp; moisture going in steams the skin instead of roasting it.擦干表皮是脆皮的前提；带水分进炉，皮会焖软而不是烤香。
  • Judge doneness in the thickest thigh, not the breast; at 82°C in the thigh, the breast is also safe.安全温度看大腿最厚处，不要只看胸肉；大腿达到 82°C 时胸肉也刚好熟透。
  • Resting for 30 minutes lets the juices settle; otherwise they run out onto the board when carved.静置 30 分钟让肉汁回流，切开时汁水不会全部流到盘底。
  • Cavity herbs are only aromatics; do not pack them tight, so air can circulate and the bird cooks evenly.膛内香料只做底味，不要塞太满，留出空气循环，受热才均匀。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The breast is dry and stringy胸肉又干又柴
    Rub butter under the skin and keep foil on early to stop moisture loss. Next time tent just the breast loosely with foil in the later stage and baste the legs more, but still pull the bird only once the thigh reaches 82°C, not earlier by feel.黄油抹进皮下保湿，前期盖锡纸防表皮失水；下次可在后段单独给胸肉松松盖一层锡纸、腿肉多淋汁，但出炉时间仍以大腿测到 82°C 为准，不要提早凭感觉取出。
  • The skin is pale and chewy皮色发白、嚼着发韧
    Remove foil for the last 20 minutes and raise to 190°C for a golden skin. Keep the skin dry before roasting and avoid splashing liquid onto it while basting.出炉前 20 分钟揭开锡纸，调高到 190°C 烤到皮色金棕；烤前皮要擦干，淋汁时别把水反复浇在皮上。
  • The thigh near the bone is undercooked腿根还带血丝、没熟透
    Return it to the oven and recheck the thigh every 15 minutes until 82°C. Next time let the bird sit at room temperature for an hour so it heats evenly.回炉继续烤，每 15 分钟复测大腿厚处到 82°C；下次整只先在室温放 1 小时再进炉，冷热一致更易熟透。
  • The gravy is lumpy with flour肉汁结块有面疙瘩
    Cook the flour smooth in the pan fat first, whisking in cold stock gradually. If lumps form, strain the gravy through a sieve and bring it back to a simmer.面粉先在烤盘底油里炒散，再一边搅一边分次加冷鸡汤；已结块就过一遍细筛再回炉煮开。
