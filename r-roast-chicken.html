Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables烤全鸡（西式，配根茎蔬菜）

A whole chicken salted ahead and left to air-dry, roasted hot to crisp the skin and then lower to cook through, over potatoes, carrots and onion that soak up the drippings. The one thing to do properly is measure doneness with a thermometer in the thigh.整只鸡提前抹盐风干，高温先烤出脆皮，再降温烤透，下面垫的土豆、胡萝卜和洋葱吸足鸡油。难点是不要凭感觉判断熟度，用温度计量最厚的鸡腿肉。

170 min total分钟 (25 + 70)4 servings份Medium中等Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Whole chicken整鸡 1600 g · 1 whole chicken (about 1.6 kg), giblets removed整鸡 1 只（约 1.6 公斤），去掉内脏
  • Salt盐 13 g · a little over 2 tsp fine salt: 11 g for the chicken, 2 g for the vegetables约 2 茶匙多一点细盐，其中 11 克涂鸡、2 克拌蔬菜
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 2 g · 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper1 茶匙现磨黑胡椒
  • Butter黄油 30 g · 2 tbsp, softened2 汤匙，室温软化
  • Potatoes土豆 500 g · baby or yellow potatoes, cut in 4 cm chunks小土豆或黄土豆，切 4 厘米块
  • Carrots胡萝卜 300 g · 3 carrots, cut in 4 cm pieces, thick ones halved lengthwise3 根胡萝卜，切 4 厘米段，粗的对半切
  • Onions洋葱 200 g · 2 small onions, each cut in 6 wedges2 个小洋葱，各切 6 瓣
  • Garlic大蒜 50 g · 1 whole head, halved across1 整头，横切对半
  • Lemons柠檬 100 g · 1 lemon, halved1 个，对半切
  • Dried thyme干百里香 2 g · 1 tsp; or 6-8 fresh thyme sprigs1 茶匙；有新鲜百里香用 6–8 枝
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels and rub 11 g of salt all over, inside and out.鸡洗净后用厨房纸擦干，内外均匀抹 11 克盐。
  2. Set it on a plate uncovered in the fridge for at least 1 hour, ideally overnight.不盖盖放在盘里，冷藏 1 小时以上，最好过夜。
  3. Put a rack in the lower-middle position and preheat the oven to 220 C (425 F).烤箱架放中下层，预热到 220°C（425°F）。
  4. Toss the potatoes, carrots and onions with the olive oil, 2 g salt and half the thyme, and spread them in a large roasting pan.土豆、胡萝卜、洋葱加橄榄油、2 克盐和一半百里香拌匀，平铺在大烤盘里。
  5. Dry the chicken again, rub the butter under and over the skin and season with pepper and the remaining thyme.鸡再擦一次表面，皮下和表面抹上软化的黄油，撒胡椒和剩下的百里香。
  6. Stuff the cavity with the lemon and garlic, tie the legs together with string and tuck the wing tips behind the back.鸡腔里塞入柠檬和蒜头，用棉线把两只鸡腿绑在一起，翅尖折到背后。
  7. Set the chicken breast-side up on the vegetables and roast 20 minutes, until the skin begins to colour.鸡胸朝上放在蔬菜上，送入烤箱烤 20 分钟，直到鸡皮开始上色。
  8. Lower to 190 C (375 F) and roast another 40-50 minutes, stirring the vegetables once.把温度降到 190°C（375°F），再烤 40–50 分钟，中途翻一次蔬菜。
  9. Push a thermometer into the thickest part of the thigh, away from the bone; 82 C (180 F) means it is done.把温度计插进大腿最厚处，避开骨头，读数到 82°C（180°F）就是熟了。
  10. Move the chicken to a board, tent loosely with foil and rest it 15 minutes.鸡移到砧板上，松松盖一层锡纸，静置 15 分钟。
  11. Leave the vegetables in the oven at 220 C for another 10 minutes so the edges brown and crisp.蔬菜留在烤箱里，调到 220°C 再烤 10 分钟，让边缘上色变脆。
  12. Carve off the legs and breasts, plate them and spoon over the pan juices.切下鸡腿和鸡胸，装盘，浇上烤盘里的鸡汁。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Salt early and rest uncovered; the salt seasons the meat and the dried skin crisps in the oven.提前抹盐并不盖盖冷藏：盐渗进肉里调味，表皮风干后烤出来才会脆。
  • Hot then lower: 220 C sets and colours the skin, 190 C finishes the inside gently so it is not burnt outside and raw within.先高温后降温：220°C 让鸡皮上色定型，190°C 让里面温和烤熟，不会外焦里生。
  • Probe the thigh, not the breast; it is the slowest part to cook and the breast stays moist while carryover heat finishes the job.测大腿而不是鸡胸：腿肉最慢熟，腿到 82°C 时鸡胸还嫩，因为静置时温度还会再升一点。
  • Always rest the bird; cutting right away lets the juices run out, while 15 minutes lets them settle back in.烤完一定静置：马上切肉汁会流光，静置 15 分钟肉汁回到肉里。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The skin is not crisp鸡皮不脆
    Return the chicken to a 230 C oven for 5 minutes to crisp the skin; next time dry it well and rest it uncovered in the fridge.取出鸡后把烤箱调到 230°C，只烤鸡皮 5 分钟；下次要把鸡擦干、提前不盖盖冷藏。
  • The breast is dry鸡胸又干又柴
    Slice, spoon hot pan juices over and cover with foil for 5 minutes; next time pull the bird as soon as the thigh reaches 82 C.鸡肉切片后浇热鸡汁盖上锡纸焖 5 分钟；下次提前出炉，测大腿到 82°C 就要取出。
  • The meat near the thigh bone is pink with juice鸡腿近骨处还有血水
    Put the carved leg back in a 190 C oven for 10 minutes until it reads 82 C; next time check the thermometer is not touching bone.切好的腿肉放回 190°C 烤箱再烤 10 分钟，直到读数 82°C；下次先确认温度计没有碰到骨头。
