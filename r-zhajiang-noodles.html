Beijing Zhajiang Noodles老北京炸酱面

The everyday noodle bowl of Beijing's alleys. Diced pork and bean pastes are slowly fried until glossy and deeply savoury, then tossed with noodles and a pile of crisp vegetables. Make a big batch of sauce: it keeps a week in the fridge for quick dinners.北京胡同里最家常的一碗面。肉丁和黄酱、甜面酱小火慢慢「炸」出酱香和油亮，配上黄瓜丝等一堆菜码，拌开就吃。酱可以一次多做，冷藏一周，下班煮个面就是一顿。

40 min total分钟 (15 + 25)3 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Chinese sweet bean sauce (tianmianjiang), Shaoxing wine另购：黄酱/甜面酱、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Wheat noodles挂面/面条 360 g · 360 g thick wheat noodles (hand-cut is best)粗面条 360 克（手擀面更好）
  • Pork shoulder猪梅花肉 250 g · 250 g pork shoulder, cut into 5 mm dice猪梅花肉 250 克，切 5 毫米小丁 pork belly is more traditional and richer五花肉更传统，也更油
  • Chinese sweet bean sauce (tianmianjiang)黄酱/甜面酱 100 g · 3 tbsp each of yellow soybean paste and sweet bean sauce (about 100 g total)黄酱和甜面酱各 3 汤匙（共约 100 克）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 21 g · 1.5 tbsp1.5 汤匙
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Ginger姜 5 g · a few slices, minced几片，切末
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks, chopped3 根，切葱花
  • English cucumber黄瓜 200 g · 1 cucumber, cut into fine strips1 根黄瓜，切细丝
  • Carrots胡萝卜 80 g · half a carrot, cut into fine strips半根胡萝卜，切细丝

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Thin both bean pastes with 100 mL water into a loose paste.两种酱放碗里，加 100 mL 水调成稀糊。
  2. Fry the diced pork in the oil over medium-low heat until the fat runs and it colours a little, about 5 minutes.锅里放油，中小火把肉丁煸到出油、表面微黄，约 5 分钟。
  3. Add the ginger and half the green onion, then splash the wine down the side of the pan.下姜末和一半葱花炒香，沿锅边淋料酒。
  4. Pour in the paste, turn the heat to low and keep stirring as it bubbles.倒入酱糊，转小火，不停翻动慢慢熬。
  5. Cook 10-15 minutes until the sauce darkens and oil rises to the surface, stir in the sugar and the rest of the onion.熬 10–15 分钟到酱色变深、表面浮出一层油，加糖拌匀，撒剩下的葱花。
  6. Cook the noodles as the packet says and rinse briefly in warm water.面条按包装时间煮好，过一下温开水。
  7. Top each bowl with two spoonfuls of sauce and the cucumber and carrot, and toss at the table.面盛碗里，放两勺炸酱和黄瓜丝、胡萝卜丝，吃时拌开。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cook the sauce slowly until the paste coats the meat and oil glistens on top; that is what removes the raw bean taste.酱要小火慢熬，熬到「酱包肉、油包酱」，生酱的豆腥味才会去掉。
  • One paste is salty and one sweet, so use them half and half; either alone is too sweet or too salty.两种酱一咸一甜，按 1 比 1 配；只用甜面酱会太甜，只用黄酱会太咸。
  • The more toppings the more authentic: cucumber, radish, bean sprouts, peas, whatever is in season.菜码越多越地道：黄瓜、心里美萝卜、豆芽、青豆，按季节换。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The sauce caught on the bottom酱糊锅了
    Too much heat or not enough stirring. Keep it low, scrape the bottom, and add 2 tbsp water if it gets too thick.火大或不搅。全程小火、勺子贴锅底搅；酱太干时加 2 汤匙水。
  • Too salty太咸
    Bean paste brands vary a lot; cook it with more water, or use less sauce and more cucumber per bowl.黄酱品牌咸度差别大，多加一些水熬，或拌面时少放酱、多放黄瓜丝。
