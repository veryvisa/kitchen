Xinjiang Big Plate Chicken大盘鸡

Chicken, potatoes and green peppers simmer together in a rich sauce made for sharing at the table. The key is to brown the chicken first and add the vegetables in stages so they keep their shape.鸡肉、土豆和青椒同锅慢炖，汤汁浓厚，适合直接端上桌分享。关键是先煎香鸡肉、再分时加入土豆和青椒，避免配菜炖散。

90 min total分钟 (25 + 65)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dried chili, Star anise, Sichuan peppercorn, Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine另购：干辣椒、八角、花椒、老抽、料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 1300 g · about 6 bone-in chicken thighs, chopped into large pieces带骨鸡腿约 6 个，斩成大块
  • Potatoes土豆 600 g · about 3 medium potatoes, cut into 4 cm chunks约 3 个中等土豆，切 4 厘米块
  • Bell peppers彩椒 300 g · about 2 green bell peppers, seeded and cut into chunks (Anaheim or long green peppers are closer to the traditional Xinjiang pepper if you can find them)约 2 个青椒，去籽切块；也可用安纳海姆辣椒或细长青椒代替，更接近新疆原味
  • Onions洋葱 200 g · 1 large onion, cut into chunks1 个大洋葱，切块
  • Tomatoes西红柿 250 g · 1 large tomato, cut into chunks1 个大番茄，切块
  • Garlic大蒜 18 g · about 5 cloves, lightly crushed约 5 瓣，拍裂
  • Ginger姜 15 g · sliced切片
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · about 3 stalks, cut into pieces约 3 根，切段
  • Dried chili干辣椒 3 g · about 4 dried red chilies, snipped; adjust to taste约 4 个，剪段；按口味调整
  • Star anise八角 2 g · 2 whole2 颗
  • Sichuan peppercorn花椒 1 g · about 1/2 tsp whole Sichuan peppercorns约半茶匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 30 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 450 g · about 1 3/4 cups约 1¾ 杯
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp, adjust to taste约 ⅔ 茶匙，按口味调整

Method做法

  1. Pat the chicken pieces dry with paper towels for about 3 minutes; exposed bone at the cut edges is fine.鸡腿块用厨房纸擦干，切面露出骨头也没关系，约需 3 分钟。
  2. Cut the potatoes into 4 cm chunks and the peppers, onion and tomato into large pieces, about 8 minutes.土豆切成约 4 厘米块，青椒和洋葱切成大块，番茄切块，约需 8 分钟。
  3. Heat the oil in a wide pot over medium heat and brown the chicken in batches for 8 minutes until golden, then set it aside.宽锅中火烧热油，鸡肉分批煎 8 分钟，表面金黄后盛出。
  4. Add the onion to the pot and stir-fry over medium heat for 2 minutes until the edges start to colour.原锅放入洋葱，中火炒 2 分钟至边缘微黄。
  5. Add the ginger, garlic, chilies, star anise and Sichuan pepper; push the onion to one side so the spices sit in the clear oil, fry over low heat for 15-20 seconds until the chilies turn deep red, then stir in the onion with the green onion.加入姜、蒜、干辣椒、八角和花椒，把洋葱推到锅边露出油面，小火炒香料 15–20 秒至辣椒变深红，再与洋葱拌匀；放入葱段一起炒出香气。
  6. Add the tomato and cook over medium heat for 3 minutes until softened and juicy.加入番茄，中火炒 3 分钟至软塌出汁。
  7. Add the soy sauces and cooking wine, and stir over medium heat for 30 seconds until fragrant.倒入生抽、老抽和料酒，中火翻匀 30 秒至酱香明显。
  8. Return the chicken and pour in the stock, bring to a boil over high heat, then cover and simmer on low for 20 minutes; check the liquid level at the 10-minute mark and top up with 100 mL hot water if it is running low.放回鸡肉并倒入鸡汤，大火煮开后转小火，加盖炖 20 分钟；炖到第 10 分钟时打开看一下汤面，不够可以加 100 mL 热水，避免煮干。
  9. Check the centre of the thickest chicken piece with a thermometer and confirm it has reached 74°C (if not, cover and simmer a few more minutes before rechecking); once confirmed, add the potatoes and salt, then cover and simmer over medium-low heat for 15 minutes until a chopstick enters the potatoes but they still hold their shape.用温度计测最厚鸡肉中心，确认达到 74°C（没到就加盖再炖几分钟后复测）；确认后加入土豆和盐，中小火加盖炖 15 分钟，直到筷子能插入土豆但土豆仍完整。
  10. Add the peppers, cover and cook over medium heat for 5 minutes until bright and just softened.放入青椒，中火加盖炖 5 分钟至颜色鲜亮、刚刚变软。
  11. Before serving, do a final check: the sauce should lightly coat a spoon and the chicken should be easy to pierce with chopsticks — then it is ready.上桌前再看一眼：汤汁应该略挂勺，鸡肉用筷子能轻松戳透，这样就可以关火上桌了。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Dry the chicken before browning so it colours well; work in batches if the pot is crowded to prevent steaming.鸡肉下锅前擦干，煎时才容易上色；锅里太挤就分批煎，避免出水变成焖煮。
  • Cut the potatoes into large chunks and add them later so they absorb the sauce without falling apart; once they go in, stir gently by lifting from the bottom with a spatula rather than stirring hard, or they can stick and break apart.土豆切大块并在后段加入，能吸收汤汁又不容易碎；下锅后翻动要轻，用锅铲从底部托起，不要大力搅拌，否则容易粘锅底、炖散。
  • Add the peppers near the end to keep their colour and a little bite.青椒最后几分钟再放，保持颜色和一点脆度。
  • Stock and soy sauce are already salty, so start light on salt and adjust after tasting.鸡汤和酱油已有咸味，盐先少放，收尾尝过再补。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The potatoes broke down and clouded the sauce土豆炖散，汤汁发浑
    If the sauce has caught on the bottom, switch to a clean pot rather than scraping the bottom; lift out the chicken and any whole potatoes first, pour the remaining sauce into the new pot, bring it back to a boil with a little hot water, then return the chicken and potatoes. Next time cut the potatoes larger, add them after the chicken has simmered, and keep the heat low.汤汁糊底就换一个干净的锅，不要刮锅底；把鸡肉和完整的土豆先盛出，剩下的汤倒进新锅，加一点热水煮开后再把鸡肉和土豆浇回去。下次切大块、等鸡肉炖过再加土豆，并用小火。
  • The chicken looks pale and the sauce tastes thin鸡肉表面发白、汤汁寡淡
    Simmer uncovered over medium heat for 3-5 minutes until the sauce lightly coats a spoon; next time dry and brown the chicken in batches.继续开盖中火煮 3–5 分钟至汤汁略挂勺；下次先擦干鸡肉并分批煎上色。
  • The chicken is not cooked through鸡肉中心还没熟
    Cover and simmer on low for another 5 minutes, then recheck the thickest centre for 74°C; do not judge doneness by colour alone.加盖小火继续炖 5 分钟，再测最厚处中心达到 74°C；不要只凭表面颜色判断。

This pot combines chicken, potatoes and vegetables and can be served with rice or noodles. Soy sauce and stock add sodium; choose lower-sodium stock and adjust the salt to taste. Tossing the sauce with wide hand-pulled noodles is the traditional Xinjiang way to finish the dish; udon or any wide pasta works as a substitute.这锅有鸡肉、土豆和蔬菜，适合搭配米饭或面食分餐食用；酱油和鸡汤会增加钠含量，可选低钠鸡汤并按口味减盐。汤汁拌宽面（皮带面）是新疆的传统吃法，没有宽面可以用乌冬面或意面代替。
