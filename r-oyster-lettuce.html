Lettuce with Oyster Sauce蚝油生菜

The cheapest and most ordered greens in any Cantonese restaurant. Lettuce gets a few seconds in boiling water and a garlicky oyster sauce glaze; ten minutes start to finish. Crunchy North American romaine works especially well.粤菜馆里最便宜也最常点的青菜。生菜在开水里烫十几秒，淋上蒜蓉蚝油汁，十分钟上桌。北美的罗马生菜（romaine）口感更脆，做这道菜正合适。

10 min total分钟 (5 + 5)2 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Romaine lettuce罗马生菜 400 g · 2 heads romaine (about 400 g), leaves separated罗马生菜 2 棵（约 400 克），掰成大片
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 4 cloves, minced4 瓣，切末
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 27 g · 1 1/2 tbsp1.5 汤匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 6 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Sugar白糖 2 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 3 g · 1 tsp, mixed with 3 tbsp water1 茶匙，加 3 汤匙水调匀
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (a few drops in the water, the rest for the sauce)1 汤匙（焯水几滴，其余炒汁）
  • Salt盐 1 g · a small pinch, for the water一小撮，焯水用

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil with the salt and a few drops of oil.烧一大锅水，加盐和几滴油。
  2. Blanch the lettuce for 15-20 seconds, just until the leaves soften, and lift it out.放生菜烫 15–20 秒，到叶子刚变软就捞出。
  3. Shake off the water and arrange the lettuce on a plate.生菜甩干水，摆在盘里。
  4. Fry the garlic gently in the rest of the oil for about 30 seconds.小锅放剩下的油，小火炒香蒜末，约 30 秒。
  5. Add the oyster sauce, soy sauce, sugar and slurry and cook until it bubbles and thickens.加蚝油、生抽、糖和水淀粉，煮到冒泡变稠。
  6. Pour the sauce over the lettuce and serve right away.把汁淋在生菜上，马上上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Only a few seconds in the water; any longer and it goes limp and watery.生菜只烫十几秒，烫久了就软烂出水。
  • The oil in the water gives the leaves a glossy green.水里的油让生菜颜色更亮。
  • Make and pour the sauce at the last moment, or the lettuce weeps.汁要现做现淋，提前淋上会让生菜出水。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The lettuce is limp and watery生菜软塌出水
    It was blanched too long or not drained; lift it at 15 seconds and shake it well.烫太久或没甩干；下次 15 秒就捞，捞出用力甩干。
  • The sauce is too salty汁太咸
    Oyster sauces differ in saltiness; skip the soy sauce and add another tablespoon of water.蚝油品牌咸度不同；生抽可以不放，多加一汤匙水。
