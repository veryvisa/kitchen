Fish Tacos炸鱼塔可

A North-American street classic: crisp battered white fish tucked into warm tortillas with shredded cabbage, avocado and a lime sour-cream sauce. At home the priorities are drying the fish, mixing the batter fresh and frying in small batches; otherwise the crust turns soft within minutes.北美街头常见的一份酥脆炸鱼塔可：薄脆面糊裹白鱼，配上卷心菜丝、牛油果和酸奶油青柠酱，凉热搭配。家里做的关键是鱼身擦干、面糊现调现炸、分批下锅不挤锅，否则外皮很快回软。

Method做法

  1. Pat the cod dry with paper towels, cut into strips and season lightly with salt and pepper.鱼片用厨房纸吸干表面水分，切成长条，撒少许盐和黑胡椒。
  2. Shred the cabbage, dice the onion, slice the avocado, chop the cilantro and squeeze the limes.卷心菜切细丝，洋葱切丁，牛油果切片，香菜切碎，青柠榨汁。
  3. Whisk the flour, cornstarch, cumin, chili powder, remaining salt and pepper together in a bowl.面粉、淀粉、孜然、辣椒粉、剩余盐和胡椒倒入大碗拌匀。
  4. Stir in cold water in stages to make a thick batter that clings slowly to a lifted spoon.分次加入冷水搅成厚面糊，提起筷子面糊能缓慢挂住。
  5. Pour the oil into a wide shallow pan to form a layer about 1–1.5 cm deep, and heat over medium heat to about 170°C; a small batter drop should bubble at once.宽口平底锅倒油，形成约 1–1.5 厘米油层，中火加热到约 170°C，丢一小块面糊周围立刻起泡。
  6. Coat each strip in batter and fry in 2-3 batches; fry until the bottom is golden, then flip, for about 4-5 minutes total, until both sides are golden. Allow extra time for thicker strips.鱼条逐条挂糊，分 2–3 批下锅，炸至底面金黄后翻面，两面共约 4–5 分钟至外壳金黄，鱼条较厚时适当延长。
  7. Check the thickest part with a thermometer at 70°C, then lift the fish onto a rack to drain.用温度计插到鱼条最厚处，达到 70°C 即熟透，捞出放在晾架上沥油。
  8. Warm each tortilla in a dry pan over low heat for 30 seconds per side until soft and lightly puffed.另取干锅，小火把玉米饼每张两面各加热 30 秒，变软冒小泡。
  9. Stir half the lime juice and a pinch of salt into the sour cream to make the sauce.酸奶油加入一半青柠汁和一小撮盐搅匀成酱汁。
  10. Layer cabbage on each tortilla, add one fish strip, then avocado, onion, sauce and cilantro, with lime wedges on the side.每张饼先铺卷心菜丝，放一条炸鱼，再加牛油果、洋葱、酱汁和香菜，配青柠角上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Dry the fish thoroughly: surface moisture makes the batter slip and drops the oil temperature, so the fish soaks up grease.鱼身一定要擦干，水分会让面糊打滑，也会把油温拉低造成吸油。
  • Mix the batter with cold water just before frying; cold limits gluten development and gives a thin crisp crust.面糊用冷水现调现炸，面筋收紧慢，外壳才薄而脆；调好放久了会回软。
  • Fry in small batches so the oil does not drop; crowded pieces come out soggy. Drain on a rack, not a plate.分批下锅，锅太挤会让油温骤降，外皮湿软不脆；炸好放在晾架而不是盘子上。
  • Warm tortillas to order and keep them wrapped in a clean, lightly damp kitchen towel to stay warm and pliable; cold ones crack, so soften each one in your hands before filling.饼要现吃现加热，叠放后用微湿并拧干的干净厨房布包住保温，避免水汽过多使饼变湿软；凉饼容易裂，卷的时候先在手里按软一点。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The batter slips off during frying面糊挂不住、一炸就脱落
    Dry the fish, thicken the batter so it clings, and leave each strip undisturbed for the first minute so a shell forms.鱼条表面水分擦干，面糊调到能挂在鱼身上的稠度；下锅后先别动，等 1 分钟结壳再轻推。
  • The fish is greasy and the crust is limp鱼很油、外皮发黏
    The oil was too cold. Reheat to 170°C, fry smaller batches and drain on a rack to shed surface oil.油温不够就下了锅；把油升到 170°C 再炸，分批操作，炸好放在晾架上沥掉浮油。
  • The tortillas crack and will not roll饼皮裂开、卷不住
    The tortillas were under-warmed. Toast 30 seconds per side, keep them under a damp cloth and soften each one in your hands before rolling.饼加热时间不足；每张两面各烘 30 秒，出炉用湿布盖住保温，卷前先用手捂软。
