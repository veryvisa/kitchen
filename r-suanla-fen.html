Hot and Sour Glass Noodles酸辣粉

A great bowl of hot and sour glass noodles depends on chewy sweet potato noodles and a punchy seasoned bowl. Soaking the dried noodles first lets them cook through quickly without turning mushy outside; once soaked, sizzling hot oil over minced garlic and chilies builds instant street-stall aroma in about 10 minutes.一碗酸辣粉好不好吃，全看粉条筋不筋道、碗底调料香不香。红薯粉提前温水泡软再短时间煮透，才不会外面化了里面还有硬芯；醋放足、热油激香蒜末和辣椒，泡粉之外约十分钟就能端出一碗开胃夜宵。

Method做法

  1. Soak the dried sweet potato noodles in warm water for 20 minutes until pliable.干红薯粉条用温水提前浸泡 20 分钟，泡到弯曲不断。
  2. Toast the Sichuan pepper in a dry pan over low heat for 1 minute until fragrant, then grind to a powder.花椒放干锅小火炒 1 分钟出香，碾成细粉。
  3. Divide the chili flakes, sesame seeds, and ground Sichuan pepper evenly between two large serving bowls, then place the minced garlic and ginger on top.两个大面碗里先平分放入辣椒面、白芝麻和花椒粉，再把蒜末和姜末放在上面。
  4. Heat the canola oil in a small pan over medium-high heat for about 90 seconds until shimmering. Remove from the heat, wait 10 seconds, then pour half the oil into each bowl in two batches, stirring as you pour to release the aroma without scorching.小锅加热菜籽油约 90 秒到油面起纹，离火停 10 秒后，往每个碗里分两次泼入热油，边泼边搅出香味。
  5. Add the black vinegar, soy sauce, dark soy, sesame oil, sugar, and salt evenly into both bowls.往两个碗里分别加入陈醋、生抽、老抽、香油、糖和盐。
  6. Bring a pot of water to a rolling boil over high heat, add the soaked noodles, and boil for 3-4 minutes until translucent with no white core.锅里烧开一大锅水，放入泡软的粉条，大火煮 3–4 分钟到粉条通体透明、无白芯。
  7. If the bok choy heads are large, halve or quarter them lengthwise; add them stem-side down for the final 1 minute until bright green and tender-crisp.青菜大棵对半或四等分切开，出锅前 1 分钟梗朝下先放进锅里，同煮到翠绿断生。
  8. Ladle about 250 mL of boiling noodle water into each seasoned bowl and stir to dissolve the salt and sugar.先舀约 250 毫升滚烫的面汤进每个碗里，把盐和糖冲匀。
  9. Lift the noodles and bok choy into the bowls, top with chopped zha cai, peanuts, green onion, and cilantro, and serve immediately.捞入煮好的粉条和青菜，表面撒榨菜碎、花生米、葱花和香菜，趁热拌吃。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Presoaking the dried starch noodles in warm water lets them heat evenly, so the center turns tender without the outside turning mushy or slimy.红薯粉提前温水泡软能让粉条受热均匀，不容易外烂内硬，表面也不会糊汤。
  • Generous aged black vinegar gives the signature tang, while a single teaspoon of sugar softens the sharp edge without making the broth sweet.酸辣粉的「酸」一定要在碗底放足陈醋，少许糖用来压住醋的尖酸、提鲜而不发甜。
  • Sizzling hot oil directly over the garlic and chili in the serving bowl builds oil aroma and broth seasoning in one quick step.直接用热油泼碗底的蒜末和辣椒面，一碗就能同时做出红油和蒜香，省去另熬一锅底料的麻烦。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • Noodles have a hard core or turned mushy粉条嚼不动、中间有白芯，或者煮久了全糊成一团
    Boiling unsoaked noodles makes the outside mushy before the core softens. If still firm, boil 2 more minutes; if overcooked, rinse briefly in cool water to stop cooking before returning to the hot broth.没泡透直接干煮最容易外烂内硬。硬了就回锅多煮 2 分钟；已经煮太软的立刻捞进凉开水冲一下再回热汤，能恢复一点弹性。
  • The broth tastes only salty and hot without bright tang汤头只有咸辣味，吃不出酸香
    Hot broth mellows vinegar quickly. Stir another 1/2 to 1 tbsp black vinegar directly into the finished bowl to wake up the flavour.热汤会挥发一部分醋酸。往碗里再补半汤匙到 1 汤匙陈醋即可，用山西老陈醋或保宁醋香气最正。
  • The soup is too salty太咸
    Both soy sauce and zha cai carry salt. Thin the bowl with 80 mL hot water, and rinse or halve the zha cai next time.榨菜和生抽都有盐分。往碗里多冲 80 毫升热水稀释，下次榨菜少放一半或先过一遍清水。
