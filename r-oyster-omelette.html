Oyster Omelette蚵仔煎

The oyster omelette is a Taiwan street favourite: a sweet-potato starch base with crisp edges and a soft centre, topped with plump oysters and egg, finished with a sweet-sour sauce. At home, pat the oysters dry, mix the slurry thoroughly, and cook the starch base until set before adding the egg, adjusting the heat as needed to brown the edges without overcooking the oysters.蚵仔煎是台湾街边常见的海味小食：地瓜粉浆煎出外层微焦、中间软黏的底，铺上鲜嫩蚵仔和蛋液，再浇上酸甜酱汁。家里成败的关键是蚵仔要沥干，粉浆比例调匀，火力按情况调整让底定型又不把蚵仔煎老。

Ingredients食材

  • Eggs鸡蛋 150 g · 3 large eggs3 个大鸡蛋
  • Sweet potato starch地瓜粉 60 g · sweet potato starch, 60 g (about 6 tbsp, weigh if possible)地瓜粉 60 克（约 6 汤匙，以称重为准）
  • Bok choy小白菜 100 g · 2 bok choy, washed and cut小白菜 2 棵，洗净切段

Method做法

  1. Rinse the oysters gently, pick out any shell fragments, and pat them dry on kitchen paper — leftover moisture will thin the slurry and keep the base from setting.鲜蚵用清水轻轻淘洗，挑去碎壳，摊在厨房纸上吸干表面水分——吸不干水分浆底容易散开、出水变稀。
  2. Cut the bok choy, mince the garlic, chop the scallion, and beat the eggs with a pinch of pepper.小白菜洗净切段，蒜切末，葱切花；鸡蛋打散加少许白胡椒。
  3. Mix the starch with 120 ml water; stir again right before use since the starch settles at the bottom.地瓜粉加 120 毫升清水搅匀，搅拌时先沉底的粉要再次拌匀。
  4. Whisk the ketchup, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sugar and 2 tbsp water in a small bowl for the sweet-sour sauce.小碗里放番茄酱、生抽、米醋、糖和 2 汤匙清水，调成酸甜酱汁。
  5. Heat half the oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat, fry the garlic 15 seconds, then add the bok choy and cook about 1 minute until just wilted; lift out, draining off any extra water, and leave the oil in the pan.平底锅中放一半油，中火下蒜末炒香 15 秒，放入小白菜炒到刚断生，约 1 分钟，盛出沥去多余水分，锅里留底油。
  6. Add the remaining oil, stir the slurry once more, and pour it into the pan in a round; cook until the base starts to set.补入剩余的油，把粉浆搅匀后倒入锅中摊成圆片，煎到底部开始定型。
  7. Scatter the bok choy and oysters on top, lower to medium-low and cook 2-3 minutes, adjusting for the size of the oysters and covering briefly if needed, until the base sets with the edges lifting slightly and the oysters turn opaque and firm with the center reaching 74°C; check larger oysters with a food thermometer.铺上炒好的小白菜和鲜蚵，转中小火煎 2–3 分钟，视蚵仔大小调整，必要时加盖短焖，直到底部定型、边缘微微翘起，蚵仔边缘卷起、肉质不透明结实，中心温度达到 74°C；个头大的蚵仔可用食品温度计确认。
  8. Pour in the beaten egg, tilt the pan to spread it, and once the underside is mostly set, flip the whole thing with a wide spatula, or cover and let it steam until the top sets — browning both sides is not required.淋入蛋液，转动锅身让蛋液铺满表面；底面基本凝固后，用宽锅铲整张辅助翻面，或加盖焖到表面蛋液也凝固，不必强求两面都煎出色。
  9. Cut into pieces, plate, drizzle with the sauce, scatter scallion on top and serve hot.切块装盘，淋上酸甜酱汁，撒葱花趁热食用。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Pat the oysters dry to remove excess moisture; this helps prevent the slurry from becoming watery and makes a well-set base easier.蚵仔表面必须吸干，水分会让粉浆散成一团、变得稀薄。
  • About 1 part starch to 2 parts water gives crisp edges and a soft centre; stir the slurry every time before pouring because it settles.地瓜粉和水的比例约 1 比 2，边缘才会微焦、中间保持软黏；粉浆静置后每次用前都要再搅。
  • Wait until the base sets and the edges start to lift before adding the egg or flipping — no need to wait until the whole thing turns hard.等粉浆底部定型、边缘开始翘起后再处理蛋液或翻面，不必等到整张饼变硬才动手。
  • Balance the sweet and sour in a bowl first; do not pour the raw condiments straight into the hot pan.酱汁酸甜要平衡，糖和米醋先在小碗调匀，不要直接往热锅里倒。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The oysters shrink and break up, and the omelette will not hold蚵仔缩小、翻烂，饼不成形
    Shrunken oysters usually mean they were heated too long; shorten the time they spend on the heat, let the base set before touching the egg, and flip the whole thing with a wide spatula rather than stirring the oysters hard.蚵仔缩小大多是加热过久；下次缩短蚵仔受热时间，等粉浆底部定型再处理蛋液，翻面时用宽锅铲整张辅助，不要用力搅动蚵仔。
  • The batter is thin and runs like water粉浆太稀、成片流水状
    The starch settled and was not stirred, or too much water went in. Rebalance 60 g starch with 120 ml water and stir just before pouring.粉浆沉淀后没搅匀，或水放多了；按地瓜粉 60 克配 120 毫升水重调，倒浆前再搅一次。
  • It tastes briny or fishy吃起来有腥味
    First check how the oysters were refrigerated, their smell and freshness. Discard any with an off smell or questionable storage — ginger, garlic, scallion or extra vinegar can balance flavour but cannot make spoiled oysters safe to eat.先检查蚵仔买回来时的冷藏状况、气味和新鲜度；闻到异味或保存条件存疑的蚵仔直接丢弃，不要指望姜葱蒜或米醋能掩盖变质蚵仔的风险，调味只用来平衡风味。
