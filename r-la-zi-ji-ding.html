Spicy Diced Chicken辣子鸡丁

Spicy diced chicken is all about crisp, savoury chicken bites tucked inside a pile of fragrant dried chilies and Sichuan peppercorns. Two pitfalls trip up home cooks: leftover moisture makes the crust soggy instead of crisp, and high heat scorches the chilies bitter in seconds.正宗辣子鸡丁讲究「外酥里嫩、干香麻辣」，鸡丁要在成堆的干辣椒和花椒里挑着吃。家里做最怕两点：一是鸡丁没煎干水汽、回软发柴，二是干辣椒一进热锅就变黑发苦。

45 min total分钟 (25 + 20)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dried chili, Sichuan peppercorn, Shaoxing wine另购：干辣椒、花椒、料酒(绍兴)

Method做法

  1. Cut the chicken thighs into 1.5 cm cubes and pat them completely dry with paper towels.鸡腿肉切 1.5 厘米见方的丁，用厨房纸吸干表面水分。
  2. Toss the chicken with cooking wine, light soy sauce and half the salt (about 2 g), then marinate 10 minutes.鸡丁加料酒、生抽和一半盐（约 2 克）抓匀，腌制 10 分钟。
  3. While the chicken marinates, snip the dried chilies into 2 cm pieces and shake out most of the loose seeds in a strainer.趁腌肉时把干辣椒剪成 2 厘米段，放在滤网里抖掉大部分辣椒籽。
  4. Pat the marinated chicken dry again with paper towels, then toss with the cornstarch just before frying for a thin, even coating.腌好的鸡丁用厨房纸再吸一下表面水分，下锅前才拌入玉米淀粉，薄薄裹一层。
  5. Using a wide 30 cm (12 in) wok or skillet, heat most of the oil (about 4 tbsp) over medium-high heat until small bubbles form around a wooden chopstick.用约 30 厘米的宽口炒锅或平底锅，放大部分油（约 4 汤匙），中高火烧至五成热（木筷插入周围冒细泡）。
  6. Fry the chicken in two batches, spreading each in a single layer (crowding the pan drops the oil temperature and the crust will not crisp); set each batch for 1-2 minutes without moving it, then fry over medium heat for 6-8 minutes until golden and crisp and the centre reaches 74°C (cut open the largest piece to check there is no pink), then move it to a clean plate that has not held raw chicken.鸡丁分两批平铺进锅（一次全下会挤在一起、油温掉下去炸不脆），每批先煎 1 到 2 分钟定型，再中火翻煎 6 到 8 分钟至金黄酥脆、中心熟透（达 74°C，切开最大的一块确认没有粉红色），盛到干净的盘子里，不要用装过生鸡肉的盘子。
  7. Using the oil left in the pan, drop to low heat and stir-fry the dried chilies and Sichuan peppercorns first for about 1 minute until crisp and glossy deep red.用锅里剩下的油转小火，先下干辣椒段和花椒粒，翻炒约 1 分钟至辣椒发脆、颜色暗红发亮。
  8. Add the ginger, garlic and scallion whites, and keep stir-frying on low heat for 30 seconds until fragrant; work quickly, as they can scorch next to the chilies you just toasted.下姜末、蒜片和葱白，继续小火煸香 30 秒，动作要快，姜蒜很容易在刚炒香的辣椒旁边焦掉。
  9. Mix the sugar with the remaining salt (about 2 g) first, then return the chicken to the pan and toss over medium heat for 30 to 45 seconds to coat evenly — too hot or too long will scorch the chilies you just toasted.糖和剩下的一半盐（约 2 克）先拌匀，倒回鸡丁后一起下锅，中火快速翻炒 30 到 45 秒裹匀，火太大或太久会把刚炒香的辣椒烧焦。
  10. Turn off the heat, fold in the roasted peanuts, sesame seeds and scallion greens, and serve right away; the peanuts soften if the dish sits, so you can also scatter them on just before eating.关火，撒入熟花生米、白芝麻和葱绿，拌匀立刻出锅；花生放久会回软，也可以上桌前再撒。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Cut the chicken into uniform 1.5 cm cubes so every piece crisps evenly before the center dries out.鸡丁切 1.5 厘米左右且大小尽量一致，受热才均匀，外皮酥了里面也刚好熟嫩。
  • Patting the meat dry and dusting lightly with cornstarch gives a crisp crust instead of a gummy coating.吸干水分后只裹薄薄一层淀粉，才能煎出干香脆壳，不会粘成厚糊。
  • Always toast dried chilies and peppercorns on low heat; high heat turns them black and bitter in seconds.干辣椒和花椒一定要小火慢煸，油温一高几秒钟就会炒糊发苦。
  • Shaking out loose chili seeds softens harsh heat and stops the seeds from burning first in the pan.筛掉部分辣椒籽能减少燥辣，也能避免细小的籽在锅里先焦黑。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • Chicken is soft instead of crisp, or tastes dry鸡丁不够酥，或者吃起来发柴
    Pat the meat dry, use less starch, fry in batches and avoid crowding the pan next time; to rescue soft pieces, dry-fry them in a skillet over medium heat for 1-2 minutes with no added oil.下次先把鸡丁吸干再用少许淀粉、分批煎、不要挤在一起；已做好的可把鸡丁挑出，不加油在干锅里中火干煸 1 到 2 分钟。
  • Dried chilies turned black and bitter干辣椒炒黑、整盘菜发苦
    The wok was too hot when the chilies went in; pick out the scorched chilies and toss with a few fresh ones on low heat.辣椒下锅前火没降下来；把发黑的辣椒挑出去掉，补几段新辣椒小火拌匀。
  • Spicy heat without enough numbing aroma只有辣味、麻香不够
    Old or sparse peppercorns lose their tingle; crush half a teaspoon of fresh peppercorns and toss them in just before serving.花椒放久跑味或下得太少；出锅前现碾半茶匙新鲜花椒粉拌匀可补救。
