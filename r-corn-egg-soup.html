Sweet Corn Egg Drop Soup粟米蛋花汤

The Cantonese café sweet corn soup, sweeter still with fresh corn at home. A light thickening and a slow pour of egg give you fine silky ribbons.粤式茶餐厅的粟米羹，家里用新鲜玉米做更清甜。汤稍微勾一点芡，蛋液慢慢淋下去，就能得到一丝丝的蛋花。

25 min total分钟 (10 + 15)4 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Corn on the cob玉米 600 g · 2 cobs of corn (about 600 g with the cob), kernels cut off2 根玉米（约 600 克，带芯），刮下玉米粒 or about 250 g canned kernels没有新鲜玉米可用罐装玉米粒约 250 克
  • Eggs鸡蛋 100 g · 2 large eggs, beaten2 个大鸡蛋，打散
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 500 g · 500 mL chicken stock (plus 500 mL water)鸡汤 500 mL（另加 500 mL 水）
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 15 g · 1 1/2 tbsp, mixed with 3 tbsp water1.5 汤匙，加 3 汤匙水调匀
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp (taste first)约 1/3 茶匙（先尝再加）
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 0.3 g · a pinch (white pepper is traditional)少许（有白胡椒更好）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 3 g · a few drops几滴
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 5 g · a little chopped green onion葱花少许

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Stand each cob in a bowl and cut the kernels off from top to bottom.玉米竖着立在碗里，用刀把玉米粒从上往下刮下来。
  2. Blend half the kernels with 100 mL water to a coarse purée.取一半玉米粒加 100 mL 水打成粗糊。
  3. Simmer the stock, 400 mL water, the kernels and the purée for 8 minutes after it boils.锅里放鸡汤、400 mL 水、玉米粒和玉米糊，煮开后小火煮 8 分钟。
  4. Season with the salt and pepper.加盐和胡椒调味。
  5. Stir in the slurry and cook until the soup thickens slightly.淋入水淀粉，边倒边搅，煮到汤微微浓稠。
  6. Turn the heat to the lowest setting and pour the egg in a thin stream in circles.关到最小火，蛋液细细地绕圈淋下去。
  7. Wait 10 seconds for the ribbons to float, then stir gently once.等 10 秒蛋花浮起，再轻轻推一下。
  8. Turn off the heat and add the sesame oil and green onion.关火，淋香油、撒葱花。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Thicken before adding the egg; the slight body keeps the ribbons fine and stops them sinking in clumps.先勾芡再下蛋，汤有一点稠度，蛋花才细、不会沉底结块。
  • Do not stir as the egg goes in; let it set first.蛋液下锅后先别搅，等它定型再推。
  • Store-bought stocks vary a lot in salt, so taste before salting.市售鸡汤咸淡差别大，先尝再放盐。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The egg formed lumps蛋花成了一坨一坨
    The soup was boiling hard or the egg went in too fast; use the lowest heat and a thin, slow stream.汤滚得太厉害或倒得太快；转最小火，蛋液要细流慢倒。
  • Too thin or too thick汤太稀或太稠
    Add the slurry in stages and check after each; thin with hot water if it gets too thick.水淀粉分次加，每次搅匀看稠度；太稠就加点热水。
