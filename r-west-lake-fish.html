West Lake Vinegar Fish西湖醋鱼（家常版）

West Lake vinegar fish relies entirely on gentle poaching and a glossy sweet-and-sour glaze without a drop of oil. Whole sea bass works well in North America in place of grass carp, offering tender white flesh with fewer fine bones. The tricky parts are poaching below a rolling boil so the skin stays intact and thickening the vinegar sauce so it clings evenly.西湖醋鱼全靠「水煮」和「糖醋芡汁」见功夫，不用一滴油就能做出嫩滑酸甜的口感。北美用整条海鲈鱼替代草鱼，肉质细嫩、小刺更少；难点在于保持微沸把鱼煮熟而不破皮，以及把醋汁收到浓稠发亮、正好挂住鱼身。

Ingredients食材

  • Sea bass鲈鱼 700 g · 1 whole sea bass (about 700 g, scaled and gutted)整条海鲈鱼 1 条（约 700 克，刮鳞去内脏） grass carp or branzino also works; live fish is best, and ask the fishmonger to clean the belly cavity; tilapia is not recommended here as its flavour and texture are too muddy and loose for whole poaching草鱼或欧洲鲈（branzino）也可，买活鱼最好，买时请鱼档清理干净腹腔黑膜；罗非鱼土腥味较重、肉质偏松，不建议整条水煮
  • Ginger姜 20 g · 3 smashed slices for poaching, plus 10 g finely minced for the glaze3 片拍松（煮鱼用）加 10 克切极细姜末（浇汁用）
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 2 whole stalks knotted for poaching, plus 1 stalk chopped2 根整葱打结（煮鱼用）加 1 根切葱花
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 30 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)香醋/陈醋 60 g · 4 tbsp (60 mL) Chinese black vinegar4 汤匙（60 mL，镇江香醋或米醋）
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp (7.5 mL), for color半汤匙（7.5 mL，调色用）
  • Sugar白糖 40 g · 3 tbsp plus 1 tsp3 汤匙加 1 茶匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 12 g · 4 tsp, stirred into 2 tbsp water4 茶匙，加 2 汤匙水调成水淀粉
  • Salt盐 3 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Rinse away any blood and dark membrane inside the sea bass cavity, score both sides twice, and pat dry.洗净海鲈鱼腹腔内的血块和黑膜，在鱼身两面各斜划两刀后擦干水分。
  2. Rub the fish inside and out with the salt and 1 tbsp cooking wine and let it rest for 10 minutes.用盐和 1 汤匙料酒里外抹匀鱼身，静置腌 10 分钟。
  3. Use a wide pot or oval Dutch oven long enough to lay the whole fish flat (a round skillet will bend or break it), fill with enough cold water to submerge the fish, add the smashed ginger, knotted green onions, and remaining 1 tbsp cooking wine, and bring to a boil over high heat.找一个够长的宽口锅或椭圆形炖锅（能平放 30 厘米以上的整鱼，普通圆平底锅容易把鱼弄弯甚至弄断），加足量冷水没过鱼身，放入拍松的姜片、葱结和剩下的 1 汤匙料酒，大火烧开。
  4. Turn the heat down to low until the water barely trembles, then slide the fish in gently.转最小火，让水面保持几乎静止的微颤状态，再轻轻滑入整条鱼。
  5. Poach at a bare simmer without rolling bubbles for 7 to 9 minutes until the eyes turn white and the thickest flesh reaches 70°C.保持微沸不翻滚的状态浸煮 7 至 9 分钟，直到鱼眼变白鼓起、最厚处鱼肉中心温度达到 70°C。
  6. Lift the fish onto a warm deep platter using two wide spatulas, pick off the ginger and green onions clinging to it, then strain about 180 mL of the poaching broth through a fine sieve and skim off any foam.用两把宽铲从鱼头鱼尾托起装入温热的深盘中，夹走鱼身上的姜片葱结，再用细筛把煮鱼汤滤出约 180 毫升并撇去浮沫备用。
  7. In a small saucepan, combine the reserved broth, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, and minced ginger, and bring to a boil over medium-high heat.另起小锅倒入留出的鱼汤、生抽、老抽、白糖和姜末，中大火煮至沸腾。
  8. Stir the cornstarch slurry again and pour it into the boiling liquid while stirring constantly for 30 to 45 seconds until glossy and thick, then turn off the heat and stir in the black vinegar (adding the vinegar only after the heat is off keeps its aroma from cooking away).把水淀粉再次搅匀倒入滚开的汤汁中，边倒边搅拌煮 30 至 45 秒至浓稠发亮，关火后倒入香醋拌匀（醋留到关火才加，酸香才不会被煮散）。
  9. Spoon the hot vinegar glaze evenly over the fish, sprinkle with chopped green onions, and serve immediately.将滚烫的糖醋汁均匀浇在鱼身上，撒上葱花即可趁热上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Never boil the poaching water rapidly; rolling bubbles tear the fish skin and overcook the exterior before the center is done.浸鱼的水一定不能大火滚煮，沸水翻滚会把鱼皮冲破、鱼肉震碎，还容易让外部过老。
  • The dark belly membrane and blood along the backbone carry most of the muddy flavor, so scraping them clean keeps the poached fish sweet.腹腔内的黑膜和贴骨血是腥味的主要来源，下锅前刮洗干净才能用清汤煮出鲜味。
  • Keeping sugar and black vinegar around a 1 to 1.5 ratio by weight yields a rounded tang that highlights the fish without harshness.糖和醋的比例接近 1 比 1.5（重量比），既能带出柔和酸香，又能用焦糖色的鲜甜压住鱼腥。
  • Bring the broth to a full boil before adding the slurry, then stir in the vinegar only after the heat is off; this way the starch gelatinizes cleanly and the vinegar keeps its aroma instead of boiling away.勾芡前汤汁先煮滚再下水淀粉，关火后才倒醋，淀粉能充分糊化发亮，醋的酸香也不会被煮跑。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The fish skin tore or the fish broke apart when lifted鱼皮破了，或者捞出时鱼身断成两截
    Next time keep the water at a bare simmer and slide the fish in on a wide spatula; lift it with two wide spatulas under the head and tail rather than tongs.下次火力调小到水面只冒小泡，用宽铲托着滑入锅中；捞鱼用两把宽铲同时托底，不要用夹子单点夹鱼身。
  • The glaze is too watery to cling, or turned lumpy芡汁太稀挂不住鱼身，或者结块发浑
    Simmer a thin glaze 30 seconds longer to thicken; if it turned lumpy, whisk together a fresh small batch of broth, sugar, and slurry, then stir in the vinegar off the heat.芡汁太稀可回锅中火再煮 30 秒收浓；已经结块则重新用少许清水、糖和现调水淀粉另煮一碗，关火后再加醋浇上。
  • The vinegar tastes sharp or the fish is dry and fishy酸味刺鼻，或者鱼肉发柴带腥
    If the vinegar tastes too sharp, stir in another teaspoon of sugar; dry, fishy flesh usually means it poached too long, so pull the fish as soon as the centre hits 70°C next time.酸度太冲可补 1 茶匙糖拌匀；鱼肉发柴多因煮太久，下次用温度计盯住中心刚到 70°C 就出锅，不要多煮。
