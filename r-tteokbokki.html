Tteokbokki辣炒年糕

Tteokbokki is a comforting one-pan staple with chewy rice cakes coated in a thick, sweet-and-fiery glaze. The two common pitfalls at home are skipping the soak so firm rice cakes stick and stay hard inside, or stopping before the sauce reduces into a glossy coat.韩式辣炒年糕做法简单，一锅二十分钟左右就能端上桌，年糕软糯弹牙，裹满红亮浓稠的甜辣酱汁。在家做最容易失手的是年糕没提前泡软导致粘连夹生，或者汤汁没收浓就关火，酱汁稀薄不挂味。

Method做法

  1. Separate any stuck rice cakes first.把粘在一起的年糕条先掰开。
  2. Fresh or soft-packaged cakes just need a quick rinse; refrigerated firm ones need 10 minutes in warm water, and frozen ones need about 30 minutes in cold water until fully softened. Drain well.新鲜或软包装年糕冲洗一下就行，冷藏变硬的泡 10 分钟温水，冷冻的泡 30 分钟冷水至完全回软，捞出沥水。
  3. Stir the dashi powder, gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, sugar, and garlic into 450 mL water in a wide skillet until smooth.取宽口平底炒锅，把高汤粉、韩式辣酱、辣椒粉、生抽、糖、蒜末和 450 毫升清水搅匀。
  4. Bring the sauce broth to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring a few times so the paste dissolves completely.中大火把酱汤煮开，其间搅动几次让辣酱彻底化开。
  5. Add the drained rice cakes, cabbage, and onion, and bring back to a gentle boil.倒入沥干的年糕、圆白菜和洋葱，再次煮到微微滚沸。
  6. Reduce to medium heat and simmer uncovered for 7 to 9 minutes, scraping across the bottom every minute so the rice cakes do not stick; if the sauce reduces too fast while the cakes are still firm, add 50 mL hot water and keep simmering.转中火敞盖煮 7–9 分钟，每分钟贴着锅底轻推翻拌一次以防粘锅；汤汁收得太快而年糕还硬，就加 50 毫升热水继续煮。
  7. Add the green onion and cook 2 to 3 more minutes until the rice cakes are soft and chewy and the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon.放入葱段再煮 2–3 分钟，直到年糕弹软、汤汁收浓到能挂住勺背。
  8. Turn off the heat, drizzle in the sesame oil, and toss to combine.关火后淋入香油，翻拌均匀。
  9. Transfer to a shallow bowl, scatter with sesame seeds, and serve hot.盛入浅碗，撒上熟白芝麻，趁热食用。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Rice cakes vary a lot in firmness: soak refrigerated firm ones in warm water and frozen ones in cold water for longer, so the inside and outside heat through together and the cakes do not turn mushy outside while staying hard in the center.年糕的软硬差别很大：冷藏变硬的先用温水泡软，冷冻的要用冷水泡足时间，下锅后内外受热才会同步，不易外烂内硬。
  • A wide skillet speeds evaporation so the starch released by the rice cakes thickens the sauce naturally, while stirring prevents scorching.用宽口锅并勤推锅底，既能加快水分蒸发让年糕自身的淀粉把汁自然勾浓，也能避免糊底。
  • Simmering the cabbage and onion early sweetens the spicy broth, whereas the green onion goes in late to stay bright and aromatic.圆白菜和洋葱先煮能带出清甜底味，葱最后下锅则能保留清香。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The sauce is too watery to cling汤汁太稀挂不住年糕
    The starch has not thickened the broth yet; keep simmering uncovered over medium heat for 3-4 more minutes while stirring. If the rice cakes are already soft, lift them out with a slotted spoon and reduce the sauce alone, or stir in 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 1 tbsp water instead of simmering longer.收汁时间不够、年糕淀粉尚未充分释出；保持中火敞盖再煮 3–4 分钟并勤搅动。如果年糕已经够软了，先用漏勺捞出年糕单独收汁，或用 1 茶匙淀粉加 1 汤匙水勾芡，避免年糕继续煮烂。
  • The rice cakes stuck and scorched on the pan年糕粘锅底烧糊了
    The heat was too high without scraping the bottom; transfer the unburned portion to a clean pan with 2-3 tbsp hot water and lower the heat, but do not scrape up the scorched bits — they carry a bitter taste.火力太大且未贴底搅动；立刻把上层未糊的年糕和汤汁换到干净锅里，加 2–3 汤匙热水改中小火续煮，不要把烧焦的部分一起刮进新锅，会带出焦苦味。
  • The sauce tastes too spicy or salty吃起来过辣或偏咸
    Gochujang brands vary in salt and heat; stir in 50 mL hot water, 1 tsp sugar, and another handful of cabbage for 2 minutes.各品牌韩式辣酱咸辣度不同；加 50 毫升热水和 1 茶匙糖，再抓一把圆白菜同煮 2 分钟柔和味道。
