Shrimp Scampi Pasta蒜香黄油虾意面

A North American Italian restaurant staple that is really garlic, butter, lemon and shrimp, done in the time the pasta boils. Restaurants use white wine; stock plus lemon keeps it bright without it. The key is using pasta water to emulsify the sauce so it coats the noodles instead of pooling.北美意餐馆的常客，主料只有大蒜、黄油、柠檬和虾，煮面的时间就能做完。餐馆常用白葡萄酒，这里换成高汤加柠檬汁，味道照样清爽；关键是用煮面水把酱汁乳化，让它挂在面上而不是沉在盘底。

25 min total分钟 (10 + 15)3 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Spaghetti意大利面 340 g · 340 g spaghetti or linguine意大利面 340 克
  • Frozen raw shrimp冷冻生虾 450 g · 450 g raw shrimp, peeled and deveined生虾 450 克，去壳去虾线
  • Butter黄油 42 g · 3 tbsp, used in two parts3 汤匙，分两次用
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 28 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Garlic大蒜 20 g · 6-7 cloves, thinly sliced6–7 瓣，切薄片
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 125 g · 1/2 cup (125 mL)1/2 杯（125 mL）
  • Lemons柠檬 100 g · 1 lemon, zested and juiced1 个，擦皮屑、挤汁
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp (plus salt for the pasta water)约 2/3 茶匙（另加煮面水的盐） pasta-water salt is mostly poured away and not counted煮面水的盐大部分倒掉，没计入
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · a few grinds少许
  • Fresh parsley欧芹 10 g · a handful, chopped一把，切碎

Method做法

  1. Boil the pasta in well-salted water for 1 minute less than the packet says.大锅水烧开加盐，下意面按包装时间少 1 分钟煮。
  2. Pat the shrimp dry and season with half the salt and the pepper.虾用厨房纸擦干，撒一半盐和黑胡椒。
  3. Heat the oil and 1 tbsp butter in a wide pan and lay in the shrimp for 1 minute.平底锅烧热橄榄油和 1 汤匙黄油，虾平铺煎 1 分钟。
  4. Turn and cook 1 more minute until just pink, then lift them out.翻面再煎 1 分钟到刚变粉红，盛出。
  5. Lower the heat and fry the garlic until pale gold, about a minute.转小火，下蒜片炒到微黄，约 1 分钟。
  6. Add the stock and lemon juice and simmer 2 minutes to reduce by a third.倒入高汤和柠檬汁，煮 2 分钟收掉三分之一。
  7. Lift the pasta straight into the pan with half a cup of pasta water and the remaining butter.捞出意面直接放进锅里，加半杯煮面水和剩下的黄油。
  8. Toss over high heat for a minute until the sauce turns creamy and clings.大火翻拌 1 分钟，到酱汁变浓挂在面上。
  9. Return the shrimp, add the zest and parsley, toss and taste for salt.虾放回锅里，撒柠檬皮屑和欧芹，翻匀尝味。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Searing the shrimp separately and returning them at the end keeps them from turning rubbery in the sauce.虾分开煎、最后回锅，才不会在酱汁里煮老变硬。
  • The starch in pasta water is what binds the butter and liquid into a glossy sauce.煮面水里的淀粉是天然的乳化剂，黄油和汤汁靠它融成一体。
  • Fry the garlic on low and stop at pale gold; browned garlic is bitter.蒜片用小火，颜色到浅金就停，焦了会苦。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The sauce is greasy and pools on the plate酱汁油水分离、沉在盘底
    Add 2-3 tbsp more pasta water and toss hard over high heat until it turns milky.再加 2–3 汤匙煮面水大火快速翻拌，直到变成乳白色。
  • The shrimp are tough and shrunken虾又韧又缩
    They were overcooked. Pull them when they curl into a C; an O means too far.煎过头了。虾一弯成 C 形就起锅，变成 O 形就老了。
  • Too sour太酸
    Lemons vary, so add half the juice first and the rest after tasting; a knob of butter softens too much acid.柠檬大小差别大，先放一半汁，最后尝过再加；酸了加一小块黄油压一压。
