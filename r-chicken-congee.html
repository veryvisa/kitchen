Chicken Congee鸡肉粥

Cantonese chicken congee pairs a velvety rice base with tender slices of chicken in under an hour. Tossing the raw rice with a touch of oil and salt before dropping it into boiling broth helps the grains burst quickly, and slipping the velveted chicken in at the very end keeps it juicy.广式生滚鸡肉粥米汤绵滑、鸡肉鲜嫩，一锅就能暖胃。关键在于生米先拌少许油和盐再下滚水，半个多小时就能煮到米粒开花；鸡腿肉切薄片最后才滚熟，才不会发柴。

63 min total分钟 (15 + 48)3 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Short-grain rice短粒米(寿司米) 185 g · 1 cup short-grain rice (about 185 g)1 杯短粒大米（约 185 克） short-grain rice turns creamier, or use half short-grain and half jasmine短粒米胶质多更绵滑，也可一半短粒米配一半茉莉香米
  • Boneless chicken thigh鸡腿肉 300 g · about 300 g boneless skinless chicken thighs去骨去皮鸡腿肉约 300 克 chicken breast works too, though thigh stays juicier鸡胸肉也可以，但鸡腿肉更嫩滑
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 480 g · 2 cups unsalted chicken stock (plus about 1.5 L water)2 杯无盐鸡汤（另加约 1.5 升清水）
  • Ginger姜 20 g · about 20 g, half sliced for the pot and half julienned约 20 克，一半切片熬粥、一半切细丝
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 25 g · 2-3 stalks, finely chopped2–3 根，切葱花
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp (7.5 mL)半汤匙（7.5 mL）
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 6 g · 2 tsp2 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 14 g · 1 tbsp (14 g), divided for rice and chicken1 汤匙（14 克），拌米和腌鸡分用
  • Salt盐 6 g · about 1 tsp, divided for rice and seasoning约 1 茶匙，拌米和调味分用
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · about 1/4 tsp white pepper (white pepper is traditional; ground black pepper works too)约 1/4 茶匙白胡椒粉（白胡椒最地道，没有用现磨黑胡椒也可）

Method做法

  1. Rinse the short-grain rice until the water runs mostly clear, then drain thoroughly.短粒米淘洗至水基本清澈，充分沥干。
  2. Toss the drained rice with 1/2 tbsp canola oil and 1/4 tsp salt, and let it stand 15 minutes.沥干的米拌入半汤匙植物油和 1/4 茶匙盐，静置 15 分钟。
  3. Slice the boneless chicken thighs across the grain into 3 mm thin strips.去骨鸡腿肉逆纹切成约 3 毫米厚的薄片。
  4. Mix the chicken with soy sauce, cooking wine, cornstarch, half of the julienned ginger, and the remaining 1/2 tbsp canola oil, and marinate 10 to 15 minutes.鸡片与生抽、料酒、淀粉、一半姜丝和剩下半汤匙植物油抓匀，腌制 10–15 分钟。
  5. Bring the chicken stock, 1.5 L water, and ginger slices to a rolling boil in a large pot over high heat.深汤锅倒入鸡汤、1.5 升清水和姜片，大火烧到剧烈沸腾。
  6. Pour in the oiled rice, stir immediately from the bottom so nothing sticks, and return to a boil.倒入拌好油的生米，立即用勺抄底搅散，再次煮沸。
  7. Reduce to medium-low, set the lid slightly ajar, and simmer 38 minutes, stirring from the bottom every 8 minutes, until the grains burst and the congee turns creamy.转中小火，盖子留一条缝，熬煮 38 分钟并每隔 8 分钟抄底搅一次，至米粒开花、粥汤绵滑。
  8. Drop in the marinated chicken a few pieces at a time while stirring gently so the slices do not clump.分次放入腌好的鸡片，边放边轻轻搅散避免粘连。
  9. Simmer gently for 3 to 4 minutes until the chicken turns completely opaque and reaches 74°C inside.保持微沸煮 3–4 分钟，至鸡片完全变白熟透且中心温度达 74°C。
  10. Stir in the remaining salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste.加入剩下的盐和现磨黑胡椒粉拌匀调味。
  11. Ladle into bowls, drizzle with sesame oil, top with chopped green onion and the remaining ginger threads, and serve hot.盛入碗中，淋香油并撒葱花和剩余姜丝趁热喝。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Coating raw rice with a little oil and salt helps the starch granules split open faster in boiling broth for a creamy texture.生米先拌少许油和盐再下滚水，米粒更容易受热胀裂，不用久熬也能绵滑起胶。
  • Always add the rice to boiling liquid rather than cold water so the grains stay suspended and do not scorch.一定要滚水下米，冷水下米容易沉底粘锅。
  • Velveting the sliced chicken with cornstarch and oil and cooking it only at the end keeps the meat tender.鸡片提前用淀粉和油抓匀，并且只在出锅前几分钟滚熟，能保住鲜嫩口感。
  • Leaving the lid slightly ajar maintains a steady simmer while keeping the starchy broth from boiling over.熬粥时锅盖留缝透气，既能保持微沸又不会扑锅溢出。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The rice scorched on the bottom of the pot锅底糊了有焦味
    Do not scrape the burnt bottom; pour the clean upper congee into a fresh pot to finish, and stir from the bottom more often next time.千万别刮锅底，直接把上层好粥倒进干净锅里继续煮；下次下米时搅散，并勤抄底搅拌。
  • The congee turned out too thick or too watery粥太稠或太稀
    Whisk in boiling water a splash at a time if too thick, or simmer uncovered 5 to 8 minutes longer while stirring if too thin.太稠就分次冲入滚开水搅匀；太稀就开盖中小火多煮 5–8 分钟并勤搅拌。
  • The chicken clumped together or turned tough鸡肉粘成一团或变老
    Slice the chicken thin, add it gradually while stirring to separate, and take it off the heat as soon as it reaches 74°C.鸡片切薄并分次下锅划散，达到 74°C 完全变白就立刻关火。
