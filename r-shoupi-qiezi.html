Hand-torn Eggplant手撕茄子

Hand-torn eggplant delivers deep flavor without deep-frying: steaming slender eggplants tender and tearing them along their fibers creates rough edges that cling to a punchy Hunan garlic-chili dressing. The two common pitfalls are steaming over weak heat, which darkens the flesh, and failing to drain off the steaming liquid.手撕茄子不用油炸也能特别下饭，关键在把长茄子大火蒸软后顺纤维撕成毛茸茸的长条，再泼上激香的蒜末剁椒酸辣汁。做法省油又快手，但容易踩的坑是茄子蒸黑或盘底留水太多导致味道发淡。

Ingredients食材

  • Eggplant茄子 400 g · 2 long purple Chinese or Japanese eggplants (about 400 g)细长紫茄子 2 根（约 400 克） choose slender Asian eggplants rather than large globe eggplants, with glossy skin, a fresh stem end and a little give when pressed; no need to pre-soak in salt water选细长嫩茄，避免圆胖多籽的大茄子；挑表皮光亮、蒂部新鲜、按压有弹性的，不需要提前泡盐水
  • Garlic大蒜 25 g · 6 cloves, finely minced6 瓣，切细蒜末
  • Hunan chopped salted chili剁椒（瓶装） 20 g · a generous 1 tbsp (about 20 g) Hunan chopped chili湘式剁椒 1 汤匙多（约 20 克）
  • Long green chilli peppers青尖椒（螺丝椒） 30 g · 1 long green chili (about 30 g), thinly sliced into rings青线椒或尖椒 1 根（约 30 克），切细圈
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 15 g · 2 stalks, finely chopped2 根，切葱花
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)香醋/陈醋 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL) Chinese black vinegar1 汤匙（15 mL）陈醋或香醋
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Salt盐 1 g · a small pinch (about 1/6 tsp)1 小撮（约 1/6 茶匙）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 7 g · 1/2 tbsp半汤匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 21 g · 1.5 tbsp (22 mL), for sizzling the aromatics (any neutral vegetable oil works; avoid olive oil)1.5 汤匙（22 mL），泼热油用（也可用其他无异味植物油，不要用橄榄油）

Method做法

  1. Put the minced garlic, chopped chili, green chili rings, and green onion into a small heatproof bowl first — do this while the steamer water comes to a boil so there is no scramble later.蒜末、剁椒、青椒圈和葱花先放入耐热小碗备好，利用等水烧开和蒸茄子的时间准备，不用临时手忙脚乱。
  2. Rinse the eggplants, trim the stems, halve them lengthwise, and arrange cut-side up on a deep, rimmed steaming plate (the rim catches the liquid that steams out, making it easy to pour off later).茄子洗净去蒂，纵向对半切开，切面朝上摆进带边的深蒸盘（深盘能接住蒸出的汁水，方便后面倒掉）。
  3. Set the plate in a boiling steamer, wrap the lid's edge with a dry cloth before covering (this keeps condensation from dripping onto the eggplant), and steam over high heat for 10 minutes until a chopstick slides easily into the thickest section.蒸锅水大火烧开后放入茄子，锅盖边缘包一层干布再盖上，大火蒸 10 分钟，至筷子能轻松戳穿最厚处（干布能挡住冷凝水滴到茄子上）。
  4. Remove the eggplants, let cool about 3 minutes, and pour off all liquid collected on the plate.取出茄子摊凉约 3 分钟，倒净盘底蒸出的水分。
  5. While still warm, tear the eggplants lengthwise along their fibers into even strips and arrange on a serving plate.趁温热顺着纵向纤维把茄子撕成粗细均匀的长条，码入盘中。
  6. Heat the canola oil in a small pan over high heat for about 1 minute until faintly smoking, then pour it over the garlic and chilies in two batches to sizzle.小锅加菜籽油大火烧约 1 分钟至微微冒烟，分两次浇进小碗把蒜椒激香。
  7. Stir the soy sauce, black vinegar, sugar, salt, and sesame oil into the bowl, then spoon the dressing evenly over the torn eggplant. Best eaten right away; refrigerate leftovers sealed and finish within a day, and do not leave the dressed eggplant at room temperature for more than 2 hours.碗中加入生抽、陈醋、糖、盐和香油拌匀，淋在手撕茄子上即可食用；建议当餐吃完，吃不完的密封冷藏，次日内吃完，不要在室温放超过 2 小时。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Tearing instead of slicing leaves rough, fibrous edges that grip the garlic-chili dressing much better than smooth knife cuts.茄子用手撕而不是用刀切，断口凹凸粗糙，能像海绵一样吸附住蒜香和酸辣汁。
  • Steaming cut-side up over full boiling steam cooks the flesh rapidly so it stays pale and creamy instead of turning dark.切面朝上并用滚水大火足汽蒸，能缩短接触空气氧化的时间，蒸出的茄肉更白嫩。
  • Pouring the hot oil in two batches blooms the garlic and chilies evenly without scorching the minced garlic bitter.热油分两次泼进蒜椒碗，第一次烫出辛香，第二次补足熟度，比一次性猛浇更不容易把蒜末烫苦。
  • Large Western globe eggplants have thick skins and dense seeds; slender Chinese or Japanese eggplants tear cleanly and stay silky.北美普通大圆茄籽多肉粗，务必买细长的中国紫茄或日本茄子，撕出来才细腻。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The eggplant turned dark and dull茄子蒸好颜色发黑
    Starting in a cold steamer or losing steam lets the flesh oxidize; wait for a full rolling boil before putting the plate in.冷水上锅慢蒸或锅盖漏气会让茄子氧化发黑；下次等水大开后再上锅，保持大火足汽快蒸 10 分钟。
  • The dish tastes watery and bland盘里水汪汪的、味道不入味
    Leftover steaming water dilutes the sauce; drain the plate well, press the strips gently to release excess water, and toss with the dressing 2 minutes before eating.蒸后茄水没倒净或茄条里还含很多水；倒掉蒸水后可用筷子轻压茄条挤出多余水汽，再淋料汁拌匀静置 2 分钟。
  • The garlic tastes bitter蒜末发苦
    Overheated oil poured all at once burns fine garlic; pull the pan off the burner as soon as faint wisps of smoke appear and pour in two stages.油烧太热且一次全倒进去容易炸糊蒜末；看到油刚冒细烟就离火，分两次少量泼油并马上搅开。

Steaming with a modest hot-oil drizzle uses far less fat than frying, keeping calories low while providing dietary fiber. Chopped chili and soy sauce add sodium, so cut back on the chili paste and skip the extra salt for a lighter version.全程蒸制只用少量泼油，比红烧或油炸茄子大幅减少用油，热量低且提供膳食纤维。剁椒和生抽含一定钠，口味清淡可减少剁椒并省去额外加盐。
