Crispy Roast Pork Belly脆皮烧肉

Cantonese roast pork belly pairs crackling, blistered skin with juicy, spiced layers underneath. A home oven can achieve that airy crunch if you do three things right: prick and air-dry the skin thoroughly, shield the meat in foil, and finish with high top heat.广式脆皮烧肉最迷人的是咔嚓作响的蜂窝脆皮和下面多汁带脂香的五花肉。家庭烤箱也能做出气泡脆皮，关键只有三件事：把猪皮扎透并彻底风干，用锡纸护住肉边，最后再用上火高温把皮烤爆。

Ingredients食材

  • Pork belly五花肉 900 g · 1 slab skin-on pork belly (about 900 g, even thickness)带皮整块五花肉（约 900 克，厚薄尽量均匀） choose a rectangular piece with clear fat and lean layers选肥瘦分层明显的方块
  • Salt盐 12 g · 2 tsp, divided (1 tsp for meat, 1 tsp for skin)2 茶匙，分次用（腌肉 1 茶匙、擦皮 1 茶匙）
  • Sugar白糖 16 g · 4 tsp, divided4 茶匙，分次用 2 tsp in the rub and 2 tsp for dipping2 茶匙腌肉，另 2 茶匙作蘸料
  • Chinese five-spice powder五香粉 4 g · 1.5 tsp1.5 茶匙
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Soy sauce生抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp半汤匙
  • Rice vinegar米醋 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 7 g · 1/2 tbsp, for brushing the skin半汤匙，最后刷皮用

Method做法

  1. Simmer a shallow layer of water in a pan, place the pork skin-side down for 3 minutes until pale and pliable, then lift out and pat dry.锅中烧开浅水，将五花肉猪皮朝下放入微沸水中焯烫 3 分钟至猪皮变白微软，捞出擦干。
  2. Turn to the meat side and score parallel cuts 2.5 cm apart down to the fat without cutting the skin.翻到肉面，每隔 2.5 厘米划几刀深至脂肪层（不要切断猪皮）。
  3. Mix the wine with the soy sauce, five-spice, 1 tsp salt and 2 tsp sugar, and rub into the meat and cuts while keeping the skin clean.将 1 汤匙料酒、生抽、五香粉、1 茶匙盐和 2 茶匙糖调匀，仔细抹进肉面和刀口里，避免沾到猪皮。
  4. Flip skin-side up and prick the skin densely with a metal skewer or tenderizer, piercing just through the rind without digging deep into the fat.翻回猪皮面，用钢签或松肉针密密扎出数百个小孔（刚扎穿皮即可，不要扎太深出油）。
  5. Blot away any moisture, brush the skin with rice vinegar, and rub the remaining 1 tsp salt evenly over the rind.用厨房纸吸干渗出的油水，先刷上一层米醋，再把剩下 1 茶匙盐均匀揉在猪皮表面。
  6. Set the slab skin-side up on a wire rack over a tray and chill uncovered at least 8 hours until the rind feels dry and taut.将五花肉猪皮朝上放在烤网上，底下垫盘，不盖保鲜膜放入冰箱冷藏风干至少 8 小时，至猪皮摸起来干硬发亮。
  7. Preheat the oven to 190 °C (375 °F), and wrap double foil snugly up the sides of the pork so only the top skin stays exposed.烤箱预热 190 °C（375 °F），把猪肉从冰箱取出后，用双层锡纸沿四周包紧成盒状，只露出顶部猪皮。
  8. Bake on the middle rack 60-75 minutes. Starting at 60 minutes, check every 10 minutes: the thickest meat needs at least 71 °C and the fat layer should look clearly softened and glossy, not just juices running clear.放进中层烤 60–75 分钟，从 60 分钟起每 10 分钟检查一次：肉层中心温度需达到至少 71 °C，且肥肉和皮下脂肪明显软化透亮才算到位，不要只看肉汁是否变清。
  9. Pull out the tray, pour off any pooled fat in the foil boat, and brush the skin lightly with 1/2 tbsp oil.取出烤盘，倒掉锡纸盒内渗出的多余油脂，在猪皮表面薄刷半汤匙食用油。
  10. Switch to high broil, start with the tray about 15 cm below the element as a reference distance, watch closely and adjust position as needed, until the skin puffs into even golden blisters (usually 8-12 minutes, depending on your broiler's strength).烤箱改开高火上火炙烤（High Broil），把烤盘放在距上发热管约 15 厘米处作为起始参考距离，持续盯着烤并随时调整位置，直到猪皮均匀爆出金黄气泡（通常 8–12 分钟，视各家烤箱火力而定）。
  11. Rest 10 minutes on a board, flip skin-side down to slice cleanly into bite-sized pieces, and serve with the remaining sugar for dipping.取出在室温静置 10 分钟，再把猪皮朝下翻面用锯齿刀或厚菜刀切成块，配白糖上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Uncovered fridge drying is the real secret: once the surface moisture is gone, the moisture left under the skin flashes to steam under high heat and puffs the rind into airy, shattering blisters.提前冷藏不加盖风干是脆皮的关键：猪皮表面水分蒸发越彻底，高温下皮下残留的水分才能迅速形成蒸汽，把猪皮顶出金黄酥脆的蜂窝气泡。
  • Prick just through the skin; deep holes let fat seep up onto the surface, which stalls puffing and scorches quickly.猪皮只扎穿表皮层，扎太深会把肥肉里的油逼到表面，油水混在一起反而不容易爆脆，还容易焦黑。
  • Broilers vary wildly, so stay by the oven window; rotate the tray or tent fast-browning spots with foil as soon as they blister.炙烤阶段不同烤箱火力差异极大，千万不要离开厨房；哪一角先鼓泡发深就转一下烤盘，或临时加一块锡纸挡住。
  • Turn the slab skin-side down before slicing so the knife cuts cleanly through the meat without shattering the crisp rind.切烧肉时把脆皮朝下、肉面朝上落刀，皮才不会被硬压碎裂、飞溅满案板。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The skin turned leathery instead of crisp and blistered猪皮发硬咬不动，没有爆出脆泡
    First check whether the skin is fully dried and whether large patches never blistered; if it is just uneven, rotate the tray or broil that spot a little longer. If the whole piece stays stiff, do not keep extending the time, which risks burning it.先判断猪皮是否已经干燥、是否还有大片没爆开的区域；如果只是局部不均，可转动烤盘，或对该处稍微延长高火时间；若整片仍坚硬发死，不要再盲目延长，容易烤焦。
  • Parts of the skin charred black and taste bitter猪皮表面局部烤黑发苦
    Usually spice, sugar or soy sauce touched the skin during marinating, or the tray sat too close to the element; scrape off scorched spots lightly, and next time keep the marinade off the skin — if any splashes on, blot it dry right away before chilling to dry.往往是五香粉、糖或酱油腌肉时沾到了猪皮，或者离上发热管太近；用刀背轻轻刮掉发黑焦斑，下次腌肉时注意别让调料碰到猪皮，万一沾上就立刻用厨房纸吸干再冷藏风干。
  • The lean meat turned dry肉层吃起来偏干偏柴
    The first bake ran too long or the sides were left uncovered; starting at 60 minutes, check with a probe and move to broiling as soon as the center hits 71 °C and the fat has softened, rather than extending the bake indefinitely.第一段烤太久或没用锡纸包住四周；从 60 分钟起用探针检查，一旦肉层中心达到 71 °C 且脂肪软化就立刻进入炙皮，不要无限制延长烘烤。
