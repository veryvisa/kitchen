Store and portion储存与分装

Refrigerate 3-5 days; steaks and roasts freeze 6-12 months, ground beef 3-4 months. Thaw in the fridge.冷藏 3–5 天；牛排和大块肉冷冻 6–12 个月，碎肉 3–4 个月。解冻放冷藏。

Break big packs into meal portions, wrap each one, and put parchment between steaks.大盒肉回家后分成一餐的量，每块单独包好再装袋，牛排之间垫烘焙纸。
