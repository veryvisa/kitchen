Shanghai Crispy Shrimp油爆虾

A Shanghai favorite served hot or at room temperature, where shell-on shrimp are flash-fried until crisp and blistered outside yet snappy inside and glazed in a bright sweet-savory sauce. Two habits make it reliable: drying the shells completely before frying, and premixing the sauce for a fast finish.上海本帮经典冷热皆宜的菜，带壳整虾大火快炸到外壳酥脆起泡、虾肉依然弹嫩，裹上亮红酸甜汁连壳都能嚼。成败全在两步：虾壳下锅前一定要擦干，酱汁提前调好一锅拌匀。

37 min total分钟 (25 + 12)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)另购：老抽、料酒(绍兴)、香醋/陈醋

Ingredients食材

  • Frozen raw shrimp冷冻生虾 500 g · 500 g shell-on raw shrimp (medium, thin-shelled shrimp work best)带壳生虾 500 克（挑中等个头、壳薄的虾）
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks, white parts in batons and green tops minced3 根，葱白切段、葱绿切末
  • Ginger姜 15 g · 5 slices, julienned5 片，切细丝
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1.5 tbsp (22.5 mL)1.5 汤匙（22.5 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 9 g · 1/2 tbsp (7.5 mL)半汤匙（7.5 mL）
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL）
  • Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang)香醋/陈醋 15 g · 1 tbsp (15 mL)1 汤匙（15 mL） Chinkiang black vinegar gives the classic tang用镇江香醋风味最正
  • Sugar白糖 24 g · 2 tbsp (24 g)2 汤匙（24 克）
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 4.5 g · 1 tsp (5 mL)1 茶匙（5 mL）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 20 g · have about 150 mL (10 tbsp) on hand for frying, but pour off most of it afterward and keep only about 1 tbsp as the base oil for the sauce—together with what clings to the fried shells, about 20 g ends up eaten炸制需备约 150 毫升（10 汤匙），炸完倒出大部分、只留约 1 汤匙底油炒料汁，连同虾壳表面挂的油，实际入口约 20 克

Method做法

  1. Trim the whiskers and legs with scissors, remove the sand sac behind the eyes and the vein along the back, and keep the shells intact.剪去虾须和虾脚，挑出虾头沙包和背部虾线，保留完整虾壳。
  2. Rinse the shrimp, press them thoroughly between paper towels until the shells feel dry, and let them air-dry spread out for 10 minutes.洗净后把虾摊在厨房纸上正反按压，吸到虾壳摸起来干爽不滴水，再摊开晾干 10 分钟。
  3. Whisk the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, cooking wine, black vinegar, sugar, and 1 tbsp water (not listed in ingredients) in a small bowl until the sugar mostly dissolves.小碗里放生抽、老抽、料酒、香醋、糖和 1 汤匙清水（清水不计入食材表），搅匀到糖大致融化。
  4. Heat the canola oil in a wok over medium-high heat to about 190°C, when the surface ripples and a wooden chopstick bubbles vigorously.炒锅倒入菜籽油，中大火烧热至约 190°C（油面起波纹、木筷下锅立刻冒密集气泡）。
  5. Spread the shrimp in the hot oil (in two batches if crowded) and fry over high heat for 40 seconds until the shells turn bright red and blistered, then drain.把虾平铺下锅（锅小就分两批），大火炸 40 秒至虾壳亮红起泡发脆后捞出控油。
  6. Bring the oil to about 200°C until faintly smoking, return all the shrimp for a 15-second second fry to crisp the shells further, and drain again.把锅里油温升高到约 200°C 微微冒烟，倒回全部虾复炸 15 秒后捞出控油。
  7. Pour off the frying oil leaving about 1 tbsp in the wok, and stir-fry the julienned ginger and white scallion batons over medium heat for 20 seconds.倒出炸油只留约 1 汤匙底油，中火下姜丝和葱白爆香 20 秒。
  8. Pour in the sauce mixture and boil over medium-high heat for about 30 seconds until bubbling thickly and able to coat the spatula.倒入调好的碗汁，中大火煮约 30 秒至冒大泡、汁能挂住锅铲即可。
  9. Return the shrimp and toss over high heat for 20 seconds until evenly glazed and heated to 74°C inside, then finish with sesame oil and green scallions.倒回炸虾大火颠炒 20 秒至均匀挂上亮汁且中心温度达 74°C，淋香油撒葱花即可出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Two brief high-heat fries blister and crisp the shells before the meat inside has time to dry out.高油温短时间快炸再复炸，能让虾壳在虾肉还没变老前就脆到起泡，实现壳酥肉嫩。
  • Any moisture left in the shells or heads causes violent oil splatter and cools the oil so the shrimp steam instead of crisping.虾身和虾头里的水分不吸干，下油锅不仅剧烈溅油，还会瞬间把油温拉低让虾壳发软。
  • Premixing the sauce lets you boil it over medium-high heat until it coats the spatula and add the shrimp right away; adding seasonings one by one risks uneven mixing or scorching the sugar.酱汁提前调成一碗再下锅，中大火熬到能挂住锅铲就立刻下虾，现加调料容易来不及翻匀或把糖熬苦。
  • To serve them crisp at room temperature, reduce the sauce 10 seconds thicker and let the shrimp cool uncovered so steam does not soften the shells.想留作冷菜吃，熬汁时多收 10 秒让汁更浓，出锅后摊凉不盖盖，外壳依然酥脆。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The shells are chewy instead of crisp and stick to the meat虾壳发软咬不动、还粘在虾肉上
    The oil was too cool or overcrowded; fry in smaller batches above 190°C next time and keep the 15-second second fry.多半是油温不够高或一次倒太多虾进去；下次分批保持 190°C 以上快炸，不要省掉 15 秒复炸。
  • The hot oil splattered violently下锅炸时油花四溅
    Water trapped in the heads and shells caused the splatter; press dry with fresh paper towels and air-dry longer next time, then slide them in from the wok rim.虾头和壳缝没擦干；下次剪好后多换两张厨房纸按干再晾几分钟，下锅时贴着锅边滑入，旁边备好锅盖。
  • The glaze is watery or tastes burnt糖汁挂不住，或者吃出焦苦味
    If watery, lift the shrimp out and boil the sauce 20 seconds longer before tossing; if bitter, the sugar caramelized too far—add the shrimp as soon as the bubbles thicken and coat the spatula.太稀就把虾先盛出，单把锅里的汁多收 20 秒再拌回；发苦说明糖汁熬过头，下次见大泡能挂住锅铲就立刻下虾。
