Vegetable素菜 · Northeastern Chinese东北

Di San Xian (Potato, Eggplant and Pepper)地三鲜

Northeast China's "three treasures of the earth": potato, eggplant and pepper. Restaurants deep-fry them; at home a skillet uses less than half the oil, and you still get crisp-edged creamy potato and soft, intact eggplant that are perfect with rice.东北人的「地里三样鲜」：土豆、茄子、青椒。饭店是过油炸的，家里用平底锅煎，油少一半以上，土豆外酥里糯、茄子软而不烂，一样下饭。

35 min total分钟 (10 + 25)3 servings份Medium中等Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Potatoes土豆 300 g · 2 medium potatoes, cut into chunks2 个中等土豆，切滚刀块
  • Eggplant茄子 400 g · 1 globe eggplant or 2 long ones, in chunks1 个圆茄子或 2 根长茄子，切滚刀块
  • Bell peppers彩椒 150 g · 1 green pepper, torn into pieces1 个青椒，掰成块
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 15 g · 2 tbsp cornstarch (1 1/2 for the eggplant, 1/2 for the sauce)淀粉 2 汤匙（裹茄子 1.5 汤匙，调汁半汤匙）
  • Canola oil菜籽油 56 g · 4 tbsp (60 mL)4 汤匙（60 mL）
  • Garlic大蒜 12 g · 4 cloves, minced4 瓣，切末
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 10 g · 1 stalk, chopped1 根，切葱花
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Oyster sauce蚝油 9 g · 1/2 tbsp半汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 6 g · 1 1/2 tsp1.5 茶匙
  • Salt盐 1 g · a small pinch一小撮

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Make the sauce: stir the soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, 1/2 tbsp cornstarch and 80 mL water together.调汁：生抽、蚝油、糖、半汤匙淀粉和 80 mL 水搅匀。
  2. Toss the eggplant with the salt, wait 5 minutes and blot it dry.茄子块撒盐拌匀，放 5 分钟后用厨房纸吸干水。
  3. Coat the eggplant with 1 1/2 tbsp cornstarch.茄子块裹上 1.5 汤匙淀粉。
  4. Fry the potato in 2 tbsp oil over medium heat until golden all over and tender, about 10 minutes, then take it out.平底锅放 2 汤匙油，中火把土豆块煎到四面金黄、能用筷子扎透，约 10 分钟，盛出。
  5. Add 2 tbsp oil and fry the eggplant until golden and soft inside, about 6 minutes, then take it out.补 2 汤匙油，把茄子块煎到表面金黄、内部变软，约 6 分钟，盛出。
  6. Fry the garlic in the oil left in the pan and stir-fry the pepper for 1 minute.锅里余油炒香蒜末，放青椒炒 1 分钟。
  7. Return the potato and eggplant and pour in the sauce.土豆和茄子回锅，倒入调好的汁。
  8. Toss over high heat until the sauce thickens and coats everything, add the green onion and serve.大火翻炒到汁变稠、均匀裹住食材，撒葱花出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Salting the eggplant before coating it means it soaks up less oil and holds its shape.茄子先用盐杀水再裹淀粉，煎的时候吸油少，也不容易煎烂。
  • Cook the potato through first; the final toss is too short to finish it.土豆一定要先煎熟，回锅后时间很短，生的不会再熟。
  • Add the sauce all at once and reduce it fast over high heat for a glossy finish.汁要一次倒进去，大火快速收，菜才亮。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The eggplant soaked up all the oil茄子吸了很多油
    It was not salted or coated; salt and blot it, then coat it thinly in starch.没杀水、没裹淀粉；下次先盐腌吸干，再薄裹淀粉。
  • The eggplant turned to mush茄子煎烂成泥
    It was turned too often; let each side set before turning.翻动太勤；煎的时候每面等定型再翻。
