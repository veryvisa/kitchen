Quiche Lorraine洛林咸派

A crisp pastry shell holds a savory bacon and cream custard, making this quiche a good fit for brunch or a gathering. Blind-bake the crust first, then cook the filling just until set so the base stays crisp and the custard stays tender.酥脆派皮托住培根与奶油蛋奶馅，切片后适合做早午餐或带去聚会。关键是派皮先烤定形，蛋奶馅则要烤到中心凝固，避免底部湿软或表面过老。

125 min total分钟 (35 + 60)6 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Ground nutmeg另购：肉豆蔻粉

Ingredients食材

  • All-purpose flour面粉 250 g · 2 cups all-purpose flour中筋面粉 2 杯
  • Butter黄油 125 g · 125 g cold unsalted butter, cubed冷的无盐黄油，切丁
  • Eggs鸡蛋 250 g · about 5 large eggs, 1 for the crust and 4 for the filling约 5 个大鸡蛋，1 个做派皮、4 个做馅
  • Bacon培根 180 g · bacon, cut into small pieces培根，切小片
  • Whipping cream淡奶油 300 g · about 1 1/4 cups约 1¼ 杯
  • Milk 2%牛奶 100 g · about 1/3 cup plus 1 tbsp约 ⅓ 杯加 1 汤匙
  • Salt盐 4 g · about 2/3 tsp, divided约 ⅔ 茶匙，分次使用
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · about 1/2 tsp, freshly ground约 ½ 茶匙，现磨
  • Ground nutmeg肉豆蔻粉 0.5 g · a pinch, about 1/4 tsp一小撮，约 ¼ 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Whisk the flour and 2 g of the salt together in a large bowl.把面粉和 2 克盐放入大碗拌匀。
  2. Rub the cold butter into the flour with your fingertips until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs.用指尖把冷黄油搓进面粉，直到混合物呈粗面包屑状。
  3. Add 1 beaten egg and mix gently just until the dough holds together.加入 1 个打散的鸡蛋，轻轻拌到面团刚能成团。
  4. Press the dough into a disk, wrap it and chill for 30 minutes.把面团压成圆饼，包好冷藏 30 分钟。
  5. Preheat the oven to 190°C.烤箱预热至 190°C。
  6. Roll the dough into a roughly 30 cm round, fit it into a 23 cm pie plate and trim the edge.把面团擀成约 30 厘米圆片，铺入 23 厘米派盘并修齐边缘。
  7. Prick the base evenly with a fork and chill it for 10 minutes.用叉子在派底扎出均匀小孔，冷藏 10 分钟。
  8. Bake the crust for 18 minutes until the edges are pale golden and the base looks dry.派皮烤 18 分钟，直到边缘浅金黄、底部看起来干燥。
  9. Cook the bacon in a skillet over medium heat for 6–8 minutes until the fat renders and the edges turn crisp.培根放入平底锅，中火煎 6–8 分钟，直到脂肪析出、边缘微脆。
  10. Transfer the bacon to a paper towel to drain.把培根捞到纸巾上沥油。
  11. Whisk the 4 eggs with the cream, milk, remaining salt, black pepper and nutmeg.把 4 个鸡蛋、奶油、牛奶、剩下的盐、黑胡椒和肉豆蔻搅匀。
  12. Scatter the bacon over the crust and pour in the custard.将培根均匀铺在派皮底部，倒入蛋奶液。
  13. Bake at 175°C for 35–40 minutes until the edges are set and the center still wobbles slightly; confirm with a thermometer that the center has reached 74°C, then take it out.以 175°C 烤 35–40 分钟，直到边缘凝固、中心仍有轻微颤动，用温度计确认中心达到 74°C 即可出炉。
  14. Rest the quiche on a rack for 15 minutes before slicing.把咸派放在架上静置 15 分钟再切片。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Keep the butter cold for a flaky crust; chill the bowl and flour first if your hands run warm.黄油要保持冷硬，派皮才容易烤出酥松层次；手温高时可先把碗和面粉冷藏。
  • Blind-baking until the crust looks dry helps keep custard from making the base soggy.派皮先烤到表面干燥，能隔开蛋奶液，减少底部湿软。
  • Whisk the custard just until combined; excess air can make it puff and then sink.蛋奶液只需搅匀，不要打出很多泡沫，表面才不容易鼓起后塌陷。
  • A slight wobble at 74°C is normal; judge doneness by the thermometer, not by how much the center still moves, or the edges will overcook first.74°C 时中心仍会有轻微颤动，这是正常的；熟度判断只看温度计，不要靠'还在抖就是没熟'的感觉多烤，否则边缘会先变老。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The crust is soggy underneath派底湿软
    Next time, bake the crust a few minutes longer until the base looks dry; reheat slices on an oven rack for 5 minutes to crisp them.下次把派皮多烤几分钟至底部干燥再加馅；已经烤好的可切片后放烤箱架上回烤 5 分钟。
  • The custard is grainy or weeping蛋馅粗糙、渗水
    Use a lower oven temperature next time and remove the quiche as soon as the center sets; let a baked quiche rest 15 minutes before cutting.下次降低烤箱温度并在中心刚凝固时出炉；已经出炉的咸派静置 15 分钟再切。
  • The pastry edges are burnt派皮边缘焦黑
    Loosely cover the edges with foil once they turn golden, and check that the pie plate is not too close to the top of the oven.烤到边缘金黄后用铝箔松松盖住派边，并确认烤盘没有贴近烤箱顶部。
