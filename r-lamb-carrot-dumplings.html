Lamb and Carrot Dumplings羊肉胡萝卜水饺

A northern winter classic. Lamb's richness meets the sweetness of carrot, which is tossed in oil first so it also softens any gamey edge. Mincing Costco boneless leg at home is fresher than bought mince and lets you choose the fat.北方冬天的经典馅。羊肉的鲜配胡萝卜的甜，胡萝卜先用油拌过，香甜也能压住羊肉的膻味。Costco 去骨羊腿自己剁馅，比买现成的肉馅新鲜，肥瘦也自己定。

85 min total分钟 (70 + 15)5 servings份Needs care需要用心Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • All-purpose flour面粉 500 g · 4 cups (500 g) all-purpose flour中筋面粉 4 杯（500 克）
  • Boneless leg of lamb去骨羊腿肉 450 g · 450 g boneless leg of lamb, minced with a little fat去骨羊腿肉 450 克，剁成馅（带一点肥）
  • Carrots胡萝卜 300 g · 2 carrots, grated and chopped2 根胡萝卜，擦细丝后剁碎
  • Onions洋葱 100 g · half an onion, finely chopped半个洋葱，切碎
  • Ginger姜 10 g · a knob, grated1 块，磨蓉
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks, minced3 根，切末
  • Soy sauce生抽 27 g · 1.5 tbsp1.5 汤匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 28 g · 2 tbsp, for the carrot2 汤匙，拌胡萝卜
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 7 g · 1/2 tbsp1/2 汤匙
  • Salt盐 7 g · about 1 tsp (1/4 tsp in the dough, the rest in the filling)约 1 茶匙（和面 1/4 茶匙，其余调馅）
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · 1/4 tsp1/4 茶匙

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Mix the flour with 1/4 tsp salt and about 250 mL cold water, knead into a fairly firm dough, cover and rest 30 minutes.面粉加 1/4 茶匙盐和约 250 mL 冷水，揉成稍硬的面团，盖上醒 30 分钟。
  2. Season the lamb with ginger, soy, salt and pepper, then beat in 80 mL water in three parts in one direction until sticky.羊肉馅加姜蓉、生抽、盐和胡椒，分三次加 80 mL 水，朝一个方向搅到起胶。
  3. Toss the chopped carrot with the canola and sesame oils.胡萝卜碎先拌上菜籽油和香油。
  4. Fold the carrot, onion and green onion into the lamb.胡萝卜、洋葱和葱末倒进肉馅拌匀。
  5. Roll the dough into ropes, cut 10 g pieces and roll each into a round thicker in the middle than at the edge.面团搓条，切约 10 克一个的剂子，擀成中间厚边缘薄的皮。
  6. Fill with a spoonful, fold, pinch shut and squeeze into a plump crescent.包入一勺馅，对折捏紧，挤成元宝形。
  7. Boil, stirring once so they do not stick, and add a cup of cold water each time it boils, three times.水开下饺子，推一下防粘，每次水开点一碗冷水，共三次。
  8. Lift them out when they are all floating and puffed.饺子全部浮起、鼓胀后捞出。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Leg meat is lean, so keep some of the white fat when mincing and beat in water for a juicy filling.羊腿肉偏瘦，剁馅时留一点白色脂肪，再分次打水，馅才嫩、有汁。
  • Oiling the carrot brings out its sweetness, keeps it from weeping and tames any gamey taste.胡萝卜先拌油，甜味出来、不出水，还能把羊肉的膻味压下去。
  • Onion is lamb's traditional partner and adds more flavour than green onion alone.洋葱和羊肉是老搭配，比单放葱更香。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The filling is dry馅发干发柴
    The meat was too lean or short of water. Mix in 1 tbsp oil and beat in 2 more tbsp water.肉太瘦或水打得不够。加 1 汤匙油、再分次打 2 汤匙水。
  • It tastes gamey有膻味
    Blot the meat dry before mincing, and double the ginger and pepper.剁馅前用厨房纸吸干血水；调馅时姜蓉和胡椒可多放一倍。
