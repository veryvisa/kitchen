Soy Sauce Fried Noodles豉油皇炒面

A classic Cantonese cafe and home staple that turns plain noodles, bean sprouts, scallions, and a little onion into a smoky, savory dish with just soy sauces. At home it easily sticks to the pan or clumps together, so the trick is parboiling the noodles until just shy of tender, airing them dry and tossing with a little oil, and flash-frying the bean sprouts at the very end so they stay crisp.茶餐厅和家常饭桌上的经典炒面，面条、豆芽、葱、一点洋葱和几种酱油就能炒出锅气。在家做的难点在于面条容易粘锅或炒成一坨，关键是面煮七八分熟就捞起摊晾拌油，最后下豆芽大火快炒断生不出水。

Ingredients食材

  • Wheat noodles挂面/面条 200 g · 200 g dried thin wheat or Hong Kong-style egg noodles细干碱面或细鸡蛋面 200 克 thinner strands fry up drier and bouncier面条越细越容易炒出干爽口感
  • Mung bean sprouts绿豆芽 150 g · 150 g mung bean sprouts (about 2 cups)绿豆芽 150 克（约 2 杯） trim the roots for a cleaner crunch掐掉根须更爽脆
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 30 g · 3 stalks, white parts sliced and greens cut into 5 cm lengths3 根，葱白切段、葱绿切 5 厘米长段
  • Onions洋葱 60 g · 1/2 small onion, thinly sliced半个小洋葱，切细丝
  • Soy sauce生抽 30 g · 2 tbsp (30 mL)2 汤匙（30 mL）
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 12 g · 2 tsp (10 mL)2 茶匙（10 mL） for deep color and roasted soy aroma上色和提亮酱香用
  • Sugar白糖 4 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 7 g · 1/2 tbsp (7 mL)半汤匙（7 mL）

  • Sesame seeds白芝麻 5 g · 1.5 tsp toasted white sesame seeds (not traditional, purely a garnish — optional)1.5 茶匙，熟白芝麻（传统做法没有芝麻，纯装饰，可省略）

Method做法

  1. Stir the soy sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, and 1 tbsp water together in a small bowl.生抽、老抽、糖和 1 汤匙清水在小碗里拌匀备用。
  2. Boil the noodles in a pot of water for about 1 minute (check the package timing), stirring with chopsticks to separate them, until just shy of tender with a firm core, then drain well.烧一锅沸水，下入面条煮约 1 分钟（按包装时间调整）至七八分熟且面芯略硬，用筷子抖散，捞出沥干水分。
  3. Spread the noodles on a large plate for 3 minutes to steam dry, then toss with 1 tbsp canola oil to prevent sticking (save the sesame oil for the very end, off the heat, so its aroma does not cook off).把面条摊在大盘里晾 3 分钟散去水汽，拌入 1 汤匙菜籽油防粘（香油留到最后离火再淋，高温久炒会把香味烧散）。
  4. Heat a wok or wide skillet until lightly smoking, add the remaining 2 tbsp canola oil, and stir-fry the onion and scallion whites over medium-high heat for 1 minute until lightly browned at the edges.炒锅或宽平底锅烧到微微冒烟，倒入剩下 2 汤匙菜籽油，下洋葱丝和葱白中大火炒 1 分钟至边缘微焦。
  5. Raise the heat to high, spread the noodles in the pan, sear undisturbed for 30 seconds, and toss for about 1 minute until dry and springy.转大火铺入面条，先静置煎 30 秒让底面略焦，再抖散翻炒约 1 分钟至面条干爽有弹性。
  6. Pour the mixed sauce around the pan edges and toss vigorously over high heat for 30 to 45 seconds until every strand is evenly glazed.沿锅边淋入调好的酱汁，大火快速兜炒 30–45 秒让每根面条均匀挂上油亮酱色。
  7. Toss in the well-washed bean sprouts and scallion greens over high heat for 20 to 30 seconds until just heated through but still crisp, then turn off the heat.倒入洗净的豆芽和葱绿，大火翻炒 20–30 秒至刚刚变热但仍挺脆后关火。
  8. Off the heat, drizzle in the sesame oil and toss, transfer to a plate, scatter the toasted sesame seeds on top if using, and serve hot.关火后淋入香油拌匀，装盘撒上熟白芝麻（可省略），趁热上桌。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Stop boiling while the noodles still have a firm core; they finish cooking in the pan, so fully boiled noodles turn mushy.面条绝对不能煮到全熟，下锅还要再炒两三分钟，七八分熟捞出才能保持筋道不糊。
  • Letting the drained noodles steam dry before oiling removes surface moisture so they sear cleanly instead of steaming in the pan.煮好后摊开散掉表面水汽再拌油，面条干爽才容易炒出锅气和焦香。
  • Bean sprouts hold plenty of water, so add them only at the end over high heat for 20 to 30 seconds; cooking them too long floods the noodles. For children, older adults, pregnant women or anyone with a weaker immune system, wash the sprouts thoroughly and use the longer end of that range so they are fully heated through.豆芽含水量很高，必须最后下锅大火快炒二三十秒，炒久了会出水把面泡软；给孩子、老人、孕妇或免疫力弱者吃，炒足这个上限时间，并确保豆芽洗净、炒到完全变热。
  • Premixing the sauces and pouring them against the hot pan rim sears the soy aroma right away and coats the noodles evenly.酱汁提前在碗里调匀再沿锅边淋入，高温瞬间激发酱香，面条也不会咸淡不均。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The noodles stuck to the pan or clumped together面条黏锅底甚至糊成一团
    Heat the pan well before adding oil, let the noodles sear briefly before tossing, and loosen clumps with chopsticks; next time boil 15 to 20 seconds less and air-dry longer.锅一定要先烧热再放油，面条下锅先煎定型再翻动，用筷子配合锅铲抖散；下次煮面少煮 15–20 秒并多摊晾一会儿。
  • The bean sprouts released water and made the dish soggy豆芽出水，整盘面湿哒哒的
    This usually means the noodles were not drained well enough, or the pan was overcrowded: use a large wok or fry in two batches, and drain and air-dry the noodles fully before frying. For a soggy batch, push the sprouts aside and reduce their liquid separately rather than overcooking the noodles with them.多半是面条没沥干或锅太挤：用大锅或分两批炒，面条彻底沥干摊晾后再下锅；这次已经出水的，把豆芽拨到一边单独收干汤汁，不要连面条一起久煮。
  • Pale color or flat salty taste without roasted soy aroma颜色太浅或吃起来只有咸味没有酱香
    Dark soy brings color and depth while light soy adds savoriness; pour the sauce onto the sizzling pan edge. If you want more color at the end, dilute an extra 1/2 tsp dark soy with an equal amount of water before drizzling it in — undiluted dark soy poured straight on can leave dark, bitter patches.老抽负责上色和焦香，生抽提鲜，酱汁要沿滚烫的锅边淋下；出锅前如果还想补色，先把半茶匙老抽用等量清水稀释再淋入拌匀，直接倒浓老抽容易花色发苦。

The soy sauces add substantial sodium, so pair it with plain blanched greens for a fuller meal, and cut the soy sauce by 1 tsp if you are watching sodium.以精制小麦面条为主，碳水化合物比例较高，豆芽提供少量膳食纤维；酱油含钠不少，搭配一盘白灼青菜会更均衡，减钠可把生抽减少 1 茶匙。
