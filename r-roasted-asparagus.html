Garlic Roasted Asparagus蒜香烤芦笋

The easiest side for a Western dinner. Snap the woody ends off a Costco bunch, rub with olive oil and salt, roast ten minutes, finish with lemon and cheese. A standard partner for steak, salmon or roast chicken.西式晚餐最省事的配菜。Costco 一捆芦笋，掰掉老根，抹橄榄油和盐，烤箱里十分钟，挤柠檬撒奶酪就能上桌。和牛排、三文鱼、烤鸡都是标准搭配。

17 min total分钟 (5 + 12)3 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

Ingredients食材

  • Asparagus芦笋 450 g · 1 bunch asparagus (about 450 g)芦笋 1 捆（约 450 克） pick tight tips and firm, straight stems选笋尖紧密、茎直挺的
  • Extra virgin olive oil橄榄油 21 g · 1.5 tbsp1.5 汤匙
  • Garlic大蒜 6 g · 2 cloves, minced2 瓣，切末
  • Salt盐 2 g · about 1/3 tsp约 1/3 茶匙
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · a few grinds少许
  • Lemons柠檬 50 g · half a lemon, juiced半个，挤汁
  • Parmesan帕玛森奶酪 15 g · 15 g parmesan, grated (optional)帕玛森奶酪 15 克，擦碎（可不放）

Weights are as bought, before trimming or peeling. Costco pack sizes and availability vary; see each ingredient page.重量为买回来的原重（去皮去骨之前）。Costco 规格与有无随时变化，点食材名看详情。

Method做法

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F).烤箱预热到 220°C（425°F）。
  2. Bend each spear and snap off the woody end where it breaks naturally.芦笋握住两端一弯，从自然断开处掰掉老根。
  3. Lay the asparagus on a tray, drizzle with oil, add the garlic, salt and pepper and roll to coat.芦笋铺在烤盘上，淋橄榄油，撒蒜末、盐和胡椒，滚匀。
  4. Spread in one layer and roast 10-12 minutes, until a fork pierces the stems and the tips are lightly crisp.单层摊开，烤 10–12 分钟到茎能被叉子扎透、笋尖微焦。
  5. Squeeze over the lemon and scatter the parmesan.出炉挤柠檬汁，撒帕玛森奶酪。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Snapping rather than cutting lets each spear break exactly where the tough part ends.用手掰而不是刀切，芦笋会自己在老嫩交界处断开。
  • Do not crowd the tray; overlapping spears steam instead of roasting.烤盘不要挤，芦笋重叠会蒸出水，烤不出焦香。
  • Thick spears take about 12 minutes, thin ones about 8.粗的芦笋烤 12 分钟，细的 8 分钟就够。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The asparagus is limp and drab芦笋软塌发黄
    Overcooked. Check 3 minutes earlier and pull it as soon as a fork goes in.烤太久了。提前 3 分钟检查，茎刚能扎透就出炉。
  • The garlic burned蒜末烤焦发苦
    It was too fine or in too long; add it for the last 4 minutes instead.蒜末太细或烤太久，可以在最后 4 分钟再撒上。
