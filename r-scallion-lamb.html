Scallion Lamb Stir-fry葱爆羊肉

A classic from Beijing home cooking: thin lamb slices and a lot of scallion, flashed over very high heat in under a minute. The scallion turns sweet and slightly charred and the lamb stays tender. It all depends on a screaming-hot pan and fast hands.这是京菜的家常经典：薄羊肉片加大量葱段，大火几十秒爆熟，葱焦香微甜，羊肉嫩而不膻。难在火要旺、动作要快，肉一变色就得出锅。

36 min total分钟 (15 + 6)2 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Shaoxing wine另购：料酒(绍兴)

Ingredients食材

  • Lamb slices羊肉片 250 g · about 250 g thinly sliced lamb羊肉片约 250 克 thawed frozen hot-pot lamb works well冷冻火锅羊肉卷也可以，先解冻
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 200 g · about 10 stalks, cut on the bias into 4 cm pieces约 10 根，斜切成 4 厘米的段 plenty; it is the star葱要多，是主角
  • Garlic大蒜 10 g · 3 cloves, sliced3 瓣，切片
  • Ginger姜 8 g · minced, about 2 tsp切末，约 2 茶匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 18 g · 1 tbsp, half for the marinade and half to finish1 汤匙，腌肉半汤匙，调味半汤匙
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 15 g · 1 tbsp1 汤匙
  • Cornstarch玉米淀粉 3 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Rice vinegar米醋 8 g · 1.5 tsp, drizzled down the side of the pan1.5 茶匙，沿锅边淋入
  • Sugar白糖 2 g · 1/2 tsp半茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 42 g · 3 tbsp (45 mL)3 汤匙（45 mL）
  • Black pepper黑胡椒 0.5 g · a pinch少许

Method做法

  1. Put the lamb in a bowl with 1/2 tbsp soy sauce, the wine, cornstarch, ginger, sesame oil and pepper, and mix by hand.羊肉片放碗里，加生抽半汤匙、料酒、淀粉、姜末、芝麻油和黑胡椒，抓匀。
  2. Stir in 1 tbsp oil to seal the surface and marinate for 15 minutes.再加 1 汤匙油拌匀封住表面，腌 15 分钟。
  3. Keep the scallion whites and greens separate, cutting the whites thick and the greens on the bias.葱白和葱绿分开放，葱白切粗段，葱绿斜切。
  4. Heat a wok until it just smokes, then add 2 tbsp oil.炒锅烧到冒烟，倒入 2 汤匙油。
  5. Spread the lamb out fast and stir over the highest heat for 20 seconds until it just loses its pink, then take it out.下羊肉片迅速摊开，大火炒 20 秒，变色即盛出。
  6. In the same pan add the garlic and scallion whites and stir over high heat for 30 seconds until the edges brown slightly.锅里直接下蒜片和葱白，大火炒 30 秒，到边缘微焦。
  7. Return the lamb and add the scallion greens, sugar and the rest of the soy sauce.羊肉回锅，加入葱绿、糖和剩下的半汤匙生抽。
  8. Drizzle the vinegar down the side of the pan, toss for 15 seconds and serve as soon as the greens soften.沿锅边淋入醋，快速翻炒 15 秒，葱绿刚软就出锅。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Thin slices with a little oil worked in cook in seconds over high heat and stay tender.羊肉片要薄、油要先拌进去，水分被锁住，大火几秒钟就熟，不会柴。
  • Pour the vinegar down the hot side of the wok so it flashes into aroma; poured onto the meat it just tastes sour.醋要沿锅边淋，遇热锅气化，去膻提香；直接倒在肉上味道会发酸。
  • On a weak home burner, cook the lamb in two batches, because a cooled pan makes it release water and stew.家用炉火力不够时，羊肉分两次炒，不要一次下全部，锅一冷就出水。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The lamb is watery and chewy羊肉出水、发柴
    The pan was crowded or not hot enough. Next time cook in batches, start with a smoking pan and remove the lamb the moment it changes color.肉量太多或锅不够热；下次分批炒，锅烧到冒烟再放肉，变色就盛出。
  • It tastes too gamey膻味明显
    Do not skimp on ginger, vinegar and pepper. For very strong lamb, soak the slices in cold water for 10 minutes and squeeze them dry first.加足姜、醋和胡椒，羊肉腌的时候不要省姜；实在受不了先用冷水泡 10 分钟再挤干。
