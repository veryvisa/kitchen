Korean Braised Tofu (Dubu Jorim)韩式酱烧豆腐

The most rice-friendly of Korean home side dishes. Thick slices of firm tofu are fried golden, then simmered a few minutes in soy, chilli paste and garlic, so the outside soaks up the sauce while the inside stays soft. It keeps for two meals and is good cold in a lunchbox.韩国家常小菜里最下饭的一道。老豆腐切厚片煎到两面金黄，再用酱油、辣酱和蒜调的汁小火烧几分钟，豆腐外层吸满酱汁、里面还是嫩的。做一次能吃两顿，冷了带饭也好吃。

20 min total分钟 (5 + 15)3 servings份Easy简单Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Firm tofu另购：老豆腐

Ingredients食材

  • Canola oil菜籽油 21 g · 1.5 tbsp1.5 汤匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙

  • Sesame oil香油(芝麻油) 5 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙

Method做法

  1. Lay the tofu on paper towel and press for 5 minutes to draw out water.豆腐片放在厨房纸上，撒一点盐，压 5 分钟吸水。
  2. Mix the soy, gochujang, sugar, garlic, sesame oil and 100 mL water into a sauce.生抽、辣酱、糖、蒜末、香油和 100 mL 水调成酱汁。
  3. Fry the tofu in the hot oil over medium heat for 3 minutes until golden underneath.平底锅烧热油，豆腐片中火煎 3 分钟到底面金黄。
  4. Turn and fry 3 more minutes.翻面再煎 3 分钟。
  5. Scatter over the onion, pour in the sauce and simmer 5 minutes on low, spooning the sauce over the tofu.铺上洋葱丝，倒入酱汁，小火烧 5 分钟，中途舀汁浇在豆腐上。
  6. When the sauce has reduced by half, turn off the heat and add the green onion and sesame seeds.汁收到剩一半时关火，撒葱花和芝麻。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Drying the tofu first gives a firm golden crust that does not break in the sauce.豆腐先吸干水再煎，表皮才会金黄结实，烧的时候不碎。
  • Do not rush to turn it; once the crust forms it releases from the pan on its own.煎的时候别急着翻，底面定型后自己就能和锅分开。
  • Leave a little sauce in the pan; the tofu keeps soaking it up as it cools.酱汁留一点不要收干，放凉后豆腐还会继续吸。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The tofu stuck and broke豆腐粘锅、煎碎了
    The pan was not hot or the tofu was wet. Heat the pan first and dry the tofu; a non-stick pan is safest.锅没烧热或豆腐太湿。锅烧热再放油，豆腐吸干水；不粘锅最稳。
  • Too salty太咸
    Dark soy or a salty chilli paste. Next time cut the soy to 1.5 tbsp and add 2 tbsp more water.用了老抽或辣酱太咸。下次生抽减到 1.5 汤匙，多加 2 汤匙水。

Good to know适合 / 需注意
