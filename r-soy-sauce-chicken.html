Soy Sauce Chicken豉油鸡

Soy sauce chicken has glossy skin, rich savory-sweet flavor and stays juicy even at room temperature, making it ideal for weekend dinners or potlucks. The usual mistake at home is boiling hard the whole time, which tears the skin and dries out the breast. A gentle simmer followed by an off-heat steep makes juicy, thoroughly cooked chicken in a regular Dutch oven. Check the innermost thigh, innermost wing and thickest breast with a thermometer; each should reach 82°C (180°F), away from the bone.豉油鸡皮滑肉嫩、酱香浓郁，放温了也好吃，很适合周末加菜或带去聚会。家里做最容易翻车的是全程大火滚煮，把鸡皮煮破、鸡胸煮柴；改用微沸加关火焖浸，用普通铸铁锅也能做出肉质熟透又多汁的豉油鸡。整鸡烹调后要用温度计检查最厚腿肉、翅膀内侧和鸡胸最厚处，均达到 82°C，并避开骨头。

Method做法

  1. Pat the chicken thoroughly dry inside and out with paper towels, removing any loose fat pads near the cavity.用厨房纸把整鸡内外彻底擦干，顺手去掉腹腔口多余的块状油脂。
  2. Heat the canola oil in a Dutch oven over medium-low heat and cook the ginger, green onion, garlic and star anise for 2 minutes until fragrant.铸铁锅或深汤锅放食用油，中小火把姜片、葱段、蒜瓣和八角炒 2 分钟至飘香。
  3. Add the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, cooking wine, sugar and 1.2-1.5 L water. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves, then lower the heat until the liquid settles to 85-92°C, just trembling.倒入生抽、老抽、料酒、白糖和 1.2–1.5 L 清水，中大火煮开并搅拌至糖融化，再调低火力让卤汁降至 85–92°C、只微微颤动。
  4. Use a deep enough pot for the liquid to reach about three-quarters of the chicken, adding more water if needed.选用足够深的锅，确保放入鸡后卤汁能到鸡身四分之三处，水量不足时再加清水。
  5. Hold the chicken steady, dip it into the gently trembling liquid for 10 seconds, then lift it so the cavity drains back into the pot. Repeat three times.提起整鸡放进微微颤动的卤汁中浸烫 10 秒，再提起让腹腔汁水流回锅中，重复三次。
  6. Set the chicken breast-side down in the pot. If needed, weigh it down with a heatproof plate so it stays as submerged as possible, basting any exposed areas with hot liquid.把鸡胸朝下放入锅中，必要时用耐热盘压住，让鸡尽量浸在卤汁中，露出液面的部位用热卤汁淋浇。
  7. Cover, wait for the liquid to return to a bare simmer, then cook for 10 minutes with only tiny bubbles at the rim.盖上锅盖，待卤汁恢复微沸后，保持锅边只有细小气泡，煮 10 分钟。
  8. Gently flip the chicken breast-side up using two wide spatulas. Cover and simmer gently for 8–10 more minutes, for 18–20 minutes of total poaching at a bare simmer.用两把大铲轻轻把鸡翻成鸡胸朝上，盖回锅盖，继续微沸煮 8–10 分钟，使下锅后的微沸煮制时间共计 18–20 分钟。
  9. Turn off the heat and leave the pot covered for 20-25 minutes.关火并保持锅盖紧闭，浸泡 20–25 分钟。
  10. Check the thickest thigh, innermost wing and thickest breast with a thermometer, avoiding bone; the chicken should reach 82°C (180°F).用温度计检查最厚腿肉、翅膀内侧和鸡胸最厚处，避开骨头，整鸡应达到 82°C。
  11. If any spot is below 82°C, cover the pot and return it to the stove at a bare simmer until it reaches temperature.若有部位未达 82°C，盖好锅盖，将锅放回炉上保持微沸，继续加热至达标。
  12. Lift the chicken onto a wire rack to rest for 10 minutes, brushing the skin lightly with sesame oil for sheen. After removing the chicken, bring the poaching liquid that contacted the raw chicken to a full boil and keep it boiling for at least 1 minute before serving or storing.捞出整鸡放在晾网上静置 10 分钟，同时把香油薄刷在鸡皮上增亮。鸡取出后，将接触过生鸡的卤汁煮至完全沸腾并持续至少 1 分钟，再留作食用或保存。
  13. Chop into bite-size pieces, arrange on a platter and spoon only a little of the boiled poaching sauce over the top.斩成适口鸡块装盘，只淋少量已煮沸的豉油卤汁。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Dipping and draining the chicken three times helps hot liquid fill and preheat the cavity, encouraging more even cooking and preventing a cold pocket.下锅前把鸡腹放入热卤汁再提起沥出，重复三次，有助于热液进入腹腔、让鸡受热更均匀，并避免腹腔形成冷区。
  • Never let the poaching liquid come to a rolling boil; vigorous bubbling tears the skin and squeezes moisture out of the breast.全程不能大火翻滚，剧烈沸腾不仅会把鸡皮煮裂，还会逼出肉汁使鸡胸发柴。
  • The off-heat steep gently finishes cooking; use a thermometer to confirm the thickest thigh, innermost wing and thickest breast each reach 82°C (180°F).关火焖浸靠余温把肉慢慢熟透；用温度计确认最厚腿肉、翅膀内侧和鸡胸最厚处均达到 82°C。
  • Boil leftover sauce for at least 1 minute before storing. Cool it quickly, then refrigerate or freeze. Strain and skim the fat before reusing it for braised eggs, tofu or chicken wings.剩下的豉油汁煮沸至少 1 分钟后再保存；快速冷却后冷藏或冷冻。过滤撇去浮油，可留作下次卤蛋、豆干或鸡翅。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The chicken breast is dry and stringy鸡胸肉发柴发干
    Usually caused by boiling too hard or too long. Shred the dry breast and toss it with a little boiled poaching sauce. Next time, keep the liquid at a bare simmer and check doneness with a thermometer.多半是煮时火力太大或时间过长；把鸡肉顺纹撕细，拌少量已煮沸的热卤汁吃。下次只保持锅边微冒小泡，并用温度计确认熟度。
  • The meat near the bone is bloody and undercooked切开后骨边还有明显血水，没熟透
    Bring the poaching liquid to a bare simmer, return the chicken pieces, and cook or steep them until the thickest pieces reach 82°C (180°F). Check with a thermometer rather than relying on a fixed time.把豉油汁加热至微沸，放回鸡块，继续煮或焖至最厚处达到 82°C；用温度计检查，不要只按固定时间判断。
  • The skin tore or colored unevenly鸡皮破了或上色不均匀
    Vigorous boiling or piercing with a fork tears skin. Lift with two spatulas when flipping, and baste any exposed skin with hot liquid during the steep.沸水翻腾或用叉子扎肉都会破皮；翻面用宽铲托起，浸泡期间若鸡胸露出水面，中途舀热汁淋上去。
