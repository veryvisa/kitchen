Braised Beef Noodle Soup红烧牛肉面

The best seller of noodle shops in Taiwan and the mainland alike. Beef chunks braised two hours with chilli bean paste, soy and spices give a glossy red broth and meat that is tender but holds together; add noodles and greens for a big bowl. Braise on the weekend and freeze the beef and broth for ten-minute weeknight bowls.台湾和大陆面馆里都卖得最好的一碗面。牛肉块用豆瓣酱、酱油和香料炖两个小时，汤色红亮、肉软而不散，下一把面、烫几棵青菜就是一大碗。周末炖一锅，牛肉和汤分装冷冻，平日十分钟就能吃上。

140 min total分钟 (20 + 120)4 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Doubanjiang, Dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, Sichuan peppercorn, Bok choy另购：豆瓣酱、老抽、料酒(绍兴)、花椒、小白菜

Ingredients食材

  • Beef chuck / stewing beef牛肩肉(炖牛肉用) 600 g · 600 g beef chuck or brisket, cut into 3 cm chunks牛肩肉或牛腩 600 克，切 3 厘米块
  • Wheat noodles挂面/面条 400 g · 400 g wide wheat noodles宽面条 400 克
  • Doubanjiang豆瓣酱 24 g · 1.5 tbsp doubanjiang豆瓣酱 1.5 汤匙
  • Soy sauce生抽 36 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Dark soy sauce老抽 6 g · 1 tsp1 茶匙
  • Shaoxing wine料酒(绍兴) 30 g · 2 tbsp2 汤匙
  • Sugar白糖 8 g · 2 tsp (rock sugar is traditional)2 茶匙（冰糖更传统）
  • Tomatoes西红柿 200 g · 1 large tomato, cut in wedges1 个大番茄，切块
  • Onions洋葱 100 g · half an onion, cut in chunks半个洋葱，切块
  • Ginger姜 20 g · a knob, sliced1 块，切片
  • Garlic大蒜 20 g · 6 cloves, smashed6 瓣，拍扁
  • Green onions大葱/小葱 40 g · 4 stalks, cut into lengths, a little sliced for garnish4 根，切段，留一点葱花
  • Bay leaves月桂叶 1 g · 2 leaves (add 2 star anise if you have them)2 片（有八角加 2 个）
  • Sichuan peppercorn花椒 2 g · 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns花椒 1 茶匙
  • Canola oil菜籽油 21 g · 1.5 tbsp1.5 汤匙

Method做法

  1. Put the beef in cold water, bring to a boil, skim, boil 3 more minutes, then drain and rinse.牛肉块冷水下锅，烧开撇沫再煮 3 分钟，捞出洗净。
  2. Fry the doubanjiang in the oil over low heat for a minute until the oil turns red.锅里放油，小火炒豆瓣酱 1 分钟到出红油。
  3. Add the ginger, garlic, green onion, onion and Sichuan pepper and stir until fragrant.加姜、蒜、葱段、洋葱和花椒炒香。
  4. Add the beef, stir 2 minutes and splash the wine down the side of the pan.下牛肉翻炒 2 分钟，沿锅边淋料酒。
  5. Add the soy, dark soy, sugar, tomato, bay leaves and 2 L boiling water and bring to a boil.加生抽、老抽、糖、番茄、月桂叶和 2 升开水，烧开。
  6. Simmer with the lid ajar for 1.5-2 hours, until chopsticks slide easily into the beef.转小火，盖盖留缝炖 1.5–2 小时到牛肉用筷子能轻松戳透。
  7. Fish out the aromatics and taste the broth for salt.捞出香料渣，尝汤咸淡。
  8. Boil the noodles in a separate pot, adding the greens for the last minute.另一锅水煮面，最后 1 分钟放青菜一起烫。
  9. Lift noodles and greens into bowls, ladle over beef and hot broth and add the green onion.面和青菜捞进碗里，浇牛肉和热汤，撒葱花。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Blanching the beef first gives a clean, clear broth.牛肉先焯水再炖，汤才清亮，没有腥味。
  • The tomato melts into the broth and adds a little sweet-sour depth beyond salt.番茄会炖化在汤里，带出一点酸甜，让汤不只是咸。
  • Always cook the noodles separately; boiling them in the broth makes it cloudy and thick.面一定另锅煮，在汤里煮会把汤弄浑、弄稠。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The beef is still tough after hours牛肉炖了很久还是硬
    The cut was too lean or the boil too hard. Use chuck or brisket, keep it at a bare simmer and give it 30 more minutes.部位太瘦或火太大。用带筋的牛肩或牛腩，保持微滚，再多炖 30 分钟。
  • The broth is too salty汤太咸
    Bean pastes vary. Dilute with boiling water and next time halve the soy, adding more at the end to taste.豆瓣酱品牌咸度不一。加开水稀释，下一次生抽减半，最后再按口味补。
