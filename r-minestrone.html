Soup汤 · Italian意

Minestrone意式蔬菜汤（Minestrone）

The clear-out-the-fridge soup of Italian homes, which takes any vegetable. Onion, carrot and celery make the base, then tomatoes, beans, zucchini and a handful of small pasta, finished with parmesan. Vegetables, beans and pasta in one pot make a meatless dinner with bread.意大利家庭清冰箱的汤，什么蔬菜都能放。洋葱、胡萝卜、西芹打底，加番茄、豆子、西葫芦和一把小面，最后撒帕玛森奶酪。一锅有蔬菜、豆子和主食，配面包就是一顿素食晚餐。

55 min total分钟 (15 + 40)6 servings份Easy简单Costco-friendlyCostco 可买

  • Onions洋葱 150 g · 1 small onion, diced1 个小洋葱，切丁
  • Carrots胡萝卜 150 g · 2 small carrots, diced2 根小胡萝卜，切丁
  • Celery西芹 150 g · 3 celery stalks, diced3 根西芹，切丁
  • Garlic大蒜 15 g · 4 cloves, minced4 瓣，切末
  • Zucchini西葫芦 200 g · 1 zucchini, diced1 根西葫芦，切丁
  • Canned diced tomatoes番茄罐头(碎) 800 g · 1 can (796 mL) crushed tomatoes番茄碎罐头 1 罐（796 mL）
  • Canned chickpeas鹰嘴豆罐头 540 g · 1 can (540 mL) chickpeas, drained (cannellini beans are more traditional)鹰嘴豆罐头 1 罐（540 mL），沥水（白芸豆更传统）
  • Chicken broth鸡高汤 1000 g · 4 cups (1 L) stock; vegetable stock for a vegetarian pot高汤 4 杯（1 升），素食可用蔬菜高汤

  • Black pepper黑胡椒 1 g · a few grinds少许

Method做法

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat and soften the onion, carrot and celery for 8 minutes.大锅中火热橄榄油，下洋葱、胡萝卜、西芹炒 8 分钟到变软。
  2. Add the garlic and oregano for 30 seconds.加蒜末和牛至炒 30 秒。
  3. Add the tomatoes, stock, bay leaf and 2 cups water, bring to a boil and simmer 15 minutes.倒入番茄碎、高汤、月桂叶和 2 杯水，烧开后小火煮 15 分钟。
  4. Add the zucchini, chickpeas and pasta and simmer 10 minutes until the pasta is done.加西葫芦、鹰嘴豆和面段，煮 10 分钟到面熟。
  5. Stir in the kale for 2 minutes, remove the bay leaf and season with salt and pepper.放羽衣甘蓝煮 2 分钟，取出月桂叶，加盐和胡椒。
  6. Serve with parmesan and a drizzle of olive oil.盛碗后撒帕玛森奶酪，淋几滴橄榄油。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Slowly softening the onion, carrot and celery builds the base sweetness; do not rush to add liquid.洋葱胡萝卜西芹慢炒出甜味，是整锅汤的底味，别急着加水。
  • Drop in a parmesan rind while it simmers for deeper savouriness, and remove it before serving.有帕玛森奶酪硬皮就丢进汤里一起煮，鲜味会更足，吃前捞出。
  • The pasta keeps absorbing broth, so add water when reheating the next day.面在汤里会一直吸水，第二天要加水再热。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • It has turned thick and stodgy汤太稠像糊
    The pasta drank the broth. Thin with boiling water or stock and re-season.面吸了汤。加开水或高汤调稀，再补一点盐。
  • The vegetables are mushy蔬菜煮得太烂
    Add the zucchini and greens last, in the order given.西葫芦和绿叶菜最后才放，按本页顺序下锅。
