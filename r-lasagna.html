Classic Lasagna千层面

Layers of ragù, béchamel and pasta, finished with bubbling cheese, make a generous dish for family gatherings. The real work is a thick meat sauce, a smooth white sauce, and a finished lasagna that holds its shape when cut.肉酱、白酱和面皮层层叠起，出炉时芝士冒泡，静置后切面整齐，适合家庭聚餐一次做多份。难点在于肉酱收得够浓、白酱顺滑不结块，以及烤透后不散架。

120 min total分钟 (30 + 75)6 servings份Medium中等Costco for the main itemsCostco 买主料 · buy elsewhere: Red wine另购：红酒(烹饪用)

Method做法

  1. Dice the onion, carrot and celery, and mince the garlic.洋葱、胡萝卜、芹菜切成小丁，蒜切末。
  2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium and soften the diced vegetables for about 5 minutes.炖锅放橄榄油，中火下蔬菜丁炒软约 5 分钟。
  3. Add the garlic and cook for 30-60 seconds until fragrant, then add the beef.加入蒜末炒 30–60 秒出香，再下牛肉末。
  4. Break up the beef with a spoon and cook about 8 minutes until it browns lightly on the surface and the moisture has evaporated.用勺把牛肉炒散，炒到肉散开、表面上色微焦、水汽收干，约 8 分钟。
  5. Stir in the tomato paste for 1 minute until dark, pour in the wine and reduce by half.下番茄膏炒 1 分钟到颜色变深，淋入红酒，煮到收浓一半。
  6. Add the canned tomatoes, bay leaf and oregano along with about half the salt, crush the tomatoes, cover and simmer 30 minutes over low heat; remove the bay leaf and season with the rest of the salt and pepper after it reduces.倒入罐装番茄、香叶和牛至，先加约一半盐，把番茄压碎，小火加盖炖 30 分钟；拣出香叶，收汁后再补盐和胡椒调味。
  7. In another pot melt the butter, whisk in the flour and cook 2 minutes over low until the raw smell is gone.另取小锅融化黄油，拌入面粉，小火炒 2 分钟到无生粉味。
  8. Off the heat, gradually whisk in the warm milk until smooth, return to low heat and cook 3 minutes until thick; season and stir in half the parmesan.离锅分次冲入温牛奶，边倒边搅到顺滑，再回小火煮 3 分钟变稠，加盐、胡椒和一半帕玛森。
  9. Using a baking dish about 20x30 cm (about 3 L), spread béchamel on the bottom, lay in pasta, then ragù, béchamel and a little mozzarella; repeat for three layers, using about 1/3 of the ragù and 1/3 of the béchamel per layer.用约 20×30 厘米（约 3 升）的烤盘，底部抹一层白酱，铺面皮，依次铺肉酱、白酱和少量马苏里拉，重复叠三层，每层约用 1/3 的肉酱和 1/3 的白酱。
  10. Cover the top with the rest of the béchamel and scatter over the remaining parmesan and mozzarella.最上面盖满白酱，撒剩余帕玛森和马苏里拉。
  11. Preheat the oven to 180°C. Oil the underside of the foil, or tent it so it does not touch the cheese, cover and bake 30 minutes, then uncover and bake 15-20 minutes until the edges are bubbling and a knife inserted in the center slides in with no resistance.烤箱预热 180°C，锡纸内侧刷一点油或略微撑起不贴着芝士，盖锡纸烤 30 分钟，揭掉锡纸再烤 15–20 分钟，至边缘冒泡、刀尖插入中心顺滑无阻力。
  12. Let it rest 15 minutes before cutting so the layers set.出炉静置 15 分钟再切，让每层定型。

Keys to success成功要点

  • Finely diced vegetables give a smooth ragù. Brown the beef until dry before adding the paste, so the sauce stays deep rather than watery and sour.蔬菜丁切得越细，肉酱口感越细腻；牛肉先炒干水汽再下番茄膏，酱才不会发酸发水。
  • Warm milk added gradually while whisking prevents lumps; cook the roux until the raw flour smell disappears.白酱用温牛奶、分次冲入并持续搅拌，最不容易结块；黄油面粉糊要炒到没有生粉气味。
  • Dried sheets need enough sauce to hydrate; do not pack the layers tight, or they stay undercooked and hard to cut.干面皮要靠足量酱汁润湿，铺层时不要压得太满，否则烤不透也切不开。
  • Resting sets the layers; cutting straight out of the oven makes it fall apart. Fifteen minutes gives neat slices and stretchy, not spilling, cheese.出炉静置是定型的关键，刚出炉切会散；放 15 分钟后切面整齐，芝士也不会烫到拉丝满盘。

Common problems常见失败与补救

  • The pasta is hard and undercooked面皮发硬、夹生
    There was not enough sauce to hydrate the sheets. Parboil the pasta next time, add more béchamel between layers, and let it rest longer after baking.酱汁量不足或烤前面皮没润湿；下次按包装先把面皮煮软，或在两层之间多加白酱，烤后再静置久一点。
  • The ragù is thin and too acidic肉酱稀、偏酸
    Simmer uncovered for 10 more minutes and a pinch of sugar balances the tomato acidity. Reduce the sauce on the stove before assembling if it is already thin.开盖多收 10 分钟，加一小撮糖平衡番茄酸；已经偏稀的肉酱可以先回锅煮浓再入烤碗。
  • The béchamel is lumpy白酱结块
    Lumps come from pouring in milk too fast or too hot. Pass the sauce through a sieve, return to low heat and keep whisking until smooth.牛奶一次倒太多或温度太高会结块；把过筛后的白酱放回小火，持续搅拌几分钟，结块能部分化开。
